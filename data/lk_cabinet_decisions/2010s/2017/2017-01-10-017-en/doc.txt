Allocation of a land at Denzil Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Battaramulla to the Department of Divineguma Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to release a plot of land belonging to the Urban Development Authority situated at the Denzil Kobbekaduwa Mawatha, Battaramulla, to the Department of Divineguma Development  to construct its Head Office, was approved by the Cabinet.
