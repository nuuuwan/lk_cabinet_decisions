Development of the Vattarama Sri Arahantha Maliyadewa Raja Maha Vihara sacred area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-03

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Buddha Sasana, to generate the additional provision of Rs.35 Million required for the completion of the development activities of the Vattarama Sri Arahantha Maliyadewa Raja Maha Vihara sacred area  situated in the Divisional Secretary's Division of Galigamuwa in the Kegalle District where the State Vesak Festival of 2017 is scheduled to be held, was approved by the Cabinet.
