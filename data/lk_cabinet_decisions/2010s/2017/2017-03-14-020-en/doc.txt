Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and Russia for Cooperation on Science, Technology and Innovation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-03-14

- The Ministry of Science, Technology and Research of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Education and Science of Russia have agreed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries with a view to promoting Cooperation on Science, Technology and Innovation. In terms of the MoU it is expected to exchange  specialists and scientific information, organize seminars and symposia, joint research, technological studies and set‑up joint scientific research centers and groups in the relevant fields coming under the  MoU. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to sign the MoU during the scheduled Heads of States visit of H.E. the President to the Russian Federation in March 2017, was approved by the Cabinet.
