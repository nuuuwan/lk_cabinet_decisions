Establishment of the Anuradhapura Regional Office of the Ayurvedic Drugs Corporation of Sri Lanka, the Sales Centre and the Medicine Purchasing  Centre in one and the same building

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-09-26

- The requirement of establishing the Regional Office of the Ayurvedic Drugs Corporation of Sri Lanka, the Sales Centre and the Medicine Purchasing Centre located at different places in the Anuradhapura town, in one and the same premises, has been identified.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to vest the block of Government land and the abandoned building situated at the Public Stadium Mawatha in Anuradhapura, given on lease to M/s. Lanka Salusala Ltd.,  with the Ayurvedic Drugs Corporation of Sri Lanka, on a long term lease basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
