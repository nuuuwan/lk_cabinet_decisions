Award of the Contract for the Construction of the New Habarana Grid substation and Augmentation of the Veyangoda Grid substation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-06-20

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract  for the Construction of the New Habarana Grid substation and Augmentation of the Veyangoda Grid substation to M/s Siemens Ltd., India, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
