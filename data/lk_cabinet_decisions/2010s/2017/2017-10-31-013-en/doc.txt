Upgrading of the Municipal Car Park in the Kandy City

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-31

- It has been designed to upgrade the  Municipal Car Park Roof Top in the Kandy City under the Strategic Cities Development Project. The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to award the above contract to M/s Sierra Construction (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs 654.11 million, excluding taxes, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
