Issuance of passenger transport permits for deployment of Buses in the Southern Expressway

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala De Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, for the amendment of the relevant sections of the National Transport Commission Act, for the revision of the relevant existing criteria presently in force regarding the issuance of passenger transport permits for deployment of buses in the Southern Expressway,  in accordance with the  advice of the Attorney General and the Order made by the Supreme Court and to call for fresh tenders for the issuance of the Passenger Transport Permits of the Southern Expressway, giving priority to the bus route permit holders operating in the Galle Road, was approved by the Cabinet.
