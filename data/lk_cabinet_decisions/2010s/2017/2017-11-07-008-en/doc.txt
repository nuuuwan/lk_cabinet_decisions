Implementation of the expansion of Pipe Borne Sewerage Coverage in the Dehiwala-Mt. Lavinia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to secure funds for the project for the expansion of Pipe Borne Sewerage Coverage in the Urban Area of Dehiwala-Mt.Lavinia subject to high urbanization and to award the contract of the project to M/s Vinci Construction Grands Projects/LUDWIG PFEIFFER Hoch‑u Tiefabau GmBH & Co., was approved by the Cabinet.
