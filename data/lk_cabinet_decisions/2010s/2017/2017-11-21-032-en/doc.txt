Supply of fertilizer for the Maha Season - 2017/2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Agriculture, to award the tender for procuring 72,000 MT of Urea required for the distribution of fertilizer for the Maha Season 2017/2018 by the Government Fertilizer Companies, to M/s JAT Holding (Pvt.) Ltd. which has successfully submitted the bid in this regard, for a sum of US$ 23.57 million being US$ 327.40 per MT, was approved by the Cabinet.
