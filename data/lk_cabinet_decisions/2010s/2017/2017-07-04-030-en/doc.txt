Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Bangladesh and Sri Lanka in the field of Information and Communication Technology and the related Industries

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- Recommendations have been made to sign a MoU between Bangladesh and Sri Lanka with the objective of broadening the development of corporation pertaining to the field of  Information and Communication Technology and the field of Digital Technology.  The two countries have reached consensus on the diplomatic level in this regard and accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication and Digital Infrastructure to sign the MoU during the forthcoming State visit of H.E. the President to Bangladesh, was approved by the Cabinet.
