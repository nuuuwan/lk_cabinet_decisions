Construction of an Office Complex for the Ceylon Electricity Board in the City of Anuradhapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the construction of an office complex in the land belonging to the Ceylon Electricity Board, located at  Maithripala Senanayake Mawatha, Anuradhapura, to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder at the cost of Rs.213.4 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
