Awarding of contracts for buildings to be constructed at Universities of Moratuwa, Uva Wellassa and the University of Visual and Performing Arts

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for the construction of a building extension for the Department of Textile and Clothing Technology of the University of Moratuwa, development of infrastructure facilities at the Uva Wellassa University for conducting Degree Programmes under the Technology Stream and construction of a building complex for the Faculty of  Music and Common Amenities of the University of the Visual and Performing Arts, on the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
