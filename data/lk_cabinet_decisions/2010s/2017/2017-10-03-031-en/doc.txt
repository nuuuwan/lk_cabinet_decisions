Procurement of Oil and Lubricant for the Sri Lanka Transport Board buses

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the procurement of Oil and Lubricant for the Sri Lanka Transport Board buses to the M/s. Lanka IOC PLC and M/s. Chevron Lubricant Lanka PLC, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
