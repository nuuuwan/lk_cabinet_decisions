Construction of the Nadukuda Grid Substation and augmentation of the 	Mannar Grid Substation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the construction of the Nadukuda Grid Substation and augmentation of the Mannar Grid Substation under the Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme for strengthening the transmission network of the Ceylon Electricity Board, to M/s. Seimens Ltd., India, for a sum of Rs.2,697.96 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
