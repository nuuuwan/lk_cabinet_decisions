Procurement for the Supply of Hypodermic Syringes necessary for the Government Hospitals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the procurement of Hypodermic Syringes of various sizes to inject substances and extract fluids, in accordance with the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and the Procurement Appeals Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
