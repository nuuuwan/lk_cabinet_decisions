Renovation of the Sabaragamuwa Saman Devalaya, Ratnapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-10

- In a survey carried out by  the Department of Archaeology and some institutions, it has been revealed that the parts decayed in the roofs and the other buildings of the Ratnapura Maha Saman Devalaya where a large number of pilgrims visit daily need to be renovated expeditiously. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to finance the renovation process of the Devalaya safeguarding its  authenticity, was approved by the Cabinet.
