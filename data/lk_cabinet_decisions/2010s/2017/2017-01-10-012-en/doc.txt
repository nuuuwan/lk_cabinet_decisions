Agreement between Sri Lanka and Iran on Science, Technology and Innovation Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-10

- Submissions made to the Cabinet by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Iran during his official visit to Iran between 14 to 17 January, 2017, to enhance the cooperation between the two countries in matters related to the  implementation of technology development projects and programmes on Food and Herbal Technology, Bio-technology, Nano Technology and Advanced Material Technology Sectors, Integrated Research and Development, Exchange of Scientific and Technology Personnel and Management of Innovative Projects, was approved by the Cabinet.
