Upgrading of Kundasale-Haragama Water Supply Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-24

- Approval of the Cabinet has already been granted to implement Kundasale-Haragama Water Supply Scheme with foreign aid assistance. However, taking into consideration the difficulties faced by 6000 families in 14 villages  in the Pathahewaheta and Kundasale Divisional Secretariat Divisions, due to the lack of safe drinking water caused through  the delay in implementing the project, the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, the Minister of Social Empowerment, Welfare and Kandyan Heritage, to upgrade the Kundasale‑Haragama Water Supply Scheme to fulfill the water needs of the public in 14 villages including Anuragama, Pubudugama and Mudunakade where Samurdhi beneficiaries and low income earning families live, utilizing the funds of the Samurdhi Development Fund established in respect of the common development activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
