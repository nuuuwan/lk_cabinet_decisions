State Vesak Festival of the year 2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-17

- The proposal  made by Hon. Gamini Jayawickrema Perera, the Minister of Buddhasasana, to conduct  the State Vesak Festival of the year 2018 of 2562nd year of Buddhist Era, centralizing  the historic Bingiriya Raja Maha Viharaya in the Kurunegala District and to  declare the 26th April to 02nd May of 2018 as the 'Vesak Week', was approved by the Cabinet.
