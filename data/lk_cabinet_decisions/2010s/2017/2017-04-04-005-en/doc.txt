Implementation of the Kandy Tunnel Construction Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-04

- It has been planned to construct a Tunnel of 4.36 km. in length from  Thennekumbura area up to Suduhumpola and a four (04) lane road way 1.2 km. in length, in order to mitigate the heavy traffic congestion existing in the  Kandy City at an estimated cost amounting to US$ 252.30. The Economic Development Cooperation Fund of Korea has agreed to provide necessary financing facility of US$ 199.27 for the project and accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to implement the Kandy Tunnel Construction Project and to vest the authority with the Department of External Resources to conduct negotiations on the  terms and conditions of the loan, was approved by the Cabinet.
