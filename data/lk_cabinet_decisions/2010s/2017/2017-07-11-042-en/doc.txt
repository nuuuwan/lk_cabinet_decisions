Mid-Year Fiscal Position Report

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to table the 'Mid-Year Fiscal Position Report' inclusive of the updated information pertaining to the first four (04) months of the year 2017, in Parliament as stipulated in section 10 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No.03 of 2003, was approved by the Cabinet.
