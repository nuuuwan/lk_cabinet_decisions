Memorandum of Understanding in the field of Tourism between the Russian Federation and Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-03-21

- The proposal made by Hon. John Amarathunga, the Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on cooperation in the field of tourism between the Ministry of Culture in the Russian Federation and the Ministry of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs of Sri Lanka during the scheduled forthcoming official visit to Russia by Maithripala Sirisena, H.E. the President, with a view to promoting friendly relations between the two countries and for the contribution in the field of economic and tourism development, was approved by the Cabinet.
