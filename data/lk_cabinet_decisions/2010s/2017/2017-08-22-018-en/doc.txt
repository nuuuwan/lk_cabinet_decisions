Re-laying of the synthetic running track of the Sugathadasa National Sports Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-22

- The proposal made by Hon. Dayasiri Jayasekara, the Minister of Sports, to award the contract for the re‑laying of the 400 meter Class 1 synthetic running track and the 200 meter synthetic running track of the Sugathadasa National Sports Complex to Access - Conica JV for a sum of Rs.269.33 million, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
