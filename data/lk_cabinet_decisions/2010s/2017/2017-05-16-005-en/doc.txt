Transfer of plots of land to construct the Office of the Superintendent of Police and the Divisional Secretariat, Elpitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala De Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to transfer a plot of land in extent of 140 perches from the Elpitiya Depot land owned by the Sri Lanka Transport Board to construct the Office of the Superintendent of Police and a land in extent of 01 acre for the construction of  the Divisional Secretariat, Elpitiya under the proposed Elpitiya Town Development Plan, was approved by the Cabinet.
