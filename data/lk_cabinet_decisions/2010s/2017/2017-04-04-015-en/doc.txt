Award of contract for providing consultancy services for design review and construction supervision for Section-1 of the Central Expressway Project from Kadawatha to Meerigama  (36.59 km)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for providing consultancy services for design review and construction supervision for Section-1 of the Central Expressway Project from Kadawatha to Meerigama (36.59 km), for a sum of Rs. 2,548.91 million to a joint venture of Resources Development Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd. with Green Tech Consultant (Pvt.) Ltd. and Consulting Engineers and Architects Associated (Pvt.) Ltd., as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
