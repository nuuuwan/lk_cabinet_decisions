Agreement to be signed between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Korea  on Cooperation in the fields of Culture, Arts, Education, Youth activities and Sports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Thilak Marapana, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to sign an Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Korea with a view to strengthening the mutual friendly relations existing  between the two countries and promoting cooperation in the fields of Culture, Arts, Education, Youth activities and Sports, was approved by the Cabinet.
