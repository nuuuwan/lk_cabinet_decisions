Procurement of the drugs used for the treatment of patients with immunity deficiencies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-09

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the procurement of 6,500 Vials of Human Immunoglobulin for IV use BP, 5-6g, an essential drug used for the treatment of patients with immunity deficiencies, for a sum of US$ 637,000 as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
