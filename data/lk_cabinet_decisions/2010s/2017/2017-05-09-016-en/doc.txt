Kandy North - Pathadumbara Integrated Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-09

- The Kandy North -  Pathadumbara Integrated Water Supply Project aims at providing safe pipe born water supply facilities to 6 Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Pathadumabara, Pujapitiya, Akurana, Harispattuwa, Gangawatakorale and Thumpane.  The Cabinet has granted approval to enter into commercial contracts for the implementation of these projects. The EXIM Bank of China has agreed to provide a concessionary loan to cover the entire cost of the project. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, to enter into a loan Agreement to obtain the loan, was approved by the Cabinet.
