Establishment of a Task Force and an Operating Office for the conduct of Beautification of the Colombo City and Suburbs

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-25

- Considering the problematic situation created by the informal disposal of waste in the Colombo city and of the closest suburbs, by the Local Government  Institutions, the proposal made by Hon. Faiszer Musthapha, Minister of Provincial Councils and Local Government, to establish a Task Force and an Operating Office for the management of waste with the collaboration of the state institutions directly linked with the management of solid waste, and to create awareness among the public of the relevant formal methods of waste disposal concurrent to waste management, was approved by the Cabinet.
