Allocation of a land to the Sri Lanka Police for the establishment of the Galewela Police Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-06-27

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a portion of land in extent of 1½ Acres from the land of about 24 Acres situated in the Galewela Town, acquired by the Urban Development Authority under the Greater Dambulla Development Plan, with the Sri Lanka Police for the construction of the Galewela Police Station, was approved by the Cabinet.
