Amendments to the Sugarcane Research Institute Act, No.75 of 1981

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-31

- The Sugarcane Research Institute Act, No.75 of 1981 has been enacted with a view to providing facilities for researches conducted in the field of sugarcane cultivation. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Wasantha Perera, the Acting Minister of Plantation Industries, to amend the Sugarcane Research Institute Act, to extend the activities of the Sugarcane Research Institute to enable the   development of the sugarcane cultivation sector as well without limiting it to that of the institute only, by further expanding the scope of the Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
