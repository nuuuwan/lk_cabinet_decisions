Provision of the required facilities for the Medical Faculties of Rajarata and Eastern Universities and the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences of the University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-10

- It has become an urgent need to provide the necessary facilities with the required medical equipment and accessories essential for the faculties of Rajarata and Eastern Universities and the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences of the University of Peradeniya to uplift the quality and teaching and training facilities of the above faculties in par with the other State Universities. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, to conduct negotiations to obtain  the necessary funds from a Bank in Australia under concessionary terms and to enter into a loan Agreement in that regard, was approved by the Cabinet.
