Construction of access roads, drains and street lights of the housing complex in the Victoria Estate, Norochcholai, Puttalam

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-09-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the construction of the access roads, drains and street lights of the housing complex in the Victoria Estate, Norochcholai, Puttalam under Phase II of the Lakvijaya Power Station to M/s. Sierra Construction (Pvt) Ltd., the lowest substantially responsive bidder for a sum of Rs.332.7 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
