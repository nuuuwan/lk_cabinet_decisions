Agreement for the establishment of a Joint Commission on cooperation between Sri Lanka and Ethiopia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to sign the Agreement for the establishment of a Joint Commission on cooperation between the Government of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia during his official visit to Ethiopia, with a view to promoting and reinforcing the cooperation  between the two countries, on political, economical, technical and cultural based on the principle of equality and mutual advantages, was approved by the Cabinet.
