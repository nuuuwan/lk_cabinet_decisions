Galle Port Yacht Marina Development of Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga,  the Minister of Ports and Shipping, to introduce a suitable developer/investor willing to carry out the development and operation of the Galle Port Yacht Marina and to call for proposals following the 'Swiss Challenge' procedures to operate the related business, in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Economic Management Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
