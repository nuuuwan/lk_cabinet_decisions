Procurement of Fertilizer required for the Government Fertilizer Companies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Agriculture, to purchase the required 18,000 MT of Urea and 3,000 MT of Triple Super Phosphate (TSP) by Ceylon Fertilizer Company Limited (Lak Pohora) and Colombo Commercial Fertilizer Company Limited (Commercial Fertilizer) operating under the Ministry of Agriculture, from M/s. Quantum Fertilizers Ltd. and M/s. Haiyork Holding Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
