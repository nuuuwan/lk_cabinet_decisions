Construction of a building complex for the National Institute of Social Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-09-12

- The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, the Minister of Social Empowerment, Welfare and Kandyan Heritage, to award the contract to construct a building complex on design and build basis for the National Institute of Social Development in the Seeduwa area, to the State Engineering Corporation of Sri Lanka, at a cost of Rs.818.63 million, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
