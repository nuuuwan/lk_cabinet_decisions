Allocation of a plot of land for the High Commission of Pakistan in Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-28

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to allocate a plot of land in extent of 1.5 Acres, located at Barnes Place, Colombo 07, to the High Commission of Pakistan earmarked as suitable to build its Chancery Building, was approved by the Cabinet.
