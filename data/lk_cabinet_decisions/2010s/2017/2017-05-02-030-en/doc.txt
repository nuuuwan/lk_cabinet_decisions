Submission of the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2016 to the Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-02

- Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs submitted the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka 2016 prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Monetary Law Act which includes the state of the Sri Lankan economy, Accounts of the Central Bank, the administrative measures adopted in the previous year, the important Bills passed and data to the Cabinet and  approval was granted by the Cabinet to present it in Parliament.
