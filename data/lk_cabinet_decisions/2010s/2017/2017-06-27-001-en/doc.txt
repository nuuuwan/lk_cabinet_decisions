Extradition Treaty between Sri Lanka and Vietnam

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-06-27

- The Extradition Treaty signed between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Socialist Republic of Vietnam with the objective of providing for more effective cooperation between the two countries in the prevention and suppression of crime was presented to the Cabinet by H.E. the President, the Minister of Defence, and approval was granted by the Cabinet for its implementation.
