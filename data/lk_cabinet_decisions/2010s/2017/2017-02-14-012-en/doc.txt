Providing relief for those who have faced various problems due to political reasons

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-14

- The Ministry of Public Administration and Management has received 31,666 appeals in terms of the Public Administration Circular No.09/2015 of 17-04-2015 issued with a view to providing relief for the officers of the public sector who were subject to political victimization.  Out of these, the recommendations in respect of 23,093 appeals have already been submitted to the Cabinet under 03 Phases and the Cabinet has granted approval for the implementation of those recommendations.     The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister of Public Administration and Management, to get the recommendations pertaining to 3,440 appeals relevant to six (06) Ministries of the balance of 8,573 appeals submitted as the 04th Phase, implemented by the relevant administrative authorities, was approved by the Cabinet.
