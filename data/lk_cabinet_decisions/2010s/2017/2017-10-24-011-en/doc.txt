Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Republic of Korea for sending workers under the Employment Permit System (EPS)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-24

- The proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Justice,  to extend the period of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed between the Ministry of Employment and Labour of the Republic of Korea and the Ministry of Foreign Employment of the Democratic Socialist Republic of  Sri Lanka in the year 2015 for a further period of 02 years, with a view to making provisions for implementing an employment permit system to enhance the transparency and efficiency of the process of providing employment for Sri Lankan workers in the Republic of Korea and to carry out suitable measures for cooperation between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
