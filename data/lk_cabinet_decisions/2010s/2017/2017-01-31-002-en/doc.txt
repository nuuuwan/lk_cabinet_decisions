The Programme on the Exchange of Students and Teachers of the Skilled Technology Field between Sri Lanka and Germany

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The College of Technology (BBS III) in Mainz, Germany has expressed its willingness to cooperate with the Department of Technical Education and Training of Sri Lanka functioning under the Ministry of Skills Development and Vocational Training, for the implementation of a Programme on the Exchange of Students and Teachers.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, the Minister of Skills Development and Vocational Training, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) for the Exchange of Students and Teachers between the Department of Technical Education and Training of Sri Lanka and the College of Technology of (BBS III) in Mainz, Germany, was approved by the Cabinet.
