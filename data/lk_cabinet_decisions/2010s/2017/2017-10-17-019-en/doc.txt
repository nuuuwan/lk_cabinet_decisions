Construction of a New Clinical Building at the Rehabilitation Hospital in Ragama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-17

- A proposal has been made to construct the new clinical building at the Ragama Rehabilitation Hospital which is the only institution in the country developed solely for the purpose of rehabilitation treatment.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the construction of the above building to M/s Link Engineering (Pvt) Ltd., for a sum of Rs 195 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the cabinet.
