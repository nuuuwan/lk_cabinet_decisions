Contract for the Supply and delivery of DI Pipes and Fittings for the Angunakolapelessa Pumping Main of Angunakolapelessa Water Supply Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-01

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply to award the contract for the supply and delivery of DI Pipes and Fittings required for the Angunakolapelessa Pumping Main of the Angunakolapelessa Water Supply Scheme which was implemented with the objective of providing safe drinking water to the new prison complex located in Eraminiyaya, Angunakolapelessa and for 2,000 families living in Angunakolapelessa area, at a cost of Rs.166.74 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
