Allocation of  the land called 'Sathyawadi' at Kurunegala for a Mixed Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to lease the above mentioned land in extent of  01Acre, 01Rood, 21.15Perches to M/s. Fairway Holdings (Pvt.) Limited for a mixed development project, in accordance with the recommendation of the Ministry Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
