Purchasing of Crew Cabs necessary for the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract to purchase 50 Short Wheel Crew Cabs necessary for the Distribution Division 2 of the CEB for a sum of Rs.185.6 million to M/s. Sathosa Motors PLC and the contract to purchase 15 Long Wheel Crew Cabs for a sum of Rs.56.5 million to the M/s. United Motors Lanka PLC as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
