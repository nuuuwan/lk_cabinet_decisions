Obtaining Consultancy Services for the Feasibility Study and the Detailed Design of Colombo Suburban Railway Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-12-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the Consultancy Services Contract for the   Feasibility Study and the Detailed Design of the project implemented with the objective of upgrading the railway system in Colombo and Suburban areas to M/s DOHWA Engineering Co. Ltd., and its partners for a sum of 256,030 Sterling Pounds, US$ 6,481,575 and Rs.379,213,616 as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
