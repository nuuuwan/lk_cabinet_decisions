Procurement of equipment required for the Combat Battalion participating in Peace Keeping Operations in Mali

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-25

- Limited International Competitive bids have been called to procure the equipment  required for the Combat Battalion to engage in peace keeping duties in Mali on behalf of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as  the Minister of Defence, to purchase relevant equipment from the three (03) substantially responsive institutions, as recommended by the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
