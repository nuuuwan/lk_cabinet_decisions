Making a Feasibility Study on the Greater Mannar Water Supply Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-16

- A proposal has been made for the implementation of the Greater Mannar Water Supply Scheme with the objective of alleviating the difficulty in supplying of drinking water which has become a major problem for the people of the Mannar District. As such, in accordance with the discussion, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply had with the Minister of Irrigation and Water Resources Management with regard to the fulfillment of the future water supply needs of the Manner area and the issues that may crop up while distributing water resources with several water supply projects, the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to make a feasibility study and to conduct the study at the expense of the institution which had made the proposal and to adopt the Swiss Challenge Procurement Procedure for the implementation of the project, was approved by the Cabinet.
