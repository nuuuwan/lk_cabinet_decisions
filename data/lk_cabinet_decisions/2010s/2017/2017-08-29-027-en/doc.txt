Providing relief for Temples situated in areas affected as a result of the drought prevailing in the Island

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Gamini Jayawickrema Perera, the Minister of Buddhasasana and the Minister of Sustainable Development and Wildlife, to grant  relief to the Temples situated in areas affected by floods as well similar to the relief granted to the people affected by the severe drought prevailing in some parts of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
