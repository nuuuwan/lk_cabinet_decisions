Construction of a new seventeen storey office building and four workshops in lieu of the affected buildings that belong to the Sri Lanka Ports Authority  due to the Port Access Elevated Highway Project funded by the  Asian Development Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, for the construction of a new seventeen storey office building and four workshops in lieu of the affected buildings that belong to the Sri Lanka Ports Authority  due to the Port Access Elevated Highway Project funded by the  Asian Development Bank, was approved by the Cabinet.
