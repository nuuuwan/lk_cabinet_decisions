Construction of the memorial of the Most. Venerable Maduluwawe Sobitha Thero

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-22

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, to construct a memorial in memory of the Most. Venerable Maduluwawe Sobitha Thero for the magnificent role played by the prelate in stabilizing true democracy, religious harmony and national reconciliation based on the principles of Good Governance, and to provide the financial contribution needed for the purpose by the Government, was approved by the Cabinet.
