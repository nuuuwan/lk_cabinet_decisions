Memorandum of Cooperation (MoC) between the National Science Foundation of Sri Lanka and the Science and Technology Agency of Japan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-09

- The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research to sign a Memorandum of Cooperation between the National Science Foundation of Sri Lanka and the Science and Technology Agency of Japan for the promotion of cooperation in the fields of science and technology, was approved by the Cabinet.
