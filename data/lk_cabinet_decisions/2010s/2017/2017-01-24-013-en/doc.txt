Digital Summit scheduled to be held in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-24

- It has been planned to hold a Digital Summit on the 23rd and 24th March, 2017, in Sri Lanka, with the objectives of attracting the innovators of the new technologies of the next generation to Asia and Sri Lanka to become a digital hub connecting the countries across South Asia and South East Asia. It is anticipated that experts from the internationally reputed Companies and the Executive Officers in the field such as Facebook, Google, Social Capital, Infosys will grace the occasion.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication and Digital Infrastructure, to initiate necessary activities pertaining to holding this Summit in Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
