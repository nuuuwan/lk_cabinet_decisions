Awarding the Contract for the Design and Building of St. Sebestian North Lock Gates and Pumping Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-17

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to award the contract for the Design and Building of St. Sebestian North Lock Gates and Pumping Station implemented under the Metro Colombo Urban Development Project financed by the World Bank Loan for controlling floods in the Colombo City, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
