Acquisition of the land where the Sri Lanka Police College of the Mahiyanganaya Unit is situated to the Sri Lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayake, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development to vest the land called Madayayawatta situated in Mahiyangana, in extent of Acres 141 Roods 3 and 11.03 Perches where the Sri Lanka Police College of the Mahiyanganaya Unit is established, with the Sri Lanka Police, was approved by the Cabinet.
