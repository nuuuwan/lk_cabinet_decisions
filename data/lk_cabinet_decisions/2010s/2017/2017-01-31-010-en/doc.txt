Procurement of Equipment for the Special Task Force (STF) troops to be deployed for UN Peacekeeping Operations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayaka, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, for the procurement of the items and equipment necessary for the Special Task Force (STF) troops to be deployed for UN Peacekeeping Operations, was approved by the Cabinet.
