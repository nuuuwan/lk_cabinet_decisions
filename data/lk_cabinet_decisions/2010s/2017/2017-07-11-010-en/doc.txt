Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Standards Institution and the Bangladesh Standards and Research Institution

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Standards Institution and the Bangaladesh Standards and Research Institution for cooperation, during the state visit of H.E. the President to Bangladesh, was approved by the Cabinet.
