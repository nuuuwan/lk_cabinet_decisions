Procurement for the purchase of Surgical Items

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-01

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contracts for the purchase of essential items and equipment used for Oral and Maxillofacial surgeries in accordance with the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
