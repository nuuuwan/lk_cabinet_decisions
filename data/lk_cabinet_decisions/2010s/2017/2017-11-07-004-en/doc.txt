Vesting of the lands utilized for the Parliamentary Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-07

- A part of the lands in extent of about 1,000 acres acquired from the areas of Battaramulla, Kaduwela, Sri Jayawardenapura and Maharagama by the Urban Development Authority (UDA) under the Administrative Capital City Development Programme of Sri Jayawardenapura, Kotte, have been utilized for the construction of the Parliamentary Complex and another part apportioned as water reserves. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest such portions of land which are utilized for the Parliamentary Complex but not formally vested up to now, with the Sri Lanka Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
