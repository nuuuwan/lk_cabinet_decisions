Improvement of the Navigational Services Complex Building at the Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-09

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation to award the tender for the construction of the proposed new extension to the Navigational Services Complex Building at the Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake for a sum of Rs.201.39 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
