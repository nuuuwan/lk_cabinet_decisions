Establishment of a 400 MW Natural Gas Power Plant in Hambantota

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-12-05

- Considering the importance of the introduction of natural gas as a resource for the energy generation field in Sri Lanka, deliberations have been made to establish a 400 MW Natural Gas Power Plant in Hambantota by the Governments of China and Sri Lanka jointly, on Government to Government basis. With the main objective of providing electricity to the industrial zone proposed to be established in Hambantota and avoiding the possible scarcity of electricity in future, it is due to be implemented as a special project. Accordingly, the  proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, to set up this Natural Gas Power Plant and the Natural Gas Unloading Terminal between the China Machinery Engineering Corporation (CMEC) nominated by the Government of China and the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) as a Joint Venture, was approved by the Cabinet.
