Implementation of the Hasalaka Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The Hasalaka Water Supply Project expects to provide 12,500 water supply connections covering 33 Grama Niladhari Divisions which would be beneficial to around 49,370 people. Therefore, the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, for the implementation of the Hasalaka Water Supply Project, in accordance with the recommendations of the Cabinet Committee on Economic Management and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
