Amendment to the Code of Criminal Procedure (Special Provisions) Act, No.2 of 2013 (Right of Detainees - Access to Legal Counsel)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-25

- In accordance with the approval granted by the Cabinet of Ministers to publish the Code of Criminal Procedure (Special Provisions) Act, No.02 of 2013 and to present it in Parliament for approval, the Bill, has been published in the Government Gazette of 03rd March 2017. The Cabinet noted that the facts submitted by Hon. Ranil Wickramasinghe, the Prime Minister to the effect that there is a recommendation made as appropriate to amend the procedure further, more formally, to ensure the right of access of a detainee to a legal counsel and that the proposed amendments will be included in the Bill during the Committee Stage of the Parliament.
