Development and Improvement of Godigamuwa Tank in the Matale District

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-10

- The Godigamuwa Tank situated in the Naula Divisional Secretary's Division in the Matale District has a capacity of about 45 acre feet and paddy and several other crops are cultivated using its water. Therefore, the proposal made by Hon. Vijith Vijayamuni Zoysa, the Minister of Irrigation and Water Resources Management, to implement  the project for the development and improvement of the Godigamuwa tank, with a view to ensuring the supply of irrigation water for 162 farmer families living in Dambagalla, Bambaragahawatta and Helambagahawatta in the Naula area, by increasing its capacity from 45 acre feet up to 202 acre feet, at an estimated cost of Rs.165.9 Million, was approved by the Cabinet.
