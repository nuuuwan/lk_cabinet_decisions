Memorandum of Understanding between the United Arab Emirates and Sri Lanka on Cooperation in the field of Higher Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Education - Higher Education Affairs of the United Arab Emirates and the Ministry of Higher Education and Highways of Sri Lanka on cooperation in the field of Higher Education, with a view to further strengthening the mutual cooperation and also to further improve the cooperation between the higher educational sectors of the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
