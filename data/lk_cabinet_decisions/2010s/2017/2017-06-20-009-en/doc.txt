Providing a Land to H.V.A. Farms (Private Limited) for an Environment Friendly Organic Fruits and Vegetable Cultivation Project on a Long Term Lease

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-06-20

- The proposal made by Hon. Gayantha Karunathilaka, the Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms, to provide a plot of land in extent of 69.9 hectares situated in the  Grama Niladhari Division of Ralmaduwa in the Wanathwilluwa Divisional Secretary's Division in the Puttlam District, to H.V.A. Farms (Private Limited) for an Environment Friendly Organic Fruits and Vegetable Cultivation Project on a 30 year long term lease basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
