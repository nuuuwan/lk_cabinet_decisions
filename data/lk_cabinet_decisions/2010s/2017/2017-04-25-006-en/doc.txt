Restoration of the damaged Agro Wells of the Dry Zone to mitigate the effects of the  drought condition

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-25

- The need has arisen to restore the damaged Agro Wells of the dry zone to a suitable standard to conduct agricultural activities successfully even under the drought conditions which may occur. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Agriculture, to implement the programme for the restoration of 14 Agro Wells of the dry zone, was approved by the Cabinet.
