Award of contract for the supply of services for the 02nd Phase of the Lanka Government Network Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication and Digital Infrastructure, to award the contract to the Sri Lanka Telecom, for the supply of services to the 2nd Phase of the Lanka Government Network (LGN) Project, implemented by the Information and Communication Technology Agency with a view to providing public services to the citizens and the business community without interruption by providing high speed and safe Internet connections to the Government Organizations, in accordance with the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Negotiation Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
