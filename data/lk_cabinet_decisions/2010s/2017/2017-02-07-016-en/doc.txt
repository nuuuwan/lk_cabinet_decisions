Holding of the 08th Meeting of SAARC Law & Order Ministers and the meeting of Secretaries and SAARC Immigration Authorities in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayake,  the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, to hold and host the 08th Meeting of SAARC Law & Order Ministers preceded by the meeting of the Secretaries to the Ministries of Law & Order and the next meeting of SAARC Immigration Authorities in Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet as a matter of policy.
