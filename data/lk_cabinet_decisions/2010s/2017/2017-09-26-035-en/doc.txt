Establishment of an Ayurveda Research Hospital on Non?Communicable Diseases at Ninthavur in the Ampara District

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-09-26

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the construction of the proposed  Ayurveda Research Hospital on Non‑Communicable Diseases at Ninthavur in the Ampara District to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, was approved by the Cabinet.
