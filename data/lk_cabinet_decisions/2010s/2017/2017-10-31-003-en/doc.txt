State Christmas Festival -2017

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-31

- The proposal made by Hon. John Amaratunga, the Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs, to hold the 2017 State Christmas Festival under the theme "Christmas, the Origin of Love" in order to share the spirit of Christmas with a deep sense of commitment under the aegis of the Trincomalee Diocese, as one nation, in one country, was approved by the Cabinet.
