Vesting of a land to the Sri Lanka Police for the establishment of the Yatawatta Police Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-23

- The proposal made by the Hon. Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, to vest a land in extent of Acres 02, Rood 01, Perches 35 from the Yatawatta Estate belonging to the Udapussellwa Plantations Limited with the Sri Lanka Police for the establishment of the Yatawatta Police Station, was approved by the Cabinet.
