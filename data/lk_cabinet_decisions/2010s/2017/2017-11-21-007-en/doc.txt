Bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Hellenic Republic (Greece)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to sign the Agreement presently agreed upon at the official level with a view to formalizing the Air Services between Sri Lanka and the Hellenic Republic (Greece) and subsequently, to enforce the provisions of the Agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
