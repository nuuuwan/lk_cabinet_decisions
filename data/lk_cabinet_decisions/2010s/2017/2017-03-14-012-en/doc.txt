Agreement on   Mutual Cooperation between Sri Lanka and the  Czech Republic in trade matters

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-03-14

- The proposal made by Hon. Malik Samarawickrama, the Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade, to enter into an Agreement between the Ministry of Development Strategies & International Trade of Sri Lanka and the  Ministry of Industry and Trade of the Czech Republic with a view to enhancing trade and economic cooperation between the two countries, providing facilities for bilateral investment promotion and identifying economic and investment opportunities, promotion of close trade and industrial cooperation and  facilitate networking activities in the entrepreneurial institutions in the two countries promoting greater understanding between the private sectors of the two countries regarding trade and economic policies and encouraging the cooperation in their areas of mutual interest, was approved by the Cabinet.
