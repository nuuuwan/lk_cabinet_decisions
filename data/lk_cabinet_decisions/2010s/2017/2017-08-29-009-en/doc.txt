International Symposium on Traditional and Complementary Medicine, Educational Exhibition and Trade Fair (TradMed International)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-29

- Expert attention has been focused to develop the evidence based practices of traditional medical system in diagnosis, management and prevention utilizing modern science and technology, diagnostic tools, Bio‑medical information system and improvement of products with quality and standard. The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to host an International Symposium and an Educational Exhibition and a Trade Fair during 23rd to 25th November 2017, named TradMed International 2017 - Sri Lanka,  in Colombo, Sri Lanka, with the objective of sharing the expertise and noval trends, was approved by the Cabinet.
