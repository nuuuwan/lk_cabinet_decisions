Relocation and improvement of the Technical College at Anamaduwa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Chandima Weerakkody, the Minister of  Skills Development and Vocational Training, to award the contract for the relocation and improvement of the Technical College at Anamaduwa to M/s. Antony Builders, the lowest substantive responsive bidder, in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
