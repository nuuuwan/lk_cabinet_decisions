Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and Poland for the promotion of sports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Dayasiri Jayasekara, the Minister of Sports, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and Poland for the development and promotion of sports with a view to strengthening the cooperation between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
