Allocation of a Land at the New Town of Ratnapura to the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council to construct the office of the Medical Officer of Health

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-09

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a plot of land of about 60 perches belonging to the Urban Development Authority situated in the New Town of Ratnapura to the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council for the construction of  the office of the Medical Officer of Health, Ratnapura, subject to the payment of the Government valuation, was approved by the Cabinet.
