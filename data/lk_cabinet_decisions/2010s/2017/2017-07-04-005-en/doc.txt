Vesting of the 'Ruwanpura' Urban Children's Park situated at the New Town of Ratnapura with the Ratnapura Municipal Council as a Free Grant

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a block of land in extent of 01Acre, 01Rood, 16.30 Perches out of the land acquired for the Urban Development Authority under the Ratnapura New Town Developm Project, with the Ratnapura Municipal Council as a free grant to maintain and continue the 'Ruwanpura Urban Children's Park' constructed by the Urban Authority,  was approved by the Cabinet.
