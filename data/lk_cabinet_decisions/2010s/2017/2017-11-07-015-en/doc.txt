Submission of the Report on 'Recent Economic Developments: Highlights of 2017 and Prospects for 2018' of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka to  Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, to present the Report published by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka on 'Recent Economic Developments: Highlights of 2017 and Prospects for 2018' inclusive of the performance of the Real Sector, External Sector, Fiscal Sector and the Monetary Sector', up to October 2017, in Parliament, to coincide with the 2018 budget, was approved by the Cabinet.
