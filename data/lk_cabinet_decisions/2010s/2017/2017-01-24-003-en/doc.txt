Transfer of the Railways Department Stores at Matale to the Municipal Council, Matale, on Lease Basis for constructing a Public Market

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to transfer the Stores of the Railways Department at Matale to the Municipal Council, Matale, on lease basis for constructing a public market, was approved by the Cabinet.
