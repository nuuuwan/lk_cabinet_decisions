Allocation of Land at New Town, Ratnapura for the construction of  the District Cultural Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a portion of land 1 Acre  in extent from the Farm Garden Estate at the New Town in Ratnapura, owned by the Urban Development Authority with the District Secretary of Ratnapura, for the establishment of the Ratnapura District Cultural Centre, was approved by the Cabinet.
