Procurement of Medical Examination Gloves and Surgical Rubber Gloves

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-10

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the procurement of 92 million pairs of medium and large size rubber gloves for a sum of Rs.482 million and 31 million pairs of  Surgical Rubber Gloves for a sum of Rs.1,196 million, to M/s. Sisili Project Consortium (Pvt) Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
