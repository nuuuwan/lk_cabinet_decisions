Award of contract for the construction of the Road from Lunugala to Bibila (from 171.80 km to 190.80 km) under the Badulla - Chenkalady Road Improvement Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-30

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways,  to award the contract for the construction of the Road from Lunugala to Bibila (from 171.80 km to 190.80 km) under the Badulla - Chenkalady Road Improvement Project as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
