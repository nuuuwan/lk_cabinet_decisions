Procurement of Food Items (raw, dry rations and various other items) required for the Three Armed Services

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-14

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to award the contracts for the procurement of food items (raw, dry rations and various other items) required for the Three Armed Services for the year 2017, to 28 suppliers for a total sum of Rs.17,555 Million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC),was approved by the Cabinet.
