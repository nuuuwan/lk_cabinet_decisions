Signing a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Australia for the upliftment in the field of Sports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Dayasiri Jayasekara, the Minister of Sports, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Australia, in respect of cooperation in the field of sports, with a view to strengthening the cooperation between the two countries in the fields of sports identified where the two countries have excelled, was approved by the Cabinet.
