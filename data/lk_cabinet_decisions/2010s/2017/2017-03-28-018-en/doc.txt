Awarding of contract for Consultancy Services for the  Seethawaka Ganga Hydro Power Project)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-03-28

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy,  to award the contract for the consultancy services of the proposed 20 MW Seethawaka Ganga Hydro Power Project to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, to be implemented in the village of Hinguralakanda Gama Niladhari Division of the Dehiowita Divisional Secretary's Division in the Kegalle District  was approved by the Cabinet.
