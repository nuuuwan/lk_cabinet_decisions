Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Sri Lanka Institute of Textile and Apparel (SLITA) and the Chittagong BGMEA Institute of Fashion and Technology (CBIFT), Bangladesh

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, the Minister of Industry and Commerce, to enter into the MoU between the Sri Lanka Institute of Textile and Apparel (SLITA) and the Chittagong BGMEA Institute of Fashion and Technology (CBIFT), Bangladesh during the forthcoming of state visit of H.E. the President to Bangladesh, with a view to promoting the collaboration in the educational and professional activities of the fashion and technology fields, was approved by the Cabinet.
