Deduru Oya Water Supply Project - Consultancy Service Design, Review and Construction Supervision

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-03-28

- The Government of Sri Lanka has entered into an agreement with the Economic Development Cooperation Fund  of the EXIM Bank of Korea in the year 2014 to obtain a credit facility of US $ 58.15 million for the implementation of Deduru Oya Water Supply Project. The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply to award the above contract for consultancy services, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee by utilizing the said credit facility, was approved by the Cabinet.
