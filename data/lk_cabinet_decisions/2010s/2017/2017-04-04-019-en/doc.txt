Construction of a Corporate Office Building Complex for the Distribution Division    No.4 of the Ceylon Electricity Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the construction of a Corporate Office Building Complex for the Distribution Division No.4 of the Ceylon Electricity Board to M/s. Sripalie Contractors (Pvt.) Limited, the lowest substantial responsive bidder at a cost of Rs.466.66 (valued without VAT) million as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
