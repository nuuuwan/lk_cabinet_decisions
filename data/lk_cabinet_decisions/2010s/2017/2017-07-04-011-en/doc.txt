Commemoration of the World Post Day 2017 - Polonnaruwa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- Arrangements have been made to hold the 143rd Anniversary of the  Commemoration of the World Post Day which falls on the 09th of October 2017 centered around the North Central Province. Accordingly, Hon. M.H.A. Haleem, the Minister of Posts, Postal Services and Muslim Religious Affairs informed the Cabinet that action has been taken to organize activities pertaining to holding of the National Commemoration of the World Post Day on the 09th of October 2017 in Polonnaruwa under the patronage of H.E. the President, and concurrent to the above, to renovate 34 Post Offices and Sub‑Post Offices in the Districts of Polonnaruwa and Anuradhapura with modern facilities, to conduct blood donation campaigns and social service projects at Divisional Level with the contribution and participation of all employees of the Postal Department and to carry out the evaluation programmes of a Postal Staff of 25,000 employees.
