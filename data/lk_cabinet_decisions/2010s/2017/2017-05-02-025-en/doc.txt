Procurement of Military Logistic Support Vehicles required for Peacekeeping Operations in the Republic of Mali

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-02

- International Competitive Bids have been called to procure the military logistic support vehicles for peacekeeping operations of the Republic of Mali and 6 institutions have submitted their bids in that regard. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena to award the relevant bids in accordance with the recommendations made by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee having evaluated the relevant bids submitted separately by each of the institutions item-wise, was approved by the Cabinet.
