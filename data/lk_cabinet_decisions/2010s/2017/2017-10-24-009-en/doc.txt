Implementation of Storm Water Drainage & Environment Improvement Project for Kalu Oya Basin

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-24

- The Kalu Oya basin in the Gampaha District has been identified as a priority area for the future urban development activities and therefore, it has been proposed to design and implement a storm water drainage and environment improvement project for the basin within a duration of 5 years (2018 - 2022). As such, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to implement the project as a priority project under foreign aid, was approved in principle by the Cabinet.
