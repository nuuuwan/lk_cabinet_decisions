Purchasing Coal for Coal Power Plant in Puttalam through Spot Tenders

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-24

- It has been decided to purchase the coal required expeditiously for the Puttalam Coal Power Plant under the Spot Tender methodology. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the tender for the purchase of 300,000 MT of coal for US$ 91.85 per MT to M/s Swiss Singapore Overseas Enterprises Pte Ltd., was approved by the Cabinet.
