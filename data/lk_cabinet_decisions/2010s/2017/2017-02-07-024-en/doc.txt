Implementation of the Disaster Recovery Solution (DRS) for Data Centre at the Ceylon Petroleum Storage Terminals Ltd. (CPSTL)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Chandima Weerakkody,  the Minister of Petroleum Resources Development, to award the contract for the implementation of Disaster Recovery Solution (DRS), in order to ensure the implementation of the Data Centre at the Ceylon Petroleum Storage Terminals Ltd. which provides the necessary infrastructure facilities for the storage and distribution of Petroleum without any interruption, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
