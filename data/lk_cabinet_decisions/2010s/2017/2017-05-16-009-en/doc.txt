Restoration of the damages caused to the Tea and Rubber planted lands  in the Kegalle District due to torrential rains

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-16

- 	Damages were caused to a large number of tea and rubber planted lands in the Kegalle District due to the torrential rains that fell in the two days of 2016-05-16 and 17.  As such, the proposal made by Hon. Navin Dissanayake, the Minister of Plantation Industries,  to restore such tea and rubber planted lands to a state where re‑plantation could be done, was approved by the Cabinet.
