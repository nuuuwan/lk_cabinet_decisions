Awarding of the contract for the External Work and the Land Development Work of the Modern Research and Development Complex (MRDC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-12-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to award the contract for the External Work and the Land Development Work of the Modern Research and Development Complex (MRDC) at the Industrial Technology Institute (ITI) situated in the Malabe area, to M/s. Ranasiha Lanka Construction (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.215.9 million as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
