Purchasing of Drugs needed to treat Cancers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne,  the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the tender for the procurement of 3,750 vials of Bevacizumab Injection 100mg / 4ml Vial administered for the treatment of certain types of cancer, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
