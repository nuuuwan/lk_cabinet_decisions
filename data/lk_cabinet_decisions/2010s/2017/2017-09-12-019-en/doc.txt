Supply, Installation and Commissioning of Air Traffic Control Simulator Training System for the Civil Aviation Training Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-09-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the Supply, Installation and Commissioning of Air Traffic Control Simulator Training System for the Civil Aviation Training Centre to M/s. Adacel Technologies Limited as decided by the Procurement Appeal Board and  in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
