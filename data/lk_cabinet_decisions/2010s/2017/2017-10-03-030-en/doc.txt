Relocation of the Emigration area at the Passenger Terminal Building of the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-03

- With a view to providing a more efficient service to the air passengers, the proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the relocation of the Emigration Area at the Passenger Terminal Building of the BIA in Katunayake to M/s. R.N. Constructions (Pvt) Ltd. for a sum of Rs. 472.5 million, without VAT, was approved by the Cabinet.
