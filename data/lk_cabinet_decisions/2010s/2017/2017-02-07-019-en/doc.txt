Award of Contract for the Consultancy Services of the Port Access Elevated Highway Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella,  the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for the supply of consultancy services for the Port Access Elevated Highway Project, implemented with the funds provided by the Asian Development Bank, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
