Construction of the Library Building Complex at the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-12-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract of Phase-1 of the project for the construction of the Library Building Complex at the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka, to M/s CML-MTD Construction Ltd., for a sum of Rs.315 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
