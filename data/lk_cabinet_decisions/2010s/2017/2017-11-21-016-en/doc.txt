Awarding of Contracts for Rebuilding and Pre-curing services for tyres of the buses of the Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-11-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contracts for providing the services for re‑building and pre-curing of tyres of the buses of the Sri Lanka Transport Board over to 09 companies, as per the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
