Paying compensation to persons who died or sustained injuries due to capsizing of a boat close to Katukurunda area in Kalutara on 2017-02-19

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Anura Priyadharshana Yapa, the Minister of Disaster Management, to pay a compensation of Rs.100,000/- per deceased person and pay Rs.20,000/- for an injured person in the accident which occurred close to Katukurunda, Kalutara due to the capsizing of one of the boats transporting people from Beruwala to Kalutara on 2017-02-19 to participate in a religious activity, was approved by the Cabinet.
