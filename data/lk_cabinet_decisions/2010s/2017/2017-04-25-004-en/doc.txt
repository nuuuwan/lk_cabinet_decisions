Acquisition of the land situated adjacent to the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka for the expansion of its Faculty of Agriculture

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-04-25

- Approximately 150 students are admitted annually to the Faculty of Agriculture of the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka. Concurrent to the Government policy of increasing the intake of the number of students to the Universities by 5% to 10%, it has been identified that the above Faculty of Agriculture needs to be further developed and as such, the proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways to make available the funds necessary for the development of the land in extent of 3.6 hectares which has already been acquired for the purpose of providing the required facilities, was approved by the Cabinet.
