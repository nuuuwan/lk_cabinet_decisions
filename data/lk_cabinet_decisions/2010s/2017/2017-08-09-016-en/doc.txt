Construction of the Pambahinna Water Supply Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-08-09

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the contract for the construction of the Pambahinna Water Supply Scheme implemented with the objective of providing pipe bone safe drinking water to the people residing in the Pambahinna, Muththettuwegama, Puwakgahawela, Karagasthalawa, Kinchigune and Kumbalgama in the Ratnapura District and the University of Sabaragamuwa, for a sum of Rs.1,019.47 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
