Selection of a Consultancy Firm for supervision of the construction of the road section from Pothuhera to Galagedara of the Central Expressway Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for the consultancy services for supervision of the construction of the road section from Pothuhera via Rambukkana up to Galagedara (32.5 km) of the Central Expressway Project, at a cost of a sum of Rs.2,848 million, in accordance with the recommendation of the Consultancy Procurement Committee appointed by the Cabinet, was approved by the Cabinet.
