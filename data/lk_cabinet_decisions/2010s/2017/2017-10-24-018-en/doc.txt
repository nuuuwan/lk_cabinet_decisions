Construction of Married Quarters for the Junior Police Officers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Sajith Premadasa, the Minister of Housing and Construction, to award the contract for the construction of Married Quarters for Junior Police Officers at Kumaran Ratnam Road, Colombo 02 to M/s Construction Managers & Planners (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.482.4 million without tax, as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
