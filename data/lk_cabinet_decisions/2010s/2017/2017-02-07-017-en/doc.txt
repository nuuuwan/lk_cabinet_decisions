Procurement of Consultancy Services for the Rehabilitation of the Railway line from Maho to Omanthai

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva,  the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for procurement of consultancy services of the project for the rehabilitation of railway line from Maho to Omanthai, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
