Obtaining Grant Aid from the Government of Japan for the improvement of the Port and Nautical Activities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-03-28

- The Government of Japan has agreed to provide Grant Assistance under  non-project grant aid scheme, amounting to Japanese Yen 1 million (Approximately 1.3 billion)  for obtaining Japanese products necessary for the improvement of the Port activities and development of the Navigation System in the Trincomalee Port. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickramasinghe, the Prime Minister, in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs to sign the Exchange of Notes with the Government of Japan to obtain the Grant Aid, was approved by the Cabinet.
