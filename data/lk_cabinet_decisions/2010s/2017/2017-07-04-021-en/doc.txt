Construction of the Accident & Emergency Treatment Unit at the Teaching Hospital - Anuradhapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- A recommendation has been made for the construction of an Accident & Emergency Treatment Unit at the Teaching Hospital, Anuradhapura with a view to providing an easily accessible high quality patient care service for preventing deaths and disabilities caused by accident. National Competitive Bids have been invited to select a suitable contractor for the construction of this unit and in response 09 bidders have presented their bids. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the relevant contract to M/s. Electro Metal Pressings (Pvt.) Ltd., the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder in accordance with the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee which evaluated the bids and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
