Amending the Assistance Agreements Signed with the United States of America

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- The information presented by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs that the amendments related to the relevant Agreements were signed as the Government of the United States of America has agreed to enhance the financial contribution, it had agreed upon, in accordance with the Agreement the Sri Lanka Government entered into  with the United States of America in the year 2011 for the implementation of the programmes of 'Strengthened Partnership for Democratic Reform and Social Integration' and the 'Increased Equitable Economic Growth and Public Financial Management', was noted by the Cabinet.
