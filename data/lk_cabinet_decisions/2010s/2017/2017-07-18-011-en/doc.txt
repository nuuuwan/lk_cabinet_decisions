Appointment of a Board of Administration for the Ranmihithenna National Tele-Cinema Park

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-18

- It has been observed that it is essential to appoint a Board of Administration for the creative and efficient performance of the functions of the Ranmihithenna National Tele-Cinema Park and also to prevent unnecessary delays in the  decision making process. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to appoint a Board of Administration comprising of experts pertaining to the field and the representatives of the relevant institutions, was approved by the Cabinet.
