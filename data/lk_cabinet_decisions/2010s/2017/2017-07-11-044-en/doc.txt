Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Information and Broadcasting between Sri Lanka and Bangladesh

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to sign the MoU on Information and Broadcasting between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the People's Republic of Bangladesh, during the forthcoming State Visit of H.E. the President to Bangladesh, with a view to exchanging current news and television programmes as well as successful experiences and technology in the field of media between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
