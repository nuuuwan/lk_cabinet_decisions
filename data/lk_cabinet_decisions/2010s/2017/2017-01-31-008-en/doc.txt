Procurement for purchasing of Surgical Consumables for the Operation Theatres in Government Hospitals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-31

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the purchase of various surgical consumables essentially required for use in surgical treatment in the Operation Theatres of Government Hospitals, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
