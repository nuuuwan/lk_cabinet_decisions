Signing of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute for International Relations and Strategic Studies of Sri Lanka and the Bangladesh Institute of International & Strategic Studies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- The proposal made by the Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Foreign Affairs to sign a MoU, between the Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute for International Relations and Strategic Studies of Sri Lanka and Bangladesh Institute of International & Strategic Studies with the objective of gaining experiences, expertise knowledge and exchange of research publications in the areas of mutual interest, exchanging of  researchers and experts on short term and long term basis and conducting integrated research projects of mutual interest, during the forthcoming State visit of H.E. the President to Bangladesh , was approved by the Cabinet.
