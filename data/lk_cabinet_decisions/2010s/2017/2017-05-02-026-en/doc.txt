Colombo National Highways Project - contract for the Rehabilitation and Improvement of the Walgama - Diyagama road section (B452)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder for the rehabilitation and improvement of the Walgama - Diyagama road section (B452) under the Colombo National Highways Project funded by the OPEC Fund for International Development (OFID), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
