Allocation of Land at New Town, Ratnapura for the construction of the District Office of the Department of National Community Water Supply

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a portion of land 01 Rood 29 perches in extent from the Farm Garden Estate at the New Town in Ratnapura, owned by the Urban Development Authority with the the Department of National Community Water Supply for construction of the District Office of the Department, through the Divisional Secretary of Ratnapura, was approved by the Cabinet.
