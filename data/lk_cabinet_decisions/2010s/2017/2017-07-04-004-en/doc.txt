Construction of the Hotel of the National High Altitude Sports Training Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-07-04

- The Ministry of Sports has initiated the establishment of a  High Altitude Sports Training Centre at the Nuwara Eliya Race Course Ground with the objective of  developing athletics and other sports.  It has been planned to construct a State of the Art Sports Hotel,  concurrently, in close proximity to the training centre to be constructed.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a block of land in extent of 01Acre, 01Rood, 23.69Perches situated in Badulla Road, Nuwara Eliya belonging to the Urban Development Authority for the construction of the said hotel, was approved by the Cabinet.
