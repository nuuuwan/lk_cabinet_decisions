'Balagathu Sri Lankavak' - the Government Development Plan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-01-03

- The booklet titled 'Balagathu Sri Lankavak' which provides a snapshot of the proposed economic development plans and the ongoing progress made concerning such plans to provide a better understanding of the development process and progress made so far, to the public officers and the general public was presented by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, to the Cabinet.
