Allocating a plot of land at Perahera Mawatha, Colombo-03 to Dharmakeerthi Aramaya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-10-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to allocate a plot of land in extent of 19.7 perches, belonging to the Urban Development Authority, located at Perahera Mawatha, Colombo 03, to the Dayaka Sabhawa of Dharmakeerthi Aramaya as a free grant to construct an access road to the temple, was approved by the Cabinet.
