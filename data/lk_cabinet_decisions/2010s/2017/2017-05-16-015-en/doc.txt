Appointment of a Consulting Agency for the expansion and renovation of the Police Hospital in Narahenpita

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-05-16

- The Cabinet has granted approval to obtain a land in extent of 02 Roods 23.05 Perches from the Urban Development Authority for the expansion and renovation of the Police Hospital in Narahenpita.  As such, the proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayaka, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, to obtain the services of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau which presently provides consultancy services for several ongoing projects in the Sri Lanka Police, was approved by the Cabinet.
