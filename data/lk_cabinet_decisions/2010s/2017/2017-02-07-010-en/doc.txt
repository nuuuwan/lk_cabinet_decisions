Transfer of the portion of land at Wedamulla, Kelaniya area where a 'Dasasil Matha Aramaya' is being conducted over to the same Aramaya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-02-07

- A plot of land belonging to the Sri Lanka Land Reclamation and Development Corporation had been given over to the Sudharma Dasasil Aramaya and the adjoining land where a Bo-sapling is planted is also being used by the Aramaya.  As such, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, transfer the land of 7.56 Perches in extent to the Sudharma Dasasil Aramaya, was approved by the Cabinet.
