Amendment to the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act for the provision of welfare facilities to the Plantation Community

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2017-09-26

- The Cabinet has already granted approval to make amendments to the relevant provisions of the Pradeshiya Sabhas Act, No.15 of 1987 to enable the use of funds of the Pradeshiya Sabhas for the supply of the common amenities necessary for the plantation community. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Faiszer Musthapha, the Minister of Provincial Councils and Local Government to publish the draft Bill (Amendment) prepared accordingly in the Government Gazette, to seek the views of the Provincial Councils in respect of  the said draft Bill and subsequently, present it in Parliament, for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
