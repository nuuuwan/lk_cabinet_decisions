Tender for the Supply of Meropenum Injection 1g vial (120,000 vials) MSD/P/019/11/S - DHS/(C) P/714/2011 SR NO. 016132

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-08

- Cabinet approval was granted to award 25% of the total quantity (30,000 vials) of the above procurement to the lowest responsive bidder M/s. Marksons Pharma Ltd., India at a total C&F price of US $159,000.00 (LKR 17,917,662.00) and to purchase the balance quantity from the same supplier if the original supply is satisfactory.
