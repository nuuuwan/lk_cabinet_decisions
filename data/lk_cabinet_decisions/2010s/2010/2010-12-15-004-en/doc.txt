Pitabeddara Water Supply Scheme - Revision of the Cost Estimate

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-15

- this project is to provide safe drinking water to the people in the Pitabeddara township and surrounding areas.  The project area is situated in the Matara District, about 40 km away from Matara Town.  Cabinet approval was granted for the revised total cost estimate of the project up to a sum of Rs.130.31 million and for the project to be completed in 2011.
