Revision of Salaries and Allowances of Public Sector as per the Budget Proposals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-29

- in keeping with the budget proposals 2011 passed in Parliament on 10.12.2010 and already announced, salaries and allowances of employees in the Public Service, Government Corporations, Statutory Boards and Foreign Funded Projects as well as pension benefits of pensioners will be revised with effect from 01.01.2011.
