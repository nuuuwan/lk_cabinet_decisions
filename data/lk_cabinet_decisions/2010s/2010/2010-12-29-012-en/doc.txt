Project for Reconstruction of Railway Bridges under Belgium Assistance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-29

-Cabinet approval was granted for the supply of six bridges and other items from M/s. Transurb Technical for the places as indicated below: 

* Wellawatta
* Dehiwala Double Line
* Angulana
* Navatkuli
* Polgahawela
* Narahenpita
