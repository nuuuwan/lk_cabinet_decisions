Implementation of Ruhunupura Water Supply Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-08

- the proposed project intends to provide safe drinking water to Ruhunupura and Mahaweli Development areas  in the Hambantota District catering to above 112,000 people.  The project is to be partly financed by the Republic of Korea.  Cabinet approval was granted to award the project to M/s. Kolon - Pankorea - Yooshin  Consortium for a total sum of US $39,512,000.00 and SLR 4,117,960,000.00.
