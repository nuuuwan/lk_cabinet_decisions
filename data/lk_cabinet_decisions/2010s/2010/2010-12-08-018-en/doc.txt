Greater Colombo Wastewater Management Project Funded by the Asian Development Bank : Award of Contract for Institutional Development Consultancy (IDC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-08

- the Government has received a loan from the Asian Development Bank (ADB) for the above project with a total cost of US $116.63 million.  The total value of the contract is US $1,714,102.00 with a foreign currency component of US $1,001,000.00 and a local currency component Rs.81,293,729.00.  Cabinet approval was granted to implement the project.
