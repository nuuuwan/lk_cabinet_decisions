Provincial Component of Northern Road Connectivity Project Funded by Asian Development Bank. Awarding of Supervision Consultancy Contract for Supervision of Civil Works Contracts in Northern Province

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-08

- the Asian Development Bank has allocated a sum of US $173 million for rehabilitation and upgrading of National and Provincial roads in the Northern Province.  The total value of the contract is US $2,514,365.49 with a foreign currency component of US $766,158,00 and and a local currency component Rs.197,495,000.00.
