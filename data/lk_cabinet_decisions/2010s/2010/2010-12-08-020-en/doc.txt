Loan Facility of US $500 million from China Development Bank Corporation to finance Improvement and Rehabilitation of priority roads

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-08

- Of this amount, US$ 158.2 million will be provided by the Bank in China in 2010 and the balance  in 2011 to finance Roads Projects. A total length of 1,174 Km throughout the country will be rehabilitated under this project.
