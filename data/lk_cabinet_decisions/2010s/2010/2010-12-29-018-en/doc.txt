Hambantota Port Development Project (Phase II)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2010-12-29

- Cabinet approval has already been granted for the construction works of the Hambantota Port Project (Phase I) to be awarded to the Chinese Consortium.  Since it has been observed that work pertaining to Phase I is progressing satisfactorily, Cabinet approval was granted to award the works of Phase II of the project also to the same Consortium.
