Procurement of New Tyre Kits to Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-04-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala De Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the procurement of New Tyre Kits required for the Sri Lanka Transport Board, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.  This will cost nearly Rs.1,108 million.
