Implementation of the Matara Water Supply Project - Stage IV

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-01

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, to enter into an Agreement with the National Development Bank to obtain a loan amounting to US$76.83 Million and  Rs.2,448.81 Million for the implementation of the Matara Water Supply Project - Stage IV, was approved by the Cabinet.
