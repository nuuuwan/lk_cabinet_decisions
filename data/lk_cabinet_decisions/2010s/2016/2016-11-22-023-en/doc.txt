Purchase of 820,000 of Blue Base Dry Imagine Films (35 cm x 43 cm) used for X-Ray Machines in Hospitals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-22

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine to award the tender for the purchase of 820,000 of Blue Base Dry Imagine Films (35 cm x 43 cm) used in Fuji Laser Printers in X-Ray Machines at Government Hospitals as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
