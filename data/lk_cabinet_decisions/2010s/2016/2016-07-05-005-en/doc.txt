Restructuring of Tea Shakthi Fund

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-07-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Navin Dissanayake, the Minister of Plantation Industries, to restructure the Tea Shakthi Fund, as recommended by the Officials' Committee appointed to submit the recommendations on the restructuring of the Tea Shakthi Fund and to improve the management of the twelve (12) factories belonging to the Tea Shakthi Fund, under the direct supervision of the Ministry, was approved by the Cabinet.
