Memorandum of Understanding between the National Science Foundation of Sri Lanka and the National Natural Science Foundation of China

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-30

- The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to sign the Memorandum of Understanding between the National Science Foundation of Sri Lanka and the National Natural Science Foundation of China with a view to enhancing co-operation in the field of Science and Technology, during the Prime Minister's visit to China, was approved by the Cabinet.
