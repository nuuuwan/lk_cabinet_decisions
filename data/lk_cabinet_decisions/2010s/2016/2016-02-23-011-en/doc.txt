Presentation of the Regulations made by the Minister of Sports under Section 34 (1) of the Convention against Doping in Sports Act, No.33 of 2013, in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-02-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Dayasiri Jayasekara, the Minister of Sports, to present the Regulations published in the Gazette (Extraordinary) Notification No.1950/1 dated 2016-01-18, made under Section 34(1) of the Convention against Doping in Sports Act, No.33 of 2013, in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
