Proposed Bill to amend the Trust Ordinance, No.9 of 1917

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-30

- The Proposal made by Hon. (Dr) Wijayadasa Rajapaksha, the Minister of Justice, to amend  the Trust Ordinance, so as to widen its scope to meet the international standards in order to minimize the risk of money laundering and raising money for Terrorism, was approved by the Cabinet.
