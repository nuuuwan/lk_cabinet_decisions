Housing Development Programme for families suffering from renal failure and living in abject poverty

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-06-07

- The Joint proposal made by H. E. the President Maithripala Sirisena and Hon. Sajith Premadasa, the Minister of Housing and Construction, to initiate and implement a Housing Programme with the basic infrastructure facilities for the families suffering from renal failure and living in  abject poverty, in view of the 'National Housing Week' commencing from the National Housing Day which falls on 23rd June, was approved by the Cabinet.
