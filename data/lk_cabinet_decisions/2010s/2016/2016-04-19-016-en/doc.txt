Facilitation of a meeting of the relevant Cabinet  Ministers for the Members of Parliament to discuss the issues that they confront in their areas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-04-19

- The Cabinet concurred with the proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickramasinghe, the Prime Minister that the Cabinet Ministers should be available from 2.00 pm onwards in their offices within the Parliamentary complex, on Tuesdays of the second sitting week of Parliament of every month, to enable the Members of Parliament to meet the Ministers to resolve the issues prevailing in their electorates.
