Physical and Spiritual Development Programme implemented under the ‘Punyagrama’ Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-18

- The ‘Punyagrama Programme' implemented under the Ministry of Buddhasasana, is expected to promote moral values as well as develop the physical facilities related therewith while strengthening the relationship between the village and the temple. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Buddhasasana, to implement a project of  installing water filter (Reverse Osmosis) machines to provide  safe  drinking water, centering  the temples which are being maintained amidst various difficulties, situated in the areas  in the Northern, North Central and Eastern Provinces, where the  Chronic Kidney Disease (CKDu) is prevalent and in the other Provinces where water borne diseases are prevalent, utilizing the provision of Rs.24 million allocated for this project, in the year 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
