Colombo National Highways Project - Improvement of the Road section from Godagama to Bope in the Kotte-Bope Road (B240)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-12-13

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for the improvement of Road section from Godagama to Bope in the Kotte-Bope Road (B240) from 16.740 km to 28.004 km under the Colombo National Highways Project funded by the OPEC Fund for International Development (OFID), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
