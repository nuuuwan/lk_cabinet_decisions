The State Wesak Festival - 2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-02-10

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksha, the Minister of Buddhasasana, to hold the State Wesak festival – 2016, at the premises of Naigala Rajamaha Viharaya, which has a historical and archaeological importance, in the Weeraketiya Divisional Secretariat Division of the District of Hambantota of the Southern Province, was approved by the Cabinet.
