Completion of partially completed projects in the areas of Tangalle, Angunukolapellessa and Middeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to complete the partially done construction work of the projects pertaining to Tangalle and Angunukolapellessa Commercial Complex and the Side Walks under Middeniya Town Development through the new contractors selected after a competitive bidding process, at an estimated cost of Rs.60.8 Million, was approved by the Cabinet.
