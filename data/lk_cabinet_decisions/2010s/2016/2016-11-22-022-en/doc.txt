Purchase of Equipment used to Inject Drugs to Patients

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-22

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine to award the tender for the purchase of I.V. Cannula (with and without Injection Port) in various sizes, used to inject drugs to patients,  as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
