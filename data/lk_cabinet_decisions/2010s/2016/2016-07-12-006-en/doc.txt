Implementation of the Tranche - 3 of the Greater Colombo Water and Wastewater Management Improvement Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-07-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, to sign a Credit Agreement between the Government of Sri Lanka and the European Investment Bank, to implement the Tranche - 3 of the Greater Colombo Water and Wastewater Management Improvement Programme, at a cost of Euro156 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
