Supply and Installation of Operation Theater/ICU Equipment for the Epilepsy Unit at the National Hospital of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- It is essential to provide Operation Theater/ICU Equipment for the Epilepsy Unit newly established at the National Hospital of Sri Lanka in order to provide high quality healthcare services. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement to the relevant bidders, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and to invite fresh bids for the lots not quoted with offers and with requests to increase the prices after extension of bid security, was approved by the Cabinet.
