Presenting the International Labour Organization Recommendation 204  in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-22

- The proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, the Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations to present the 'Recommendation 204', adopted at the 104th session of the International  Labour Conference, concerning the transition from the informal to the formal economy,  in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
