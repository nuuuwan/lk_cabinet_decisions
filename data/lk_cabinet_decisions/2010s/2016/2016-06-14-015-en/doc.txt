Annual Report of the Ministry of Finance - 2015

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-06-14

- In terms of Section 13 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No.03 of 2003, the Minister of Finance is required to submit a Report on the Final Budgetary Position (Annual Report) to Parliament and to the public before 31st of May of the subsequent year.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, to present in Parliament, the Annual Report of the Ministry of Finance for the year 2015, containing the updated information of the Government's Fiscal Position, consisting of 13 Chapters, was approved by the Cabinet.
