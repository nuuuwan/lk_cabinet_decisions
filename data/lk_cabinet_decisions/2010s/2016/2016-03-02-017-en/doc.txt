Tender for the purchase of 580,000 Vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 6,000 IU in 60mg/0.6ml Prefilled Syringe

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-02

- The proposal made by Hon.(Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of the above vaccine, which is used for the treatment of heart patients, to the bidder recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
