Bill to amend the Mediation Boards Act, No.72 of 1988

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-23

- The Cabinet of Ministers has already granted approval, to increase the limit of monetary value in respect of disputes to be referred to compulsory mediation, from Rs.250,000/- to Rs.500,000/-. Therefore, the proposal made by Hon.(Dr) Wijayadasa Rajapaksha, the Minister of Justice, to  publish the draft bill in the Government Gazette and present the same in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
