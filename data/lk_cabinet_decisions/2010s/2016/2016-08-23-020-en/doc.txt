Award of Contract for the Construction of Habarana-Veyangoda 220kV Transmission Line

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-08-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the construction of Habarana- Veyangoda 220 KV Transmission Line - (Lot B), funded by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
