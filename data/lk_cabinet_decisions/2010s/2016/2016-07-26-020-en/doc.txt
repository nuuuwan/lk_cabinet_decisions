Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Political Consultation between the Ministries of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and Georgia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-07-26

- With a view to enhancing the bilateral relations including economic, trade, scientific, technical, tourism promotion, cultural as well as political cooperation, between the two countries, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to sign the Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Political Consultation, during the consultations at the level of Deputy Ministers of Foreign Affairs scheduled to be held in Colombo from 3rd to 4th August 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
