Revoking the Homeopathy Act, No. 07 of 1970, and getting a new	Act 	passed in its place

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-02-03

- Approval of the Cabinet has already been granted to repeal the Homeopathy Act, No. 07 of 1970 and pass a new Act in its place. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to present the  Homeopathy Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman that provides for the establishment of the Homeopathic Medical Council, registration of  Homeopathic practitioners and Homeopathic pharmacists, registration and regulation of Homeopathic institutions, promotion, fostering and regulation of the Homeopathic system of medicine, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
