Tender for the purchase of 80,000 bottles of Human Albumin	Solution BP (PH EUR) 20% in 50ml bottle

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-02

- The proposal made by Hon.(Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of the above medicine which is used for the treatment of kidney patients, to the bidder recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
