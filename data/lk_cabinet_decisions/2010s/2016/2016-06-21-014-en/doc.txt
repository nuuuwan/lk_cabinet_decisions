Supply of Medical Equipment and Services for the Epilepsy Unit	at the National Hospital of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-06-21

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contracts for the supply of ward medical equipment and services to the Epilepsy Unit at the National Hospital of Sri Lanka, as per the recommendations made by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
