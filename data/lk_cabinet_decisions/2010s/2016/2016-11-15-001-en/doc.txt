Extradition Treaty between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Belarus

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-15

- For the purpose of effective cooperation between the two countries in the prevention and suppression of crime and facilitating cooperation between them, in the extradition of accused persons/offenders to the relevant countries, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to enter into an Extradition Treaty between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Belarus, was approved by the Cabinet.
