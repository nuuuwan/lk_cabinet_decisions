Memorandum of Understanding on the Establishment of a Political Consultation Mechanism between Slovenia and Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-06

- With a view to promoting, strengthening and expanding bilateral relations including political, economic, consular, technological and scientific and cultural cooperation between Slovenia and Sri Lanka, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to sign the above Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Slovenia, during his official visit to be made to Slovenia from  04th to 08th September, 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
