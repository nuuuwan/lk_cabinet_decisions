Contract for System Rehabilitation for the reduction of Non Revenue Water of the Southern Part of the Colombo City

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-22

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, for awarding the contract for system rehabilitation of  project 2 implemented under the Asian Development Bank assisted  Greater Colombo Water and Wastewater Management Improvement Investment Programme, for reducing pipe-borne water leakages non revenue water within Colombo and the suburbs, with the objective of improving the water supply services in the areas of  Colombo South and Colombo West, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
