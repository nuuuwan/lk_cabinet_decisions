Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation in the Fields of Radio, Television and Film between Sri Lanka and   Bangladesh

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-13

- With a view to exchanging current news and television programmes,  success stories and technology in the Media field between the two countries, the proposal made by Hon. Gayantha Karunathilaka, the Minister of Parliamentary Reforms and Mass Media, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Ministry of Parliamentary Reforms and Mass Media of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Information of the People's Republic of Bangladesh, was approved by the Cabinet.
