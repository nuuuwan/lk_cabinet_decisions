Renovation and Construction of Access Roads and Internal Roads of the 	Dedicated Economic Centers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-11

- The proposal made by P. Harison, the Minister of Rural Economy, to get the construction work of the internal roads, access roads, vehicle parks and the network of drains of the Dedicated Economic Centers of Meegoda, Nuwara Eliya, Keppetipola and Veyangoda, carried out by the relevant Provincial Road Development Authority, at an estimated cost of Rs.25.2 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
