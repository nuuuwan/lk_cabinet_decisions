"The Nearest School is the Best School" National Programme (2016-2020), intended to create a generation of students imbued with Virtues, Wisdom and Vigour through development of a System of Smart Focal Schools

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-01-13

- "The Nearest School is the Best School" National Programme (2016-2020) is scheduled to be implemented, to achieve the objectives of the five-fold education manifesto of the Government, under the theme of 'ensuring equity in education, in order to create an aesthetically minded, creative and philanthropic child, who is imbued with virtues, wisdom and vigour', for the process of uplifting the education qualitatively and quantitatively. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Akila Viraj Kariyawasam, the Minister of Education, to develop 650 schools by selecting one or two schools from each Divisional Secretariat division, to develop capacity of the Principals and the Deputy Principals of these schools, by providing them with foreign and local training, was approved by the Cabinet.
