Renovation of Roads under the China Development Bank funded Priority Roads Project 3 - Phase II

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-04-06

- The  proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to renovate 20 roads at a total length of 128.85 km, located in the Central and Sabaragamuwa Provinces, under the China Development Bank funded Priority Roads Project 3 - Phase II, was approved by the Cabinet.
