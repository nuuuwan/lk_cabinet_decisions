Completion of the Construction Work of Polpithigama Police Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-06

- The construction work of the  Polpithigama Police station, belonging to the Police Division of Nikaweratiya and with 130 Police personnel in service, had been initiated in the year 2007, but however, has been suspended midway due to various reasons.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Sagala Rathnayake, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, to complete the construction work of the Polpithigama Police Station, at a total estimated cost of Rs.49.7 million, also incorporating the subsequently identified requirements, was approved by the Cabinet.
