'Let's Awaken Polonnaruwa' District Development Programme -  Implementation of Priority Water Supply Projects

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-06

- With the objective of providing safe drinking water for the upliftment of the living standard of the people of Polonnaruwa District, the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to implement nine (09) water supply projects under the 'Let's Awaken Polonnaruwa' District Development Programme and to get the required sum of Rs.6,464.36 million set apart for the purpose, for the years 2017 and 2018, was approved by the Cabinet.
