Conducting the 3rd Meeting of Expert Group on Natural Rubber 	Price Stabilization of the Association of Natural Rubber Producing 	Countries (ANRPC) in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-06-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Navin Dissanayake, the Minister of Plantation Industries, to facilitate the holding of the 3rd Meeting of the Expert Group on Natural Rubber Price Stabilization of the Association of Natural Rubber Producing Countries, which is an international organization comprising of 11 natural rubber producing member countries, including Sri Lanka, in the year 2016 in Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
