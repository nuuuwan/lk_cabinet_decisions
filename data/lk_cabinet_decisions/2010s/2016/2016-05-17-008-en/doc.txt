Anuradhapura South Phase II Integrated Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-05-17

- The proposed Anuradhapura South Phase II Integrated Water Supply Project, to be implemented covering Nachchaduwa, Thalawa, Rambawa, Mihinthale, Tirappane small town, pocket areas located in the Anuradhapura Municipal Council area and the Nuwaragampalatha Central and  Nuwaragampalatha East Divisional Secretariat Divisions, will generate benefits to around 280,956 people therein. Hence, the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the Tender relating to the implementation of the Project, as per the recommendation made by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
