Awarding  the Contract for the Construction of  Anchorage Facilities at Peraliya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-27

- The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Amaraweera, the Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development, to award the contract for the construction of  the proposed anchorage facilities to be established at Peraliya, Galle district, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
