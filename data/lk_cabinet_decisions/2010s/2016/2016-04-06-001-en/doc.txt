Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and China on Cooperation in the field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-04-06

- The proposal made by Hon. John Amaratunga, the Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Government of Sri Lanka and the People's Republic of China in the field of Tourism Cooperation, during the forthcoming official visit to China by the Hon. Prime Minister, as proposed at its 7th meeting of the China - Sri Lanka Joint Committee on Economic Cooperation, was approved by the Cabinet.
