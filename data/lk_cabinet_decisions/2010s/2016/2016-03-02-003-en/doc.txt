Bilateral Air Services Consultations between Sri Lanka and Austria

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-02

- Consultations on executing a new bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri	Lanka and Austria  were held in the month of November, 2015 in Vienna, Austria, with the participation of the aeronautical authorities	of both countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Nimal	 Siripala De Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to sign the new bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and Austria by an accredited representative of Sri Lanka, as agreed upon at the above consultations and to take appropriate measures thereafter for the effective implementation of the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
