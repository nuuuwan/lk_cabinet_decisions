Allocation of Land at Kalutara to the Kalutara Bodhi Trust for a Public Car Park

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-09

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to legally transfer the block of land known as 'Diyambathalawa' of 33.75 perches in extent, situated in between the two bridges across the Kalu Ganga, in the Kalutara city, in Galle Road to which the Kalutara Bodhi Trust  has already paid  compensation and the interest related thereto in respect of the transfer of the said land and the possession had been transferred to the said Trust, to the Kalutara Bodhi Trust, by the Urban Development Authority, for the enhancement of the parking facilities of the pilgrims who daily arrive at the Kalutara Bodhi, was approved by the Cabinet.
