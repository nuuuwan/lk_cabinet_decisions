Improvement of the Badulla-Chenkalady Road - Road sections from Badulla to Passara and Passara to Lunugala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contracts for improving the road sections of 20 km in length from Badulla to Passara and 21 km in length from Passara to Lunugala, under the Badulla-Chenkalady Road development project funded by the OPEC Fund for International Development (OFID), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
