Tender for the purchase of 120,000,000 Tablets of Clopidogrel 75mg

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-02

- The proposal made by Hon.(Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of the above essential medicine used to inhibit blood clots in heart patients, to the bidder recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
