Establishment of the National Science Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-30

- The Cabinet of Ministers has already granted approval to establish a National Science Centre. Therefore, having considered the submission made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science,Technology and Research, the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers was granted to establish the proposed National Science Centre, in a plot of land located at Mahena area, Homagama, belonging to the Urban Development Authority.
