Air Services Consultations between Sri Lanka and the Kingdom of Norway/ Sweden/Denmark (The Parties)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to formally sign the Air Services Agreement agreed on 2015-10-21 by the Aeronautical Authorities of the states concerned, during the International Civil Aviation Negotiations Conference (ICAN) held in Antalya, Turkey, for the purpose of conducting both passenger and freighter operations between Sri Lanka and the Kingdom of Norway/Sweden/Denmark and to take appropriate measures for its entry into force, was approved by the Cabinet.
