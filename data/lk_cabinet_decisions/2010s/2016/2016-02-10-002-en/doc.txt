Amendment to the Regulations on Payment made to the 	Arbitrators and the members of the Industrial Court 	appointed under the 	Industrial 	Dispute Act, for conducting 	Inquiries

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-02-10

- Having considered the necessity of revising the rates paid to the Arbitrators and the members of the Industrial Court for conducting inquiries, to suit their  professional competency,  the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Senevirathne, the Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations,  to amend the Regulations published in the Gazette Notification (Extraordinary) No. 1542/35 dated 2008-03-28, which are presently in operation in that regard, was approved by the Cabinet.
