Federation of Asian Bishops’ Conference

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-08-23

- The Federation of Asian Bishops whose headquarters is located in Hong Kong works to ensure that there is a proper coordination among the Catholic Community in activities related to Catholics in the Asian Countries. The Committee Meeting of this Federation is held once in four years and it has been planned to hold a meeting this time in Negambo from 28th November to 4th December, 2016.  Eight (08) Cardinals and over one hundred and fifty (150) Bishops representing all countries in Asia are scheduled to participate in that meeting. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. John Amarathunga, the Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs, for the Government to provide its contribution to hold that meeting in Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
