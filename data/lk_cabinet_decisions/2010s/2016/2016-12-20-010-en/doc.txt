Construction of a New Building for the Divisional Secretariat,  Homagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-12-20

- The proposal made by Hon. Vajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Home Affairs, to award the contract for the construction of a new 03 storeyed building for the Divisional Secretariat, Homagama, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
