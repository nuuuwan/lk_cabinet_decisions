Greater Colombo Wastewater Management Project - Stage-II -  Design Supervision and Management Consultancy Service

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-16

- The Greater Colombo Drinking Water and Wastewater Management Project and the Development Investment Programme are being implemented with the objective of streamlining the Wastewater disposal process and improving the public health of the residents of the Greater Colombo area. The Asian Development Bank has provided financial facilities to the Government of Sri Lanka for Stage-II of the Programme and accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Faiszer Musthapha, the Minister of Provincial Councils and Local Government, to award the Contract for Design Supervision and Management Consultancy Service, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultant Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
