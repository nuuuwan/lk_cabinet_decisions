Budget 2017 - the Second Reading Debate and the Committee Stage Debate

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-11

- The information submitted to the Cabinet by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, that the First Reading related to the 2017 Budget (the Budget Speech) is scheduled to be held on 2016-10-11, and the dates proposed for the Second Reading Debate and the Committee Stage Debate are scheduled for 26 days during the months of November and December, was noted by the Cabinet.
