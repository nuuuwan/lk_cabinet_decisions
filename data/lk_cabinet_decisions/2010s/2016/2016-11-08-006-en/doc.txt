Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Consultations between Sri Lanka and Sudan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Consultations between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Sudan (Northern Sudan), with a view to further strengthening the existing friendship and cooperation between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
