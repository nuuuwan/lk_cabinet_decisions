Tender for the supply of Vials for treatment of patients with immunity deficiencies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the tender for the supply of 30,000 Vials of 5-6g of Human Immunoglobulin, for treatment to the patients with immunity deficiency, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
