Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Cuba, on Cooperation in Science, Technology and Innovation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to sign the  'Agreement on the Cooperation in Science, Technology and Innovation', to be entered into between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Cuba, with a view to encouraging the cooperation between Sri Lanka and Cuba, in the fields of Science, Technology and Research and providing assistance for the said purpose,  was approved by the Cabinet.
