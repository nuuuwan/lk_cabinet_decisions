Tender for the supply of Anti Biotic Drugs required by the Governmebnt Hospitals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the tender for the supply of 19,000,000 Co-Amoxiclav Tablets BP 625mg or Amoxicillian and Clavulanate Pottassium Tablets USP 625 mg, which are widely used antibiotic drugs as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
