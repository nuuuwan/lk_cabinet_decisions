Purchasing of 42,000 Metric Tons of Urea by the two State Fertilizer Companies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-06-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Agriculture, to purchase 42,000 Metric Tons of Urea through the two State Fertilizer Companies,  to continuously address the fertilizer requirements of the farmer community of the country, at a reasonable price, as per the recommendation of the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
