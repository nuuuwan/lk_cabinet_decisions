Awarding of Contracts for the Buildings to be Constructed in the Jaffna & Wayamba Universities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-12-06

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contracts for the construction of a sports and exercise hall in the University of Jaffna, a science laboratory in the Wayamba University and also a building for the Agriculture and Plantation Management Faculty of the same University, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
