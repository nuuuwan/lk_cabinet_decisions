Procurement of Oil and Lubricant for Sri Lanka Transport Bord

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-30

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala De Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contracts for procurement of Oil and Lubricant for Sri Lanka Transport Board buses, to the bidders recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
