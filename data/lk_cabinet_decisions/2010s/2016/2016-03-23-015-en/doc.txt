Entering into Four (04) Long Term Contracts for the purchase of Refined Petroleum Products for the period of Eight (08) Months  from 01st March 2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Chandima Weerakkody, the Minister of Petroleum Resources Development to award 04 long term contracts, for the purchase of refined petroleum products, as per the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
