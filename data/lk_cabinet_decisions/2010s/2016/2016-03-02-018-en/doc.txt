Tender for the purchase of 300,000 Vials of Rabies Vaccine (Human Use (1ml/0.5)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-02

- The proposal made by Hon.(Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of the above vaccine which is used to prevent rabies, to the bidder recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
