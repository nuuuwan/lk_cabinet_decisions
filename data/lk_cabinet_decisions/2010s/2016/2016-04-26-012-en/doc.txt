Tender for  the Supply of 950,000 Vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin (Human) Injection BP, 1000 IU / 10ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-04-26

- The proposal made by Hon.(Dr) Rajitha Seneratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the tender for the  purchase of the above drug used for the treatment of diabetic patients, as  recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
