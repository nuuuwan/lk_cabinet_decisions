Procurement Plan of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation for Crude Oil & Petroleum Products - 2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-30

- The  Procurement Plan of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation to purchase  Crude Oil and Petroleum Products for the year 2016 submitted by Hon. Chandima Weerakkody, the Minister of Petroleum Resources Development, was approved by the Cabinet, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC).
