Implementation of Wastewater Disposal Infrastructure for Chilaw Town and the Puttalam Town and the Wastewater Disposal  System for Maharagama -  Boralesgamuwa Areas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-06-21

- The joint proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply and Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, to implement the project for the development of Wastewater Disposal Infrastructure for the Chilaw Town and the Puttalam Town and the Wastewater Disposal System for Maharagama -  Boralesgamuwa areas, and to obtain a loan of US$105 million from the EXIM Bank of China on concessionary terms, was approved by the Cabinet.
