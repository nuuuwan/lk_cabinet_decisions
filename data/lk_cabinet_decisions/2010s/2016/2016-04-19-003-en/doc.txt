Reverting the Land 'Thennankuriyawa Watta' at Anamaduwa to the Land Reform Commission to be distributed among the landless residents of the area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-04-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to revert the land 'Thennankuriyawa Watta' at Anamaduwa, in extent of 9.6 hectares, to the Land Reform Commission, to be  distributed among the landless residents of the area, selected after conducting a Land Kachcheri under the supervision of the Divisional Secretary, Anamaduwa, was approved by the Cabinet.
