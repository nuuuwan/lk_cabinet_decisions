Agreement between Sri Lanka and Georgia on Visa Exemption for Holders of Diplomatic and Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-08-23

- With a view to furthering the friendship between the two countries and to improving diplomatic and official tour facilities, the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinne, the Minister of Internal Affairs, Wayamba Development and Cultural Affairs, to enter into an agreement between Sri Lanka and Georgia on Visa Exemption for Holders of Diplomatic and Official Passports, was approved by the Cabinet.
