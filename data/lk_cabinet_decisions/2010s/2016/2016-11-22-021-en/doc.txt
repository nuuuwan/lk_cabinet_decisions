Awarding of the Non Revenue Water Management and Construction Supervision Consultancy Contract

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-22

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, for awarding the contract for the Non Revenue Water Management and the Construction Supervision Consultancy Contract of  project 1 implemented under the Asian Development Bank assisted  Greater Colombo Water and Wastewater Management Improvement Investment Programme, for reducing pipe-borne water leakages and managing the non revenue water within Colombo and the suburbs, in a more efficient and productive manner, with the objective of improving the water supply services in the areas of  Colombo North and Colombo East, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
