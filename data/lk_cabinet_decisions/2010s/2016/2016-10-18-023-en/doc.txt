Procurement of 20 Container Carrier Wagons and 30 Fuel Tank Wagons for the Department of Sri Lanka Railways

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the supply of 20 Container Carrier Wagons and 30 Fuel Tank Wagons for the Department of Sri Lanka Railways under the Indian Line of Credit, in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Negotiation Committee (CANC), was approved by the Cabinet.
