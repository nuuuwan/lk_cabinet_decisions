Bilateral Air Services Consultations between Sri Lanka and Finland

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to formally sign the Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and Finland, agreed on 2013-12-12 in Durban, South Africa in order to  exercise the freedom traffic rights for both passenger and cargo flights of the two countries without any restrictions and to accept the designated airlines of the two countries, and thereafter to take appropriate measures for its entry into force, was approved by the Cabinet.
