Construction of a New Four Storied Building for the District Secretariat, Polonnaruwa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-07-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Vajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Home Affairs, to award the Contract on the first phase of the construction of a new four storied building complex for the District Secretariat, Polonnaruwa, to the lowest substantially responsive bidder, as per the recommendation made by  the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
