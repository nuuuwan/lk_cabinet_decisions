Payment of Compensations and gratuity - The Department of Divineguma Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-23

- The joint proposal made by Hon. S.B Dissanayake, the Minister of Social Empowerment and Welfare and Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, for obtaining funds required for payment of Compensation and gratuity, in stages, for the officers who served in the institutions that were amalgamated to form the Department of Divineguma Development, was approved by the Cabinet.
