Review of the Government Paddy Purchasing Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-27

- The Government Paddy Purchasing Programme, under this government, has been implemented up to now throughout the three consecutive harvesting Seasons of Maha 2014/15, Yala 2015 and Maha 2015/16. The Cabinet approved the implementation of the proposals made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of  Finance, to dispose the remaining paddy stock of the paddy purchased in the year 2015, following the government approved procurement procedure, prior to the  commencement of the reaping of harvest of Yala 2016; to dispose  the paddy  stocks purchased in the year 2016 and stored in warehouses of the Paddy Marketing Board, during the months of October and November, where the higher  price is reported; and to settle the outstanding balances to the General Treasury; and to settle the dues to the State Banks pertaining to  paddy purchasing through utilizing  the money derived from paddy disposal, with a view to implementing the said programme successfully in the future too, as per the recommendations made by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Management (CCEM).
