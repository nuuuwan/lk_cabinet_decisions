Awarding the contract for the supply and distribution of Ariel Bundle Conductors

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the supply and distribution of Ariel Bundle Conductors Alu-ABC, for the distribution region 1, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
