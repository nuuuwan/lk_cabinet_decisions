Proposed Amendments to the Institute of Valuers of Sri Lanka Law No. 33 of 1975

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-01

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance,  to instruct the Legal Draftsman, to draft legislation to amend the Institute of Valuers of Sri Lanka Law No.33 of 1975, incorporating timely amendments needed therein, was approved by the Cabinet.
