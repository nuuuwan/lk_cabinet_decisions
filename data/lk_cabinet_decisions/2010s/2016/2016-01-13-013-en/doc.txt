Awarding the Tender for the purchase of 150,000 Vials of Meropenem Injection 500 mg Vial & 310,000 Vials of Meropenem Injection 1 g Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-01-13

- The Meropenem injection is used as an antibiotic for infections. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of 150,000 Vials of Meropenem Injection 500 mg Vial and 310,000 Vials of Meropenem Injection 1g Vial, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
