Expansion to the Departure Public Concourse at the Bandaranayke International Airport (BIA), Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the expansion of the Departure Public Concourse at BIA, Katunayake, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
