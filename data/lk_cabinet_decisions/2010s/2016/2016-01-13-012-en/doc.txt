Awarding the Tender for the purchase of 30,000 Vials of Human Immunoglobulin for IV use 5-6g Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-01-13

- Human Immunoglobulin for IV use is utilized to treat patients with immune deficiencies. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of 30,000 Vials of Human Immunoglobulin for IV use 5 - 6g Vials, to the lowest responsive bidder, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
