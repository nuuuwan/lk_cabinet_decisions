Construction of the Arrivals Passenger Terminal Building at the Bandaranayke International Airport (BIA), Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the construction of  the New Immigration Hall and the Electronic Mall, at the Arrivals Passenger Terminal Building at the BIA, Katunayake as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
