Submission of the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the Year 2015 in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-05-10

- The Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the Year 2015, prepared to be presented in Parliament, in accordance with the provisions of the Monetary Law Act, which includes the state of the economy, accounts and operations of the Central Bank, major administrative measures adopted regarding the Monetary Policy, major legislative enactments and statistical appendix relating to the past year,  was submitted to the Cabinet by Hon. Prime Minister Ranil Wickremasinghe, in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs and the Cabinet granted approval to present same in Parliament.
