Signing  of  a  Memorandum  of  Understanding   between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Germany for the Development of Sports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-02-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Dayasiri Jayasekara, the Minister of Sports, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Germany, on the co-operation in the field of Sports, by providing the framework for bilateral cooperation in the fields of Sports, Sports Administration, Sports Research, Sports Development and the excellence in Sports, was approved by the Cabinet.
