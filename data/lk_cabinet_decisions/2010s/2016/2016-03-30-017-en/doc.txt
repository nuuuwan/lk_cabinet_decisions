Procurement of Rebuilding and Pre-curing services for the tyres of Sri Lanka Transport Board buses

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-30

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala De Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to obtain the Pre-curing  and  Rebuilding services for tyres of Sri Lanka Transport Board buses, from the bidders recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
