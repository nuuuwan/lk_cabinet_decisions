Improvement of the High Frequency Transmitting Station, at Attidiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for installing 03 Aeronautical High Frequency Transmitters with Antennas required for the High Frequency Transmitting Station, at Attidiya, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
