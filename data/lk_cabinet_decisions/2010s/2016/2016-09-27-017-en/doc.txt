Supply of Medical Equipment for the Epilepsy Unit of the National Hospital of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-27

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement/tender for the supply and installation of the Monitoring Medical Equipment essential for providing an advanced healthcare service through the newly constructed Epilepsy Unit of the National Hospital of Sri Lanka, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
