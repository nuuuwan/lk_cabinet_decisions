Purchase of Sports Shoes for the Sri Lanka Army

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-22

- The proposal made by H.E. the President, Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to award the contract, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee for the purchase of  200,000 pairs of Sports Shoes necessary for the Sri Lanka Army, was approved by the Cabinet.
