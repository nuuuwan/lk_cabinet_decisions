Leasing out of a block of land of 10 Acres at Muthurajawela for a Project for generating Energy out of Solid Waste

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-08-30

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to lease out a block of land in extent of 10 acres situated in Muthurajawela area, belonging to the Sri Lanka Land Reclamation and Development Corporation, to a private company, on a 30 year lease basis, for the implementation of a project for energy generation out of solid waste, as a solution to the issue on generating of solid waste disposal, was approved by the Cabinet in principle.
