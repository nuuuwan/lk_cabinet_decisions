Allocation of UDA land at Perahera Mawatha, Colombo 03 to the Colombo Plan Secretariat

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-03-02

- The Colombo Plan Secretariat, an international bureau located in Sri Lanka, which is currently housed in a rented building has been proposed to be established in a more spacious premises. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to provide a plot of land at Perahera Mawatha, Colombo 03, identified as suitable for the establishment of the Secretariat, to the said Secretariat, on a lease term of 50 years, was approved by the Cabinet.
