Awarding the construction activities of the Kataragama Circuit Bungalow for the migrant workers, to the State Engineering Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-01-13

- The proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Foreign Employment, to award the contract for construction of a circuit bungalow in Kataragama, for the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment, to the State Engineering Corporation functions under the Ministry of Housing and Construction, at an estimated cost of Rs.141 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
