Enforcement of the Memorandum of Understanding relevant to Youth development due to be signed between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Islamic Republic of Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-09-27

- The Cabinet has already granted approval to sign the Memorandum of Understanding pertaining to Youth development between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. As such, the proposal made by the Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, for the revised Memorandum of Understanding, incorporating the subsequent amendments made by the Government of Pakistan, to be signed between the Ministry of National Policies and Economic Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Inter Provincial Coordination of Pakistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
