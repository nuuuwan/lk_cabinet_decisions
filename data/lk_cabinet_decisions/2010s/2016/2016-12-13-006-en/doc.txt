Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and  Tajikistan on Cooperation in the Field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-12-13

- The proposal made by Hon. John Amarathunga, the Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of  Sri Lanka and the Government of the Republic of Tajikistan, on Cooperation in the Field of Tourism, during the official visit of the President of Tajikistan to Sri Lanka, scheduled between 13th to 15th of December 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
