Allocation of 100 Housing Units to the Sri Lanka Police from the ‘Nila Sevana’ Project at Wekunagoda, Galle

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-29

- ‘Nila Sevana’ Project at Wekunagoda, Galle is a project jointly carried out under a tripartite Agreement by the Board of Investment, Ministry of Public Administration and Management and the Private Sector. The proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayaka, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, seeking the allocation of Treasury Funds of Rs.333 to the Sri Lanka Police to provide 100 units from the said Project to the Police personnel such as Police Constables, Police Sergeants and Police Officers in the Inspector Grades serving in the Galle area needing housing facilities, was approved by the Cabinet.
