Consultancy Services for the Supervision of the construction of Section 2  from Meerigama to Kurunegala of the Central Expressway Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-15

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for Consultancy Services for the Supervision of Construction of Section 2 from Meerigama to Kurunegala (39.29 km) of the Central Expressway Project, in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Consultant Procurement Committee (CPCC), was approved by the Cabinet.
