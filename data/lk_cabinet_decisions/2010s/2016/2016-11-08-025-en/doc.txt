Tender for the supply of 7,000,000 sets of equipment used for Injecting drugs to patients

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-08

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the tender for the supply of 7,000,000 sets of Disposable Intravenous solution giving  for single use for injecting drugs to patients, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
