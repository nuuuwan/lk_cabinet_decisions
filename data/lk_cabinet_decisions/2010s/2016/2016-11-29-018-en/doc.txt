Establishment of a Research and Training Complex at the Faculty of Agriculture in the University of Jaffna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-11-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for consultancy services of the above project funded by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
