Awarding the Tender for the purchase of 210,000 Vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 4,000 IU in 0.4 ml Prefilled Syringe / Vial & 322,500 Vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 6,000 IU in 0.6 ml Prefilled Syringe / Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-01-13

- Enoxaparin Sodium Injection is used to treat heart diseased patients.  As per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine,  to purchase 210,000 Vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 4,000 IU in 0.4 ml Prefilled Syringe / Vial & 322,500 Vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 6,000 IU in 0.6 ml Prefilled Syringe / Vial, was approved by  the Cabinet.
