Construction of a Warehouse Complex at Embilipitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-10-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance,  to award the contract in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), for the construction of a warehouse complex at Embilipitiya with a floor area of 28,500 sq.ft. consisting of an office building, a laboratory, a building with accommodation facilities, a water tank, and a transformer room etc.,  with the objective of providing the farmers of the Ratnapura District, with quality storage facilities to store their agricultural produce, was approved by the Cabinet.
