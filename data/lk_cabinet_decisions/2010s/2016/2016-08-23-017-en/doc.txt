Development of Infrastructure Facilities of the Sri Lanka Institute of Advanced Technological Education, Jaffna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-08-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for construction of a four (04) storied building complex for the Sri Lanka Institute of Advanced Technological Education, Jaffna, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
