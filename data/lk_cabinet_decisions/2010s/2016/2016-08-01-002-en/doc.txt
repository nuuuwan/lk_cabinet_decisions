Granting of State Land on Long Term Lease towards the Construction of a 'Resource Centre' for the Sri Lanka Administrative Service (SLAS) Officers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-08-01

- The proposal made by the Prime Minister, Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, to grant a block of land located in Battaramulla area, to the Sri Lanka Administrative Service Association (SASA), for the construction of a Resource Centre for the officers of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service (SLAS), which is recognized as the premier and most important  public service involved in the process of implementing public policies at National, Provincial and Regional levels, was approved by the Cabinet.
