The Tender for the Supply of 475,000 Vials of Desferrioxamine 	Mesylate for Injection BP, 500 mg

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2016-02-03

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the Tender for the purchase of the above medicine which is essential for the treatment of thalaseemia, to the Bidder recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, at a price of US$ 1,413,125.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
