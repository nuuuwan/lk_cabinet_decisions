Construction of New Three Storied Building for the Divisional Secretariat Ratnapura and New Official Quarters

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-29

- the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Pubic Administration and Home Affairs, to construct the above buildings for the Divisional Secretariat, Ratnapura, to provide better facilities to the people living in the area  with funds to be provided through the Medium Term Budgetary Framework from 2012 to 2014, after acquisition of the relevant land is duly completed, was approved by the Cabinet.
