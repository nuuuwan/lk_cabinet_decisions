Amendments to the Tax Appeal Commission Act No. 23 of 2011

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-10-19

- the amendments will further clarify the composition of the members who can be appointed to the Commission and will broaden the type of taxes that fall under the purview of the Commission.   These amendments will also amend the time frame within which appeals referred to the Commission, should be determines by the Commission.   The amendment Bill submitted by H.E. the President,  in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, was approved for presentation in Parliament.
