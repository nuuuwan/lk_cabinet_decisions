Strengthening the patient care services of District General Hospital - Hambantota, District Hospital - Tissamaharama and Base Hospital - Karawanella by providing essential Medical Equipment

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-04-06

- on a proposal made by Hon.Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, Cabinet approval was granted to sign the commercial contract of this project.  Provision of these equipment to the hospitals will provide better health care services to the people living in the relevant areas.
