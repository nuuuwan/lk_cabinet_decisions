State Literary Festival 2011 to be held at Vijayaba National School, Maho

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-17

- on a joint proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education and Hon. T.B. Ekanayake, Minister of Culture and the Arts, Cabinet approval was granted to hold the above Festival, with the Vijayaba National School, Maho functioning as its main centre and with the participation of Government Institutions in the area.
