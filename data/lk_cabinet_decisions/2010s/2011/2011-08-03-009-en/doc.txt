Construction of Priest Ward - Stage I, Renovation of Administrative Building & Kitchen - Teaching Hospital, Anuradhapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health to construct a four storied building for the Priests at the Anuradhapura Teaching Hospital and to award the construction of the first floor as Stage I of the project and also the renovation of the Administrative Building and the kitchen to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, was approved by the Cabinet.
