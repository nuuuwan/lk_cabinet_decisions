Amendments to the National Film Corporation Act No.47 of 1971 as amended by Act No.45 of 1980

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-07

- the proposal made by Hon. Keheliya Rambukwella, Minister of Mass Media and Information, to introduce necessary amendments to the above Act  in keeping with the recommendations of the Cabinet Sub-Committee  on Legislation and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare amending legislation for the purpose,  was approved by the Cabinet.
