Construction of Courts Complex at Homagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-24

- the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to construct a four storied building to accommodate two Magistrate Courts, a District Court, a High Court and a Commercial High Court at Homagama at a cost of Rs.464.4 million with funds to be provided from 2011 to 2013, was approved by the Cabinet.
