Awarding the contract for construction of District Secretariat, Kegalle

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-30

- on a proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, Cabinet approval was granted for the award of the contract for the construction of the new building complex of the District Secretariat, Kegalle.  This will provide better facilities for the people in the Kegalle District to have their needs attended to.
