Field training for newly recruited officers to the Sri Lanka Administrative Service SLAS

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-03

- H.E. the President extended his profound gratitude to the people of Jaffna who provided accommodation and looked after the newly recruited SLAS officers from South who visited Jaffna for field training. His Excellency was made to understand that these officers were looked after with warm feelings by the residents in Jaffna which reminded him of the very close relations the people of the two communities enjoyed before the conflict situation in the North.
