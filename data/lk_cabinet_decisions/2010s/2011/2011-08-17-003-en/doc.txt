Lease of land at Hikkaduwa to the Hikkaduwa Urban Council

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-17

- The Urban Development Authority has prepared a Development Plan for Hikkaduwa to provide better services to the Public as well as to the Tourists visiting Hikkaduwa. The proposal made by H.E. the President to allocate a block of land to the Hikkaduwa Urban Council for conducting the Weekly Fair, was approved by the Cabinet.
