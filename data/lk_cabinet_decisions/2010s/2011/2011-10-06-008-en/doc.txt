Construction of Building Complex for Students Hostel and Staff Quarters at South Eastern University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-10-06

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education to award the construction of the above for a total cost of Rs.92.508 million as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC) and to commence construction work with available funds in 2011 and complete it  with funds to be provided in 2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
