Construction of a three storied building for Divisional Secretariat Bandaragama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-03

- the proposal made by Hon.W.D.J.Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs to construct the above building at a total cost of Rs.86.5 million and to initiate preliminary activities of the project utilizing Rs.10 million allocated in 2011 and obtaining the balance financial requirement in 2012 and 2013, was  approved by the Cabinet.
