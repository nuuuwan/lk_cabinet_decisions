Construction of Water Tank - General Hospital, Badulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-01

? this is a major hospital in the Uva Province which caters to a large number of patients in and around Badulla.  The proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to construct a new water tank for this hospital at a cost of Rs.33,197,378.56 and to award the construction work to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) which is a Government concern, was approved by the Cabinet.
