Declaration on Environmental Cooperation between the Ministry of Environment of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Environment of the Czech Republic

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-03

- the above declaration was signed by Hon. Anura Priyadarshana Yapa, Minister of Environment on behalf of Sri Lanka.  This includes co-operation on the sectors such as, waste water treatment, solid based management, water resources and river basin management, support for renewable energy, environmental risk management etc.
