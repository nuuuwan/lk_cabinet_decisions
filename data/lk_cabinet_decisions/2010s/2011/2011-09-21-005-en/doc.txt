Annual Report of the Ceylon Fertilizer Company Limited for the year 2010

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-21

- Ceylon Fertilizer Company contributes actively to the supply of the necessary fertilizers to cultivators at subsidized rates, in keeping with the policy of the government.  The total amount of fertilizer distributed throughout the country during the year is 249,332 MT.  The company has earned a profit of 42.4 million before taxation.
