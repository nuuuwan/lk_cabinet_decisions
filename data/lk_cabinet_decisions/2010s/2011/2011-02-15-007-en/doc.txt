Revision of Total Cost Estimate for the Development of Trincomalee Campus at Konesapuri  Phase 1 - Relocation of Existing facilities of Trincomalee Campus of Eastern University

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-02-15

- Cabinet approval has already been granted for the development of Trincomalee Campus at Konespuri, phase 1, for the relocation of existing facilities of the Trincomalee Campus of the Eastern University, to accommodate hostels for 35 male students and 35 female students, building for academic activities, library building, administrative building etc. On a proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, Cabinet approval was granted to increase the total cost estimate for the said project from Rs.91.1 million to Rs.128.304 million.
