Amendment of the Act of the National Institute of Business Management

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-02-09

- On a proposal made by Hon. Dullas Alahapperuma, Minister of Youth Affairs and Skills Development to change the composition of the Governing Council of the institute, for the smoothing functioning of the administrative activities of the institute, Cabinet approval was granted for same and for the draft Bill prepared for the purpose to be presented in Parliament.
