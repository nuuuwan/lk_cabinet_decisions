Memorandum of Understanding on Judicial Corporation between the Ministry of Justice of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Justice of the Arab Republic of Egypt

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-29

- the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to sign this Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries to promote the mutual understanding and amicable cooperation in the field of Law and Justice between the two countries, was approved the Cabinet.
