Midyear Year Fiscal Position Report - 2011

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-07-27

- this contains the information of government revenue, expenditure, cash flow activities and borrowings within the first four months of the year. This also contains data on macro-economic performance, price development, foreign aid, government debt, and external sector performance. The proposal made by H.E. the President to present this report in Parliament was approved by the Cabinet.
