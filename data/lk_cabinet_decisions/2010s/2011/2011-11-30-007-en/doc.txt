Tender for the Supply of 650,000 of Deferasirox Tablets 400mg

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-30

- the  proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health to award the tender for the purchase of the above, to the lowest responsive bidder, on the recommendation of Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, for a total cost of US $1,199,220.00 (LKR131,644,013.60), was approved by the Cabinet.
