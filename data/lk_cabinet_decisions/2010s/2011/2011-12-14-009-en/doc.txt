Construction of stage I of the seven storied building for the Faculty of Graduate Studies, University of Colombo using Generated funds

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-14

- Cabinet approval has already been granted for the construction of  a seven storied building for the above Faculty of the University of Colombo at a total cost of 405 million.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to construct first two floors of the above under Stage I of the project with an amount of Rs.152 million from the generated funds of the Faculty of Graduate Studies, was approved by the Cabinet.
