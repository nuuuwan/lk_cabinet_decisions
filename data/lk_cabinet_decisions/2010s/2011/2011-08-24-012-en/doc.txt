Improving the Cath Lab Building of the Cardiac Unit - National Hospital, Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-24

- The National Hospital, Colombo is to receive two Cardiac Catheter Machines within a period of three months and the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to make necessary improvements to buildings at a cost of Rs.24.14 million and to entrust the work to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, was approved by the Cabinet.
