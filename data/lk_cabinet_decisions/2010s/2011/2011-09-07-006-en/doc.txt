Annual Reports of the State Timber Corporation for the years 2008 and 2009

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-07

-  the 	Corporation has increased its income from Rs.3141 million to Rs.3581 million, the	gross profit from Rs.1210 million to Rs.1351 million and the net profit from Rs.647	million to Rs.810 million, in 2009.  It contributed Rs.1635 million to the National	Income in 2009.  With the view to protect natural resources for the benefit of the future	generation, the Corporation has instituted an island-wide tree planting programme to	promote developing the forest cover in the country. The above report submitted by	Hon. Anura  Priyadharshana Yapa,  Minister  of  Environment, was  approved by the	Cabinet for presentation in Parliament.
