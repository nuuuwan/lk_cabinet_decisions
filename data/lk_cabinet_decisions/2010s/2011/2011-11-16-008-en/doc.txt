Annual Report and Statement of Accounts of the Palmyrah Development Board for the year 2009 in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-16

- this is the only state institution engaged in  the Palmyrah Industry and during the year under review 2,268,120 seeds have been planted in the Districts of Jaffna, Mannar, Vavuniya, Kilinochchi, Mullaithevu, Batticaloa, Trincomalee, Ampara, Puttlam, Hambantota and Moneragala with the view to increase Palmyrah vegetation in the country.  The Palmyrah Development Board is also involved in conducting vocational training programmes in Handicraft, Fibre production and Jaggery  production.  Handicraft Training Programme is for a period of six months and the other two for a period of three months each. The above Annual Report  submitted by Hon. Douglas Devananda, Minister of Traditional Industries and Small Enterprise Development, was approved for presentation in Parliament.
