Construction of a Building for the Faculty of Management Studies and Commerce Stage III/Phase II, University of Sri Jayewardenepura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-28

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayaka, Minister of Higher Education, to revise the total cost estimate of the above project, so as to complete the six storied building for the above University from Rs.65 million to Rs.157.5 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
