Memorandum of Understanding between China Development Bank 	Corporation and Government of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

- On a proposal made by  H.E. 	the President, China Development Bank Corporation has agreed to provide 	financial assistance in a sum of US$ 1.5 billion within 03 years for the 	construction of roads, bridges, water supply schemes and also for irrigation 	and power. The proposal made by H.E. the President to sign a Memorandum 	of Understanding for the purpose with the China Development Bank 	Corporation by the Ministry of Finance and Planning, was approved by the 	Cabinet.
