Implementation of Modernization Activities of the Maligawatte Housing Scheme by the Sri Lanka State Engineering Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-07-27

- the proposal made by Hon. Wimal Weerawansa, Minister of Construction, Engineering Services, Housing and Common Amenities to entrust the modernization activities of the Maligawatta Housing Complex, to the Sri Lanka State Engineering Corporation at a cost of Rs.160 million made available in the Budget for 2011, was approved by the Cabinet.
