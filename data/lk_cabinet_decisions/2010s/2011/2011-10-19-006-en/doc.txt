Tender for the Supply of Paracetamal Tablets BP 500mg; 625,000,000 Tablets

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-10-19

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health to award the above tender to the lowest responsive bidder as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee for a total cost of  Rs.226,032,875.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
