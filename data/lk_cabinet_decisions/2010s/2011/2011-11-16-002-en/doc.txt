Preparation of a National Policy Framework and Action Plan on Consumer Welfare

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-16

- it has been observed that the country needs a more comprehensive and coordinated effort to have a National Policy Framework and Action Plan on Consumer Welfare in which all government Ministries will work under one umbrella to facilitate and coordinate the process of building a developed society where consumer welfare is part and parcel of the society. The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinna, Minister (Senior) for Consumer Welfare,  to conduct discussions with stakeholders etc. and to prepare  a National Policy and Programme for the purpose, in association with the Ministry of Co-operatives and Internal Trade, was approved by the Cabinet.
