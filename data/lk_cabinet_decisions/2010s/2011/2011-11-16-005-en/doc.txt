National Sports Policy of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-16

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahindananda Aluthgamage, Minister of Sports for a National Sports Policy of Sri Lanka, for the creation of a healthy younger generation to ensure national unity, peace and development  through proper development of sports in Sri Lanka and proper scientific and technical training for the younger generation and for the formulation of National Sports Policy in association with the relevant Line Ministries, was approved by the Cabinet.
