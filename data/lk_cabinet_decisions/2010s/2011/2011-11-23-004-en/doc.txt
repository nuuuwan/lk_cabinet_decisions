Tender for the Supply of the Normal Immunoglobulin for Intravenous Use BP 5G-6G 9000 vials

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-23

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the above tender to the lowest responsive bidder, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee,  at a total cost of US$ 1,980,000.00 (LKR 216,788,616.00), was approved by the Cabinet.
