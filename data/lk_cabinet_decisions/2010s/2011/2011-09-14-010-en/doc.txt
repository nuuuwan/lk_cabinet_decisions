Establishment of a Heritage Information and Activity Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-14

- the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Jagath Balasuriya, Minister of National Heritage, for the establishment of a Heritage Information and Activity Centre to provide information about all heritage sites in Sri Lanka on Provincial and District Basis through a well designed Web Site, leaflets and brochures at a cost of Rs.106 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
