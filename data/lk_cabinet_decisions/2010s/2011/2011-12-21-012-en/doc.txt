Payment of Bonus to the Employees of  Government Corporations and Fully Owned Government companies for the Year 2011

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-21

- the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, for the payment of bonus to the employees of  Government Corporations and Fully Owned Government companies for the Year 2011, as was paid in the year 2010, was approved by the Cabinet.
