Procurement of 25 Nos. Super Luxury Buses on Easy Credit Basis to Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-21

- the proposal made by Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of Transport, to procure 25 super luxury buses to the Sri Lanka Transport Board, in order to minimize the traffic congestion on approach roads to Colombo city, such as Galle Road, Kandy Road, High Level Road, Negombo Road, Kaduwela Road, Horana Road  and to provide better facilities to commuters, was approved by the Cabinet.
