Colombo Consumers? Price Index (Base 2006/07)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

? the current Colombo 	Consumer Price Index is the country's official price index and is computed 	monthly. The pattern used in computing is based on Household Income and 	Expenditure Survey 2002 and the average household expenditure of a sample 	of 1300 household from the Colombo urban area. This Price Index required 	regular revision based on current Consumer Expenditure Surveys. The 	proposal made by H.E. the President to adopt a new and improved Price 	Index which reflects more realistically the changes in aggregate level of the 	Consumer Price,  based on the Household Income and Expenditure Survey 	2006/07, with effect from June 2011 and to update it every 03 to 05 years 	based on expenditure survey, was approved by the Cabinet.
