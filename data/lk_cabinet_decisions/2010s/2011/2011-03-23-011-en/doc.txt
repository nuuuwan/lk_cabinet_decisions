Expansion of Sangaramaya Bhikku Hostel of the University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- Government has observed that the annual intake of student monks to the University has increased significantly over the past two decades and as such, the required hostel facilities have to be expanded.  On a proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, Cabinet approval was granted to expand the existing Sangaramaya Bhikku Hostel of the University of Peradeniya through construction of a two storied building at a total cost of Rs.34.2 million.  The Project will be commenced with funds provided to the University Grants Commission for 2011.
