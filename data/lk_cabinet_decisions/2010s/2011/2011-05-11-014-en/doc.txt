Development of INTRA Provincial Roads and Incomplete Public Assets

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-11

- It is proposed to develop 25 Km. of selected roads in each electorate under this program. The Ministry of Economic Development will release funds for the purpose through the respective District Secretaries. The total length of road that will be laid with asphalt concrete during the year 2011 will be 3740 km.
