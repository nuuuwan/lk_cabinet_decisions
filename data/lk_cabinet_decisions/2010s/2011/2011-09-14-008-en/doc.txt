"Deyata Sevana" National Tree Planting Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Anura Priyadharshana Yapa, Minister of Environment, to inaugurate this year's "Deyata Sevana" National Tree Planting Programme on 15th November 2011 at the premises of the Ranaviru Upahara "Sandahiruseya" in Anuradhapura and to implement this programme islandwide, in collaboration with all Ministries, Departments, Corporations,  Statutory Boards, Provincial Councils, Local Authorities and District and Divisional Secretariats, was approved by the Cabinet.
