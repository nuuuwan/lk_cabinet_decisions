Renovation and Development of Cultural Infrastructure of John de Silva Memorial Theatre and National Arts Gallery (Jathika Kalabhavana) Premises

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-07

- the proposal made by Hon. T.B. Ekanayaka, Minister of Culture and the Arts to renovate and develop the cultural infrastructure of John de Silva Memorial Theatre and the National Arts Gallery (Jathika Kalabhavana) Premises at a cost of Rs.322.7 million to provide better facilities to the visiting public, was approved by the Cabinet.
