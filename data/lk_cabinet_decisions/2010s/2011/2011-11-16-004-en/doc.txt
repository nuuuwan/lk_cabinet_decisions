Relocation of Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-16

- in keeping with the government policy to ensure a dignified resettlement to all IDPs, the Ministry of Resettlement has already resettled almost 97% of the IDPs who were displaced soon after the humanitarian operation, in their native villages by October 2011. The proposal made by  Hon. Gunaratne Weerakoon, Minister of Resettlement, for the relocation of 1687 families in the Mullaitivu District, 1272 families in Muttur in the Trincomalee District and 166 families in the Mannar District, with the assistance of the Security Forces in the North and East, was approved in principle by the Cabinet.
