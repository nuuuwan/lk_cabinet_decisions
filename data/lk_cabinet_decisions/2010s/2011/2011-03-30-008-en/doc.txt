A Credit of Special Drawing Rights 63,600,000 from the International 	Development Association of the World Bank for the Road Sector 	Assistance Project ? Second Additional Financing

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-30

?  The Government has 	concluded negotiations with the International Development Association (IDA) of the 	World Bank to obtain additional financing of Special Drawing Rights (SDR) 	63,600,000 (approximately US$ 100 million) for the Road Sector Assistance Project. 	The main objective of the project is to reduce transportation cost and to improve the 	connectivity to the Northern and Eastern Provinces by re-surfacing and rehabilitation 	of 134 Km. of the Road Sections from Ambepussa to Dambulla (91 Km.) and 	Kantale to Trincomalee (43 Km.) of the A6 Road. The total cost of the project is US$ 	110 million, of which US$ 100 million will be provided by the IDA and the balance 	of US$ 10 million by the Government of Sri Lanka.  This credit facility is interest 	free.  The proposal made by H.E. the President to enter into a Financing Agreement 	with the IDA  of the World Bank was approved by the Cabinet.
