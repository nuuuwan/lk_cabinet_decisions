Extension of the Project on Delimitation of the Outer Edge of the Continental Margin of Sri Lanka under the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (DEOCOM Project)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

-  Sri Lanka is a party to the above convention.  The objective of the above project is to conduct a Geophysical Survey in the seabed area adjacent to Sri Lanka's 200 nautical miles, Exclusive Economic Zone and process, interpret the data gathered from the survey and to prepare Sri Lanka's claim under the United Nations Convention of the Law of the Sea for submission to the UN Commission on the Limits of the Continental Shelf.  On a proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development, Cabinet approval was granted to extend the above project up to the end of 2012 or until such time the project is completed.
