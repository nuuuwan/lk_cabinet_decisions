Transfer of Title Deeds to Relocated families at Terrance Avenue, Dehiwala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-04

- Sri Lanka Land Reclamation and Development Corporation (SLLR&DC) has acquired approximately 6.5 acres of marshy land at Terrence Avenue, Dehiwala in 1989 and about 45 families who were scattered in this land at the time of acquisition were relocated in a part of the same land.  On a proposal made by H.E. the President, as Minister of Defence, Cabinet approval was granted to issue title deeds to these occupants.
