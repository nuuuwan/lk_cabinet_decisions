Procurement of Sterilized Surgical Gloves and Examination Gloves to the Government Hospitals under the Ministry of Health

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-30

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health for the purchase of a total quantity 18,259,500 surgical gloves and examination gloves at a total cost of Rs.202,386,000.00, from the relevant suppliers indicated in the Memorandum, was approved by the Cabinet.
