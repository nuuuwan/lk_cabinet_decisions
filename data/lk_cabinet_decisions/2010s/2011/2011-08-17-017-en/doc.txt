Installation of the Main Electricity Supply System at the Operating Theatre, Intensive Care Unit, Labour Room and Premature Infants Unit - Hambantota General Hospital

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-17

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, for the installation of the Main Electricity Supply System at the General Hospital, Hambantota which caters to a large number of people living in and around Hambantota area and entrust the work to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau which is a government concern, was approved by the Cabinet.
