Completion of Construction of Phase II of the Building for Colombo Regional Centre of the Open University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-10-06

- construction of a two storied building for the purpose at an initial total cost estimate of Rs.70 million,  phasing it out over a period of two years, has already been approved by the Cabinet.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of  Higher Education, to increase the floor areas of the building to accommodate additional class rooms, within the same cost estimate, was approved by the Cabinet.
