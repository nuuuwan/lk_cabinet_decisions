"Deyata Kirula" Programme -  2012  - Archaeological Conservation Work

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-09

- the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Jagath Balasuriya, Minister of National Heritage, to commence archaeological conservation work at a number of places in the Anuradhapura District, including Mihintale and Thanthirimale, in parallel with the "Deyata Kirula" National Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
