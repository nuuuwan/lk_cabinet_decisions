Repairs and Extension of Wards and the Office Complex in the O.P.D. Building - Stage II - Base Hospital, Kantale

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-11

- this is a major hospital in the Eastern Province serving the people living in an around Kantale in the Eastern Province.  Construction of two stories of this building have been completed as the first stage of the project.  On a proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, Cabinet approval was granted to award the construction of the third storey and repairs to others, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, which is a Government concern.
