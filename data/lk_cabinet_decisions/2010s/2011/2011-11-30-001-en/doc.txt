Allocation of a block of land at Medabowala, Kandy to the National Water Supply 	& Drainage Board for Kandy City Waste Water Disposal Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-30

- the proposal 	made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban	Development, to lease out a block of land situated at William Gopallawa Mawatha,	Kandy, to the National Water Supply and Drainage Board for the establishment of the	Kandy City Waster Water Disposal Pump House, was approved by the Cabinet.
