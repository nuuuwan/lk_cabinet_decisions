Construction of a Building for Installation of Telecobolt Unit Bhabhatron II & Simulator at District General Hospital - Hambantota

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- the Government of India has expressed its willingness to donate the above unit to be installed at the District General Hospital, Hambantota, to facilitate Cancer Treatment for patients in remote areas in and around Hambantota.  This was on a proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health.  Cabinet approval was granted to award the contract to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) at a cost of Rs.127.44 million, on approval of estimates by the Department of National Planning.
