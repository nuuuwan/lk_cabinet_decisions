Entrusting the work on Stage II of the Lumbini Vidyalaya, Sports Complex to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

- Cabinet approval was granted to the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to award the work pertaining to the construction of Stage II of the above Sports Complex, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau at an estimated cost of Rs.62.78 million.
