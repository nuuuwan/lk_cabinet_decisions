Agreement on Economic, Commercial and Technical Cooperation between the Government of the State of Qatar and the Government  of the Democratic  Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-07

-  the proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, Minister of Industry and Commerce, to sign the above Agreement between the State of Qatar and the Government of Sri Lanka to enhance economic relations between the  two countries and to promote and facilitate export and import of industrial and agricultural raw material and value added products, was approved by the Cabinet.  The proposed Agreement will promote and develop cooperation between the two countries in economic, commercial and technical fields.
