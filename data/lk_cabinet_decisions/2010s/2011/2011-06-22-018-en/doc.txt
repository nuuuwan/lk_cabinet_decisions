Census of Population and Housing 2011

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

- Cabinet approval has already 	been granted to hold the 14 Census of Population and  Housing in the year 	2011 and the relevant Gazette notification has already been published for the 	purpose.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, on recommendation on 	the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, to proceed with the Data 	Processing Activities and for the designing and printing of Census schedules  	etc., was approved by the Cabinet.
