Acquisition of land and payment of compensation to the residents under the 	second 	phase of Hambantota Port Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-10-12

- lands required for the second	phase of the Hambantota Port Development Project and the basic survey work pertaining 	to these lands have been finalized. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his	capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, for the appointment of a Committee of	Officials to identify the occupants who are entitled for compensation in respect of the	lands which are to be acquired for the second phase of the above project, with the	concurrence of the District Secretary/Divisional Secretary, Hambantota and other	relevant authorities, was approved by the Cabinet.
