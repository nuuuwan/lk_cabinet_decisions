Construction of Sethsiripaya Stage II Administrative Complex Building at Battaramulla. Award of the Contract for Supply & Installation of Standby Diesel Generators; and Construction of an Additional Floor

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-11

- this building complex is being constructed by the Government with the view to citing Government institutions, presently occupying  rented out buildings, outside the Colombo City area.  It has also been proposed to construct an additional floor in the building to accommodate more Government offices, considering the present demand.   On a proposal made by H.E. the President on the recommendations of the relevant Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committees, Cabinet approval was granted to award the contract for the supply and installation of standby generators and for the construction of an additional floor.
