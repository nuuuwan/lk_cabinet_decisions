Housing   Development   Programme   for  Underserved  Settlements  in Colombo and adjoining areas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

- on a proposal made by H.E. the President, 	the Government has launched a development programme through the Urban 	Development Authority (UDA) to provide housing facilities to the Underserved Settlements in and around Colombo. Cabinet approval has	already been granted for the appointment of a Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee and a Project Committee to evaluate the proposals received from investors. Separate Contracts have already been awarded on	the recommendation of the above Committees, for the construction of 4393 housing units. Under the third step of this programme 1034 housing units are to be constructed, and the first phase  will consist of 216 housing units at Salamulla Kolonnawa and the work will be handled by the Sri Lanka Land Reclamation and Development Corporation. The fourth step of the project will consist of 31,500 housing units with 16,500 in the first phase to be funded by Unicorn Investment Bank of Bahrain. 7,500 Housing Units will be constructed initially within a period of one (01) year.
