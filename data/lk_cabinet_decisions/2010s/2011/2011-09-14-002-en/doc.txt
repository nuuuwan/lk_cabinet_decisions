Ibbagamuwa Water Supply Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to inaugurate a water supply scheme to Ibbagamuwa Town and Suburbs at a cost of Rs.239 million, with funds to be provided from 2012 to 2014, was approved by the Cabinet.  The proposed project will bring direct benefits to the residents of Ibbagamuwa, Meddegampola, Siyambalawehera, Ihala Gokarella, Hiddana, Polgolla, Ipalawa, Thalgodapitiya, Dehelgamuwa, Udawala, Pannala, Bakmeegolla, Galkattigama Grama Niladhari Divisions.
