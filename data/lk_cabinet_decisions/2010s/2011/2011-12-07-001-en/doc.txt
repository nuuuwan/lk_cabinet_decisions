Allocation of land at Pannipitiya to the Ministry of Education of the Western Province

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-07

- the proposal made by H.E. the President,  in his  capacity  as  Minister of Defence and Urban Development to allocate a block of land over one acre in extent, situated at Depanama, Pannipitiya to the Ministry of Education of the Western Province, to be used for expansion of school premises of the Depanama Dharmapala Junior School, was approved by the Cabinet.
