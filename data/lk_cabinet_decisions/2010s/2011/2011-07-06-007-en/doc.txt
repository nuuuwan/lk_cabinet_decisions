Repairs to Internal Road Network at Rukmalgama Housing Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-07-06

- Rukmalgama Housing Scheme is situated in Rukmale village, Kottawa in the Maharagama Urban Council area and the Housing Scheme consists of 501 Housing Units.  The proposal made by Hon. Wimal Weerawansa, Minister of Construction, Engineering Services, Housing and Common Amenities to improve the internal road network of the Housing Scheme at a cost of Rs.21 million by utilizing funds of the National Housing Development Authority, was approved by the 	Cabinet.
