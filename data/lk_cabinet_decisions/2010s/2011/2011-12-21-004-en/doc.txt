Implementation of Dikkumbura Water Supply System

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-21

- Dikkumbura is located in the Galle District about 6 Km. away from Ahangama. This area lies within the  administrative area of the Imaduwa Pradeshiya Sabha. The objective of the proposal is to supply safe drinking water facilities to the people living in the area. The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage to implement this project at a total cost of Rs.317 million with funds to be provided during 2013 and 2014 , was approved by the Cabinet.
