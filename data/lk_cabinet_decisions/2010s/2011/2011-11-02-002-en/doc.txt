Implementation of Gonapinuwala Water Supply Scheme - Approval of Total Cost Estimate (TCE)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-02

- with the implementation of Gonapinuwala Water Supply Scheme, it is expected to distribute drinking water to the people in Gonapinuwala town and its suburbs and considerably enhance the living standards of residents in the area. The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to implement the above project with an allocation of Rs.349 million within the Medium Term Budgetary Framework of 2013-2015 , was approved by the Cabinet.
