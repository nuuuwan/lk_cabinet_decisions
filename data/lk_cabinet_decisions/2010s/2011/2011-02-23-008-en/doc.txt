Restructuring the Dedicated Economic Centres

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-02-23

- in order to streamline the activities of the Dedicated Economic Centres located at Narahenpita, Dambulla, Meegoda, Veyangoda, Ratmalana and Piliyandala, Cabinet approval was granted to set up a National Steering Committee to provide the much needed guidance, supervision and advise for the purpose.
