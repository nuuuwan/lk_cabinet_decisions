Repairing the Building complex with class rooms and the old Boarding House at the Visakha National School, Colombo 04

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-21

- the proposal by Hon. Bandula Gunawardane, Minister of Education, to award the contract for repair and modernization, planning and supervision of the three storied building complex and the Boarding House building on a competitive bidding basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
