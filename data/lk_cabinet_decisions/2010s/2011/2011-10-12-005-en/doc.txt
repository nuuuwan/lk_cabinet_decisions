Annual Report of Central Consultancy Bureau (CECB) for the year 2008

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-10-12

- this is a Public Sector Engineering Organization with a strong human resource base that consists of multi-disciplinary professionals and technical staff representing various engineering disciplines. The CECB was able to enter the International Engineering Market successfully which in turn earns valuable foreign exchange. The Bureau entered into projects with the Republic of Maldives, United Kingdom, India, Rwanda, Malawi and Tanzania.
