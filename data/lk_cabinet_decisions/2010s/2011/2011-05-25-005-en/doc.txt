Construction of Internal Road Network to access the buildings of Sabaragamuwa University of  Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-25

- this University was inaugurated as a National University in 1996.  Since there is no access road to the buildings of this University, on a proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, Cabinet approval was granted to construct an internal road network to provide access to these buildings at a total cost of Rs.126 million, to be provided by the General Treasury in 2011 and 2012.
