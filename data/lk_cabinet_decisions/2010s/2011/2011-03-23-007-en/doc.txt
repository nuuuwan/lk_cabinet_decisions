Asian Regional Ministerial Meeting on Aquaculture for Food Security, Nutrition and Economic Development - 28th to 29th July 2011, Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- on a proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development, this meeting is to be held in July in Colombo with the participation of the representatives from 115 countries.  The meeting will be jointly organized by the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), the Network of Aquaculture Centres in the Asian and Pacific Region and the Ministry of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development.  This will facilitate to develop bilateral relationship with Asian countries which can be beneficial in respect of technology transfer and attracting investments to Sri Lanka in the field of Aquaculture.  Cabinet approval was granted to hold this conference in Colombo in July 2011.
