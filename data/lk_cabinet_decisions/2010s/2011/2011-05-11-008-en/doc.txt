Construction of a two storied building for the Divisional Secretariat, Rajanganaya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-11

- this Divisional Secretariat provides services to 21 Grama Niladhari  Divisions and to a population of about 36,500 living in these areas.  On a proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, Cabinet approval was granted to construct a new two storied building for the Divisional Secretariat, Rajanganaya and to provide financial provisions in a sum of Rs.22.5 million in 2012 and 2013 under the Vote of the District Secretary, Anuradhapura, for the purpose.
