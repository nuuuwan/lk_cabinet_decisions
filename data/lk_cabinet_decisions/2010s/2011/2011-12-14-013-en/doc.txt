Tender for the purchase of New Buses to Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of Transport, to award the tender, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, for the procurement of 54 seater buses at a unit price of Rs.4,740,940.00, to Lanka Ashok Layland PLC., utilizing Rs.500 million allocated for the year 2011, was approved by the Cabinet.
