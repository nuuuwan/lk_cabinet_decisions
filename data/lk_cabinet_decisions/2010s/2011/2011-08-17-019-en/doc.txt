Seminar of Asian Productivity Organization in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-17

- this is an International Agency based in Japan which provides assistance to its member countries to enhance productivity in the fields of agriculture and industry.  20 countries of the Asia-Pacific Region, including Sri Lanka, are members of this organization.  The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, to hold a Seminar on Training Course on Diversification and Value  Addition for Agricultural Products in Sri Lanka from 03rd to 09th October 2011, was approved by the Cabinet.
