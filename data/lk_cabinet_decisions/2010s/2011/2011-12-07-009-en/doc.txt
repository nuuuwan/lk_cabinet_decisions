Construction of 50m, 8 lane Swimming Pool with diving facilities at Piliyandala Central Collage

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-07

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahindananda Aluthgama, Minister of Sports to construct a swimming pool at the Piliyandala Central College at a total revised contract sum of Rs.43,882,660.00 was approved by the Cabinet.
