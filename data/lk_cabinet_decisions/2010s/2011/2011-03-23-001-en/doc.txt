Establishment of an Air Travel Agency by the Sri  Lanka Army

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- at present Military and other Public Personnel traveling abroad for official purposes are required to make their travel arrangements, including purchasing of Air Tickets, through the Agencies maintained by the State Banks and about Rs.275 million is being paid to these Agencies by the Ministry of Defence, Sri Lanka Army, Sri Lanka Navy, Sri Lanka Air Force and the Police Department.  On a proposal made by H.E. the President, on the recommendation of the Commander of the Sri Lanka Army, Cabinet approval was granted to permit the  Sri Lanka Army to operate an Air Travel Agency for the purpose, so that a substantial amount of money spent on this account could be diverted for the welfare of the soldiers.
