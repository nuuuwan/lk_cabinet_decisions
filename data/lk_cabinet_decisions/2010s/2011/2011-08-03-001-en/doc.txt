Recommendations of the Finance Commission for the Financial Year 2011

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-08-03

- in terms of the provisions of the Constitution, the Government has to apportion  adequate funds by the Annual Budget to meet the needs of the Provinces on the recommendation of, and in consultation with the Finance Commission.  Accordingly, the said recommendations submitted by H.E. the President were approved by the Cabinet for presentation in Parliament.
