Providing Uniform Material to School Children - 2012 Decisions of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-11-02

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education to import  school uniform materials, through State Trading Corporation spending Rs.1,054,222,500.00 to ensure timely delivery of school uniform material for the year 2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
