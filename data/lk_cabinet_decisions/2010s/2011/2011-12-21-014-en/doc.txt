Investment Opportunities under the Second International Airport at Mattala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-12-21

- the proposal made by Hon. Piyankara Jayaratne, Minister of Civil Aviation, to call for request for proposals for various investment opportunities relating to aviation services, to be associated with the Mattala Airport, in addition to passenger handling and cargo operations, was approved by the Cabinet. Blocks of land are to be leased out for the investors selected to launch their projects.
