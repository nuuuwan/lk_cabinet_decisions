Loan of US$ 48 million from the International Development Association of the World Bank for the second and third additional financing for livelihood development of the community in conflict affected areas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-07-13

- the proposal made by H.E. the President to enhance the financial assistance provided by the IDA of the World Bank from US$ 38 million to US$ 48 million for the ongoing community livelihood development project, was approved by the Cabinet.
