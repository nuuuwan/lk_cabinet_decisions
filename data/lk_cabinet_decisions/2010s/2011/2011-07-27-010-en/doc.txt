Development of Sports Infrastructure facilities - Construction of Provincial and District Sports Complexes

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-07-27

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahindananda Aluthgamage, Minister of Sports to construct 07 sports complexes in the 07 Districts, other than the Western Province and the Southern Province where the construction is under way, during the period from 2011 to 2015, was approved by the Cabinet. The locations will be Nuwaragam Palatha East, Kilinochchi, Badulla, Kundasale, Trincomalee, Kurunegala and Ratnapura.
