Financial Assistance of Euro 35 million from AFD (Agence Francaise de 	Developpement) of France for the Implementation of Jaffna and 	Kilinochchi 	Water Supply and Sanitation Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

- the objective of this 	project is to improve the water supply in the Jaffna and Kilinochchi Districts 	and to provide sanitation infrastructure to the residents in the Jaffna 	Municipal Council area. France has agreed to provide Euro 35 million (US$ 	48 million) to co- finance the project. Asian Development Bank has already 	agreed to provide US$ 90 million and the contribution of the Government of 	Sri Lanka will be US$ 26.04 million. The total cost of the project is US$ 	164.04 million. In addition, the International Fund for Agricultural 	Development is to provide US$ 20 million for the downstream 	development of the Iranamadu Tank. The proposal made by 	H.E. the 	President to enter into a loan agreement for the 	purpose with the Investor 	was approved by the Cabinet.
