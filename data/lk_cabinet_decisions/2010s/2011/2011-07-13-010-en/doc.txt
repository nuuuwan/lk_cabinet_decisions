Printing of School Textbooks for the year 2012

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-07-13

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to award the printing and supply of school textbooks for the year 2012 to 17 printing establishments, on the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, for distribution to schools in December 2011, was approved by the Cabinet.
