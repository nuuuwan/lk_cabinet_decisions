Integrated Implementation of National Housing Programme under Janasevena National Housing & Settlement Development Policy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- the Government has decided that all Ministries and Institutions that contribute to develop houses throughout the Island should work in an integrated manner under a common policy so that these programmes could be implemented efficiently and effectively.  This was on a proposal made by Hon. Wimal Weerawansa, Minister of Construction, Engineering Services, Housing and Common Amenities. An Inter-Ministerial Committee with relevant Senior Ministers of the Cabinet as permanent members of the Committee and also consisting of the representatives of relevant Ministries and Institutions directly involved in the matter, is to be set up for the purpose.
