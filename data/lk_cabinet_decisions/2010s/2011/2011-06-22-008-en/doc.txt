Construction  of  two  storied buildings  for   Divisional  Secretariats at Lahugala, Padiyathalawa and Ninthavur

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-06-22

- the proposal made by Hon.  	W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, 	to construct new two storied buildings for the above Divisional 	Secretariats to provide better facilities to the people living in these areas, 	with funds to be allocated 2012 and 2013, was approved by the Cabinet.
