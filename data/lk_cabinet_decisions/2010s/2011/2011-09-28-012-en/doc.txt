Construction of a Building for the Faculty of Language Studies Under Relocation of the Buddhasravaka Bhikshu University, Anuradhapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-28

- in view of the  urgent need to complete relocation of the above University and also to implement this project early, the proposal made Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education to implement the project in 2011 at a total cost of Rs.383.6 million and to complete the construction work within 15 months, was approved by the Cabinet.
