Construction of Court Building at Hingurakgoda

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-05-04

- Hingurakgoda District/Magistrate's Court	has been upgraded to a permanent Court and on a proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, Cabinet approval was granted to construct a new building complex for the purpose at an estimated cost of Rs.32,540,547.69, to provide better facilities to the people living in the area. The preliminary work will be commenced in 2011 and the construction work will be completed in 2012.
