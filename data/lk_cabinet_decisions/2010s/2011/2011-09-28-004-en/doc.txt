Construction of Administration Building Complex for Wayamba University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-28

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, for the revision of the total cost estimate of the above project from Rs.40 million to Rs.85.1 million, which increase has been approved by the Department of National Planning in keeping with the future development of the University,  was approved by the Cabinet.
