Construction of a three storied building for the Divisional Secretariat, Nuwaragampalatha - East

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-04-27

- this Divisional Secretariat situated in the Anuradhapura District serves a population of nearly 60,000 and consist of 29 Grama Niladhari Divisions.  With the view to provide better facilities to the people in the area, the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, to construct a three storied building for this Secretariat at a cost of Rs.22.5 million to be provided in 2012 and 2013, was approved by the Cabinet.
