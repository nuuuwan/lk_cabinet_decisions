Hosting of the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) in Sri Lanka in 2013

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- at the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting held in Trinidad & Tobago in November 2009, it has been unanimously agreed that the next Meeting for the year 2013 will be held in Sri Lanka. Thereafter, Sri Lanka will serve as the Chair of the Commonwealth for two years from 2013.  This will be the largest international event to be hosted by Sri Lanka since 1976.   54 Heads of State or Government of the Commonwealth or their representatives will participate at this Meeting.  On a proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, Cabinet approval was granted to establish the relevant Committees for the purpose.
