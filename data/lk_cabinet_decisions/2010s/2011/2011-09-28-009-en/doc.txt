Constructing the Police Stations of Point Pedro, Jaffna and Biyagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-09-28

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, for the construction of new buildings for the Police Stations of Point Pedro, Jaffna and Biyagama with funds to be provided in 2011 and 2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
