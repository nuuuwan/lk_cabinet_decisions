Memorandum of Understanding between the Coconut Research Institute of Sri Lanka and the International Centre for Agro Forestry Research (ICRAF), Kenya for the Development of Coconut Based Agro Forestry Systems

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2011-03-23

- on a proposal made by Hon. Jagath Pushpakumara, Minister of Coconut Development and Janatha Estate Development, Cabinet approval was granted to enter in to the above Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).   Facilities available under this MOU will be exchange of Scientific Staff and Technicians, Training Scientists and Coconut Small Holders, establishment of field demonstrations, exchange of scientific information on research and development, exchange of planting material of agro-forestry species, promotion of contacts between relevant public and private enterprises and universities and preparation of joint projects for funding for the coconut sector of Sri Lanka.
