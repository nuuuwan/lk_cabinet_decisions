Establishment of a Task Force under the Ministry of Education for the implementation of Proposals for New Educational Reforms

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-12-18

- It has been identified that the establishment of a 'Task Force pertaining to Education' is essential for providing technological and policy guidelines for the implementation of the proposals for educational reforms, in accordance with the Government policy, in a strategical and transparent manner. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education, to appoint a Task Force comprising of a group of experts with the subject specialists in the relevant field in the identification of proposals for educational reforms, programmes and  strategies, and to assist in the implementation of same, provide guidance, monitor and feedback for the relevant institutions, was approved by the Cabinet.
