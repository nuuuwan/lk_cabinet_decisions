Procurement of Trastuzumab Injection 440 mg with solvent in 20 ml vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-21

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the procurement of 1,875 vials of Trastuzumab Injection 440 mg with solvent in 20 ml vial, which is used as a medication for cancer to M/s Medmart Pharma (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.89.7 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
