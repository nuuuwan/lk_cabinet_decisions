Holding of the Third Meeting of the Asia-Europe Political Forum in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- It has been proposed to hold the 3rd Meeting of the Asia-Europe Political Forum in Colombo from 5th to 8th of April, 2019, under the theme 'The Challenges of Social Security and Employment for increasing aged population in the developing countries'. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by Hon. Thilak Marapana, the Minister of Foreign Affairs and Hon. Daya Gamage, the Minister of Primary Industries and Social Empowerment, for hosting the event and providing other facilities necessary related thereto, was approved by the Cabinet.
