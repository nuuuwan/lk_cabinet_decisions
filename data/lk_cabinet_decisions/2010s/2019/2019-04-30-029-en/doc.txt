Presenting of the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2018 in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-30

- The Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka has been prepared by the Monetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka in terms of the provisions of the Monetary Law Act incorporating status of the Sri lankan economy and Central Bank of Sri Lanka and information pertaining to the financial policies and measures adopted in the year, at issue. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Eran Wickremaratna, the Acting Minister of Finance to present this report in Parliament which is forwarded to the Minister in charge of the subject of Finance as per the provisions of the Monetary Law Act, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
