Renovating the Housing Complex of the Sri Lanka Police at Ketawalamulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-26

- There are two housing complexes situated at Ketawalamulla which provide accommodation facilities for the senior officers of the Sri Lanka Police and as one out of those is in a very dilapidated condition and renovation activities should have to be carried out expeditiously. As such, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to provide measures to carry out the essential repairs and renovation activities of the said building expeditiously, was approved by the Cabinet.
