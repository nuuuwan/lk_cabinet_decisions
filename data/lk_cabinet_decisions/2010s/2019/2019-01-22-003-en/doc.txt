Renovation of the three storey building where the Ahangama Police 	Station is housed

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-22

- The Ahangama Police Station, which extends its services to the people living in 35 Grama Niladhari Divisions, encounters difficulties while providingservices due to the dilapidated condition and non renovation for a long period of the three storey building in which the police station is housed. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to allocate Rs.33.4 million for the renovations and improvements required to be carried out in the said building, was approved by the Cabinet.
