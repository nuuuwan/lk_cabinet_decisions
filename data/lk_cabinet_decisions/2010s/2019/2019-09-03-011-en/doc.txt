The Governing Council and the Executive Committee Meeting - 2019 of the Centre for Integrated Rural Development in the Asia and Pacific Region

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-03

- The Executive Committee Meeting of the Centre for Integrated Rural Development in the Asia and Pacific Region is scheduled to be held on the 08th and 09th of October 2019 while the Governing Council Meeting is to be held on the 11th of October 2019 in Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the estimated cost  pertaining to the said Meetings presented by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, was noted by the Cabinet.
