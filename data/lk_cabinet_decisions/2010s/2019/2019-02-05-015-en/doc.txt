Procurement of Lubricating Oil for the Barge Mounted Power Plant of the Ceylon Electricity Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development to award the procurement for the supply of 750,000 liters of Cylinder Lubricating Oil and 175,000 liters of System Lubricating Oil required for the Barge Mounted Power Plant of the Ceylon Electricity Board for the year 2018/2019 to M/s Chevron Lubricants Lanka PLC, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
