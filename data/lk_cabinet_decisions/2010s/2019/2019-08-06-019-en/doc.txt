2019 Budget Proposal No.143: Acquiring user - Friendly buses with modern facilities for the Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-06

- The proposal made by the Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation to procure a possible number of diesel, low floor air conditioned, user friendly buses with modern facilities by utilizing the allocation of Rs.1500 million set apart from the Budget of 2019, was approved by the Cabinet.
