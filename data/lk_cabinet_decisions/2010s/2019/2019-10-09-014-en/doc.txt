Procurement for the Supply, Delivery, Installation, Commissioning and Maintenance of 14 Phacoemulsification Machines on Repeat order

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-09

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to place a repeat order with the original contractor M/s Delmage Forsyth & Co. Ltd., by enhancing the total cost, insurance and freight charges for the supply, delivery, installation, commissioning and maintenance of 14 Phacoemulsification Machines, used for conducting cataract surgeries, was approved by the Cabinet.
