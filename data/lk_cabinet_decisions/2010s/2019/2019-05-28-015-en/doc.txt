Contract for the piling works of the Maritime Facilitation Centre of the Sri Lanka Ports Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-28

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development, to award the contract for the piling works of the  Maritime Facilitation Centre of the Sri Lanka Ports Authority of the Port Access Elevated Highway Project funded by the Asian Development Bank, to M/S Access Engineering PLC, for a sum of Rs.624 million, as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
