Sale of Mineral Sands manufactured by the Lanka Mineral Sands Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-14

- The proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, the Minister of Industry & Commerce, Resettlement of Protracted Displaced Persons and Co‑operative Development, to award the tenders for the sale of mineral sand manufactured at the Government owned Lanka Mineral Sands Limited, 40,000 MT of Non‑Magnetic Heavy Mineral Concentrate at US$ 283.91 per MT, 13,000 MT of Low Grade Zircon Crude at US$ 25.11 per MT and 250 MT of Zircon at US$ 1,403 per MT to M/s Alchemy Heavy Metals (Pvt.) Ltd. and 800 MT of Rutile at US$ 1,113 per MT to M/s Sell & Parker Pty Ltd. and 2,000 MT of Hi‑Ti Ilmenite at US$ 136.86 per MT to M/s Project Prospects (Pvt.) Ltd., as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Tender Committee and the Technical Evaluation Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
