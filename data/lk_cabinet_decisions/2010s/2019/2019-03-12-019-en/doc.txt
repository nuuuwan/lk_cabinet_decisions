Procurement of Consultancy Services for the Light Rail Transit Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to award the contract for Design, Procurement Assistance, Construction, Supervision and Project Management of the proposed Light Rail Transit from Malabe to Fort, to the Joint Venture comprising of the Oriental Consultants Global Co., Ltd., Japan International Consultants for Transportation Co., Ltd., Chodai Co., Ltd, Nippon Koei Co., Ltd of Japan and Consulting Engineers & Architects Associated (Pvt.) Ltd. of Sri Lanka as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee (CPCC), was approved by the Cabinet.
