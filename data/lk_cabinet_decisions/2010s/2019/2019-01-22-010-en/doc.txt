Plant and Design Build Contract for the Civil, Machinery and Electricity 	(M&E) Work of the Valachchenai Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-22

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, to award the Plant and Design Build contract for the civil, machinery and electrical work of the Valachchenai Water Supply Project to be implemented covering the 06 Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Koralaipattu, Koralaipattu West, Koralaipattu Central, Koralaipattu South, Koralaipattu North and Eravurpattu to M/s China Geo Engineering Company Limited & M/s China National Aero Technology International Engineering Corporation ‑ JV, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee (CANC), was approved by the Cabinet.
