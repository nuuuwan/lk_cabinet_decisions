Construction of a new hostel block for the National Police Academy at Katana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- The proposal made by the Minister of Housing, Construction and Cultural Affairs, to award the contract of the project for the construction of a new hostel block for the National Police Academy at Katana to M/s Weerasooriya Builders (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.310 million, as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
