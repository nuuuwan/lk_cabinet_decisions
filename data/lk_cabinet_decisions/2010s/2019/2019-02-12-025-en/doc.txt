Holding of the Regional Conference on Sustainable Sanitation Solutions in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-12

- The Regional Conference on Sustainable Sanitation Solutions organized by the Regional Sanitation Centre for the South Asian Regional Collaboration Conference on Sanitation will be held on 21st to 23rd of February, 2019 in Colombo with the sponsorship of Sri Lanka. As such, the submissions made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, to the effect that it is scheduled to be held with the participation of the representatives of the countries of the South Asian Region, Experts of the sanitation Sector and representatives of the local and foreign non‑governmental organizations engaged in the Sector, was noted by the Cabinet.
