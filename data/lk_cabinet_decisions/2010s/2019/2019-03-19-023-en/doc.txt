The contract for the supply and delivery of 1,500 km of Aerial Bundled Conductors

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development, to award the contract for the supply and delivery of 1,500 km of Aerial Bundled Conductors to M/s Sierra Cables PLC., in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
