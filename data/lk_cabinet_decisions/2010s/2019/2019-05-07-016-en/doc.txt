Bilateral Air Services Agreements to be entered into with Guyana and Portugal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, the Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation to enter into the Air Services Agreements agreed upon and implement them productively for the promotion of Bilateral Corporation between Sri Lanka and  Guyana and Sri Lanka and Portugal, was approved by the Cabinet.
