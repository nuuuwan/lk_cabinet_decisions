Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Postgraduate Institute of Management of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the Faculty of Business Administration of the University of Kasetsart in Thailand

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-29

- The proposal made by the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, to sign a  Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Postgraduate Institute of Management of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the Faculty of Business Administration of the University of Kasetsart in Thailand, with the objective of developing the educational cooperation within  the Higher Education Sector,  was approved by the Cabinet.
