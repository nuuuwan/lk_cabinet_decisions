Presenting the Code of Regulations for the formalization of the involvements of the Sports Associations in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports to present the Gazette Notifications dated 2018‑09‑22, 2018‑10‑18 and 2019‑01‑10 including the regulations pertaining to the National Sports Associations, declared by the Minister of Sports in accordance with the provisions of the Sports Act, No.25 of 1973 for the formalization of the involvement of National Sports Associations by conducting elections in a fair and orderly manner, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
