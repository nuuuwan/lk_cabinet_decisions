Report of the Auditor General on the President's Fund - 2017

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-15

- The President's Fund for the financial year ended 31st December 2017 has been audited by the Auditor General in accordance with section 9(2) of the President's Fund Act No.07 of 1978 read with the provisions laid down in Article 154(3) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance to present the Auditor General's Report pertaining to same, in Parliament in accordance with section 10 of the President's Fund Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
