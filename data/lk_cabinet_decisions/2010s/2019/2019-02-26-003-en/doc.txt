Construction of the Polpithigama Police Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-26

- The approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously for the construction of the Polpithigama Police Station, but its construction activities have been delayed so far due to various practical issues. As such, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to carry out its construction activities formally, under the guidance of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, was approved by the Cabinet.
