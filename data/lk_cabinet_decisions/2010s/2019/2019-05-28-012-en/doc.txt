Procurement for the supply of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgical Consumables

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-28

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the procurement of Surgical Consumables used for the Oral and Maxillofacial surgeries, to M/s Osteonic Co. Ltd. of Korea for a sum of US$ 201,542.90 as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board (PAB), was approved by the Cabinet.
