Entering into Agreements for collaboration in research in regard to the patients of Chronic Kidney Disease / Chronic Kidney Disease of unknown etiology

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to enter into Data Sharing Agreements to initiate integrated research in regard to Chronic Kidney Disease / Chronic Kidney Disease of unknown etiology which has affected the people of the North Central Province, with the Australian Nuclear Science Technology Organization (ANSTO) and to conduct research on early diagnosis of Chronic Kidney Disease of unknown etiology with the University of Stanford of USA, was approved by the Cabinet.
