Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between the National Institute of Social Development and the University Colleges Leuven - Limburg (UCLL) Belgium

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-07

- The proposal made by the Minister of  Primary Industries and Social Empowerment to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the National Institute of Social Development and the University Colleges Leuven  Limburg (UCLL) Belgium with a view to obtaining professional expertise for the promotion of academic studies of the National Institute of Social Development, was approved by the Cabinet.
