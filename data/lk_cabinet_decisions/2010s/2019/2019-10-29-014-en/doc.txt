National Child Protection Policy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-29

- It has been decided by the Cabinet previously, that an amended National Child Protection Policy should be formulated in consultation with all relevant stakeholders and present same to the Cabinet. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Women & Child Affairs and Dry Zone Development for  the implementation of the National Child Protection Policy prepared by the National Child Protection Authority in consultation with all stakeholders in order to create a safe and violence free environment for the children in the future, was approved by the Cabinet.
