Procurement for the Supply of 3,250 vials of Activated Prothrombine Complex Concentrate Injection 500IU

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-15

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the procurement for the supply of 3,250 vials of Activated Prothrombine Complex Concentrate Injection 500IU used as the treatment to prevent bleeding in hemophilia patients to M/s Baxalta GmbH of Switzerland as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
