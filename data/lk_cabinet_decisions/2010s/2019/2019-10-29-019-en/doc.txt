The Global Adult Tobacco Survey - 2019/2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-29

- The proposal made by H.E. the President on the request of the Non-Cabinet Minister of Economic Reforms and Public Distribution, seeking approval for the Department of Census and Statistics to conduct a Global Adult Tobacco Survey - 2019/2020 with an estimated cost of US$ 217,000 being a grant provided by the World Health Organization in collaboration with the Centre for Disease Control at the  National Institute of Health in the United States of America, was approved by the Cabinet.
