Procurement of Drug Injections used in the treatment of Kidney Patients

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, for the procurement of 850,000 Epoetin Injections used in the treatment of Kidney patients, from M/s Reliance Life Science of India, for a sum of US$ 918,000, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
