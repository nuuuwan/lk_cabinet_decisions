Establishment of an Inter Ministerial Task Force for Industrial and Entrepreneur Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-12-18

- The proposal made by the Minister of Industries and Supply Chain Management to establish an Inter-Ministerial Task Force for Industrial and Entrepreneur Development under the Chairmanship of H.E. the President and the membership of the Minister of Industries and Supply Chain Management and the participation of other relevant Ministers, Secretaries to Ministries and Officers, with a view to minimizing the delays and problematic situations arising in the process of making policy decisions in the manufacturing industry sector which provides a major contribution to the economic development, was approved by the Cabinet.
