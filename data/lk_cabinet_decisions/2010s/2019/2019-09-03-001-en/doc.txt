Management of Public Expenditure

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-03

- Taking into consideration the matters embodied in the  Circular on the “Management of Public Expenditure” issued in the year 2010 by the Secretary to the President, specifying the limits applicable to the personal staff of the Hon. Ministers, Hon. Non-Cabinet Ministers, Hon. State Ministers and Hon. Deputy Ministers and the other facilities, the draft Circular on same prepared inclusive of the appropriate amendments and fresh matters so as to meet the present trends and presented by H.E. the President, was approved by the Cabinet.
