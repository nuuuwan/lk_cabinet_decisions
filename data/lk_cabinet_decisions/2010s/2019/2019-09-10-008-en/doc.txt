Construction of the Out Patients Department (OPD) building of the District General Hospital in Kalutara

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- It has been identified that the accommodation facilities available in the existing Out Patient Department building of the District General Hospital in Kalutara are inadequate, due to the increase in the demand for Out Patients treatment services during the past years.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to construct the Out Patients Department (OPD) building of the said hospital, was approved by the Cabinet.
