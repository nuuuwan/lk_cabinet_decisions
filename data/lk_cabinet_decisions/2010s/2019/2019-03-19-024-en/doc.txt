Rehabilitation and improvement of the Jaffna-Ponnalai-Point Pedro Road (AB 21) from Jaffna to Ponnalai

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to award the civil work contract for the rehabilitation and improvement of the Road Section from 1.83 km up to 14.6 km along the Jaffna-Ponnalai-Point Pedro Road (AB 21) from Jaffna to Ponnalai, which has been designed as a sub project under the Strategic Cities Development Project, to M/s Sierra Construction (Pvt.) Ltd., in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
