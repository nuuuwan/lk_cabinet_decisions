Liquidation of Natural Resources Management Services (Pvt.) Ltd., Mahaweli Venture Capital Company (Pvt.) Ltd., and Mahaweli Engineering Services (Pvt.) Ltd., companies incorporated under Section 13(18) of the Mahaweli Authority of Sri Lanka Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-17

- The proposal made by the Minister of Mahaweli Development and Environment, to liquidate the Natural Resources Management Services (Pvt.) Ltd., Mahaweli Venture Capital Company (Pvt.) Ltd., and Mahaweli Engineering Services (Pvt.) Ltd., companies incorporated under section 13(18) of Mahaweli Authority of Sri Lanka Act, which can not be maintained as of now, was approved by the Cabinet.
