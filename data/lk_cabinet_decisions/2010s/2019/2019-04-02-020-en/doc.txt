Holding of the Enterprise Sri Lanka' Exhibition in Anuradhapura and Jaffna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-02

- The Enterprise Sri Lanka Programme is implemented with the objective of creating 100,000 new entrepreneurs Island wide and thereby enhancing the national production and export promotion. The first exhibition of this programme was held successfully in the Monaragala area and its second exhibition is expected to be held in Anuradhapura and the third in Jaffna, under 'Government 2025 Vision', respectively in the months of May and July of this year. Accordingly, 	the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance to hold these exhibitions with the private sector sponsorship and financial assistance, was approved by the Cabinet.
