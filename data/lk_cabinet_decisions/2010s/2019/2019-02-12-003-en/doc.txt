Settlement of Payments in respect of Rehabilitation and Reconstruction activities of the areas affected by floods and landslides

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, to provide the District Secretaries of the relevant Districts with the necessary funds to enable them to make payment in respect of Rehabilitation and Reconstruction activities of the areas affected by floods and landslides where payments have not been settled up to date, was approved by the Cabinet.
