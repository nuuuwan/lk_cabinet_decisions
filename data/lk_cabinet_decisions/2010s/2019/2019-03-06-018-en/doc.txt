The National Policy on Civil Aviation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-06

- The Cabinet has granted approval previously to review the draft Bill submitted for the amendment of the National Policy on Aviation and to re-submit the same. Accordingly, action has been taken to further amend the draft Bill of the policy after discussions had with the relevant parties. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon.Arjuna Ranatunga, Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation, to implement the final amended draft within Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
