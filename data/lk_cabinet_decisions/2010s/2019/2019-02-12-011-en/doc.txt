Geological Investigations for the Preliminary Detailed Engineering Designs in the Kelani Flood Protection Bund

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-12

- The proposal made by Hon. P. Harison, the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Livestock Development, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, to carry out expeditiously, the geological investigations needed for the proposed Kelani Flood Protection Bund under the Climate Resilience Improvement Project (CRIP) for the mitigation of floods around the Kelani River Basin by the National Building Research Organization (NBRO), was approved by the Cabinet.
