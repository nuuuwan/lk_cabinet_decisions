Procurement of Lubricating oil for the Sapugaskanda Power Plant

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development to award the procurement for purchasing 800,000 litres of lubricating oil Taro 40 XL 40 for Pielstick  plant of Sapugaskanda and 300,000 litres of lubricating oil Taro 40 XL 40 for the MAN plant of Sapugaskanda, to M/s Chevron Lubricants Lanka PLC as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
