Lease of a Land at Buthgamuwa Road in Rajagiriya to M/s Baili Investment Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-01

- The proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to lease a plot of land in extent of 2 Acres and 3 Roods facing Rajagiriya, Buthgamuwa Road and Parliament Road for a 99 year period, to M/s Baili Investment Lanka (Pvt) Ltd., for a Mixed Development Project according to the recommendations made by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
