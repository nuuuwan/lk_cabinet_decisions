Procurement of Consultant Services for the Development of Infrastructure Facilities under the Health System Enhancement Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- It has been planned to develop the infrastructure facilities in the Health Services Centres of the North Central, Central, Sabaragamuwa and Uva Provinces and the District Hospitals under the  Health System Enhancement Project. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for Design and Supervision Consultancy of the Infrastructure development to M/s Resources Development Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd. of Sri Lanka, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
