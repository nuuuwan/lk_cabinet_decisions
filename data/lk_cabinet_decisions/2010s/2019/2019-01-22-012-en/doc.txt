Procurement for the Supply of 12,000 MT of Maize required for Thriposha Production

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-22

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the purchase of 12,000 MT of Maize, which is one of the main ingredients used for manufacturing Thriposha for Sri Lanka Thriposha Limited, to M/s KST Evergreen (Pvt)Ltd., for a sum of Rs 670.8 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
