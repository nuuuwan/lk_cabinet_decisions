Improvement of the dialysis treatment programme for patients with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) and Chronic Kidney Disease of uncertain etiology (CKDu)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-01

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to appoint a Technical Evaluation Committee and a Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee to evaluate and to make recommendations on the project proposal made by the Hospinorm Project GmbH, Frankfurter Str, 92, Eschborn, to provide 643 dialysis machines throughout the country inclusive of the supply of 33 new dialysis units and expansion of 50 dialysis units with  improved facilities and also to secure the provisions required for the said project, was approved by the Cabinet.
