Awarding of a 03 year contract for the supply of catering from London Heathrow for SriLankan Airlines flights

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-06-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance, to award the contract for the supply of catering for a period of 03 years, for the SriLankan Airlines flights departing from the London Heathrow Airport to Newrest Catering Services, for a sum of £4.5 million, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
