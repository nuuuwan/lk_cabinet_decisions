Submission of Recommendations for the Waste Management

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-09

- Mismanagement of garbage has badly affected the General Public and has become a cause for the destruction of nature. Further, objections have also been leveled against the projects designed to be implemented by the Government on proper disposal of garbage. As such, the Cabinet has decided to appoint a committee comprising of the Ministers of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government; Megapolis & Western Development and the Non-Cabinet Minister of Economic Reforms and Public Distribution to submit recommendations on the measures to be taken on the proper management of garbage, which has become a national issue, was approved by the Cabinet.
