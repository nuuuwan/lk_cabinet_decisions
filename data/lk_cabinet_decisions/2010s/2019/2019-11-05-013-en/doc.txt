Procurement for the supply of 55,000 vials of Concentrated Monoclonal Purified and Detergent Treated Dried Factor VIII Fraction 200 IU - 350 IU

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the procurement of 55,000 vials of Concentrated Monoclonal Purified and Detergent Treated Dried Factor VIII Fraction           200 IU 350 IU used for the treatment of Haemophilia patients, for a sum of US$ 3.35 million from M/s Baxalta GmbH of Switzerland, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
