Entering into a Collaborative Agreement to provide facilities to secure foreign employment opportunities in Japan for the skilled workers of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-06-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports, to enter into a Collaborative Agreement between the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare and the National Policy Agency of Japan and the Ministry of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports of Sri Lanka regarding the Specified Skilled Workers to provide facilities to secure foreign employment opportunities in Japan for the youth of Sri Lanka who have attained certain specific skills, was approved by the Cabinet.
