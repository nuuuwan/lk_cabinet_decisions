Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority and the LEPL Georgian National Tourism Administration in the field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-20

- 	The proposal made by the Minister of Tourism Development, Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority and the LEPL Georgian National Tourism Administration in the field of Tourism which will be valid for a period of three (03) years and will be automatically renewed for a further period of three (03) years and also to authorize the Chairman of Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority to sign the said Memorandum of Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
