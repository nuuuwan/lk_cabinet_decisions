Payment of compensation for the damages caused as a result of the conflict situation which occurred recently in several places of the Island

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, for the payment of compensation in regard to the rehabilitation of the damages caused to the Mosques, as a result of the conflict situation which occurred recently in several places of the Island, adopting the same methodology for making payments for the damages caused by the bomb explosions that occurred at the Easter Sunday, was approved by the Cabinet.
