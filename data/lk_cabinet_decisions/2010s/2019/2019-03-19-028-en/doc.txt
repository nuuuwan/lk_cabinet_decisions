Procurement of Drug Injections used in the treatment of Heart Patients

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, for the procurement of 690,000 Enoxaparin Sodium Injections of different types used in the treatment of Heart patients, from M/s Shenzhen Techdow Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd. of China, for a sum of US$ 1.82 million, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
