Purchasing of two spare engines required for the maintenance of the Aircraft fleet of SriLankan Airlines Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-19

- The proposal made by the Minister of Finance to purchase 02 Leap Spare Engines for the maintenance of the Aircraft fleet of SriLankan Airlines Limited from the CFM International Institute, in terms of the Agreement signed between the SriLankan Airlines Limited and the CFM Institute, was approved by the Cabinet.
