Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme - Construction of Primary Substations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-09

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development, to award the contract for the construction of 33/11kV primary substation coming under Tranche 2 of Package 05 of the Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme to M/s Siemens Ltd. in Consortium with Dimo (Pvt.) Ltd., at No.36, Kynsey Road, Colombo 08, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
