Amendments to the Penal Code and the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No.15 of 1979

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- The approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously, to draft legislation on hate speech, as an amendment to the Penal Code and  the Code of Criminal Procedure. The draft Bill prepared accordingly, has been presented to the Sectoral Oversight Committee in Parliament and the proposal made by the Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms to publish the new draft Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman in accordance with the recommendations of the said Committee, in the Gazette and subsequently, present it in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
