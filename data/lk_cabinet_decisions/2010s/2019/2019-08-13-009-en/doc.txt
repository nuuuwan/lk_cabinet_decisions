An Act to Incorporate the Sri Lanka Psychological Association

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-13

- Hon. Thilanga Sumathipala, member of Parliament has presented a Private Member's Bill in Parliament to Incorporate the Sri Lanka Psychological Association. Accordingly, the proposal made the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to present the proposed draft Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman in accordance with the recommendations of the Cabinet Sub Committee on Legislation, and to publish it in the Government Gazette in tri languages, was approved by the Cabinet.
