Recruitment and Training of Graduate Trainees

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- In accordance with the approval granted by the Cabinet previously, the intimation made by the Hon. Prime Minister and Minister of National Policies, Economic Affairs, Resettlement & Rehabilitation, Northern Province Development and Youth Affairs, to the effect, that graduates from the year 2012 will be recruited for training on chronological order for the remaining 4,667 posts of Graduate Trainees, in September, 2019, giving priority to the external and internal degree holders who are on the threshold of the age limit of 45 years, was noted by the Cabinet.
