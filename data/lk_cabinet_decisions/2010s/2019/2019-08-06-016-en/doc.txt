Providing of 25 Local Authorities in Sri Lanka with urban infrastructure facilities under the Secondary Town Sustainable Urban Development Project funded by Asian Development Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-06

-The proposal made by the Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government to award the consultancy services for detailed design and procurement services of urban infrastructure of 25 local authorities,  to the joint venture of the Resources Development Consultants (Pvt.) Limited (Lead Consultants) and IPE Global Limited (IPEG), India (JV partner) at No.55-2/1, Galle Road, Colombo 03, with a view to developing the main towns of the Island with development initiatives as model towns, was approved by the Cabinet.
