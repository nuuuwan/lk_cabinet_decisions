Revamping of the entire building complex of the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- The proposal made by the Minister of Education to obtain a status report, after having examined the entire building complex of the Department of Examinations of Sri Lanka and to award the contracts using the National Competitive Bidding System in regard to the constructions relevant to the identified priority renovations and the relevant consultancy services to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, was approved by the Cabinet.
