The Development (Widening) of Kirimandala Mawatha

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-07

- The Kirimandala Mawatha is one of the four roads identified to be developed within the Colombo Municipal limits under the new township development project. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development, to award the contract of same to M/s AMSK - GC Joint Venture for a sum of Rs.358.9 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
