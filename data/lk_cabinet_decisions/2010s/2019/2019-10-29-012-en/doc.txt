Transfer of the land and buildings where the Kaltota Police Station is located, to the Sri Lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-29

- The proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, to transfer a plot of land situated in the Grama Niladhari Division of Kaltota in the Balangoda Divisional Secretariat Division and the buildings therein, used by the Sri Lanka Police for over a period of 30 years to the Sri Lanka Police, was approved by the Cabinet.
