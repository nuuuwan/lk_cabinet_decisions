Widening and  Improving the (B275) first two kilometers of the Matara - Hakmana Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-06

- The proposal made by the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development to award  the contract for Widening and  Improving the (B275) first two kilometers of the Matara - Hakmana Road, to M/s Maga Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd. as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
