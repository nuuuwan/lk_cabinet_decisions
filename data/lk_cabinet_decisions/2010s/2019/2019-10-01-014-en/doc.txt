Procurement of Dry Ration Packets to the Sri Lanka Army - Supply Year 2019

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-01

- The proposal made by the Minister of Defence, to award the contract for the procurement of 200,000 Dry Ration Packets for the Sri Lanka Army for the year 2019, for a sum of Rs.175,704,347.83 (without VAT), to M/s Rodesha Enterprise (Pvt.) Ltd., the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
