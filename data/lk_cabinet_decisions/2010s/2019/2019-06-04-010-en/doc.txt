Procurement of an IT system for the foreign reserves management in the Central Bank of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-06-04

- The license of the existing Treasury Management System used for the management of foreign reserves by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka is due to expire by the year 2021. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance, to commence the process of procuring to purchase a new foreign reserve management system, to replace the existing IT system, was approved by the Cabinet.
