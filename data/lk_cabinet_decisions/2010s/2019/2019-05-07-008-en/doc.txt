Memorandum of Understanding  to be entered into between Sri Lanka and Bahrain  on Visa Exemption for Holders of Diplomatic, Special and Official  Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Wajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government, to enter into Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Bahrain on Visa Exemption for Holders of Diplomatic, Special and Official Passports, was approved by the Cabinet.
