Obtaining continued monetary donations towards the 'Protection of Children - National Trust Fund

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by H.E. the President to launch a Lottery Ticket on 2019 11 11 operated by the National Lotteries Board of which the draw would be held once a week and credit 10% of the proceeds of the sale of the tickets to the fund in order to strengthen the  'Protection of Children - National Trust Fund', was approved by the Cabinet.
