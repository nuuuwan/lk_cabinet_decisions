The 72nd National Day Celebrations falling on 04th February, 2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-12-18

- The proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils & Local Government to appoint a Ministerial Committee under the Chairmanship of Hon. Prime Minister, to draw up the relevant programme for the 72nd National Day Celebrations falling on the 04th of February, 2020 and to organize the necessary arrangements, was approved by the Cabinet. The Cabinet also decided to hold the 72nd National Day Celebrations at the Independence Square in Colombo 07.
