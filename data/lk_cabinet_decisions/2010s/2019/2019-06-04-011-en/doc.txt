Purchase of Full Cream Milk Power to Manufacture Thriposha

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-06-04

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the procurement for the purchase of 600 MT of full cream milk power to Sri Lanka Thriposha Limited for producing Thriposha which is a nutritious food supplement provided to the pregnant mothers with nutrition problems and lactating mothers and also to the children with malnutrition, to Milco (Private) Limited,  as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
