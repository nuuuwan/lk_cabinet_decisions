Bilateral Air Service Agreement between Sri Lanka and Hungary

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-27

- The officials of the two countries have reached an Agreement on the draft Agreement prepared by the aeronautical authorities of the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Hungary on the Air Service Agreement after discussions held by them during the International Civil Aviation Negotiation Conference held in Bali, Indonesia in the year 2014. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation to sign the initialed Air Service Agreement between the Governments of Sri Lanka and Hungary by an accredited representative of Sri Lanka and thereafter, to take appropriate measures for the effective implementation of the Agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
