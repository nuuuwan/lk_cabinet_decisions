Entering into an Agreement between Sri Lanka and Denmark at Government level in respect of taxation on revenue

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-06-04

- With the expectation of improving the economic relations between the two countries, Sri Lanka and Denmark has entered into an agreement at official level for avoidance of double taxation and prevention of fiscal evasion pertaining to economic activities. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon.Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance to sign the agreement so entered into at Government level and present it in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
