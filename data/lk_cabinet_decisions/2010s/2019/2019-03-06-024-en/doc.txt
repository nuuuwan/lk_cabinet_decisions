Procurement of Services of Firm/s with International Experience for Forensic activities of the  Central Bank of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-06

- The Cabinet of Ministers has granted approval previously to procure the services of firms with a global practice and international experience for the forensic audit activities of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance to award the relevant contract to M/s BDO India LLP and M/s KPMG Sri Lanka selected on the results of the evaluation carried out in terms of the above approval of the technical proposal and the financial proposal and in accordance with the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
