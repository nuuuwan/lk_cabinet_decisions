National Cooperative Policy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to seek the public opinion with regard to proposed National Cooperative Policy. As such, the proposal made by Acting Minister of Industry & Commerce, Resettlement of Protracted Displaced Persons, Co operative Development and Vocational Training & Skills Development to implement the amended National Cooperative Policy prepared incorporating the views and suggestions submitted in this respect, was approved by the Cabinet.
