Report of the Sectoral Oversight Committee on Education and Human Resources Development on the Observations regarding the Batticaloa Campus (Pvt.) Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-02

- The observation report of the Chairman of the Sectoral Oversight Committee on Education and Human Resources Development, on the Batticaloa Campus (Pvt.) Limited, which has been presented to the Parliament was submitted to focus the attention of the Cabinet and the Cabinet decided to appoint a Ministerial Sub-Committee comprising of the Ministers of Finance, City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, National Integration, Official Languages, Social Progress and Hindu Religious Affairs and Development Strategies and International Trade, to study the recommendations made by the said Sectoral Oversight Committee and report to the Cabinet.
