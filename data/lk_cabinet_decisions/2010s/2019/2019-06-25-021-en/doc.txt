Supply of Aviation fuel for SriLankan Airlines at Dammam Airport of 	Saudi Arabia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-06-25

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance, to award the contract for the supply of Aviation fuel for SriLankan Airlines at the Dammam Airport of Saudi Arabia during the period from 2019 July 01st to December 31st, to M/s Modern Consortium for Refueling Aircraft Co. Ltd (MCRA), for a sum of US $ 2.0 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
