Implementing the recommendations of the Committee appointed to provide relief to media persons who were subject to various harassments and persecutions during the period from 2005 to 2015

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by H.E. the President, at the request of the Non Cabinet Minister of Mass Media for payment of compensation to 78 media persons, paying heed to the recommendations made by a Committee appointed to provide relief to media persons who were subject to various harassments and  persecutions during the period from 2005 to 2015, was approved by the Cabinet.
