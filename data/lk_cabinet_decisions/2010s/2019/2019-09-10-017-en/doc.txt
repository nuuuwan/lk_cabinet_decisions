Awarding of the Contract for the A330-200 Aircraft Landing Gear Overhaul programme of SriLankan Airlines for the period from 1st August 2019 to 31st July 2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- The proposal made by the Minister of Finance, to award the contract for the   Aircraft Landing Gear Overhaul programme of six A330-200  aircrafts of the SriLankan Airlines, for the period from 1st August 2019 to 31st July 2020, to M/s Safran Landing Systems Services Singapore, who is the lowest responsive bidder at the open competitive bidding procedure followed for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
