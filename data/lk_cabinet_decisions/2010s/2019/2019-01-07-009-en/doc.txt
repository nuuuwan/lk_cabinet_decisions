Construction of the new access road from Kottawa to Mahenwatta from High Level Road near Walawwatta to Diyagama Road Near Katuwana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development, to award the contract for the construction of the new access road from Kottawa to Mahenwatta from High-Level road near Walawwatta to Diyagama road near Katuwana to M/s Hoveal Construction (Pvt) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.713 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
