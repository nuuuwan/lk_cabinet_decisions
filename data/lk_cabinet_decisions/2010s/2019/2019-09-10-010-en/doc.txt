Implementation of Development Projects utilizing the Treasury funds allocated to the Ministry of Megapolis and Western Development for the year 2019

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- The proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development for the implementation of the five (05) projects in which the total estimated cost exceeds Rs.20 million, namely, 'Seruwavila Development - Stage II', 'Jaffna Town Development - Bustand - Stage II', 'Improvement of Welikanda Town' - 'Eluwankulama - Aruwakadu Road Improvement' and 'Aththidiya Childrens' Orphanage - Stage II', utilizing the Treasury funds allocated for the year 2019, was approved by the Cabinet.
