Awarding of the contract for the water supply extension to Dambulla and Kandalama through the Greater Dambulla Water Scheme in the Central Province

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Wajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government to award the contract for the water supply extension to Dambulla and Kandalama through the Greater Dambulla Water Scheme in the Central Province implemented as a sub - project of the Local Government Enhancement Sector Project, to M/s Squire Mech Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.565.39 million as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
