Establishment of a Tamil Medium National School in the Kelaniya	Education Zone

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Mano Ganesan, the Minister of National Integration, Official Languages, Social Progress and Hindu Religious Affairs, to establish a Tamil Medium Hindu National School in the name of Arun Maanikkawaasagam Hindu College in the Kelaniya Education Zone utilizing the property to be vested with the Ministry of Education by way of a Deed of Gift by the Arun Prasanth Foundation with a view to uplifting the educational standard of the Tamil medium students in the Wattala area in Gampaha, was approved by the Cabinet.
