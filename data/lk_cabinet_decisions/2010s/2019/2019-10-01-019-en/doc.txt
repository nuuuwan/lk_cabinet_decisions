Vote on Account - 2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-01

- As the next Presidential election is scheduled to be held on the 16th of November 2019, in accordance with the decision of the Cabinet at its meeting held on 2019‑08‑13, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance to present a Vote on Account prepared incorporating the financial provisions required to maintain the activities of the Government during the period of the first four months from 01st of January to 30th of April 2020 in Parliament, until the Appropriation Act for the year 2020 is passed in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
