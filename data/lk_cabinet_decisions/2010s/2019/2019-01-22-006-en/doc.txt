Obtaining approval of Parliament for the Gazette Notifications published 	under the Revenue Protection Act, No 19 of 1962

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-22

- The Gazette 	Notifications issued under the Revenue Protection Act, No 19 	of 1962, revising the import duty should be presented in Parliament for 	approval. As such, the proposal made by Hon.	Mangala Samaraweera, the 	Minister of Finance & Mass Media to present the Gazette Notifications No. 	2079/32, 2092/8 and 2097/36 issued for introducing new H.S code 	and duty 	enabling to receive concessions to the local manufactures in 	levying import 	duty and revising the import duty on petrol for the adjustment of the fuel price 	in the 	country relatively to the decrease in fuel price in the international 	market, in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
