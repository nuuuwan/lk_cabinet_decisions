Calling for proposals for the up-liftment of the Tourist Industry

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-30

- It was decided by the Cabinet to establish a Committee under the Chairmanship of the Minister of Tourism Development, Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs comprising of a further five relevant Ministers, to make a study of the measures to be taken to overcome the situation faced by the tourist industry due to the tragic incident that took place on the last Easter Sunday.
