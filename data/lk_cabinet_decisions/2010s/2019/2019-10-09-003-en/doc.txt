Memoranda of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Colombo and Foreign Universities / Higher Education Institutes on Cooperation in the Field of Higher Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-09

- The proposal made by the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education for the University of Colombo to enter into Bilateral Memoranda of Understanding with the University of Zurich, Switzerland, the Hubei University, P.R. China and the Tsinghua University, P.R. China with the objective of developing and expanding the educational cooperation within the Higher Education Sector of those countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
