The Project proposal to manage Colombo East Teaching Hospital (CETH) as a Public-Private Mixed Care Model on Free and Paid basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to implement the  Colombo East Teaching Hospital (CETH) as a Public-Private Mixed Care Model on Free and Paid basis and as a Semi-Government entity and to continue its activities pertaining to medical education, was approved by the Cabinet.
