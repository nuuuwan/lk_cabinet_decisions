Procurement for Consultancy Services for Design, Tender Assistance, Supervision and Training to improve facilities and equipment for the diagnosis and treatment mainly of Cardiovascular Diseases in Tertiary Care Hospitals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the consultancy services contract of the Health & Medical Service Improvement Project implemented under the loan facility of the Japan International Corporation Agency to the Joint Venture of Koei Research & Consultancy (Koei Research & Consultancy Incorporation, Binko International Ltd, Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau and Philkoei International Incorporation), was approved by the Cabinet.
