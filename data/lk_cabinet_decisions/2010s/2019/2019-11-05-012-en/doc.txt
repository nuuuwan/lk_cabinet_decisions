Procurement for the supply of 300,000 Sterile Hollow Fibre Dialyzers Polysulphone / Polynephrone used for the patients affected with kidney ailments

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to procure 300,000 Sterile Hollow Fibre Dialyzers Polysulphone / Polynephrone used as the dialysis membrane for blood purification of patients affected with kidney ailments, for a sum of US$ 1.35 million from M/s Nipro Asia Pte. Ltd. of Singapore, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
