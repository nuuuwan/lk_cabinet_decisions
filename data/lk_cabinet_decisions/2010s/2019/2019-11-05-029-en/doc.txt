Awarding the Contract for supplying of Aviation Fuel for Sri Lankan Airlines

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The Sri Lankan Airlines is presently operating its services to 37 destinations of 19 countries.  In order to maintain its services, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance to award the contract for the supply of aviation fuel for SriLankan Airlines at 12 airport locations worldwide, was approved by the Cabinet.
