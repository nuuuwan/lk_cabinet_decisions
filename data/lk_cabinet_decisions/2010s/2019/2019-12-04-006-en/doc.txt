National Policy Framework of the Government - Vistas of Prosperity and Splendour for a Reconstructed Country

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-12-04

- The proposal made by the Minister of Finance, Economy and Policy Development for the Policy Statement 'Vistas of Prosperity and Splendour for a  Reconstructed Country' presented by H.E. the President with ten fold policies aimed at achieving the four fold outcomes namely, a productive citizenry, a contented family, a disciplined and just society and a prosperous nation, to be accepted as the National Policy Framework of the Government and to direct all Ministries, Departments, Government Institutions, Provincial Councils and Local Authorities etc, to perform their duties and functions accordingly, was approved by the Cabinet.
