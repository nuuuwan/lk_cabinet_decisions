Memorandum of Understanding to be entered into between Sri Lanka and 	Japan on Defence Co operation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-07

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as  the Minister of Defence, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Japan on developing Defence Cooperation and exchanges in the field of Defence, was approved by the Cabinet.
