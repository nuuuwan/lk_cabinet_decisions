Development of infrastructure facilities at the Advanced Technological Institutes of Mannar and Nawalapitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-30

- In the Advanced Technological Institute in Nawalapitiya, 119 students are engaged in following 03 courses while in the Advanced Technological Institute in Mannar 601 students are engaged in fallowing 04 courses. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education to secure provision amounting to Rs.1208 million for the implementation of the projects proposed for the development of a more qualitative educational environment and to provide adequate infrastructure facilities for the said students, was approved by the Cabinet.
