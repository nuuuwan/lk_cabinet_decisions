Bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and Tanzania

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-19

- The approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously, to sign the Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and the United Republic of Tanzania. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation, to amend and implement the Agreement based on the matters agreed upon at the consultation between the Aeronautical Authorities of both the countries held in Kenya in December 2018, was approved by the Cabinet.
