Renovations of the Kandy-Getambe Court Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-26

- The proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms, to renovate the Kandy-Getambe Court Complex consisting of 11 courts, by the State Engineering Corporation at an investment of Rs.303.5 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
