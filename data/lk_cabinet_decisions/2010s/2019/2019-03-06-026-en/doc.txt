Entry into a Long Term Contract for the procurement of Crude Oil

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-06

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development to award the contract for the procurement of Murban Crude oil for a period of 8 months during the year 2019, to M/s Swiss Singapore Overseas Enterprise Pte. Ltd., in accordance with recommendation of the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SSCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
