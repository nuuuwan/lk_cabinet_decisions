Awarding of the contract for a building to be constructed at the South Eastern University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-09

- The proposal made by the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education to award the contract for the construction of a building complex of staff quarters for the Academic and the Non‑academic Staff of the South Eastern University of Sri Lanka, in accordance with the procurement procedure  carried out, to M/s Nazeeha Hardware & Construction Ltd., for a sum of Rs.272.1 million, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
