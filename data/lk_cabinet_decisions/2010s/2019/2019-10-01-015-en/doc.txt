Awarding of the contract for the supply of catering from Narita International Airport Japan, for SriLankan Airline flights from 15th November 2019 for a 02 year contract period

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-01

- The proposal made by the Minister of Finance to award the contract for the supply of Catering Uplift at Narita International Airport, Japan, for a period of 02 years commencing from 15th of November, 2019, at a total contract value of Japanese Yen 307,695,073 for covering 520 SriLankan Airline flights, to M/s Gate Gourmet Japan Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
