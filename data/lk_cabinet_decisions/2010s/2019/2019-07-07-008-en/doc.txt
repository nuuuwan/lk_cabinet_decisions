Allocation of financial provision for the construction of the Gandara Fishery Harbour

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-07

- In relevance to the construction of the Gandara and Gurunagar Fishery Habours, although the decision taken by the Cabinet at a meeting held previously, was for the project proposer company Bouygues Trvaux Publics to submit a Voluntary Feasibility Assessment and a Domestic Engineer Estimate, the said company failed to submit a comprehensive project proposal before the due date and accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Finance and the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock Development, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, to terminate the procurement procedure implemented in respect of the company and also to construct the Gandara Fishery Harbour by utilizing local funds, was approved by the Cabinet.
