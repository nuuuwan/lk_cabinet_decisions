Designing and Building of the St. Sebastian South Pumping Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development, to award the contract for Designing and Building of the St. Sebastian South Pumping Station proposed to be implemented under the Metro Colombo Urban Development Project (MCUDP), to M/s China Geo Engineering Corporation, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
