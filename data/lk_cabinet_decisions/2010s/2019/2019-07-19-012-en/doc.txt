Project for the Livelihood Enhancement of the Small Scale Farmers in the SAARC Region countries

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-19

It has been proposed to implement the Small Scale Agro Business focusing on the value chain development of the Livelihood Enhancement of the Small Scale Farmers of the SAARC Region countries, with a view to enhancing the  livelihood level of the small scale farmers. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock Development, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, to implement the project on the contribution of the SAARC Development Fund and the Local Funds, was approved by the Cabinet.
