Procurement of 37 New Luxury buses to the Sri Lanka Transport Board(SLTB) on lease basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-26

- With a view to expanding the public transportation facilities and providing a more qualitative service to the general public, the proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the procurement of 37 new luxury buses to the Sri Lanka Transport Board on lease basis, to M/s Softlogic Automobile (Pvt) Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
