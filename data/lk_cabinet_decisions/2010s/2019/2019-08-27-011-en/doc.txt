The 42nd Asia Pacific Dental Congress and Exhibition organized by the Sri Lanka Dental Association

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-27

- The 42nd Asia Pacific Dental Congress organized by the Sri Lanka Dental Association is due to be held at the Bandaranayake Memorial International Conference Hall, from 9th to 13th of June, 2020. Dental Professionals and experts including American, Asian, European and most of the countries of the Pacific Region are expected to participate in this Congress and an international dental trade exhibition with the participation of the worldwide premier manufacturers and suppliers of dental equipment and healthcare material will also be held to coincide with the event.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to declare the said conference and the exhibition as a nationally recognized event and to secure provisions for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
