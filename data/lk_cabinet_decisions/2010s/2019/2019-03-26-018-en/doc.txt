Rehabilitation and improvement of the Priority Roads

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development to rehabilitate and improve the additional roads to be selected on priority basis utilizing a balance of US$16.8 million under the Priority Road Project implemented as per the Memorandum of Understanding made between the Government of Sri Lanka and the China Development Bank to provide financial facilities to the infrastructure development project, was approved by the Cabinet.
