Allocation of a Land at the Ratnapura New Town to the Ratnapura Tamil School

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-03

- The proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to set apart a land of about 3 acres for the Ratnapura Tamil School Development Project from the Ratnapura New Town precincts, to the Ministry of Education, Information Technology and Cultural Affairs of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council, subject to the recommendation of the Secretaries Committee pertaining to the allocation of state lands for development purposes, was approved by the Cabinet.
