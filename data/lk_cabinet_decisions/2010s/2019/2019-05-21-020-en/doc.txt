Postponement of the 18th Meeting of the Conference of the Parties of Convention on International Trade on the Endangered Species of Fauna and Flora (CITES) which was scheduled to be held in Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-21

- The postponement of the 18th Meeting of the Conference of the Parties of Convention on International Trade on the Endangered Species of Fauna and Flora (CITES) which was scheduled to be held in the months of May and June of 2019 at the Bandaranaika Memorial International Conference Hall, to enable it to be held on another future date, was brought to the notice of the Cabinet by Hon. John Amarathunga, the Minister of Tourism Development, Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs.
