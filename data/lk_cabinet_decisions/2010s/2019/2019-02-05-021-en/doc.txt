Procurement of Oil and Lubricant required for the buses of the Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation, to award the contracts for the procurement of Oil and Lubricant required for the buses of the Sri Lanka Transport Board to M/s Lanka IOC PLC, M/s Cheveron Lubricant Lanka PLC and M/s McLarens Lubricants Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
