Obtaining a Loan of United States Dollars (USD) 25 million from the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) of the World Bank for the Implementation of the Public Sector Efficiency Strengthening Project (PSEP)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-15

- With a view to improving the transparency and the efficiency of core‑Government and the public financial management functions, through the introduction of the e‑Government Procurement system, Public Financial Management system and the Excise Revenue Management system as priority information systems, the Sri Lanka Government, the World Bank and the European Union have planned to implement a collectively designed Public Sector Efficiency Strengthening Project. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance, to authorize the Ministry of Finance to enter into a Financing Agreement with the International Bank of Reconstruction and Development of the World Bank in consultation with the Monetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, as well, on the monetary implications of the loan facility, to borrow a loan amounting to US$ 25 million, which has several favourable financing terms, was approved by the Cabinet.
