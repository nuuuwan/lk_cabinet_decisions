Entering into Bilateral Air Services Agreements

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-29

- With a view to enhancing the international cooperation in relation to the Air Services, the proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, the Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation, to sign the initialed Air Services Agreements with Afghanistan, the State of Qatar, the State of Israel, and Zimbabwe by an accredited representative of Sri Lanka thereafter implement the said agreements enabling to contribute to the development process of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
