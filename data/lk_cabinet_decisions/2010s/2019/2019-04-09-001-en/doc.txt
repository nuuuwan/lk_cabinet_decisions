National Programme on Environmental Conservation - 2019

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-09

- The Cabinet of Ministers has granted its approval previously to implement the 'Punarudhaya' three years 2015 - 2018, National Programme on Environmental Conservation under the leadership of Presidential Secretariat. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President, Maithripala Sirisena, to implement the proposed National Programme on Environmental Conservation with a view to creating a sustainable society in Sri Lanka by continuing the scheduled programmes, was approved by the Cabinet.
