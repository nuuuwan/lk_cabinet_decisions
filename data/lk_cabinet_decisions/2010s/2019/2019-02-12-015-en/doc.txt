Hosting of the Commonwealth Law Ministers' Meeting (CLMM)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-12

- A proposal has been made for Sri Lanka to host the Commonwealth Law Ministers' Meeting to be held with the participation of the Law Ministers and the Attorney Generals of the Commonwealth countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms to provide the required facilities for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
