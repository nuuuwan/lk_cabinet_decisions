Implementation of the Meethotamulle Redevelopment Project (MRDP)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-07

- The approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously, to appoint an Officials' Committee to examine the proposals for the implementation of the Meethotamulle Redevelopment Project and make recommendations.  Accordingly, the Officials' Committee has submitted the Report inclusive of its recommendations and the proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Development to implement the Meethotamulle Redevelopment Project subject to the said recommendations , was approved by the Cabinet.
