Awarding of the contract for the Consultancy Services for the St. Sebastian South Pumping Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development, to award the contract of the consultancy services for design review, contract management and construction supervision of the works contract of the Plant Design, Supply and Installation of the St. Sebastian South Pumping Station designed under the Metro Colombo Urban Development Project (MCUDP), to EPTISA Servicios de Ingenieria of Spain, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee (CACPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
