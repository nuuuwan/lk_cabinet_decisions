Award of the Contract for the appointment of consultants for Planning, Detailed design and Procurement of Urban Upgrading, Heritage Conservation and Capacity Building Component for the Trincomalee, Dambulla, Kurunegala and Ratnapura Towns in Package 02 of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) assisted Sustainable Urban Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-27

- The proposal made by the Minister of  Megapolis & Western Development to award the consultancy contract for Planning, Detailed Design and Procurement of Urban Upgrading, Heritage Conservation and Capacity Building Component for the Trincomalee, Dambulla, Kurunegala and Ratnapura Towns in Package 02 of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) assisted Sustainable Urban Development Project to Engineering Consultants Ltd., Sri Lanka (Lead Consultants) JV with KEIOS Srl Development Consulting, Italy, on the concurrence of the Asian Development Bank and the recommendation of the Consultancy Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
