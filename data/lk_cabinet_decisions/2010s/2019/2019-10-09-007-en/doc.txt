Consultative meeting of Regional Experts on Investment Promotion for Sustainable Agricultural Development - State Private Farmer Participation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-09

- The Cabinet noted the information submitted by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development that it has been decided to hold the 'Consultative meeting of Regional Experts on Investment Promotion for Sustainable Agricultural Development - State Private Farmer Participation' in Sri Lanka from 23rd  to 25th October 2019 with the objective of exchanging knowledge and experiences gained in various fields among the member countries of the SAARC Organization.
