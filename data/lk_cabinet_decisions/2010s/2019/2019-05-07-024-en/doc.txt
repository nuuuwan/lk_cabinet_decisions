Puechase of Rituximab vials which is a treatment for cancer patients

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-07

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the purchase of 1750 vials of Rituximab injection 100 mg/10 ml and 2000 vials of said  injection 500 mg/50 ml which are used for the treatment of certain types of cancers, to M/s Transfarma Dis Ticaret Anonim Sirketi, Turkey  as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
