Providing facilities for a National Multi Stakeholder Coordination in respect of Drinking Water and Sanitation Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education to provide the necessary facilities to establish a formal mechanism of coordination with the collaboration of the relevant institutions for mobilizing of resources and commitments productively with a view to achieving the national and global goals in respect of the drinking water and sanitation services, was approved by the Cabinet.
