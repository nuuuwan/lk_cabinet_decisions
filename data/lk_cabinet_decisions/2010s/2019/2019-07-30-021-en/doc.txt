Procurement of Thirty (30) Terminal Tractors for the Jaya Container Terminal of the Sri Lanka Ports Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- The proposal made by the Minister of Ports & Shipping and Southern Development to commence the procurement process for the purchase of Thirty (30) Terminal Tractors for the Jaya Container Terminal of the Sri Lanka Ports Authority under the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, following the International Competitive Bidding Procedure, was approved by the Cabinet.
