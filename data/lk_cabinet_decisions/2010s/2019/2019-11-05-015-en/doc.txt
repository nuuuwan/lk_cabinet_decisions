Procurement for the Supply of 1,300,000 Nos. films of Film Laser Blue Base Dry CR, DR, CT and MRI

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the procurement of 1,300,000 films of Film Laser Blue Base Dry CR, DR, CT and MRI compatible with Fuji laser printer size of 35cm x 43cm for a sum of US$ 1.89 million to M/s Fujifilm Corporation of Japan, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
