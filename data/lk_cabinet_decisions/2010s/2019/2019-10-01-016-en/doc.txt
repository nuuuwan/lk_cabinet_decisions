Procurement for the supply of 180,000 vials of Anti Rabies (TC) Vaccine

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-01

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the supply of 180,000 vials of Anti Rabies (TC) Vaccine (Human Use) (0.5 ml/1 ml) which is used to prevent the rabies disease, to M/s ABC Pharma Services (Pvt) Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
