Agreement between the Consumer Affairs Authority and the Importers of Milk Powder

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- The information submitted by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock Development, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development as Chairman of the Cabinet Sub Committee on Cost of Living, that the mechanism of regulating the maximum retail price for imported milk power would be implemented hereafter by adopting the methodology introduced by the Consumer Affairs Authority in accordance with the agreement entered into between the Importers of Milk Powder and the Consumer Affairs Authority, was noted by the Cabinet.
