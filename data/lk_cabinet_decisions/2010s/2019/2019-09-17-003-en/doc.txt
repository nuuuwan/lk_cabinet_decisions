Granting Permanent Appointments to the employees recruited on Temporary, Casual (on Daily wages), Substitute, Contract or Relief Basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-17

- The joint proposal made by the Prime Minister, the Minister of Finance and the Minister of Public Administration, Disaster Management and Rural Economic Affairs, to enforce the provisions of the following circulars, to grant permanent and pensionable appointments to those who have failed to get permanency due to the inability  to fulfill the requirements stipulated in the Public Administration Circular No.25/2014 and 25/2014(I), at the time of their issuance and also for the employees recruited on temporary, casual (on daily wages), substitute, contract or relief basis to primary grades in the public institutions on exigencies of service and who have completed a period of service exceeding 180 days, subject to fulfilling the required qualifications of the said posts as at 2019 09 01, was approved by the Cabinet.
