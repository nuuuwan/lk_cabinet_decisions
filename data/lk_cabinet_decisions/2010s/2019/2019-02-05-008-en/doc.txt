The Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Government of Ukraine in respect of Taxation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance & Mass Media to enter into the Agreement at the Government level which is initialed by now at official level, between Sri Lanka and Ukraine for the avoidance of double taxation and prevention of Fiscal Evasion on income in line with the Government Policy for enhancing mutual economic relations between Sri Lanka and Ukraine, was approved by the Cabinet.
