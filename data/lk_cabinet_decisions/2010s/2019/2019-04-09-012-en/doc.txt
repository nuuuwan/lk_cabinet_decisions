Purchase of Surgical Non-consumables used in the surgical treatments

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-09

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for purchasing 07 types of items used as Surgical Non-consumables in the surgical treatments to M/s CIC Holdings PLC at a sum of Rs.428.8 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
