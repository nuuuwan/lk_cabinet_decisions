Submission of Regulations including the Anti-Doping prohibited list against doping in sports in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, Minister of Telecommunication, Foreign Employment and Sports to present the Regulations No.4 of 2018 published in the Government Gazette (Extraordinary) No.2110/2 of 11 February, 2019 including the Anti-Doping (prohibited list) while engaging in sports under section 34(I) of the Convention against Doping in Sports Act, No.33 of 2013, in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
