Procurement of food items for the Tri Forces - Supply Year 2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-12-18

- As per the recommendation made by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) relating to the Procurement of food items  for the Sri Lanka Army, Sri Lanka Navy and Sri Lanka Air Force for the Supply Year 2020, the proposal made by the Minister of Mahaweli, Agriculture, Irrigation and Rural Development and State Minister of Defence to award the said procurements, was approved by the Cabinet.
