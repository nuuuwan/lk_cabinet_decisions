Replacement of the facilities at the Chiller Plant Room in the Departure Terminal Building in the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation to award the contract for the replacement of Chillers, Cooling Tower, Pumps, Pipes with Ancillary items in the Chiller Plant in the Room No: 2KK1 of the Departure Terminal Building at the Bandaranaike International Airport, to M/s Softlogic Retail (Pvt.) Ltd., in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
