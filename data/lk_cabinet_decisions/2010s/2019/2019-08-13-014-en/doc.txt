Construction of the Moragolla Hydro Power Plant under the Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-13

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power, Energy and Business Development to award the Main Civil Works Tender for the construction of the Moragolla Hydro Power Plant Lot - A 2, to China Gezhouba Group Co. Ltd. of Tower F, Ocean International Centre, 208, Ciyunsi, Beili, Chaoyang District, Beijing of China as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet
