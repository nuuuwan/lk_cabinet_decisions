Presenting a new luxury house as the Super Prize of the Sevana Lottery Draw

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-09

- Considering the practical problems which had cropped up when presenting a new house as the super prize for the Sevana Lottery Draw, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance, to present the money allocated for the super price for making the housing dream of the prize winner a reality, subject to taxes prevailing in the country, was approved by the Cabinet.
