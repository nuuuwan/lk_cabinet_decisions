Procurement for the supply of 10,000,000 bottles of 500ml Sodium Chloride Intravenous Infusion BP 0.9% w/v or Sodium Chloride Injection USP, 0.9% w/v

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-19

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the supply of 10,000,000 bottles of 500ml Sodium Chloride Intravenous Infusion BP 0.9% w/v or Sodium Chloride Injection USP, 0.9% w/v, to M/s Aculife Healthcare (Pvt.) Ltd. of India for a sum of US$ 2.6 million, being the total cost inclusive of the freight charges, as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
