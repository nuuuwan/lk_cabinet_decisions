Declaring 21st of March as the Surveyors' Day

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to declare 02nd of August as the Surveyors' Day and the International Federation of Surveyors, in turn, has declared 21st March as the Surveyors' Day. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Lands and Parliamentary Reforms, to declare 21st of March every year as the Surveyors' Day, was approved by the Cabinet.
