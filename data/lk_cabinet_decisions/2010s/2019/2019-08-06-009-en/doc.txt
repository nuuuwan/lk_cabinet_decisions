Allocation of the lands in the IT Park at Malabe to the Institute of Chemistry Ceylon and the University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-06

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously, to allocate a land in extent of 01 A - 0 R - 6.40 P in the IT Park at Malabe to the IESL College of Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd. on a 30 year lease. However, due to the non payment of the relevant lease to the Government by the said Institute, the proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to revoke the above decision and allocate the said plot of land to the Institute of Chemistry Ceylon and in addition, to allocate a plot of land in extent of 02 A - 0 R - 7.90 P to the  University of Peradeniya, was approved by the Cabinet.
