Completion of the remaining construction work of the New Court Complex in Matara

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-19

- The Cabinet of Ministers has granted its approval for the construction of the new court complex in Matara and presently, its construction work is in progress. It has been planned to complete the said construction work by May 2019 and accordingly, the proposal made by Hon.	(Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms to secure the funds needed for carrying out further improvements necessary and providing of facilities in order to use the courts complex for the proper commencement of court functions, was approved by the Cabinet.
