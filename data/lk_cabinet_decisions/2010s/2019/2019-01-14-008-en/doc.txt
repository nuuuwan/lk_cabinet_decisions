Procurement of Uniforms and other Clothing Materials for the Tri Forces, the Department of Civil Security, the National Cadet Corps, the Sri Lanka Police and the Special Task Force

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-01-14

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to procure  uniforms and other clothing materials  for the Tri‑Forces, the Department of Civil Security, the National Cadet Corps, the Sri Lanka Police and the Special Task Force from local textile manufactures for a sum of Rs.1,596.4 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
