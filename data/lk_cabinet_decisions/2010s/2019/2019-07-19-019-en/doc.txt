Procurement for the supply of 45,000 vials of 5g - 6g of Human Immunoglobulin required for Intravenous use

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-19

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the supply of 45,000 vials of 5g - 6g of Human Immunoglobulin required for Intravenous use, to M/s Reliance Life Science (Pvt.) Ltd. of India for a sum of US$ 4.8 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
