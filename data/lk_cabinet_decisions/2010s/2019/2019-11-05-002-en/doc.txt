Amendments to the Parliament (Powers and Privileges) Act, No.21 of 1953

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to publish the draft (Amendment) Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman amending the Parliament (Powers and Privileges) Act, No.21 of 1953, in the Government Gazette and subsequently, to present it in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
