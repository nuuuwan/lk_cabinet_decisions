Presenting the Gazette Notification under the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No.40 of 1979 in Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade to present the Gazette Notification issued in regard to the removal of the CESS levy on importation of goods in accordance with the budget proposals of 2019 and the policy decisions taken by the Government, in Parliament, for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
