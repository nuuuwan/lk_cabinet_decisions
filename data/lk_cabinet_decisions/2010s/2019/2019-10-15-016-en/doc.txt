Awarding of the Project for 'The rectification of unstable slopes in the premises of Teaching Hospital, Kandy' through the Austrian Soft Loan Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-10-15

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract of the project for the rectification of unstable slopes in the premises of the Teaching Hospital, Kandy, to M/s Porr GmbH, through the Austrian Soft Loan Scheme, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
