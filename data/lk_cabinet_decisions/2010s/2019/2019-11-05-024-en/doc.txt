Consortium Architectural Consultancy Service of the proposed Disaster Risk Reduction Observation, Forecasting and Communication Hubs for the Department of Meteorology and the Department of Irrigation' under the Climate Resilience Improvement Project (CRIP)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, to award the contracts of Consortium Architectural Consultancy Service of the proposed Disaster Risk Reduction Observation, Forecasting and Communication Hubs for the Department of Meteorology and the Department of Irrigation under the Climate Resilience Improvement Project, to M/s Design Group International (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of US$ 63.43 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
