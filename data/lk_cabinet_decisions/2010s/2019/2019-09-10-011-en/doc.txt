Allocating the plots of land in the Ratnapura New Town for the construction of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Health Training Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- The proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to release the two plots of land in extent of 3 Roods 1.5 perches in the Ratnapura New Town belonging to the Urban Development Authority to the Land Commissioner General enabling to allocate the lands for the Provincial Health Department of the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Council, to construct the Sabaragamuwa Provincial Health Training Centre subject to the payment of Rs.30 million being the valuation of the Government Valuer, was approved by the Cabinet.
