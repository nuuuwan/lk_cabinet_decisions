Establishment of a new Mixed National School of Tri-lingual Education in the Mirigama area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-06

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously, for the establishment of a new Mixed National School of Tri-lingual Education in the Mirigama area.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education, to secure the necessary funds required for the construction and other development activities of the school to commence its educational activities and enable the admission of children to Grade 6  by February 2020, was approved by the Cabinet.
