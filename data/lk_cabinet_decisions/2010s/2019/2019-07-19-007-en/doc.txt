Allocation of land at Kandawalawatta, Ratmalana to the National Housing Development Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-19

- The proposal made by the Minister of Megapolis & Western Development to grant a block of land of 37.16 perches of the land in extent of 80 perches belonging to the Urban Development Authority to the National Housing Development Authority as a free grant, enabling to issue Deeds of Transfer to the occupants who have been already settled there, was approved by the Cabinet.
