The contract for the construction of the National Tsunami Early Warning Centre and Laboratory Building of the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-12

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Mahaweli Development and Environment, to award the contract for the construction of the National Tsunami Early Warning Centre and Laboratory Building of the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau to the Central Engineering Services (Pvt.) Ltd., (CESL) for a sum of Rs 383.2 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
