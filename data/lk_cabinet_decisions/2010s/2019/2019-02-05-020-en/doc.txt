Rehabilitation and improvement of 25km road section from  Maradankadawala to Habarana in the Maradankadawala - Habarana-Tirikkondiadimadu Road (A11)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development, to award the contract for the rehabilitation and improvement including the routine maintenance work for 5 years after its construction, of the 25km road section from  Maradankadawala to Habarana on the Maradankadawala-Habarana-Tirikkondiadimadu Road (A11) under the Integrated Road Investment Programme, to Consulting Engineers and Contractors (Pvt) Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
