Mid - Year Fiscal Position Report - 2019

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-07

- The proposal made by the Minister of Finance to table the  Mid - Year Fiscal Position Report - 2019 prepared, explaining the macroeconomic management of Sri Lanka in a difficult local and global environment and the Government revenue, expenditure and cash flow management, including the updated information on the fiscal status of Sri Lanka  during the first four (04) months of 2019, in terms of the provisions of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No.03 of 2003, was approved by the Cabinet.
