Entering into a Term Contract for the importation of Gasoline from 2019 10 01 to 2020 05 31

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-17

- The proposal made by the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development, to award the contract for the importation of  900,000 Bbls of Gasoline during the period of eight months from 2019 10 01 to 2020 05 31, to M/s Vitol Asia Pte. Ltd., of Singapore, as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee,  was approved by the Cabinet.
