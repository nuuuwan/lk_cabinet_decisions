Obtaining Consultancy Services for the Second Integrated Road Investment Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development and Petroleum Resources Development, to award the contract for providing consultancy services including construction supervision, contract management and other implementation activities for project implementation in the Eastern Province under the Second Integrated Road Investment Programme funded by the Asian Development Bank to the joint venture of Egis International and Greentech Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd., as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
