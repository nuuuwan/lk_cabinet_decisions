Construction of a New Building Complex for the Gampaha District Secretariat

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-02-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Wajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government, to award the contract for the design and construction of a new building complex for the Gampaha District Secretariat, to M/s Sanken Construction (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.3,054.2 million, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
