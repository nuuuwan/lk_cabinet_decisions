Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) in the fields of Science, Technology and Innovation between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Nepal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-06

- The proposal made by H.E. the President at the request of the Non-Cabinet Minister of Science, Technology & Research  to enter into  an MoU in the fields of Science, Technology and Innovation between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Nepal on the basis of equality and mutual benefits and with the objective of establishing the cooperation in the said field, was approved by the Cabinet.
