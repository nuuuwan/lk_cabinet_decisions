Drafting an identical Act for the Ceylon Fishery Harbours Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-24

- The proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development, to draft a new Parliamentary Act identical to the Ceylon Fishing Harbour Corporation by the Legal Draftsman with a view to converting the said corporation to enhance the generation of income sufficient for self existence and to be profitable by strengthening the same legally and increasing the income generation capability, was approved by the Cabinet.
