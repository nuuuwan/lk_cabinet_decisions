Implementation of a short term public relation campaign for the revival and rebuilding 'Sri Lanka Tourism Brand'

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- The proposal made by the Minister of Tourism Development, Wildlife and Christian Religious Affairs for obtaining covering approval to select M/s J Walter Thompson (Pvt.) Ltd., deviating from the National Procurement Guidelines to implement a short term public relation campaign without any delay for the revival and rebuilding 'Sri Lanka Tourism Brand' and to manage the crisis situation with a view to achieving the key objectives to support the economy of the country after Easter Sunday attacks, was taken into consideration by the Cabinet.
