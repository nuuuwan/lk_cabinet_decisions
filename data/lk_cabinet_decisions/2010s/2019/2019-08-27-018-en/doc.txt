Procurement of 5000 Rails  for the Sri Lanka Railways

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-08-27

- The proposal made by the Minister of Transport & Civil Aviation to award the contract for the procurement of 5000 EN 45E1 Rails (45'00") of R 260-EN 13674 to  M/s Angang Group International Panzhihua Co. Ltd. for a sum of US$ 2,320,230 on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
