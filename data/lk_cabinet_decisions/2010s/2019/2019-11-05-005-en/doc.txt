Economic Service Charge (Amendment) Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Finance to publish the draft Bill to amend the Economic Service Charge Act, No.13 of 2006 to give effect to the tax proposals made in the National Budget 2019 in the Government Gazette and subsequently, to present the said draft Bill in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
