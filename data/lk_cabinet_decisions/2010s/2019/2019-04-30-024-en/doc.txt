Completion of the construction work of the new building constructed for the District Secretariat in Kegalle

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-04-30

- Construction activities of the new building for the Kegalle District Secretariat have been initiated as per the approval granted by the Cabinet previously and accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Wajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government, to carry out the further construction activities and to have other accessories fixed by the State Engineering Corporation for the completion of the necessary construction activities, to make the said building practicably usable, was approved by the Cabinet.
