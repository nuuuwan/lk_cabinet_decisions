Agriculture Sector Modernization Project - Contract for the consultancy services to design, establish, operate, manage Agriculture Technology Demonstration Parks and transfer to the Farmer Producer Organizations supported by the Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-03

The proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Rural Economic Affairs, Irrigation and Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development to award the contract for the consultancy services to design, establish, operate, manage  Agriculture Technology Demonstration Parks and transfer to the Farmer Producer Organizations under the Agriculture Sector Modernization Project implemented with the World Bank Financing to the Joint Venture of M/s FCG ANZDEC Ltd, New Zealand and FCG Swedish Development AB, Sweden, was approved  by the Cabinet.
