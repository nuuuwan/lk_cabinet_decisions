Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Jaffna and the University of Bristol in the United Kingdom

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-17

- The proposal made by the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Jaffna and the University of Bristol in the United Kingdom, with a view to promoting educational corporation within the higher education sector, was approved by the Cabinet.
