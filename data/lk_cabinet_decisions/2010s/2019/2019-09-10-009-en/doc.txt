Construction of a Building for the Accident and Emergency Care Unit of the District General Hospital in Ampara

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-10

- Considering the urgent need for providing facilities for accident and emergency care services at the District General Hospital in Ampara, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the construction of a two storey building for the Accident and Emergency Care Unit of the said hospital, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau on Turn-Key (Design and Build) Basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
