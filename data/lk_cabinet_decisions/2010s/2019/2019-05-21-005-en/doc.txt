Development of the Polonnaruwa and Batticaloa Waste Water Collection, 	Treatment and Disposal Systems

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, to get the feasibility studies, for the projects on Waste Water Collection, Treatment and Disposal Systems proposed to be implemented for providing facilities for the people living in 16 Grama Niladhari Divisions, in Thamankaduwa Divisional Secretariat Division of the Polonnaruwa District and for the people living in 26 Grama Niladhari Divisions in the Manmunai North Divisional Secretariat Division of the Batticaloa District , was approved by the Cabinet.
