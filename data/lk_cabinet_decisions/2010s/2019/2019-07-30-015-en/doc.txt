Construction of the building for the proposed Help Zone of the University of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-07-30

- The proposal made by the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education to award the contract for the construction of the building for the proposed Help Zone of the University of Colombo, to M/s Kandy Construction at No.264/2, Koswatta, Battaramulla, for a sum of Rs.520 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
