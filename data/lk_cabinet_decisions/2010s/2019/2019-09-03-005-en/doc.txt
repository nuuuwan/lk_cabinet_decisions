Kelani Right Bank Water Supply Project - Stage II

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-09-03

- The proposal made by the Minister of City Planning, Water Supply and Higher Education, to extend the project period of the Kelani Right Bank Water Supply Project - Stage II, which is being implemented for the supply of water to Kelaniya, Wattala, Mahara, Biyagama and Ja-Ela as well as the Katunayake area including the International Airport, the Free Trade Zone and the Air Force Camp, up to 22nd June, 2020, was approved by the Cabinet.
