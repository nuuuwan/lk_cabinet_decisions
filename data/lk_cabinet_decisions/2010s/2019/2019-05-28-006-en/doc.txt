Ensuring the security of the Atamasthana inclusive of  the Sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi and the Ruwanveli Maha Seya of Anuradhapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-28

- Proposals have been made by the Maha Sanga including various parties for  ensuring the security of the Atamasthana inclusive of  the Sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi and the Ruwanveli Maha Seya of Anuradhapura which has earned the kind respect of the above local and foreign Buddhists. Taking  into consideration, the proposal made by Hon. Gamini Jayawickrema Perera, the Minister of Buddhasasana & Wayamba Development, providing security measures inclusive of erecting a high security fence around the Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi and the Ruwanveli Maha Seya, was approved by the Cabinet.
