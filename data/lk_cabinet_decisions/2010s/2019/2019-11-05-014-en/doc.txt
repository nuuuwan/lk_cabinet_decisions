Procurement for the  supply of 60,000 Vials of Human Immunoglobulin for Intravenous use

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-11-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine to award the contract for the procurement of 60,000 Vials of Human Immunoglobulin for Intravenous used for the treatment of patients with immune deficiencies, for a sum of US$ 6.76 million from M/s Reliance Life Science of India, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
