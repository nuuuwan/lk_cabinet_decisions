Procurement of Coronary Stents used in the treatment of patients with Coronary Heart Diseases

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-05-07

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition & Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the procurement of Coronary Stents and Drug Eluting Coronary Stents of various sizes for the procedure of percutaneous Coronary Intervention in the treatment of patients with coronary heart diseases, to the suppliers selected as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, for a sum of Rs 446.39 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
