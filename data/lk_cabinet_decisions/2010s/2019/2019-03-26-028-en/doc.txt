Water supply schemes in the areas of Bathalayaya, Belaganwewa and Girandurukotte

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2019-03-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Wajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Internal & Home Affairs and Provincial Councils & Local Government to award the contract for the water supply extension to Bathalayaya and Belaganwewa areas and the contract for the augmentation of the Girandurukotte Treatment Plant and the intake, to M/s Squire Mech Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd., in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
