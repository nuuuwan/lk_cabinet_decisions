Development of the Divisional Level Hospitals in Polonnaruwa District

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-09

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the contracts for the improvement of facilities in Base Hospitals in Bakamuna, Aralaganwila, Welikanda, Hingurakgoda and Medirigiriya in the Polonnaruwa District, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), at a total cost of Rs.463,365,050.40, was approved by the Cabinet.
