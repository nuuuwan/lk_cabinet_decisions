Establishing the Seethawaka Wet Zone Botanical Garden at Illukowita, in  Avissawella

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-02-20

- the proposal made by Hon. Jayaratne Herath, Minister of Botanical Gardens and Public Recreation, to establish the Seethawaka Wet Zone Botanical Garden at Illukowita, in  Avissawella in a land with an extent of 32 hectares, adjacent to the Indikada Mukalana Forest Reserve in Avissawella, was approved by the Cabinet.
