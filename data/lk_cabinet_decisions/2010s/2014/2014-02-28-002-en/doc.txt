Amendments to the Buddhist Temporalities Ordinance, No.19 of 1931

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-02-28

- the proposal made by Hon. D.M. Jayaratne, Minister of Buddha Sasana and Religious Affairs, to refer the proposed amendments to the Buddhist Temporalities Ordinance, No.19 of 1931, to the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Legislation, for its recommendations in the first instance, was approved by the Cabinet.
