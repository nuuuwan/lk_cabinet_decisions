Construction of Model Police Buildings under the Special Project for Construction of New Buildings - 2014/2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-13

- 39 buildings  consisting of 26 Police Stations, 07 DIG Offices, 04 Barracks etc., falling under the purview of the Department of Police are to be constructed during the next three (03) years at a total cost of Rs.6,367 million under this project.  The above proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Law and Order, was approved by the Cabinet.
