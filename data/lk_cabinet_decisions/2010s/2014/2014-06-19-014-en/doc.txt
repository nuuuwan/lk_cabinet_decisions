Amendment to the Articles of Agreement and Subscription to the Sri Lanka Shares under 2010 Selective Capital Increase of the International Finance Corporation (IFC) of World Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-06-19

- the International Finance Corporation (IFC) is the Development Finance Institution of the World Bank Group which offers investment and advisory services to encourage private sector development and related employment opportunities in developing countries.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to proceed with the activities relating to the above Amendment to the Articles of Agreement and Subscription to the Sri Lanka Shares, was approved by the Cabinet.
