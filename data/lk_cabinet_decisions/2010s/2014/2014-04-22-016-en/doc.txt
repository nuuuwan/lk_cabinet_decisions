Widening and Improvements to Airport Runway and Apron at Ampara Airport - Approval for Variations to the Contract

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-04-22

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Highways, Ports and Shipping, to revise the contract price of the above project as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and Cost Estimate Examination Committee (CEEC), due to the changes made to the design of Runway and Apron at the Ampara Airport, was approved by the Cabinet.
