Printing of School Text Books to be Distributed for the year,  2015

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-10

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to entrust the printing of 29,115,800 copies of 351 categories of school text books for a total cost of Rs.1,840,639,050.00 to 22 Printing Institutions, as recommended by the Standing (Special) Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
