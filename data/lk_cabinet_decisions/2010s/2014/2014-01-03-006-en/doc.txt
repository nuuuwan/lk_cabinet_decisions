Purchasing a Block of Land for the Development of the Infrastructure Facilities of the University of Moratuwa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-03

- the University of Moratuwa comprises of three Faculties in the disciplines of Engineering, Information Technology and Architecture and the number of students admitted for the Academic Year 2011/2012 was 1747.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to purchase the block of land at Katubedda, Moratuwa, to provide more accommodation to the students and the staff of the University, was approved by the Cabinet.
