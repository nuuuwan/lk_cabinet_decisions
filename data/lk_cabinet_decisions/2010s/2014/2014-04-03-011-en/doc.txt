Supply and Installation of Protection Systems at 200 Unprotected Level Crossings

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-04-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of Transport, to award the procurement for the supply and installation of protection systems at 200 unprotected level crossings at a cost of US $6,478,190.00 to the lowest bidder, was approved by the Cabinet.
