Construction of a Building for the Jaffna Municipal Council

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-13

- the proposal made by Hon. A.L.M. Athaullah, Minister of Local Government and Provincial Councils, for the construction of a three storeyed building complex for the Jaffna Municipal Council to provide a better service for the people living in the area and to proceed with the relevant activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
