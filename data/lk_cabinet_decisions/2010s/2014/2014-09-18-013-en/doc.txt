Renovation of Nursing Schools in Kandana, Matara & Hambantota

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the contract for the modernization and renovation of the Nursing School in Kandana, Hambantota and Matara, to the Mahaweli Consultancy Bureau was approved by the Cabinet.
