Construction of Doctors Quarters for the National Hospital of Sri Lanka at the premises of the Base Hospital, Mulleriyawa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-09

- based on the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, for the construction of quarters for the Doctors of the National Hospital of Sri Lanka at the premises of the Base Hospital, Mulleriyawa, Cabinet approval was granted for the construction of a multi-storied housing complex at the said premises.
