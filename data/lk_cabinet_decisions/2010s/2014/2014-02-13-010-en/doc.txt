Construction of three storied building for the establishment of Professorial Units at the Teaching Hospital, Batticaloa for the Faculty of Health Care Sciences of the Eastern University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-02-13

- the  proposal  made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above contract to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder at a cost of Rs.185 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
