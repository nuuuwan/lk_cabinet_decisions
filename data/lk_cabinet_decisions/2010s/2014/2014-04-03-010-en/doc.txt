Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan on Co-operation in the Field of Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-04-03

- on the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education,  covering approval was granted by the Cabinet for the above MoU signed between the two countries and to take steps for its implementation.
