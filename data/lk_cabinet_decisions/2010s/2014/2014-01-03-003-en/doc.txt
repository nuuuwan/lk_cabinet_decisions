Transferring the Water Supply System in Ratnapura Provincial General Hospital to the National Water Supply and Drainage Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to transfer the Water Supply System in Ratnapura Provincial General Hospital from the Buildings Department to the National Water Supply and Drainage Board to provide a better service to the hospital, was approved by the Cabinet.
