Establishment of a Welfare Fund for Journalists, Artistes and Writers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-29

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, to establish a Fund to assist aged Artistes, Journalists and Writers who had been previously engaged in creative work as professional Artistes and Journalists on full time basis and are spending their retired life, to ensure their well-being, and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft necessary legislation for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
