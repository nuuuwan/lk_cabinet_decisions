3rd International Conference on Community and Water Services - August 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-10

- the above Conference is to be held in Kandy from 22nd to 24th August 2014 under chairmanship of Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage and a number of countries within the region such as India, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Afghanistan and Maldives are expected to attend to this Conference.  This Conference is aimed at providing a platform for sharing knowledge, strategies and best practices and to promote dialogue, mutual understanding and cooperation among communities, managing their water schemes.  About 500 delegates from different Community Based Organizations, Policy Makers, Researchers, Academia, UN Agencies and International Development Agencies are expected to participate at this Conference.  The above information was furnished to the Cabinet by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage.
