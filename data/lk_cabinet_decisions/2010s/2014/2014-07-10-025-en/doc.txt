Mid Year Fiscal Position Report - 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-10

- H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, submitted the Mid Year Fiscal Position Report - 2014 to the Cabinet, which includes information on Government revenue, expenditure, cash flow activities and borrowings within the first four months of the year, and also data on macroeconomic performance, foreign financing, Government debt, external sector performance and performance of State Owned Enterprises depending on the availability of data.   Cabinet approval was granted to submit the above Report to Parliament.
