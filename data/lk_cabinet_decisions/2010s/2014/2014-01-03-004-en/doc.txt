Urban Forest Resort Project, Sri Jayewardenepura Kotte

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Basil Rajapaksa, Minister of Economic Development, to develop the land at Battaramulla with an extent of about 11 acres owned by the Urban Development Authority, which was used for the exhibition of "Reflection of Sri Lanka" as an Urban Forest Resort without any adverse impact upon the landscape of the area, to cater to the tourists both local and foreign, and to proceed with the activities, was approved by the the Cabinet.
