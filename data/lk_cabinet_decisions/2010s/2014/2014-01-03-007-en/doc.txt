Construction of Official Quarters Complex for the District Secretariat, Badulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-03

-  Badulla District consists of 15 Divisional Secretary's Divisions and the total population is around 811,758.  The proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, to construct official quarters for the District Secretariat, Badulla at a total cost estimate of Rs.57.4 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
