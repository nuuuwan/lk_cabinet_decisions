Tender for the Supply of 10,000,000 Tablets of Co-Amoxiclav Tablets BP 625mg or Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium Tablet USP 625mg

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the supply of 25% of the above contract initially to the sole responsive bidder  at a cost of US$ 236,250.00 and thereafter to award the balance 75% quantity at a total cost of US$ 708,750.00 depending on the satisfactory performance of the supply of the initial quantity, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
