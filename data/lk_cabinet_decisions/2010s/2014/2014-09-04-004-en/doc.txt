China Development Bank funded Priority Roads Project 3 - Phase II -  Award of Civil Works Contracts in Matara and Hambantota Districts

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-04

-  nine roads located in Matara and Hambantota Districts in the Southern Province have been identified for rehabilitation under the above programme in view of the National Exhibition - 'Deyata Kirula' 2015.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of  Highways, Ports and Shipping, to award the above contracts as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
