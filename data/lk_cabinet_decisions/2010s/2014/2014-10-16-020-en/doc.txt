Award of Contract for the construction of Migrant Resource Centre of Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment at Hali-Ela, Badulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-16

- Cabinet approval has already been granted to construct the Migrant Resource Centre of the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment at Hali-Ela, Badulla.  The proposal made by Hon. Dilan Perera, Minister of Foreign Employment Promotion and Welfare, to award the above contract as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
