Construction of the 3rd stage of the Maternity Ward Complex in Teaching Hospital, Kurunegala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-04

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of  Health, to award the above contract to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) at a cost of Rs. 914,946,852.90, to be completed within a period of 24 months, was approved by the Cabinet.
