Construction of a Five Storied Office Building at the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWS&DB) Head Office Premises, Ratmalana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-17

- the proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to construct a Five Storied Office Building at Ratmalana for the National Water Supply and Drainage Board and to proceed with the related activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
