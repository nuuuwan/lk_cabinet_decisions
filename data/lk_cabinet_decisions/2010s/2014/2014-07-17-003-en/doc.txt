Vesting of State Lands for Kandy City Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-17

- the Urban Development Authority (UDA) has initiated work to renovate the Ehelepola Walluwa in Kandy which was formally used as Bogambara Remand Prison, to be developed as a Cultural Centre and an Urban Facility Centre.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to vest the land in extent of 0.3866 ha. to the UDA as a free grant for the Kandy City Development Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
