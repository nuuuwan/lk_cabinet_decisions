Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Information & Communication Technology Authority (ICTA), Turkey and the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-06-19

- this MoU provides a mechanism for technical and institutional cooperation with the purpose of contributing to telecommunication development in both Sri Lanka and Turkey.  Particular areas of cooperation envisaged are technological development, universal access to telecommunication services, convergence  and next generation networks etc.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between TRCSL, Sri Lanka and ICTA, Turkey, was approved by the Cabinet.
