Social Integration Week - July 14 - July 20, 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-10

- the Ministry of National Languages and Social Integration had carried out activities Island?wide successfully with the assistance of the Government Institutions and other stakeholders under theme “Education for Social Integration” during the Social Integration Week in the year 2013. This year's Social Integration Week will be held from 14th to 20th July 2014 for the 3rd consecutive year, under theme 'National Unity through Social Integration' and several activities / programmes such as Kite Festival, Launching of Trilingual Magazine, Inter-School Speech Competitions etc., will be held during this week.  The above information was furnished to the Cabinet by Hon. Vasudeva Nanayakkara, Minister of National Languages and Social Integration.
