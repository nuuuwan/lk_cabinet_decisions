Provision of Equipment for Mahindodaya Technological Laboratories under the Programme of Redesigning of 1000 Secondary Schools

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-13

- a proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to award the contract for the supply of Computers with all other accessories,  with networking for 200 schools in which construction work has been completed and electricity facilities provided, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
