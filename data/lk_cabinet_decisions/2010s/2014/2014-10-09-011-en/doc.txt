Hosting 49th Session of the UN affiliated International Sugar Organization to be held in Colombo in mid - 2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-09

- the proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Seneviratne, Minister of Sugar Industry Development, to host the 49th Session of the International Sugar Organization which is affiliated to the United Nations with a membership of 88 countries, in mid 2016, in Colombo, was approved by the Cabinet.
