Thambuttegama Water Supply Scheme - Plant & Design-build Contract for the Civil and M&E Works

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- this project is to enhance the pipe borne water supply coverage for safe drinking water to 42 Grama Niladhari Divisions in Thalawa, Thambuttegama and Galnewa Divisional Secretaries' Divisions.  The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to implement the above water supply project and to award the contract as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
