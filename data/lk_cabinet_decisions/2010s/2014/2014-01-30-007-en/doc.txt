Relocation of the Tangalle Prison and a Section of the Welikada Prison in Colombo at Eraminiyaya, Angunakolapelessa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-30

- Tangalle Prison and a Section of the Welikada Prison in Colombo are to be re?located at Eraminiyaya, Angunakolapelessa. The proposal made by Hon. Chandrasiri Gajadeera, Minister of Rehabilitation and Prison Reforms, to award the above contract as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
