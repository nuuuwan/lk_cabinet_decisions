Construction of Extension of Southern Expressway Section 4 - from Mattala to Hambantota via Andarawewa Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-04

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to proceed with the activities relating to loan arrangements to finance the Construction of Extension of Southern Expressway Section 4 from Mattala to Hambantota via Andarawewa, was approved by the Cabinet.
