Polgahawela-Pothuhera-Alawwa Integrated Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-03-20

- the proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to award the design and build contract for the Polgahawela-Pothuhera-Alawwa Integrated Water Supply Project, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
