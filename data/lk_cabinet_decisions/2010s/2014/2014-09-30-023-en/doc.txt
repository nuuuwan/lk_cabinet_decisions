Procurement of 80 Nos. Small New Buses for Remote Villages

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-30

- the proposal made by Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of Transport, to award the contract to the supply of 80 nos. New 35 seater two door Ashok Leyland Lynx Buses at a unit price of Rs. 3,600,000.00  and at a total price of Rs. 288.0 million, to Lanka Ashok Leyland Plc, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
