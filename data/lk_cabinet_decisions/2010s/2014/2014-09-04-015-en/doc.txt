Procurement of  50 buses for Department of Prisons

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-04

- the proposal made by Hon. Chandrasiri Gajadeera, Minister of Rehabilitation and Prison Reforms, to award the contract for the supply of 44 seater 20 buses at a cost of Rs.103,000,000.00 and 30 seater 30 buses at a cost of Rs.121,350,000.00 for the Department of Prisons as recommended by the  Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC) taking into consideration the views of the Procurement Appeal Board (PAB),  was approved by the Cabinet.
