Introduction of Sri Lanka Standards for Imported Items

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-13

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to submit to Parliament, the Gazette Extraordinary No.1844/49 dated 2014?01?08, which amends the Import (Standardization and Quality Control) Regulations No.01 of 2006 and introduces new Sri Lanka Standards for 23 imported items, was approved by the Cabinet.
