Provincial Component of Additional Financing of Northern Road Connectivity Project Funded by Asian Development Bank - Awarding of Project Implementation Consultancy Contract for Supervision of Civil Works Contracts in Northern and North Central Provinces

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-13

- the Asian Development Bank has allocated funds for rehabilitation and upgrading of National and Provincial Roads in the Northern Province and the North Central Province.  The objective of the consultancy services contract referred to above is to professionally supervise the construction of Provincial Road contracts in the 02 Provinces to ensure that the works are completed as stipulated in the contracts. The proposal made by Hon. A.L.M. Athaullah, Minister of Local Government and Provincial Councils, to award the above contract as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee (SCACPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
