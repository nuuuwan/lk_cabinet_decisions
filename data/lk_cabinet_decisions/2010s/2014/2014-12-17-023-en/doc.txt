Proposed Training Centre to be constructed at Kotawilawatta, Matara in parallel to the 'Deyata Kirula' National Exhibition

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-17

- the proposal made by Hon. Chandrasiri Gajadeera, Minister of Rehabilitation and Prison Reforms, to construct the above training centre to be utilized as the training school for the officers of the Department of Prisons, was approved by the Cabinet.
