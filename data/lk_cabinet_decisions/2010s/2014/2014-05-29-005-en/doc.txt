Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Ministry of Interior of the Russian Federation and the Ministry of Law and Order of Sri Lanka on Combating Crimes and Promotion of Police Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-29

- the objective of this MoU is to promote cooperation between the two countries by way of exchanging information on major criminal issues such as terrorism, crimes against lives and property, corruption, drug trafficking, human trafficking and money laundering etc.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Law and Order, was approved by the Cabinet.
