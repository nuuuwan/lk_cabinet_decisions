SAARC Framework Agreement for Energy Cooperation (Electricity)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-23

- the proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Power and Energy, to ratify the above Agreement which has already been signed at the 18th SAARC Summit held in Kathmandu, Nepal, with the minor amendments proposed by the Government of Pakistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
