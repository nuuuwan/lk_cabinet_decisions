Completion of Balance work of the Millennium Ward Complex of the Colombo South Teaching Hospital

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-08-14

- on the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, for the completion of the balance work of the above hospital, Cabinet approval was granted to award the consultancy services contract of the above to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) and to select a suitable contractor for construction work through the National Competitive Bidding Procedure.
