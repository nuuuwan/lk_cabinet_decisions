Construction of a four storied building complex for the Department of Statistics and Computer Science of the Faculty of Science of the University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-19

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayaka, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above contract to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder at a cost of Rs.156,220,000.00, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
