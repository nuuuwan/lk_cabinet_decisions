Annual Executive Council Meeting of Asia Pacific Postal Union  (APPU) in Sri Lanka from September 15-19

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-10

- the above Meeting is to be held from 15th to 19th of September  2014 in Colombo, where about 150 representatives from 32 Member countries will participate.  The inaugural ceremony is to be held on 15th of September under  the patronage of H.E. the President.  The above information was furnished to the Cabinet by Hon. Jeevan Kumaranathunga, Minister of Postal Services.
