Greater Colombo Waste Water Management Project - Rehabilitation / Augmentation of 06 Nos. Waste Water Pump Stations at Dehiwala / Mount Lavinia and Kolonnawa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-06-19

- the proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to award the contract relating to the above project to be implemented with financial assistance from the Asian Development Bank (ADB), for an amount of US $7,795,674.05, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
