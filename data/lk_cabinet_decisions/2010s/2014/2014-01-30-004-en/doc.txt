Aquaculture Management Regulations made under paragraph (c) of sub Section (2) of Section 37 of the National Aquaculture Development Authority of  Sri Lanka Act, No.53 of 1998

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-30

- the proposal made by Hon. Rajitha Senaratne, Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development, to submit the above regulations to Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.  Through these regulations, aquaculture farming without obtaining a license for same is prohibited and an inspection fee too has been included in the regulations.
