Scarcity of Tables and Chairs in Schools in Remote Areas of the Country

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-17

- the proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, Minister of Education Services, for the provision of Rs.575 million under the Medium Term Budgetary Framework 2015-2017, to provide tables and chairs for students and teachers in schools in remote areas of the country where there is a shortage, was approved by the Cabinet.
