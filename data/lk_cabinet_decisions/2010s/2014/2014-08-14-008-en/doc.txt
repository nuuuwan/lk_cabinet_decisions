Infrastructure Development of Jaffna, Sabaragamuwa, Kelaniya, Peradeniya, Buddhist and Pali and Eastern Universities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-08-14

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above contracts relating to infrastructure development of the above Universities, on a Turnkey Basis by calling for limited bids from five eligible contractors who have been successfully engaged in construction work,  was approved by the Cabinet.
