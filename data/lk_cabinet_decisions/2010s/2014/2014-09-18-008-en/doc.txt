Divineguma National Development Programme - Phase VI - 2014 - Supply of Vegetable Seeds and Fruit Plants

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, to procure vegetable seed pouches, fruit plants and other agricultural crop plants for the Divineguma National Development Programme, from nurseries of the Department of Agriculture and private owned nurseries registered with the Department of Agriculture at rates approved by the Department, was approved by the Cabinet.
