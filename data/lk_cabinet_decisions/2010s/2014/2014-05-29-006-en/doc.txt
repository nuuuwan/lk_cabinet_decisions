Establishment of an Inter-Ministerial Operational Committee on Law and Order

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-29

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Law and Order, to establish an Inter-Ministerial Operational Committee on Law and Order, to implement the policies and programmes of the Ministry, the Department of Police and the Special Task Force, with active participation of other relevant Ministries and Institutions, for creating a disciplined and law abiding society, was approved by the Cabinet.
