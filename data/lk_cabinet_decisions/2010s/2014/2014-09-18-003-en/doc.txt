Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Power and Energy of  Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Environment and Energy of the Republic of Seychelles to Promote Technical and Investment Cooperation in the Power Sector Development in General and the Implementation of Renewable Energy Projects in Seychelles

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- Cabinet approval was granted for signing of the above Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Seychelles as proposed by Hon. (Mrs.) Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Power and Energy.
