Amendment to the National Institute of Plantation Management Act, No.45 of 1979

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, Minister of Plantation Industries, to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft amending legislation  to amend the National Institute of Plantation Management Act, No. 45 of 1979, in keeping with the present day needs, was approved by the Cabinet.
