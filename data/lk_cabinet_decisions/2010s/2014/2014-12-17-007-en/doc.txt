Supply and Installation of 10 Nos. Solar Universal Pumps and Drinking Water Treatment Plants

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-17

- the proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, for the Supply and Installation of 10 Nos. Solar Universal Pumps and Drinking Water Treatment Plants, with the view to providing safe drinking water to the people living in areas where  the chronic kidney disease prevails, was approved by the Cabinet.
