Provision of Infrastructure Facilities for the Universities to facilitate the additional intake of students for 2013

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the contract for the construction of a five storied new building for Lecture Halls, Reading Rooms and Multipurpose Auditorium for the University of Colombo School of Computing, to the lowest evaluated responsive bidder as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
