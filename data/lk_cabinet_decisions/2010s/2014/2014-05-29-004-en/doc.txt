Birth Centenary Programme of the late Professor Ediriweera Sarachchandra

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-29

- the proposal made by Hon. T.B Ekanayake, Minister of Culture and the Arts, to conduct several programmes on the occasion of the Birth Centenary of the late Professor Ediriweera Sarachchandra which falls on 03rd June 2014, was approved by the Cabinet.
