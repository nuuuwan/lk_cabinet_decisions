Moratuwa, Lunawa Urban Housing Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-23

- the contract for the construction of above low income housing scheme consisting of 356 housing units at Lunawa situated in the coastal belt of Moratuwa has been awarded to the State Engineering Corporation.   The proposal made  by Hon. Wimal Weerawansa, Minister of Construction, Engineering Services, Housing and Common Amenities,  to increase the cost estimate of the project due to additional items of work included, was approved by the Cabinet.
