Recommendations of the Finance Commission for the Financial Year 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-24

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, to give effect to the recommendations made by the Finance Commission for the Financial Year 2014, on apportionment of  funds to Provinces allocated by the Annual Budget and setting out other administrative measures that need to be adopted, and to present the said recommendations in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
