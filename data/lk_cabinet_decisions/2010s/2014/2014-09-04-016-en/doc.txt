Construction of a new building for the Mallavi Police Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-04

- Mallavi Police Station covers two Divisional Secretaries' Divisions and 23 Grama Niladhari Divisions and serves a population of 14,711.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Law and Order, to proceed with the related works, was approved by the Cabinet.
