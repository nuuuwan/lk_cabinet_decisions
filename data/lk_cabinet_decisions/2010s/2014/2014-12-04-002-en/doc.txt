Dutugemunu Pilgrims Rest established at Lumbini City, Nepal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-04

- the proposal made by Hon. D.M. Jayaratne, Minister of Buddha Sasana and Religious Affairs for the allocation of financial provisions of Rs.20.72 million by the General Treasury for the purchase of furniture and equipment to the Dutugemunu Pilgrims Rest at Lumbini City, Nepal for the use of visiting pilgrims, was approved by the Cabinet.
