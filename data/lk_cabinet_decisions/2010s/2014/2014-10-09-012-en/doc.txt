Tender for Design and Construction of Housing Project for Government Employees at Borella

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-09

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to award the tender for the design and construction of 608 housing units at a total cost of Rs.4,499,200,000.00 to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, as recommended by Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
