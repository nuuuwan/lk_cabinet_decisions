Northern Expressway Project - Award of Contracts for Consultancy Services for Construction Supervision of Contract Packages No.02 to 08

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-04

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Highways, Ports and Shipping, to award the contracts for providing consultancy services for construction supervision for Contract Packages 02 to 08, for the construction of the portion of the Northern Expressway from Veyangoda to Galewela and the portion of the Link Expressway from Pothuhera to Galagedara, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
