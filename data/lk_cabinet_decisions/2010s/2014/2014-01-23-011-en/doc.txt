Project for the Construction of 1000 Rural Bridges

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-23

- it is proposed to construct 1000 bridges in rural areas to establish connectivity for transportation and through this initiative, a large number of remote villages will be benefited  and in turn will provide easy access to market, education and healthcare facilities to villagers.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to enter into loan agreements with the relevant foreign Banks for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
