Jaffna - Killinochchi Water Supply and Sanitation Project - Construction of Water Towers at Puttur, Meesalai, Poonakari, Navatkuly, Kaddudai, Velani and Mandaitivu (Package 1)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-16

- Jaffna - Killinochchi Water Supply and Sanitation Project is being implemented by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board with assistance from the Asian Development Bank, Agence Francaise de Development (AFD) and the Government of Sri Lanka.  There are 13 major packages to be implemented for various components of this project and the above is one such package.   The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to award the above tender to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
