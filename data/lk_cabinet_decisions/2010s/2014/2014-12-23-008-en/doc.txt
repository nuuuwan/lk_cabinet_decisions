Construction of a Flyover Across the Railway Crossing at Maho Junction

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-12-23

- Nikaweratiya - Moragollagama - Siyambalangamuwa Road (B 326) crosses the Main Railway Line leading to Northern, North Central and Eastern Provinces at Maho town. Long delays to the movement of traffic on the above road are experienced due to frequent gate closures and congestion in the Maho town area. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Highways, Ports and Shipping, to award the contract for the purpose as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board (PAB), was approved by the Cabinet.
