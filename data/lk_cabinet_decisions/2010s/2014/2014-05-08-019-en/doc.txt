Construction of an Eight Storied Building for the ‘Medical Education Resource Centre (MERC)’ of the Postgraduate Institute of Medicine (PGIM), University of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-08

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayaka, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above contract to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder at a cost of Rs.142.7 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
