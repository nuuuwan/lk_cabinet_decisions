Vesting of State Land at Mount Lavinia for a Housing Scheme for Judicial Officers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-08-14

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to vest the block of State Land in an extent of 02 Roods and 07 Perches situated near the Mount Lavinia Court premises   to construct   a 12  storied building consisting of about 60 housing units, to be allocated to Judicial Officers on cost recovery basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
