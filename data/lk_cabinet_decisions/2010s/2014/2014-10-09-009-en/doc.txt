Establishment of a Fully Equipped Modern ICT Classroom as a Donation from the Ministry of Education of the Republic of Korea

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-10-09

- subsequent to the visit of H.E. the President and the Minister of Education Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, to South Korea, the Minister of Education of the Republic of Korea has consented to provide a fully equipped modern ICT Classroom to the Mahinda Rajapaksa College, Pitipana, Homagama, as a donation from the Republic of Korea.  The proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to proceed with the related activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
