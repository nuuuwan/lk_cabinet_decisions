Transferring a Block of Land to C/Devi Balika Vidyalaya, Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-01-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to transfer a block of land owned by the Department of Railways, to the Ministry of Education to provide more space to develop the sports activities of Devi Balika Vidyalaya, Colombo, was approved by the Cabinet.
