Obtaining Membership of International Seed Testing Association for Testing of the Quality of Local and Imported Seeds

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-04-22

- International Seed Testing Association is an Institution having global recognition which undertake seed testing and distribute its research outcome among member countries, providing opportunities for them to ensure uniformity in seed testing.  The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, for the payment of the Annual Membership Fee, was approved by the Cabinet.
