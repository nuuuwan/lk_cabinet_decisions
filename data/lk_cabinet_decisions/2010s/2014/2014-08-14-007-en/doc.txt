Construction of Stage II of the Ward Complex, Accident Service Unit, Surgical Theatre Complex of Teaching Hospital, Ragama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-08-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the construction of the eight storied building (Stage II of the project) and the contract for the completion of the rest of the construction work of the above (Stage III of the project) to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), was approved by the Cabinet.
