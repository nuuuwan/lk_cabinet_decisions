Construction of Sathipola Building at Horana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-17

- the Urban Development Authority (UDA) has prepared a Master Plan for the development of the Horana Town Centre and its surroundings and it comprises of the development of the Bus Stand, renovation of the Market, construction of the Sathipola etc.   It is proposed to develop the Sathipola to comprise of 400 stalls and with parking and other facilities.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to proceed with the relevant activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
