Amendments to the Intellectual Property Act, No.36 of 2003 to enhance the protection given for Geographical Indications of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-30

- the proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, Minister of Industry and Commerce, to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft amending legislation for the Intellectual Property Act, No.36 of 2003, to make regulations to safeguard the interests of the producers and exporters of Tea and Cinnamon, was approved by the Cabinet.
