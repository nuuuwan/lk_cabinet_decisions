Signing of the Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Culture and the Arts of the Government of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Culture of the Kingdom of Bahrain on Cooperation in the Field of Culture and Art

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-17

-  Cabinet granted covering approval for the already signed above Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries which was presented to the Cabinet by Hon. T.B. Ekanayake, Minister of Culture and the Arts.
