Memorandum of Understanding between the National University of Singapore, Institute of South Asian Studies and the Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute for International Relations and Strategic Studies (LKIIRSS), Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-02-13

- the objective of this Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is to promote joint research and development activities of mutual interest.  The proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, for the Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute for International Relations and Strategic Studies of Sri Lanka to enter into a MoU with the National University of Singapore, Institute of South Asian Studies, was approved by the Cabinet.
