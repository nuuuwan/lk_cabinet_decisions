Establishment of Screening Clinics for screening and treatment of Chronic Kidney Disease of unknown etiology

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-05-08

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to establish Chronic Kidney Disease Screening Clinics in the relevant hospitals at Padaviya, Aranaganvila, Bakamoona, Hettipola, Padavi Sri Pura, Dehiattakandiya, Wellawaya, Tissamaharama, Vavuniya South (Mamamaduwa), Sampath Nuwara and Tangalle, and to award the contract for the construction of the above to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), was approved by the Cabinet.
