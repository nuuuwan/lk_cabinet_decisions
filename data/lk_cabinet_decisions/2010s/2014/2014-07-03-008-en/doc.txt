Enhancement of Scope of Work of the Projects Implemented under ADB and JICA Loans by Utilizing the Balance Funds of Loans

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-07-03

- the proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Power and Energy, to enhance the Scope of Work of the Projects Implemented under Asian Development Bank (ADB) and Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) loans, in view of the savings generated by awarding the contracts at lessor prices than the cost estimates, due to competition, was approved by the Cabinet.
