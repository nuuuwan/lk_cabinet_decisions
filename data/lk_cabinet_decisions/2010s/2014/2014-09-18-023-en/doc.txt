Bilateral Agreement between Sri Lanka and the People’s Republic of  China

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2014-09-18

- the Treaty on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters between  Sri Lanka and the People’s Republic of  China provides for the Parties to grant each other  mutual legal assistance in criminal matters, including investigations, prosecutions, trials and resulting proceedings. The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to sign and ratify the above Agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
