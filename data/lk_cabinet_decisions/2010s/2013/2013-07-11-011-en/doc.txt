Construction of Elephant Pass Station in the name of "Senehasaka Thotupala" with the contribution of students and teachers of Government Schools

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to construct  the  Elephant Pass Railway Station, comprising of a passenger terminal, rest rooms for passengers, restaurant etc., with the concurrence of the Ministry of Transport, utilizing funds amounting to Rs.15.5 million being contributions made by the  teachers and students under the purview of the Ministry of Education, was approved by the Cabinet.
