Renovation of  Dutugemunu Pilgrims' Rest  at Lumbini, Nepal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-18

- the proposal made by Hon. D.M. Jayaratne, Minister of Buddha Sasana and Religious Affairs, to provide an additional allocation of Rs.25 million to complete the renovation work of the above Pilgrims' Rest, for the benefit of the pilgrims of Sri Lanka visiting Nepal, was approved by the Cabinet.
