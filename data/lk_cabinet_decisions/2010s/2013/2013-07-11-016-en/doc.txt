Financial  assistance  from  the  Asian  Development  Bank (ADB) for  the implementation of the Southern Road Connectivity Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

a loan of US$ 75 million (approximately 9.6 billion) is to be obtained from the Asian Development Bank for the improvements of 5 access roads to the Southern Expressway.  In addition 33.5 km of the following 5 National Highways linking to 3 interchanges of the Southern Expressway are to be improved under this project:*    Moratuwa - Piliyandala - 2.5 km*     Ratmalana - Mirihana   - 2.1 km*    Southern Expressway  - Mathugama - 12.4 km*    Kirulapona - Homagama - 2.1 km*    Kesbewa - Pokunuwita  -  14.4 kmThe above proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, was approved by the Cabinet.
