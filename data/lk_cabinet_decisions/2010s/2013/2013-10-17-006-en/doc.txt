Construction of Four Storied Lecture Hall Building to cater to the additional student intake of the Faculty of Management Studies and Commerce, University of Sri Jayawardenapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-10-17

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education for the construction of a  four storeyed building for the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, for the above purpose at a total cost of Rs.137.30 million, and to complete the construction work within the stipulated time frame, was approved by the Cabinet.
