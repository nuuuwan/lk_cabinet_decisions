Signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the National Zoological Gardens of Sri Lanka and the Pretoria Zoological Gardens in the Republic of South Africa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-06-20

the intention of this MoU is to lay a foundation for collaboration, exchange of ideas and support in the fields of animal husbandry, public presentation of zoological species, related research, veterinary and keeper  training. The proposal made by Hon. Jayaratne Herath, Minister of Botanical Gardens and Public Recreation, to enter into the above Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
