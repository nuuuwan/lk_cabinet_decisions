Construction of a New Building adjoining the existing building in the premises of the General Treasury

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-25

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to Construct a New Building adjoining the existing building in the premises of the General Treasury with the view to providing additional accommodation needed, to commence the construction work with the financial provision of Rs.575 million allocated in the Budget Estimate for 2013 and the balance provision to be provided in 2014 and 2015, was approved by the Cabinet.
