Release of land for Sewer Treatment Plant for Kandy City Waste-water Management Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- a Waste-water Treatment System has been planned to serve the Kandy City Centre, Kandy Lake Round, Peradeniya - Kandy Road, William Gopallawa Mawatha and Mahaiyawa covering an area of 733 hectares. The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to release the identified block of land to the National Water Supply and Drainage Board to construct the proposed Sewer Treatment Plant for the Kandy City Waste-water Management Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
