Allocation of a block Land at Bibile Town Centre to the Bibile Pradeshiya Sabha

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-14

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to allocate a block of land at the Center of Bibile Town belonging to the Urban Development Authority, to the Bibile Pradesiya Sabha, for the purpose of the development of the said land in keeping with the Bibile Town Development Plan, was approved by the Cabinet.
