Proposed Pola Development Projects at Padukka, Delkanda and Embilipitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to develop the existing Padukka Pola and the Embilipitiya Pola and to relocate the existing Delkanda Pola which is currently functioning either side of the High Level Road, at Delkanda area for the benefit of the public and the vendors, was approved by the Cabinet.
