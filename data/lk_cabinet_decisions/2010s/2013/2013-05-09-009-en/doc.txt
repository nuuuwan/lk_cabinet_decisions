International Conference on Natural Fibre, proposed to be held in Sri Lanka in collaboration with the World Food and Agriculture Organization

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

Hon. Jagath Pushpakumara, Minister of Coconut Development and Janatha Estate Development  informed the Cabinet of Ministers through a Note submitted by him, that a request has been received from the World Food and Agriculture Organization to hold the above Conference in Sri Lanka during the 1st week of November, 2013 and that the said Conference will be held as scheduled.  The cost of air travel and hotel accommodation will be borne by the international participants themselves.
