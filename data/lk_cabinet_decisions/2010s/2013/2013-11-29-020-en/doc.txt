The Convention on National Unity in Diversity to be held at the BMICH on 04th March 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- having overcome the conflicts that constrained the development of Sri Lankan nationality based on the unity in diversity, numerous programes are being implemented by Government institutions towards strengthening the feeling of belongingness as one nation. Hon. Vasudeva Nanayakkara, Minister of National Languages and Social Integration informed the Cabinet of Ministers that his Ministry intends to convene a Convention in National Unity in Diversity at the BMICH  on March 04, 2014 with the participation of experts in various segments of the society.
