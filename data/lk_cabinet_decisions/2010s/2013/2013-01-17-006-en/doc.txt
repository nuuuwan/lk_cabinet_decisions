Construction of Two Hostels for 100 Female students and 100 Male students 	of the Vavuniya Campus at Pampeimadu, Administered by the Jaffna University - Stage II

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-01-17

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education to construct a two storyed building at the above campus to accommodate 100 male students and 100 female students, at a total estimated cost of Rs.229 million, in keeping with the increased intake of students to universities, with funds allocated under the Medium Term Budgetary Framework, was approved by the Cabinet.  The project is intended to be completed in 2014.
