Establishment of a National College of Education for Entrepreneurship and Management Studies which offers the Course in teaching the subject of Entrepreneurship Studies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to upgrade the Teachers' College at Maharagama to a National College of Education for Entrepreneurship and Management Studies in a manner that the Course in teaching Commerce can be conducted to provide training in teaching the subject of Entrepreneurship Studies, was approved by the Cabinet.
