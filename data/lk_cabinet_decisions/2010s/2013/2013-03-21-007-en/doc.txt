Construction of a New Two Storied Building for Divisional  Secretariat,  Medawachchiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the Divisional Secretary's Division consists of 37 Grama Niladhari Divisions and the total population is 50141.  With the view to providing better facilities to the people in the area, the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, to construct the above new building at a total cost of Rs.24.96 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
