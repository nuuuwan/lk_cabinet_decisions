Construction of pre-cast two storied lecture halls for the Vavuniya Campus at Pampaimadu - University of Jaffna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to construct pre-cast two storied lecture hall buildings at a total cost of Rs.59.37 million, to commence the project in 2013 and complete the construction work within six (06) months, to meet the additional accommodation facilities required for the increased student intake, was approved by the Cabinet.
