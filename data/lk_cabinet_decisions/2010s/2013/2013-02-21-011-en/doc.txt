Credit Agreement with Hatton National Bank PLC to finance the Construction of 210 Steel Bridges in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-21

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to enter into a loan agreement with the Hatton National Bank to borrow US  Dollars equivalent to Sterling Pounds 6,779,243 for the above project, was approved by the Cabinet.
