The 5th South Asian Conference on Sanitation (SACOSAN-V) from 21st to 24th  October, 2013

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-22

- this Conference is scheduled to be held from 21st to 24th  October, 2013 in Nepal and will be attended by the Ministers of Water Supply and Drainage; Health; and Education and Government Officials.  The representatives from the Civil Society, Private Sector Institutions,  Academia and School Children from the South Asian Region viz. Pakistan, India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Afghanistan, including Sri Lanka are expected to participate in this International Conference.   Cabinet noted the above information provided by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of  Water Supply and Drainage in this regard.
