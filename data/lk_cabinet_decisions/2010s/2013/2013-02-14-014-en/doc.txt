Purchase of the Building for the Sri Lanka Embassy in Oman

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-14

- it has been observed that nearly 75,000 Sri Lankan migrant workers are employed in Oman at present. The proposal made by Hon. Dilan Perera, Minister of Foreign Employment Promotion and Welfare, to purchase the building occupied by the Sri Lanka Embassy in Oman, was approved by the Cabinet.
