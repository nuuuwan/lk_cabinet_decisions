Rehabilitation of the Old Electrical System, University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to rehabilitate the electrical system of the University of Peradeniya at a cost of Rs.56.4 million, to commence the project in 2013 and complete same within two years, was approved by the Cabinet.
