Implementation of the Second Health Sector Development Project funded by the World Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-15

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, for the implementation of the Second Health Sector Development Project at National and Provincial levels, utilizing US $200 million to be received from the International Development Association (IDA) for a five (05) year period from 2013 to 2017, was approved by the Cabinet.
