Upgrading of Karagahathenna FM Transmission Station of the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-10-10

- the proposal made by Hon. Keheliya Rambukwella, Minister of Mass Media and Information to upgrade the Karagahathenna FM Transmission Station of the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation so that the quality of transmission in the North Central, North Western, Central and Eastern Provinces could be improved to a great extent, was approved by the Cabinet.
