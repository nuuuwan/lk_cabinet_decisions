Tender for the Supply of 25,000 Vials of Normal Immunoglobulin for Intravenous Use BP 5g-6g Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-07

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the tender for the supply of 25,000 Vials of the above to the lowest responsive bidder for a sum of US $3,941,000.00 (LKR 521,803,767.50) was approved by the Cabinet.
