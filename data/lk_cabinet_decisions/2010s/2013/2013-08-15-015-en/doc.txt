State Visit by H.E. President of Sri Lanka to Seychelles

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-15

-  Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs  furnished information pertaining to the recent visit of H.E. the President to  Seychelles and covering approval was granted for the Agreements / MoUs concluded between the two countries during the visit.   the leaders of the two countries welcomed the signing of MoUs / Agreements on Bilateral Air Services, Vocational Training and Youth Development.  They also welcomed the enhanced collaboration between the two countries in a broad range of areas such as education, fisheries, trade and investment, judiciary and health.
