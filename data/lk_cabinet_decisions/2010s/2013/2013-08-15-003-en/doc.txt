Amendments to the Tertiary and Vocational Education Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-15

- the proposal made by Hon. Dullas Alahapperuma, Minister of Youth Affairs and Skills Development, to instruct the Legal Draftsman, to draft amending legislation to the Tertiary and Vocational Education Act, No.20 of 1990, as proposed in the Memorandum, subject to incorporating the amendments recommended by the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Legislation, was approved by the Cabinet.
