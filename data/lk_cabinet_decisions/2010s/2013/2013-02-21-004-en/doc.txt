Vesting of legal ownership of the blocks of land situated at No. 18 and No.20, Ward Place, Colombo 07, in the Ministry of Higher Education and the University Grants Commission

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-21

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to vest the ownership of the premises No.18 and No.20 of Ward Place, Colombo 07 and the buildings presently occupied by the Ministry of Higher Education and the University Grants Commission, in the said Ministry and the Commission respectively, was approved by the Cabinet.
