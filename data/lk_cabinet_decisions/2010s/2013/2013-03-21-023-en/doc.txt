Vesting of Deniyaya Bus Stand in the Kotapola Pradeshiya Sabha as a Free Grant

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to allocate the block of land where the Deniyaya Bus Stand is situated, to the Kotapola Pradeshiya Sabha as a free grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
