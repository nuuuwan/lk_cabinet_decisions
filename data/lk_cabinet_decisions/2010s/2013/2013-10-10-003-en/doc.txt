Eastern Province Water Supply Development Project - Tender for laying of PE Pipes, Fittings, DI/CI Valves & Accessories for Distribution System of Koneshpuram & Dadayanthalawa Areas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-10-10

- the above project is being implemented  by  the  National  Water Supply and Drainage Board with Government of  Sri  Lanka  contribution  and  funds  provided  by  the  Japan  International  Co-operation Agency (JICA).  The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to award the above tender as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
