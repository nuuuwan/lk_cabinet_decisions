Establishment of 04 New National Schools in Kilinochchi and Mullaitivu Districts

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to grant the status of National Schools to Kilinochchi M.M.V. and Mulangavil M.M.V. in the Kilinochchi District and Vidiyananda College and M/Mallavi M.M.V. in the Mullaitivu District and to develop them through absorbing into the 1000 Secondary Schools Development Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
