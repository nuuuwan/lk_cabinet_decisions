Construction of Four Storied Para Clinical Building - Stage II, for the Faculty of Medicine, University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to construct the above four storied building, to commence the construction work with Rs.200 million already provided and to complete the work with funds to be provided under the Medium Term Budgetary Framework - 2014, was approved by the Cabinet.
