Cooperation Agreement between the Sri Lanka Export Development Board of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the National Centre for Marketing & Price Study of the Republic of Belarus

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- with the objective of further enhancing the existing trade and economic relations between the two countries, covering approval  of the Cabinet of Ministers was granted to the proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, Minister of Industry and Commerce for signing of the above Agreement.
