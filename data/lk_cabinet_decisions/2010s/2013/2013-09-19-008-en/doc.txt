Resurfacing and Improvements to Baseline Road from Kelanitissa Roundabout to Kanatta Roundabout (0.00 km - 4.80 km)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-09-19

the Section of the Baseline Road from Kelanitissa Roundabout to Kanatta Roundabout (0.00 km - 4.80 km) has a dual carriageway with six traffic lanes.  This section of the road requires resurfacing urgently in order to provide a better service.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to proceed with the work relating to the above, was approved by the Cabinet.
