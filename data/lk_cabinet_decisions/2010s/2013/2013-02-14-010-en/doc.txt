Construction of Proposed Building (Stage II) for the Department of Computer Science and Computer Unit of Faculty of Science, University of Ruhuna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-14

- Stage I of the project has already been completed for a total cost of Rs.63 million.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above construction work of Stage II of the project to the lowest evaluated bidder for a sum of Rs.179,463,752.94 as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
