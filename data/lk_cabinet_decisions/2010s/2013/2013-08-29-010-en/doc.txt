Memorandum of Understanding for Agricultural Co-operation to be signed between the Republic of Turkey and the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-29

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, to sign the above Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) during the visit to Sri Lanka of the Minister of Food, Agriculture and Livestock of the Republic of Turkey in October 2013, was approved by the Cabinet.
