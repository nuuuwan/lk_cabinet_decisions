Protocol on Cooperation between the Ministry of External Affairs of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Azerbaijan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-18

-  the objective of this protocol is to further strengthen and consolidate the close and friendly relations between Sri Lanka and Azerbaijan.  The proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to sign the above protocol between the Ministry of External Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Azerbaijan, was approved by the Cabinet.
