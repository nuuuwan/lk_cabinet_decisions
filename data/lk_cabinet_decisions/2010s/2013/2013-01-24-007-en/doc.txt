Programme to Develop thousand secondary schools with five thousand primary schools network - Construction of Mahindodaya Technical Laboratories

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-01-24

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to award the construction of Mahindodaya Technical Laboratories to 400 schools among the remaining schools where the said laboratories are expected to be constructed under Phase II of the thousand secondary schools programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
