Dry Zone Urban Water and Sanitation Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-18

- Tender for Supply and Laying of  HDPE, DI, PVC Pipes, Specials, Fittings and Valves for Transmission Main and Distribution System in Puttalam Water Supply Scheme - this Project is being implemented by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board, with funds provided by the Asian Development Bank and the Government of Sri Lanka.  The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to award the contract for the supply of above items as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
