Entrusting the Printing of Required School Text Books to Private Printing Institutions to be Distributed for the year - 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to entrust the printing of 27,611,500 copies of 315 Textbook categories to 21 Printing Institutions at a cost of Rs.1,587,958,705.00, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC),  for distribution among school children and student monks   for the year 2014, was approved by the Cabinet.
