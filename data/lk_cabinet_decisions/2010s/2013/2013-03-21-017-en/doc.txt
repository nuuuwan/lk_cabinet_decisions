Construction of a Hostel Complex for female students - Eastern University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- with the view to providing more accommodation facilities for the female students of the above University, the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above contract at a total cost of Rs.262 million, to commence the project in 2013 and complete same within a period of 18 months, was approved by the Cabinet.
