Memorandum of Understanding between Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute for International Relations and Strategic Studies (LKIIRSS), Sri Lanka and Africa Institute of South Africa (AISA), South Africa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-18

- for the purpose of improving the level of scientific and academic co-operation of the two Institutes and intensifying the friendship between the two countries, the proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to sign and ratify the above Memorandum of Understanding and to proceed to take all necessary steps for the effective implementation of same, was approved by the Cabinet.
