Lease of UDA land at Ratnapura New Town to Geological Survey and Mines Bureau

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-07

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to lease out a block of land in the Ratnapura New Town to the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau for a period of 30 years for the construction of its Regional Office, was approved by the Cabinet.
