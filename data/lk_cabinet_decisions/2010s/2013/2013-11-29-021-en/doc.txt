Fifth South Asian Conference on Sanitation (SACOSAN)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, informed the Cabinet with regard to the representations made by the delegation from the Sri Lanka led by him at the above Conference which was held in Kathmandu, Nepal recently.  SACOSAN is a Government led biennial Convention held on rotation basis in each SAARC country providing a platform for interaction on sanitation.
