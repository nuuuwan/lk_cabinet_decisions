Rehabilitation and upgrading of Muttetugala - Hiripitiya Road  (B300) from 0 to 18.50 km

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity  as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to include this Section of the Muttetugala - Hiripitiya Road in the Programme for the Rehabilitation of National Highways through domestic contractors and to award the above contract as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and to seek funding for the purpose from local banks, was approved by the Cabinet.
