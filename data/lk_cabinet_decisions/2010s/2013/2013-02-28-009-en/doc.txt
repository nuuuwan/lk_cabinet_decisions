Construction of a new Three Storied Building for Divisional Secretariat, Hatharaliyadda

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-28

- this Divisional Secretary's Division in the Kandy District consists of 57 Grama Niladhari Divisions and the total population is 29,866.  The proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, to construct a new three storied Building for this Divisional Secretariat, at a total cost of Rs.30 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
