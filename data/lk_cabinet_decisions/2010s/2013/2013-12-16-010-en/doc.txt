Purchase of 02 Ships for the Ceylon Shipping Corporation Limited under  Phase - 1 of the Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-12-16

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Highways, Ports and Shipping, to purchase two (02)  Bulk Carriers for the Ceylon Shipping Corporation, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee (CANC) and to instruct the Director General of the Department of External Resources to finalize formalities of loan arrangements, was approved by the Cabinet.
