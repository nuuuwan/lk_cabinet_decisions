Agreement between the Ministry of External Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Romania on Bilateral Consultations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-18

-  the proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to sign the above Agreement between the Ministry of External Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Romania.  The objectives of the proposed Agreement is to further develop and strengthen the close and friendly relationship between the two countries.
