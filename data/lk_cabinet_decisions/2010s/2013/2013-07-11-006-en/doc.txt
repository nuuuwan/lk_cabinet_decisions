Town Centre Development at Kegalle

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

Relocation of SLTB Bus Depot and Development of Central Bus Stand - the existing bus stands are located in 03 locations creating traffic congestion in the Kegalle town.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to shift the existing bus depot to the land identified in Paragammana and the proposed bus stand to be developed in the existing depot land, to provide better facilities to the general public in Kegalle town and to allocate funds for the purpose, from 2013 to 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
