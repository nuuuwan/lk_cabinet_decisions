Development of the Faculty of Agriculture according to the Master Plan  - University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- a Master Plan has been prepared with the assistance of the World Bank to develop the above Faculty and it has been proposed to develop the infrastructure facilities of this Faculty under five phases in order to meet the present day requirement.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the above contract at a total cost of Rs.1378 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
