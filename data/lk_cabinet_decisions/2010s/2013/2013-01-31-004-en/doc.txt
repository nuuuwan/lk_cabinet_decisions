Amendment to the Tertiary and Vocational Education Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-01-31

- since many changes have taken place in the field of Tertiary and Vocational Education during the last two decades, the proposal made by Hon. Dullas Alahapperuma, Minister of Youth Affairs and Skills Development, to make necessary amendments to the Tertiary and Vocational Education Act, No.20 of 1990, was approved by the Cabinet.
