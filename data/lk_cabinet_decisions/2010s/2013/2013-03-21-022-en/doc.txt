Memorandum of Understanding  (MOU) between the Ministry of Environment of the Republic of Czechoslovakia and the Ministry  of Environment and Renewable Energy of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the main objective of this MOU is to facilitate the joint project implementation, exchange of technology, information and experience in the field of environment including renewable energy. The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, Minister of Environment and Renewable Energy, to sign the above Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
