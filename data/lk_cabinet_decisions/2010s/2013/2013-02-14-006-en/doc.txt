Tender for supply of 44.5 Million of Locally Manufactured Unsterile Latex Examination Gloves on Long Term Agreement (for year 2013-2014)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the above tender to the only responsive domestic bidder, for a total cost of LKR 199,293,873.00, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
