Providing Training Opportunities for the Foreign Officers at the Defence Services Command and Staff College

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-01

- this College was inaugurated in 2007 and provides training facilities to Middle Level Officers of the three Armed Forces and the Police.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to provide opportunities to personnel of similar status in countries such as India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Maldives, Nepal, China, Malaysia, Indonesia, Rwanda, Senagal and Sudan for  training at this college, was approved by the Cabinet.
