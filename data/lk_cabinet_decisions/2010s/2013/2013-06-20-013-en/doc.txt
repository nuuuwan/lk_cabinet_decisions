Procurement of 01 No. 1.5 Tesla MRI System for Military Hospital at Narahenpita

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-06-20

the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to purchase the above MRI system and other related accessories for the 10 storied hospital building of the Sri Lanka Army at Narahenpita, for an approximate total cost of Rs.192,821,590.00, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), to provide better facilities for personnel in the Army Forces, was approved by the Cabinet.
