Supply, Delivery, Installation and Maintenance of a number 03  Tesla MRI Scanner Machines for Anuradhapura General Hospital as repeat order

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, for the purchase of a MRI Scanner machine for the Anuradhapura General Hospital and to award the contract, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
