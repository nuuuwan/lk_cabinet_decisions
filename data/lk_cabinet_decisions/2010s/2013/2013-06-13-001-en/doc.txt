Allocation of Land at Vauxhall Street, Colombo 02 to Lanka Mahila Samithi as Provision of Alternative Accommodation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-06-13

the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development to allocate a land at Vauxhall Street, Colombo 02, to the Lanka Mahila Samithi on 30 year lease basis, for the construction of its Office and Sales Outlet, was approved by the Cabinet.
