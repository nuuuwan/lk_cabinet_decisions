Rehabilitation of the Road Section from Ma-oya Bridge to Elephant Orphanage at Pinnawala in Kegalle - Rambukkana Main Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-10-17

- the proposal made by Hon. Jayaratne Herath, Minister of Botanical Gardens and Public Recreation, to proceed with the activities relating to the rehabilitation of the above road section for the benefit of the domestic as well as foreign tourists who visit the elephant orphanage at Pinnawala in Kegalle, was approved by the Cabinet.
