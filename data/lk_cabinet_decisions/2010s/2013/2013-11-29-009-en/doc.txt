Establishment of a Sri Lanka Resident Diplomatic Mission in Kabul, Afghanistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- Sri Lanka and Afghanistan have maintained close bilateral relations for several decades.  These historical relations between the two countries have been further enhanced with Afghanistan's entry into the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC) in 2007.  The proposal made by Hon. (Prof) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to proceed with the work relating to the establishment of a Sri Lanka Diplomatic Mission in Kabul, Afghanistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
