Memorandum of Understanding between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh to train Bangladeshi Nurses and Healthcare Workers in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-11

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to enter into the above Memorandum of Understanding to provide training for Nursing Officers and Healthcare Workers of Bangladesh in selected training institutions in Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
