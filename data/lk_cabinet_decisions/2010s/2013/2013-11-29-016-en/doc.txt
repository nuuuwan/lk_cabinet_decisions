Memorandum of Understanding between the Governments of Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and People’s Republic of China in the Field of Labour

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- the objective of this Memorandum of Understanding is to strengthen the mutual relationship between the two countries and to promote bilateral exchange and co-operation in the field of labour policy.  The proposal made by Hon. Gamini Lokuge, Minister of Labour and Labour Relations, to sign the above Memorandum of Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
