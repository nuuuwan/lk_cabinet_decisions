Enhancement of Scope of Work of the Projects Implemented under ADB and JICA Loans by Utilizing the Balance Funds of Loans

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-12-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Power and Energy, to utilize the balance funds of 03 loans provided by the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and 01 loan provided by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), for related activities of Grid Substations of Chunnakam, Polonnaruwa, Moneragala, Valachchenai, Maho, Naula, Puttalam, Kilinochchi, Vavuniya, Kelaniya and Kiribathkumbura, with the consent of the donor agencies and to proceed with the relevant activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
