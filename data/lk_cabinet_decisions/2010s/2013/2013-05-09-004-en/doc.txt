Government of Sri Lanka Presidential Scholarship Programme for Foreign Students

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

Cabinet approval has already been granted to implement the scheme of granting 100 scholarships by the Ministry of Higher Education for Foreign Students in 48 identified countries from the year 2011.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to increase the facilities offered for foreign students and also to include 10 new countries namely, Oman, Uganda, Ethiopia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Iceland, Swaziland, Seychelles and Burkina Faso, to be eligible for the above scholarships, was approved by the Cabinet.
