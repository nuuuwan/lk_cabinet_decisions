Construction of Proposed Six Storied Building Complex for the Lecture Halls and Examination Halls (Stage II), University of Kelaniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-14

- Stage I of the above project has been completed at a cost of Rs.40 million.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the tender for the construction of Stage II of the project to the lowest evaluated bidder for a sum of Rs.246,005,998.18, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
