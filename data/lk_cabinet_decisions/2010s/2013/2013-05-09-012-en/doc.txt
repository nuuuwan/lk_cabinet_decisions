Establishment of Clinical Waste Management Systems in the Needy Hospitals in Sri Lanka under Australian Assistance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

considering the need for the implementation of a long felt need of safe practice of healthcare waste management in hospitals and since this project has been identified as a priority requirement of the Ministry of Health which will provide a total solution for the proper management of clinical waste in the country, the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to proceed with the project as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
