Release of confiscated sand and timber to religious places of worship

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to release sand and timber confiscated by relevant authorities at a concessionary rate to places of religious worship subject to a maximum limit of 10 cubes of sand and 100 cubic feet of timber per place of religious worship, for renovation work, on requests made by them, and the balance quantities of sand and timber to the Government construction institutions, was approved by the Cabinet.
