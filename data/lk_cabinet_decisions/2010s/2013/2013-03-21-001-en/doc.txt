Agreement between the Republic of Ukraine and the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on Co-operation in the field of tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the proposal made by Hon. Basil Rajapaksa, Minister of Economic Development, to give effect to the above Agreement entered into between the Republic of Ukraine and the Government of Sri Lanka, on Co-operation in the field of tourism between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
