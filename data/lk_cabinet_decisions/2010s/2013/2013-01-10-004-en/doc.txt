Granting permanent  appointments to Trainee Graduates as per the Service Minute of Development Officers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-01-10

- on the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, Cabinet approval was granted to grant permanent  appointments to Graduate Trainees in terms of the Service Minute of Development Officers, for posts in Government institutions approved by the Department of Management Services of the Ministry of Finance and Planning.
