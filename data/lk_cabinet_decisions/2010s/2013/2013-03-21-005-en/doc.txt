Dry Zone Urban Water and Sanitation Project - Tender for Surface Water Development in Vavuniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the above project is being implemented by the National Water Supply and Drainage Board with the contributions from the Government of Sri Lanka and loan funds provided by the Asian Development Bank.  The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to award the above contract as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
