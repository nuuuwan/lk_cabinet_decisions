Construction of a Pre-Cast Building for the Faculty of Commerce and Management - University of Kelaniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to commence construction work of the above three storied building in 2013 at a total cost of Rs.25 million and to entrust the work to the State Engineering Corporation, was approved by the Cabinet.
