Construction of four storied building for the expansion of Canteen and Students facilities for the Faculty of Science, University of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to construct the above building and to commence construction work with funds provided for 2013 and complete the work with funds to be provided under the Medium Term Budgetary Framework 2014-2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
