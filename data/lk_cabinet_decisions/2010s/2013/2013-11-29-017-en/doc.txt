Interest Free Loan of RMB Yuan 200 million from the People’s Republic of China

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- with the view to further strengthening friendly relations and economic and technical co-operation between the two countries, the Government of the People's Republic of China has agreed to provide an interest free loan of Yuan 200 million to the Government of Sri Lanka. The loan will be utilized to implement priority projects to be agreed upon through consultations between the two Governments. The proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the  Minister of Finance and Planning, to enter  into an Agreement for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
