Awarding of Six Contracts under the Programme funded by the Local Banks for the Rehabilitation of National Highways through Domestic Contractors - Stage 2

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-15

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to award the following contracts for rehabilitation/widening/improvements to National Highways as recommended by the  Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet:*    Hasalaka - Handungamuwa - Hettipola Road (B 484) from 0.00 km to 20.00 km;*    Kelanimulla - Mulleriyawa - Koswatta Road - Phase I - construction of a viaduct and approach from 0.00 km to 0.60 km;*    Kurunegala -Narammala - Madampe Road from 22.26 km to 40.87 km;*    Seeduwa - Katunayake Road from 0.00 km to 3.90 km and Seeduwa to Udugampola from 0.00 km to 11.70 km;*    Jaffna - Ponnalai - Point Pedro Road 1.5 km between 28.5 km to 31.5 km;*    Road Network associated with development of Defence Headquarters Project at Akuregoda.
