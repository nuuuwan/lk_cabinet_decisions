Construction of a new three storied building for the Divisional Secretariat, Bulathsinhala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-06-20

this Divisional Secretary's Division consists of 54 Grama Niladhari Divisions and the total population is 69,902. The proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, for the construction of a three storied building at a total cost of Rs.79.9 million, with funds to be provided under the Medium Term Budgetary Framework 2014 - 2016, to provide sufficient facilities for the people in the area and the staff, was approved by the Cabinet.
