Construction of the Building for Faculty of Applied Sciences - Final Phase - Wayamba University of Sri Lanka, Kuliyapitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissasnayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award the construction work of the above at a total cost of Rs.367 million, to commence this project in 2013 and complete same within two years, was approved by the Cabinet.
