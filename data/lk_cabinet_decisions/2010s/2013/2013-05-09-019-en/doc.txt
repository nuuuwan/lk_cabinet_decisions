Establishment of a Resident Diplomatic Mission of Sri Lanka in Kampala, Uganda

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

in accordance with the Government's policy on enhancing relations with Africa, there is a need to have a Resident Diplomatic Mission of Sri Lanka in Uganda and accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to establish the said Diplomatic Mission of Sri Lanka in Kampala, Uganda with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
