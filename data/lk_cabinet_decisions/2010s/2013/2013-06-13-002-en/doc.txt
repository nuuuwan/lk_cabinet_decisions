Allocation of Land at D.M. Colombage Mawatha, Kirulapone to the persons displaced due to the Baseline Road Widening Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-06-13

the Road Development Authority (RDA) has utilized a land in an extent of about 3 acres belonging to the Urban Development Authority (UDA), to re-locate the families who were displaced due to the Baseline Road Widening Project. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to allocate the said land to the RDA, to enable it to execute the transfer of deeds in favour of the allottees who were displaced due to the Baseline Road Widening Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
