Proposed Agreement between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Republic of Belarus on Trade and Economic Co-operation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-22

- the objective of this Agreement is to further enhance the existing trade and economic relations between the two countries.  The proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, Minister of Industry and Commerce, to sign the above Agreement during the forthcoming visit of H.E. the President of Sri Lanka to Belarus, was approved by the Cabinet.
