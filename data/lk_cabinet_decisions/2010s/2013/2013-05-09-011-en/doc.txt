Southern Transport Development Project - leasing out Lot No.1 and Lot No.9 in the Rest Areas for the establishment of Fuel Filling Stations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to lease out the above blocks of land  in the Rest Areas at the 44th Km. on the Southern Expressway to two separate parties selected on competitive basis, for a lease period of 30 years, for the establishment of Fuel Filling Stations, was approved by the Cabinet.
