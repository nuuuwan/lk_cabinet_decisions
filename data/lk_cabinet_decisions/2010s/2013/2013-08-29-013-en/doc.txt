Rehabilitation of 2x37.5 MW Samanala Power Station - Polpitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-29

- the proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Pavithra Wanniarachchi, Minister of Power and Energy, to authorize the Ceylon Electricity Board to proceed with the International Competitive Bidding process to select a contractor for the rehabilitation of Samanala Power Station and with matters connected thereto, was approved by the Cabinet.
