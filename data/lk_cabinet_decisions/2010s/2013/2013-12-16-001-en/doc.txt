Allocation of Land at Hulftsdorp to the Attorney General's Department

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-12-16

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to allocate a vacant plot of land around 80 perches in extent situated at Hulftsdorp, Colombo 12, owned by the Urban Development Authority, to the Attorney General's Department, as the existing space in the buildings is inadequate to meet the present requirements of the Department, was approved by the Cabinet.
