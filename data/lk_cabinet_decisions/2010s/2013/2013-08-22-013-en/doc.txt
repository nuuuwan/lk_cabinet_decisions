Bilateral Agreement and Treaty between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Republic of Belarus

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-08-22

- the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to sign the above Agreement and the Treaty on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal matters between Sri Lanka and Belarus during the forthcoming visit of H.E. the President of Sri Lanka to Belarus, was approved by the Cabinet.
