Procurement of Fertilizer for the Maha Season 2013/2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-12-16

- the two proposals made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, for the award of the relevant contract for the procurement of fertilizer for the Maha Season 2013/2014 as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committees (SCAPCs) subject to the stipulations made by the General Treasury, was approved by the Cabinet.
