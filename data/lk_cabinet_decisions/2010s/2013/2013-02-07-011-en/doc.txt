Repeal of the Sri Lanka Institute of Development Administration Act, No.9 of 	1982 and drafting of a new Bill for the purpose

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-07

- the proposal made by Hon.	W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, to	instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft legislation to repeal the Sri Lanka Institute of 	Development Administration Act, No.9 of 1982 and to draft a new Bill	incorporating all necessary amendments, was approved by the Cabinet.
