Construction   of   a  Fly-over   across   Maho  level  crossing

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-01-24

- Nikaweratiya  -Moragollagama-Siyambalangamuwa Road crosses the Main Railway Line leading to Northern, North Central and Eastern Provinces, in Maho town and long delays to the movement of traffic on the above road are experienced due to frequent gate closures.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to proceed with the initial activities relating to the above project, was approved by the Cabinet.
