Deyata Kirula National Exhibition 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

this exhibition which is to be commenced on 4th February 2014, will be held at the premises of Wayamba University, Technical College and Central College of Kuliyapitiya. Permanent buildings to suit the needs of the exhibition and which could be used by these educational institutions after the exhibition, are to be constructed in these premises.  The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, Minister of Telecommunication and Information Technology, to proceed with the relevant activities in association with other government institutions involved, was approved by the Cabinet.
