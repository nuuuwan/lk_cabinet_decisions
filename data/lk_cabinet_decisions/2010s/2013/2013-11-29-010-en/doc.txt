Transfer of a Land for the Hostel of Mahinda Rajapaksa Model National School, Pitipana, Homagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- this is  the first Model National School constructed under the Programme of Developing 1000 Secondary Schools and at present, the student population of the school is 2447 and the majority of the students travel to school from distant places.  It has been observed that it is essential to provide residential facilities to students as well as teachers who travel to the school from distant places.  The proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to transfer the identified block of land owned by Lanka Salusala Ltd., to the Ministry of Education to be utilized for the fulfillment of the proposed residential requirement of the school, was approved by the Cabinet.
