Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy (CARP) and Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-01-03

- the above MoU between Bangladesh and Sri Lanka has already been signed by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs on behalf of Sri Lanka to coincide with the visit of H.E. the President to Bangladesh.  The MoU will continue its activities in the field of agriculture between the two countries.  The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture for the implementation of activities indicated in the MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
