Procurement of 81 Nos. Small Buses for Rural Areas on Repeat Order Basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-07-11

the Ministry of Transport has already purchased 200 Nos. 33 seater buses as per the Cabinet decision of 2013-01-17, to be used for rural areas.  The proposal made by Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of Transport, to award the above procurement to M/s. Lanka Ashok Leyland PLC as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
