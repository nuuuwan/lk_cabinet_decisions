The Project funded by the Saudi Fund for Development - award of contract for the Rehabilitation and Improvements to the road section from Palapathwela to Naula  (from 32.54 km to 54.18 km ) on Kandy - Jaffna Road (A9)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- the Government of Sri Lanka has received a loan of Saudi Riyal 225,000,000	(approximately US$ 60 million) from the Saudi Fund for Development for the rehabilitation and improvement of about 144 km of the National Road Network of Sri Lanka.  The project will also support to develop rural areas by providing connectivity through links with other parts of the existing roads enabling to enhance easy access for industries and agricultural activities. The proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Highways,Ports and Shipping to utilize a part of this loan for the rehabilitation and improvement of the road section from Palapathwela to Naula  (from 32.54 km to 54.18 km) on Kandy - Jaffna Road (A9) as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
