Construction of Bridge No. 38/3 on Peliyagoda - Putalam Road (A3) at Kochchikade over Mahaoya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-04-18

-  the  Peliyagoda - Putalam Road (A3) is one of the trunk roads which links several districts and provincial centres.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to award the above contract as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
