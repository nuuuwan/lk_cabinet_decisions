Joint International Dental Conference

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-03-21

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to hold the Joint International Dental Conference from 27th June to 01st July 2013 at the BMICH where  Dental Professionals from American, Asian, European and Pacific Regions are expected to participate, was approved by the Cabinet.  A Dental Trade Exhibition will also be held parallel to the above and Dental Manufactures and Traders from Japan, China, India, Pakistan, Singapore and several other regional countries are expected to participate in this exhibition.
