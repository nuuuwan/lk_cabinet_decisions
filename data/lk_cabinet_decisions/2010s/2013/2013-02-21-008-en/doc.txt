Construction of the Official Residence and the Staff Quarters, Sri Lanka 	High Commission in London

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-02-21

- Cabinet approval has already been granted to 	design and construct a new building for the purpose, to the Central Engineering 	Consultancy Bureau (CECB). The proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, 	Minister of External Affairs, to revise the total cost estimate of the project to 	Rs.881,463,040.26, to incorporate several additional architectural features on the 	advice of the Consultant Architect in the United Kingdom, such as increased floor 	areas, various planning requirements of the Westminster Council, Geological 	conditions at the site etc., was approved by the Cabinet.
