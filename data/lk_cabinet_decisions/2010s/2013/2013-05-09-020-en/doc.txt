Registration of Electors (Special Provisions) Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-05-09

the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft legislation to enable persons who have been internally displaced from the Northern Province and who are now temporarily residing in other parts of Sri Lanka, to get themselves registered as electors, on submission of satisfactory proof thereof as stipulated, was approved by the Cabinet.
