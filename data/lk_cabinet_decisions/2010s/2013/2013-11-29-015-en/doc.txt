Fertilizer Procurement - Maha Season 2013/2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-11-29

- supply of fertilizer under the subsidy scheme is being handled by the Ceylon Fertilizer Company Ltd. and Colombo Commercial Fertilizer Ltd. functioning under the purview of the Ministry of Agriculture. The procurement process to procure the fertilizer requirement for Maha Season - 2013/2014 has already commenced through pre-qualified list of registered fertilizer suppliers for the year 2013.  The proposal of  Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture  to award the contracts for the supply of fertilizer to the successful bidders as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
