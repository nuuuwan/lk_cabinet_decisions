Provision of Equipment for Mahindodaya Technological Laboratories under the Programme of Redesigning of 1000 Secondary Schools

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2013-12-16

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to purchase 6,600 computers with all accessories, 400 printers, 100 scanners and 85 laptop computers for 100 Mahindodaya Technological Laboratories, as per the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and the Technical Evaluation Committee (TEC), was approved by the Cabinet.
