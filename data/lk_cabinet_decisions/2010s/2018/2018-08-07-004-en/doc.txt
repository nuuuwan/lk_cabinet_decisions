Signing of Terms of Reference of the Joint Russian - Sri Lankan Working group on Military - Technical Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as  the Minister of Defence, to sign the Terms of Reference containing the  functions and conditions of  the Joint Russian - Sri Lankan Working group on Military - Technical Cooperation proposed to be established as per the Agreement  on Military - Technical Cooperation signed between Russian Federation and Sri Lanka in 2004, was approved by the Cabinet.
