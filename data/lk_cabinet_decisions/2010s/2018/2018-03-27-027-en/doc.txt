The Ambathale Water Treatment Plant Expansion Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-03-27

It is expected that the daily water demand of the Colombo district will increase up to 863,602 m³ of water by the year 2020.  Therefore, it has been planned to increase the water purification capacity of the Ambathale Water Treatment Plant by 50,000 m³ of water per day. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to implement the Ambathale Water Treatment Plant Expansion Project treating it as a priority project, was approved by the Cabinet.
