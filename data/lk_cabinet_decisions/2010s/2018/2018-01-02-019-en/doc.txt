Issuance of International Sovereign Bonds under the Government Borrowing Programme - 2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to take further action to raise funds up to a sum of US$ 2,000 million through the issuance of International Sovereign Bonds within the approved Government borrowing limits for the year 2018, was approved by the Cabinet.
