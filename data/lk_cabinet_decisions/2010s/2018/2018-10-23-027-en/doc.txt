Purchase of a Chiller for the Central Air Conditioning System of the Superior Courts Complex Building.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms, to secure funds amounting to Rs 50 million to purchase a Chiller for the Central Air Conditioning System of the Superior Courts Complex, in order to supply the air conditioning facilities necessary to carry out the functions of the Supreme Court and the Court of Appeal, uninterruptedly, was approved by the Cabinet.
