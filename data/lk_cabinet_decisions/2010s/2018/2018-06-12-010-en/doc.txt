Deltota Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-12

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to make a request to M/s M-U-T GambH Austria for the issuance of project proposals for the implementation of the Deltota Water Supply Project which is a priority project included in the master plan of investment of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
