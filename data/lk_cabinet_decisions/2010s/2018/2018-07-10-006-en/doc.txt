Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and 	Finland for cooperation in the field of Health

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Social Activities and Health of the Republic of Finland for enhancing the cooperation between the two countries on the matters pertaining to the Health Sector for adopting Digital Technology, Dental Care, development of Medical Equipment and Hospital Infrastructure facilities, Genomics, Blood Plasma Fractionation, Diagnostics, Medical and Health Care Education and Non‑Communicable Diseases, was approved by the Cabinet.
