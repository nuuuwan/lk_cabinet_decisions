Amendments to the National Institute of Plantation Management Act, No.45 of 1979

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Navin Dissanayake, the Minister of Plantation Industries, to amend the National Institute of Plantation Management Act, No.45 of 1979, so as to transform the National Institute of Plantation Management as an institution which creates professionals with high competency required in the field of plantation and awards skills oriented plantation management degrees, was approved by the Cabinet.
