Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and Vietnam in the field of Information and Communication

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Vietnam pertaining to Information and Communication, with a view to strengthening the friendly relations between the two countries for the promotion of bilateral cooperation, was approved by the Cabinet.
