Bilateral Air Services Agreements with Hellenic Republic (Greece) and Italy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-24

- The proposals made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to sign the initialed Bilateral Air Services Agreements with the Republic of Greece and the Italian Republic by an accredited representative of Sri Lanka and thereafter take appropriate measures for the entry into force of these Agreements, was approved by the Cabinet.
