Development of the Borella Ayurveda Hospital as a Teaching Hospital

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-21

- Under the project for the Development of the Borella Ayurveda Hospital as a Teaching Hospital, the construction of a new ward complex is underway. The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, for the supply of electricity, the construction of the Water and Sewage System to initiate healthcare services expeditiously in the said building, was approved by the Cabinet.
