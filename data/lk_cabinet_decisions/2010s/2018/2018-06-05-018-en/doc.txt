Purchasing of Medical Examination Gloves

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-05

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the supply of  79,430,000 locally manufactured unsterile Medical Examination Gloves made of Rubber latex (in various sizes), used in medical examinations, to M/s Sisili Project Consortium (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.448.3 million, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
