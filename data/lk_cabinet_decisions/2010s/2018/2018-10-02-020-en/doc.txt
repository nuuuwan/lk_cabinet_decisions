Procurement of Aviation Fuel for SriLankan Airlines worldwide

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Public Enterprise & Kandy City Development, to award the procurement for the supply of aviation fuel for the air crafts belonging to the SriLankan Airlines at 24 airports to 11 suppliers, for a sum of US$ 190.2 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
