The Agreement between the Government Sri Lanka and the Government of Oman

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-14

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to enter into an Agreement between Sri Lanka and Oman relating to the avoidance of double taxation and the prevention of fiscal evasion covering the areas pertaining to interest, royalty and profit motivation on shipping services in anticipation of enhancing economic relations and corporation between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
