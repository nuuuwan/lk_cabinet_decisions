The Project of Introducing Home Dialysis System using the Automated Peritoneal Dialysis, for Kidney Disease patients.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- A proposal has been submitted to the Cabinet previously on the implementation of a project of introducing a Home Dialysis System using the Automated Peritoneal Dialysis, for Kidney Disease patients and accordingly it was decided to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee to examine the said proposal and submit its recommendations to the Cabinet. As such, considering the recommendations made by the said Sub-Committee, Hon. Mohomed Casim Mohomed Faizal, the Acting Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, submitted  the proposals to be implemented for the  notice of the Cabinet.
