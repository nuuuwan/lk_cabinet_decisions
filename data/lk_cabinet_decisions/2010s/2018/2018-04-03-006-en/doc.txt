Vesting of a land for the construction of  a Playground and a Community Centre at Badanagoda in Beruwala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-04-03

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine,to vest a portion of land in extent of 127.6 perches from the land called Pallegodawaththa managed by the Namunukula State Plantation Company for the construction of a playground and a community centre at Badanagoda in Beruwala for the use of the people living in the area around Grama Niladhari Divisions of Galpoththawila and Punsalamulla belonging to the Divisional Secretariat Divisions of Beruwala and Dodangoda, was approved by the Cabinet.
