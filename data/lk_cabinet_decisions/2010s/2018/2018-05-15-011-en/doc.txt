Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and Korea for the development in the field of Navigation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-05-15

- The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, to sign a MoU between the Ministry of Ports and Shipping of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries of the Republic of Korea with a view to exchanging  technological information between the two countries for the development of the field of navigation, was approved by the Cabinet.
