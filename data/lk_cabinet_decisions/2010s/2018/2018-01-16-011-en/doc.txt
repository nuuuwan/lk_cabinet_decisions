Installation of the main water supply system in the Defence Headquarters Complex (DHQC) at Akuregoda

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-16

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to award the contract for Design, Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning of the Water Mains for the Defence Headquarters Complex (DHQC) Project at Akuregoda, to M/s Frigi Engineering Services (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.494.48 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
