Providing permanent building facilities for the Nuwara Eliya Regional Office of the Tea Small Holdings Development Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-04

- With a view to providing permanent building facilities in place of the building on lease where the Nuwara Eliya Regional Office of the Tea Small Holdings Development Authority is functioning presently, the proposal made by Hon. Navin Dissanayake, the Minister of Plantation Industries, to establish the said Office in a building situated in a block of land in extent of 0.0452 hectares in the Kotmale New Town after renovation and to allocate the required funds for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
