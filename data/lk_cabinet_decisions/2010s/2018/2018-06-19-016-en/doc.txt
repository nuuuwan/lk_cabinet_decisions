The Action Plan of the Spices and Allied Products Marketing Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-19

- The Action Plan submitted by the Hon. Daya Gamage, the Minister of Social Welfare and Primary Industries, to develop the Spices and Allied Products by the Spices and Allied Products Marketing Board and to promote the services with a view to enhancing the quality, organic products, upgrading the technology and research studies for promoting them locally and internationally, was approved by the Cabinet.
