Vesting of the lands of the National Institute of Education and the Aesthetic Education Institute belonging to it established in the land at Meepe in Padukka with the National Institute of Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Higher Education & Cultural Affairs, to vest the land in extent of 17.53 acres in Maharagama where the National Institute of Education is situated and the land in extent of 16.26 acres where the Aesthetic Education Institute is situated at Meepe in Padukka, formally with the National Institute of Education, was approved by the Cabinet.
