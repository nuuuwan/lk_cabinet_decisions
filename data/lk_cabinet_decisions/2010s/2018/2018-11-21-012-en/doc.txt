The ongoing Government Procurements to be carried out without any delay

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-11-21

- The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Rajapaksa, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs, to provide the necessary instructions to the Line Ministries to carry out the ongoing Government Procurement without any delay in accordance with the recent changes in the Ministerial portfolios, was approved by the Cabinet.
