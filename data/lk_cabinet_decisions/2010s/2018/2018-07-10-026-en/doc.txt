Mid - Year Fiscal Position Report - 2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to present the Mid - Year Fiscal Position Report which contains updated information on the Government's Fiscal Position pertaining to the first four (04) months of 2018 as stipulated in Section 10 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No.03 of 2003, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
