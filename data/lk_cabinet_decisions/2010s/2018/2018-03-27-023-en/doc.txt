Presentation of Regulations made by the Minister of Sports under section 34(1) of the Convention against Doping in Sports Act, in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-03-27

The proposal made by Hon. Dayasiri Jayasekara, the Minister of Sports, to present the Gazette Notification issued, incorporating the Anti-Doping Regulations (Prohibited List) No.3 of 2017 made by the Minister of Sports in terms of section 34(1) of the Convention against Doping in Sports Act, No.33 of 2013, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet
