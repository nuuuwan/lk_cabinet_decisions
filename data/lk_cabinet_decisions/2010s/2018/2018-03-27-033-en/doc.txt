Feasibility Study on the North Port Development Project of the Port of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-03-27

Considering the need to perform a feasibility study for the development of the Port of Colombo towards the Northern side by the Government of Sri Lanka for the development activities of the transport sector, the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, to call for  Expression of Interest (EOI) and Request for Proposal (RFP) to select and appoint an appropriate consultancy agency for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
