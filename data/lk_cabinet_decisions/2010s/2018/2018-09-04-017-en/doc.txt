Establishment of Standing Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committees to accelerate  the implementation of projects

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-04

The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to appoint Senior Officials such as Secretaries to the State Ministries, Additional Secretaries, Chief Financial Officers as members of  the Standing Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committees in order to expedite the procurement decision making process, to accelerate the projects inclusive of development projects implemented by the  Government, was approved by the Cabinet.
