Purchase of Two Pilot Launches for the Sri Lanka Ports Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, to award the procurement for the purchase of two pilot launches necessary to provide an efficient navigation service in the Port of Colombo for the Sri Lanka Ports Authority for a sum of US$ 3.12 million to M/s Colombo Dockyard PLC, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
