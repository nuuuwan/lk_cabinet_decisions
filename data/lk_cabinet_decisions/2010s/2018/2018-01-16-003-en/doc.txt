Agreement between Turkmenistan and Sri Lanka on Mutual Visa Exemption for Holders of Diplomatic and Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-16

- The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinne, the Minister of  Internal Affairs, Wayamba Development and Cultural Affairs, to enter into an Agreement  between Sri Lanka and Turkmenistan on Mutual Visa Exemption for citizens who are Holders of Diplomatic and Official Passports when traveling between the two countries, with the objective of furthering friendly relations between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
