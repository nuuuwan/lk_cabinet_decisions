Granting National Vocational Qualification Level 3 (NVQ 3) Certificate for 10,000 Cinnamon Peelers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Daya Gamage, the Minister of Social Welfare and Primary Industries, to prepare a programme for granting  National Vocational Qualification Level 3 (NVQ 3) certificate for 10,000 Cinnamon Peelers for carrying out the cinnamon peeling process with accurate technical usages and to secure other facilities required for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
