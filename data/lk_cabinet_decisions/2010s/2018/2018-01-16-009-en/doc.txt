Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Vietnamese People's Police Academy and the National Police Academy of Sri Lanka on Cooperation in Police Training and Scientific Research

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-16

- Vietnam and Sri Lanka have agreed to enhance bilateral cooperation between the two countries for the prevention of crimes. As such, with a view to strengthening the cooperation through the exchange of updated information on Police Training and Scientific Research, the proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayake, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Vietnamese People's Police Academy and the National Police Academy of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
