Supply of water to the areas of Inamaluwa, Kitulhitiyawa, Sigiriya, Habarana, Galewela and Thiththawelgolla under the Local Government Enhancement Sector Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-11-13

It has been planned to implement water supply schemes to cater to the water supply needs of the people living in the areas of Inamaluwa, Kitulhitiyawa, Sigiriya, Habarana, Galewela and Thiththawelgolla under the Grater Dambulla Water Scheme of the Local Government Enhancement Sector Project. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Faiszer Musthapha, the Minister of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports to award the contracts for the water supply extension to Inamaluwa and  Kitulhitiyawa areas, for a sum of Rs.491.9 million and the water supply extension to Sigiriya and Habarana areas, for a sum of Rs.415.3 million  to M/s PND, E&C Joint Venture, and the water supply extension to Galawela and Thiththawelgolla areas to M/s Squire Mech Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.660.6 million, in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
