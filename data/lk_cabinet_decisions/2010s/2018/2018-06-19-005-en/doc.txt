Granting of Ports and Airports Development Levy Tax Concessions on High tech Automated Machinery

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to present the Gazette Notification issued under the Ports and Airports Development Levy Act, No.18 of 2011 for granting a 75% tax concession in respect of the High tech Automated Machinery used for the production activities of the domestic production industry, in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
