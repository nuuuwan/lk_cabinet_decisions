Purchase of 75,000 pairs of Boots (High Leg) required for the Sri Lanka Army

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-02-14

- The proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to award the contract for the purchase of 75,000 pairs of Boots (High Leg Black) for the members of the Sri Lanka Army, to M/s Irosha International (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.232 million, as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
