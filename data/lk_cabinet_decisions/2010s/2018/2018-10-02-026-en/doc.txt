Purchasing of Aerial Bundled Conductors (ABC) for the transmission of electricity

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the procurement for the purchase of 750km of Aerial Bundled Conductors required for the 02 distribution zones of the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) to M/s Sierra Cable PLC, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
