Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Belarus on Science, Technology and Innovation Cooperation.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Sarath Amunugama, the Minister of Science, Technology, Research, Skills Development & Vocational Training and Kandyan Heritage to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on strengthening and expansion of the Scientific and Technological Cooperation between the Republic of Belarus and the Government of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
