Annual Report of the Ministry of Finance - 2017

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-12

In terms of the provisions of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No.03 of 2003, the Minister of Finance is required to table a 'Final Budgetary Position Report' in Parliament, for information of the Parliament and the general public. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to table the Annual Report of the Ministry of Finance pertaining to the financial year 2017, was approved by the Cabinet.
