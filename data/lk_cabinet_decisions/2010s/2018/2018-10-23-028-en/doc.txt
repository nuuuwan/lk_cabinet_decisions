Presentation of the Extraordinary Gazette Notification that includes the list of prohibited substances in Parliament.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- A list that includes prohibited substances and methods which are banned to be used by athletes is published by the World Anti-Doping Agency annually. At the same time, in terms of section 34 of the Convention against Doping in Sports Act, No.33 of 2013, the list of such substances declaring the prohibition of such substances in sports has been published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No.2083/32. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Faiszer Musthapha, the Minister of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports, to present the said Gazette Notification, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
