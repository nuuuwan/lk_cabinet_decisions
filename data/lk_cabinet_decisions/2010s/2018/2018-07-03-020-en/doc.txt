Memorandum of Understanding on Strategic Economic Partnership 	between Thailand and Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Malik Samarawickrema, the Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Thailand covering the sectors such as investments, agricultural development, promoting and facilitating the fisheries industry, promoting the tourism industry and expanding and promoting information technology, was approved by the Cabinet.
