Selecting a Contractor for the expansion of the Nanotechnology and Science Park at Mahenwatte, Pitipana in Homagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-30

-	The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to award the contract for the expansion of the Nanotechnology and Science Park at Mahenwatte, Pitipana in Homagama under Phase 1B, to M/s Access Engineering PLC, for a sum of Rs.3,088 million, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
