Construction of the Pesalai Fishery Harbour

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- It has been planned to construct the Pesalai Fishery Harbour under the Northern Province Sustainable Fisheries Development Project implementing with a view 	to developing further fisheries infrastructure in Northern Province, aquaculture development and livelihood development. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Vijith Vijayamuni Zoysa, the Minister of Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Development and Rural Economic Affairs, to appoint a Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and a Technical Evaluation Committee in order to select a construction firm for the said project and to 	implement this project with an investment of Rs.5,267 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
