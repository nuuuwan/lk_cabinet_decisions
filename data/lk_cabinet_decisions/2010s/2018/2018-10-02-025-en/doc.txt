Awarding the Contract  for replacing 04  old turbochargers with new  04 turbochargers at the Sapugaskanda Power Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the procurement for the installation of new  04 turbochargers in place of the 04 old turbochargers at the Sapugaskanda Power Station to M/s MAN Diesel & Turbo SE of Germany as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
