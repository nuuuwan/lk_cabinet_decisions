Supply of 120,000 Bottles of Human Albumin Solution - 50ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the purchase of 120,000 bottles of  Human Albumin Solution BP/PH EUR, 20% in 50ml, a drug used for the treatment of kidney patients, to M/s Shire Singapore Pte. Ltd. for US$ 2.82 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and the Procurement Appeal Board (PAB), was approved by the Cabinet.
