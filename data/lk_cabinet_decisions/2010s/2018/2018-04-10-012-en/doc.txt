Procurement for Issuing of Vehicle Emission Test Certificates

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-04-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation,  to award the contract for the Issuing of Vehicle Emission Test Certificates to the three companies namely M/s Laughs Eco Sri (Pvt.) Ltd., M/s Cleanco Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd. and M/s Auto Test Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd.,  as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee (CANC), was approved by the Cabinet.
