Construction of a new building for the National Medicines Regulatory Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-31

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to allocate a block of land in extent of 27.24 perches located at Thimbirigasyaya area for the construction of the Head Office building and the National Quality Assurance Laboratory of the National Medicines Regulatory Authority, was approved by the Cabinet.
