Strengthening the functioning of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption plays a vital role in maintaining and improving the fiscal discipline in the Public sector. As such, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, to implement the Training Programme formulated to provide specialized   training to the Officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption with a view to strengthening the state economy by further improving the fiscal discipline, was approved by the Cabinet.
