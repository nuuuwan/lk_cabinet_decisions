Organizing the 71st  National Day Celebration

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Vajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Home Affairs, to appoint a sub-committee comprising of 13 people's representatives chaired by  the Hon. Prime Minister to prepare the relevant programme and organize and monitor necessary arrangements pertaining to the 71st National Day Ceremony which falls on 04th  February, 2019, was approved by the Cabinet.
