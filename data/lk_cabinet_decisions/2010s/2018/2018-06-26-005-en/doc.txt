The procurement for the supply of Inflight Entertainment material and Guide 'Onboard' for Air Flight Passengers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Public Enterprise & Kandy City Development, to award the procurement for the Supply of Inflight Entertainment (IFE) Material and Inflight Entertainment Guide 'Onboard' in the SriLankan Airlines to M/s Inflight Dublin Ltd. for a period of three (03) years effective from 2018‑10‑01 for a sum of US$ 5.92 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
