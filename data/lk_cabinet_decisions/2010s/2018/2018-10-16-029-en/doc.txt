Implementation of the National Information Technology initiative for export development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The Budget of 2018 had proposed to provide the necessary facilities for the Export Managers of the Information Technologies/Exporters in the Business Process Management, by the implementation of an export growth initiative as a promotional strategy for the development of exports within a period of 03 years from the year 2018 to 2020. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Malik Samarawickrema, the Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade, to implement the Information Technology Initiative Programme so designed, was approved by the Cabinet.
