Construction of Teldeniya, Pugoda and Welimada Court Complexes

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms, to carry out the construction activities by the Government owned construction institutions, with a view to providing facilities required to carry out the functions of the courts efficiently and productively, taking into consideration the need for the construction of new buildings for the Court Complexes in Teldeniya, Pugoda and Welimada, was approved by the Cabinet.
