Renovation of 'I, H and N' buildings of the Anderson Flats

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- A total of 144 housing units of 'I,H and N' buildings consisting of 48 housing	units in each building are used as official quarters of the Inspectors of Police	and the junior grade officers. These houses are nearly 50 years old and	accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the	Minister of Public Administration & Management and Law & Order, to secure	required funds for immediate renovation of these buildings, was approved by	the Cabinet.
