Allocation of a block of land to the Kumara Vidyalaya - Ratnapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest a block of land, located at Ratnapura, New Town with Kumara Vidyalaya-Ratnapura identified to be necessary for further development activities of the school, was approved by the Cabinet.
