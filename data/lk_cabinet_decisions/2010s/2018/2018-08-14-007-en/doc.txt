Vesting of lands with the Police Station in Kottawa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-14

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to vest the block of land in extent of 49.80 perches with the Police Department, to set up the Police Station in Kottawa, which has been vested with the Urban Development Authority (UDA) by the Government, was approved by the Cabinet.
