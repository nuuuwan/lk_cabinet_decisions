Extending the beneficiary base of the 'Sonduru Piyasa' Concessionary Loan Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Sajith Premadasa, the Minister of Housing and	Construction, in order to extend the beneficiary base by increasing the	existing floor area of the house to be eligible under 'Sonduru Piyasa'	Concessionary Loan Scheme from 1000sq.ft. to 1500sq.ft., was approved by 	the Cabinet.
