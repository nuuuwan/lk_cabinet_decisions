Public Sector Efficiency Enhancement Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-04-03

- The World Bank has agreed to provide Sri Lanka with a loan facility of US$ 60 million while the European Union has agreed to provide with a grant approximately US$ 10 million for institutional strengthening and the capacity  building of the public sector institutions pertaining to the public financial management. Focusing on the following, with the utilization of these funds it has been planned to implement the Public Sector Efficiency Project (PSEP), strengthening the efficiency of the public investment and debt management improving the operational efficiency of the budget execution and the procurement process, strengthening the Governance transparency and the  efficiency of the public services, and the sustainable public sector skill development and capacity building and public participation. Accordingly,  the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to provide the facilities necessary for the implementation of the project, was approved by the Cabinet.
