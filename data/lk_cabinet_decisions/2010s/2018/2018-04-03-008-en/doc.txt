Procurement of Vehicles  for the Government Institutions

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-04-03

- The Cabinet has granted approval for procuring of vehicles for the use of  Government Institutions under the methods of Outright Purchase, Operational Leasing and Financial Leasing. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to issue Circular Instructions indicating the criteria to be adopted when procuring of vehicles adopting each method, was approved by the Cabinet.
