Construction of the new building with seven storeys for the Faculty of Arts of the University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-11-21

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Education & Higher Education, to award the contract for the construction of a building with seven storeys for the Faculty of Arts of the University of Peradeniya to M/s Sathuta Builders (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.397.3 million in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
