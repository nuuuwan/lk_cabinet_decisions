Purchasing of 93,767nos. of Foldable Hydrophobic Intra Ocular Lenses

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the purchase    of 93,767 Foldable Hydrophobic Intra Ocular Lenses which are to be implanted in the eye during cataract surgery to M/s Hemas Pharmaceuticals (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.1,062.2 million as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
