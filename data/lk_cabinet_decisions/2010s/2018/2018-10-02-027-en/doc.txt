Rehabilitation and improvement to the road section from 53.74km to 58.00km on the Kandy - Jaffna Road (A9) including Naula Town

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development, to award the contract for the rehabilitation and improvement of the road section from 53.74 km to 58.00 km on the Kandy - Jaffna (A9) Road  including the Naula Town under the Project for the Rehabilitation and Improvement of 144 km of the National Highways Network of Sri Lanka, to M/s Consulting Engineers & Contractors (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rupees 590.62 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
