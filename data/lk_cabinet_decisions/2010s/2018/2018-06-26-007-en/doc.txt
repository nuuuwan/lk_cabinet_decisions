Construction of a 1500cum capacity water tower and the Transmission and Distribution network in the Naiwala area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-26

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the contract for the construction of a 1500cum capacity water tower in the Naiwala area under the Gampaha, Attanagalla, Minuwangoda Integrated Water Supply Project and laying of the required water pipes for transmission and distribution activities and the installation of other accessories, to the Joint Venture of M/s Ceylex Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd. and Prathiba Industries Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
