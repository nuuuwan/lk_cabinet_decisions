Katupota - Bamunakotuwa - Panduwasnuwara Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to refer the proposal made by M/s Kolon Global Corporation pertaining to the implementation of the Katupota - Bamunakotuwa - Panduwasnuwara Water Supply Project, with a view to supplying  drinking water to about 133,000 people living in the Kurunegala District, to the Standing Cabinet Appointed Negotiation Committee and the Project Committee, for evaluation and recommendations, was approved by the Cabinet.
