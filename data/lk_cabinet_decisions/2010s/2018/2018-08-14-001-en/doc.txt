Improvement of the Security Buildings of the Parliament Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-14

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, to improve and refurbish the Jayanthipura security building at the main entrance of the Parliament complex and the Pinniyara security building at the rear entrance of the Parliament inclusive of new technological facilities required for the operational activities for the security of  the Parliament complex, was approved by the Cabinet.
