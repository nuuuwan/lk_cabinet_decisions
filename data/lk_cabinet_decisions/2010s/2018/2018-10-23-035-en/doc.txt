Development of the Panadura Base Hospital and provision of medical equipment

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the development of the Panadura Base Hospital and provision of medical equipment which provides medical services to the people living in the areas of Beruwala, Kalutara, Bandragama, Ratnapura, Moratuwa and Ratmalana for a sum of Euro million 106.4 to M/s GITEC IGIP Consultant GmbH of Germany, in accordance with the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
