Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and Turkmenistan  on Bilateral Consultations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-30

-	The proposal made by Hon. Thilak Marapana, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Turkmenistan on Bilateral Consultations, was approved by the Cabinet.
