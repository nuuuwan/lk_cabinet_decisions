Amending the Shrama Vasana Fund Act, No.12 of 1998

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravindra Samaraweera, the Minister of Labour & Trade Union Relations, to publish the draft bill drafted to amend the Shrama Vasana Fund Act, No.12 of 1998 so as to make provisions for further promotion of the Shrama Vasana Lottery which contributes to the Shrama Vasana Fund that provides diverse relief to the employees of Sri Lanka, in the Government Gazette and present the same thereafter in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
