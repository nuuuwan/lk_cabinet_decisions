Modernization of the electrical cable system of the Government Factory in, Kolonnawa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-24

- The low voltage underground electrical cable system which is presently being used to obtain electricity for the Department of the Government Factory which provides specific mechanical services to various Government institutions has to be modernized. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Sajith Premadasa, the Minister of Housing and Construction, to implement the proposed project at a cost of Rs.34.6 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
