Programme to Grant Houses for the Middle Income Sector for an affordable price

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The Government has focused attention on the supply of houses for the increasing housing demand of  the Middle income community. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to implement a housing project of 1,900 housing units, in the areas of Kottawa, Orugodawatta, Anuradhapura, Nuwara Eliya, Awissawella and Horana, to meet the housing requirements of the professionals and the Executive Grade Officers, was approved by the Cabinet.
