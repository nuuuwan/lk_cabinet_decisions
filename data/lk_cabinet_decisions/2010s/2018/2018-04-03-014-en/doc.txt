Establishment of Technical Collaboration in the field of Agriculture

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-04-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Agriculture, to enter into an Agreement to foster Cooperation in Education and Research for Long-term Technical Collaboration between  the Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy functioning under the purview of the Ministry of  Agriculture and the Michigan State University (MSU) of the United States of America, was approved by the Cabinet.
