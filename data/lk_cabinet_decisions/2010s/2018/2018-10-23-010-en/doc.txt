Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Vocational Technology (UNIVOTEC) of Sri Lanka and the Seychelles Institute of Technology (SIT) of Seychelles.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Sarath Amunugama, the Minister of Science, Technology, Research, Skills Development & Vocational Training and Kandyan Heritage on the Memorandum of Understanding entered into between the University of Vocational Technology of Sri Lanka and the Seychelles Institute of Technology of Seychelles in the fields such as academic research training, departmental exchange, capacity building and joint student activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
