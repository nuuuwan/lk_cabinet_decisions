Establishment of a new Trilingual Mixed National School in the Nanuoya area in Nuwara Eliya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-21

- In order to further upgrade the educational facilities available in the Nuwara Eliya District, the proposal made by Hon. Akila Viraj Kariyawasam, the Minister of Education, to establish a fully fledged new Trilingual Mixed National School, in the Nanuoya area, with an investment of Rs.800 million to conduct classes from Grade 6 to Grade 13 and to secure the facilities required for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
