Development of the infrastructure facilities at the Kelaniya, Sri Jayawardenepura and Sabaragamuwa Universities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- With a view of improving the facilities in the National University System, it has been planned to construct a four storey hostel building at the University of Kelaniya, 03 storeys in the seven storey Multi Purpose building at the University of Sri Jayawardenepura and the Faculty building complex for the Faculty of Agriculture of the Sabaragamuwa University. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Higher Education & Cultural Affairs, to carry out those construction works, was approved by the Cabinet.
