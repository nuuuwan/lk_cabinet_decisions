Promotion in the Cooperation on Scientific and Research Studies between Sri Lanka and Germany

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-02-20

- The proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, the Minister of Science, Technology and Research, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the National Science Foundation of Sri Lanka and the German Academic Exchange Service for exchanging German Scientists to further promote Cooperation on Scientific and Research Studies between Sri Lanka and Germany, was approved by the Cabinet.
