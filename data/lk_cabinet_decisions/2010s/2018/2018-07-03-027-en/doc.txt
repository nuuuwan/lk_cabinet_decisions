Development of Infrastructure facilities at Higher Educational Institutions

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-03

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Higher Education & Cultural Affairs, to implement projects amounting to Rs.2,384.7 million, for the development of infrastructure facilities at the University of Ruhuna, Biksu University of Sri Lanka, South Eastern University, University of Sri Jayawardhanapura and Sri Lanaka Institute of Advanced Technological Education, was approved by the Cabinet.
