Supply, Installation and Commissioning of a Communication System for the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) and the Ratmalana Airport (RMA)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the supply, installation and commissioning of a Communication System to enhance the efficiency of the operations at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) and the Ratmalana Airport (RMA) to M/s SITTI S. P. A, for a sum of Euro 1.7 million and Rupees 6 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
