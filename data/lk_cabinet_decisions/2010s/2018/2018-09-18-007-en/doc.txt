Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on the Establishment of a Bilateral Consultation Mechanism between Sri Lanka and Laos

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Thilak Marapana, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding to strengthen the existing bilateral relations between Sri Lanka and Laos and for the mutual co‑operation on regional and international matters, was approved by the Cabinet.
