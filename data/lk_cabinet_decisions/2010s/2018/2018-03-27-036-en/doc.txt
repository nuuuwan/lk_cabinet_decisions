Augmentation of Kotugoda, Kolonnawa, Padukka, Horana Dehiwala and Madampe Grid Substations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-03-27

Taking into consideration the recommendations made by the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Procurement Committee, the proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contracts to M/s ABB India Ltd., for a sum of Rs. 2,214.16 million, to upgrade the Kotugoda, Kolonnawa, Padukka, Horana, Dehiwala and Madampe Grid Substations under the Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
