Improvement of the storage capacity of the Paddy Marketing Board (PMB)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- With a view to purchasing and storing the paddy harvest in the Yala Season 2018, it has been planned to construct 02 new paddy stores with the capacity of 2,000 MT at Beliatta and Meegahajadura and to repair 39 existing paddy stores. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Amaraweera, the Minister of  Agriculture, to obtain the engineering consultancy services required for conducting those activities with scientific and modern technology, selection of contractors and supervision of work through the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), was approved by the Cabinet.
