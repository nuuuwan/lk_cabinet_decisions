Hosting of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) Annual Meeting - 2021 in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-28

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to communicate the Asian Development Bank; a long time development partner of Sri Lanka, the intention of hosting the Asian Development Bank Annual Meeting - 2021 in Sri Lanka and to appoint a National Monitoring Committee and Sub‑Committees for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
