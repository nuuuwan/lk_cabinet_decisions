Purchasing of uniform  material for the Sri lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister of Public Administration & Management and Law & Order, to purchase 400,000 meters of uniform materials from Vanguard Industries Ltd and 100,000 meters from Creative Textile Mill (Pvt) Ltd at Rs. 346/- per each  for the uniform requirement of  officers of  the Sri Lanka Police, was approved by the Cabinet.
