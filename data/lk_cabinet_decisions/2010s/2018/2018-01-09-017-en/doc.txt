Rehabilitation of the Underground Storm Water Drainage System in Kandy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-09

- It has been planned to rehabilitate the underground storm water drainage system in Kandy to coincide with the Kandy City Development activities taking place under the Strategic Cities Development Project. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to award the civil contract to the Joint Venture of Ludwig Pfeiffer (Germany) - Gypsum Structural India (Pvt) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.1,016.3 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
