Supply and delivery of new Tyre Kits for the Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation,	to award the procurement for the purchase of new Tyre Kits in various sizes required for the buses of the Sri Lanka Transport Board to M/s. Ceat Kelani International Tyres (Pvt.) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.1,124.14 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
