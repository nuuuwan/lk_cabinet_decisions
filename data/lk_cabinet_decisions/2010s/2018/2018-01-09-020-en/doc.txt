Anuradhapura North Water Supply Project - Phase - II - Consultancy Contract for Detailed Design and Bidding Assistance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-09

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the contract for the Anuradhapura North Water Supply Project - Phase - II - Consultancy Contract for Detailed Design and Bidding Assistance to M/s NJS Concultants Co. Ltd. of Japan in association with M/s Ceywater Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd. and M/s EML Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd. of Sri Lanka, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee (SCACPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
