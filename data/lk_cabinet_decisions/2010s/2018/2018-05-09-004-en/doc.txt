Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Cooperation in the field of Tourism between Sri Lanka and Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-05-09

The proposal made by Hon. John Amaratunga, the Minister of Tourism Development and Christian Religious Affairs, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to promote Cooperation in  the field of Tourism between Sri Lanka and Pakistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
