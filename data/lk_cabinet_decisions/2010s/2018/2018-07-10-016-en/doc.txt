Purchasing of Gas Cylinders used by the Litro Gas Lanka Ltd., for the distribution of Gas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- The proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Public Enterprise & Kandy City Development to purchase 656,298 of LP Gas Cylinders (of different sizes) with safety valves used by the Litro Gas Lanka Ltd., for distribution purposes, from M/s Mauriya Udyog Ltd., in India for a sum of Rs.2,057.1 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
