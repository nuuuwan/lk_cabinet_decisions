Establishment of a Tamil Medium National School in Kelaniya Education Zone.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Mano Ganesan, the Minister of National Integration, Reconciliation and Official Languages, to establish a Tamil medium Hindu School named Arun Manickavasagam Hindu College, in Kelaniya Educational Zone to uplift the educational standards of the children studying in the Tamil medium in the Gampaha and the Wattala area, was approved by the Cabinet.
