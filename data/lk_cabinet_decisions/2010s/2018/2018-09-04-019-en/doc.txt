Housing Project with 450 Housing Units at Cyril C. Perera Mawatha at Bloemendhal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-04

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to award the contract of the Housing Project with 450 Housing Units at Cyril C. Perera Mawatha at Bloemendhal to M/s Access Engineering PLC, for a sum of Rs.2,737.35 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
