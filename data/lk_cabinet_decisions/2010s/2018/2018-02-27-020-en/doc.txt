Purchasing of Injections required for treating the patients with immune deficiency

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-02-27

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for purchasing  33,000 vials of Human Immunoglobulin for Intravenous use BP 5-6g vial for treating the patients with immunity deficiency, to M/s Reliance Life Science (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of US$ 3.06 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
