Matara Nilwala Eliya District Development Project: 2018 - 2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to secure the provision for the proposed Nilwala Eliya project for the improvement of infrastructure facilities such as Education, Health, Highways, Irrigation and  Common Amenities found as essential to be effected in the areas specially outside the major towns of the Matara District, within the period from 2018-2020,  was approved by the Cabinet.
