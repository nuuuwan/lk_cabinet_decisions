Construction of a 03 storey barrack at the Gonahena Camp of the Special Task Force

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- A large number of officers of the Special Task Force of the Sri Lanka Police has been deployed for special security tasks in Colombo and its suburbs. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister of Public Administration & Management and Law & Order, seeking further provision to construct a 03 storey building equipped with modern facilities so as to provide the officers with residential facilities due to the lack of residential facilities for such officers by replacing the dilapidated building in the Gonahena Camp, was approved by the Cabinet.
