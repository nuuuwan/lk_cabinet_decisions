Appointment of Consultancy Service Agencies for the Construction of the new buildings of the Sri Lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-04-24

A sum exceeding Rs.1,000 million is allocated annually to the Sri Lanka Police, for the construction of new buildings and for the improvement and renovations pertaining to the existing buildings. Taking into consideration a proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister of Public Administration and Management and  the Minister of Law & Order, to select a consultancy service agency for the new building construction projects of the Sri Lanka Police during the period from 2018 to 2020, approval of the Cabinet was granted to obtain the  services intended from the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB), State Engineering Corporation and  the Department of National Buildings.
