Installation of the an Information Technology Application System for the Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, to commence a new procurement process for the procurement of two insurance systems based on the information technology to be utilized for life insurance and general insurance purposes, with a view to enhancing the efficiency of the business operational activities of the Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation Limited and providing more qualitative insurance services while maintaining a highly competitive market environment, was approved by the Cabinet.
