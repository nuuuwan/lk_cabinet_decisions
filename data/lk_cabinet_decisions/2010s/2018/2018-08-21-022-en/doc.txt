Procurement for the construction of the Accident and Emergency Treatment Unit at the Ashroff Memorial Hospital in Kalmunai

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-21

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the construction of the Accident and Emergency Treatment Unit at the Ashroff Memorial Hospital in Kalmunai to M/s Link Engineering (Pvt) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.926.5 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
