The Project for the Correction of Drug Addictive Offenders

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-04

- A pilot project, has been implemented within the North Western Province to preclude drug addictive offenders from drug addiction through corrective measures and making them engaged in self employment opportunities enabling their economic stability. The proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms, to secure the provisions required to implement the said project in the North Western, Southern, Sabaragamuwa and Eastern Provinces as well, from the year 2019 onwards, based on its success, was approved by the Cabinet.
