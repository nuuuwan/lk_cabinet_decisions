Establishment of a National Level University to provide the officers of   the Sri  Lanka Police with Higher Educational Facilities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- A concept paper has been drafted with the recommendations of an expertise committee in the relevant field on the establishment of a national level university to provide the officers of the Sri Lanka Police with higher educational facilities. As such, the Joint Proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister and Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister of Public Administration & Management and Law & Order, to draft a Bill to establish the proposed university, taking into consideration the said concept paper as well, was approved by the Cabinet.
