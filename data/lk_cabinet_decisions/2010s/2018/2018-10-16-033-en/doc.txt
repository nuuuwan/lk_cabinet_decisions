Infilling of the Distribution System in the adjacent areas of Ampara and Batticaloa Districts

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to refer the project proposal presented by M/s Sunpower Constructions (Pvt.) Ltd. for the implementation of the Infilling Distribution System in the adjacent areas of Ampara and Batticaloa Districts with a view to providing water supply facilities expeditiously to the areas where there is a scarcity of water, to the Standing Cabinet Appointed Negotiation Committee (SCANC) for evaluation and to make recommendations, was approved by the Cabinet.
