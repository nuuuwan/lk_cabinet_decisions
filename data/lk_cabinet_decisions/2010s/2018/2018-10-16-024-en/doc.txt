Stabilization of the land prone to landslide risk where the Kegalle Court Complex is located

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The land where the Kegalle Court Complex is located comprising of buildings of more than 80 years seems at risk of landslide. As such, the proposal made by Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala, the Minister of Justice & Prison Reforms, to secure funds for the stabilization of the land preventing such risk, was approved by the Cabinet.
