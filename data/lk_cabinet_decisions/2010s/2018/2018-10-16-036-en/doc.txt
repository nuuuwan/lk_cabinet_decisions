Awarding of contract for the construction of 100 MW Wind Farm

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of	Power and Renewable Energy, to award the contract for the construction of	100MW Wind Farm in the Southern coastal strip of the Mannar Island with the	financial assistance of the Asian Development Bank, to M/s Vestas Asia Pacific 	A/S - Denmark and enter into the agreements as recommended by the Cabinet	Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
