Obtaining Consultancy Services for the Construction Supervision of the Western Province Road Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-30

-	The Western Province Road Development Project is implemented with the funds provided by the OPEC Fund for International Development (OFID). Under this project, the proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to award the contract for the provision of consultancy services for the supervision of the construction in the roads namely, Kaduwela-Athurugiriya, Walgama-Athurugiriya, Wadduwa-  Moronthuduwa, Bellana-Moragala,  to M/s Green Tech Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd., for a sum of Rs.150.75 million, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
