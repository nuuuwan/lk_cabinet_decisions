Infrastructure Development under the Jaffna Kilinochchi Water Supply and Sanitation Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the contract for supplying and laying distribution networks and improving and developing other relevant infrastructure facilities in Nallur, Kattudai, Vaddukkodai, Pungudutivu, Karainagar,  Nawatkuli, Puttur, Poonakary, Meesalai, Kodikamam, Palai, Karaveddy,  Poonery, Pokkani and Velanai areas under the Jaffna Kilinochchi Water Supply and Sanitation Project, to M/s Sierra Construction (Pvt.) Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
