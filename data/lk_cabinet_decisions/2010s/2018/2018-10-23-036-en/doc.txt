Improvement of the NAVIS Terminal Management System at the Jaya Container Terminal (JCT) and the Unity Container Terminal (UCT) at the Sri Lanka Ports Authority.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, to award the contract for the improvement of the NAVIS Terminal Management System at the Jaya Container Terminal (JCT) and the Unity Container Terminal (UCT) at the Sri Lanka Ports Authority to ACCESS NAVIS Joint Venture in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
