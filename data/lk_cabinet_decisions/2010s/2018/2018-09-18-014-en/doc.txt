Supply of equipment for the Research Unit of the Sri Lanka Anti-Doping Agency

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Faiszer Musthapha, the Minister of Provincial Councils, Local Government and Sports, to supply laboratory equipment for the Research Unit of Sri Lanka Anti-Doping Agency, with the objective of preserving the clean and spiritual sport by minimizing the risk of the Sri Lankan Athletes who participate in National and International level tournaments being alleged on the usage of  prohibited substances, was approved by the Cabinet.
