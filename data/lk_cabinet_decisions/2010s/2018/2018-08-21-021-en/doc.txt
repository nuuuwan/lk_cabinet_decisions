Procurement for the construction of the Renal Care Unit at the General Hospital in Badulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-21

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the construction of the Renal Care Unit at the General Hospital in Badulla to provide specialized care for the patients suffering from Chronic Kidney Diseases of Unknown Aetiology to M/s Orient Construction Company (Pvt) Ltd. for a sum of Rs.668.2 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
