Proposed Amendments to the Institute of Valuers of Sri Lanka Law, No.33 of 1975

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, the Minister of Finance and Mass Media, to publish the Bill drafted in accordance with the approval of the Cabinet granted to update the Institute of Valuers of Sri Lanka Law, No.33 of 1975 to suit the present socio-economic conditions, in the Government Gazette and subsequently to present the said Bill, thereafter, in Parliament, for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
