Modernization of the Lunawa Hospital, Moratuwa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- Lunawa Hospital, Moratuwa provides an immense service in providing health care services to the people in Moratuwa area. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the contract for the construction of  an Out Patient Department and a building for clinics under the renovation of the Hospital expeditiously to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) and Engineering Service (Pvt) Ltd. at a sum of Rs.1,035 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
