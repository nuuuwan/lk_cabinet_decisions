Conducting the Asia-Pacific Region Annual Conference on Express Mail Service (EMS) - 2019 in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-28

- The proposal made by Hon. M.H.A. Haleem, the Minister of Posts, Postal Services and Muslim Religious Affairs, to conduct the Asia-Pacific Region Annual Conference on Express Mail Service (EMS) - 2019 in Sri Lanka, in September 2019 with the participation of foreign representatives, was approved by the Cabinet.
