Payment of Compensation for the lands acquired for the construction of Demodara Reskuru Reservoir.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to pay compensation of Rs 61.37 million to the land owners of the acquired lands from Ella and Hali Ela divisional secretaries for the construction of Demodara Reskuru Reservoir of the Badulla Hali Ela, Ella Integrated Water Supply Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
