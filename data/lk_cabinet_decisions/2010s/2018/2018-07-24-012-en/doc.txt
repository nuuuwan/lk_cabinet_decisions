Procurement of 5000 Rails for Railway Network

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the procurement of 5000 EN 45 E1 Rails required for the Railway Network to M/s. Angang Group International Panzhima Co. Ltd., for a sum of US$ 2.13 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
