Visa Exemption between Ukraine and Sri Lanka for the holders of Diplomatic, Service and Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-02

- The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinne, the Minister of  Internal Affairs & Wayamba Development, to enter into an agreement between Sri Lanka and Ukraine to exempt from obtaining visa for the citizens of the two countries holding diplomatic, service and official passports while traveling between those two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
