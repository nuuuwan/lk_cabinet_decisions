Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Cooperation in the areas of Digitalization between Sri Lanka and Finland

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Harin Fernando, the Minister of Telecommunication, Digital Infrastructure and Foreign Employment, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Cooperation in the fields of Digitalization so as to cover the areas of  Cyber Security, E-Government, Digital Services and Critical Online Communication Methods, between Sri Lanka and  Finland during the official visit of the Deputy Minister of Economic Affairs and Employment of Finland, to Sri Lanka from 10th to 13th June 2018, was approved by the Cabinet.
