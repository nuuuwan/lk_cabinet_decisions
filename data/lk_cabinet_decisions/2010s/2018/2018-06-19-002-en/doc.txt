Development of 06 Mini Milk Processing Centres in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-19

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of National Policies and Economic Affairs, to secure the funds necessary for the development of 30 milk collecting centres and 06 milk processing centres in the areas of Kaduwela, Attanagalla, Polonnaruwa, Batticaloa, Wariyapola and Wennappuwa with the expectation of enhancing the income level of dairy farmers through the increase of the capability of producing fresh milk in the rural areas, was approved by the Cabinet.
