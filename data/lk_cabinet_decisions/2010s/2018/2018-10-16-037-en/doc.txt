Construction of a steel sheet pile hood protection wall on the left bank of 	the Kelani River which has eroded

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-10-16

- It has been identified that nearly 200 families have been affected due to banks	of Kelani river have severely eroded in the Kolonnawa, Halmulla areas. As a	remedial measure, it has been proposed to construct a steel sheet pile hood	protection wall along the river bank. Accordingly, the proposal made by	Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Irrigation and Water Resources & 	Disaster Management, to carry out the said constructions with an investment of 	Rs.604 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
