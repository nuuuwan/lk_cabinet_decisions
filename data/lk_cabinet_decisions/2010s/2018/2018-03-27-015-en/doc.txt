Supply of electricity to the State Vesak Festival of 2562nd  Year of Buddhist Era

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-03-27

The approval of the Cabinet has been granted to conduct the State Vesak Festival this year at the historical Bingiriya Rajamaha Viharaya in the Kurunegala District. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Gamini Jayawickrema Perera, the Minister of Buddhasasana, to secure the funds further required for the supply of electricity to the Bingiriya Rajamaha Viharaya, where the main function will be conducted and the surrounding areas, was approved by the Cabinet
