Construction of 02 Wave Rider Inshore Patrol Crafts for the Coast Guard of the Republic of Seychelles

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-11-21

- During a state visit by H.E. the President in the Republic of Seychelles, H.E.  had acceded to a request made to construct two Wave Rider Inshore Patrol Crafts for the coast guard of the Republic of Seychelles. Accordingly, with a view to strengthening the diplomatic relations between the two countries, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as  the Minister of Defence, to carry out the construction activities of the Wave Rider Inshore Patrol Crafts by the Sri Lanka Navy, was approved by the Cabinet.
