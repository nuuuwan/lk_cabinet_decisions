Conducting a feasibility study on the construction of the Civil Airport at Digana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to conduct a feasibility study on the construction of a domestic airport in Maberiyatenna of Digana in the Kandy District for the  operation of medium size aircrafts linking the Kandy City and the other ares of the Island, was approved by the Cabinet.
