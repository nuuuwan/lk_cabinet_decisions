Construction of flyovers in Kohuwala, Gatambe and Heerassagala.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road 	Development, to construct 03 flyovers in Kohuwala, Gatambe and	Heerassagala and to award the contract for the said purpose to the Betonutepito 	Plc recommended by the Government of Hungary with an investment of 	Euro 58.68 million and with the financial assistance of the Government of 	Hungary, was approved by the Cabinet.
