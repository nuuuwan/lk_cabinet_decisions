Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Institute of National Security Studies of Sri Lanka (INSSSL) and the Institute of  Strategic Studies Islamabad (ISSI) of Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-03-20

- A proposal has been made to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Institute of National Security Studies of Sri Lanka (INSSSL) and the Institute of  Strategic Studies Islamabad (ISSI) of Pakistan with the objective of promoting cooperation between Sri Lanka and Pakistan on the activities relating to National Security Study. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as  the Minister of Defence, to enter into the proposed MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
