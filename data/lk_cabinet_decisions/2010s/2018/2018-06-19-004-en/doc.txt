Bilateral Consultations between Sri Lanka and Thailand pertaining to Air Services

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-06-19

- The Delegations representing Sri Lanka and Thailand Aeronautical Authorities held discussion with regard to the air services plans of the two countries at the Civil Air Services Conference held in December 2017 in Colombo. The 2 parties reached consensus with regard to the  air services agreement scheduled to be signed between the two countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Ashoka Abeysinghe the acting Ministers of Transport and Civil Aviation,  to enter into force of this Agreement and to implement it effectively, was approved by the Cabinet.
