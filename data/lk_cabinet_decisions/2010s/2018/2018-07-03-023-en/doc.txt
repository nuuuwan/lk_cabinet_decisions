Constructing of 1,500 cum capacity water tower in Minuwangoda Area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-03

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the contract for constructing of 1,500 cum capacity water tower and laying transmission and distribution mains scheduled to be constructed in Minuwangoda area as a part of the Gampaha Attanagalla and Minuwangoda Integrated Water Supply Project to M/s Finite Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd. as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee,  was approved by the Cabinet.
