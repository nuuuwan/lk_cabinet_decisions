Agreement with the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Morocco on Visa exemption for holders of Diplomatic, Official and Service Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinne, the Minister of  Internal Affairs & Wayamba Development to enter into an Agreement with the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Morocco on Visa exemption for holders of Diplomatic, Official and Service Passports, for their visits between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
