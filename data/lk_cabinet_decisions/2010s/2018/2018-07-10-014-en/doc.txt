Development of the Deniyaya Weekly Fair

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

The proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayake, the Minister of Youth Affairs, Project Management and Southern Development, to improve and develop the  Deniyaya Weekly Fair which is present in a dilapidated condition as a trade complex with 80 to 120 shops inclusive of a new road net work around the  trade complex and proper sanitary facilities with a view to providing necessary facilities for daily commuters of 25,000 arriving in the Deniyaya town, was approved by the Cabinet.
