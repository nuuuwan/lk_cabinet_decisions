Construction of the Stage II of the Accident and Emergency Treatment Unit at the Teaching Hospital in Jaffna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-10

- As Stage II of the Construction of a five storey building for the Accident and Emergency Treatment Unit at the Teaching Hospital in Jaffna, the construction activities of the 2nd floor up to the 5th floor of the said building has been planned to be constructed. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the construction work of Stage II of the Accident and Emergency Treatment Unit at the Teaching Hospital in Jaffna to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau and the Central Engineering Services Private Limited, for a sum of Rs.1,224.6 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
