Pilgrims' Facility and Landscape Improvement Project at Sellakatharagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-07

- The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, the Minister of Megapolis and Western Development, to secure the funds required for the  implementation of a project to improve common amenities for pilgrims visiting Sellakatharagama and Landscape Improvement, was approved by the Cabinet.
