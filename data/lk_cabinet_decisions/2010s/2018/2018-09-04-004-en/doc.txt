Implementation of the Tharapuram Co-operative Village Programme in the Mannar District

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-04

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously for the development work carried out under the Tharapuram Co-operative Village Programme in the Mannar District. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, the Minister of Industry and Commerce, to construct a Convention Centre as the next stage of the multipurpose building under construction, utilizing the provision set apart under the said project, was approved by the Cabinet.
