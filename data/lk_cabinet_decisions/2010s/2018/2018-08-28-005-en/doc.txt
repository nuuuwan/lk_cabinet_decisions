Amendments to the Sri Lanka Institute of Development Administration Act, No.9 of 1982

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-28

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister of Public Administration & Management and Law & Order, to make amendments to the Sri Lanka Institute of Development Administration Act, No.9 of 1982, incorporating the necessary amendments to suit the present needs of the functions and the administrative structure of the Sri Lanka Institute of Development Administration, was approved by the Cabinet.
