Rehabilitating and improving Medawachchiya-Horowupathana Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-09-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Kabir Hashim, the Minister of Highways & Road Development, to rehabilitate and improve 27.8 km long road section from 10 km to 37.8 km on  Medawachchiya-Horowupathana Road by using the balance funds after the completion of project activities scheduled to be implemented under the Northern Road Connectivity Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
