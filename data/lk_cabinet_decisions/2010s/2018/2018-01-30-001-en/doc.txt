Directions to prevent the use of the property belonging to the State  during the period of election for the purpose of promoting political activities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-01-30

- With a view to preventing the use of any movable or immovable property belonging to the State or any Public Corporation during the period of election, for the purposes of promoting political parties, groups and candidates or causing injustice or obstructing the conduct of a free and fair election, the Direction, No.2048/2 made by the Election Commission under sub paragraph (a) of paragraph(4) of Article 104(B) of the Constitution, has been published in the Gazette Extraordinary Notification dated 2017-12-04. As such, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, to present the Gazette Notification, incorporating the said provisions in Parliament by the adoption of a resolution, was approved by the Cabinet.
