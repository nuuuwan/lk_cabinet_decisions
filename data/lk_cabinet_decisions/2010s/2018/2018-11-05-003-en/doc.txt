Tariffs Revision for the Services provided by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority and  the Jaya Bahalu Container Terminal (Pvt.) Ltd

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-11-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Samarasinghe, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, to revise the tariff charges for the services provided by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority and  the Jaya Bahalu Container Terminal (Pvt.) Ltd in keeping with the present market trends and enabling to provide a more qualitative service and to introduce a uniform method of  tariff rates among the harbours, was approved by the Cabinet.
