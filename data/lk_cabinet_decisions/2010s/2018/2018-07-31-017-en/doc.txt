Procurement for the Supply of 13,000 Vials of Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-07-31

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health, Nutrition and Indigenous Medicine, to award the procurement for the supply of 13,000 Vials of Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vials used to prevent a person's  death suffering from a heart attack, to Boehringer Ingelheim India Private Limited of India for a sum of US$ 5.525 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
