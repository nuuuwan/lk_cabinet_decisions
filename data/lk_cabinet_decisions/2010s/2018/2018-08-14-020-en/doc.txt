Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Sultanate of Oman on Visa Exemption

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-08-14

- The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinne, the Minister of  Internal Affairs & Wayamba Development, to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding on Visa Exemption for holders of diplomatic, Special, Service and Official Passports, between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Sultanate of Oman, was approved by the Cabinet.
