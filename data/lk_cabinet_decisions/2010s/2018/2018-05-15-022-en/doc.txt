Granting of permission for the operation of Currency Exchange Counters in the Arrival Terminal of the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), at Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-05-15

- The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to avail the permission for maintaining the operation of Currency Exchange Counters in the Arrival Terminal beyond the Customs area at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayake, to the Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC, Sampath Bank PLC, Thomas Cook Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd., Bank of Ceylon and the People's Bank, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
