Climate Change Mitigation Action Support Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2018-11-05

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to implement the Climate Change Mitigation Action Support Project for the mitigation of the greenhouse gas emission, under the World Bank assistance. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President Maithripala Sirisena, in his capacity as the Minister of Mahaweli Development and Environment, to establish a Project Management Unit comprising of the officials of the Ministry of Mahaweli Development and Environment and the persons with expertise knowledge in the relevant fields, for the proper management of the project, was approved by the Cabinet.
