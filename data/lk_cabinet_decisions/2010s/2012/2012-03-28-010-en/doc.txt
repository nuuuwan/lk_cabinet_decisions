Awarding the contract of conducting Pre-feasibility Studies for the preparation of a new Railroad Plan through Embilipitiya, Moneragala, Kataragama, Ampara, Batticaloa areas covering of the South Eastern areas of the Island, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-03-28

- the proposal made Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of Transport,  to award the contract of conducting the pre-feasibility study for the construction of a new rail road from Embilipitiya to Batticaloa via Moneragala and Ampara and for the construction of a new rail road from Kataragama to Moneragala, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) which is a government concern, was approved by the Cabinet.
