Annual Report and Accounts of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year  2011

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-03

- according to the above Annual Report, the Gross Domestic Production of Sri Lanka has reported a growth of 8.3%. The per capita income of the year 2010 which was US $2400 has increased up to US $2836 during the year. The above Report submitted by H.E. the President, in his capacity of the Minister of Finance and Planning, was approved to be presented in Parliament.
