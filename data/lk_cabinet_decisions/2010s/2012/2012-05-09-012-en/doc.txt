Aircraft Refueling Facility at New Southern Airport at Mattala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-09

- the proposal made by Hon. Susil Premajayantha, Minister of Petroleum Industries, to authorize the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation, to award the contract for the construction of a Fuel Hydrant System outside the Apron Area and the Aviation Fuel Storage Tank Farm for a lump sum price of US $31,251,584.02, on the recommendations made by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
