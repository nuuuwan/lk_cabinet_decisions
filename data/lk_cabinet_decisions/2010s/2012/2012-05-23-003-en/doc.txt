Amendment of the Notaries Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-23

? the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs,  to amend the Notaries Ordinance pertaining to regulations on attesting of documents by Notaries and fines to be imposed for offenses committed by Notaries and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare amending legislation for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
