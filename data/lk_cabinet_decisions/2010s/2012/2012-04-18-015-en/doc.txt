Extension of the Agreement between Ceylon Petroleum Corporation  (CPC) and Fujairah Petroleum Products  LLC, UAE (FPP, UAE) for a further period of six  (06) months for the Import of Low Sulphur Fuel Oil (1.8% Sulphur Max)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Sarana Gunawardena, Acting Minister of Petroleum Industries, to extend the existing agreement for the import of Low Sulphur Fuel Oil, for a further period of six months from 01.05.2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
