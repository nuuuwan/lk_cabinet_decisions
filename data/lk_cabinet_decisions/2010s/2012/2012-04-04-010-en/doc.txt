Construction of a Building for the Extension to Administration Building, University of Moratuwa - Revision of Total Cost Estimate

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education to construct a five-storied building for the purpose and for the extension to the existing administration building of the University of Moratuwa and to revise the total cost estimate from Rs.171 million to Rs.282 million, was approved by the Cabinet.  The project is to be commenced in 2012 and completed in 2016, with funds to be provided under the Medium Term Budgetary Frame Work 2012 - 2016.
