Providing Uniform material to school children for the academic year 2013

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-09

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to import 50% of the total quantity of School Uniform material for the year 2013 through the Sri Lanka State Trading (Miscellaneous) Corporation, following the competitive bidding procedure, was approved by the Cabinet.
