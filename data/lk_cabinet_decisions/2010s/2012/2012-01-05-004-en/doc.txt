Project for the supply, installation, testing and commissioning of 02 Nos. Cardiac Catheterization systems and 02 Nos. Echo Cardiography Machines to the Cardiology Department of the National Hospital of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-05

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health to award the above contract to a company in Austria, on the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
