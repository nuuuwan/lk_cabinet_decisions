Allocation of the land in extent of 05 acres owned by the Coconut Research Institute to the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

? the joint proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education and Hon. A.P. Jagath Pushpakumara, Minister of Coconut Development and Janatha Estate Development, to allocate 05 acres of land from the Makandura Research Centre, belonging to the Coconut Research Institute, to the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka, for the purpose of constructing the required buildings needed for the expansion of the University, was approved by the Cabinet.
