Improvement of physical facilities at the Technical College, Matale

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-26

- the proposal made by Hon. Dallas Alahapperuma, Minister of Youth Affairs and Skills Development, to construct a two storied building consisting of six class rooms, a computer room, and a room for plan drawing and other ancillary buildings etc. and for the provision of other necessary equipment at a cost of Rs.135 million, with funds to be provided through the Tertiary and Vocational Education Development Programme to be financed by the Asian Development Bank during 2013-2016 and to obtain the services of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau for the preparation of the relevant plans and consultations with regard to the construction work, was approved by the Cabinet.
