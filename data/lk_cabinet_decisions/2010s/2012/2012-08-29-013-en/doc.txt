Construction of an overlay for the Runway at Ampara Airport

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-08-29

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to construct an overlay for the Runway at Ampara Airport, under the "Deyata Kirula 2013 Programme" and to invite competitive bids from domestic contractors registered with the Institute for Construction Training and Development (ICTAD) for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
