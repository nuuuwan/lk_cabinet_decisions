Establishment of Sri Lanka Mission in Bahrain

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-08-29

-  	the joint proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, and Hon. Dilan Perera, Minister of Foreign Employment Promotion, to establish a Sri Lanka Mission in Bahrain, to deal with matters on welfare of migrant workers and promotion of employment opportunities, trade and investment along with other consular and diplomatic matters, was approved by the Cabinet.
