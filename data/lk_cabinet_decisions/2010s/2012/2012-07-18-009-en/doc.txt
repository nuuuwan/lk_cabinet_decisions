Award of Contract for Design & Construction of 01 Bridge and Construction of 02 Bridges on Moragahakanda - Wellawela Road under Kaluganga Left Bank Development to the State Development and Construction Corporation  (SD & CC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-18

-   the proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, Minister of Irrigation and Water Resources Management, to award the above contract to the State Development and Construction Corporation (SD & CC), depending on its capacity, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), for a sum of Rs.99.50 million, utilizing funds allocated for the Kaluganga Development Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
