Rehabilitation of National Highways with concrete surfacing through Domestic Contractors - balance and urgent improvement works on the Road Section from Nugegoda to Homagama from 1.00 Km. to 15.36 Km. on Colombo-Ratnapura Road (A4)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-28

- on the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to carry out the balance and urgent improvement works that are required for the above road section, Cabinet approval was granted to include the above project in the Annual Programme of the Road Development Authority.
