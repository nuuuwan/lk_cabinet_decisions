Handing over of the Vaishakya Girls? College, Wellawatte to St. Pauls Girls? College, Milagiriya for development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-03-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to hand over the Vaishakya Girls' College, Wellawatta which has been handed over by the Western Provincial Council to the Ministry of Education, to St. Pauls Girls' College, Milagiriya, for development of the school, was approved by the Cabinet.
