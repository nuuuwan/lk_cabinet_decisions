Agreement between Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan and the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on Cooperation in the field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Basil Rajapaksa, Minister of Economic Development to sign and ratify an Agreement on Co-operation in the field of tourism between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.  The proposed Agreement will enhance the bilateral relationship between the two countries and strengthen the tourism flow into Sri Lanka and open new avenues for trade and investments.
