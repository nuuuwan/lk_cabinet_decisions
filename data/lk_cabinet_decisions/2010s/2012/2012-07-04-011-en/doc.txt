Construction of a Telecommunications Media Centre by the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka at Hambantota IT Park

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

- the site earmarked for this project is within the proposed IT Park at Meegahajandura, Sooriyawewa which is owned by the Forest Department. The site possesses high potential for development as it is located adjacent to the Sooriyawewa International Sports City and is close to the Mattala International Airport. The proposal made by H.E. the President to proceed with the project, was approved by the Cabinet.
