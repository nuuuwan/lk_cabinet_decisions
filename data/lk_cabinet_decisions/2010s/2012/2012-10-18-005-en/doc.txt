'Deyata Sevana' National Tree Planting Programme - 15th November 2012

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Anura Priyadharashana Yapa, Minister of Environment, to conduct this year's National Ceremony of the Deyata Sevana National Tree Planting Programme on 15th November 2012, with the participation of all Ministries, Departments, Corporations, Statutory Boards, Provincial Councils, District and Divisional Administration, was approved by the Cabinet.
