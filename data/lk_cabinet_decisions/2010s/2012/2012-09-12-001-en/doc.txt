Allocation of land at Rajagiriya to the  Excise Department of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-12

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to allocate a block of land 30.26 perches in extent at Rajagiriya Town, owned by the Urban Development Authority, to the Excise Department, through the Commissioner General of Lands, for the construction of its Head Office building, was approved by the Cabinet.
