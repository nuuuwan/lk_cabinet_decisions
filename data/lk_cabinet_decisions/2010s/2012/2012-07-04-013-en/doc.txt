Rehabilitation of National Highways with Concrete Surfacing through Domestic Contractors - Rehabilitation and Upgrading of sections from 0 to 8.40 km and 19.00 to 33.50 km on Naula-Elahera-Laggala-Pallegama-Hettipola Road (B312)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

- the above road is one of the roads approved for rehabilitation under the above programme. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, to award the contract at the negotiated contract price of Rs.2,053,529,400.33, was approved by the Cabinet.
