Supply of 500MT of Whole Milk Powder for the Thriposha Programme 2012

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-03-28

- on the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health pertaining to the above, Cabinet approval was granted to call for International Competitive Bidding for the supply of 500 Mt. of Whole Milk Powder for the Thriposha Programme 2012.   In the meantime it was decided that the Ministry should explore the possibility of obtaining the required quantity of milk powder for the manufacture of Thriposha from MILCO, as an interim measure.
