License to Import Bitumen - Grade 60/70

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-14

- the proposal made by Hon. Wimal Weerawansa, Minister of Construction Engineering, Housing and Common Amenities, to grant a license to the State Development and Construction Corporation (SD&CC) to import 10,000 mt. of Bitumen for the rehabilitation of 370 km. of Provincial Roads in Kegalle and Polonnaruwa Districts,  was approved by the Cabinet.
