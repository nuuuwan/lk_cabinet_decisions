Construction of four storied Pre-Cast Building Complex for the Faculty of Humanities  - University of Kelaniya (Second Stage of Building K20)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-26

- the University of Kelaniya intends to improve the teaching and learning environment of the Faculty of Humanities through the construction of a building containing Lecture Rooms, Auditorium, Laboratories, Libraries etc.  The proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayaka, Minister of Higher Education, to award the construction of the above at a total cost of Rs.25 million, to the State Engineering Corporation, was approved by the Cabinet.
