Bandaranaike International Airport Development Project - Phase 2 - Japan International Co-operation Agency (JICA)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-02-29

-  the Government of Japan has agreed to provide a loan of Japanese Yen 28,969 million (approximately Rs.40,557 million) through the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) for implementation of the above project.  The objective of this project is to improve and modernize the facilities of the Bandaranaike International Airport, so as to contribute towards the promotion of tourism as well as to the economic development of the country.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to enter into a loan agreement for the purpose with JICA, was approved by the Cabinet.
