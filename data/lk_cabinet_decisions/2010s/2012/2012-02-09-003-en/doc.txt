Construction of 792 Housing Units (02 Nos. 12 storied building blocks) at 	Salamulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-02-09

- this is a Low Income Housing Project to be implemented by the Sri Lanka 	Land Reclamation and Development Corporation, at Vijaya Road, Salamulla, 	Kolonnawa, for the people living in shanties in the area. The housing complex consists 	of 3 blocks and the piling work of  block 'A' for the construction of 216 Housing Units, 	has already been completed. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as 	the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, on the recommendation of the 	Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, to award the contract for piling  	for the construction of 792 Housing Units in blocks 'B' and 'C' at a total cost of 	Rs.270,636,190.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
