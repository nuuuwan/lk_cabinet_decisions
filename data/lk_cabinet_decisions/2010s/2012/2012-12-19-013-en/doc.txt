Air Conveyance of Mails from Colombo to Foreign  Destinations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-19

- the proposal made by Hon. Jeevan Kumaranatunga, Minister of Postal Services to award the contract for the conveying of mails from Colombo to 99 foreign destinations, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
