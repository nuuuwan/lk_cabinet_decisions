Supply of uniforms and other clothing materials for the Army, Air Force, Police, Special Task Force, Department of Civil Security and National Cadet Crops - 2012

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-03

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, for the purchase of uniform material required for the above services for the year 2012, from domestic manufactures, on the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee, at a total cost of Rs.2,034,031,720/-, was approved by the Cabinet.
