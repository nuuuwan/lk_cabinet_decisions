Construction of a new Conference Hall for District Secretariat Kandy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-03-14

- the proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home 	Affairs, to construct a new Conference Hall with modern facilities at the premises of the District Secretariat, Kandy, at a total cost of Rs.200 million was approved by the Cabinet.  Initial work of the project is to be commenced with the financial provision provided to the Ministry for the year 2012.
