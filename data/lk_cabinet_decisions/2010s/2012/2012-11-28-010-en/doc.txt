Construction of a five storied Building Complex for the Faculty of Social Sciences - University of Kelaniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-28

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayaka, Minister of Higher Education, for the construction of the above building complex at a total cost of Rs.45 million, with the view to providing enhanced accommodation for the increased intake of students for the University in 2013 and to award the construction work to the State Engineering Corporation, was approved by the Cabinet.  Cabinet also decided to provide 25% of the total cost estimate from the Consolidated Fund as a token of appreciation of the endeavours to develop the University from the income generated by the|University itself.
