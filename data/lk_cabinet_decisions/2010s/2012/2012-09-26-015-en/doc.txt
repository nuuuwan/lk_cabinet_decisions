Financial Assistance for the Reconstruction of 6 Railway Bridges under Belgium Assistance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-26

- 6 bridges at Wellawatta, Polgahawela, Narahenpita, Navatkuli, Dehiwela and Angulana have been identified to be reconstructed under the above project.  The proposal made by H.E. the President, to enter into a loan agreement with Belgium to borrow Euro 4,438,681.70 for implementation of the above project, was approved by the Cabinet.
