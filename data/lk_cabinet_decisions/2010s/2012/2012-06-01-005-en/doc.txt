Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between Sri Lanka and Seychelles for Co-operation in Fisheries Activities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development, to sign a fresh Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and Seychelles, during the proposed visit by H.E. the President of Seychelles to Sri Lanka, in order to  intensify the economic, scientific and technical co-operation in the field of fisheries between the two countries,was approved by the Cabinet.
