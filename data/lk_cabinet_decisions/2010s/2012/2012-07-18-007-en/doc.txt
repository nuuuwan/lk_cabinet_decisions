Memorandum of Understanding with the Republic of Korea for sending workers under the Employment Permit System (EPS)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Dilan Perera, Minister of Foreign Employment Promotion and Welfare, to enter into the above Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Korea, to enhance the existing friendly relations between the two countries through cooperation in the fields of employment and labour affairs and human capacity building, was approved by the Cabinet.
