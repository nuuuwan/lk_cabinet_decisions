Memorandum of Understanding on Cultural Cooperation Between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Indonesia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-25

- the proposal made by Hon. T.B. Ekanayake, Minister of Culture and the Arts, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on Cultural Cooperation between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Indonesia, to promote and develop cultural cooperation between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
