Allocation of land at Ratnapura New Town, to the Lions Vision for Sight Hospital Trust, Ratnapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to lease out a block of land to the ?Lions Vision for Sight Hospital Trust?, Ratnapura, was approved by the Cabinet.  Funds for the construction of the hospital building are to be provided by the International Lions Club of  Finland and on completion, it will be a fully equipped Eye Hospital with 50 beds.
