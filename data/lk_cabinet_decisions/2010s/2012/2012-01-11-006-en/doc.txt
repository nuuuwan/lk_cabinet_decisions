Rehabilitation of National Highways with Concrete Surfacing through	Domestic	Contractors - Rehabilitation of Anamaduwa-Uswewa-	Galgamuwa Road (39.1	km)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-11

- this is a road identified for rehabilitation under	the rehabilitation programme for	662.04 Km. of roads through domestic	contractors by obtaining the required funds amounting to US $500 million	fromlocal Banks. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as	the Minister of Ports and Highways, to award the above contract, on the	recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, at 	the negotiated contract sum of Rs.2,561,638,611.91, was approved by the 	Cabinet.
