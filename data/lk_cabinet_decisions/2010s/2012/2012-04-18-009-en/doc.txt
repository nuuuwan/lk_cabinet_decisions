Tender for Supply of Sodium Chloride Intravenous Infusion BP 9% w/v 500ml (4,200,000 bottles)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the above tender to the lowest responsive bidder, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, for a total cost of US $1,591,800.00 (LKR 181,305,180.00), was approved by the Cabinet.
