Construction of a Building for the Library of Faculty of Engineering - University of  Ruhuna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-09

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to construct a three storied building for the Library of the Faculty of Engineering of the University of  Ruhuna at a total cost of Rs.128.6 million, to commence this project in 2012 and complete construction work within three years, was approved by the Cabinet.
