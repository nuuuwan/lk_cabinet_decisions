The Memorandum of Understanding signed between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-03-21

- covering approval of the Cabinet of Ministers was granted for the above Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) which was signed on 11th February 2012 between Pakistan and Sri Lanka and submitted by  Hon. Dullas Alahapperuma, Minister of Youth Affairs and Skills Development.  This MOU has been prepared with the aim of developing the long term cooperation between the two countries through the provision of competency standards, course curricula, teaching and assessment material, which were developed under the National Vocational Qualification Framework of Sri Lanka.
