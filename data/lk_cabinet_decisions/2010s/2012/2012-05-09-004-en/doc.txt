4th  Session of the Bangladesh - Sri Lanka Joint Commission on Economic Cooperation -7th to 8th March, 2012 in Dhaka, Bangladesh

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-09

? Cabinet noted that several important decisions in the fields of trade, commerce and industry, agriculture, aquaculture, shipping industry, tourism, information and communication technology, education and fisheries sector, had been taken at this meeting to strengthen the cooperation between the two countries. The Sri Lanka delegation for the meeting had been led by Hon. Rishad Bathiudeen, Minister of Industry and Commerce.
