Protocol on Political Consultations between the Ministry of External Affairs of    Sri Lanka and the Ministry of foreign Affairs of the State of Palestine

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-05

- this agreement would enhance friendly relations between Sri Lanka and the State of Palestine and would contribute to international and regional  peace, security and prosperity. The proposal made by Hon. (Prof) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to sign the above agreement during the visit of the Hon. Minister of Foreign affairs of Palestine to Sri Lanka in January 2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
