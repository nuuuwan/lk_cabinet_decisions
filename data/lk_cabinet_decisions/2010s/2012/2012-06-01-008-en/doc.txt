Construction of Forty Six (46) Bridges on National Highways with Financial Assistance from the Government of France

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- the Government of France is to provide a loan of Euro 22,021,702.00 for the construction of 46 bridges on the national highways. The proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways,on the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), to award the above contract to a company in France, for a sum of Euro 22,021,702.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
