Project for the construction of the proposed Ten Storeyed Building for the Army Hospital

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-03

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to construct a 10 storeyed building with modern facilities for the Army Hospital at a total cost of Rs.4105 million with financial provision to be provided between 2013 - 2015, was approved by the Cabinet.
