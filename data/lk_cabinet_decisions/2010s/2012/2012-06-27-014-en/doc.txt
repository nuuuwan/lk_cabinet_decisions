Construction of an Extension to the Building of the Ministry of Higher	Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-27

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher	Education, to construct an extension to the existing  building of the Ministry of	Higher Education, on the recommendation of the Department of National Planning, 	with the view to providing more accommodation to the Ministry office as well as	the University Grants Commission, was approved by the Cabinet. Initial	construction	work of the project is to be commenced in 2012, utilizing funds	allocated to the Ministry for the year 2012.
