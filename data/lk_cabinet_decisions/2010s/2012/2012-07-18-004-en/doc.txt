Establishment of Formal Diplomatic Relations between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Republic of Congo (Brazzaville)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-18

- the proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, for the establishment of Diplomatic Relations between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Congo (Brazzaville), to strengthen the relations of friendship and cooperation between the two countries in the political, economic and cultural fields, was approved by the Cabinet.
