Bilateral Air Services Negotiations between Sri Lanka and Indonesia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-12

- the proposal made by Hon. Piyankara Jayaratne, Minister of Civil Aviation, to amend the existing Air Services Agreement signed in December 1993, to meet the present day needs, was approved by the Cabinet.  Under this Agreement, Sri Lanka and Indonesia will be entitled to operate up to a total of 14 Weekly Services with any type of Air Craft.
