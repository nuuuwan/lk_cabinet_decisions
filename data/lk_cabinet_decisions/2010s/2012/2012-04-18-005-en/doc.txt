Panduwasnuwara - Hettipola Town Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

- Hettipola town and Panduwasnuwara sacred area fall under the Panduwasnuwara Pradesheeya Sabha in the Kurunegala District.  A sum of Rs.40 million has been allocated for this project in the 2012 Budget Estimates. The proposal made by Hon. A.L.M. Athaullah, Minister of Local Government and Provincial Councils, to proceed with the project, was approved by the Cabinet.
