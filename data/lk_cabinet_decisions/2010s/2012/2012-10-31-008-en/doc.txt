Constructing a New Building at Sangamitta Girls' College, Galle for the continuation of Grade 1 Classes

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-31

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to construct the ground floor and the first floor of the proposed 3 storeyed building for the above school, utilizing Rs.15 million to be provided by the School Development Society and to entrust the construction and related activities to a State Institution engaged in construction work on limited competitive bidding basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
