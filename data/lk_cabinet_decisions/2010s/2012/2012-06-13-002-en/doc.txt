Upgrading the Chest Hospital, Welisara

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-13

- This is the only Chest Hospital in Sri Lanka which caters to the patients throughout the country.  Due to the increase in respiratory diseases prevailing in the country and to provide better facilities to patients, the proposal made by  Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to  upgrade the Chest Hospital, Welisara, was approved by the Cabinet.
