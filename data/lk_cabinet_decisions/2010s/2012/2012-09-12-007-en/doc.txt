Shifting of CEB Transmission line to facilitate the constructions and operations of Outer Circular  Highway North Section - 1, Kaduwela to Kadawatha

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-12

- the construction of Phase II of the above project is underway and it is necessary to permanently relocate the high voltage electricity transmission lines of the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) to facilitate the ongoing construction works of this project.  The proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Power and Energy, to proceed with the work pertaining to the shifting of the said electricity transmission lines and to entrust the work to M/s. LTL Holdings which is a subsidiary of the Ceylon Electricity Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
