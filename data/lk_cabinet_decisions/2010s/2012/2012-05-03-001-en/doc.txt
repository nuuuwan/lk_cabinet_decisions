Amending the Ranaviru Seva Authority Act, No. 54 of 1999

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-03

- the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to amend paragraphs (b), (c) and (d) of Sub Section (2)(I) of the above Act to include the Chief of Staff of the Sri Lanka Army, Chief of Staff of the Sri Lanka Navy and the Chief of Staff of the Sri Lanka Air Force respectively, in the Board of Management, was approved by the Cabinet.
