Consultancy Services for the Construction of Railway Lines from Pallai to Kankesanthurai

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

- the proposal made by  Hon. Kumara Welgama, Minister of  Transport, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed  Procurement Committee (SCAPC), for the appointment of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) as the Consultant for this project, was approved by the Cabinet.
