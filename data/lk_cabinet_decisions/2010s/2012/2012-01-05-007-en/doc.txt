Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) & Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-05

- this MOU provides a framework for technical and institutional co-operation in the field of telecommunication between India and Sri Lanka.  The proposal made by H.E. the President to enter into the above MOU, was approved by the Cabinet.
