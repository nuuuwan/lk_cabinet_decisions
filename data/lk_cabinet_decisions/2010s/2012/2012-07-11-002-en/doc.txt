Draft Agreement between the Governments of Sri Lanka and India for the	avoidance of Double Taxation and prevention of Fiscal Evasion with respect to	Taxes on Income

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-11

-  Cabinet approval has already been granted for signing of the above 	Draft	Agreement at Government level and for its presentation in Parliament.  Certain	amendments proposed by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance	and Planning, based on the negotiations held subsequently in Colombo, was approved by 	the Cabinet for inclusion in the above Draft agreement.
