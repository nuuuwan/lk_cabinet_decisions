Allocation of a block of land at Madiwela to the Parliament of Sri Lanka for the construction of an Administrative Office Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

? the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to allocate a block of land at Madiwela belonging to the Urban Development Authority (UDA) to the Sri Lanka Parliament, for the construction of an administrative office complex to house the offices which are not directly connected to its legislative and related matters, was approved by the Cabinet.
