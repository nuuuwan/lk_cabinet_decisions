Proposal to amend the High Court of the Provinces Act No. 10 of 1996

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, Minister of Justice, to increase the  monetary value of actions that will be heard by the Commercial High Court  from its present value of Rs. 3 million which was introduced nearly 16 years ago, to Rs.5 million and to make such increase to be effective from 31st May 2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
