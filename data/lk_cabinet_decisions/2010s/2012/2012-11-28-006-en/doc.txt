Construction of Paediatric Eye Unit (Stage II) - Construction of the Neuro and Kidney Unit and Orthopedic Ward - Lady Ridgeway Hospital - Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-28

- construction work of the ground floor and the 1st floor under Stage I of the project has been completed and the proposal of Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to proceed with the work relating  to the construction of the 2nd and 3rd floors of the building under Stage II of the project consisting of the Neuro and Kidney Units (2nd floor) and the  Orthopedic Ward (3rd floor), was approved by the Cabinet.
