Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of External Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of Thailand on Bilateral Political Consultations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- this Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) will cover bilateral relations and cooperation of mutual interest between the two countries and will also provide a frame work for co-operation in the field of political, economic, trade, investment, cultural, technological and scientific co-operation between the two countries. The above proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, was approved by the Cabinet.
