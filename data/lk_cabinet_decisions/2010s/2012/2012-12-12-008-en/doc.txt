Procurement for the Supply, Installation & Commissioning of Doppler VOR & Distant Measuring Equipment at Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-12

- the proposal made by Hon. Piyankara Jayaratne, Minister of Civil Aviation, to award the procurement for supply, installation and commissioning of the above at the Bandaranaike International Airport for a total foreign component of US $919,437 and local component of Rs.14,405,000.00, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
