Shilpa 2012 Craft and Small  Medium Industry Exhibition and Trade Fair

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-26

- this Exhibition and Trade Fair will be held for 4 days from 27th December 2012 at the BMICH.  This event plays a pivotal role in supporting handicraftsmen and small and medium scale entrepreneurs to strengthen and develop their craft skills and marketing channels.  The National Crafts Council, the National Design Centre, Industrial Development Board and Palmyrah Development Board will be associated in making necessary arrangements for this Exhibition and Trade Fair.  The proposal made by Hon. Douglas Devananda, Minister of Traditional Industries and Small Enterprise Development, to proceed with the programme was approved by the Cabinet.
