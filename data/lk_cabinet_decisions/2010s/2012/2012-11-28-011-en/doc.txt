Widening and Improvements to Airport Runway and Apron at Ampara Airport

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-28

-  the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to award the above contract to the lowest evaluated responsive bid for a contract price of Rs.749,336,167.05, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet. The work is intended to be carried out in line with the activities of 'Deyata Kirula, 2013 National Exhibition'.
