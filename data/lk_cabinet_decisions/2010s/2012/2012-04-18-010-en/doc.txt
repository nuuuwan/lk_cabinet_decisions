Construction of Head Office Building of Lanka Electricity Company (Private) Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of  Power and Energy to construct an eight storied building for the Head Office of Lanka Electricity Company (Private) Ltd. at a total estimated cost of Rs.500 million and to obtain the services of the University of Moratuwa for designing, preparation of tender documents etc., was approved by the Cabinet.
