Tender for the Supply of Dried Factor VIII Fraction BP, 18000 Vials

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health to award the above tender to the lowest responsive bidder, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee for a total cost of US $1,196,640.00 (LKR 131,750,640.00), was approved by the Cabinet.
