Reconstruction of Janakala premises at Battaramulla to promote Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-10

- the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Jagath Balasooriya, Minister of National Heritage to utilize about two acres of Janakala Premises at Battaramulla to be developed as a model traditional village for the benefit of foreign as well as local tourists, was approved in principle by the Cabinet.
