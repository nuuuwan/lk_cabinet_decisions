Construction of Headworks of Moragahakanda Reservoir

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

? Cabinet approval has already been granted for the construction of  Moragahakanda Reservoir along with Kaluganga Reservoir. Construction of these reservoirs will facilitate to meet the long term demand for domestic and industrial water needs in the North Central, Northern and Eastern Provinces. The proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, Minister of Irrigation and Water Resources Management, to award the above contract, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Review Committee, for a sum of US$ 252,302,221, was approved by the Cabinet.
