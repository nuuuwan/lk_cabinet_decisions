Upgrading the National Institute of Traditional Medicine and the Bandaranaike Memorial Ayurvedic Research Institute to the Status of Autonomous Statutory Institutions

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-18

- the proposal made by Hon. Salinda Dissanayake, Minister of Indigenous Medicine, to upgrade  the above two (02) institutions to the status of autonomous statutory institutions and to draft necessary legislation for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
