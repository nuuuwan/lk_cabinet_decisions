Procurement of a 4000 m3 Trailing Suction Hopper Dredger

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-14

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, to appoint a Technical Evaluation Committee (TEC) to assist the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) for the purpose, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Review Committee (SCARC), was approved by the Cabinet.
