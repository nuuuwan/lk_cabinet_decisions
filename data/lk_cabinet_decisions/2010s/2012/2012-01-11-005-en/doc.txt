Pilgrims Facility Center at Sellakatharagama Development of Sellakatharagama Sacred Area and its Surrounding Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-11

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to implement the  development activities in and around Sellakatharagama Sacred area, was approved by the Cabinet.  The relevant activities will be carried out with funds provided under the Urban Development Authority for the year 2012 and any additional financial provision required for the purpose will be provided under the 2013 Budget.
