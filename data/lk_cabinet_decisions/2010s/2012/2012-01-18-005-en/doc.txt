Construction of Lecture Theatre Complex of Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-18

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to complete construction work of the above project so as to provide additional facilities very early to the University students, was approved by the Cabinet.  The work will be prioritized and funds allocated to the University Grants Commission for the year 2012 will be utilized for the purpose.
