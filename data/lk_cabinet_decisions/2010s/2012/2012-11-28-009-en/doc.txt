Construction of the Six (06) Storeyed Building for National Agricultural Information and Communication Center, Department of Agriculture, Gannoruwa, Kandy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-28

- on a joint proposal made by Hon. Wimal Weerawansa, Minister of Construction, Engineering Services, Housing and Common Amenities; and Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, Cabinet decided to select a suitable contractor through open bidding from among construction contractors registered with ICTAD including State Institutions to entrust the above construction work.
