Construction of Stage - I of the Seven Storied Building for the Faculty of Graduates Studies, University of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-19

- the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to award construction of Stage I of the above building for a total cost of Rs.125,427,539.73 as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
