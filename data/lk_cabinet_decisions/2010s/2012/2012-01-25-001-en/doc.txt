Allocation of land at Ratnapura New Town to the Ministry of Higher Education to be allocated to the Sri Lanka Institute of Advanced Technological Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-25

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to release a block of land one (01) acre in extent, situated at Hidellana in the Ratnapura New Town, held by the Urban Development Authority, to the Ministry of Higher Education, through the Land Commissioner General, for the Ministry to allocate the block of land to the Sri Lanka Institute of Advanced Technological Education (SLIATE), was approved by the Cabinet.
