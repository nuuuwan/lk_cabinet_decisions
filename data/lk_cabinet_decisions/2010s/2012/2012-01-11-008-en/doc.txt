Construction of Building Complex for the District Secretariat Badulla

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-01-11

- Cabinet approval has already been granted for the construction of a five storied building for the District Secretariat, Badulla. The proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs, to revise the total estimate up to Rs.332.9 million and to carryout further activities of the project using the Rs.25 million allocated under the Vote of the District Secretary, Badulla for 2012, was approved by the Cabinet.
