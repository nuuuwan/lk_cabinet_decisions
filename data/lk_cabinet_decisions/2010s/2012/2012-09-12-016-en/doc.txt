Packeting and Supply of 40 Lakhs (4.0 Mn.) of Vegetable Seed Packets for Home Gardening under Phase IV of "DIVI NEGUMA" National Development Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-09-12

- the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, for the procurement of 40 lakhs of seed packets from six institutions, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (CAPC), for distribution among farmers, was approved by the Cabinet.
