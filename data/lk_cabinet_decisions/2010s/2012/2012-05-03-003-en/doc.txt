Allocation of 26.25 Perches of land at Galpotta Road, Nawala to Ruwanweli Maha Seya Temple, Anuradhapura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-03

- the proposal made by  H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to lease out the above block of land for the construction of a building for accommodation of Ven. Priests of Ruwanweli Maha Seya Temple at Anuradhapura, was approved by the Cabinet.
