Ambagasdowa Water Supply Scheme - Revision of the Cost Estimate (TCE)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-02-15

- this project intends to provide safe drinking water to the people living in the Uva Paranagama Township and surrounding areas in the Badulla District.  The proposal made by Hon. Dinesh Gunawardena, Minister of Water Supply and Drainage, to revise the total cost of the project up to Rs.382 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
