Constructions of Seed Certification and Plant Protection Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-05

- on the proposal made by Hon. Mahinda Yapa Abeywardena, Minister of Agriculture, Cabinet approval was granted for the designing construction and contract monitoring of the above centres at Paranthan, Jaffna and Katunayake to be done by the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) at a cost of Rs.88 million, Rs.31 million and Rs.45 million respectively.
