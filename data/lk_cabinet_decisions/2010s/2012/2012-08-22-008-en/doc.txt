Tender for the Supply of 200,000 of Merepenem Injection 1g. Vials

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-08-22

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to purchase 30,000 vials from the local market at the lowest price, as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board (PAB) and to call for fresh worldwide tenders for the balance amount of 170,000 vials, was approved by the Cabinet.
