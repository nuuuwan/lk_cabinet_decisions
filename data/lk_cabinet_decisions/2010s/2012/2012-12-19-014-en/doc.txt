Bilateral Agreement on Economic, Educational, Scientific, and Technological Co-operation between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-19

- the proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to sign and ratify the above Agreement between Sri Lanka and Afghanistan and to take necessary steps for its implementation, was approved by the Cabinet.
