Special Competitive Examination for filling Grade III Vacancies of the Sri Lanka	Accountants? Service in Northern and Eastern Provinces - 2012

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-02-01

- the proposal	made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to	conduct a special competitive examination to recruit officers for 63 vacancies of Grade	III posts in the Sri Lanka Accountants' Service in the Northern and Eastern Provinces,	as per provisions of the Service Minute of the said service and to select a batch of	officers with language proficiency to meet the expectations of the general public living	in the said areas, was approved by the Cabinet. Those selected for appointment will	serve a minimum compulsory period of ten (10) years in the Northern and Eastern	Provinces.
