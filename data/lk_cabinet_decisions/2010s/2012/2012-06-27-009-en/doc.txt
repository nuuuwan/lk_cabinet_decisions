Hambantota Port Development Project - Commencement of Business Ventures

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-27

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as Minister of Ports and Highways, to award the contract for Warehousing Facilities to three bidders, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee, and to lease out the relevant land lots for operation of Warehouses, was approved by the Cabinet.
