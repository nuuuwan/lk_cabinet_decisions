Multi-purpose  HD  Cinema   and   TV   Studio  for  the Mahinda Rajapaksa National Tele Cinema Park, Ranminitenna

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- the proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Yapa Abeywardhena,  Acting Minister of Mass Media and Information, to commence the initial work of the Multi-purpose HD Studio at Mahinda Rajapaksa National Tele Cinema Park, Ranminitenna, was approved by the Cabinet.
