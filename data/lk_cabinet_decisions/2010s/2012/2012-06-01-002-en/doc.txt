Financial provisions for construction work at Rajopavanarama temple, Getambe in conjunction with the 68th Upasampada Ceremony of the Sri Lanka Ramanna Nikaya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- arrangements have been made to conduct the 68th Upasampada Ceremony of the Sri Lanka Ramanna Nikaya from 2012?07?23 to 2012?08?01 at Rajopavanarama temple, Getambe. The proposal made by Hon. M.K.A.D.S. Gunawardena, Acting Minister of Buddha Sasana and Religious Affairs, to complete the remaining construction work of the temple before the commencement of the above ceremony of the Sri Lanka  Ramanna Nikaya, with funds available with the Ministry, was approved by the Cabinet.
