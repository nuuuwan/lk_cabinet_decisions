Transmission System Strengthening - New Galle Transmission Development Project Lot B - Construction of 40 km 132 kv Double Circuit Transmission Line from Ambalangoda Grid Substation to New Galle Grid Substation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-05-03

- the proposal made by Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Power and Energy, to award the above tender, on the recommendation of the Cabinet appointed Procurement Committee, for a total cost of US$ 5,218,790.65 and LKR 607,226,805.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
