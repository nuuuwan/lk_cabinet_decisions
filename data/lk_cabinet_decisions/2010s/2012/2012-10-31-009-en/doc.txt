Construction of the Two storied Building with a Lecture Hall and an Assembly Hall at the Peradeniya National College of Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-31

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to entrust the 2nd stage of the above project to a State Institution engaged in construction work on limited competitive bidding basis, at a cost of Rs.80 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
