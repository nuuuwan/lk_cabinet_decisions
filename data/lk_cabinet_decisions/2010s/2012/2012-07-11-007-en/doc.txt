A proposal to construct a New Headquarters Complex for the Sri Lanka Police at	Mirihana through a project to be implemented under the	Ministry of Defence and	Urban Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-11

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as	the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to relocate the Police Headquarters	presently situated in	Colombo Fort, at the premises presently occupied by the Mirihana	Police, so as to release the existing premises of the Police Headquarters, for	development activities of the government, was approved by the Cabinet.
