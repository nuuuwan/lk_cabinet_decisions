Establishment of a Secondary School in the Southern Province

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-04

- on the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education to establish a secondary school  in a new building complex constructed under the programme of reconstruction of schools damaged due to the Tsunami, under the 6000 schools development project, to cater to the G.C.E. (A/L) students from Hambantota, Matara and Galle Districts from  this year, was approved by the Cabinet.
