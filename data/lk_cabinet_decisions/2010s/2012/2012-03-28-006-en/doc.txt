Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Labour Co-operation between Iraq and Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-03-28

- the proposal made by Hon. Dilan Perera, Minister of Foreign Employment Promotion and Welfare, to enter into the above Memorandum of Understanding between the two countries, so as to create more employment opportunities for Sri Lankans and also to increase foreign exchange earnings to the country, was approved by the Cabinet.
