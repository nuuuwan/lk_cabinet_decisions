Seeking  Observer Status in the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia (CICA)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-11-14

- the objective of this Conference is to enhance cooperation towards promoting peace, security and stability in Asia.  The Organization presently has 24 member countries of Central Asia, Middle East, East Asia and South Asia and India and Pakistan are also among the member states.  The proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to seek observer status in the Conference, was approved by the Cabinet.
