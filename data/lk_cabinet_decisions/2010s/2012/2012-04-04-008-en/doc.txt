Memorandum of Understanding proposed to be signed  between the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on the Establishment of Chinese Cultural Center in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. T.B. Ekanayake, Minister of Culture and the Arts to sign the above Memorandum of Understanding between the Governments of the People's Republic of China and Sri Lanka, to further strengthen the friendly relationship between the two countries,was approved by the Cabinet.
