Registration of Newscasting Websites - Amendments  to Sri Lanka Press Council Act No.5 of 1973

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-11

- the proposal made by Hon. Keheliya Rambukwella, Minister of Mass Media and Information, to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft amending legislation to the  Sri Lanka Press Council Act No.5 of 1973, to make amendments necessary for the Registration of Newscasting Websites, was approved by the Cabinet.
