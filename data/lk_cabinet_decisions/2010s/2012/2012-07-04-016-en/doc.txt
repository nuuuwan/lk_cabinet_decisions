Supply of 500 MT of  Whole Milk Powder for the Thriposha Programme  - 2012

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, for the purchase of the requirement of 500 MT of milk powder for the manufacture of Thriposha for the year 2012, from MILCO (Pvt.) Ltd., was approved by the Cabinet.
