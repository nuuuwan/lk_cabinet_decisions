Memorandum of understanding between the Ministry of External Affairs of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia on Diplomatic Education and Training

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-02-29

- Cabinet of Ministers granted approval for the above Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed between the Republic of Indonesia and Sri Lanka, developing and strengthening friendly relations and cooperation between the two countries.  The above MoU submitted by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs.
