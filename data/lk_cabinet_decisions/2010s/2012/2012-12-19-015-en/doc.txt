Removal of the necessity of the extension of service of the employees of the Public Enterprises beyond the 57 years of age

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-19

- the government has already granted an opportunity for Public Officers to serve in Public Institutions up to the compulsory retirement age of 60 years, without making requests for extension of service beyond 57 years.  Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President in capacity as the Minister of Finance and Planning, to permit employees in Public Enterprises as well, to serve up to the compulsory retirement age of 60 years, was approved by the Cabinet.
