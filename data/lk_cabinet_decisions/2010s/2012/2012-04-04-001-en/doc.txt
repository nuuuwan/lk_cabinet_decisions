Granting of financial allocations for the construction of Mahaweli Maha Seya of Kotmale

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-04-04

- the proposal made by Hon. D.M. Jayaratne, the Prime Minister, to grant an allocation of Rs.145 million for the construction of the Mahaweli Maha Seya commencing from 2013 with funds to be provided under the Medium Term Budgetary Frame Work, was approved by the Cabinet.
