Repealing the National Museum Ordinance No.31 of 1942 and formulating a new Act in keeping with the present and future needs of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-18

- the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Jagath Balasuriya, Minister of National Heritage, to present the National Museums Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman, incorporating  the amendments recommended by the the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Legislation, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
