Allocation of land at Hikkaduwa to the Samurdhi Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-04

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to lease out a block of land at Hikkaduwa, belonging to the Urban Development Authority (UDA),  to the Hikkaduwa Samurdhi Bank on a  30 year lease, was approved by the Cabinet.
