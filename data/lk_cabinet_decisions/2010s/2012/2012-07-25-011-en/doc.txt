Purchase of 250 Nos. Ambulances for the Ministry of Health

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-25

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to obtain 250 Ambulances under the leasing system implemented by the General Treasury, for distribution to Hospitals which are greatly in need of such vehicles to provide better healthcare services to the Public, was approved by the Cabinet.
