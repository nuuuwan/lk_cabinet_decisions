Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between the two Ministries of Education of the Seychelles Republic and Sri Lanka, in respect of Education and Training

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-08-15

- the proposal made by Hon. Bandula Gunawardena, Minister of Education, to enter into the above Memorandum of Understanding between the two Ministries of Education of the Seychelles Republic and Sri Lanka , with the view to further strengthening the relationship between the two countries , was approved by the Cabinet.
