Declaring Teaching Hospital Anuradhapura and Teaching Hospital Batticaloa as End-Stations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-08-08

- twenty two (22) health institutions have already been declared as End-Stations by Gazette Notification in 2011.  Anuradhapura General Hospital and the Batticaloa General Hospital have been upgraded as Teaching Hospitals and they provide clinical training facilities for Medical students of the Faculties of Medicine of the University of Rajarata and the Eastern University.   The proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to declare the General Hospitals of Anuradhapura and Batticaloa as End-Stations, was approved by the Cabinet.
