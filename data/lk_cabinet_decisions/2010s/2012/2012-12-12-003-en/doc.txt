Urban Development activities in Colombo and Adjoining Areas - Housing Development Programme for underserved settlements - Award of construction of 792 Housing Units contract to Sri Lanka Land Reclamation & Development Corporation (SLLR&DC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-12-12

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to award the construction of 792 housing units at Salamulla in Kolonnawa, to the Sri Lanka Land Reclamation & Development Corporation (SLLR&DC), was approved by the Cabinet.
