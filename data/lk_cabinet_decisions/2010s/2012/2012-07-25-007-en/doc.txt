Tender for the Supply of 500,000 Vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin BP, 30/70, 1000IU/10ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-07-25

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the above tender, at a cost of US $1,025,000.00 (LKR128,082,350.00), to the lowest responsive bidder of Denmark, was approved by the Cabinet.
