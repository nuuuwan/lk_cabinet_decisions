Construction of a Learning and Training Campus for the Sri Lanka Institute of Development Administration (SLIDA), in an outstation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-27

- The proposal made by Hon. W.D.J. Seneviratne, Minister of Public Administration and Home Affairs for the construction of a new building for this institute which provides intensive training facilities to Public Officers was approved in principle. A detailed feasibility study will be made on this proposal, after which, funds will be provided to proceed with the project.
