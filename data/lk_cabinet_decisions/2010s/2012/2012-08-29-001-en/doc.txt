Establishment of a Centre for Dengue Research at the University of Sri Jayawardenepura

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-08-29

- on the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Dissanayake, Minister of Higher Education, to establish a Centre for Dengue Research at the Department of Microbiology in the Faculty of Medical Sciences, at the University of Sri Jayawardenepura, at a cost of Rs.92,632,040/-, Cabinet approval was granted to implement the project within a period of two years.
