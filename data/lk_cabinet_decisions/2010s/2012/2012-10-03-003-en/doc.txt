Allocation of Financial Provisions to supply necessary facilities for the commencement of Immigration and Emigration duties at the Mattala International Airport

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-03

- the proposal made by H. E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Defence and Urban Development, to purchase the necessary equipments and make other arrangements to provide an effective and efficient service in accordance with international standards to passengers passing through Mattala Airport once it is commissioned, was approved by the Cabinet.
