Proposed Revision to Convention on the Suppression of Terrorist Financing Act No.25 of 2005

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-10

- the proposal made by Hon. (Prof.) G.L. Peiris, Minister of External Affairs, to amend the Convention on the Suppression of Terrorist Financing Act No.25 of 2005 and to submit the relevant amending Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
