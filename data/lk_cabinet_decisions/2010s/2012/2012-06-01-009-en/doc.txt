Tender for Piling Work for Proposed Eight Storied Epilepsy Unit at National Hospital Sri Lanka under Saudi Fund

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- the proposal made by Hon. Maithripala Sirisena, Minister of Health, to award the above tender, financed under the Saudi Fund, to the  lowest responsive bidder as recommended by the Standing  Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee for a total cost of Rs.91,373,499.12, was approved by the Cabinet.
