Procurement of 02 Nos. Top Heavy Lift Trucks (40 Ton) for Sri Lanka Ports Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-10-18

- the proposal made by H.E. the President, in his capacity as the Minister of Ports and Highways, for the procurement of the above on the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, at a total cost of Rs.168,167,649.59,  was approved by the Cabinet.
