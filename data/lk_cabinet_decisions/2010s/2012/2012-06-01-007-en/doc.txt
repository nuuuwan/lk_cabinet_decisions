Bilateral Air Services Consultations between Sri Lanka and Japan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2012-06-01

- consultations between the Aeronautical Authorities of Japan and Sri Lanka  have been held in Tokyo and it has been agreed to allow daily flights between Narita Airport and Sri Lanka with immediate effect and in addition, from Summer of 2013, designated Airlines of Sri Lanka will be able to operate unlimited flights to Narita Airport. The proposal made by Hon. Piyankara Jayaratne, Minister of Civil Aviation, to implement the new route schedule through exchange of Diplomatic Notes, was approved by the Cabinet.
