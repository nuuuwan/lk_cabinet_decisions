Memorandum of Understanding on Mutual Cooperation between  the  Ceylon  Shipping  Corporation  Ltd.  (CSC) and the Pakistan  National Shipping Corporation (PNSC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-04-01

- Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding between the National Flag Carriers with the sanction of the two Governments will pave the way for the two National Flag Carriers to further strengthen cooperation between them. The proposal made by Hon.Arjuna Ranatunga, Minister of Ports and Shipping to sign the Memorandum of Understanding during the State Visit of H.E. the President of Sri Lanka to Pakistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
