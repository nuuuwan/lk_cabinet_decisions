Annual Report of the Ministry of Finance - 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-06-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, to present the Report that should be issued to the Parliament and to the public, evaluating the final Budget Situation Report,  updated information on the Government's fiscal position pertaining to the financial year - 2014 and the execution of the fiscal strategy of the Government, in terms of the provisions of Section 13 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act No.3 of 2003, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
