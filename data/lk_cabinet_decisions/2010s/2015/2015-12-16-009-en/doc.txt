Plastering 3,000 Houses each in 25 Districts to mark the Induction of His Excellency the President

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-12-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Sajith Premadasa, the Minister of Housing and Construction, for the implementation of an Islandwide programme of  providing building materials required to plaster 75,000 houses selected from the 25 districts of the country (3,000 houses from each district), to mark the first anniversary of the induction of H.E. the President, was approved by the Cabinet.
