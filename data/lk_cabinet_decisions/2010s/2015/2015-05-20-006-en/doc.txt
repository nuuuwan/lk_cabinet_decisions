Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Russian Federation and the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on Co-operation in the field of Mass Communication

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-20

- The Proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media, Hon. Gayantha Karunathilaka to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Russian Federation with a view of enhancing co?operation in the field of Mass Communication, was approved by the Cabinet.
