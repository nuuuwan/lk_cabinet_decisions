Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the Year 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-13

- The proposal made by the Prime Minister Hon. Ranil Wickramasinghe as the  Minister of Policy Planning, Economic Affairs, Child, Youth and Cultural Affairs to present the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka - 2014 in Parliament, in accordance with the provisions of the Monetary Law Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
