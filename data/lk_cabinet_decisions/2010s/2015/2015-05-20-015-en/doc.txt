Further activities relating to the Bill for Registration of Theravada Bikkhu Kathikawatha

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-20

- The proposal made by the Minister of Buddha Sasana, Hon. Karu Jayasuriya, to get the recommendations of the Attorney General in regard to the above Draft Bill, to publish it in the Government Gazette and thereafter to present it in the Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
