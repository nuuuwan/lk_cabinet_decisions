Amendment to the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-12-02

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe,  the Prime Minister, to amend the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance for the purposes of increasing the women's representation in Local Authorities, introducing of 'Ward System' during the elections in said institutions and the taking of necessary measures to make the electoral system more efficient and effective, was approved by the Cabinet.
