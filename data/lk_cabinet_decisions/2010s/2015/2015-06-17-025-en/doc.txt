Agreement on the Articles of Association on setting up of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-06-17

- The Agreement on the Articles of Association on establishment of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank is scheduled to be signed on the 29th June 2015 in Beijing, China and the proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, seeking approval to sign the Agreement on behalf of Sri Lanka at that event, was approved by the Cabinet.
