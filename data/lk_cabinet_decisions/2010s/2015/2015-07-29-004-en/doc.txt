Local  Banks  Funded   Water  Supply  Projects for Towns  East  of  Colombo  –  Package II and  III

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-29

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage, to proceed with the implementation of the Packages II and III of the Towns East of Colombo Water Supply Project utilizing the funds secured from local banks, under the implementation of priority water supply projects funded by local banks, was approved by the Cabinet.
