The marking of the National Safety Day - 2015

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-08

- The National Safety Day falls on 26th December  every year.  The proposal made by Hon. A.H.M. Fowzie, the Minister of Disaster Management, to conduct the national programme to mark the National Safety Day of this year, in Polonnaruwa district, under the main theme of 'A safe Sri Lanka' and the sub-theme of 'let us unite to eliminate disasters', and  to implement a series of  island?wide programmes simultaneously in collaboration with the other Ministries and Departments, was approved by the Cabinet.
