Payment of Retirement benefits to Dr. Wickramabahu Karunaratne, who served as a Senior Lecturer at the University of Peradeniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-12-16

- The Proposal made by Hon. Lakshman Kiriella, the Minister of Higher Education and Highways, to pay the retirement benefits to Dr. Wickramabahu Karunaratne, who served as a Senior Lecturer at the University of Peradeniya, treating that he has served in the relevant post from 1982-11-01, the date of termination of his post until retirement on reaching the age of 65 years, was approved by the Cabinet.
