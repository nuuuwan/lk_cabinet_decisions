Amendment of Tea Shakthi Fund Act, No. 47 of 2000

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Navin Dissanayake, the Minister of Plantation Industries, to take action to make appropriate amendments to the Tea Shakthi Fund Act, No. 47 of 2000, with a view to conducting the  affairs of the Tea Shakthi Fund in a more formal and effective manner, was approved by the Cabinet.
