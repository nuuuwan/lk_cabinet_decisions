Establishment of  Clinical Waste Management Systems for the hospitals in Sri Lanka, under the Australian Assistance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-29

- Lack of a proper management methodology for the disposal of clinical waste with infectious materials, without causing adverse effects to public health  is a serious problem faced by the hospitals in Sri Lanka. As such, the previous Cabinet has granted its approval for a proposal made voluntarily, to establish a formal clinical waste management system for the Government hospitals, under the Australian loan assistance. As per the recommendations made by the Committee appointed to review and report the project proposal, and by the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Economic Affairs, and taking into consideration the importance of such a project, the proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senaratne, the Minister of Health and Indigenous Medicine, to implement the above project, subject to making changes to the scope of the project as appropriate,  was approved by the Cabinet.
