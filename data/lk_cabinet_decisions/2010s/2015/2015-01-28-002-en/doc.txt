The Cultural Show to be held concurrent to the Commemoration of the 67th Independence Day

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-01-28

- the Cabinet noted that the Mayor of Colombo has made arrangements to hold a Cultural Show on various cultural aspects on the theme of "National Unity" on the evening of 4th February opposite the Town Hall with the participation of Artists representing all communities to coincide with the commemoration of the 67th Independence Day.
