Obtaining a Technical Cooperation Grant of eruo 7 million from the 	Government of Federal Republic of Germany

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-05

- The Government of the Federal Republic of Germany has agreed to provide a Technical Cooperation Grant amounting to euro 7 million for the implementation of three projects, namely, the Management project of Wilpattu National Park and the Buffer Zone around it, Education Project for Social Integration and the Small and Medium Scale Enterprise Development Project and as such, the proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance to sign the exchange notes between the two countries for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
