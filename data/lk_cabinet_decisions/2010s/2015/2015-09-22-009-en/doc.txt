Obtaining the Approval of Parliament for the Gazette Notifications Published

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-09-22

The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance,  to present the Gazette Notifications issued in accordance with the decisions taken time to time by the Government with regard to various tax revisions made for the welfare of the people or with regard to the collection of  revenue for the purpose, in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
