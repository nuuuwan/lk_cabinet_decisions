The Collective Agreement between Ceylon Electricity Board and the Trade Unions

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-20

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy, Hon. Patali Champika Ranawaka for the approval of the Collective Agreement made between the Ceylon Electricity Board and the relevant Trade Unions on 2014?12?01 to enhance the performance of the Electricity Board and to have an Administrative Agreement between the Employees and the Management, was approved by the Cabinet.
