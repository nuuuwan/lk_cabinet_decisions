Conducting the presidential election in a just and fair manner

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-01-21

It was decided to convey the appreciation  of the Cabinet to the Commissioner  of Elections, and all the officers including District Secretaries involved in the election duties, and all the officers in the Police Department including the Inspector General of Police and officers of the Police Special Task Force who provided security in vulnerable areas paving the way for holding of a just and fair election.
