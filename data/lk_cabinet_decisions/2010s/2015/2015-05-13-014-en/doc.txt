Participation of Hon. Minister of Finance at the 48th Annual Meeting of the Asian Development Bank held from 2015?05?02 to 05 in Baku, Azerbaijan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-13

- The Cabinet noted that the Minister of Finance, Hon. Ravi Karunanayake and several officials of the Ministry have represented Sri Lanka at the 48th Annual Conference of the Asian Development Bank which was held in Baku, Azerbaijan.  The Cabinet further noted that the Minister of Finance had participated in the informal meeting of Finance Ministers of the SAARC region and also had other meetings with the President and the Vice President of the Asian Development Bank during this visit.
