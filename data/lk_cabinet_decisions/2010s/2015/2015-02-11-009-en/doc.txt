Agreement between Sri Lanka and India on Exemption from Visa Requirements for the Holders of Diplomatic and Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-02-11

- the proposal made by the Minister of Public Order, Disaster Management and Christian Affairs, Hon. John Amarathunga to enter into the above agreement to exempt the holders of valid diplomatic and official passports between Sri Lanka and India from the visa requirements,  was approved by the Cabinet.
