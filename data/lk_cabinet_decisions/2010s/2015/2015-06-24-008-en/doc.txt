Transferring the Ownership of Houses in the Housing Complex situated at Mahawela Lane, Dematagoda, to the Occupants

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-06-24

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage, to execute the deeds of transfer in favour of 21 occupants reside at the above housing complex, who have completed the payment of the prescribed value of their housing units, was approved by the Cabinet.
