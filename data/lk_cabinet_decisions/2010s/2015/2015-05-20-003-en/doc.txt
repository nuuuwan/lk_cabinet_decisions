Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-20

- The proposal made by Hon. John Amaratunga, the Minister of Public Order and Christian Affairs to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Romanian Government on releasing Diplomatic Passport holders from the obligation to obtain visa, with a view to further strengthening friendly relations between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Romania, was approved by the Cabinet.
