Completion of the construction work of the Kothmale Mahaweli Maha Seya - 2015

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-20

- The proposal made by the Minister of Tourism and Sports, Hon. Navin Dissanayake to obtain additional provisions from the General Treasury to complete the construction work of the Kothmale Mahaweli Maha Seya, which commenced in 1983, was approved by the Cabinet.
