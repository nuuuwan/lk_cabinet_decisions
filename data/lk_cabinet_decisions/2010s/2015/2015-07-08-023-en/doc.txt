Procurement of Fertilizer for the Year 2015 - (September Delivery)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-08

- Supply of fertilizer to farmers at a subsidized price under the fertilizer subsidy programme is handled by the Ceylon Fertilizer Company Limited and the Colombo Commercial Fertilizer Limited,  under the purview of the Ministry of Agriculture.  The proposal made by Hon. Duminda Dissanayake, the Minister of Agriculture, to award the  contract for the supply of fertilizer for distribution during the month of September 2015, as per the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Special  Procurement Committee and of the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
