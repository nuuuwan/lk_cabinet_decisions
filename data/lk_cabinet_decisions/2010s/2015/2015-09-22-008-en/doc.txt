68th Independence Day Celebration

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-09-22

The proposal made by Hon. Vajira Abeywardena, the Minister of Home Affairs, for the appointment of a Ministerial Committee under the Chairmanship of the Prime Minister Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe and with the participation of other relevant Ministers, to make policy decisions and provide guidance with regard to the conducting of the 68th Independence Day  Celebration which falls on 04th February 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
