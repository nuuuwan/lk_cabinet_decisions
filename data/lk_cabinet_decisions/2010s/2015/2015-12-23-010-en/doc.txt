Increase of Wages of Private Sector Employees

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-12-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravindra Samaraweera, the Acting Minister of Labour and Trade Union Relations, to obtain the approval for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman, in relation to the increase of wages of the Private Sector employees, as recommended by the Budget Proposals of 2016, was approved by the Cabinet.
