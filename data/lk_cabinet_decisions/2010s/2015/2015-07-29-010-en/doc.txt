Submission of ILO Protocol to the Conventions and Recommendations to the Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-29

- Sri Lanka is a member country of the International Labour Organization (ILO). Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. S.B. Navinna, the Minister of Labour, to present the Protocol to  Convention 29, that is the ILO Protocol to the Convention on Forced Labour, and the Recommendation 203, that is the recommendation on supplementary measures for the effective suppression of forced labour, which was adopted in the 103rd Secession of the International Labour Conference held in 2014, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
