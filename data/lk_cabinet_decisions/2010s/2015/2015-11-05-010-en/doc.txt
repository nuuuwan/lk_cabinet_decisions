Supply of Official Uniforms and other Clothing Materials for the Sri Lanka Police - 2015

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Tilak Marapana, the Minister of Law & Order and Prison Reforms, to purchase the official uniforms required for the Sri Lanka Police for the year 2015, from the local textile manufacturers, as recommended by the Local Textile Allocation Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
