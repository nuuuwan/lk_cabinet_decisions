The present status of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-29

- Hon. Ranil Wickramasinghe, the Prime Minister, brought to the notice of the Cabinet the situation that has arisen in the CIABOC, as a result of the failure on the part of the Chairman and the Commissioners of the CIABOC, who have been appointed to the said posts by the previous Government, to provide necessary guidance and cooperation to conduct its operations properly.  As the functions of the Commission has been utterly blocked due to the conduct of its Chairman and Commissioners, and as such, the Cabinet noted the difficulty of taking legal action against the large number of complaints where the investigations have been concluded.
