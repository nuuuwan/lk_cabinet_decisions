Proposed Memorandum of Understanding on Recruitment of Domestic Workers / Employment of Workers between the Ministry of Human Resources of the Government of Malaysia and the Ministry of Foreign Employment of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-13

- The proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Employment Hon. (Mrs.) Thalatha Atukorala to enter into two (02) Memoranda of Understanding between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of Malaysia on Employment of Domestic Workers and Other Personnel so as to ensure  safeguarding their rights and welfare facilities, was approved by the Cabinet.
