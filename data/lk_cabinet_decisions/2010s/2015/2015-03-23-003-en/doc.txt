Augmentation of Killinochchi Grid Substation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-03-23

- The proposal made by Mr. Patali Champika Ranawaka, Minister of Power and Energy, to award the contract for the Augmentation of Killinochchi Grid Substation utilizing the balance funds available under the Japan International Corporation Agency (JICA) loan scheme, on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
