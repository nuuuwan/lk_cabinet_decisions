Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme - Construction of Kalutara,Kesbewa, old Anuradhapura Grid Substations and the Augmentation of New Anuradhapura Grid Substations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy,  to award the contract for the construction of Kalutara,  Kesbewa an old Anuradhapura Grid Substations and  the augmentation of New Anuradhapura Grid Substation, under the Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme, to the lowest responsive bidder, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
