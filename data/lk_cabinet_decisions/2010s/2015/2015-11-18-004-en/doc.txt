Bi-lateral Air Service Agreement between Sri Lanka and Bulgaria

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-18

- Considering the increasing demand in the Tourism Sector in Sri Lanka, it appears that the air service links established with other countries are essential  for the development of the Air Services and the Tourism Industry in Sri Lanka.  Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Nimal Siripala de Silva, the Minister of Transport and Civil Aviation, to sign the Air Services Agreement already signed between Sri Lanka and Builgaria at the officer level, by an accredited representative of Sri Lanka and thereafter to give effect to the said agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
