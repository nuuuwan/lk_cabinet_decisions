Amendments to the Appropriation Act No. 41 of 2014

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-01-28

- the functions of the 60 Cabinet Ministries which existed when allocating funds for the year 2015 under the Appropriation Act No. 41 of 2014 are now categorized under the 31 Cabinet Ministries and 10 State Ministries and the provisions set apart for the previous 60 Ministries should be reallocated to the new Ministries based on their scope and as such the draft Bill submitted by the Minister of Finance Hon. Ravi Karunanayake to amend to the Appropriation Act No. 41 of 2014 to provide provisions to implement the 100 Day Instant Relief Programme and to present it in Parliament as an urgent Bill in the national interest, in terms of Article 122 of the Constitution, was approved by the Cabinet.
