Removal of the Pensions Anomalies  of the Three Armed Forces

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-29

- The Public Administration Circular No.16/2015 has been issued in accordance with the  approval granted by the Cabinet on 2015?06?10, to adjust the pensions of the salary structure effective from 2006?01?01, to revise the pensions of those who retired before the year 2006 by removing their salary anomalies. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Karu Jayasuriya, the Minister of Public Administration, Local Government and Democratic Governance, to revise the pensions of those retired from the services of Three Armed Forces before the year 2006, was approved by the Cabinet.
