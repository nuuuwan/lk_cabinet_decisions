Asian Development Bank Assisted Greater Colombo Water & Waste Water Management Improvement Investment Programme - Ambatale Water Supply System Improvement and Energy Saving Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to award the contract for the laying of water supply pipelines along a distance of 09 km. from Ambatale to Ellie House reservoir, under the Ambatale Water Supply System Improvement and Energy Saving Project under the Greater Colombo Water and Wastewater Management Improvement Investment Programme assisted by the Asian Development Bank (ADB), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
