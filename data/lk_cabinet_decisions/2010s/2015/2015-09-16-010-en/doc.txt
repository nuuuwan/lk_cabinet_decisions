Colombo Port City Development Project - Extension of Agreement by a period of six months

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-09-16

- The proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, the Minister of Ports and Shipping,  to extend the agreement signed on 2014-09-16 in respect of the Colombo City Development Project between the Government of Sri Lanka and the relevant Project Company by six months with effect from 2015-09-16 which is the date of expiry of that agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
