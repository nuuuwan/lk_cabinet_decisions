Hemmathagama Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-09-16

The approval of the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Economic Affairs has also been received for the above project which is implemented utilizing Rs.3,303.26 million of Government funds and Euro 81.96 million of foreign funds. The people of the Divisional Secretariat Divisions of  Mawanella, Aranayake and Rambukkana will be benefited from this project. The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to proceed with the activities of the project, was approved by the Cabinet.
