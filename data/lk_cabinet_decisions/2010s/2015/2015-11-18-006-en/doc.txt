Amendment to the Animal Feed Act, No.15 of 1986

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-18

- The proposal made by Hon. P. Harison, the  Minister of Rural Economy, to amend the Animal Feed Act, No.15 of 1986, for the purposes of appointing a Registrar of Animal Feed to implement the relevant laws more formally, altering the composition of the Animal Feed Advisory Committee, safeguarding the rights of Animal Feed purchasers, increasing the penalties for violation of the provisions of the Act and regularizing the animal feed production and distribution in a better way, was approved by the Cabinet.
