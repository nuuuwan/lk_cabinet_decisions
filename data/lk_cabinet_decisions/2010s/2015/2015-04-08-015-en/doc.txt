Establishment of an Office for National Unity

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-04-08

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, for the  establishment of an Office for National Unity, with a view to building an inclusive and peaceful society in Sri Lanka, where diversity will be respected and celebrated, and in which all communities will co?exist in harmony and unity, with a vision to  guarantee every citizen equal opportunities in the economic, social, cultural and political spheres, whilst safeguarding the Sri Lankan identity, was approved by the Cabinet.
