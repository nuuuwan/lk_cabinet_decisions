Gampaha, Attanagalle and Minuwangoda Integrated Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to implement the above project at a total cost of US$229.5 million and Rs.3,910 million,  providing benefits to the people living in the areas of Gampaha, Attanagalle and Minuwangoda and to secure the required funds for the purpose,  was approved by the Cabinet.
