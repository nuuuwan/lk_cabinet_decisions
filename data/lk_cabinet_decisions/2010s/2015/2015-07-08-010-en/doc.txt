Loans obtained for the Development Projects of the Sri Lanka Ports Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-08

- The Cabinet noted that the total credit due by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority (SLPA) to date amounts to Rs.215 billion, on the information submitted by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, as per the  instructions he had received to submit particulars of the loans obtained by the SLPA for its development projects.
