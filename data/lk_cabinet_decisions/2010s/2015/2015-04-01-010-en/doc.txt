The Academic Co-operation Agreement (ACA) between Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute of International Relations & Strategic Studies (LKIIRSS) of Sri Lanka and National Defence University (NDU) of Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-04-01

- The proposal made by Hon. Mangala Samaraweera, Minister of Foreign Affairs, to sign the above Academic Co-operation Agreement during the proposed state visit to Pakistan by H.E. the President  between the Lakshman Kadirgamar Institute of International Relations & Strategic Studies (LKIIRSS) of Sri Lanka and the National Defence University (NDU) of Pakistan with a view to enhancing cooperation in sharing of knowledge, experiences and information in areas of mutual interest, was approved by the Cabinet.
