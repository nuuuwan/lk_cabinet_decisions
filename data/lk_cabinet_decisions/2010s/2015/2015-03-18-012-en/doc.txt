Contract on Bandaranaike International Airport, Runaway Widening, Overlay and Associated Work

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-03-18

- The proposal made by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation, to cancel the award of the above contract, as it has been established that the award of the above contract had not been made in a transparent manner following  the competitive tender procedure, was approved by the Cabinet.
