Polgahawela, Pothuhera, Alawwa Integrated WS Project - Kundasale - Haragama WS Project - Mathugama, Dodangoda, Agalawatta and Neboda Integrated WS Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-06-10

- The proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Drainage Rauff Hakeem, to proceed with the implementation and the activities of the above water supply projects and to make a request to the Department of External Resources to hold negotiations with  the relevant institutions to secure funds required for the above projects, was approved by the Cabinet.
