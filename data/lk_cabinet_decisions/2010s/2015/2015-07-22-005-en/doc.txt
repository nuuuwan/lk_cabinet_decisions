Construction of a Dockyard at Hambantota Port

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-07-22

- The information submitted by Hon. Arjuna Ranatunga, the Minister of Ports and Shipping, with regard to the steps taken to explore the possibility of constructing a Dockyard at the Port of Hambantota and prepare relevant comprehensive plans and feasibility reports for the purpose, was noted by the Cabinet.
