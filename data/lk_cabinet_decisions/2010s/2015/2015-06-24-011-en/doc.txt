Implementation of a Health Insurance Scheme for the Judges

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-06-24

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Wijayadasa Rajapaksa, the Minister of Justice, to grant a health insurance cover of Rs.500,000.00 to all Judges of the Courts, subject to outdoor treatments of Rs.35,000.00, and to bear 75% of its subscription by the Government, was approved by the Cabinet.
