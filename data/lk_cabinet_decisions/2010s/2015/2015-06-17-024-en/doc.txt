Submission of the Gazette Notifications for the Revision of Import Taxes to the Parliament for Approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-06-17

- The proposal made by Hon. Ravi Karunanayake, the Minister of Finance, to present 06 Gazette Notifications issued for the Revision of Import Taxes, in Parliament for approval in terms of the provision of relevant Acts, was approved by the Cabinet.
