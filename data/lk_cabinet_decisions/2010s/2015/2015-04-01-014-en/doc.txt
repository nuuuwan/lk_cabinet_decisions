Recruitment of a Team of Retired Police Officers to assist the activities of Anti-Corruption Committee Secretariat

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-04-01

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe, the Prime Minister, to recruit a team of retired police officers to the Anti-Corruption Committee Secretariat for a period of 03 months on temporary basis in order to assist the carrying out of the activities of the Anti-corruption Committee Secretariat expeditiously and efficiently, was approved by the Cabinet.
