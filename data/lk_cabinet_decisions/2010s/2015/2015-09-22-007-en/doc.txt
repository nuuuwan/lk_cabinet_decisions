The Cabinet Sub-Committee on Economic Management and the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Cost of Living

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-09-22

- Appointment of the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Economic Management under the Chairmanship of the Prime Minister Hon. Ranil Wickremasinghe and comprised of the relevant Ministers, to make recommendations to the Cabinet with regard to the proposals on overall economic development of the country, and the appointment of the Cabinet Sub-Committee on Cost of Living under the Chairmanship of Hon. Malik Samarawickrema, the Minister of Development Strategies and International Trade and comprised of the relevant Ministers, to make recommendations to the Cabinet with regard to the matters affect the living condition of the people of the country, was approved by the Cabinet.
