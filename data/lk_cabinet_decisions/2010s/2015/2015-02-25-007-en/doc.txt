The Plan to convert the Ayurvedic Hospital of Borella into a Teaching Hospital -Second Phase

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-02-25

- The proposal made by Hon. (Dr.) Rajitha Senarathna, Minister of Health and Indigenous Medicine, to revise the contract cost from Rs.728 million to Rs.1,113 million as recommended by the Cost Estimate Examination Committee to develop the Ayurvedic Hospital, Borella as a Teaching Hospital, was approved by the Cabinet.
