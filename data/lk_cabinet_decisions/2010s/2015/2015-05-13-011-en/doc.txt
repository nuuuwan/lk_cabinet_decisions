Celebrating the Republic Day

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-05-13

- With the adoption of the first Republican Constitution on 1972?05?22, Sri Lanka became a sovereign and independent state accountable to the people. Circular instructions are issued to celebrate the Republic Day falls due on the 22nd of May every year to commemorate the event and the proposal made by the Minister of Home Affairs, Hon. Joseph Michael Perera to issue a similar circular this year too, was approved by the Cabinet.
