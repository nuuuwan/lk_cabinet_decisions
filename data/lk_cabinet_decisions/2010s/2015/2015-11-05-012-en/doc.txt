Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme - Construction of Kappalthurai Grid Substation and the Augmentation of Kerawalapitiya, Trincomalee and Katunayake Grid Substations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-05

- The proposal made by Hon. Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, the Minister of Power and Renewable Energy,  to award the contract for the construction of Kappalthurai Grid Substation and the augmentation of Kerawalapitiya, Trincomalee and Katunayake Grid Substations, under the Green Power Development and Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Programme, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
