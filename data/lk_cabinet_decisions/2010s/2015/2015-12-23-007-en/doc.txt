Police Hospital Expansion Project at Narahenpita - Completion of the Balance Work

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-12-23

- The proposal made by Hon. Sagala Ratnayaka, the Minister of Law & Order and Southern Development, to award the Contract for the completion of the balance work remaining under the Police Hospital Expansion Project at Narahenpita, in accordance with the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
