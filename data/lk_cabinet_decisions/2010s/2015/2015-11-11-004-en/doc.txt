Galagedara and Mawathagama Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-11-11

- The proposal made by Hon. Rauff Hakeem, the Minister of City Planning and Water Supply, to implement the above project with a view to providing safe drinking water to the people of Galagedara and Mawathagama areas and also to proceed with the activities related to it, was approved by the Cabinet.
