Memorandum of Understanding on mutual co?operation and  exchange of news between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the State of Qatar

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2015-03-23

- The proposal made by Mr. Gayantha Karunathilaka, Minister of Mass Media and Parliamentary Affairs, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and the Government of State of Qatar with a view to developing bilateral corporation in the field of news exchange	between the two countries, in parallel with the official visit of Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, the Emir of the State of Qatar scheduled on 24th March 2015, was approved by the Cabinet.
