# 🏛️#SriLanka 🇱🇰 Cabinet Decisions `Dataset`

![LastUpdated](https://img.shields.io/badge/last_updated-2025--11--03_19:09:21-green)

[https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions)

A Sri Lanka Cabinet Decision is an official policy or action agreed by the Cabinet of Ministers, shaping governance, law, and national development in the country.

- [**10,444** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions) (**138.9 MB**), from **2010-09-27** to **2025-10-27**, scraped from [https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk](https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk)

- In **JSON**, **TXT** (99%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (99%)

- In **සිංහල** & **English**

- 🎓 Cite as **[arXiv:2510.04124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04124) [cs.CL]**

Following the evaluation process of the said expressions of interest, proposals have been invited from Giesecke & Devrient Asia Pacific Limited, Hong Kong, which fulfilled the relevant qualifications as per the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee. After evaluating the technical and financial proposals submitted by the said institution, the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee has recommended that the procurement for the revamping the Currency Operations of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka be awarded to the said institution. Based on the said recommendation, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to award the relevant procurement, to Giesecke & Devrient Asia Pacific Limited, Hong Kong, was approved by the Cabinet."
