A Sri Lanka Cabinet Decision is an official policy or action agreed by the Cabinet of Ministers, shaping governance, law, and national development in the country.

- In **JSON**, **TXT** (93%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (93%)

- In **සිංහල** & **English**
