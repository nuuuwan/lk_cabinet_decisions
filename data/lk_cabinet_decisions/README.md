# 🏛️#SriLanka 🇱🇰 Cabinet Decisions `Dataset`

![LastUpdated](https://img.shields.io/badge/last_updated-2026--07--19_19:05:09-green)

[https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions)

A Sri Lanka Cabinet Decision is an official policy or action agreed by the Cabinet of Ministers, shaping governance, law, and national development in the country.

- [**10,969** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions) (**142.3 MB**), from **2010-09-27** to **2026-07-13**, scraped from [https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk](https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk)

- In **JSON**, **TXT** (95%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (95%)

- In **සිංහල** & **English**

- 🎓 Cite as **[arXiv:2510.04124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04124) [cs.CL]**
