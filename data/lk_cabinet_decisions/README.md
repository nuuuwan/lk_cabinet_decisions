A Sri Lanka Cabinet Decision is an official policy or action agreed by the Cabinet of Ministers, shaping governance, law, and national development in the country.

- [**11,142** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions) (**143.5 MB**), from **2010-09-27** to **2026-10-05**, scraped from [https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk](https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk)

- In **JSON**, **TXT** (93%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (93%)

- In **සිංහල** & **English**

- 🎓 Cite as **[arXiv:2510.04124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04124) [cs.CL]**
