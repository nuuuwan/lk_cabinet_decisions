A Sri Lanka Cabinet Decision is an official policy or action agreed by the Cabinet of Ministers, shaping governance, law, and national development in the country.

- [**10,458** documents](https://github.com/nuuuwan/lk_cabinet_decisions/tree/data/data/lk_cabinet_decisions) (**139.0 MB**), from **2010-09-27** to **2025-11-03**, scraped from [https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk](https://www.cabinetoffice.gov.lk)

- In **JSON**, **TXT** (99%) & **🤗 Hugging Face** (99%)

- In **සිංහල** & **English**

- 🎓 Cite as **[arXiv:2510.04124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04124) [cs.CL]**

Accordingly, a dedicated unit called the \u2018National Initiative for Research and Development Commercialization' has been established under the Presidential Secretariat, enabling to evaluate the technical and economic potential of the projects and set up a proper investor friendly path for the implementation of those projects thereby contributing to the development of the country. As of now, applications worth about Rs. 2.2 billion have been submitted by local researchers and entrepreneurs to the said unit. The unit has taken steps to prepare a methodology called \u2018National Initiative for Research and Development Commercialization' to evaluate project proposals under 02 main phases namely Technical Evaluation and Administrative Evaluation in order to select the most economically, socially and environmentally effective projects from among the applications. Accordingly, measures will be taken to classify unique and essential projects through the proposed evaluation process as well as to identify projects that make a high contribution to economic progress. President to implement the proposed methodology for providing Grants to project proposals selected under the \u2018National Initiative for Research and Development Commercialization', was approved by the Cabinet."
