Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Consultations between the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs of the Grand Duchy of the Luxembourg and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-02

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Consultations between the Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs of the Luxembourg and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Through the said Agreement it is expected to promote, strengthen and expand bilateral relations including political, economic, commercial, scientific, technical and cultural aspects of bilateral cooperation between the two countries. The proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to sign the aforesaid Memorandum of Understanding at the level of Foreign Ministers of both countries to mark the 50th anniversary year of the establishment of diplomatic relations between Sri Lanka and Luxembourg, was approved by the Cabinet.
