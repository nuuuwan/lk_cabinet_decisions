Regulations imposed for revision of Fees Charged by the Department for Registration of Persons for Providing Services

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-20

- Approval has been given in a previous Cabinet meeting to revise the fees charged by the Department for Registration of Persons. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security to present the Draft regulation prepared by amending the Registration of Persons Regulations No.01 of 2017 to Parliament for approval and then to publish the said Regulation in the Government Gazette, was approved by the Cabinet.
