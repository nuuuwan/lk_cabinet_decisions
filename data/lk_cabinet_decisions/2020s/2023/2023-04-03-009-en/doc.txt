National competitive bidding for the operation of a restaurant of	international food chain and/or a shop for international branded apparel	and accessories in the Departure/Transit area at Bandaranaike  	International Airport, Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-04-03

- Bids have been invited under national competitive bidding procedure for the operation of a restaurant of international food chain and/or a shop for international	branded apparel and accessories in the departure/transit area at Bandaranaike International  Airport,  Katunayake  and  3  bids  have been submitted. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the respective contract to M/s.Flemingo Duty Free (Pvt) Ltd, the substantially responsive highest evaluated bidder as per the recommendation of the Standing	Cabinet Approved Procurement Committee for a period of 3 years at the total	amount of USD 2,502,624.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
