Awarding the Contract for the Ground Handling Services at Sydney International Airport, Australia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-08

- Bids have been invited from all licensed ground handling service providers currently providing ground handling services at the Sydney Airport, Australia, for the provision of ground handling services at that Airport, under the Limited International Competitive Bidding procedure, and two bids have been received for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the relevant contract to Menzies Aviation (Australia) Pty Ltd for a period of three years as per the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
