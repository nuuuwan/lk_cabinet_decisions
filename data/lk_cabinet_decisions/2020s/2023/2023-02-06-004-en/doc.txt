Entering into a Combined Agreement of Cooperation between the Sri Lanka 	Rupavahini Corporation and the Turkish Radio Television Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-06

- During the second session of the Combined Committee on Sri Lanka Turkey Economic and Technical Cooperation 2021, It has been proposed to enter into an Agreement of Cooperation between the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Cooperation and the Turkish Radio Television Cooperation with the aim of further strengthening trade, economic and technical cooperation between the two parties. The draft Agreement prepared accordingly has been received the clearance of the Attorney General and the concurrence of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media to sign the said Agreement on Cooperation, was approved by the Cabinet.
