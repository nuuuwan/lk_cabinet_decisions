Accession to the Protocol to amend the Convention on Offenses and	Certain Other Acts Committed on Board Aircraft (Montreal Protocol	2014)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-30

- The Proposals made by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to accede to the Protocol on ''Amend the Convention on Offenses and Certain Other Acts Committed on Board Aircraft'' (Montreal Protocol-2014) and to take necessary actions to enact legislation for implementation of the provisions of the said Protocol in Sri Lanka, was granted approval by the Cabinet.
