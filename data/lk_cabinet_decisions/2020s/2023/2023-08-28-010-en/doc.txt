Office for National Unity and Reconciliation Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-08-28

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2023‑01‑23 has granted approval to draft the Office for National Unity and Reconciliation Bill. Clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman accordingly.  As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same, thereafter to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
