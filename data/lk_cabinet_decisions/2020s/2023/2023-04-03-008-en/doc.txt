Supply of fertilizer subsidy for paddy cultivation in Yala Season of 2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-04-03

- Providing a financial subsidy to the farmers for maximum of two hectares as Rs.20,000/- per hectare to purchase fertilizers, taking into consideration a proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture related to supply of fertilizer for paddy	cultivation in Yala Season on 2023, was approved by the Cabinet.
