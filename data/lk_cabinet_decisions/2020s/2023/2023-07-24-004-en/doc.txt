Signing a License Agreement between the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation	and the National Radio and Television Administration of the People's Republic	of China

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-07-24

- It has been proposed to sign a License Agreement between the National Radio and Television Administration of the People's Republic of China and the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation in order to exchange of television programmes, with the aim of strengthening the mutual understanding, friendship and cooperation between the two countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media to sign the License Agreement prepared for the said purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
