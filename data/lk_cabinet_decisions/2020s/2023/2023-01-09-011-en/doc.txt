Balapitiya Sri Rahularama Purana Viharastha Samanera Akalpa Sangwardena  Bhikshu  Vidyalaya  (Incorporation)  Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-09

- Balapitiya Sri Rahularama Purana Viharastha Samanera Akalpa Sangwardena Bhikshu Vidyalaya (Incorporation) Bill has been submitted by the Hon. Gayantha Karunathilaka, Member of Parliament, as a Private Member's Bill. The relevant Bill has properly been prepared by the Legal Draftsman and the clearance of the Attorney General has been received to the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to submit a Report to Parliament recommending the relevant Bill as per the provisions of the Standing Order 52(6) of Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
