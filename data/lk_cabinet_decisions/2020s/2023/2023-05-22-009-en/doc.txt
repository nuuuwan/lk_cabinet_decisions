Memorandum of Understanding Regarding Political Consultations between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-22

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Political Consultations between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It is expected to promote bilateral ties while enhancing the positive roles of other international organizations and regional and global affairs, political developments and topics of mutual interests. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
