Awarding the procurement for the provisioning of a computer software	solution for revenue optimization at Srilankan Airlines

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-04-03

- Bids have been invited following the international competitive bidding procedure	for the provisioning of a computer software solution for revenue optimization   at Srilankan  Airlines, accordingly, 04 bids have been received. The proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to  award  the  respective  procurement to PROS, Inc  in  United  States of America,  the  lowest  evaluated   substantially	responsive bidder as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Approved Procurement	Committee for a period of 5 years at the total amount of USD 3,243,600, was approved by the Cabinet.
