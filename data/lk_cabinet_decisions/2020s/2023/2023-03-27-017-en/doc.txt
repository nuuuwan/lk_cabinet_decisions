Procurement of Coal required for the Norachcholai Lakvijaya Coal Power Plant for the Season of 2023/2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-27

- It has been estimated that 2.25 million MT of coal is required for the Norachcholai Lakvijaya Coal Power Plant for the season of 2023/2024. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister Power and Energy to implement the relevant procurement process, was approved by the Cabinet.
