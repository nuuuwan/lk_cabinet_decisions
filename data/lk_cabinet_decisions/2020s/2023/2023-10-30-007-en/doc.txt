Submission of Regulations issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969 to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-10-30

- The proposal made by the Hon.President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations Act No.14 of 2023 made under the provisions of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969 and published by the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2353/16 dated 2003‑10‑09 to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
