"Venturing into New Frontiers" - First International Mining and Mineral	Conference, 	Investor Forum and Exhibition

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-22

- With the aim of providing a platform to all stakeholders involved in the current and future mineral industry in Sri Lanka, the International Mining and Mineral Conference, Investor Forum and Exhibition named "Venturing into New Frontiers" has been planned to be held at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall. Through that it is expected to build global brand value for the Sri Lankan mineral industry and create large-scale investment and economic opportunities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Environment to conduct the proposed International Mining and Mineral Conference, Investor Forum and Exhibition in October 2023, was approved by the Cabinet.
