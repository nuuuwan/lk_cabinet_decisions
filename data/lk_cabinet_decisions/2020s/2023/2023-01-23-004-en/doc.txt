Revision of En-Route Navigation Charges levied on International Flights	Overflying Colombo Flight Information Region

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-23

- Collection of En-route Navigation charges on International Flights Overflying Colombo Flight Information Region comes under the purview of Airport and Aviation Services (Sri Lanka) Limited and the charges have not been revised since year 1985. Therefore, the proposal directed by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to revise the charges for Overflying aircraft for the use of Air Navigation Services from 2023‑02‑01 with immediate effect, was granted approval by the Cabinet.
