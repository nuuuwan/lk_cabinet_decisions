Japanese Grant Aid for Human Resource Development Scholarship Programme - 2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-07-17

- The Government of Japan has agreed to grant Yen 284 million (approximately Rs. 611 million) for the second batch of the 4th Framework of the Human Resources Development Scholarship Programme implemented under the Japanese Grant Aid. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to sign the Exchange Notes with the Government of Japan and enter into an Agreement with the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) to obtain the said Grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
