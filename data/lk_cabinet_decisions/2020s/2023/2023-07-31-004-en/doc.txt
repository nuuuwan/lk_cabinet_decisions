Obtaining Services for Aircraft of SriLankan Airlines at the Cochin International Airport (COK), India and the Jakarta International Airport (CGK), Indonesia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-07-31

- Bids have been invited by Srilankan Airlines Limited to select service suppliers for passenger and ramp and cargo handling services at the Cochin International Airport, India and the Jakartha International Airport, Indonesia. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the service supply contract of the  the Cochin International Airport, India, to AI Airport Services Limited and that of the Jakarta International Airport, Indonesia, to PT. Gapura Angkasa, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
