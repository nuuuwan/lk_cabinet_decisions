Asean Regional Forum

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-02

- Sri Lanka, a member of Asean Regional Forum holds the Co-Chair of the Inter- Sessional Meeting on Nuclear Proliferation and Disarmament of the said Forum for the period of 2022-2024. The 14th Inter-Sessional Meeting of Asean Regional Forum has been scheduled to be conducted for the members of the Asean Regional Forum on 16th  and 17th  of May, 2023. The measures taken by his Ministry to host the Inter- Sessional Meeting were brought to the notice of the Cabinet by the Minister of Foreign Affairs and it was decided to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet for the same.
