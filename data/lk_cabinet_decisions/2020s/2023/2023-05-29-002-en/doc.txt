Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding on the Field of Health Cooperation between Maldives and Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-29

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on the Field of Health Cooperation between Maldives and Sri Lanka with a view to improving health care services, promoting research and development and enhancing the overall well being between the two countries thereby strengthening and formalizing the health cooperation between the two countries. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the proposed draft Agreement. Accordingly the proposal made by the Minister of Health to sign the said  Memorandum of Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
