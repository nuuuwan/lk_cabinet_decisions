Agreement for Cooperation on Climate Change between Sri Lanka and 	the Republic of Korea

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-11-27 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- An agreement has been reached between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Korea to sign an Agreement for Cooperation on Climate Change. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Environment to sign the proposed Agreement and to establish a Joint Committee on Climate Change to facilitate and coordinate the implementation of the said Agreement.
