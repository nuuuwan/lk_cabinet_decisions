Submission of Orders made under the Sri Lanka Export Development Act,	No.40 of 1979, for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-27

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to present the Orders made under section 14 of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No.40 of 1979 and published by the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2312/76 dated 2023‑01‑01, for approval in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
