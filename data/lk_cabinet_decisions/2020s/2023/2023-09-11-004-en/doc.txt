Term contract for the procurement of 04 shipments of Gasoline of 92 Unl within the next four months period

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-09-11

- Bids have been invited for a term contract from the registered suppliers of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation to purchase 04 shipments of Gasoline 92 Unl for a period of 04 months from 2023‑09‑21 to 2024‑01‑21. For the purpose, 04 companies have submitted bids. The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the said procurement to M/s. Vitol Asia Pte. Ltd, as per the recommendation of the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
