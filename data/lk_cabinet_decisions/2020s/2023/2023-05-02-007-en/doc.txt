Management of Mahinda Rajapaksha National Tele Cinema Park under the Public-Private Partnership Methodology

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-02

- The proposal submitted by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs pertaining to managing the Mahinda Rajapaksha National Tele Cinema Park under the Public-Private Partnership Methodology was taken up for consideration at the Cabinet meeting held on 2023‑02‑27. In the instance, it was decided to refer the said proposal to the National Agency for Public-Private Partnership to examine matters in that regard and submit a Report to the Cabinet. Accordingly, the Cabinet having considered the Report submitted by the said Agency, granted approval for taking necessary action to manage Mahinda Rajapaksha National Tele Cinema Park under the Public Private Partnership Methodology based on the recommendations stated in the said Report.
