Procurement of Accessories for Buoy Mooring used for offloading petroleum in the sea near Muthurajawela

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-09-25

- Bids have been invited according to the International Competitive Bidding Procedure to procure 26 marine hoses and relevant accessories required for Buoy Mooring which is used to offload shiploads of petroleum in the sea near Muthurajawela and only one bid has been received. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the relevant procurement to M/s. Industrial Products Asia Pacific (Pte) Ltd. as recommended by the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee that evaluated the said bid, was approved by the Cabinet.
