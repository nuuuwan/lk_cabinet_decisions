Treaty on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters between Sri Lanka 	and Maldives

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-12-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Although the Cabinet approval has been granted at its meeting held on 	2022‑09‑22 to enter into the Treaty on mutual legal assistance in criminal 	matters between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic 	of Maldives, the said Treaty was not signed due to suggesting few amendments 	subsequently 	for the proposed Treaty by the Maldives party. Thereafter, a new 	amended draft has been prepared upon the concurrence of both parties and the 	clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the that. Accordingly, the 	proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional 	Reforms to sign the 	said Treaty and thereafter ratify the same by Diplomatic 	Missions of both countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
