Procurement for the purchase of oil and lubricant for the Sri Lanka	Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-12-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- National competitive bids have been invited for the procurement of purchasing oil and lubricants for the Sri Lanka Transport Board. The Cabinet Appointed Standing Procurement Committee has recommended the purchase of respective oils and lubricants from Lanka IOC PLC and Chevron Lubricant Lanka PLC, based on each item. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to award the relevant procurements to those two companies, was approved by the Cabinet.
