Anti-Corruption Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-13

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2022‑07‑18 has granted approval to prepare the Anti‑Corruption Bill. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received to the said Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to publish the said Anti-Corruption Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter in Parliament for Approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
