Review of Sri Lanka's 6th Periodic Report under the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-07

- The Review of Sri Lanka's 6th Interim Report under the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights is scheduled to be held in Geneva on March 8 and 9, 2023.	The matters presented by the Minister of Foreign Affairs regarding the steps taken so far by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for the said review, was taken into consideration by the Cabinet.
