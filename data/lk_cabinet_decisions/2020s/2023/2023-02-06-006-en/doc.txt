Submission of Regulations imposed under the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No.11 of 2021, to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-06

- Colombo Port City (Registration of Offshore Companies) Regulations No.01 of 2022 made under Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No.11 of 2021, have published in the Extraordinary Gazette No.2306/54 dated 2022‑11‑17. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to submit the said regulations to Parliament to approval of the Cabinet, was approved by the Cabinet.
