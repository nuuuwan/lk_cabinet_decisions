Entering into a Partnership Agreement between the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka and the Michigan State University of the United States of America

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-11-20 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- An agreement has been reached between the two parties to enter into a Partnership Agreement between the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka and the Michigan State University of the United States of America aiming to develop capacity building, research collaboration and for the development of Innovations and Downstream Applications relevant to smart Agriculture. The clearance of the Attorney General and the concurrence of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs have been received for the proposed draft Agreement. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the proposed Agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
