Approval to table the Surcharge Orders in Parliament (the Customs Ordinance)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-20

- Two orders have been published in the Government Gazettes dated 2022‑11‑30	and 2023‑01‑04 levying a Surcharge as a percentage on the Customs Import	Duties on selected goods under section 10(a) of the Customs Ordinance. The	proposal submitted by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Ministry of	Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to table the said Orders	in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
