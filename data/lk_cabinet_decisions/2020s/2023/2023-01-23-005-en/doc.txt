Procurement for Supply of 800,000 Prefilled Syringe of Enoxaparin Sodium 	Injection, 4,000 IU in 0.4 ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-23

- Bids were invited from Indian manufacturers and suppliers through Limited International bids to purchase 800,000 Enoxaparin Sodium Injections to be used for treatment of patients with heart diseases. Accordingly, on the recommendations of PAB and SCAPC, the proposal directed by the Minister of Health to award the aforesaid procurement to M/s. Brawn Laboratories Ltd. was granted approval by the Cabinet.
