Amendment to the Penal Code (Chapter 19) - Increase of Fines

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-12-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2022‑12‑12 has granted approval to 	prepare a Bill to amend the fines referred to in the Penal Code in line with the 	current monetary value. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has 	been received to the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. As such, the 	proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional 	Reforms to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same 	thereafter, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
