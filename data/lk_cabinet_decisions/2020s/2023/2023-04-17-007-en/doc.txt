Submission of Regulations issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969, for the approval of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-04-17

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit Imports and Exports (Control) regulations No.06 of 2023 prepared under the provisions of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969 and published through the Gazette Extraordinary No.2324/45 dated 2023‑03‑24 for approval of Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
