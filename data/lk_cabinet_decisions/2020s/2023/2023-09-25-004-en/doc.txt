Procurement of 04 shipments of Gas oil within the next four (04) months period

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-09-25

- Bids have been invited under term contract basis for procurement of 04 shipments of gas oil for Ceylon Petroleum Corporation within the next four (04) months period from 2023-11-01 to 2024-02-29. It has been received 05 bids for that.  The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the relevant procurement to M/s Petrochina International (Singapore) Pte.Ltd based on the	recommendation of the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
