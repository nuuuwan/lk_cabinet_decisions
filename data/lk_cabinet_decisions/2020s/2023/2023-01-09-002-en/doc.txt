75th Independence Day Commemoration Ceremony

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-09

- The 75th Independence Day commemoration ceremony which falls on February 04, 2023 and the programme organized to be held concurrently, including the tasks and responsibilities to be performed by the respective institutions was submitted to the Cabinet by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Government. Cabinet decided to grant its concurrence to implement the said programme.
