Implementation of a project to prevent the damage caused to the people 	who are frequently affected by the floods in the Gin River

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-07-31

- It has been identified the need of implementing a project enabling to provide a permanent solution to prevent the damage caused to the people who are frequently affected by the floods in the Gin River. The proposal made by the Minister of Irrigation to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with MCC- Sinohydro Consortium to carry out a feasibility study for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
