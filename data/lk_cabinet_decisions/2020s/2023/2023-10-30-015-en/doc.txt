The Salary revisions intended to be carry out by the Budget for the year 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-10-30

- The Cabinet held discussions pertaining to increasing the salaries of public service and the private sector by the Budget for the year 2024. At the ensued discussion, the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, apprised the Cabinet that he intends to present proposals in Parliament pertaining to the revision of salaries of the public service and in the private sector through the forthcoming budget.
