Amendment to the National Authority on Tobacco and Alcohol Act, No. 27 of 2006

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-07-31

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the the National Authority on Tobacco and Alcohol Act, No. 27 of 2006, so as to appoint Medical Officer of Health and Additional Medical Officer of Health as Authorized Officers, was approved by the Cabinet.
