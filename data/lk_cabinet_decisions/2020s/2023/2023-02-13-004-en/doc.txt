Memorandum of Understanding on the Establishment of a Political 	Consultations Mechanism Between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri 	Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates of the 	Government of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-13

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to 		establish Political Consultations Mechanism between the Ministry of Foreign 		Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates of the 		Government of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. It is expected to promote, 		strengthen and expand bilateral relations between the two countries including 		political, economic, consular, technical and scientific and cultural cooperation. 		The approval of the Attorney General has been received for the proposed draft 		Agreement. Accordingly, the proposal submitted by the Minister of Foreign 		Affairs to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
