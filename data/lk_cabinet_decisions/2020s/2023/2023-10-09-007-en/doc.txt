Formulation of the National Land Commission Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-10-09

- In accordance with the Appendix II of the List 1 of the Ninth Schedule to the Thirteenth Amendment to the Constitution, provisions have been made that a Land Commission should be established. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Tourism and Lands to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft a Bill, based on the preliminary draft prepared for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
