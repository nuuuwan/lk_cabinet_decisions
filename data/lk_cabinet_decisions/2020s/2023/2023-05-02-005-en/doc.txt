Submission of the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2022 to the Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-02

- The 73rd Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2022 hasbeen submitted to the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance,	Economic Stabilization and National Policies by the Monetary Board of the	Central Bank of Sri Lanka on 2023-04-27. In terms of the provisions in the	Monetary Law Act, the Annual Report should be submitted by the Minister in‑charge  of Finance	to the Parliament within 14 days of receiving of the said	report. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as	the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to 	submit the Annual Report of Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2022 to Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
