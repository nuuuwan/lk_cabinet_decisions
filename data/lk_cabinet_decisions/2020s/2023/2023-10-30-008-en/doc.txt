Sri Lanka Institute of Economics and International Trade Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-10-30

- The Cabinet has granted approval previously to prepare a Bill to establish Sri Lanka	Institute of Economics and International Trade with the objectives of being a platform 	for research and policy formulation related to economics, international finance and	international trade, providing cutting edge research and studies in the said fields, contribution of ideas for invigorating policy and concepts relating to economics, finance and	international trade and the effective promotion of international trade 	and	regional trade integration policies. Accordingly, the proposal made by the	Hon. President to publish the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman in the Government Gazette and thereafter, submit it to Parliament for approval, was 	approved by the Cabinet.
