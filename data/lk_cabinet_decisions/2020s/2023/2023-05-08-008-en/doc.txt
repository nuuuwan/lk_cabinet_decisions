Awarding the Contract for the Repair of CFM Leap - 1A Aircraft Engines at Srilankan Airlines

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-08

- Comprehensive proposals have been invited from the qualified service providers under the Limited International Competitive Bidding procedure for the repair of 03 CFM Leap - 1A Aircraft Engines of Srilankan Airlines and three proposals have been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the contract for the Repair of 03 CFM Leap - 1A Aircraft Engines, to Lufthansa Technik AG as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
