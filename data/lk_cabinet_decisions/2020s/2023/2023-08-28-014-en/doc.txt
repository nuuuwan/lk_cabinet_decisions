Obtaining the approval of the Parliament for the Regulations promulgated under the Forest Conservation Ordinance No.16 of 1907 (Chapter 451)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-08-28

- The proposal made by the Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation to submit the Export of timber, seeds of forest free species or other forest produce Regulations No.01 of 2023, made under the Forest Conservation Ordinance No.16 of 1907 (Chapter 451), published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification No.2346/02 dated 2023-08-21 in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
