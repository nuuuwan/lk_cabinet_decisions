Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation in the Field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-06-26

- It has been reached to a consensus between the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority and the Legal Entity of Public Law of the Georgian National Tourism Administration to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation in the field of Tourism. Clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the draft Memorandum of Understanding prepared for that purpose. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Tourism and Lands, to sign the said MoU was approved by the Cabinet.
