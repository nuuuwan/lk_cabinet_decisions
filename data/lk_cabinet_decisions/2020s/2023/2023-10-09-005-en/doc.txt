Conducting outer boundary survey and preparation of boundary maps of the estates managed under the Regional Plantation Companies, Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporation and Janatha Estates Development Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-10-09

- The project for conducting outer boundary survey and preparation of boundary maps of the estates managed under the Regional Plantation Companies, Sri Lanka State Plantation Corporation and the Janatha Estates Development Board has been commenced in 2018 and surveying has been done covering a considerable extent of the relevant lands. The proposal made by the Minister of Industries to extend the said project until 2027, enabling to cover the remaining extent of lands that remains to be surveyed, was approved by the Cabinet.
