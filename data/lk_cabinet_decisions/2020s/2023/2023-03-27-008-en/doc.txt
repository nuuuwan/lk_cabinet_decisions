Procedure related to Areca Nut Industry

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-27

- The proposals on export of locally produced Areca Nuts and importation of	dried	Areca Nuts and re‑exportation after value addition have been deliberated	at the	Cabinet meeting held on 2023‑03‑13 and an Officials Committee has	been	appointed under the Chairmanship of Senior Economic Advisor to the	President to submit a report with recommendations to the Cabinet after	examining the matters thereon.  Accordingly, as recommended by the said 	Committee, it was decided to grant approval to the procedure for export of 	locally produced Areca Nuts and the import of Areca Nuts for value added 	exports re-exportation after value addition.
