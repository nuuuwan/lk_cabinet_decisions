Awarding the contract for supplying of Aviation Fuel for SriLankan Airlines at 	London Heathrow Airport, United Kingdom and Changi Airport, Singapore

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-08-28

- Limited International Competitive Bids have been invited to secure fuel for SriLankan Airlines at London Heathrow Airport, United Kingdom and Changi Airport, Singapore which have been received 3 bids. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the contracts to secure fuel for SriLankan Airlines at the above airports, to Shell Markets (Middle East) Limited, the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
