Procurement for the purchase of Intra Ocular Lens, foldable, hydrophobic (1-7 Items)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-09

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for this procurement. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant contract to M/s Vision Solutions Lanka (Pvt) Ltd., as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
