Amending the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No.9 of 2003

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-02

- It has been identified the necessity of amending the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No.9 of 2003 so as suit to the present day requirements. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft amending legislation, based on the preliminary draft prepared, was approved by the Cabinet.
