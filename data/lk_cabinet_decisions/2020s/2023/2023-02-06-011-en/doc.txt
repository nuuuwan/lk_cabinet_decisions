Submission of Regulations issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-06

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to present the Imports Control Regulations on Payment Terms No.03 of 2023 published in the Gazette Extraordinary Notification dated 2023‑01‑19 under the Imports and Exports  (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969, in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
