Negotiations of the 12th Round of the Proposed India - Sri Lanka Economic	and Technology Cooperation Agreement (ETCA)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-12-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Negotiations of the 12th Round pertaining to the proposed India - Sri Lanka 	Economic and Technology Cooperation Agreement (ETCA) has been held in 	Colombo from 2023‑10‑30 to 2023‑11‑01 and in this instance, working groups 	of both parties have reached a consensus pertaining to several fields concerned. 	Both parties have agreed to hold the 13th Round of Negotiations pertaining to the 	proposed Agreement in India from 2024‑01‑08 to 2024‑01‑10 and to finalize the 	technical level negotiations by March 2024. The Cabinet noted the information 	furnished by the Hon. President pertaining to the 12th Round of Negotiations.
