Amending the Schedule VIII of the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance No.2 of 1937

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-12-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- More than 680 plant species belonging to 121 genera have been declared as protected plants in accordance with the plant classification systems coming under the Schedule VIII of the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance No.2 of 1937 as was last amended by the Fauna and Flora Protection (Amendment) Act No.7 of 2022. However, the necessity to amend the said Schedule to suit the current day needs has been identified. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Wildlife and Forest Resources Conservation to appoint a Committee consisting of experts in the fields of plant taxonomy and nomenclature, ecology, wildlife conservation and ethanobotany and to amend the Schedule VIII of the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance as per the recommendations of the said Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
