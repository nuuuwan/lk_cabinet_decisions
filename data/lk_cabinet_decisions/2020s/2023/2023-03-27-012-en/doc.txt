Determination of Deputy Inspector General Divisional Boundaries

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-27

- In the Policy Statement presented at the opening of the 04th Session of the 09th Parliament, Hon. President pointed out that, since the existing Deputy Inspector General divisional boundaries do not coincide with the existing provincial boundaries, it is necessary to determine the Deputy Inspector General divisional boundaries according to the provinces in order to avoid practical difficulties and issues in administration. Accordingly, it was decided to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet regarding the measures taken by the Hon. President to appoint an	Officials' Committee under the Chairmanship of Secretary to the Ministry of Public Security to determine the Deputy Inspector General divisional boundaries.
