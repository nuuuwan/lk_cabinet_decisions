Awarding the Contract for the Ground Handling Services for SriLankan Airlines at the Doha International Airport and Cargo Warehouse Handling Services at the Dubai International Airport

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-22

- Bids have been invited for Ground Handling Services at the Doha International Airport and Cargo Warehouse Handling Services at Dubai International Airport. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the contract for Ground Handling Services at the Doha International Airport to Qatar Aviation Services W.L.L and to award the contract for Cargo Warehouse Handling Services at the Dubai International Airport to Dnata Company as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
