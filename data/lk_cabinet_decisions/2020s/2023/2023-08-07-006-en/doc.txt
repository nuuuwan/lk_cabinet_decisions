Achievements in managing the Economic crisis and Way forward

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-08-07

- The information presented by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, regarding the issues faced by the country when the current Hon. President was appointed as the Prime Minister and the Minister of Finance in May 2022 and after assuming duties of the Presidency on 2022‑07‑20, the actions taken so far together with the new Cabinet pertaining to the areas such as fiscal policy, monetary policy, energy sector, external sector, debt	restructuring and foreign financing in order to create the economic stability in the country by solving the said issues, was considered by the Cabinet.  Also, the Cabinet granted it's concurrence on the Reform measures that are expected to carry out in the future in order to create economic growth by restoring the economy.
