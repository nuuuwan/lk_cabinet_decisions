Submission of Final Budget Position Report - 2022 in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-06-12

- In terms of the provisions of section 13 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act No. 3 of 2003, the Minister in charge of Finance shall release the Final Budget Position Report to the public not later than five (05) months from the end of the relevant financial year, and thereafter, table the said Report in Parliament. Accordingly, the Final Budget Position Report - 2022 has been published in the website of the Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to table the said report in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
