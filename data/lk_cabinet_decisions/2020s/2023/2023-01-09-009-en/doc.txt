Procurement for the supply of raw food items to the National Hospital, Kandy for the year 2022/2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-01-09

- National Competitive Bids have been invited for the procurement for the supply of raw food items to the National Hospital, Kandy for the year 2022/2023. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant contract to Janatha Food Suppliers Private Limited as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
