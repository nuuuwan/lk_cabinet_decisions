Implementation of Dehiwala Smart Metering Project of Ceylon Electricity	Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-04-03

- Initiatives have been taken to implement a Smart Metering System targeting a customer base of 50,000 initially in Dehiwala area and its' estimated cost is LKR1,000    million.    Bids    have    been   invited   under   the international   competitive 	bidding procedure for the design, supply, install, testing and commissioning    of    its'	infrastructure  and  5  bids   have   been   received. The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the respective procurement to M/s Yantai Dongfang Wisdom Electric Co. Ltd in China, the substantially responsive bidder as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Approved Procurement Committee at the total amount of LKR 937.91 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
