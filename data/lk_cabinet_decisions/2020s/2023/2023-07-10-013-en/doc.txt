Mid-Year Fiscal Position Report

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-07-10

- In terms of section 10 of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No.3 of 2003, the Minister in charge of the subject of Finance is required to release the Report of Mid-Year Fiscal Position to the general public by the end of June of each year and thereafter to table the same in Parliament. Accordingly, the Mid-Year Fiscal Position Report up to 2023‑06‑30 has been published on the website of the Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies. As such, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to table the said Report in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
