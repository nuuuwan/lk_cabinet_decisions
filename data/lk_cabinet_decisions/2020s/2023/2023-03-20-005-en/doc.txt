Submission of Regulations issued under the Imports and Exports (Control)Act, No.1 of 1969, for Approval of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-03-20

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the following regulations enacted under the provisions of Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969, for Approval of Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.

*	Imports Control Regulations on Payment Terms No.04 of 2023, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No.2320/46 dated 2023‑02‑22.

*	Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No.05 of 2023, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No.2320/47 dated 2023‑02‑22.
