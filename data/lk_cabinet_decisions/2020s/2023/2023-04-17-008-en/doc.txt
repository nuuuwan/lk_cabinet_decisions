Submission of an order imposed under Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No.40 of 1979, for the approval of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-04-17

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to submit the order prepared under section 14 of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No.40 of 1979 and published through the Gazette Extraordinary No.2325/06 dated 2023-03-28 for approval of Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
