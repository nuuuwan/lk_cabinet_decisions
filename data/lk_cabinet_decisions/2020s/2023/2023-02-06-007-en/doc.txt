Introducing amendments to the Employees' Trust Fund Act, No.46 of 1980

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-02-06

- A proposal to provide Health Insurance similar to Agrahara Health Insurance to 	private sector employees too, was presented by the Hon. President to the	Parliament through the Budget Speech 2023. The proposal made by Hon.	President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic	Stabilization and National	Policies to prepare a Bill to amend the Employees'	Trust Fund Act No.46 of 1980, enabling the implementation of the said	Proposal, was approved by the Cabinet.
