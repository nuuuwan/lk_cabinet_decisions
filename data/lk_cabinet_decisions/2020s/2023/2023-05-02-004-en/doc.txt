Providing relief to the public officers who are entitled to the political rights 	and had submitted the nominations for the Local Authorities Election

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-05-02

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of 	Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local 	Government, taking into consideration the observations submitted by the Election 	Commission regarding the measures that should be taken in relation to public 	officers who are candidates for the Local Authorities Election 2023 until the 	election is held, to provide the opportunity for such candidates, if they are serving 	in a Government/Semi-Government Institution located within the Local Authority 	Area in which they contest as candidates at the said Election, to report for duty by 	transferring them to Government/Semi-Government Institutions located outside 	the relevant Local Authority Area, was approved by the Cabinet.
