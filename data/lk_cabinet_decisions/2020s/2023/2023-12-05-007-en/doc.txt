Amendments to the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No.15 of 1979

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2023-12-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2021‑09‑27 has granted approval to amend the 	Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No. 15 of 1979 to make legal provisions 	enabling a criminal court to decide whether the requirement of personal 	attendance of suspect/accused persons in courts under certain identified 	circumstances could 	be dispensed with even during the trial. Several 	interested	parties have submitted further matters for the Bill prepared accordingly, prior to 	the submission of the same to Parliament. As such, the proposal made by the 	Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to instruct the 	Legal Draftsman to draft a new Bill to amend the Code of Criminal Procedure 	Act, No. 15 of 1979 	by further developing the Bill already drafted, taking also 	into consideration the aforesaid matters, was approved by the Cabinet.
