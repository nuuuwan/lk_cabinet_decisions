55th Annual Meeting of the Asian Development Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-10-31

- Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies attended the 55th Annual Meeting of the of the Asian Development Bank held in Manila, Philippine on 2022‑09‑29 and the Cabinet considered the key points highlighted in the speech made by the President having presided over the Business Forum of the said Meeting as the member representing Sri Lanka at the Board of the Asian Development Bank and the information furnished with regard to the Bilateral discussions conducted with the Chairman of the Asian Development Bank, Minister of Finance of Japan and as well as the President of the Republic of the Philippines.
