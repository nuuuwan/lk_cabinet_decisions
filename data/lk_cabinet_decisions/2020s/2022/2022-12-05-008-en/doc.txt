Preparing the Bill to establish the Parliamentary Budget Office

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-05

- The Hon. President submitted a preliminary draft for the establishment of the Parliamentary Budget Office for consideration at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022‑10‑31 for consideration and it was decided to refer the said preliminary draft to all party leaders of Parliament and seek their views on the matters therein. Accordingly, the Hon. Prime Minister has discussed with the party leaders regarding the preliminary draft and the views expressed at that discussion were brought to the notice of the Cabinet. As such, The Cabinet decided to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare the draft Bill based on the preliminary draft already prepared, taking also into consideration the said views.
