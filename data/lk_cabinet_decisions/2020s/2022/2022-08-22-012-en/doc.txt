Signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Corporation in the field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-08-22

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on corporation in the field of tourism between the Ministry of Tourism and Lands of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Heritage and Tourism of the Sultanate of Oman. The clearance of the Attorney general has been received for the draft Agreement for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Tourism and Lands to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
