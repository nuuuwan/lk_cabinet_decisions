Granting approval in principal for the Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-12

- The Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission implemented with effect from 2009‑04‑02 have been redrafted to be adopted with the present day needs and rules and regulations. The final draft of the Procedural Rules has been prepared incorporating the proposals submitted after further studying the said draft Procedural Rules by the Officials' Committee appointed by the Cabinet. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to grant approval in principal for the Procedural Rules prepared by the Public Service Commission, in terms of the Article 55(1) of the Constitution and implement the same with effect from 2023‑01‑01, was approved by the Cabinet.
