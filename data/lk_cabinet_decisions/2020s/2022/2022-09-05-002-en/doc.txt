Agreement on Academic Linkage and Collaboration

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-09-05

- A proposal has been made to sign an Agreement on Academic Linkage and Collaboration between the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University and the School of Architecture of Tianjin University, China. Clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the agreement drafted incorporating the observations submitted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Department of National Budget and the Department of External Resources. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to sign the said agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
