Submission of Orders published in the Gazette, for approval in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-06-13

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit two (02) Gazette Notifications containing Orders issued under the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No.40 of 1979, six (06) Gazette Notifications issued under the Special Commodity Levy Act, No.48 of 2007 imposing Orders and a Gazette Notification issued under Revenue Protection Act, No.19 of 1962 imposing Revenue Protection Order No.02/2022, was approved by the Cabinet.
