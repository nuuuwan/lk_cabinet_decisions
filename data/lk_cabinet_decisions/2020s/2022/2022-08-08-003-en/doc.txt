Allocation of blocks of land from the Infrastructure Developed Regional Industrial Estates to set up Industries

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-08-08

- ''Regional Industrial Estates Development Programme'' is implemented by the Ministry of Industries with a view to promoting industries at regional levels. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Industries to allocate land blocks from 13 Industrial Zones for 25 investors selected under the said programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
