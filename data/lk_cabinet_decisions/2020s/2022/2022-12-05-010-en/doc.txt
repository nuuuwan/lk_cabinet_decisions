Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and 	Maldives on immigration purposes

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-05

- It has been proposed to sign an MoU between Sri Lanka and Maldives for the cooperation in immigration purposes at the bilateral relations between the two countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security to sign an MoU to develop and strengthen the cooperation in immigration purposes between the two countries with a view to assisting the management of border controlling for mutual benefits, facilitating the mutual cooperation for skill development in the areas of human resource system and technology, exchanging of training facilities among immigration officers in the promotion of human resources, prevention of international crimes such as illegal migration, human trafficking and smuggling of persons and promotion of cooperation on the issues related to blacklisted passengers, was approved by the Cabinet.
