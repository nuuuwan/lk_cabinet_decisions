International Water Conference on Sustainable Development Cooperation, Partnership and Appropriate Technology

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-11-28

- The National Water Supply and Drainage Board has taken steps to organize the "International Water Conference on Sustainable Development, Cooperation, Partnership and Appropriate Technology - 2022" from 2022‑12‑14 to 2022‑12‑16 December 2022. This conference is envisaged to achieve the objectives such as bringing together water professionals, foreign delegates, international organizations, Government Officials and Journalists; share ideas and knowledge for research; create links among water professionals and exchange of experiences and achievements in the water sector of different countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Water Supply to conduct the proposed conference involving the relevant local and international institutions, was approved by the Cabinet.
