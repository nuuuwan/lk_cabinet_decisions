Providing Permanent Houses for families who do not have a house or a land to construct a house and live mainly on rent basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-01-10

- In view of providing permanent houses at an affordable price for the low middle income families with true housing requirement who reside in rented houses due to not having either a house or a land to construct a house, the State Ministry of Rural Housing and Construction & Building Material Industries Promotion has planned to implement a housing programme named as “Sonduru Mahal Niwasa” through the National Housing Development Authority. Under the programme, the selected beneficiaries should have the ability of making a down‑payment of Rs.500,000/- and the remaining value of the house will be charged in installments within a year. The selected beneficiaries will also be given the opportunity to obtain loans from Banks as found necessary. As such, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to implement the said housing programme and to construct 72 houses in a plot of land at Ranpokunugama, belonging to the National Housing Development Authority as its pilot project, was approved by the Cabinet.
