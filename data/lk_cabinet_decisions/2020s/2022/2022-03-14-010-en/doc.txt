Awarding the Term Contract for the Import of Diesel

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-14

- Bids have been invited from the registered suppliers to award the term contract for  importing Diesel (0.05% M.S.) for a period of 08 months from 2022‑03‑01 to 2022‑10‑31. Awarding the relevant procurement to the Coral Energy DMCC has been recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the relevant procurement, was approved by the Cabinet.
