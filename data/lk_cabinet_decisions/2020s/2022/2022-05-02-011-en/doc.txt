Report of the Committee of Experts appointed to draft a new Constitution

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-05-02

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2020 09 09 has decided to appoint a Committee chaired by Mr. Romesh De Silva, President's Counsel, comprising of legal experts to draft a new Constitution. Accordingly, preliminary Report of the said Committee has been handed over to H.E the President on 2022 04 25. As such, the Cabinet has decided to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee comprising of the following Ministers to study the matters containing in the said Report and submit appropriate recommendations to the Cabinet.

	Minister of Foreign Affairs - (Chairman)
