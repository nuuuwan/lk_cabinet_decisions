Importation of 1.8 million barrels of Gasoline (92 Unl) for a period of eight (08) months from 2022-02-15 to 2022-10-14

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-28

- Bids have been called from the registered suppliers and the suppliers registered under the provisional category to award the long term contract for importing 1.8 million barrels of Gasoline (92 Unl) for a period of eight (08) months from 2022‑02-15 to 2022‑10‑14. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the said long term contract to M/s. OQ Trading Limited, United Arab Emirates, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
