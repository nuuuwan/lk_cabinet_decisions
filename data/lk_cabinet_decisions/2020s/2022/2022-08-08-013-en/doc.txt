Activation of a Revised Price Formula for Domestic Type Liquefied Petroleum	Gas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-08-08

- Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to activate a cost depicted pricing formula immediately and to revise the price of domestic gas on the 5th day of every month based on that price formula, with the aim of ensuring continuous domestic gas supply.
