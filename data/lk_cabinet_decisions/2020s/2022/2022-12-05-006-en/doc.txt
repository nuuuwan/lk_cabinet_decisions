Amendment to the Mediation Board Act, No.72 of 1988

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-05

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted on 2022‑09‑22 to amend the Mediation Board Act, No.72 of 1988 for introducing provisions pertaining to the appointment of members of the Commission, determining the jurisdiction of Mediation Boards, disputes that can not be settled and filing cases before courts in respect of such disputes, to be suited with the present day needs. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received to the draft Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
