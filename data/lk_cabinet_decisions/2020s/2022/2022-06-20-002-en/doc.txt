Submission of the Annual Report (Final Budget Position Report) of the  Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization & National Policies for the year 2021 to Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-06-20

- Annual Report (Final Budget Position Report) of the  Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization & National Policies should be issued prior to the expiry of 05 months at the end of relevant Financial year in terms of the provisions of the Fiscal Management (Responsibility) Act, No. 03 of 2003. Accordingly, the said Annual Report for the year 2021 has been published in the official website of the Ministry. As such, the proposal made by Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization & National Policies to table the said Report in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
