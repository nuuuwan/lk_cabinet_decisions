Leasing out of two blocks of land located adjacent to the Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport (MRIA), to establish 02 Airport Hotels and operate the same

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-14

- Although Hambantota area where  the Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport is located is a famous tourist destination, only few hotels are being operated in that area. It has been identified that construction of 02 hotels of 4 star category in the close proximity of the airport would attract tourists and it may create a more favorable environment.  For the purpose, two land lots each in extent of 15 hectares located on the right side of the Terminal Building of the Airport have been identified. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Tourism to Request for Proposals from the investors for the establishment of the proposed 02 hotels, was approved by the Cabinet.
