Obtaining the assistance of the Asia Foundation to continuously maintain 	the mediation related activities of the Community Mediation Boards and 	Special Land Mediation Boards

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-05

- The Asia Foundation which has supported the mediation programme in sri Lanka since its inception has agreed to grant further financial support to continuously maintain and strengthen the mediation related activities of the Community Mediation Boards and Special Land Mediation Boards. Accordingly, the Asia Foundation has agreed to provide a grant of Rs.127 million for the said purpose. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to sign a Memorandum of Understanding to obtain the aforesaid grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
