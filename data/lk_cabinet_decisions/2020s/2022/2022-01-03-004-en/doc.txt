Procurement for the supply of 75,000 Vials of Human Immunoglobin for IV use BP 5-6g for Intravenous use

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-01-03

- International Competitive Bidding has been invited for the purchase of 75,000 Vials of Human Immunoglobin for Intravenous BP 5-6g used to treat for the patients suffering from life-threatening diseases. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant procurement to the Reliance Life Sciences (Pvt) Ltd., India as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
