Obtaining additional provisions required for renovation and 	conservation of the National Art Gallery

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-19

- Although the renovation works of the National Art Gallery have been planned to complete during this year, owing to the situation prevailed in the country, it has not been possible to finish the expected task. Nearly 80% of the renovation work has been completed at present and related plans and estimates have been prepared by the Project Advisory Unit of the Faculty of Architecture, University of Moratuwa for the completion of remaining works. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to secure required provisions so that the remaining renovation works can be completed in the year 2023, was approved by the Cabinet.
