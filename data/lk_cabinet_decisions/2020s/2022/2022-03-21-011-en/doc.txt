Amending the Wills Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-21

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to amend the Wills Ordinance and accordingly, clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the draft Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to publish the said draft Bill in the Government Gazette and present the same thereafter in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
