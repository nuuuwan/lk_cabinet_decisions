Upgrading of Ampara and Mannar Urban Councils as Municipal 	Councils

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-12

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2022‑08‑08 to take necessary action to convert the capital cities which remain as urban councils in several districts, into municipal councils without spending additional allocations while using the existing infrastructure facilities, office facilities and human resources. Taking into consideration the said approval, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils and Local Governments to upgrade Ampara and Mannar in order to facilitate the fulfillment of the desired development tasks in the said two cities, which are the main cities of Ampara and Mannar districts, was approved by the Cabinet.
