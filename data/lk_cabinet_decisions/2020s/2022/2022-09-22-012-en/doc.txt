Limitation of Foreign Travel by Ministers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-09-22

- The proposal made by the Hon. President to limit the foreign travel of the Cabinet and State Ministers only to the most essentials purposes and limit the accompanying team only to a key officer or to a group consisting of few most essential officers with a view to minimizing the Government expenditure, taking into consideration the difficult economic situation currently faced by the country, was approved by the Cabinet.
