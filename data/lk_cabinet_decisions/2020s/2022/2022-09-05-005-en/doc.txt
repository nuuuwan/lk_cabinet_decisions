Establishment of the National Agency for Public Private Partnership

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-09-05

- The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to establish the National Agency for Public Private Partnership under the Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies enabling the encouragement of investments by identifying the difficulties and problems faced by investors in investing in Sri Lanka and making proposals to resolve them and identifying public-private partnership projects and taking necessary measures to implement public-private partnership projects under a specific time frame.
