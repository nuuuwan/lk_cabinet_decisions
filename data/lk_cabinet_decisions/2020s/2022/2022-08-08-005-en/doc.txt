Awarding contract for the Aviation Insurance to Sri Lankan Airlines Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-08-08

- Sri Lankan Airlines obtains insurance services under 04 categories for its aircraft. Limited National Competitive Bidding have been invited from general Insurance Companies which have registered  under Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka for the said four categories. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the relevant contract to the Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
