Revise the minimum age policy for Sri Lankan women for domestic and 	domestic related work abroad

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-06-20

- The current minimum age limit for Sri Lankan women to engage in domestic and domestic related work abroad is 25 years for Saudi Arabia, 23 years for other Middle Eastern countries and 21 years for other countries.  The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2022‑06‑06 decided to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee headed by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to study on the revision of age limits, in view of a proposal made by the Minister of Labour and Foreign Employment, and submit the recommendations to the Cabinet. Accordingly, considering the recommendations made by the said Sub-Committee, Cabinet decided to grant its concurrence to revise the minimum age limit for Sri Lankan women to engage in domestic and domestic related work abroad is 21 years.
