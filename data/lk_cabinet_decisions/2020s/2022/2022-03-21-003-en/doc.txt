Signing of the proposed Agreement between the Government of Australia and the Government of Sri Lanka to implement the second phase of the “Skills for Inclusive Growth (S4IG)” Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-21

- As per an agreement entered into with the Government of Australia in 2017, Skills for Inclusive Growth Project has been implemented in the districts of Trincomallee, Baticaloa, Ampara, Polonnaruwa, Anuradhapura and Matale during a period of four years, with a grant assistance of Australian Dollar 15 million, aiming to enhance the entrepreneurship in the tourism sector to upgrade the value chains in the tourism sector in the country. The first Phase of the project has now been completed and the Government of Australia has agreed to  provide  a grant of Australian Dollar 12 million to implement its second phase. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to enter into an agreement with the Government of Australia to implement the said project up to 2024 including Anuradhapura and Matale Districts also for the second phase of the project, in addition to the  04 districts in which the first phase of the project was implemented, was approved by the Cabinet.
