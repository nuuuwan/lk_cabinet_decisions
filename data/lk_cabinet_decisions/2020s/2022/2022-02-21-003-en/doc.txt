Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Sushma Swaraj Institute of Foreign Service (SSIFS), India and the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute (BIDTI), Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-21

- In view of improving co‑operation and exchange of training, it has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute (BIDTI), Sri Lanka and the Sushma Swaraj Institute of Foreign Service (SSIFS), India. Clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the proposed draft MoU. As such, the proposal made by the Foreign Minister to sign the respective MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
