Procurement for obtaining security services to the National Savings Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-06-20

- Bids have been called to obtain security services for the head office of the National Savings Bank and its branches  located island wide, following the National Competitive Bidding procedure. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to award the relevant contracts to the security services providing agencies qualified under each category, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
