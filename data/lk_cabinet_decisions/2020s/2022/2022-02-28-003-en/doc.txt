Asian Development Bank financed Power System Reliability Strengthening Project - Package 04

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-28

- Under the Package 04 of the Asian Development Bank financed Power System Reliability Strengthening Project, International Bids have been  invited for Design, Supply and Installation of transmission lines and sections. The Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee has recommended to assign the said Contract to the China Machinery Engineering Corporation (CMEC), China. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power to award the relevant Contract, was approved by the Cabinet.
