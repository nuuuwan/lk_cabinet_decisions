Amendment to the Powers of Attorney Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-06-27

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2021‑07‑19 has granted approval to introduce amendments to the Powers of Attorney Ordinance with a view to minimizing the fraudulent activities carried out using the forged and illegal powers of attorney. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the draft Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to publish the said draft Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
