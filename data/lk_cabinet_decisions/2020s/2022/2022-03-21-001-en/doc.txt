Appointment of the Ministerial Committee on Investment Promotion

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-21

- The proposal made by H.E the President to appoint the “Ministerial Committee on Investment Promotion” under the chairmanship of H.E the President and with the participation of relevant Ministers and State Ministers including the Hon. Prime Minister, with the objective of achieving the development goals focusing on national priority areas and providing necessary policy and practical solutions required to identify and promote investment opportunities in related sectors and to appoint an Officials Committee under the chairmanship of the Secretary to the President and comprising of the Secretaries to relevant Ministries and State Ministries and other relevant officials, to assist the said Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
