Awarding Procurements for the supply of Dental Consumables

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-28

- International Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement for the supply of dental consumables used for dental surgeries of the patients and accordingly, 09 bids have been received for the said procurement. The Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee has recommended to award the said procurements to substantially responsive lowest bidders as per the recommendation of the Technical Evaluation Committee. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant procurements as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
