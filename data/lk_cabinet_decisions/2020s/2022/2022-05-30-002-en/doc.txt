Deployment of Dry - Bulk Carrier Ships of the Ceylon Shipping Corporation during South – West Monsoon Period in 2022

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-05-30

- Expression of Interest (EOI) has been called for the deployment of the two vessels; Mv. Ceylon Breeze and Mv. Ceylon Princess belonging to the Ceylon Shipping Corporation in the service of the international charter market during the South-West Monsoon Period in 2022. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the contract for the deployment of the said two vessels in service during the prescribed period under the Commercial Management System, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee to M/s Wallem Shipping Pte Ltd., was approved by the Cabinet.
