Vesting Legal Powers to Maintain the Sri Lanka Navy Hydrographic Service 	at National Level

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-05

- The proposal for the vesting of marine hydrographic affairs in Sri Lanka's local waters and Exclusive Economic Zone to the Sri Lanka Navy as a non-naval duty was taken up for consideration at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022‑09‑05 and a Cabinet Sub‑Committee was appointed under the chairmanship of the Minister of Ports Shipping and Aviation to further study the said proposal and make recommendations.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to vest the marine hydrographic affairs in Sri Lanka's local waters and Exclusive Economic Zone to the Sri Lanka Navy as a non-naval duty by a Gazette Notification and to establish a National Hydrography Office so that all the agencies dealing with this subject can work together as per the recommendations of the said Committee , was approved by the Cabinet.
