Procurement for the supply of  3,000,000 Particulate Filtering Face Masks

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-14

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the purchase of these Face Masks which are used to prevent respiratory tract infections. Taking into account the recommendation of the Technical Evaluation Committee,  the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee has recommended the award of the relevant procurement to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, Lucky Industries (Pvt.) Limited. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant procurement, was approved by the Cabinet.
