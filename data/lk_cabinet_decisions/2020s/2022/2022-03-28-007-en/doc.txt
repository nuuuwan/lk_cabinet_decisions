Submission of the 2021 Article IV Report of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) to Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-28

- The 2021 Article IV Report of the International Monetary Fund pertaining to Sri Lanka  has been published on 2022‑03‑25. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance to submit the said Report in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
