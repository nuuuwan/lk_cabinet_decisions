Procurement of a Point of Sales System (POS) at the Sales Outlets of Multi-Purpose Co-operative Societies

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-14

- Bids have been invited for procurement of a Point of Sales System (POS) at the Sales Outlets of Multi‑Purpose Co‑operative Societies and 05 bids have been received for the purpose. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade to award the said procurement to Ewis Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd., as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
