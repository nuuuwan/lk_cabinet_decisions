Providing opportunity to the public to visit National Parks, Entertainment Parks, Botanical Gardens and to watch movies in cinema halls free of charge on the 75th Independence Day

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-11-21

- The proposal made by the Hon. President to provide opportunity to the public to visit National Parks, Botanical Gardens and Entertainment Parks/Places free of charge and also to provide opportunity to watch movies with 50% off for ticket prices of the Cinema Halls located across the country on 75th Independence Day falling on 4th February 2023, was approved by the Cabinet.
