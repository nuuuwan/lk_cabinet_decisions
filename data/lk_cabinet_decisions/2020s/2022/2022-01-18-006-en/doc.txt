Obtaining the legitimate possession of the land where Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation is located to the Corporation itself

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-01-18

- Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation operates on a land in extent of 3.37 hectares located at Torrington Square, Colombo and its  legitimate possession has not yet been vested with that Corporation. All the parties including the Urban Development Authority have agreed to vest the said land to the  Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media to vest the relevant land  to the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation by a free grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
