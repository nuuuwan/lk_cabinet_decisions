Tripartite Memorandum of Understanding on Collaboration in the field of 	Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-09-05

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding among the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University and the Institute for Research & Development in Health and Social Care, Sri Lanka and the University of Keele, United Kingdom, for Collaboration in the field of Education. The proposed tripartite Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) has been drafted also including the observations submitted by the Department of External Resources, Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Attorney General and the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence, to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
