Bilateral Air Services Agreement Between Sri Lanka and the Netherlands

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-21

- Although approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2019‑07‑30 to sign a Bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Netherlands, said agreement has not yet been signed. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Tourism to sign the said Air Services Agreement, with a view to further strengthening the relationship in the field of aviation, was approved by the Cabinet.
