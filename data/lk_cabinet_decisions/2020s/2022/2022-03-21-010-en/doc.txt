Amending the Dangerous Animals Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-21

- Approval of the Cabinet was granted previously to introduce amendments to the Dangerous Animals Ordinance. As such, the Legal Draftsman has prepared a draft Bill and clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the said draft Bill. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to publish the Dangerous Animals (Amendment) Ordinance in the Government Gazette and present the same thereafter in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
