Limiting the overseas leave granted to Ministers, State Ministers and Members of 	Parliament of the Government Group during Parliamentary Sitting days 	and Budget Debate

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-10-31

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to the effect that Ministers, State Ministers and Members of Parliament of the Government Group should not travel abroad during Parliamentary Sitting days and Budget Debate, other than in urgent medical needs or in case of very urgent tasks of the Government, was approved by the Cabinet.
