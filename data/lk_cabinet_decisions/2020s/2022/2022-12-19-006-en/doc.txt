Extending the validity period of the Loan of the Supporting Electricity Supply Reliability Improvement Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-19

- Supporting Electricity Supply Reliability Improvement Project is implemented under the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and two grants extended by the Clean Energy Financing Partnership Facility and the Japan Funds for Poverty Reduction. Action has been taken to extend the deadline for the completion of activities under Package - 4, up to 2023‑11‑29.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to extend the validity period of the ADB Loan facility up to 2024‑03‑31 enabling to secure the required funds, was approved by the Cabinet.
