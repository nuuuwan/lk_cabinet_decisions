Establishment of an Office for Overseas Sri Lankan Affairs

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-19

- In the Interim Budget 2022, the Hon. President has proposed to establish an Office for Overseas Sri Lankan Affairs to function as a central point for coordination to obtain the support of Sri Lankans living abroad. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to take necessary action to establish the proposed “Office for Overseas Sri Lankan Affairs” to obtain the service of 03 million Sri Lankans living abroad for the development of this country and to maintain the activities of the said Office properly, was approved by the Cabinet.
