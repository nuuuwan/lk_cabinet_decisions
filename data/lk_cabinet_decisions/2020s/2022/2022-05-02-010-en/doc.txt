Identifying Rapid Investment Opportunities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-05-02

- The Cabinet decided to appoint a Committee comprising of the following Ministers to identify investment opportunities that could attract foreign investments expeditiously as a solution to the prevailing foreign exchange crisis in the country.
