Submission of Regulations imposed under the provisions of the Foreign Exchange Act, No.12 of 2017 to facilitate investments in the Colombo Port City in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-28

- The Colombo Port City Economic Commission has been authorized to determine and grant exemption or incentive from prevailing laws in the country including the Foreign Exchange Act in terms of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No.11 of 2021. The Monetary Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka has approved the request made by the Colombo Port City Economic Commission to issue necessary Regulations and Directions in terms of the Foreign Exchange Act introducing a separate mechanism to facilitate investments in the area of authority of the said Commission until necessary Rules and Regulations are issued/promulgated by the Commission. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance to  promulgate regulations under section 7(1) of the Foreign Exchange Act No.12 of 2017 and to submit the said regulations for approval in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
