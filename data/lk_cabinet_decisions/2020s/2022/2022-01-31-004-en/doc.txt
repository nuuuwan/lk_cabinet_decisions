The Mobile Service and Reconciliation Programme conducted by the Ministry of Justice

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-01-31

- The Ministry of Justice has scheduled to conduct 04 Mobile Service and Reconciliation Programmes within this year, aiming Jaffna and Kilinochchi districts in the Northern Province, Batticaloa district in the Eastern Province, Matara district in the Southern Province and Nuwara Eliya district in the Central Province. The objectives of these Programmes are to provide assistance to resolve lands, official documents and legal matters related issues and to make the general public and field officers aware of the services offered by the Ministry of Justice and the institutions under the purview of the Ministry. Its first programme has been conducted in Jaffna and Kilinochchi districts in the Northern Province from 2022‑01‑26 to 2022‑01‑30. As such, the above matters submitted by the Minister of Justice regarding the said Mobile Programme, were noted by the Cabinet.
