Rural Finance Programme implemented under the Smallholder Tea and 	Rubber Revitalization Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-19

- The Government of Sri Lanka and the International Fund for Agricultural Development have entered into an agreement for the implementation of the Smallholder Tea and Rubber Revitalization Project. Under the said project, "Rural Finance Program" is being implemented and the program is due to be terminated on 2022‑12‑31. Another amount of Rs.172.35 million out of the money allocated for that program is to be released to the beneficiaries as loans. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Plantation Industries to extend the period of the duration of the said program till 2023‑06‑30, was approved by the Cabinet.
