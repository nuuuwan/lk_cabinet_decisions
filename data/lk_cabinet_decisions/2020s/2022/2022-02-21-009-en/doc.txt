Designing a Court Automation System for Judicial System of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-21

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted on 2020‑02‑05 to implement a project for Design, Supply, Implementation and Maintenance of a Court Automation System for Judicial System of Sri Lanka in collaboration with the Information and Communication Technology Agency of Sri Lanka. Seven (07) bids have been submitted for the proposed project. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to award the said procurement to the Joint Venture of Dialog Broadband Networks (Pvt) Limited, Formis Network Services Sdn Bhd and Access International (Pvt) Limited, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
