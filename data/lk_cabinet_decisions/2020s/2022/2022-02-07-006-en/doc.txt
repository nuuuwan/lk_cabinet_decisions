Adaption of the Cabinet decision relating to the removal of salary anomalies of Teachers' and Principals' services also to the Pirivena Teachers, Government Approved Private School Teachers and the Teachers of Government Approved Assisted Special Schools

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-07

- The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Education to adapt the provisions in the Public Administration Circular No.03/2016(IV) issued in terms of the cabinet decision taken with regard to the removal of salary anomalies of Teachers' and Principals' services also to the Pirivena Teachers, Government Approved Private School Teachers and the Teachers of Government Approved Assisted Special Schools.
