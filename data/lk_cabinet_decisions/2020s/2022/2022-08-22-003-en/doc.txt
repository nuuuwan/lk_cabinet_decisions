Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding for Cooperation in the Field of 	Agriculture

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-08-22

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding  between the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries Wealth and Water Resources of the Government of the Sultanate of Oman and the Ministry of Agriculture of Sri Lanka on Cooperation in the Field of Agriculture. Clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the proposed draft agreement for that purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture to sign the relevant Memorandum of Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
