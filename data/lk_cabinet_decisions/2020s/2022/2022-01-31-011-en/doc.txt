Purchase of 40,000 MT of Gas Oil from M/s Indian Oil Company Ltd.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-01-31

- The Ministry of Energy has negotiated with M/s Indian Oil Company Ltd. pertaining to the supply of 40,000 MT of Gas Oil and  40,000 MT of Gasoline from the said Company. As such, the said Company has agreed to supply 40,000MT of Gas Oil. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy to pursue action to purchase the said stock of Gas Oil, was approved by the Cabinet.
