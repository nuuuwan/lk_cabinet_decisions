Procurement for the Supply of Face mask Surgical Tie-on Type

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-14

- International Competitive bids have been invited for procurement of 89 million of Face mask Surgical Tie-on Type. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant procurement to M/s Sisili Projects Consortium (Pvt.) Ltd., primarily the lowest substantially responsive bidder, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC) and procure 30 million of Face masks initially as recommended by the Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
