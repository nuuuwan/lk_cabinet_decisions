Submission of Regulations issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) 	Act, No.1 of 1969 for approval in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-11-21

- Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to present the Imports Control Regulations on Payment Terms No.19 of 2022 and Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No.20 of 2022, issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969, for approval in Parliament.
