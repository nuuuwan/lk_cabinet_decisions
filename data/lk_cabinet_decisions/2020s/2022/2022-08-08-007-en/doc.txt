Submission of the published Gazette Notifications to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-08-08

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit a Gazette Notification including Regulations published under the Value Added tax Act, No. 14 of 2002, two Gazette Notifications including Notifications published under the Excise Ordinance (Chapter 52) and a Gazette Notification including an Regulation published under the Finance Act, No. 25 of 2003, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
