Establishment of the Institute of Economics and Trade, Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-19

- It is highly important for the Members of Parliament and Senior Public Officials to have a training on Economics and Trade affairs enabling them to reach informed decisions formulating and implementing National Policies. In order to facilitate for the purpose, it has been found appropriate to establish an Institute by the name of "Sri Lanka Institute of Economics and Trade" by an Act of Parliament. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft a Bill to establish the Sri Lanka Institute of Economics and Trade, was approved by the Cabinet.
