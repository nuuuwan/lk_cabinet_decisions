Amendments to the Inland Revenue Act, No.24 of 2017

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-03-14

- The Ministry of Finance has identified a few provisions which require amendments to improve the clarity of the provisions in the Inland Revenue Act, No.24 of 2017 and remove ambiguities. Further, it has been decided to introduce several amendments to the Inland Revenue Act, based on the proposals submitted by various parties relating to improvement of tax compliance and easing of tax payments, while encouraging investments and exports by granting reliefs to the thriving sectors of the economy. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance to amend the Inland Revenue Act, No.24 of 2017 incorporating the amendments so identified and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a draft Bill for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
