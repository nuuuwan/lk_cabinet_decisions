Making the general public aware on the actual circumstances related to the ongoing political, economic and social crisis situation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-04-25

- The proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media to implement a programme by the Department of Government Information together with the participation of the relevant Ministries in order to make aware the general public on the actual situation of the political, economical and social crisis emerged in the country at present, was approved by the Cabinet.
