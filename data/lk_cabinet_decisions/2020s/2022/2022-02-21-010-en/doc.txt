Submission of the Regulations made by the Minister of Youth and Sports  in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-02-21

- Anti-Doping Regulations (Prohibited List) No. 07 of 2021 made by the Minister of Youth and Sports under Section 34 (1) of the Convention Against Doping in Sports Act No. 33 of 2013 have been published  in the Gazette Extraordinary Notification dated 2022‑01‑18. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Youth and Sports to submit the said Regulations in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
