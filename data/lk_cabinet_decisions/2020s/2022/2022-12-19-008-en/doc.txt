Submission of Regulations issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969 to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2022-12-19

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No. 21 of 2022 and Imports Control Regulations on Payment Terms No. 22 of 2022 issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
