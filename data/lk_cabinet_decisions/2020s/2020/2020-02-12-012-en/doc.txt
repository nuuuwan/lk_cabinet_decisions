Procurement for the selection of a Consultancy Service Firm for Monitoring and Evaluation of the Health System Enhancement Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-12

In accordance with the recommendation submitted by the Consultants' Procurement Committee appointed by the Cabinet regarding the procurement for the selection of a consultant for the Health System Enhancement Project, funded by the Asian Development Bank, the proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services, to award the said contract to the Sri Lanka Business Development Centre for a sum of Rs.94.23 million inclusive of all taxes, was approved by the Cabinet.
