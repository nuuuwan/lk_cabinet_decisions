Appointment of an Expert Committee for the formulation of a National Trade	Policy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-09-02

- It has been observed that the National Trade Policy approved by the Cabinet of Ministers on 2017‑08‑01, has not been formulated properly in consultation with all relevant parties. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade to appoint an Expert Committee representing all relevant parties to formulate a new National Trade Policy in line with the present, was approved by the Cabinet.
