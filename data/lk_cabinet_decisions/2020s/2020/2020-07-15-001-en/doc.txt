Procurement for the Supply of Balloon Dilatation Catheter

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-15

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services to award the procurement for the supply of 40 types of Balloon Dilatation Catheters, which are used for the treatment of heart patients, to M/s Ceegeez Associates, to a total cost of Rs.207.74 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
