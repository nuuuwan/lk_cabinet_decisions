Consultancy Services for Feasibility Studies and Detailed Engineering Designs for Flood Risk Mitigation in Mundeni Aru River Basin and Batticaloa under the Climate Resilience Improvement Project (CRIP)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Mahaweli, Agriculture, Irrigation and Rural Development to enter into an Agreement with M/s Egis Eau & SCP & Green Tech JV to carry out feasibility studies as the Phase I of the Contract on  Consultancy Services for Feasibility Studies and Detailed Engineering Designs for Flood Risk Mitigation in Mundeni Aru River Basin under the Climate Resilience Improvement Project (CRIP) and  after the completion of Phase I,    to enter into an Agreement with the same Institute for the Consultancy Contract of preparing detailed engineering designs under its Phase II, was approved by the Cabinet.
