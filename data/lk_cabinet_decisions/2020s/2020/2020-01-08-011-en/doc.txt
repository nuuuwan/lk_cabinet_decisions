Procurement for the supply of 200,000 tests of Nucleic Acid Testing Reagents (Ultrio Plus) for Procleix Tigris NAT System for the detection of HIV 1, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C infection in blood donors

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-08

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services to award the procurement for the supply of 200,000 tests of Nucleic Acid Testing Reagents (Ultrio Plus) for Procleix Tigris NAT System for the detection of HIV 1, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C infection in blood donors, to M/s  Apcot Marketing (Pvt.) Ltd. at a total cost of US$ 3.09 million (without VAT), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
