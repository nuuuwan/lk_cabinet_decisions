Term contract for the import of Murban Crude oil for the period of eight (08) months from 2020/10/01 to 2021/05/31

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-09-16

- The proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the term contract for the procurement of the 8,400,000 +/-5% barrels of Murban Crude Oil for a period of eight (08) months from 2020‑10‑01 to 2021‑05‑31 to M/s Emirates National Oil Company (ENOC) of Singapore, as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
