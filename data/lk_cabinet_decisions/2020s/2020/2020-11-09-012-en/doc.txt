Awarding of Contract for selection of an Engineering Consultancy Service Firm for the construction of the Medical Faculty of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-09

- The Government of Sri Lanka has signed an agreement with the Saudi Development Fund to obtain a loan of 187.5 million Saudi Riyals to establish the Medical Faculty of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, competitive bids have been invited for the selection of a consultancy firm for the establishment of the said Medical Faculty. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to award the the relevant consultancy service contract to M/s DAR Engineering of Saudi Arabia, to a sum of 5.37 million Saudi Riyals, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
