Procurement for the supply of 6,000 vials of Rituximab Injection 100mg/10ml vial and 6, 500 vials of Rituximab Injection 500mg/50ml vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-08

- The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services to award the procurement for the purchase of 6,000 vials of Rituximab Injection 100mg/10ml Vial and 6,500 vials of Rituximab Injection 500mg/50ml vial, the vaccines used to treat for certain types of cancers, to M/s Intas Pharmaceuticals Limited, at a total sum of US $ 739,720.00, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee.
