Awarding of contract for the construction of the proposed building complex (Stage 2) for the Faculty of Technology, Wayamba University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-08

- The proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation to award the contract for the construction of the proposed building complex (Stage2) for the Faculty of Technology, Wayamba University of Sri Lanka to M/s Sathuta Builders (Pvt.) Ltd. located in Kurunegala at a sum of Rs. 1560.36 million (without VAT), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
