Procurement of Goods and Services by Public Sector Institutions from the Sri Lanka State Trading (General) Corporation outside the procurement procedure

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-10

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2019 10 09 to procure goods, equipment or machinery for the public sector institutions, up to a ceiling of Rs.50 million, from the Sri Lanka State Trading (General) Corporation outside the procurement procedures. The Cabinet approval was granted to extend the same procedure for a period of another one year.
