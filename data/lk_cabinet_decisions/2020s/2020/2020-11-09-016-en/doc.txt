Construction of New Bridge Across the Rajagiriya Nawala Canal connecting Angampitiya Road at Ethulkotte and School Lane at Nawala, Rajagiriya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-09

- Limited competitive bids were called to award the contract for the construction of a new bridge across Rajagiriya – Nawala canal connecting Angampitiya Road at Ethulkotte and School Lane, Rajagiriya at Nawala. Accordingly, the proposal submitted by the Minister of Highways to award the relevant contract to the State Development and Construction Corporation for a sum of Rs.1,698.55 million (without VAT), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
