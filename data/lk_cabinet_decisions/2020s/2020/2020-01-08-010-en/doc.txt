Procurement for the supply of 500,000 Prefilled Syringes of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, 4,000 IU in 0.4ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-08

- The Procurement Appeal Board having considered the recommendations made by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee of the Ministry of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services, pertaining to the supply of 500,000 prefilled Syringes of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, 4,000 IU in 0.4ml used for the treatment of heart patients, has submitted its recommendations to the Cabinet in respect of the measures to be taken relating to the said procurement. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services, to award the relevant procurement to M/s ABC Pharma Services (Pvt.) Ltd. at a total cost of Sri Lankan Rs.396.74 million, taking into consideration the said recommendations of the  Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
