Procurement for the supply of 340,000 Vials of Rabies Vaccine (Human Use) (0.5ml / 1ml)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous MedicalServices, to award the procurement for the supply of 340,000 vials of Rabbies Vaccine (Human Use) (0.5 ml/1 ml) used for preventing Rabies to M/s New Arumed (Pvt.) Ltd., at a total cost of Rs.399.84 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee,	was approved by the Cabinet.
