Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka and South Africa on Cooperation in the Field of Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-12-21

- The Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and South Africa on Cooperation in the field of Tourism was expired in 2017 and at the Sixth Session of the Sri Lanka - South Africa Partnership Forum in 2018, it was proposed to enter into a new Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation in the field of Tourism between the two parties. Accordingly the proposal made by the Minister of Tourism to sign a new MoU between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
