Construction of a Hostel, Work Shop and a Canteen for the University of Vocational Technology (UNIVOTEC), Rathmalana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-08

- The Cabinet, at its meeting held on 2017.01.03 has granted approval for the construction of three separate buildings for a hostel, workshop and a canteen for the University of Vocational Technology, Rathmalana under an estimated cost of Rs.927.34 million. Nevertheless, taking into consideration the facts that the proposed 	land for the construction of the buildings is a marshy land very close to the Weressa river, the necessity of utilizing the land effectively for future needs and the need to pile separately for the three buildings, the proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation to build a 06 storied building instead of 03 buildings within the same estimated financial limits, was approved by the Cabinet.
