Vesting of the land and the buildings with the Postgraduate Institute of Medicine of the University of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-22

- The proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation to vest the land in extent of 0.2074 hectares, at Prof. Nandadasa Kodagoda Mawatha (Norris Canal Road) in Kurunduwatta Grama Niladhari Division of the Thimbirigasyaya Divisional Secretariat Division where the Postgraduate Institute of Medicine of the University of Colombo is located and the buildings belonging therein, with the said Institution by way of a Free Grant in terms of the provisions of the State Land Ordinance, was approved by the Cabinet.
