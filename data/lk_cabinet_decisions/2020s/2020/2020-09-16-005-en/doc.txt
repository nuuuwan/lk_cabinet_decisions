Construction of 300MW Second Combined Cycle  Liquefied Natural Gas	(LNG) Power Plant at Kerawalapitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-09-16

- It has been identified that in order to remedy to the power crisis that could arise 	in the country in the future, it would be appropriate to construct 300 MW LNG	Power Plant at Kerawalapitiya and the construction and implementation of the 	same as an independent Power Project through private investments on Built	Own Operate Transfer (BOOT) business model.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power to take necessary	action to identify a suitable investor following the international competitive	bidding process enabling to complete the project within a period of three	years, was approved by the	Cabinet.
