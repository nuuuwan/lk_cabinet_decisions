Final Report of the Committee Appointed to Review the Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) Compact Agreement

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-01

- The Committee headed by Prof. Lalithasiri Gunaruwan appointed to review and submit recommendations on proposed Agreement to be signed with the Millennium	Challenge Corporation has submitted its final report. Accordingly, Hon. Prime	Minister presented the same to the Cabinet and it was decided to seek the views of	all members of the Cabinet on the recommendations of the said Report.
