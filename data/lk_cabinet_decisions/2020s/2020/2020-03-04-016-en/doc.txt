Awarding Contract for the Construction of the Multi-Purpose Building of the Biksu University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-03-04

- The proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education,Technology and Innovations, to award the contract for construction of the Multi-Purpose Building of the Biksu University of Sri Lanka, to M/s Siridantha Construction Works & Darinton Construction (Pvt.) Limited, Joint Venture at Anuradhapura for a sum of Rs.384.41 million and relevant Value Added Taxes (VAT), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
