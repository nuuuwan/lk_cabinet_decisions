Kithul Crop' to be nominated as a major plantation crop in Sri Lanka and establishment of the Kithul Development Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-12-14

- Kithul related production industry which is endemic to Sri Lanka is recognized as a highly potential industry for earning foreign exchange and various actions have been taken to uplift the Kithul industry during the previous periods. It has been found that expansion of Kithul related industry under the programme to create a public centred economy across the promotion of local products implemented by the present government is appropriate. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Plantation to nominate Kithul crop as a major plantation crop and to establish the Kithul Development Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
