The Programme for the Budget Debate - 2021

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-16

- Programme for the Budget Debate for the Financial Year 2021 prepared by the Office of the Leader of the House of Parliament and the Committee Stage Programme prepared by the Office of the Leader of the Opposition of Parliament, were submitted by the Hon. Prime Minister for the attention of the Cabinet. Accordingly, the Cabinet noted that it has been scheduled to conduct the Budget Speech on 2020‑11‑17, the Second Reading of the Budget from 2020‑11‑18 to 21, and that 16 days have been allocated for the Committee Stage from 2020‑11‑23 to 2020‑12‑10 and that the Third Reading Vote will take place on 2020.12.10.
