Establishment of the proposed National e Content Development Centre in  Pitipana area in Homagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-02

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to construct a National Content Development Centre for developing subject related contents/software and computer applications at the Narangala Estate in the Kuliyapitiya West Divisional Secretariat Division of the Kurunegala District, to formalize the use of information technology in schools and for education.  Considering the fact that the proposed Centre is situated about 90 km away from Colombo and the practical difficulties to be encountered by establishing the Centre in such a distant place, the proposal made by the Minister of Education, to establish the said Centre at Pitipana in the Divisional Secretariat Division of Homagama, was approved by the Cabinet.
