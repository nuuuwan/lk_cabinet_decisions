Award of the contract for the supply and distribution of Arial Bundled Conductor Alu. (ABC) 230/400V 3x70+54.6+16 sqmm - 2000km

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-30

- Competitive bids have been called for the supply of Arial Bundled Conductors for distribution zone 2 of the Ceylon Electricity Board. Accordingly, the proposal made by Minister of power to award the contract for the supply and distribution of Arial Bundled Conductor Alu. (ABC) 230/400V 3x70+54.6+16 sqmm - 2000km of the Ceylon Electricity Board to M/s ACL Cables PLC as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
