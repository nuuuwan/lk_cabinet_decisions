The implementation of a Shramadana Programme under the theme, 'A country for you - A future for the country - A clean workplace and A country at work'

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-27

- The importance of motivating the staff by creating a productive workplace environment for an efficient people‑centred public service under the theme 'A country for you - A future for the country - A clean workplace and A country at work' as declared in the 'Vistas for Prosperity and Splendour', the national policy framework, has been recognized. Accordingly, the concurrence of the Cabinet was given to the proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy and Minister of Transport Services Management, to hold a one day Shramadana Programme on the 28th of February 2020 without involving any Government expenditure, with the participation of the welfare associations and the productive groups of the Sri Lanka Transport Board, Sri Lanka Railways, Ceylon Electricity Board, Ceylon Petroleum Corporation and as well as the other institutions coming under the purview of the Ministry of Power & Energy and the Ministry of Transport Services Management and to hold the same programme quarterly.
