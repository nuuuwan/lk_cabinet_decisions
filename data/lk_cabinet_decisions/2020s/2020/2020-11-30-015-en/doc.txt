Awarding the term contract for the import of Gasoline for a period of eight months from 2020-12-01 to 2021-07-31

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-30

- Bids have been called from the registered suppliers of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation to import 1,564,500 Bbls +10/-5% of Gasoline (92 Unl) & 535,500 Bbls +10/-5% of Gasoline (95 Unl) during the period of eight months from 2020‑12‑01 to 2021‑07‑31. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the relevant procurement to M/s BB Energy (Asia) pte. Ltd., Singapore, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
