Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the 	University of Moratuwa and the Aalto University, Finland for the 	incorporation of Education and Research

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-10-19

-The University of Moratuwa and the University of Aalto in Finland has agreed to enter into a MoU between the two parties. It is expected to achieve the objectives of initiating and facilitating of corporation between the institutional staff, the departments and the research institutions of both parties and conducting joint research seminars and other activities by the said MoU. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education for an MoU to be entered into between the University of Moratuwa and the University of Aalto in Finland, was approved by the Cabinet.
