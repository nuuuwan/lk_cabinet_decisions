Procurement for the supply of 20,000 Vials of Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-08

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services to award the procurement for the supply of 20,000 Vials of Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vial used for the prevention of death of persons suffering from heart attacks, to M/s Boehringer Ingelheim India (Pvt.) Ltd. for a total Cost and Freight price of US$ 8.75 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
