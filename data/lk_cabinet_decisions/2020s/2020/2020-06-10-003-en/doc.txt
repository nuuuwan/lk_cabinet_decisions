Rental housing project for middle income earners.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-10

- The Urban Development Authority has planned to implement a project to construct 'studio type' apartments of a maximum of 350 square feet, so as to accommodate middle income earners who come to Colombo from far away areas for their employment. Accordingly, the cabinet approval was granted to implement these housing scheme projects within a distance of 3-5km of the main towns with main highway and railway services.
