Awarding Contract for the Construction of the Technology Faculty Building of the Sabaragamuwa Universityof Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-09

- International competitive bidding procedure has been followed to select a suitable contractor for the construction of the Technology Faculty Building of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka under the Asian Development Bank funded Science and Technology Human Resources Development Project. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to award the above contract to M/s International Constructions Consortium (Pvt) Ltd, to a sum of US$ 3.01 million and Rs 1,507.24 million (without VAT), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
