Printing and supplying of Passports and Confidential Documents	required for the Government 	through the Government Printing	Department

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-09-16

- The Cabinet considered the proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media to	purchase machinery required for the printing of passports and	confidential 	documents required for the Government by the Government	Printing Department itself 	enabling to retain the foreign exchange outflow	within the country, since they are currently being printed by foreign printing	institutions and decided to assign the Department of National Planning to	conduct a detailed evaluation of the project.
