Awarding the Contract for Construction of Gandara Fishery Harbour

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-09

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2019‑07‑07 to implement the project for the construction of the Gandara Fishery Harbor in the Devinuwara Divisional Secretariat Division in the Matara District. Accordingly, competitive bids have been called for this purpose and the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Fisheries to award this contract to RR SENOK JV to a sum of Rs. 5,367.69 million (without VAT), as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee.
