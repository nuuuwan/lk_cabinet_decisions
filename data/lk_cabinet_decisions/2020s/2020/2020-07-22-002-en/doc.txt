Modernization and refurbishment of the Lumbini Theatre at Colombo 05

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-22

- The existing facilities of the Lumbini Theatre, built in 1952, are not adequate and the sound control system, seating capacity and sanitation facilities of the theater have not been updated in a timely manner. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Housing Facilities to implement the project designed for the modernization and refurbishment of the Lumbini Theatre through the Urban Development Authority and to select a suitable contractor on Design and Build basis by following the National Competitive Procurement procedure, was approved by the Cabinet.
