Ruwanpura Expressway

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-01

- The present Government's policy statement 'Vistas of Prosperity' has proposed the construction of 	Ruwanpura Expressway linking the Southern Expresswayfrom 	Kahathuduwa - Ingiriya- Ratnapura - Pelmadulla. Although the project is planned to be implemented with foreign funds, it has been found that the	implementation of the project under the 	local funds to be more beneficial to the country. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Roads and	Highways, to take appropriate measures for the construction of the Expressway utilizing the funds of local banks and with the services of local construction	companies, was approved by the Cabinet.
