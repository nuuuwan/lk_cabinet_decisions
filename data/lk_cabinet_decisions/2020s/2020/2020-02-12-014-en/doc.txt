The proposal for doubling of the track between Kurunegala and Maho under the Indian Line of Credit

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-12

As a result of the congestion between Kurunegala and the Maho section in the Northern railway line, a delay of over one hour of office trains in the mornings and evenings is a very common occurrence.  As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport Services Management, to construct an additional track  of 43KM in length with an upgrading signaling system in addition to the existing line between Kurunegala and Maho utilizing the balance money remaining from the Indian Credit Line with a view to  reducing the travel time of the North bound passenger trains and accrue the full benefit of the re-construction of the Northern railway lines and to obtain the services of the Central Engineering Construction Bureau for the related consultancy services, was approved by the Cabinet.
