Availing provisions for the settlement of unsettled bills of the Year 2019

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- Additional budgetary provisions of Rs.367 billion are required to settle bills of completed projects implemented under various Ministries under the previous Government and unsettled so far and to account the expenditure of development projects implemented utilizing funds obtained through foreign sources that are not included within the limits of annual borrowings. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance, Economic and Policy Development to take action to submit a resolution in Parliament in order to avail of the said budgetary provisions, was approved by the Cabinet.
