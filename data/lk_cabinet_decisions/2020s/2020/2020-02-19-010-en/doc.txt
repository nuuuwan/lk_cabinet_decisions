Contract for the Construction of 220kV Switching Station at Kerawalapitiya under Package 01 of the Power System Reliability Strengthening Project implemented under the Funds of Asian Development Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-19

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy to award the contract for the  Construction of 220kV Switching Station at Kerawalapitiya under Package 01 of the Power System Reliability Strengthening Project implemented under the funds of Asian Development Bank to M/s KEC International Limited for a sum of Rs.2,774.89 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
