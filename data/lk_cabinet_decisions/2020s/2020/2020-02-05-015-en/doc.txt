Consultancy Services for the Project Implementation of the Railway Efficiency Improvement Project (REIP)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- Part of a loan received from the Asian Development Bank for the Railway Efficiency Improvement Project (REIF) is to be used for the supply of the consultancy service of the project. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport Services Management, to award the relevant consultancy services contract with the concurrence of the Asian Development Bank, to the joint venture created by Egis Rail and Egis International and Greentech Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd., as per the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Consultancy Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
