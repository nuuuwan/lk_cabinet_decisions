Continuation of the Suraksha' Student Insurance Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-27

- The proposal made by the Minister of Education to implement the Suraksha' Student Insurance Scheme existing at present covering all school childrens from the age group of 5 years to 19 years by formalizing it further, with a view to creating a healthy child population ensuring the continuous schooling of the children, was approved by the Cabinet.
