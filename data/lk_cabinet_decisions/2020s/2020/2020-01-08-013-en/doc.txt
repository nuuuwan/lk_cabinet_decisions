Procurement  for the supply of 32,000,000 Co amoxiclav Tablets BP 625mg or Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium Tablets USP 625mg

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-08

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services, to award the procurement for the supply of 32,000,000 Co amoxiclav Tablets BP 625mg or Amoxicillin and Clavulanate Potassium Tablets USP 625mg used widely as an antibiotic, to M/s  Indchemie Health Specialties (Pvt.) Ltd. at a total Cost and Freight price of US$ 1.56 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
