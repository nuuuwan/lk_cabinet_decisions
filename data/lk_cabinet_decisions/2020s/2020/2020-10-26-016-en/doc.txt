Procurement for the supply of 2,000,000 Vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection BP, 30/70, 1000 IU/10 ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-10-26

- International competitive bids were invited for the procurement of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection which is used to treat for the diabetic patients. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the procurement for the supply of 2,000,000 vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection BP, 30/70, 1000 IU/10 ml to M/s Wokhardt Limited at a total C&F price of US$ 3 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
