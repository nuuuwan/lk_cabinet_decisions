Awarding the contract for the construction of buildings and infrastructure facilities of the Faculty of Computing and Technology of the University of Kelaniya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-24

- The proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation to award the contract for the construction of the buildings and infrastructure facilities of the Faculty of Computing and Technology of the University of Kelaniya under the 'Science and Technology Human Resources Development Project' implemented through funds of the Asian Development Bank (ADB), to a sum of Rs 3,032.70 million (without VAT), to M/s Maga - ICC Joint Venture, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
