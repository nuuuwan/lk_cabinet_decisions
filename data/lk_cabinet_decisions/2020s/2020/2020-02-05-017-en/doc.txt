Entry into a term contract for the import of 520,000 Bbls of Gas Oil (0.05% Maximum Sulphur) and 680,000 Bbls of Gasoline (92 Unl) during the period of eight months from 2020-03-15 to 2020-11-14

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy,  to award the term procurement for the import of estimated 520,000 Bbls of Gas Oil (0.05% Maximum Sulphur) and 680,000 Bbls of Gasoline (92 Unl) during the period of eight months from 2020-03-15 to 2020-11-14 to M/s Petrochina International (Singapore) Pte Ltd., as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
