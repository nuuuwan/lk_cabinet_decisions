Awarding of the contract for the construction of the student hostel of the Sri Lanka German Technical Training Institute (SLGTI) at Killinochchi

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Skills Development, Employment and Labour Relations, to award the Contract for the construction of the student hostel of the Sri Lanka German Technical Training Institute (SLGTI) at Kilinochchi to M/s Wahid Construction (Pvt) Ltd at 450/4, Lakvil, Puttlam Road, Yanthampalawa, Kurunagala for a sum of Rs. 185.61 million (subject to the payment of the Value added tax, as per the government tax regulations and approved percentages) as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee,  was approved by the Cabinet.
