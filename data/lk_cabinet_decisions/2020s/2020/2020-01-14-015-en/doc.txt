Measures taken to ensure the continuous supply of petroleum in the country

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-14

- The Minister of Power & Energy made the Cabinet aware on the action so far taken by his Ministry together with Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) and Ceylon Petroleum Storage Terminals Limited (CPSTL) in order to maintain an adequate storage of fuel in the country to meet possible shortage of fuel in future  due to the international political conflicts prevailing at present.
