Procurement for the supply of 6,875 Vials of Trastuzumab Injection 	440mg vials 	with Solvent in 20ml Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-10-12

-International competitive bids have been called for the procurement of the drug called Trastuzumab used to treat certain types of Cancers. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the procurement for the supply of 6,875 Trastuzumab Injection 440mg vials with solvent in 20ml vial to M/s Transfarma Dis Ticaret Anonim Sirketi, Turkey at a total C&F price of EUR 1.62 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
