Procurement of Food Items for the Tri-Forces for the year 2021

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-12-21

- National competitive bids have been called for the procurement of food items for the Tri-Forces for the year 2021. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to award the contracts for the supply of food items required by the Tri - Forces for the year 2021, to the responsive bidders, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
