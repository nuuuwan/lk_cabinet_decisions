Allocation of a land at IT park, Malabe to the Mahamewnawa Buddhist College

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-03-18

- As per the request made by the Chief Incumbent of the Seruwila, Wilgamwehera Somawathie Rajamaha Viharaya requesting a plot of land of 04 acres located at Malabe to be granted for the establishment of the Mahamewnawa Buddhist College, on concessionary payment terms, the Urban Development Authority has identified a plot of land in extent of 02 acres, 2 roods and 0.70 perches located within the bounds of the IT park Malabe to be allocated for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Urban Development, Water Supply and Housing Facilities to allocate the said plot of land to the Mahamewnawa Buddhist College, on lease basis for a period of 30 years, was approved by the Cabinet.
