Submission of the published Gazette Extraordinary Notifications to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-30

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance to submit six Gazette Extraordinary Notifications published under the Special Commodity Levy Act, No.48 of 2007, one Gazette Extraordinary Notification published under the Revenue Protection Act, No.19 of 1962 and one Gazette Extraordinary Notification issued in respect of imposing Surcharge on customs import duty for imported fuel in terms of the provisions of Customs Ordinance, to Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
