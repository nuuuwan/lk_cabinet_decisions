Establishment of the National Science, Technology and Innovation Coordinating Authority (NASTICA)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-22

- The Cabinet has granted approval previously for the transition of the Coordinating Secretariat for Science, Technology and Innovation (COSTI)  into the National Science, Technology and Innovation Authority (NASTICA) through an Act, of Parliament. The Legal Draftsman's Department is presently in the process of finalizing the relevant draft Bill.
