Procurement for the supply of 950,000 Prefilled Syringe of Epoetin Injection 4,000 IU - 5,000IU

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical	Services, to award the procurement for the supply of 950,000 Prefilled	Syringe of Epoetin Injection 4000 IU - 5000 IU used for the treatment of	kidney patients, to M/s Reliance Life Science, India at a total cost and	freight price of US$ 1.39 	million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet	Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
