Awarding the contract of the construction of the Para - Clinical Building for inaugurating the Faculty of Medicine of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-09-16

- National competitive bids have been invited to construct the Para - Clinical Building of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka on design and build basis, for the purpose of inaugurating the Medical Faculty of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to award the contract for the construction of the  Para - clinical building for the Faculty of Medicine of the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka to M/s Maga Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd., at a cost of Rs.1,013.87 million including the relevant Value Added Tax (VAT) as recommended by  the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
