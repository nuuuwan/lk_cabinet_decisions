Procurement for the supply of 11,250 Vials of Bevacizumab Injection 	100mg/4ml 	Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-10-12

-International competitive bids have been called for the procurement of the drug called Bevacizumab used to treat certain types of Cancers. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the procurement for the supply of 11,250 Vials of Bevacizumab Injection 100mg/4ml Vial to M/s Medmart Pharma (Pvt) Ltd, at a total cost of Rs.196.88 million as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
