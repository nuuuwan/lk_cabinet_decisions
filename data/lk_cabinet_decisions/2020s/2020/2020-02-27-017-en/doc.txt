Awarding of the contract for Augmentation of Girandurukotte Water Treatment Plant, Mechanical and Electrical Works under the Local Government Enhancement Sector Project funded by the Asian Development Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-27

- Under the Local Government Enhancement Sector Project implemented with  the Asian Development Bank Financial Assistance, a sum of US$ 46.78 million has been set apart for the improvement of Water Supply Schemes in the areas of kidney affected people living in the Uva, North Central, Central and North Western Provinces. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils & Local Government, to award the contract for the augmentation of the Giradurukotte Water Treatment Plant Mechanical and Electrical Works to M/s Vasiri-Savinda Joint Venture at Kahatuduwa in Polgasowita for a sum of Rs.330.10 million as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
