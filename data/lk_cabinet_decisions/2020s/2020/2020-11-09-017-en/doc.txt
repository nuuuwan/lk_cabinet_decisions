The contract for supplying and laying of water distribution systems in the Jaffna city area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-09

- The Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Water Supply, to award the contract for the supplying and laying of water distribution system in the Jaffna city area, which is one of the components of Jaffna – Kilinochchi Water Supply and Sanitation Project, to M/s NCC Company to a sum of Rs.3,160.20 Million (with VAT) as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee.
