Approval for the Contract of Supply and Installation of 23 Priority Bridges - Proposal submitted by M/s Ellipse Projects SAS of France

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-19

- With a view to minimizing traffic congestion, accidents and high vehicular operating costs, it has been identified as appropriate to supply and fix 23  priority bridges in selected areas.  A proposal has been submitted by M/s Ellipse Projects SAS of France for same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Roads and Highways to implement the relevant project based on the said proposal and to appoint a Cabinet Appointed Negotiation Committee to evaluate the proposal, was approved by the Cabinet.
