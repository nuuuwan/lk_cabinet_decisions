The Contract for the Supply and Delivery of 1,500 km Arial Bundle Conductor Alu

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-15

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy, to award the contract for the Supply and Delivery of 1,500km Arial Bundle Conductor for 02 distribution divisions of the Ceylon Electricity Board, to M/s Sierra Cables PLC, Sri Lanka, to a sum of Rs.900.50 million (excluding VAT), as recommended by the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
