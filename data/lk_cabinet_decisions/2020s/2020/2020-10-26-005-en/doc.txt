Re-allocating the Regidale Watte land at Kahapola, Piliyandala to the National Zoological Department

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-10-26

- The Cabinet has granted approval in the year 2016 to acquire the Regidale Watte land at Kahapola, Piliyandala which was under the National Zoological Department to the then Ministry in charge of the subject of Buddhashasana and vest the same with the institution called “Light of Asia Foundation” under a Memorandum of Understanding for its project for the Erection of the Theme Park of Sakya Kingdom of Kapilawasthu in India. The said project had been scheduled to commence in the year 2017 and complete in March 2020, however its progress is still less than 5%. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Buddhashasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to cancel the Agreement entered into with the “Light of Asia Foundation” and acquire the said land back to the Ministry of Buddhashasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to re-transfer the said land to the National Zoological Department, retaining 5 acres of the land under the Ministry for its future development activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
