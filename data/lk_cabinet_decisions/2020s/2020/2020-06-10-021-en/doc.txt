Release of Pine Plantations belonging to the Department of Forest Conservation and the Land Reforms Commission for Oleoresin extraction on the basis of a charge

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-10

- Cabinet approval was granted to take necessary action for the selection of suitable institutions following the competitive bidding procedure, for Oleoresin extraction in the Pine Plantations belonging to the Department of Forest Conservation and the Land Reforms Commission in extent of 6,200 hectares.
