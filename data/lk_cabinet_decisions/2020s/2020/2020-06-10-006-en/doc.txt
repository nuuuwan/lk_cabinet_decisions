Contract for the construction of Mawella and Rekawa Anchorages in the Tangalle Divisional Secretariat, Hambanthota District

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-10

- Competitive procurement procedure has been followed for the construction of the Anchorages of Mawella and Rekawa in the Tangalle Divisional Secretariat Division in Hambantota district. Accordingly, the approval of the Cabinet has been granted to award the contracts concerned to Aarking Engineering (Pvt) Ltd joint venture with BPPE Institute and RR Construction Pvt. Ltd respectively, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee.
