Awarding of the Contract for the Supply, Installation and Supervision of two 132/33kV, 45MVA Power Transformers for the Wagawatta Grid Substation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-12-07

- Competitive Bids have been called to award the contract for the Supply, Installation and Supervision of two 132/33kV, 45MVA Power Transformers for the Wagawatta Grid Substation. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power to award the said contract to  M/s PT.CG. Power Systems, Indonesia to a sum of Rs.245.62 million (Excluding VAT), as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
