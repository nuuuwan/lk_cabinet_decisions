Construction of Ragama-Peralanda Wetland Recreational Park

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-09

- The Urban Development Authority has planned to implement a Recreational Park project in the vicinity of Peralanda Wetlands located within the Ragama-Peralanda residential area. Accordingly the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to award the relevant contract as a direct contract to the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation and to provide the necessary consultancy services by the Urban Development Authority, was approved by the Cabinet.
