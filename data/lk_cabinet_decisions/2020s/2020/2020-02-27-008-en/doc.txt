INNOTECH 2020 - Technology and Innovation Exhibition

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-27

- The Ministry of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation  has planned an exhibition named 'INNOTECH 2020' to be held from 11th to 15th March 2020, with the objective of promoting science literacy at all levels of the society as well as to showcase the research and development outcomes of the institutes and universities operating under the purview of  the said Ministry.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovation, to hold the said exhibition at Pitipana area in Homagama in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders, was approved by the Cabinet.
