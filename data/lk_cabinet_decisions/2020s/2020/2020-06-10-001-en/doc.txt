Compensation for Damaged Vehicles in the Meethotamulla garbage dump Landslide in Kolonnawa Divisional Secretariat Division, Colombo District on 2017 04 14

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-10

- A committee headed by the Additional District Secretary Colombo, has been appointed to assess the damages of 36 vehicles caused by the Meethotamulla garbage dump landslide on 2017 04 14. The compensation was approved by the Cabinet of Ministers for qualified vehicle owners according to the recommendations of the Committee.
