Providing relief measures to house holders affected by the water leaks of the Main Tunnel of the Uma Oya Multipurpose Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-22

- The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Mahaweli, Agriculture, Irrigation and Rural Development relating to the payment of the housing rent recommended by the Provincial Assessor further from 2019‑05‑31 up to 2020‑09‑30 for 46 families still living in rented houses out of the house owners affected by the water leakage in the main tunnel of the Uma Oya Multipurpose Development Project and to construct 118 new houses at another suitable location as a special project with the intervention of the Ministry in charge of the subject of Disaster Management for the families recommended to be evacuated due to the risk of landslides.
