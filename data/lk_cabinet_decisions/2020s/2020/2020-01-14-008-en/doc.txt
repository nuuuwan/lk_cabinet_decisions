Procurement for the supply of Laboratory Consumables Items

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-14

- The proposal made by the Minister of Healthcare and Indigenous Medical Services to award the procurement for the supply of 17 Laboratory Consumable Items used for ensuring the safety in Organ Transplantation, to M/s Emar Pharma (Pvt.) Ltd. at a total cost of Rs.188.82 million (without VAT) as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
