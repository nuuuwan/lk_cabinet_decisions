Entry into a term contract for the import of 1,137,500 barrels of Diesel (0.05% Max Sulphur) and 262,500 barrels of Diesel (0.001% Max Sulphur) within a period of eight months  from 2020.04.01 to 2020.11.30

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-27

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy to award the contract for the import of 1,137,500 barrels of Diesel (0.05% Max Sulphur) and 262,500 barrels of Diesel (0.001% Max Sulphur) within a period of eight months  from 2020‑04‑01 to 2020‑11‑30, to M/s Petrochina International Pte. Ltd., Singapore as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SSCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
