Tax exemptions for execution of the Railway Track Rehabilitation Project from Maho to Omanthai under the Indian Line of Credit

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-06-10

- Cabinet approval was granted to implement the Railway Track Rehabilitation Project from Maho to Omanthai under the Indian Line of Credit, at the meeting held on 2019 04 02. Now, it has been decided by the Cabinet to declare this Project as a special project to allow exemptions from the payment of the relevant taxes.
