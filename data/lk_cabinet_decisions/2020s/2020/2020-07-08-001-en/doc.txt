Providing training opportunities for Foreign Officers in the Defence Services Command and Staff College

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-08

- Since the year 2009, opportunities are also provided for the Foreign Military Officers to follow the courses on gratis basis and payment basis, in the Defence Services Command and Staff College, which provides training courses to the Middle Grade Officers in the Tri-Forces and Sri Lanka Police as well as Executive Officers in Government Institutions. Accordingly, 12 training opportunities on payment basis and 30 training opportunities on gratis basis have already been given for the officers representing the Asian, Middle East, European and African countries. As such, the proposal made by H.E the President to grant one training opportunity on gratis basis and one training opportunity on payment basis to Singapore as well from 2021 onward, was approved by the Cabinet.
