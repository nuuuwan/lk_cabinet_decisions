Purchasing of the Proposed Land for constructing the Medical Faculty of the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka and Construction of the Building Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-03-18

- An amount of Rs 70 million has been allocated with the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers, to acquire a plot of land for the construction of the Medical Faculty of the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka. However, an amount of Rs 92 million has been estimated for the said acquisition process according to the present value of the land. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Higher Education, Technology and Innovations to increase the amount allocated for said land acquisition process up to 92 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
