Securing necessary financial support for the working capital requirement of the Laksala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-12-07

- Revenue earned by Laksala  has dropped by 90% due to the absence of foreign tourists due to the Covid-19 pandemic, making it difficult to run the institution. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Industries to secure the necessary provisions from the General Treasury for salaries and working capital requirements including other essential expenses required to function the Laksala, was approved by the Cabinet.
