Purchase of additional 50% of cargo quantity of the on-going Gasoline   (92 Unl) term contract

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-07-15

- The term contract for the purchase of 1,800,000 barrels of Gasoline (92 Unl) for a period of 09 months from 2020 01 15 to 2020 10 14 has been awarded to the M/s Vitol Asia Pte. Ltd., Singapore. The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy, to purchase a stock equivalent to 50% of the ongoing contract under the same terms and conditions, as a repeat order in terms of the provisions of 3.6 of the procurement guidelines, as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee.
