Entry into term contract for the import of 2,240,000 Bbls of Gas Oil (0.05% Maximum Sulphur) during the period of eight months from 2020-03-01 to 2020-10-31

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-02-05

- The proposal made by the Minister of Power & Energy,  to award the term procurement for the import of 2,240,000 Bbls of Gas Oil (0.05% Maximum Sulphur) during the period of eight months from 2020-03-01 to 2020-10-31 to the M/s Swiss Singapore Overseas Enterprises Pte. Ltd., as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
