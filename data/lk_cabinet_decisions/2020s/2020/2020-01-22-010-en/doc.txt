Supply, Installation and Supervision of 145 kV Double Busbar Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS) and the modification of 145 kV Double Busbar (GIS) with Control, Protection, Monitoring and Metering under the extension of 132 kV Kelanitissa Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-22

- Extension of Kelanitissa 132 kv Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS) has been initiated to connect the proposed Gas Turbines to the 132 kv Busbar of Kelanitissa Grid Substation. The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the contract for the Supply, Delivery and Installation Supervision of Two Nos. of  145 kV Double Busbar Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS) and the modification of 145 kV Double Busbar (GIS) with Control, Protection, Monitoring and Metering to the original manufacturer, Asea Brown Bovari Lanka (Pvt) Ltd., was approved by the Cabinet.
