Supply of security services to hospitals and other institutions under the Ministry of Health for the year 2020/2021

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-11-02

- The contract has to be awarded for the supply of security services to hospitals and other institutions under the Ministry of Health for the year 2020/2021. Accordingly the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the supply of security services for 35 hospitals and institutions in the Western Province to Rakna Arakshaka Lanka Limited and to award the supply of security services for 26 hospitals and institutions located outside the Western Province to LRDC Services (Pvt) Ltd, was approved by the Cabinet.
