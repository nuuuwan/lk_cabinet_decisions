Memorandum of Understanding between the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University and the Indonesia Defense University

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2020-01-08

- The proposal made by the Minister of Mahaweli, Agriculture, Irrigation and Rural Development; Internal Trade, Food Security and Consumer Welfare; and State Minister of Defence, to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University and the Indonesia Defense University to work in collaboration, in matters such as, the promotion of academic cooperation, providing of mutual access to libraries, exchange of academic staff, teachers, researchers and visiting lecturers between the Institutes, providing of mutual educational trips, strategic research offers or offering of scholarships, providing of facilities for the training of academic teachers and researchers, facilities for the exchange of published academic books and records and organizing seminars, workshops, symposiums and exhibitions of mutual interest and encouraging the mutual participation at such events etc., was approved by the Cabinet.
