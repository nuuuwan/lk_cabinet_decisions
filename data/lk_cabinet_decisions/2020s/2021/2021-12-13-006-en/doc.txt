Allocation of blocks of lands from the Regional Industrial Estates to set up Industries

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-12-13

- “Regional Industrial Estates Development Programme” is a major programme carried out with the objective of promoting industries at regional level. The “Regional Industrial Services Committee” established under the Industrial Promotion Act, No.46 of 1990 and the “Project Evaluation Committee of the Ministry” identify the suitable investors for the said Programme, subsequent to a full assessment of the project proposals. Accordingly, the above Committees have studied 27 project proposals and recommended the allocation of blocks of lands from 15 Industrial Estates to implement the said Projects. It is expected by the selected investors to invest Rs.4,523.34 million and generate 2,477 direct employment opportunities. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Industries to allocate blocks of lands to the respective investors on long term lease basis for a period of 35 years, was approved by the Cabinet.
