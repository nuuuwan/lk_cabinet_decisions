Submission of the Final Report of the Presidential Commission of Inquiry to Investigate and Inquire into the Allegations and Frauds considered to have been committed in Public Institutions during the period from 2015-01-14 to 2018-12-31, in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-01

- The final report of the above Commission appointed by the warrant of the President on 2019‑01‑14 under the chairmanship of Upaly Abeyrathne Esquire, Retired Judge of the Supreme Court has been handed over to the then President. The proposal made by H.E the president to submit the said report in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
