Procurement for Supply of 840,000 Prefilled Syringe of Enoxaparian Sodium Injection, 4000IU, 0.4ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-05-03

- International Competitive Bids  have been called for the procurement of this drug which is used for the treatment of patients with heart diseases.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the above procurement to  M/s Slim Pharmaceuticals (Pvt.) Ltd of Sri Lanka at a sum of Rs.541.80 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
