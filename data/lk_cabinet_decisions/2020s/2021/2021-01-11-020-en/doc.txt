Procurement for the supply of 1,000,000 Pre-filled Syringe of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, 6000IU in 0.6ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- International Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement of Enoxaparin Sodium that has been used for treatment of patients with heart diseases. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, to award the procurement for the supply of 1,000,000 Pre‑filled Syringe of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, 6,000IU in 0.6ml, to Ms. Slim Pharmaceuticals (Pvt) Ltd., at a total delivered price to Medical Supply Division (MSD) of Sri Lanka Rupees 695 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
