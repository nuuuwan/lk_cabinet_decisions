Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-06-07

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2020‑10‑26 to draft the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka Bill. As such, the Bill drafted by the Legal Draftsman has also received the clearance of the Attorney General. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
