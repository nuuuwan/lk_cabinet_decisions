Report of the Auditor General on the President's Fund - 2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-25

- In terms of Section 9(2) of the President's Fund Act, No.7 of 1978 to be read with the provisions of Article 154(3) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the Auditor General has audited the President's Fund pertaining to the financial year ended on 31st December, 2018. As such, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, to table the said Report of the Auditor General pertaining to the financial year 2018 in accordance with the provisions of Section 10 of the said Fund Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
