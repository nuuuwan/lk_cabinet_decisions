Construction of a bridge across Benthara river along the Ittepana Horawala Thotupola road in Ittepana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- The Road Development Authority (RDA) has planned to construct a new bridge and its approaches across Benthara River along the Ittepana‑Horawala‑Thotupola road in Ittepana. The proposed bridge is about 160m in length and the total carriageway width is 10.4 m. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Highways, to construct the said bridge under the “100,000 km of Roads Improvement Project”, was approved by the Cabinet.
