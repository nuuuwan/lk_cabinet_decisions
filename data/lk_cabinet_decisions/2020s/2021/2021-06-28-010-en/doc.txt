Amendment of the National Aquaculture Development Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No.53 of 1998

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-06-28

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2017 08 15 to amend the National Aquaculture Development Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No.53 of 1998 to grant wide powers of regulation, promotion and management of the aquaculture, to the Director of the National Aquaculture Development Authority of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the Minister in charge of the subject has appointed a Committee to study the matters pertaining to the amendments to be made and submit recommendations. The said Committee has submitted proposals relating to amending the said Act and pursuant to the said proposals, it has been recommended to amend the National Aquaculture Development Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No.53 of 1998 and also concurrently, the Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Act, No.2 of 1996 amended by the Act, No.22 of 2006. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Fisheries to amend the National Aquaculture Development Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No.53 of 1998 and the Fisheries & Aquatic Resources Act, No.2 of 1996 concurrently, based on the draft prepared by the Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
