Intent of Cooperation between COMSATS University Islamabad, Pakistan and Industrial Technology Institute

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-02-22

- An Intent of Cooperation on Education and Research has been proposed to be signed between the COMSATS University Islamabad of Pakistan and the Industrial Technology Institute of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology to sign the said  Intent of Cooperation, was approved by the Cabinet.
