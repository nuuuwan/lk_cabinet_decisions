Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Sultanate of Oman on mutual visa exemption for holders of Diplomatic, Special, Service and Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- The proposal made by the Minister of Irrigation and State Minister of National Security, Home Affairs and Disaster Management to enter into an MoU between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Sultanate of Oman on mutual visa exemption for holders of Diplomatic, Special, Service and Official Passports with a view to facilitating citizens of these two countries to travel between two countries and further strengthening their mutual corporation, was approved by the Cabinet.
