Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Corporation between the Board of Investment of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka  and the Board of Investment of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-02-22

- A Memorandum of Understanding has been proposed to be entered into between the Board of Investment of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka  and the Board of Investment of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan to promote investment corporation between two countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance to enter into the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
