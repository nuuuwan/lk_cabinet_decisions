Publishing 'National Gem and Jewellery (Gem Mining License) 	Regulations 	2020'

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-04

- In terms of the provisions of State Gem Corporation by-law imposed 	under the provisions of the State Gem Corporation Act No. 3 of 1971, a 	license for gem industry could be obtained for the lands with clear and 	individual ownership and / or freehold ownership only. Accordingly, a person 	who acquires a land on a lease basis for gem mining cannot apply for a gem 	mining license . However, it has been observed that approximately 20% of 	the applications submitted are for the lands owned on shared rights and/or 	leasehold rights. As such, the proposal for the submission of regulations 	published in the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2165/1 dated 	2020‑03‑02 by the Minister of Industries as per the provisions of the National 	Gem and Jewellery Authority Act No.50 of 1993 in Parliament for approval so 	as to issue license for such applicants as well, was approved by the 	Cabinet.
