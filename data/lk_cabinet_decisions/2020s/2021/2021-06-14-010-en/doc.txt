Awarding the term contract for the import of Murban Crude Oil

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-06-14

- Bids have been invited from the registered suppliers of Ceylon Petroleum Corporation for the term contract for importing Murban Crude Oil for a period of 09 months from 2021‑06‑01 to 2022‑01‑31. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the relevant contract to M/s Conscio Ltd, Nigeria as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
