Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the National Korea Maritime & Ocean University and the Ocean University of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-07-05

- The National Korea Maritime & Ocean University and the Ocean University of Sri Lanka have agreed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between each other encouraging the collaborative activities such as the exchange of faculty, researches and administrative staff, exchange of students, academic co‑operation on collaborative research, lectures and conferences and exchange of academic information and materials. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the relevant Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), was approved by the Cabinet.
