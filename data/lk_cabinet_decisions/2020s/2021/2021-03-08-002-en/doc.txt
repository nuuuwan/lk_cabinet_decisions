Document of Understanding between the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University and COMSATS University, Pakistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-08

- The Interdisciplinary Research Center in Biomedical Materials (IRCBM) of COMSATS University, Pakistan is an institute that sustains a number of international Patent Licenses pertaining to the field of Biomaterials Tissue Engineering. It has been proposed to sign a Document of Understanding to exchange knowledge on the fields such as Biomaterials, Tissue Engineering, Regenerative Medicine, Sensors and Biosensors and to conduct cooperative researches on the sectors that are mutually important to both countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to sign the proposed Document of Understanding between the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University and the COMSATS University, Pakistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
