Awarding of six (06) Civil Works Contract Packages for rehabilitation, improvement and maintenance of the Kekirawa - Thalawa - Ganewalpola - Dachchihalmillewa Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-01

- Competitive bids have been invited for six (06) Civil Works Contract Packages for rehabilitation, improvement and maintenance of the Kekirawa - Thalawa - Ganewalpola - Dachchihalmillewa Road in the North Central Province, under the Asian Development Bank funded Integrated Road Investment Programme. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Highways to award the relevant contracts as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee (SCAPC), was approved by the Cabinet.
