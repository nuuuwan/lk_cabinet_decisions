Establishment of the Media Centre for National Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-04

- It has been found that the Government Policy contained in the the Government Policy Frame Work“Vistas of Prosperity and Splendor”, the strategies to be achieved therein and and their actual progress are not properly communicated to the public. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Mass Media to establish a unit called " Media Centre for National Development" under the Ministry of Mass Media, enabling to disseminate accurate information on Government policies and development efforts to the public, was approved by the Cabinet.
