Obtaining assistance of Television Services for continuous implementation of the Learning - Teaching process in the Education System during the period of Covid 19- pandemic

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-15

- The 'Guru Gedara' programme, prepared by the National Institute of Education was telecast combining the 'Channel Eye' and 'Nethra TV' of Sri Lanka Rupawahini Corporation to continue the academic activities of the students, sitting for Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, G.C.E (O/L) Examination and G.C.E (A/L) Examination, during the period when schools were closed due to the Covid-19 pandemic from March, 2020. In order to prevent the disruption of students' education due to the delay caused in the commencement of the third school term in 2020, telecasting Sinhala and Tamil medium educational programmes from Grade 3 to Grade 13 under the phase 2 of 'Guru Gedara' programme has been started on 16 November, 2020. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to continue the 'Guru Gedara' educational programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
