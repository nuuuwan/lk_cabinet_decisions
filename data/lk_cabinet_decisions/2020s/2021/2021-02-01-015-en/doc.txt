Amendments to the Civil Procedure Code

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-02-01

- The Civil Procedure Code makes provisions to set aside the judgment given by a court of law upon a request made by a defendant to the court of law with notice to the plaintiff stating that he has reasonable grounds for such default, within 14 days of the decree entered against him for default. It has been observed that by taking advantage of these provisions, it has enabled the defendant to employ delaying tactics and to resist a just claim of the plaintiff. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to amend the section 88(2) of the Civil Procedure Code, enabling to prevent such adverse conditions, was approved by the Cabinet.
