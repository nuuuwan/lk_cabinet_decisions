Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Sri Jayawardenepura and Chengdu Agricultural College, China

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-07-12

- The proposal made by the Minister of Education to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding on developing collaborations between the University of Sri Jayawardenepura and Chengdu Agricultural College, China with the objectives such as exchanging data and information on the educational and scientific researches between the two parties, exchanging staff and students, developing and disseminating integrated curricula, conducting combined researches and production of tea and herbal crops, was approved by the Cabinet.
