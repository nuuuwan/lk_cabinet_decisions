Preparation of the National Drinking Community Water Development Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-29

Subsequent to the establishment of the Department of National Community Water Supply, the said Department regulates and empowers the Community Based Organizations managed by the Community. Yet, the said Department has faced with a number of issues for not possessing the legal power required for the purpose. Taking into consideration these matters, the proposal made by the Minister of Water Supply to draft the National Drinking Community Water Development Act to enable formal and effective functioning of the Department of National Community Water Supply, was approved by the Cabinet.
