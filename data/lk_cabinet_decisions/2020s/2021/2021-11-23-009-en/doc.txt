Green Power Development & Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Project - Development of Ambalangoda and Pannala Grid Substations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-11-23

- Green Power Development & Energy Efficiency Improvement Investment Project is implemented under the funds of the Asian Development Bank and it has been planed to develop the Ambalngoda and Pannala Grid Substations and to provide 02 Spare Transformers under lot B of the Package 8 of the said Project. International Competitive Bids have been invited for this under the Single-Stage Two Envelope procedure and 04 Bids have been received. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Power to award the relevant procurement to the M/s Ceylex Engineering (Pvt) Ltd as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
