Procurement for the supply of 17,000 Vials of Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- International Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement of this injection that has been used for treatment of patients who have suffered from heart attacks. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, to award this procurement, to Ms. Boehringer Ingelheim India (Pvt.) Ltd. of India, at a total cost of USD 7.82 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
