Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka and the Galilee International Management Institute, Israel

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-07-19

- In view of exchanging the scientific and managerial knowledge and skills in the field of agriculture, it has been agreed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka and the Galilee International Management Institute, Israel. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the relevant MoU between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
