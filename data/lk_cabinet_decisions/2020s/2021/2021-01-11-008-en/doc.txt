Allocating a land on long term lease basis to the Pelwatte Diary Industries Ltd.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- The proposal made by the Minister of Lands to allocate a state owned land in extent of 22 hectares located at Buttala Divisional Secretariat Division to Pelwatte Diary Industries Ltd on long term lease basis for a period of 30 years, was approved by the Cabinet.
