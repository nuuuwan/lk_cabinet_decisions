Construction of OPD, Administration Block and Ward Complex at the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children, Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-05-31

- The proposal made by the Minister of Health to implement the project for construction of the OPD, Administration Block and Ward Complex at the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children, Colombo within the Medium Term Budgetary Framework 2022‑2024 and to obtain the labour contribution of the Sri Lanka Navy for the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
