Donation of August salary of the Cabinet Ministers to the COVID 19 Fund

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-08-23

- In response to a proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister, it was unanimously decided by the Cabinet to donate the August salary of the Ministers of the Cabinet to the COVID 19 Fund, with a view to strengthening the said Fund to assist in the controlling of the prevailing COVID 19 pandemic situation which has been spread over the country.
