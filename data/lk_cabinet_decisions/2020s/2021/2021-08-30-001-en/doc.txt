Entering into an Agreement between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Kingdom of Cambodia on the exemption of Visa requirement for holders of Diplomatic and Service & Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-08-30

- It has been agreed by the two parties to enter into an Agreement between Sri Lanka and the Kingdom of Cambodia on the exemption of visa requirements for holders of Diplomatic and Service & Official Passports. The clearance of the Attorney General and concurrence of the Foreign Ministry have been received for the draft Agreement. Accordingly, the proposal made by H. E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to sign the said Agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
