Procurement for the supply of 300,000 bottles of Human Albumin Solution BP/Ph Eur, 20% in 50ml bottles

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- International Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement of this drug that has been used for treatment of patients with kidney diseases. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health, to award the procurement for the supply of 300,000 bottles of Human Albumin Solution BP/Ph Eur, 20% in 50ml bottles, to Ms. Reliance Life Science Pvt. Ltd. India, at a total cost of USD 6.27 million, as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
