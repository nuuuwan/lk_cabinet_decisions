Amending the Land Development Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-12-13

- Since it was observed that it is not possible to make the optimum use of the blocks of lands made on the Grants issued as per the provisions of the Land Development Ordinance due to certain conditions incorporated in the said Grants, approval of the Cabinet of Ministers has been granted at its meeting held on 2021‑01‑04 to amend the Land Development Ordinance, enabling to amend said conditions to be favorable to the public. Accordingly, concurrence of the Attorney General has been received for the draft Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Lands to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
