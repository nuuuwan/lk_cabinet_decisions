Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the International Centre for Chemical and Biological Sciences, University of Karachi of Pakistan and Industrial Technology Institute

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-02-22

- A Memorandum of Understanding on Education and Research has been proposed to be entered into between the International Centre for Chemical and Biological Sciences, University of Karachi of Pakistan and the Industrial Technology Institute. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
