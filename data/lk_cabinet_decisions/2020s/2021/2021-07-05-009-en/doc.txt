Aranya Senasana Conservation Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-07-05

- It has been identified the necessity of making a formal arrangement to conserve and maintain Aranya Senasana while protecting the identity of the Bikkhus living in Aranyas. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs ,to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft legislation for the purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
