Procurement of Tyre Kits for the Sri Lanka Transport Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-10-25

- Open Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement of 21,600 new Tyre Kits to meet the annual requirement of the Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB) and 02 bids have been received for the same. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport to award the relevant procurement to M/s CEAT Kelani International Tyres (Pvt) Ltd., the substantially responsive lowest bidder, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
