Submission of the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2020 to Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-05-03

- The Monetary Board of the  Central Bank of Sri Lanka should submit a report containing the condition of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, the policies and measures adopted by the Monetary Board during the year, to the Minister of Finance within 04 months from the commencement of the following year. Accordingly, the Annual Report of the  Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2020 has been submitted to the Minister of Finance  on 2021‑04‑30 and it should be submitted to Parliament by the Minister of Finance within 14 days from the receipt in terms of Monetary Law Act. Accordingly, proposal made by the Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Finance to table the Annual Report of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka for the year 2020 in Parliament , was approved by the Cabinet.
