Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the Edith Cowan University, Australia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-08-09

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the Edith Cowan University, Australia for the objectives of improving research collaborations in the fields where the two parties are mutually agreed, implementing mobility programmes for students according to the requirements of both parties, implementing the Executive Education Programme on Hospitality and implementing curricula and study development programmes and joint Doctoral Degree programmes. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the said MoU between two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
