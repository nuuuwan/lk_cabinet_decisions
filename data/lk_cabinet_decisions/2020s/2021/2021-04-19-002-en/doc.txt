Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the University of Sungkyunkwan in Republic of Korea

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-04-19

- It has been proposed to enter into an MoU between the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the University of Sungkyunkwan in Republic of Korea for academic and cultural exchange in the areas  of Immunology and Molecular Medicine through enhancing the collaboration in undergraduate curriculum development in Immunology and Molecular Medicine, assisting in commencing the study programme and development and operation of innovative research programmes for secondary and junior college students in the community. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the relevant MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
