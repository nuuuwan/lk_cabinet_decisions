Proposed Port related logistics sector development in Bloemandhal Area

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-25

- Sri Lanka Ports Authority has identified the urgent necessity of developing the   logistics facilities of Colombo Port to meet the growing demand with efficiency to enhance the competitiveness of the Port. Accordingly, Sri Lanka Ports Authority intends to establish a  state of art Port related logistics sector  in  Bloemandhal Area. As the first stage of the said programme, it is scheduled to  establish a large warehouse complex for  Less than Container Load (LCL), Full Container Load (FCL), Multi Country Consolidation (MCC), cold storage facility, entrepot cargo handling and other port related logistics activities. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports and Shipping to take necessary action to call for Request for Proposals to identify a suitable investor/ investors to implement the proposed project on Build, Operate, Transfer basis as a Public Private Partnership Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
