Calling for Expression of Interest (EOI) to implement Renewable Energy Projects under Private Sector Investments

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-08-30

- It has been aimed at meeting 70% of the electricity requirement in the country from the Renewable Energy sources by the year 2030. Since the hydro‑electricity development in the country has reached its peak, more attention has to be paid towards Solar Power and Wind Power for the purpose. In order to reach the expected goal, the established Solar Power capacity of 458MW and the established Wind Power capacity of 248MW have to be improved by another 4,800MW and 3,500MW, respectively. Further, the National Transmission System needs to be renovated, enabling to receive the electricity so generated, to the said Transmission System. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Power, to call for Expression of Interest (EoI) from local and foreign investors showing interest to finance the said Projects, through Print and Electronic media, giving priority to the identified Projects above 50MW, was approved by the Cabinet.
