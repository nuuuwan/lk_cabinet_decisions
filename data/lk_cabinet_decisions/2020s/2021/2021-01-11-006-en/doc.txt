Submission of the Gazette Notifications published under Sri Lanka Export 	Development Act, No.40 of 1979 to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-01-11

- The Gazette Notification dated 2020‑10‑28 was issued to amend the CESS Levy applicable to the import of footwear under the provisions of Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No.40 of 1979. Subsequently, the Gazette Notification dated 2020‑10‑29 was issued to further amend the provisions coming under the said Gazette and to revise the CESS Levy applicable to another few commodities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade to submit the said Gazette Notification to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
