The Agreement entered into between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of Bahrain on Mutual Visa Exemption for holders of Diplomatic, Special and Official Passports

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-09-27

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2019‑05‑07 has granted approval to enter into an Agreement between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of Bahrain on Mutual Visa Exemption for holders of Diplomatic, Special and Official Passports. Nevertheless, the relevant Agreement could not be signed within the due time frame. Accordingly, the proposal made by H.E. the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to enter into the relevant Agreement between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
