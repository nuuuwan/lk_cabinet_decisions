Disposal of confiscated sand and timber

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-02-22

- The Cabinet has granted its approval to provide sand and timber confiscated by the Courts located countrywide for construction and renovation purposes of the places of religious worship at concessionary prices and the said approval has lasted on 2020‑12‑31. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to revise the methodology adopted at present under the said Programme and implement further, was approved by the Cabinet.
