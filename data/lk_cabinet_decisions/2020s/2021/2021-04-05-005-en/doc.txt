Award of contracts to the suppliers for the supply of food items to the Department of Prisons for the year 2021/2022

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-04-05

- National competitive bids have been called to select contractors for the supply of food items for the Department of Prisons for the year 2021/2022. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to award the relevant contracts for the selected suppliers for the supply of food items to the Department of Prisons for the period of 2021‑04‑16 to 2022‑04‑15, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
