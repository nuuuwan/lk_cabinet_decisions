Providing a MRI (I.5T) Scanner to Apeksha Hospital, Maharagama

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-06-14

- National Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement of Supply, Delivery, Installation, Commissioning and Maintenance of a MRI (I.5T) Scanner for Maharagama Apeksha Hospital. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the procurment of providing a MRI (I.5T) Scanner to M/s Technomedics International (Pvt) Ltd as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and the recommendation made by the Procurement Appeal Board in that regard, was approved by the Cabinet.
