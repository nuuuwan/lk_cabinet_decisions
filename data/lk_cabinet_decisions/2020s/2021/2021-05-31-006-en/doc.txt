Procurement of 32,000 vials of Bevacizumab Injection 100mg/4ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-05-31

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for purchasing this medicine which is used for the treatment of certain types of cancers. 04 bids have been submitted in this regard and the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the procurement of 32,000 vials of Bevacizumab Injection 100mg/4ml, at a sum of LKR 220.8 million, to M/s Medmart Pharma (Pvt) Ltd., as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
