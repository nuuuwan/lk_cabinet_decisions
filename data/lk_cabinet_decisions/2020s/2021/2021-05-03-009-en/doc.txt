Procurement for supply of 112,500 Vials of Human Immunoglobulin for Intravenous use BP, 5g -6g Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-05-03

- International Competitive Bids  have been called for the procurement of this drug which is used for the treatment of patients with  immune deficiencies.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the above procurement to  M/s Reliance Life Science (Pvt.) Ltd of India at a sum of Rs. 13.10 million as recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
