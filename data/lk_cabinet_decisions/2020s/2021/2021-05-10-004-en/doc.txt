Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Faculty of Technology, University of Jayewardenepura and the Tropical Biosphere Research Centre of University of Ryukyus, Japan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-05-10

- It has been agreed to enter into an MoU between the Faculty of Technology, University of Jayewardenepura and the Tropical Biosphere Research Centre of University of Ryukyus, Japan on collaboration in the field of inter-institutional research, development of innovation programmes, providing facilities to the staff and student exchange programmes and sharing experiences. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to enter into the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
