Purchasing another 6 million doses of ‘Sputnik V’ Vaccine to prevent the spread of Covid – 19

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-04-05

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2021‑03‑23 to purchase 7 million doses of ‘Sputnik V’ Vaccine at a cost of US$ 9.95 each to a sum of 69.65 US$ million upon the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee. The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Health to purchase another 6 million doses of ‘Sputnik V’ Vaccine at the same price in addition to the aforesaid doses of vaccines.
