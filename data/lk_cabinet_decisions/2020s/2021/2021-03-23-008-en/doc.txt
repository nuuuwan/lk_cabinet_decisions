Presentation of Regulations made under section 34(1) of the Convention Against Doping in Sports Act No.33 of 2013, in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-23

- The proposal made by the Minister of Youth and Sports to present the regulations made under section 34(1) of the Convention Against Doping in Sports Act No.33 of 2013 and published in the Gazette (Extraordinary) Notification No 2216/4 dated 2021‑02‑23, in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
