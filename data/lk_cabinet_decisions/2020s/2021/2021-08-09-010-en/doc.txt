Importation of organic fertilizer and natural minerals and chelated plant micro nutrients for Maha season 2021/2022

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-08-09

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2021 05 31 for the importation of fertilizer required for Maha Season 2021/2022 which cannot be secured from the local organic fertilizer producers, by following the open competitive bidding procedure. Accordingly, international competitive bids have been called to secure the required quantity of fertilizer for the Ceylon Fertilizer Company and Colombo Fertilizer Company Limited. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minster of Agriculture to award the relevant procurements, as recommended by the Cabinet Appointed Special Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
