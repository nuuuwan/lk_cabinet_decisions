Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Ruhuna and Hokkaido Bunkyo University, Japan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-12-13

- It has been proposed to enter into an MoU between the University of Ruhuna and Hokkaido Bunkyo University, Japan for higher education collaborations between two parties. Under this MoU which is valid for  05 years, it is expected to carry out activities such as exchange of faculty, researchers and other research and administrative staff, implementing students exchange programmes, promotion of joint research activities between two parties, exchange of academic information and materials. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
