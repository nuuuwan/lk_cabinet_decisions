National Action Plan to Monitor and Combat Human Trafficking

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-02-22

- The Action Plan to Monitor and Combat Human Trafficking to be implemented  during the period from 2021-2025 has been prepared by reviewing the Action Plan operated from 2015-2019 and taking into consideration the experiences gained in the implementation of the said plan and the views expressed by the others parties. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to implement the National Action Plan to Monitor and Combat Human Trafficking 2021-2025, was approved by the Cabinet.
