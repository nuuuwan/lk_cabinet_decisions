Amendments to the Dangerous Animals Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-06-28

- The Sub Committee for Criminal Law Reforms of the Special Unit of the Ministry of Justice has recommended that the Dangerous Animals Ordinance should be amended to suit the present needs. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice to take necessary action to amend the Dangerous Animals Ordinance, No.38 of 1921, was approved by the Cabinet.
