Establishment of a Committee for development of the rubber cultivation and related industries in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-03-01

- The global demand for sanitary devices including the gloves that are manufactured using rubber has been tremendously increased due to the prevailing global pandemic situation of COVID – 19 and therefore, there is a national requirement to produce raw rubber locally to meet the requirement of local rubber products. For the purpose, the need to appoint a coordinating committee which will function consolidating planters and industrialists encouraging the local rubber industry together with the public and private sector institutions directly linked with the manufacturing raw rubber as well as rubber manufacturing industries has been identified. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Plantation to appoint a Committee by the name of “Inter-institutional Coordinating Committee for Development of Rubber Cultivation and Industries” under the chairmanship of the Secretary, Ministry of Plantation and comprising of the representatives of other relevant Ministries and Institutions to continue and maintain the value chain of rubber industry in Sri Lanka with a sound performance, to discuss and settle the available issues in the sector, and to submit policy recommendations to the Government.
