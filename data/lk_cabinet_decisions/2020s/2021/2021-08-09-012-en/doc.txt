Scarcity of Milk Powder in the Market

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-08-09

- The Cabinet focused its attention on the prevailing scarcity of milk powder in the domestic market. Therefore, the Cabinet decided to authorize the Minister of Finance to examine the prevailing situation in this regard and take necessary action to ensure the adequate supply of milk powder to the domestic market by revising the existing import duty on milk powder or by adopting any other means, without permitting any price increase of milk powder.
