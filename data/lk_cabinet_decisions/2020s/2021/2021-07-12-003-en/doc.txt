Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka and the Meijo University, Japan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2021-07-12

- The proposal made by the Minister of Education to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding for developing higher education collaboration between the University of Peradeniya and the Meijo University, Japan with the objectives of organizing student and staff exchange programmes, exchanging academic publications and study materials in areas of common interest and organizing lectures, seminars and conferences under joint studies and scientific activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
