Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education and the National University of Singapore for Higher Education Collaboration

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the National University of Singapore, Republic of Singapore, which is ranked at a top position in global university rankings and the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education of Sri Lanka to establish higher education collaborations including the access to Postgraduate scholarship opportunities for Graduate students in Sri Lanka. Entering into the proposed MoU will enable Sri Lankan students to pursue Postgraduate Degrees including Doctoral Degrees at the National University of Singapore, participate in capacity building programmes and conduct joint research. The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
