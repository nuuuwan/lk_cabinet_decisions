Establishment of Diplomatic relations with Antigua and Barbuda

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to establish formal diplomatic relations with 13 countries in the Latin American and Caribbean region. Accordingly, required measures have already been taken by the Government of Sri Lanka as of now to establish diplomatic relations with the countries of the region, other than Antigua and Barbuda. The proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the Agreement on the Establishment of Diplomatic Relations between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Antigua and Barbuda, aiming to accrue mutual benefits by both countries and enhance existing friendly relations and cooperation in the political, socio-economic and cultural fields, was approved by the Cabinet.
