Term Contract for the procurement of four (04) shipments of Murban Crude Oil during the period from 2026‑01‑01 to 2026‑04-30

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited from the registered suppliers of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation for the purchase of 04 shipments of Murban crude oil for a period of four (04) months from 2026-01-01 to 2026-04-30, under two options: payment prior to the date of Quantity Drawn (100% Ex. Storage Modality) and thirty days credit (L/C) opened through the Bank of Ceylon. Six (06) bids have been submitted for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the relevant procurement, to M/s BB Energy Asia Pte. Ltd., Singapore, based on the recommendation made by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee after evaluating the relevant bids, was approved by the Cabinet.
