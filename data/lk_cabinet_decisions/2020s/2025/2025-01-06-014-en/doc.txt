Signing the Agreement related to the Protocol for Exporting Poultry Meat to the Chinese Market

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-01-06 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign an Agreement between the Sri Lanka and China, including Inspection, Quarantine and Health Requirements, to be prepared regarding the procedures of the two countries to export poultry meat from Sri Lanka to China, with the intervention of the General Administration of Customs of China, under a specific procedure. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation for signing the said Agreement between the General Administration of Customs of China and his Ministry, was approved by the Cabinet.
