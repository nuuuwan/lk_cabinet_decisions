Appointment to the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent in the Administrative District of Batticaloa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mrs. J.J. Muralitharan, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, serving as the District Secretary/Government Agent in the Administrative District of Batticaloa, is scheduled to retire from the Public Service with effect from 2025-09-26. The proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to appoint Mr. J.S. Arulraj, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as the Secretary to the Governor of the Eastern Province, to the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent in the Administrative District of Batticaloa falling vacant accordingly, with effect from the said date, was approved by the Cabinet.
