Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation between the 	Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute and the Rio 	Branco Institute of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Federative 	Republic of 	Brazil

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Although approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑09‑02 to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and the Rio Branco Institute under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Federative Republic of Brazil, the said Agreement has not yet been signed. The Rio Branco Institute, as an internationally renowned diplomatic academy, has contributed to the training of diplomats in about 57 countries of the world for 08 decades and has entered into cooperation agreements with more than 70 countries. Through the proposed Memorandum of Understanding, it envisages the training of diplomats and facilitating them for training programmes, exchanging information and experiences concerning their respective programmes of study and research, exchanging information on tools related to e-learning and information on national as well as international publications and promoting closer cooperation, especially in the areas of mutual interest. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the proposed MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
