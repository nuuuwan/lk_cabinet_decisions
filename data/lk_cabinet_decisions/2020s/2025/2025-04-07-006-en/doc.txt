Procurement for the supply of 3 million inactivated doses of Rabies Vaccine (Human Use) in 0.5ml/1 ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited under the procurement for the supply of 3 million inactivated doses of Rabies Vaccine in 0.5ml/1 ml used to treat for the prevention of Rabies. 05 bids have been received for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to M/s Gulf Pharma (Pvt) Ltd., Sri Lanka, (Manufacturer: M/s Chiron Behring Vaccine (Pvt) Ltd, India), the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, based on the recommendation of the Standing High Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
