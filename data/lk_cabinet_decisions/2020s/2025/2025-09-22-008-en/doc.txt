Completion of works of stalled sub-projects implemented through the “Nearest School is the Best School” Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A project to develop infrastructure facilities in schools called 'The Nearest School is the Best School' has been implemented during the period 2016-2020. At the beginning of the year 2025, out of the sub-projects initiated under the said project, 29 projects under the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education and 03 projects under the Provincial Education Departments remained to be completed. Due to these stalled projects, the school community is facing many problems, and the money spent on those works has also become a waste of money. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to resume the remaining construction works of the remaining construction projects initiated under the 'Nearest School is the Best School' project and complete them on a priority basis in the years 2025, 2026 and 2027, was approved by the Cabinet.
