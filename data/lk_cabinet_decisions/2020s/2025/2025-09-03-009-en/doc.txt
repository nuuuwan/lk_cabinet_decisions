Conducting inquiries into complaints made with regard to missing persons

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Office on Missing Persons has been established under the provisions of the Office on Missing Persons (Establishment, Administration and Discharge of Functions) Act, No.14 of 2016. The Office is responsible for conducting investigations into missing and disappeared persons and issuing reports to their relatives. Out of the 16,966 complaints received by the said Office so far, 10,517 complaints are still pending for investigation. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to implement a special project to complete the investigations and related duties by the end of 2027 in order to provide justice to the aggrieved parties and to appoint 25 panels comprising of 75 experts including retired Judges, Senior Administrative Officers, Lawyers and other qualified persons for the relevant task, was approved by the Cabinet.
