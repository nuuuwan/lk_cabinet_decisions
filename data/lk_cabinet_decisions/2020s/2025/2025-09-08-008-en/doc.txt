Procurement for Designing, Developing, Printing and Supplying of computer based draw lottery tickets, to the Development Lotteries Board for a period of one year

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Development Lotteries Board has invited bids under the National Competitive Bidding Procedure for the procurement for designing, developing, printing and supplying of computer based draw lottery tickets such as Lagna Vasanawa, Kapruka, Ada Kotipathi, Super Ball, Sasiri, Shanida, Supiri Dhana Sampatha and New Draw Lottery Ticket No. 01 (Jaya Sampatha) and New Draw Lottery Ticket No. 02, for a period of one year. Seven (07) bids have been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon.
