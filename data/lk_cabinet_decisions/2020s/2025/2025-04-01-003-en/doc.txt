Procurement for the supply of 150,000 vials of 1,000,000 IU Vial Colistimethate Sodium for injection

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International competitive bids have been invited for the purchase of 150,000 vials of Colistimethate Sodium Injection in 1,000,000 IU, which is an antibiotic used to treat serious infections caused by certain gram-negative bacteria. 03 bids have been received for the purpose. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to M/s Slim Pharmaceuticals (Pvt) Ltd., of Sri Lanka (Manufacturer: M/s Gufic Biosciences Ltd, India), the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, based on the recommendation of the Standing High Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
