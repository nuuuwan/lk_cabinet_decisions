Implementation of the recommendations of the Committee appointed to review the recruitment process and cadre management in the Public Service

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Two (02) Reports submitted by the Officials' Committee appointed under the Chairmanship of the Secretary to the Prime Minister to review the recruitment process and cadre management in the Public Service, have recommended recruitment for 15,073 vacant posts in 18 Line Ministries, 04 Provincial Councils and 02 Special Spending Units. Accordingly, the approval of the Cabinet was granted to make the recruitments recommended in the above reports.
