Hosting the 78th Session of the WHO Regional Committee Meeting for South-East Asia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The 78th Session of the WHO Regional Committee Meeting for the South-East Asia Region is scheduled to be held in Sri Lanka from 13th  to 15th  October 2025. This Regional Committee meeting will shape the regional health agenda, review progress and adopt policies to address pressing health issues. Official delegates from 11 member countries, including Health Ministers and senior officials from the Ministries of Health are expected to participate in the said meeting. In addition, officials from the Ministry of Health and Mass Media, representatives from the WHO Secretariat and United Nations Agencies, development partners and representatives from non-governmental organizations will also participate in this. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to host the 78th  Session of the WHO Regional Committee Meeting for South-East Asia, was approved by the Cabinet.
