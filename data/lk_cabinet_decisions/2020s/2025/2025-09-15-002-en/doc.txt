Implementation of a Value-Addition Project under the Public Private Partnership for Graphite in Kahatagaha Mine

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Kahatagaha mine, which is spread over an area of about 102 acres, is the deepest graphite mine in Sri Lanka. The Kahatagaha mine is currently operated under Kahatagaha Graphite Lanka Limited, a fully state-owned company, which produces and exports the high quality graphite. It had been decided under the previous Government to restructure Kahatagaha Graphite Lanka Limited, and it was decided at the Cabinet meeting held on 2023 12 11 that action should be taken to implement a Value Addition Project for graphite in the Kahatagaha mine by an investor to be identified. However, no further steps have been taken accordingly. It has been found that the locally available technology is insufficient to determine the graphite reserves in the mine. Taking into account the above facts, the proposal made by the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to revoke the Cabinet decision dated 2023 12 11 pertaining to restructuring of Kahatagaha Graphite Lanka Limited and call for Expressions of Interest by following the approved procurement procedure to implement the entire project of exploring the graphite reserves of the Kahatagaha mine, mining the graphite in the said mine, processing the graphite and adding value to the graphite, under the Public Private Partnership method, while retaining the ownership of the graphite in the Kahatagaha mine with the said Company and safeguarding the jobs of the employees currently working in the said mine, was approved by the Cabinet.
