Project to establish a Threat Hunting and Malware Analysis Lab

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka expects to accelerate its digital transformation through major projects such as the Sri Lanka Unique Digital Identity, the National Federated Data Exchange and the Lanka Government Cloud, and other digitalization projects operating within the government institutions. Creating a secured cyber environment has been identified as a critical requirement for the successful implementation of such projects. For this purpose, a project has been planned to establish a research unit within the Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Readiness Team (Sri Lanka CERT) aiming enhancing the capability of identifying and successful countering of cyber-attacks, advanced malware analysis capabilities and human resource capacity building and research. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy to implement the proposed project to establish a sThreat Hunting and Malware Analysis Lab, was approved by the Cabinet.
