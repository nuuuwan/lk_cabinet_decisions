Amendment to the National Archives Act No.48 of 1973

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022.11.21 to appoint a Committee of Experts comprising of competent persons on the subjects of document management, document preservation and digitized document management to submit appropriate recommendations to amend the National Archives Act, No.48 of 1973. The said Committee of Experts, having considered the comments of the public and other consultations, has drafted a National Policy on Archives and Records Management. Further, an initial draft has also been prepared to use as a basis in the preparation of the National Archives and Records Management Bill by repealing the National Archives Act No.48 of 1973 and the National Archives (Amendment) Act, No. 30 of 1981. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minster of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to approve the National Policy on Archives and Records Management prepared by the Committee of Experts and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to introduce the National Archives and Records Management Bill by repealing the  National Archives Act No. 48 of 1973 and the National Archives (Amendment) Act No.30 of 1981, was approved by the Cabinet.
