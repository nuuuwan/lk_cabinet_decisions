The Convention between Sri Lanka and Austria for Elimination of Double Taxation and Prevention of Fiscal Evasion and Avoidance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to reach a Convention between Sri Lanka and Austria to enhance economic relations between the two countries by establishing the necessary legal framework for elimination of double taxation and prevention of fiscal evasion and avoidance. Although some revenue may be lost due to the concessions offered through the proposed Agreement, the benefits that could be accrued upon the foreign investment promotion and significant increase in foreign exchange earnings will be advantageous to the country. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to sign the said Convention, was approved by the Cabinet.
