Amendment of Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (Incorporation) Act, No.41 of 1980

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing has been incorporated by the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (Incorporation) Act, No.41 of 1980 and promotes marketing education by maintaining strong partnerships with the country's leading higher education institutions/universities including the Wayamba University of Sri Lanka, the Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka and the Rajarata University of Sri Lanka. The Institute annually provides education to nearly 8,000 students with marketing and entrepreneurship professional qualifications and leading companies in the country obtain the services of the Institute to provide marketing training to their employees. It has been identified that the provisions of the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (Incorporation) Act, No.41 of 1980 should be amended to further expand the services provided by the Institute and enable it to perform more efficiently. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to amend the said Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
