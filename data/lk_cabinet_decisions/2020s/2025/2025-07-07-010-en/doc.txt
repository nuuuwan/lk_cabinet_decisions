Implementing an integrated programme for the future advancement of the Sri Lankan Youth

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports is entrusted with the responsibility on the functions of formulating, monitoring and evaluating policies, strategies and programmes related to youth development in Sri Lanka. The National Youth Services Council, the Youth Corps and the National Centre for Leadership Development, Embilipitiya, under the purview of the said Ministry, work to empower the youth community through leadership development, skills training and vocational education. It has been observed that it is essential to have an integrated and collaborative approach among the government institutions with the potential of intervention for the advancement of living and economic status of the youth community receiving vocational education. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports, to arrange an integrated programme by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports; Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education; Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism; and the Ministry of Digital Economy and to appoint a Ministerial Committee to implement the said programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
