Admission of Students from Grade 2 to 11 except Grade 5 and 6 in Schools

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Maintenance of the number of students in a classroom, only within the approved limits is essential to implement the new proposed education reforms. Therefore, it has been identified that it would be appropriate to introduce a proper mechanism to provide the opportunity to admit students to intermediate grades other than Grades 5 and 6 in schools, while ensuring the right of all children in the country for education. Taking into account the said matters, it has been planned to issue new circular instructions to provide the opportunity to apply for the intermediate grades in other schools, so that priority is given to students genuinely need to be admitted to another school from the school they are currently studying, for various reasons. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to cancel the circular instructions  issued so far pertaining to the admission of students from Grade 2 to Grade 11 other than Grade 5 and 6 in schools and issue new circular instructions for the same, taking into considerations the recommendations made by the Parliamentary Sub‑Committee appointed by the Advisory Committee of the  Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, was approved by the Cabinet.
