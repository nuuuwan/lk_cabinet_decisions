Amendment to the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance and the Judicature Act, No.2 of 1978 (Prohibition against trafficking of dangerous drugs on high seas)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal submitted to amend the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance and Judicature Act, No.2 of 1978 (Prohibition against trafficking of dangerous drugs on the High Seas) was taken up for discussion at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025-02-09. In that instance, the Cabinet has granted its concurrence to obtain the views of all relevant parties and to prepare an Initial Draft/Concept Paper.  Accordingly, the views of the relevant parties were obtained and the amendments to the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance and the Judicature Act, No.2 of 1978 have been identified. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to instruct the Legal Draftsman to take steps to prepare Bills to amend the Poisons, Opium and Dangerous Drugs Ordinance and the Judicature Act, No.2 of 1978, based on the said amendments, was approved by the Cabinet.
