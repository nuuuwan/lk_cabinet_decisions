Introducing a new National Statistics Act

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The main legal framework for conducting census in Sri Lanka is the Census Ordinance No.9 of 1900. The Statistics Ordinance No.44 of 1935 has established a comprehensive statutory framework for the collection, compilation, analysis and dissemination of official statistics in Sri Lanka. However, it has been identified that the current legal framework does not provide an adequate platform for the coordination of all producers of official statistics, the integration of administrative and alternative data sources or the adoption of governance arrangements that ensure independence, impartiality and quality assurance. Therefore, a committee comprising of officers of the Department of Census and Statistics, under the technical guidance of an expert from the United Nations Statistics Division, has prepared an initial draft of the National Statistical Framework that is globally consistent and nationally compliant, combining international expertized knowledge with local institutional knowledge. The proposed initial draft seeks to modernize and unify the legal framework for the production, coordination and control of official statistics in Sri Lanka and to establish a national statistical system comprising all the producers of official statistics. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a new Bill based on the relevant initial draft, repealing the Census Ordinance No.9 of 1900 and the Statistics Ordinance No.44 of 1935, was approved by the Cabinet.
