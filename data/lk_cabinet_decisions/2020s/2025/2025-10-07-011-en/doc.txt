Procurement for supplying food items to the Department of Prisons for the Year 2025/26

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Department of Prisons has invited bids to select suppliers for the supply of food items under 11 food categories for 31 prison institutions for the year 2025/26. Based on the recommendations made by the Standing High Level Procurement Committee after evaluating 945 bids received accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to award contracts for the supply of food items to the Department of Prisons for the year 2025/26 to the selected suppliers for a total cost of Rs.4,317 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
