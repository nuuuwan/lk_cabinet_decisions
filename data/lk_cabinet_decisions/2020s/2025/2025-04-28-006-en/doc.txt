Amendment of the Rubber Control Act, No. 11 of 1956

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑02‑26 to publish the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman and cleared by the Attorney General  to amend the  Rubber Control Act, No. 11 of 1956, in the Government Gazette and submit the same to Parliament.  The said Bill has been published in the Government Gazette on 2024‑05‑22. Nevertheless, the said Bill could not be presented to Parliament due to the dissolution of Parliament on 2024‑09‑24. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Plantation and Community Infrastructure to re-publish the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman to amend the Rubber Control Act, No. 11 of 1956 in the Government Gazette and submit the same, thereafter to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
