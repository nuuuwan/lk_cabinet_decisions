Procurement for the supply of 70,000 vials of 5g-6g Human 	Immunoglobulin for Intravenous use

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited to procure 70,000 vials of 5g-6g Human Immunoglobulin for intravenous use, which is used to treat the patients with immune deficiencies. Six (06) bids have been received for the purpose. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to M/s Reliance Life Sciences (Pvt) Ltd., India, the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder for a sum of USD 6.86 million, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
