Purchasing a Dredger for the Ceylon Fishery Harbours Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A cost-benefit analysis has been conducted regarding the purchase of a dredger for the essential maintenance and development activities of the Ceylon Fishery Harbours Corporation. The said Corporation requires to dredge about 183,000 cubic meters of sand annually, and since a cost of about Rs.500 million has to be incurred in obtaining the dredging service for that purpose from the private sector, purchasing a dredger for the Corporation itself has proven to be economically beneficial in the long term. Further, it is possible to use the dredger so purchased for dredging activities to be carried out in lagoons such as Arugam Bay, Nandikadal, Nayaru, Lankapatuna under the Lagoon Conservation and Management Programme implemented under the Ministry of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources to purchase a dredger for the Ceylon Fishery Harbours Corporation, was approved by the Cabinet.
