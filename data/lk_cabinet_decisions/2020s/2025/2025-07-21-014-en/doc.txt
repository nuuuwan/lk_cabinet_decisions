Amendment of the Antiquities Ordinance No.9 of 1940

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Antiquities Ordinance, No. 9 of 1940 has been enacted for the conservation of antiquities of Sri Lanka and the sites and buildings with historical or archaeological importance in Sri Lanka. The said Ordinance was last amended in the year 1998 and the Department of Archaeology is currently facing a number of challenges in the protection of antiquities. Therefore, the need has arisen to amend the relevant Ordinance by incorporating new Laws to suit the current needs and to take prompt action, especially to prevent the destruction of antiquities, theft of antiquities, unauthorized excavations and export of antiquities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the Antiquities Ordinance No.9 of 1940, incorporating the amendments identified through conducting workshops and discussions with scholars in the relevant field, archaeologists, archaeological officers and social activists, was approved by the Cabinet.
