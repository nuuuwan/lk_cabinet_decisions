Amendment of the Tower Hall Theatre Foundation Act, No.01 of 1978

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2023-03-21 to amend the Tower Hall Theatre Foundation Act, No.01 of 1978. Accordingly, the Legal Draftsman has informed that the Bill prepared as such should receive the concurrence of the present Cabinet of Ministers. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to grant policy approval to amend the Tower Hall Theatre Foundation Act No. 01 of 1978, was approved by the Cabinet.
