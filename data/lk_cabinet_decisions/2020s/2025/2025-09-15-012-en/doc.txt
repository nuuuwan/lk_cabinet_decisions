Formulation of Laws to enable Sri Lankans living abroad for casting vote

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The sovereignty vested in the people as per Article 3 of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka shall be exercised by the people as per the provisions of Article 4 at the election. The franchise has been limited to citizens residing in Sri Lanka and whose names are registered in the register of electors as per the election law. Under the current Acts on election, neither systems for voting nor laws have been formulated thereunder to cast votes by the citizens living abroad. However, many Asian countries such as India, Bangladesh, and the Philippines have formulated legal strategies to enable their citizens abroad to vote, and it has been recognized as a timely requirement for Sri Lanka to make such legal amendments. Accordingly, the concurrence of the Cabinet was granted for the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to take measures to appoint a Committee comprising of the Officials from the Election Commission, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government and other relevant Ministries and Institutions to examine the matters related to amending the existing laws or formulating new laws to protect the right to vote of citizens living abroad.
