Granting 'Approved Enterprise' status to import Companies to re-export selected Spices

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet, at its meeting held on 2023‑12‑18, has granted approval for the procedure for the importation and processing of selected spices for re-export purpose in the form of oil extracts, oleoresins and spent. Accordingly, the Import and Export (Control) Regulations No. 10 of 2024 have been imposed for the said purpose. Several enterprises have already signed Supplementary Agreements with the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka for importing and processing selected spices as per the said Regulations. The Board of Investment of Sri Lanka has recommended to grant the 'Approved Enterprise' status to such enterprises. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon, President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to grant the 'Approved Enterprise' status to the following enterprises that have fulfilled the requirements of the procedure approved for re-export in the form of oil extracts, oleoresins and spent, was approved by the Cabinet. * Stay Naturals (Pvt) Ltd. * Verger Naturals (Pvt) Ltd. * Plant Lipids Lanka (Pvt) Ltd. * HDDES Extracts (Pvt) Ltd.
