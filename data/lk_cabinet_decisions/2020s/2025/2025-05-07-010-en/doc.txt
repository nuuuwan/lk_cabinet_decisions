The Act of Parliament for the Establishment of the Institute of Food Science and Technology Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Institute of Food Science and Technology Sri Lanka, comprising of food technology practitioners and scientists, which is maintained voluntarily was established in 2004 and is currently registered as a company limited by guarantee. The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2016‑09‑27 granted approval to legalize it by an Act of Parliament as a form of Government support receiving for maintaining the said Institute at national and international level and to strengthen the activities thereof. Accordingly, approval of the Cabinet was granted on 2024-06-11 to prepare the final Bill incorporating the amendments submitted by the Attorney General pertaining to the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. However, the said Bill has not yet been submitted to Parliament. Taking into consideration the aforesaid matters, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to obtain policy approval of the Cabinet to expedite the preparation of the said Bill, was approved by the Cabinet of Ministers.
