Establishment of the Customs Verification Centre at Kerawalapitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2025‑03‑17 to transfer a portion of land belonging to the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation to the Sri Lanka Customs for the establishment of the Customs Verification Centre in Kerawalapitiya, and a Committee has been appointed to determine the modality for the establishment of the said Customs Verification Centre. The said Committee has submitted its recommendations with appropriate criteria for selecting a more viable business modality. Accordingly, based on the recommendation of the said Committee, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to follow the procedure for calling Expressions of Interest and Requests for Proposal to select a suitable Private Partner to implement the proposed Kerawalapitiya Customs Verification Centre project under a Hybrid Public-Private Partnership Model.
