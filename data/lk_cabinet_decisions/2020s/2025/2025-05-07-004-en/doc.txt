Procurement for the supply of 30,000 vials of 50 mg Liposomal form of Amphotericin B

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International competitive bids have been invited for the purchase of 30,000 vials of 50 mg liposomal form of amphotericin B, which is used to treat life‑threatening fungal infections, including cryptococcal meningitis and visceral leishmaniasis, particularly in individuals with compromised immune systems. Three bids have been received for the purpose. Based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement, to M/s. Slim Pharmaceuticals (Pvt) Ltd., Sri Lanka, (Manufacturer - M/s. Gufic Bioscience Ltd., India), the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, for a total cost of LKR 194.26 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
