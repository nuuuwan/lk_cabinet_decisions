Bill to establish the Chartered Institute of Media Professionals of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The need to establish the Chartered Institute of Media Professionals of Sri Lanka has been identified to meet the necessity of a training institute required to carry out the relevant studies with the aim of producing chartered media professionals and to produce a group of competent  and knowledgeable media professionals to enhance the quality and standard of the media community in the country. Accordingly, approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2022.08.15 to prepare a Bill enabling to establish the said Institute through an Act passed by Parliament. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to grant policy approval of the present Government to finalize the preparation of the said Bill, was approved by the Cabinet.
