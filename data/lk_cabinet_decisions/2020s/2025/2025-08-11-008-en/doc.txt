Re-commencement of the projects related to the scope of  urban development that were temporarily suspended

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The construction activities of 18 projects that were commenced in 2021 and thereafter have been temporarily suspended, slowed down or suspended by mutual consent due to problems arising amidst the economic crisis. Out of the 18 projects that were suspended, 06 projects have now been completed. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to re-commence and implement the following projects that were not completed, was approved by the Cabinet: * Construction of the proposed Walapane sathi pola and the multipurpose building. * Development of the Daulagala sathi pola and the vehicle park. * Development of the Mullaitivu bus stand. * Improving sanitary facilities and landscapes in Habarana. * Development of the pathway from Ruwanweliseya vehicle park to the Jetavanaramaya. * Construction of the administrative building of Polonnaruwa new town. * Construction of the Ampara sathi pola. * Development of the Deiyandara bus stand. * Development of the Gampaha public market. * Development of the Peralanda wetland park. * Development of the Panadura public market and the multipurpose building.
