Sign Language Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Establishing Sign Language as a recognized language will enable the hearing impaired individuals to access information and fully participate in all aspects of the society and achieve equal opportunities without barriers. The Cabinet has granted policy approval on 2010‑09‑08 to establish the Sign Language as a recognized language. Accordingly, as per the Cabinet decisions taken on 2017‑09‑12 and 2021‑01‑18, the Legal Draftsman has been instructed to prepare a Bill to recognize the Sign Language as a formal communication medium. The proposal made by Minister of the Rural Development, Social Security and Community Empowerment to obtain policy approval of the new Cabinet to finalize the preparation of the relevant Bill, was approved by the Cabinet.
