Establishment of a fully atomated income generating system for the Colombo Municipal Council under the aid of the Korea International Cooperation Agency

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Colombo Municipal Council generates a high income through four (04) main income sources, namely Assessment Tax, License Tax, Trade Tax and Stamp Duty. The Colombo Municipal Council, the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government and the Department of External Resources have jointly submitted a Concept Paper to the Korea International Cooperation Agency to improve the overall income generation by automation of these four income sources. The Korea International Cooperation Agency has agreed to provide a grant of USD 8.5 million for this purpose. The clearance of the Attorney General and the concurrence of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and the Department of External Resources have been obtained for the said Record of Discussion. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to enter into the Record of Discussion for the implementation of the Income Generation Automation Project of the Colombo Municipal Council utilizing the assistance provided by the Korean International Cooperation Agency, was approved by the Cabinet.
