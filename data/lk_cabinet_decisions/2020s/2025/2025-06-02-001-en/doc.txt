Establishment of a New Unit for Public - Private Partnership

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In the Budget Speech 2025, the Hon. President stated that steps will be taken to introduce a new Act on Public-Private Partnership with the aim of facilitating investment promotion. Accordingly, the Cabinet granted policy approval to the proposal made by the Hon. President to prepare a Bill to establish a new unit based on a formal legal mechanism,  in lieu of the currently operating National Agency for Public Private Partnership and to establish a Public Private Partnership Unit in the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, headed by the Senior Additional Secretary to the President (Finance and Economic Affairs), to coordinate the initial tasks until the said Unit is legally established.
