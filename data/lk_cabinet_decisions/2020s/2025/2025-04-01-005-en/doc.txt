Procurement for the supply of 18,000 Vials of Trastuzumab Injection 440mg in 20 ml  Vials (with solvent)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the purchase of 18,000 Vials of Trastuzumab Injection 440mg in 20 ml Vials used to treat certain types of cancers. Four bids have been submitted for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the procurement for the supply of 18,000 Vials of Trastuzumab Injection 440mg in 20 ml Vials (with solvent), to M/s. Cliniqon Biotech (Pvt) Ltd., (Manufacturer: AryoGen Pharmed, Iran), the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
