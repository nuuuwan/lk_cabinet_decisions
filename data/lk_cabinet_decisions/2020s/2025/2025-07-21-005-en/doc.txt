Transferring the ownership of lands allocated for the establishment of the Nanotechnology Science Park and the establishment of the Faculty of Technology, University of Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Urban Development Authority has acquired a plot of land in extent of 91.40 hectares (225 acres, 3 roods, 4.14 perches) located at Mahenawatte area of Pitipana, Homagama for the purpose of enhancing science and technology-related development activities and establishment of associated institutions. The Board of Management of the Urban Development Authority has granted approval for allocation of 02 plots of land from the said lands for the establishment of the Nanotechnology Science Park and the Faculty of Technology of the University of Colombo. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to transfer the ownership of 19.25 hectares (47 acres 2 roods 10.8 perches) to the Ministry of Science and Technology for the establishment of the Nanotechnology Science Park and 7.18 hectares (17 acres 2 roods 37.74 perches) to the University Grants Commission for the establishment of the Faculty of Technology of the University of Colombo, subject to levying the relevant assessment value, was approved by the Cabinet.
