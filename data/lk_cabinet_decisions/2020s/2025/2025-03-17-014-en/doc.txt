Regulations under section 156 of the Anti-Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In terms of section 156(1) of the Anti Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023, the Minister in-charge of the subject of Justice is vested with the power to consult the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption and make regulations pertaining to specific matters stipulated under the said Act. In view of section 82(1) of the aforesaid Act, every person to whom the aforesaid part applies shall, in the form as prescribed by regulations make declarations of assets and liabilities. "Assets and liabilities" means assets, liabilities, income, expenditure and interests directly or beneficially owned in or outside of Sri Lanka, the details of which shall be prescribed by regulations. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to publish the Anti Corruption (Declaration of Assets and Liabilities) Regulations 2025, made in accordance with the provisions of the Anti Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023, in the Gazette and thereafter to present the said regulations in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
