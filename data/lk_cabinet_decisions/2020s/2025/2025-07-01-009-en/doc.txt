Improving sanitation facilities in the urban areas throughout the 	island

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Due to the lack of sanitation facilities of proper standard for the use of the public and local and foreign tourists who flock to urban areas daily, the Government has decided to construct sanitation facilities equipped with standard toilet systems. The Urban Development Authority has identified suitable locations for the proposed facilities as per the urban development plans. It is proposed to construct 20 sanitation facilities under the said project, and it is estimated that Rs. 525.29 million will be incurred for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to implement the proposed sanitation facilities construction project at the following locations, was approved by the Cabinet:

		*	Demodara Railway Station
		*	Hotel Junction, Sigiriya
		*	Lake Round, Kandy
		*	Gregory Lake, Nuwara Eliya
		*	Kinniya Beach, Trincomalee

		*	Nilaveli Beach Area,Trincomalee
