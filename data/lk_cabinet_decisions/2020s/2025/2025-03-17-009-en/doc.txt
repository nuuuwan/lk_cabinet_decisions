Submission of the amendments to the Personal Data Protection Act, No.9 of 2022

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025 02 19 to amend the Personal Data Protection Act, No.9 of 2022. Clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the amending Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy, to publish the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter, submit the said Bill to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
