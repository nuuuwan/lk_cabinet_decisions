Social Security Contribution Levy (Amendment) Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A Bill containing the amendments for the Social Security Contribution Levy Act, No.25 of 2022 has been prepared by the Legal Draftsman for the tax policy amendments proposed in the Budget 2025. The said Amending Bill has received the clearance of the Attorney General. Accordingly, the proposal made the President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to publish the Social Security Contribution Levy (Amendment) Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter to submit the same to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
