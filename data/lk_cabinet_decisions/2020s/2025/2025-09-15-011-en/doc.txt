Procurement for essential medical supplies from the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation and Local Manufacturers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024-12-02 to directly purchase 291 medicines and other medical supplies required for the year 2025 to the Medical Supplies Division from the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation, local manufacturers who have joint venture agreements with the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation, and local manufacturers who have entered into buy back agreements with the Ministry of Health and Mass Media. However, due to some medicine stocks being either unfit for use or had expired, the need to maintain buffer stocks, and the increase in the consumption of certain medicines, some of the orders placed on the basis of forecasts have not been sufficient, and it is necessary to obtain additional stocks of 123 items from among those medicines and other medical supplies. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award procurements for the purchase of locally manufactured medicines as follows, was approved by the Cabinet:

•	To award the procurement for the supply of 24 types of medicines to the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation, for a sum of Rs.1.148 billion.

•	To award the procurement for the supply of 68 types of medicines to the local manufacturers who have signed joint venture agreements with the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation, for a sum of Rs.4.159 billion.

•	To award the procurement for the supply of 62 types of medicines to the local manufacturers who have signed buy back agreements with the Ministry of Health and Mass Media, for a sum of Rs.5.116 billion.
