Drafting the Public Procurement Law

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In accordance with the powers vested by the Constitution, the National Procurement Commission has obtained the services of an international consultant and two local consultants to draft the Public Procurement Law with the support of the Asian Development Bank. The National Procurement Commission has taken steps to further improve the initial draft prepared by them by obtaining the views and proposals of all parties including foreign agencies, Heads of Ministries and Departments and Non-Governmental Organizations. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft the Public Procurement Law based on the said Draft, was approved by the Cabinet.
