Establishment of a Wildlife Office at the Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport, Hambantota

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The occasional entry of wild animals such as wild elephants into the premises of the Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport in Hambantota and movement across its access roads have frequently caused damages to the infrastructure facilities of the Airport, and its operations have also been affected. It has also been identified that the cost incurred by Airport and Aviation Services (Sri Lanka) (Private) Limited to repair such damages caused to the infrastructure is significantly high. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Environment to establish a dedicated Wildlife Office at the Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport in Hambantota enabling to ensure the safety of passengers, airport staff and airport infrastructure by identifying and properly managing such wildlife threats in advance, and to maintain uninterrupted air services while minimizing possible delays and disruptions in airport operations, was approved by the Cabinet.
