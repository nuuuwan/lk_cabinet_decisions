Procurement for the supply of 450,000 prefilled Syringes/Vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 6,000IU in 0.6ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the supply of 450,000 prefilled Enoxaparin Sodium Injection 6,000IU, 0.6ml Syringes/Vials used for the treatment of patients with heart diseases. Six (06) bids have been received for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, M/s. Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd., India (Manufacturer - M/s. Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd., India) for a sum of USD 751,500, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee on the relevant bids, was approved by the Cabinet.
