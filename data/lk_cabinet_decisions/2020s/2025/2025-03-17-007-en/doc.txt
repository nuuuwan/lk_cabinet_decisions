Procurement for the supply of 6,000 Vials of 40mg Tenecteplase Injection

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited for the purchase of 6000 vials of 40mg Tenecteplase vaccine, which is used to prevent death of people with heart attacks, following the International Competitive Bidding procedure. 03 bids have been received for the purpose. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant contract to M/s Lifecerll Interventions (Pvt) Ltd., of Sri Lanka (Manufacturer - Gennova Biopharmaceuticals Limited, India), the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, based on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
