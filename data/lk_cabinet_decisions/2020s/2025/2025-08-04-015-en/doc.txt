Procurement for the supply of 50 Polyaxial MRI compatible (Titanium/Cobolt-Chrome) Lumber pedicle screws and 20 instrument set for posterior thoracic Thoraco-lumber fusion (for scoliosis correction)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the purchase of 50 polyaxial MRI compatible lumbar pedicle screws used for spinal surgeries and 20 instrument sets for posterior thoracic Thoraco-lumbar fusion. Six bids and five bids have been received respectively for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the procurement of 50 Lumbar pedicle screws to Avenierr Pharma (Pvt) Ltd. and the procurement of 20 instrument sets of posterior thoracic Thoraco-lumbar fusion equipment to A. Baur & Co. (Pvt) Ltd., based on the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
