Appointment to the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent in the Administrative District of Matale

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mrs. Thejani Thilakaratne, presently serving in the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Matale, is due to retire on 2025-06-02, and Mr. L.P. Madanayake, a Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as the Secretary to the North Central Provincial Ministry of Transport, Sports & Youth Affairs, Cooperatives, Trade and Food and Industries Development, has been identified as suitable to be appointed to the relevant Post which falls vacant from that date. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to appoint Mr. L.P. Madanayake to the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Matale, with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
