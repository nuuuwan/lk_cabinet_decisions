Expeditiously completing the Project for renovation and conservation of the National Art Gallery

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Activities of the Project for renovation and conservation of the National Art Gallery are being carried out by the Sri Lanka Navy and approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑02‑19 to complete all the activities of the project within the year 2024 itself. Nevertheless, it has not been possible to fully complete the work in the last year and a financial provision of Rs.120 million has been allocated for the same under the budget estimates for the year 2025. However, it has now been identified that the said provisions are not sufficient to fully complete the work of the project and that an additional financial provision of Rs.32 million is required. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs for securing additional budgetary provisions to complete the renovation work of the National Art Gallery expeditiously, was approved by the Cabinet.
