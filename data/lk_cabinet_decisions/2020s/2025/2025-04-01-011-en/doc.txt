Providing relief to the people affected by the earthquake in Myanmar

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The people of Myanmar have been severely affected due to the strong earthquake that occurred on 2025-03-28 and international media have reported that about 2,700 people have died so far. It has been further reported that a large number of people are missing and another large number of people are undergoing treatment in hospitals due to injuries caused by the above incident. Taking into consideration the long-standing close relationship between the people of the two countries as two Buddhist States, the Cabinet decided to grant the following contribution of the Sri Lankan people to provide relief for the people of Myanmar:

*	 to provide a financial assistance of one million US dollars.

* 	To take necessary steps to promptly provide the material aid collected under the leadership of the Buddhist monks in this country, to the people of Myanmar, with the intervention of the Government.

* 	To keep in readiness a Relief Team consisting of the doctors and other health staff members to be sent to Myanmar, whenever necessary.
