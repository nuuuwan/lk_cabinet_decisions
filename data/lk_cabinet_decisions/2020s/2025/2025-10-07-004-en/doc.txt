Implementation of the Master Plan for Western Province Municipal Solid Waste Management

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Like many developing countries, urban solid waste management in Sri Lanka is also facing many challenges and difficulties. In order to address this situation more effectively, attention has been paid on the preparation of a Master Plan for solid waste management in order to collect, treat and dispose the relevant municipal solid waste in a safe, sustainable and environmentally friendly manner. Accordingly, taking into account the National Waste Management Policy approved by the Cabinet on 2019-10-01, a project to prepare a Master Plan for the Western Province Municipal Solid Waste Management has been implemented through a Memorandum of Understanding signed between the Japan International Cooperation Agency and the Government of Sri Lanka. The said Master Plan has been prepared through the said Project in coordination with all the stakeholders. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to implement the Master Plan for the Western Province Municipal Solid Waste Management, was approved by the Cabinet.
