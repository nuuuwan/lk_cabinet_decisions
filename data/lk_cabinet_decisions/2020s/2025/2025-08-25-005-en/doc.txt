Rehabilitation of the stretch between Kottawa and Dodangoda of the Southern Expressway from 19thkm to 34thkm

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

Extensive surface cracking and degradation of underlying structural layers have been observed in the stretch between Kottawa and Dodangoda of the Southern Expressway from 19thkm to 34thkm. Therefore, it has been identified that the said section of road needs to be rehabilitated, the base failures be rectified and a resurfacing be made. For this purpose, bids have been invited from qualified local firms, following the National Competitive Bidding procedure. After evaluating the bids received, the Standing High Level Procurement Committee has recommended awarding the contract to MAGA Engineering (Pvt) Ltd., the substantially responsive lowest bidder. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation, to award the relevant contract to MAGA Engineering (Private) Limited, based on the said recommendation, was approved by the Cabinet.
