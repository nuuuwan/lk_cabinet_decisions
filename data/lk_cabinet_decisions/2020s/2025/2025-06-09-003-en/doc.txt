Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Australian Federal Police and the Sri Lanka Police on Combating Transnational Crime and Police Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka and Australia share common interests in addressing the growing challenges posed by transnational crime including terrorism, cybercrime, drug trafficking, human trafficking, human smuggling and other forms of organized crimes. Therefore, it has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Australian Federal Police and the Sri Lanka Police with the objective of facilitating cooperation in the prevention, investigation, and prosecution of transnational organized crime, promoting coordinated law enforcement operations, strengthening existing cooperation frameworks, enhancing information and intelligence exchange and supporting capacity building and professional development initiatives. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding on Combating Transnational Crime and Police Cooperation between both parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
