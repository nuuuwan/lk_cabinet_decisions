Term contract for the Procurement of 05 shipments of Gas Oil  (0.05% M.S.) during the period from 2025.06.01 to 2025.11.30

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited from the registered suppliers of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation to purchase 05 shipments of Gas Oil (0.05% M.S.) during the period from 2025 06 01 to 2025 11 30. Seven (07) bids have been submitted for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy, to award the relevant procurement to M/s Trafigura Pte Ltd., Singapore, based on the recommendation of the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved 	by the Cabinet.
