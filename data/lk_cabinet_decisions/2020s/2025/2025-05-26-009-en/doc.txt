Project for organizing and empowering dairy farmers to enhance 	their productivity and improve the Dairy Value Chain

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation has proposed to implement a project for "Improving the Diary Value Chain by Enhancing the Dairy Farm Productivity" utilizing the US$ 10 million allocated for the development of the agriculture sector under the Inclusive Connectivity Development Project financed by the World Bank. The recommendation of the Department of National Planning has been received for the purpose. The proposed project is planned to be implemented in the 12 districts of Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, Kurunegala, Puttalam, Matale, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Gampaha, Ampara, Badulla, Galle and Hambantota with potential for dairy production. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation to implement the project of Improving the Dairy Value Chain by Enhancing the Productivity of Dairy Farms in those Districts, was approved by the Cabinet.
