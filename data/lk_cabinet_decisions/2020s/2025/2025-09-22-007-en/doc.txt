Completion of all works of the unfinished construction projects of the universities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Several projects implemented in the universities of Ruhuna, Rajarata, Sabaragamuwa, Wayamba, Sri Jayewardenepura and the Trincomalee campus of the Eastern University of Sri Lanka for construction of buildings and development of other infrastructure facilities required by the universities, have not been fully completed due to various obstacles encountered during the approved timelines of those projects. The remaining works of the projects where more than 75% has been completed need to be fully completed, in order to enroll more new students and improve academic facilities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, to fully complete the said construction works on priority basis, by managing the budgetary provisions to be allocated in the years 2026, 2027 and 2028, was approved by the Cabinet.
