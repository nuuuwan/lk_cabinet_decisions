Conducting a feasibility study on minimizing the traffic congestion at the Lotus Intersection and Surrounding Areas

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Lotus Roundabout in Colombo has become a critical traffic bottleneck, especially during peak hours, and it has been observed that this is causing a traffic congestion in the urban transport network. With the full operationalization of the Colombo Port City and the Port Access Elevated Highway and completion of the major ongoing developments in the Colombo metropolitan area, this problem is likely to exacerbate. Taking those factors into consideration, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to select a consultancy firm following the approved procurement process, to conduct a comprehensive feasibility study to develop sustainable solutions to mitigate traffic congestion around the Lotus Roundabout area and improve connectivity with key adjoining corridors, including the ramps of the Port Access Elevated Highway and the Marine Drive Extension, was approved by the Cabinet.
