Construction of a Water Treatment Plant with a capacity of 5000 cubic meters per day for the Avissawella Water Supply Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited following the National Competitive Bidding procedure for the construction of a Water Treatment Plant with a capacity of 5,000 cubic meters per day for the Avissawella water supply scheme. Ten bidders have submitted bids for the said procurement. Based on the recommendations of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee and the Procurement Appeal Board, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to award the contract for the construction of a Water Treatment Plant with a capacity 5,000 cubic meters per day for the Avissawella water supply system, to M/s Sierra Construction Limited, the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, was approved by the cabinet.
