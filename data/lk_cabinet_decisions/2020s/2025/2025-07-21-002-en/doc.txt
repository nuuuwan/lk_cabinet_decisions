Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding for Higher Education Collaboration under the Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been planned to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade of Hungary and the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education of Sri Lanka to further strengthen the mutual cooperation in the field of higher education of both countries by creating a knowledge based society. This Agreement, which is an extension to the Memorandum of Understanding entered into for the period of 2022‑2024, will provide the Sri Lankan students with Bachelor's Degree and Postgraduate Degree opportunities. Under the Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Programme, Sri lankan students will receive annually 20 scholarship opportunities as 08 for Bachelor's Degrees, 08 for Postgraduate Degrees and 04 for PhD's under the fields of Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, Medicine, Health Sciences and Agriculture as direct benefits of the Agreement. It has been planned to implement the proposed Memorandum of Understanding during the period from 2025-2027. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade of Hungary, was approved by the Cabinet.
