Memorandum of Understanding on the Establishment of a Political Consultations Mechanism between Guatemala and 	Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Guatemala and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism of Sri Lanka on the Establishment of a Political Consultations Mechanism. The proposed Agreement aims to promote, strengthen and expánd bilateral relations including political, economic, consular, cultural, scientific and technological cooperation. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the proposed MoU between the two countries for an initial period of five (05) years.
