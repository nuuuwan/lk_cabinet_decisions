Procurement for the supply of 2,200,000 vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection (Human) 30% Soluble 70% Isophane

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International competitive bids have been invited for the procurement of 2,200,000 vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection (Human) 30% Soluble 70% Isophane Injection, a drug used in the treatment of diabetic patients. Two bids have been received for the same. Accordingly, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the contract for the supply of 2,200,000 vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection (Human) 30% Soluble 70% Isophane Injection, to the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, M/s M.J. Biopharm Pvt. Ltd., India, for a sum of US$3.98 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
