Entering into Memoranda of Understanding on Educational Collaboration between Sri Lankan Universities and Foreign Universities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to sign the following Memoranda of Understanding between the Sri Lankan Universities and Foreign Universities with a view to establishing higher education collaborations with Foreign Universities to elevate Sri Lankan Universities to higher positions in world ranking indices and to promote internationalization, was approved by the Cabinet. 

•	Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Kelaniya and the Polessky State University of the Republic of Belarus, valid for a period of 05 years.

•	Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Kelaniya and the Vladimir State University of the Russian Federation, valid for a period of 03 years.

•	Memorandum of Understanding between the Department of Polymer Science, Faculty of Applied Sciences of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the Petroleum and Petrochemical College, Chulalongkorn University of the Kingdom of Thailand.
