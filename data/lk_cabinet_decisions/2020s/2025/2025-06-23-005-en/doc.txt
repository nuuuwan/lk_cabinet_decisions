Construction of a New Three Storied Building for the Thurstan College, Colombo

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A Report submitted by the Building Experts of the University of Moratuwa has indicated that an old three-storied building at the Thurstan College, Colombo is unsuitable for student use and should be demolished. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to construct a new three-storied building in lieu of the said building, was approved by the Cabinet.
