Implementation of the Sustainable Tourism Sector Development Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Asian Development Bank has agreed to provide a loan of US$ 100 million to implement a Sector Development Programme in the tourism sector. The said loan consists of two (02) components, a policy component of US$ 70 million and an investment component. Under the investment component, it is expected to carry out development activities to enhance the tourism potential and carring capacity of the tourist destinations of Trincomalee and Dambulla (including the Sigiriya area), control tourist congestion, extend the length of stay and increase contribution to the regional economy through the tourism industry. The Ministry of Urban Development, Construction and Housing will function as the executing agency and the implementing agency for the projects falling under the said investment component. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to sign the relevant Loan Agreements to obtain the proposed loan, was approved by the Cabinet.
