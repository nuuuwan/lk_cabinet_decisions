Obtaining the service of a Consulting Company for improvement of infrastructure facilities of the Head Office and Regional Centres of the Meteorology Department under the Climatic Resilience Multi-Phase Programme Approach

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Climatic Resilience Multi-Phase Programmatic Approach is being implemented by the Government of Sri Lanka since October 2021 with the assistance of the World Bank. It is an initiation of 09 years which runs until 2030, and a loan amounting to US$92 million has been obtained from the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) and the World Bank for the implementation of its first phase (2021-2026). Improving the infrastructure facilities of the Headquarters and Regional Centers of the Department of Meteorology by improving the forecasting and early warning systems of high-impact weather, floods and landslides has been identified as one of the components of the first phase of the project.  Competitive bids have been invited to select a Consulting Company to obtain necessary consultancy servìces for the implementation of the said component. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation, to award the contract for the provision of the said consultancy servìce, to M/s Green Tech Consultants (Pvt) Ltd., based on the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Consultative Procurement Committee on the received bids, was approved by the Cabinet.
