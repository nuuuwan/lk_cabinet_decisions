Implementation of Phase I of the Eppawala, Giribawa, Rajanganaya and Nochchiyagama Integrated Water Supply Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been decided to initiate the Eppawala, Giribawa, Rajanganaya and Nochchiyagama Integrated Water Supply Project as a solution to the spread of chronic kidney disease in the border areas of the Anuradhapura District and the Kurunegala District. Under the said project, it had been initially planned to cover 134 Grama Niladhari Divisions in 09 Divisional Secretariat Divisions, and considering the health risks and lack of safe drinking water in the affected areas, it has been restructured as the Eppawala, Giribawa, Rajanganaya and Nochchiyagama Integrated Water Supply Project – Phase I. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to implement the Eppawala, Giribawa, Rajanganaya and Nochchiyagama Integrated Water Supply Project - Phase I, during the period of 2026-2029, was approved by the Cabinet.
