Revision of the new National Water Resources Policy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The new Government's manifesto has emphasized the need to conserve all water sources in the country, activate the Cascade system, properly study and nourish the river basins and regulate water consumption for various uses in order to ensure safe water consumption. Therefore, it has become a timely requirement to implement a Government policy that nourishes and protects all water sources in the country which provide space for the safe use of water resources for the present and future generations. Various parties have also pointed out that the currently effective National Water Resources Policy should be further revised. Accordingly, taking into account a proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister, the Cabinet decided to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee comprising of the following Ministers to re-review the currently effective National Water Resources Policy and submit appropriate recommendations for revising the said policy, adhering to the policy of the present Government:* Hon. K.D. Lal Kantha,  Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation;* Hon. Sunil Handunneththi,  Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development;* Hon. Anura Karunathilaka,  Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing;* Hon. (Eng.) Kumara Jayakody,  Minister of Energy;and* Hon. (Dr.) Dammika Patabendi,  Minister of Environment.
