Contract for CFM 56-5B Engine repair and the Contract for maintenance, repair and operating overhaul of SriLankan Airlines Ltd.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-02-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Limited International Competitive Bids have been invited for the Contract for the CFM 56-5B Engine repair and maintenance, repair and operational overhaul of SriLankan Airlines Limited. 7 bidders have submitted bids for the same. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to award the said Contract to Lufthansa Technik (LHT)/Hamburg, the lowest substantially responsive bidder, for a period of four (04) years, based on the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
