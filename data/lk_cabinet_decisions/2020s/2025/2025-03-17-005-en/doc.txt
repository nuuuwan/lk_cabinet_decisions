Providing Financial Fertilizer Subsidy to farmers cultivating in paddy lands for the Yala season 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Taking into consideration a proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation, the Cabinet has decided to grant approval for the provision of financial fertilizer subsidy to farmers cultivating paddy in paddy lands and to farmers cultivating other field crops in paddy lands in the Yala season 2025, as follows:

* 	Rs. 25,000 per hectare for paddy cultivation in paddy lands in the Yala season 2025, up to a maximum of 02 hectares.

* 	Rs. 15,000 per hectare for cultivation of other field crops on paddy lands as decided at the Kanna meetings, up to a maximum of 02 hectares.
