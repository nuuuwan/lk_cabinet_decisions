Recommendations of the Committee appointed to review the recruitment process and cadre management in the public service

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-02-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Second Report of the Officials' Committee appointed under the Chairmanship of the Secretary to the Prime Minister, to review and make recommendations on the recruitment process and cadre management in the public service has been submitted. Recommendations have been made through the Report to make recruitments for 2,003 vacancies out of 4,987 vacancies existing in Institutions falling under 11 Ministries and in 05 Provincial Councils. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to implement the said recommendations, was approved by the Cabinet.
