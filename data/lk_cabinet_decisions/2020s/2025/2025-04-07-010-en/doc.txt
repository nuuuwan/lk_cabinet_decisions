Amending the National Minimum Wage of Workers Act, No.3 of  2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The 2025 Budget proposals have proposed to increase the wages of private sector employees in parallel with the salary increase of public sector employees. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Labour to revise the National Minimum Monthly Wage and the National Minimum Daily Wage as mentioned below and to follow other relevant legal measures related thereto,was approved by the Cabinet:

	*	to increase the National Minimum Monthly Wage by 	Rs.9,500 from Rs.17,500 to Rs.27,000, with effect from	2025.04.01.

	*	to increase the National Minimum Daily Wage by 	Rs.380 from 	Rs.700 to Rs.1,080, with effect from 	2025.04.01.

	*	to increase the National Minimum Monthly Wage by 	Rs.3,000 from Rs.27,000 to Rs.30,000, with effect from	2026.01.01.

	*	to increase the National Minimum Daily Wage by Rs.120 from 	Rs.1,080 to Rs.1,200, with effect from 2026.01.01.
