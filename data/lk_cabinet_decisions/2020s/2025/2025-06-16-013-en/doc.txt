Public Commercial Business Management Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-16 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025-04-28 to introduce a new Act titled Public Commercial Business Management Act to operate the state-owned business entities with a sound management and by introducing necessary reforms and to appoint a Cabinet Committee to review the initial draft prepared for drafting the said new Act, make appropriate amendments and further improve the said draft. The said Cabinet Committee has reviewed the initial draft and submitted the Committee Report containing its recommendations. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare the Public Commercial Business Management Bill, based on the recommendations of that Report, was approved by the Cabinet.
