Implementation of the “Seasonal Food Package” Distribution Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In keeping with the proposal made by the Hon. President under the Budget 2025 to provide a package of essential food commodities at a concessionary price during the Sinhala and Tamil New Year season, it has been planned to provide a "Seasonal Food Package" containing essential food commodities worth Rs.5,000 at a concessionary price of Rs.2,500. For this purpose, it is expected to select suitable beneficiaries from among the 812,753 applicants who have submitted new applications expecting Aswesuma benefits. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation and the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development to take necessary steps to provide the food package to the beneficiaries through the Lanka Sathosa outlet network and COOPFED outlets located throughout the country from 2025-04-01 to 2025-04-13, was approved by the Cabinet.
