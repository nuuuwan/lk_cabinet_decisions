Local assembly of a Light Train Set with three coaches

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Most of the trains currently in use are used for passenger transport in suburban areas and only a few rail buses are currently in operation to meet the transport needs of the rural community. However, practical problems have arisen in operating rail buses according to the existing train schedule. It has also been identified that it is not economically viable to deploy train sets used for operation in suburban areas to rural areas due to the lack of sufficient number of passengers. Therefore, it has been planned to introduce light train sets with three coaches for railway lines in areas such as Puttalam, Batticaloa and Mihintale which can be operated at the rural level, equipped with an electronic control system powered by a diesel engine mounted on the coaches and with other modern facilities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to take necessary steps to locally assemble a light train set with three coaches with a capacity of about 200 - 240 passengers and sufficient cargo space to meet the transportation needs of the rural community, was approved by the Cabinet.
