Implementation of Mental Health Awareness and Counseling Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

A high rate of suicide, increase in drug use and increased emergence of psychosocial issues, especially among adolescents and young adults, among Sri Lankans, have been observed. Accordingly, as per a budget proposal of 2025, provisions amounting to Rs. 250 million have been allocated to implement a special programme to address mental health issues. Under that, the Ministry of Health and Mass Media has proposed to implement a medium-term programme aiming to expand awareness and counseling services for school children and adolescents. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to implement the proposed programme in collaboration with the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, the National Dangerous Drugs Control Board, the National Institute of Mental Health, provincial and district health services, hospitals and other relevant institutions, was approved by the Cabinet.
