Signing an Action Plan between the Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy and the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet, at its meeting held on 2023‑05‑29, has granted approval to extend the Memorandum of Understanding signed in the year 2016 with the objective of formally continuing the scientific collaboration between the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute and the Sri Lanka Council for Agricultural Research Policy, for a further period of five years. As per the said Memorandum of Understanding, a consensus has been reached to sign an Action Plan for a period of five years between the two parties. It was decided to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet to the proposal submitted by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to sign the said Action Plan.
