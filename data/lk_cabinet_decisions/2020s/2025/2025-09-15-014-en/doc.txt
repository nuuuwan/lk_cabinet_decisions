Submission of the Appropriation Bill to Parliament - 2026

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025-09-08 to publish the Appropriation Bill for the financial year 2026 in the Government Gazette and present the same in Parliament. Accordingly, the following Programme of Parliamentary Sittings for the Appropriation Bill for the year 2026, prepared by the Office of the Leader of the House of Parliament was presented to the Cabinet by the Hon. Prime Minister and the concurrence of the Cabinet was granted for the same.  *	First Reading of the Appropriation Bill - 2025-09-26  *	Second Reading of the Appropriation Bill (Budget Speech) - 2025-11-07  *	Debate on the Second Reading - 2025-11-08 to 2025-11-14  *	Debate on the Third Reading (Committee Stage of the Budget) - 2025-11-15 to 2025-12-05  *	Division on the Third Reading - 2025-12-05
