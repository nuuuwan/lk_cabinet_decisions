Establishment of Diplomatic Relations with Belize

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted previously to establish diplomatic relations with Latin American countries of Guatemala, Suriname, Belize, Honduras, Nicaragua, Antigua & Barbuda, Barbados, Dominica, Grenada, Haiti, St. Kits & Nevis, St. Lucia and St. Vincent and the Grenadines. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the Joint Communique on the Establishment of Diplomatic Relations between the Government of Sri lanka and the Government of Belize in order to enhance the friendly relations and cooperation in the political, socio-economic and cultural fields for mutual benefits of the two countries, was approved by the cabinet.
