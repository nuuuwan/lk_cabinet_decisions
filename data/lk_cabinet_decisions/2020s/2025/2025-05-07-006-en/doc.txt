Implementation of High Priority Transmission Development Projects under Full Mutual Reliance Framework of the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The World Bank and the Asian Development Bank have expressed their willingness to provide financial assistance under the Full Mutual Reliance Framework for the implementation of a series of High Priority Transmission Development projects identified under the Long-Term Transmission Plan 2023 - 2032 prepared by the Ceylon Electricity Board. Ten (10) projects have been identified for implementation under the proposed Full Mutual Reliance Framework. The World Bank plans to invest US$ 40 million with a guarantee facility of US$ 10 million to be provided by the International Development Association and the Asian Development Bank US$ 30 million. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Energy, to pursue action pertaining to obtaining an investment package of US$ 60 million for the development of the proposed grid substation under the Full Mutual Reliance Framework jointly offered by the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank, and to obtain the De - risking Guarantee Facility of US$ 10 million sponsored by the International Development Association.
