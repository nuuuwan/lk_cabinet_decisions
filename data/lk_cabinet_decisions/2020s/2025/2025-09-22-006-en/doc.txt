Creating a 'Government Super App' to enable the public to obtain the Public services through a single web application

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been observed that the current fragmented approach to Public Service delivery which requires disparate systems, multiple authentication processes and repeated data entry across various Government departments cause significant inconvenience to citizens. It has also been estimated that this causes an economic loss of over Rs.500 million annually. Therefore, it has been identified that the development of the 'Government SuperApp' covering a wide range of Public Services to citizens through a single application, in line with the Sri Lanka's Digital Economy Blueprint, as a strategic priority. This Project will develop a digital platform that will enable 14 million citizens of Sri Lanka of the age of 18 years and 2 million annual visitors to access Public Services seamlessly through a single interface, consolidating all Public Services into a single, user-friendly mobile and web application. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as Minister of Digital Economy to implement the 'Government SuperApp' Project in 02 phases at a total cost of Rs. 500 million during the period 2025-2026, was approved by the Cabinet.
