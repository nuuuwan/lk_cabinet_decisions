Institutionalizing the Results - based Management in the Public Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Based on the policies and objectives of the present Government, the Department of Project Management and Monitoring of the General Treasury has prepared 'Ministerial Results Frameworks' for the period 2025-2029 for 22 Line Ministries and institutions falling thereunder in order to institutionalize results-based management in the Public Sector. The Ministerial Results Frameworks will help in timely achievement of development goals within the respective scope in line with the hierarchical responsibilities of each Ministry, thereby ensuring the timely delivery of the desired outputs and benefits to the people, strengthening the linkages between economic sectors, and enhancing public confidence in public investments. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to institutionalize Results-based Management in the Public Sector and to integrate the Ministerial Results Frameworks into the Annual Action Plans of each Ministry and the strategic phases of the budget process, and to take necessary steps to achieve the desired goals, was approved by the Cabinet.
