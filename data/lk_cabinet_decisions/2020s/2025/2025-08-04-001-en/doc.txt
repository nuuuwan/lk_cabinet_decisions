Statement on Financial Performance of the Government for the Second Quarter 2025s

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- As per section 53(1) of the Public Financial Management Act No.44 of 2024, the Minister in charge of the subject of Finance should submit a statement on financial performance including revenue and expenditure of the Government to the Cabinet at least on quarterly basis within a period of not less than 45 days after completion of each quarter. Accordingly, the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, having submitted the Report on Financial Performance prepared including cumulative revenue and expenditure reported by all Special Spending Units, Ministries, Departments, and District Secretariats for the second quarter ended on 2025‑06‑30, based on the classifications stated in Annual Budget Estimate for the year 2025, and the Cabinet Noted the said Report.
