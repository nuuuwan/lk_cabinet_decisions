Procurement of Surgical Gauze required for the Medical 	Supplies Division for the year 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Based on the Government’s policy of uplifting local small scale manufacturers, surgical gauze required for the Government hospitals is purchased from local manufacturers under a regular ration scheme. The requirement of surgical gauze for the Medical Supplies Division for the year 2025 has been estimated as 30 million meters. The Surgical Gauze Ration Determination Committee has recommended to purchase 30 million meters of surgical gauze for the year 2025 from 282 registered local manufacturers. The Pricing Committee has approved the fixed price of one meter of surgical gauze so purchased as Rs 68.97. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to purchase 30 million meters of surgical gauze for the year 2025 from 282 registered local manufacturers of the Medical Supplies Division at the said fixed price, based on the recommendation of the Standing High Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
