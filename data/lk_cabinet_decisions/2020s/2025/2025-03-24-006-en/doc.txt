Amending the Land Acquisition Act, No.9 of 1950

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Owing to the long time taken in following the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act No.9 of 1950, which is currently in force, approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2021 01 11 to amend the said Act to suit to the present day requirements, so that the time taken for the land acquisition procedure could be reduced, lands can be acquired under a simple procedure and compensation could be paid under a uniform methodology. The initial draft for the purpose has been prepared and the Legal Draftsman has informed that the policy approval of the Cabinet need to be obtained to finalize the preparation of the said Bill. Accordingly, taking into account the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation, the Cabinet decided to instruct the Legal Draftsman to expeditiously prepare the said Bill.
