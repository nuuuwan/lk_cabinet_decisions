Procurement for the supply of 400,000 vials of Desferrioxamine Mesylate for Injection BP, 500mg

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited following the International Competitive Bidding Method for the purchase of 400,000 vials of Desferrioxamine Mesylate Injection BP 500 mg, which is used to remove excess iron from the body in anemia or thalassemia patients. Two bidders have submitted bids for the purpose. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to M/s. ABC Pharma Services (Pvt) Ltd., Sri Lanka, the sole responsive bidder, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
