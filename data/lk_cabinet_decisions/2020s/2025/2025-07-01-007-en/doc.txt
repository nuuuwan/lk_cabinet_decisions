Installation of a Public Debt Management Software System

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024-10-14 to obtain a bid from the Commonwealth Secretariat for the purchase of a  Debt Management Software System required for the Public Debt Management by following the direct procurement procedure. Accordingly, a bid has been submitted by the Commonwealth Secretariat. The Bid Evaluation Committee and the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee have recommended that the contract for the installation of the Commonwealth Meridian Software System be awarded to the Commonwealth Secretariat at an annual maintenance fee of 12,500 GBP. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to award the contract for the installation of the said software and related services in the Department of External Resources, was approved by the Cabinet.
