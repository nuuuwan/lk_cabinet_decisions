Amendment of the date of imposition of Value Added Tax (VAT) on the supply of services through electronic platforms by non-resident suppliers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, No.4 of 2025 has made the supply of digital services by a non-resident subject to VAT with effect from 2025-10-01. However, non-resident digital service suppliers have requested additional time to prepare for compliance with the levying of VAT on digital services, citing several practical reasons. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to amend the effective date of the relevant tax to 2026-04-01 instead of 2025-10-01 and to incorporate the relevant amending provisions in the Bill to amend the Value Added Tax Act, which is already being drafted, was approved by the Cabinet.
