Formulating a National Policy and Action Plan on Reconciliation and Coexistence

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- As per the provisions of the Office for National Unity and Reconciliation Act, No.1 of 2024, the Office for National Unity and Reconciliation has been vested with power to formulate and implement a National Policy on Reconciliation and Coexistence and a National Action Plan. Accordingly, the Roadmap for Formulating National Policy and Action Plan on Reconciliation and Coexistence has been prepared by the Office for National Unity and Reconciliation.  The proposed National Policy and Action Plan seeks to achieve shared national aspirations such as strengthening institutional mechanisms, restoring public confidence, promoting inter-ethnic and inter-religious dialogue, and empowering youth and women as agents of peace. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to prepare a National Policy and Action Plan on Reconciliation and Coexistence for the period from 2025 - 2029 through a multi-stake holder approach, to establish an Inter-Ministerial Task Force comprising representatives of relevant Ministries and Line Agencies, and to obtain the necessary technical, logistical, financial and administrative support from the United Nations in Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
