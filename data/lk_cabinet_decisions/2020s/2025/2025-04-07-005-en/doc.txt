Procurement process for the purchase of Pre-Printed Polycarbonate 	Cards for the issuance of National Identity Cards

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Department for Registration of Persons has commenced using Smart Cards for the issuance of National Identity Cards in the year 2017. Under the new method introduced by the Electronic National Identity Card (e-NIC) project to be implemented in the future, an electronic National Identity Card has to be issued to all Sri Lankan citizens who have completed 15 years of age. It has been identified that there is a need of around 17 million cards to re-register Sri Lankan citizens and issue a new identity card. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy to purchase 15 million Polycarbonate Cards, following the international competitive bidding method, to meet the said requirement, was approved by the Cabinet.
