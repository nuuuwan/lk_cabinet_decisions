Completion of the balance work of the Kohuwala Flyover and recommencement of the construction work of the Gatambe Flyover

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The project for the construction of Kohuwala and Gatambe flyovers have been implemented with funds received under the Framework Agreement for Financial Cooperation signed by Sri Lanka with the Government of Hungary. Following the suspension of funding by the Exim Bank of Hungary, due to the economic crisis which prevailed in the country at that time, the construction of the Kohuwala flyover has now been completed under the contractor's funds and opened for traffic. However, pedestrians have to face difficulties as the at-grade roads have not been completed. Similarly, the construction work of the Gatambe flyover also remains suspended. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to complete the unfinished at-grade roads of the Kohuwala flyover and to resume the construction of the Gatambe flyover using local funds through the current contractor, was approved by the Cabinet.
