Implementation of the 'Mission Olympics' programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports has planned to implement the 'Mission Olympics 2028 - Journey to the Rings' programme to identify sportspersons who have demonstrated outstanding performance and those who may have Olympic aspirations in the future and provide them with the necessary financial and other facilities. Through this programme, sportspersons with the potential and talent to achieve international achievements in sports and win medals at the Olympic Games are to be identified and directed to long-term formal local and foreign specialized training. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports to implement the 'Mission Olympic' programme, enabling to sign contracts with such sportspersons up to the upcoming Los Angeles Olympics 2028 and to provide financial and other support for training, was approved by the Cabinet.
