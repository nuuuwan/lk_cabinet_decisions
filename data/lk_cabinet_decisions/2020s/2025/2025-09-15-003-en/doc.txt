Construction of a new Three Storied Building for the Primary Section of the Maliyadwa College, Kurunegala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- At present, 4,617 students are studying in Maliyadeva College, Kurunegala, which was established in 1888. The primary section of the College is housed in three old single-storey buildings with tiled roofs, and the facilities available in the said buildings are inadequate for the students. Therefore, the need for a child-friendly building with modern facilities for the Primary Section has been identified. Accordingly, a new three-storey building with 24 classrooms, an activity room, three teachers' rest rooms and sanitary facilities on each floor has been planned for the said College. It is a construction project included in the approved National Investment Programme for 2026-2030. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, to implement the project of the construction of the said building during the period 2026-2028, under an estimated cost of Rs. 392 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
