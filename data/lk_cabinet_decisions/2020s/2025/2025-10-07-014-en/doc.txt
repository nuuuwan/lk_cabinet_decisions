Providing Financial Subsidy for fertilizer for farmers cultivating paddy lands during the Maha Season 2025/26

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Rs.33,500 million has been allocated for the year 2025 for providing the financial subsidy for fertilizer and it has been estimated that additional provisions of Rs.05 billion will be required to grant the financial subsidy for fertilizer taking into consideration the total extent of lands of paddy and other field crops estimated to be cultivated for the Maha Season 2025/26.  Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation and the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development to secure additional provisions of Rs.05 billion for the payment of financial subsidy for fertilizer based on the recommendation of the Food Policy and Security Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
