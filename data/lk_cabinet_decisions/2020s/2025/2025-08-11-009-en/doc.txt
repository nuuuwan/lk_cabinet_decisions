'Healthy Food - Happy Life - සුවදායී දිවියකට සෞඛ්‍යවත්/සුරක්ෂිත ආහාර' -The National Media Drive

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government has prioritized its direct intervention concerning food-related diseases and the lack of compliance with regard to the legal  safety guidelines associated thereto in the present day society. Accordingly, the Ministry of Health and Mass Media and Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation intends to implement the National Media Drive, 'Healthy Food - Happy Life - සුවදායී දිවියකට සෞඛ්‍යවත්/සුරක්ෂිත ආහාර' in order to launch a health promotion programme on the nutrition of Sri Lankans. This programme has been planned to implement in liaison with women, school children, public and private sector institutions associated with food safety, doctors, nutritionists, public health inspectors, farmers and cooks/chefs. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to implement the said National Media Drive by the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation under the supervision of the Ministry of Health and Mass Media, was approved by the Cabinet.
