Appointment to the Post of Commissioner General of Inland Revenue

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mrs. W.A.S. Chandrasekara, who served as the Commissioner General of Inland Revenue, has retired from the Public Service from 2025 02 28. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to appoint with immediate effect, Mrs. R.P.H. Fernando, Special Grade Officer of the Inland Revenue Service, presently serving as a Deputy Commissioner General of the Department of Inland Revenue, to the Post of Commissioner General of Inland Revenue which has fallen vacant, was approved by the Cabinet.
