Providing underutilized lands/properties to suitable investors for optimal use

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A sum of Rs. 250 million has been allocated from the Budget 2025 to identify State owned underutilized lands and their suitability and to direct them towards private investments, including small and medium-scale enterprises. Accordingly, a project proposal has been prepared jointly by the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation and the Ministry of Plantation and Community Infrastructure. According to the proposal, the clear,  underutilized lands/properties belonging to the institutions under the purview of those two ministries, are expected to be provided for investment opportunities in the fields of plantation and agriculture, plantation-related tourism promotion, livestock development, hydropower generation, solar energy generation, factory-related manufacturing, freshwater fisheries, and other plantation-related economic innovations. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Plantations and Community Infrastructure to proceed with the proposed project, was approved by the Cabinet.
