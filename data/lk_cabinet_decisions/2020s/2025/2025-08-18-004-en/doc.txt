Project for the development of the selected locations within the premises of the Pelwatte and Sevanagala Sugar Factories with the aim of creating tourist attraction

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Based on the recommendations of a feasibility study conducted by the National Agency for Public-Private Partnership, 04 selected locations within the Pelwatte Sugar Factory premises have been identified for development as places with tourist attractions, with special focus on enhancing Eco Tourism, Agro Tourism, Wellness Tourism and Conservation based Tourism. Similarly, several locations within the Sewanagala Sugar Factory premises have also been identified as locations that can be developed with the aim of creating tourist attraction. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to implement 02 projects under the Public Private Partnership Method to transform selected locations within the premises of the Pelwatte Sugar Factory and the Sevanagala Sugar Factory owned by Lanka Sugar Company (Pvt) Ltd, into places with tourist attraction, was approved by the Cabinet.
