Printing School Textbooks/Modules required for the year 2026

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted on 2025-06-16 to have 40% of the required school textbooks/modules for the year 2026 printed by the State Printing Corporation. In addition, 27.12 million copies of 366 types of textbooks/modules need to be printed. Accordingly, bids have been invited following the National Competitive Bidding Procedure, and 29 printing institutions have submitted bids for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to have 25.49 million copies of 362 relevant textbooks/modules printed by the said 29 institutions at a cost of Rs.3,491.47 million, based on the recommendation of the Standing High Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
