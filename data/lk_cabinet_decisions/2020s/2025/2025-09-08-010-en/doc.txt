Establishment of the Dambulla Industrial Estate

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Under the "Regional Industrial Estate Development Programme" implemented by the Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, 33 Industrial Estates have been constructed across the island as of now. Similarly, development work is currently underway in 07 new Industrial Estates. The Dambulla Industrial Estate has been identified as a dedicated Industrial Estate for food manufacturing and food packaging industries. For this purpose, a land in extent of 64 acres 02 roods 10.2 perches has been identified in the village of Elen Egodayaya, in the Dambulla Divisional Secretary's Division. It has been planned to develop 24 acres of land as the first phase for the proposed Industrial Estate and the remaining land in the second phase. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to allocate Rs.750 million from the provisions of the year 2025, for the development of the infrastructure facilities of the Dambulla Industrial Park and proceed with its future activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
