Appointment to the Post of Director General of Commerce

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Post of Director General of the Department of Commerce, which functions under the Ministry of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Co-operative Development, is currently vacant. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and    Co-operative Development to appoint Ms. Ameina Shafi Mohin, the senior‑most Officer holding the Post of Director of Commerce in the said Department, to act as the Director General of Commerce on full-time basis, with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
