Memoranda of Understanding between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Turkey on Agricultural Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet, at its meeting held on 2021.02.15, has granted approval to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on Agricultural Cooperation between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Turkey, taking into consideration the benefits for the development of Sri Lanka's agriculture through the establishment of mutual cooperation with the Republic of Turkey, which uses advanced technologies in the agricultural sector. Nevertheless, the relevant Memorandum of Understanding has not been signed yet. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation to obtain the approval of the present Cabinet to sign the said Memorandum of Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
