Presenting the Presidents' Entitlements (Repeal) Bill in Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet has granted approval on 2025-06-16 to prepare a Bill to repeal the Presidents' Entitlements Act, No.4 of 1986, enabling to abolish the special privileges granted to former Presidents and their families, as stated in the Government manifesto 'A thriving nation - A beautiful Life'. Accordingly, the Presidents' Entitlements (Repeal) Bill has been prepared by the Legal Draftsman for that purpose and the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter to present the same in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
