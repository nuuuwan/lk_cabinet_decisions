Submission of Order made under the Reciprocal Recognition, Registration and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act, No.49 of 2024, for approval of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Reciprocal Recognition, Registration and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act, No. 49 of 2024 makes provisions for the recognition, registration and enforcement of judgments of Courts of other countries in Sri Lanka. Accordingly, an Order made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration in accordance with the provisions of the relevant Act and published through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2429/51 dated 2025 03 28, specifies the foreign countries which should have recognition, registration and enforcement of judgments. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to submit the said Order for approval of Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
