Five Year National Action Plan on National Policy on Child Protection

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2019-10-29 for the "National Policy on Child Protection" prepared in accordance with the provisions of the National Child Protection Authority Act, No.50 of 1998. In order to implement the said National Policy, the "Five-Year National Action Plan for the National Child Protection Policy" has been prepared, taking into account the views and suggestions of the parties working on it, covering ten (10) subject areas related to child protection. This Five-Year National Action Plan has been prepared with a multi-sectoral approach in line with the policy commitments of the National Policy Framework for "Secure world for children - Creative future generation". Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Women and Child Affairs to implement the said National Action Plan, was approved by the Cabinet.
