Removal of the concessions granted in purchasing houses from the 'Viyathpura' Housing Project by the Members of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022‑07‑04 to provide 101 Housing Units from the Viyathpura Housing Complex constructed by the Urban Development Authority, for a period of one year on rent basis to the then Members of Parliament who lost their homes due to the unrest that arose in the country on 2022‑05‑09. Subsequently, those Members of Parliament have submitted requests to provide them with the opportunity to purchase those houses under a concessionary scheme. Accordingly, the Cabinet has granted its approval on 2024‑07‑15 to provide a single housing unit which is sold to general people for a minimum value of Rs.15.5 million and maximum value of Rs.22 million at a reduced minimum value of a housing unit to Rs.13.34 million and maximum value to Rs.18.21 million, to Members of Parliament under a concessional scheme, and initially charge 25% from the sale price and recover the remaining amount from them over a period of 15 years at an interest rate of 10% per annum. Twenty Nine (29) former Members of Parliament have deposited 25% of the sale price as of 2024‑12‑31 to purchase houses under the said scheme. However, due to this concessional scheme, the Urban Development Authority had to incur a loss of about Rs.92.126 million and has not been able to cover the costs incurred. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to abolish the concessional scheme approved as referred to above with immediate effect and provide the opportunity for the 29 relevant Members of Parliament to purchase the said houses with the same method and prices applicable to the general public if they so agree, was approved by the Cabinet.
