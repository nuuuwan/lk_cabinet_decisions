Signing of a Tripartite Memorandum of Understanding among 	the South Asian Centre for Teacher Development (SACTD), 	UNESCO South Asia Regional Office (SARO) and Indira Gandhi 	National Open University (IGNOU)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The South Asian Centre for Teacher Development has been established as an autonomous and self-financed institution. The UNESCO Executive Board has recommended that the functions of the said Centre should be upgraded to promote its work and establish partnerships with other institutions and organizations in the region. Accordingly, it has been proposed to sign a Tripartite Agreement among the South Asian Centre for Teacher Development, the UNESCO South Asia Regional Office and the Indira Gandhi National Open University aiming to introduce a new high quality training programme on Media and Information Literacy thereby further developing regional cooperation and strengthening national and regional action plans, including the targets outlined in the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the proposed draft MoU. The approval of the Cabinet was granted for the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to sign the said Tripartite Memorandum of Understanding.
