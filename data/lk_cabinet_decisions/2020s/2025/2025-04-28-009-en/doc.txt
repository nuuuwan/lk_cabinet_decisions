Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute of Sri Lanka and the Diplomatic Academy of Viet Nam "

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- An agreement has been reached between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute operating under the the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism of Sri Lanka and the Diplomatic Academy of Viet Nam with the objective of establishing institutional relations with a view to maintaining mutually useful cooperation in the areas of common interest in order to bring together innovative ideas and opinions in the bilateral, regional and international affairs closely connected with each other in the complex contemporary world. It is proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding for this purpose and facilitation of sharing expertise, exchange of diplomats, academics, officials, experts and researchers, as well as the organizing and exchanging expertise related to programmes of study and research, various courses, seminars, other academic activities and training in the field of diplomacy is expected through this. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties, which will be valid for a period of three (03) years, was approved by the Cabinet.
