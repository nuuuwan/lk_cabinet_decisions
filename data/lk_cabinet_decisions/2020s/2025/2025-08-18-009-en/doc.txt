Urgent rehabilitation and repair of Cold Storages of the Ceylon Fisheries Corporation to ensure the food security and price stability of fish and sea food products

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Although the cold storage facility in Modara owned by the Ceylon Fisheries Corporation is considered to be one of the largest cold storage facilities in South Asia, due to lack of proper maintenance and management weaknesses in the past, its cold storages have now become inoperative. Therefore, it has been planned to urgently repair and renovate a cold storage with a capacity of 200 MT and a deep freezer with a capacity of 2.5 MT, so that they can be put into use. It has been estimated that the said repair will cost Rs.86 million. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources to implement the cold storage renovation activities by managing the provisions already allocated to the Ministry of Fisheries, Aquatic and Marine Resources for the year 2025, was approved by the Cabinet.
