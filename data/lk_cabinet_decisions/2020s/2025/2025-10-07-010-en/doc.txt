Procurement of 40,000 Vials of Liposomal form of Amphotericin B 50 mg Vial

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International competitive bids have been invited for the procurement of 40,000 vials of the above-mentioned vaccine, which is used to treat life‑threatening fungal infections, including cryptococcal meningitis and visceral leishmaniasis, particularly in people with compromised immune systems. Four (04) bids have been received for the same. Based on the recommendation of the Standing High Level Procurement Committee regarding the relevant bids, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to Bharat Serums and Vaccines Limited, India (Manufacturer: Bharat Serums and Vaccines Limited., India), the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, for a total cost of US$680,000.00, was approved by the Cabinet.
