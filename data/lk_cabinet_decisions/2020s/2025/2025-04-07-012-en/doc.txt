Amendment to the Community Based Corrections Act, No.46 of 1999

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024.01.24 to amend the Community Based Corrections Act, No.46 of 1999. The Attorney General has submitted several amendment proposals for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman, accordingly. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare the final Bill to amend the Community Based Corrections Bill incorporating the said proposed amendments as well, was approved by the Cabinet.
