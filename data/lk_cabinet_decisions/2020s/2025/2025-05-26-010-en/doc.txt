Awarding the term contract for the Procurement of four (04) shipments of Murban Crude Oil during the period from 2025.09.01 to 2025.12.31

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited to procure 04 shipments of Murban Crude Oil for the period from 2025 09 01 to 2025 12 31. Seven (07) bids have been submitted for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the 	Minister of Energy, to award the relevant procurement to M/s BB Energy Asia Pte Ltd., Singapore, based on the recommendation of the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
