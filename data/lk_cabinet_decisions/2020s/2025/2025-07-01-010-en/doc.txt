Selection of Consultancy Firms for the supervision activities of the Housing Programme implemented for low-income families under Government of China Assistance Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Urban Development Authority has invited Expressions of Interest to select a consultancy firm under 03 packages for residential site supervision consultancy services of 1996 housing units constructed for low-income families under the Government of China Assistance Programme. 06 institutions have submitted  Expressions of Interest for the same and 03 institutions out of them have been qualified to submit Request for Proposals. The proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to award the relevant contract for supplying consultancy services as follows, based on the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Consultants Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet. Package 1 - Awarding the Consultancy Service Contract of the Project for the construction of 575 housing units in Moratuwa and 108 housing units in Kottawa, to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau.
 
Package 2 - Awarding the Consultancy Service Contract of the Project for the construction of 586 housing units in Dematagoda and 112 housing units in Maharagama,  to the State Engineering Corporation.

Package 3 - Awarding the Consultancy Service Contract of the Project for the construction of 615 housing units in Peliyagoda  to the State  Engineering Corporation.
