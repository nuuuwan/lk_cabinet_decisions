Providing compensation to the sugarcane farmers whose crops were damaged due to burning of sugarcane plantations at the Sevanagala Sugar Factory

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been reported that a group of people had set fire to the sugarcane plantations of the Sevanagala Sugar Factory owned by Lanka Sugar Company (Pvt) Ltd. on 16th, 20th, 21st, 22nd, 24th August and 04th and 05th September 2025. It has been estimated that, as a result of this, approximately 19,000 metric tons of sugarcane belonging to about 235 farmers have been damaged. Since the sugarcane destroyed by the fire should be grinded only within the first 24 hours, purchasing and grinding of the sugarcane destroyed by the fire has been completed by 2025-08-30. As a result, the farmers whose sugarcane plantations were damaged are in a great difficulty and the attention of the Government has been focused on providing them with compensation for the damaged plantations. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development; and the Minister of Industries and Entrepreneurship Development to appoint a committee comprising of officers of the Sugarcane Research Institute and Lanka Sugar Company (Pvt) Ltd. to obtain information on the specific quantities of sugarcane destroyed by fire and the sugarcane farmers whose crops have been damaged, and to provide a compensation of Rs.7,000/- per metric ton of sugarcane destroyed by fire to those farmers, was approved by the Cabinet.
