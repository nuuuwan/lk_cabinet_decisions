Vesting of lands where the Rest Houses are located with the Urban Development Authority for the development by investors for management and operational activities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The management and governance of properties of government-owned Rest Houses established in various areas of the island since the colonial period have been assigned to the Urban Development Authority and Local Authorities. Although these Rest Houses are located in tourist-attractive areas, they have fallen into a dilapidated and deteriorated condition due to poor management and governance. Development of these Rest Houses to suit the current economic environment will enable to provide a cost-effective and higher quality service to the general public and create a source of income generation. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to transfer 44 sites where dilapidated and deteriorated Rest Houses are located at present to the Urban Development Authority as a free grant, in accordance with the provisions of the State Lands Ordinance, enabling to develop the same through calling for Project Proposals, was approved by the Cabinet.
