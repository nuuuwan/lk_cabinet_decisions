Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Standards Institution and the Federal Agency on Technical Regulating and Metrology of the Russian Federation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Federal Agency on Technical Regulating and Metrology of the Russian Federation, an institution at highly advanced status in the field of metrology and instrument calibration, has over 39,000 national standards. The Sri Lanka Standards Institution has only about 4,500 national standards. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Science and Technology to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Standards Institute and the Federal Agency on Technical Regulating and Metrology of the Russian Federation with the objectives of using the standards currently used in Russia for future standards to be developed in Sri Lanka, removing technical barriers with the Russian Federation in facilitating trade by harmonizing standards between the two countries, obtaining advanced technical cooperation to the Sri Lanka Standards Institute in the fields of metrology and calibration, and developing cooperation on standardization in accordance with international law, guidelines and standards, was approved by the Cabinet.
