Appointment to the Post of Director General of the Department of Buildings

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Since Mrs. K.G. Sandhya Kumudini, presently serving as the Director General of the Department of Buildings, is due to retire on 2025-06-02, Mrs. W.R.N.R. Premachandra, a Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Engineering Service, presently serving in the Post of Additional Secretary (Technical) of the Ministry of Defence, has been identified as suitable to be appointed to the said Post which falls vacant from that date. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to appoint Mrs. W.R.N.R. Premachandra to the Post of Director General of the Department of Buildings, with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
