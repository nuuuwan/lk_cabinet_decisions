Procurement of Project Implementation Consultants under the Power System Strengthening and Renewable Energy Integration Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Asian Development Bank has agreed to provide funds under the "Power System Strengthening and Renewable Energy Integration Project" to implement several transmission development and distribution projects for strengthening and modernization of the electricity distribution network operated by Lanka Electricity Company (Private) Limited., due to the increase in electricity demand in the areas where the company operates. The following activities have been identified to be carried out under the said project: * 	Obtaining a power injection from the Ceylon Electricity Board through a grid substation in the Kelaniya area.* 	Introducing a 33 kV power injection to strengthen the medium voltage network of Lanka Electricity Company (Private) Limited., in the areas of Kelaniya/Negombo and Kotte/Negombo. * 	Converting the identified areas in Kelaniya and Kotte/Nugegoda to underground cable networks.  * 	Implementation of the 11 kV automated feeder reconfiguration project in the branch areas of Kotte, Nugegoda, Moratuwa and Kalutara.  * Implementation of the Supervisory Controll and Data Acquisition System & the Network Automation System Accordingly, the recommendation submitted by the Minister of Energy to initiate the procurement process to select suitable Consultants to implement the said project activities, was approved by the Cabinet.
