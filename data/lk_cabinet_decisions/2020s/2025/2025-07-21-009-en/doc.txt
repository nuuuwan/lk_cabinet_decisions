Calling for Expressions of Interest to purchase medicines and other medicinal supplies from Local Manufacturers for the year 2026

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Twenty-four (24) manufacturers have been selected to locally procure 130 medicines and medicinal supplies for the year 2025 by calling Expressions of Interest from local manufacturers and having evaluated them by a Committee appointed by the Secretary, Ministry of Health and Mass Media. The agreements entered into with these manufacturers are scheduled to expire on 2025‑12‑31. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to invite Expressions of Interest/Requests for Proposals from local manufacturers for the purchase of locally manufactured medicines and other medical supplies for the year 2026, was approved by the Cabinet.
