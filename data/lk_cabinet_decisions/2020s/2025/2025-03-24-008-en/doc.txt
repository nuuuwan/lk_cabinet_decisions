Amendment of the Companies Act, No.7 of 2007

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The approval of the Cabinet has been granted to amend the Companies Act, No.7 of 2007 to address the deficiencies relating to the transparency of beneficial ownership of legal persons in line with international standards to curb Money Laundering, Financing of Terrorists and other unlawful activities. Accordingly, the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman has been further amended as per the advice given by the Attorney General based on the Supreme Court Special judgment bearing No.SC/SD/92/2024. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the final Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter, submit the same to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
