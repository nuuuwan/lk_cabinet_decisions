Programme for Providing New Houses to the Plantation Community who have lost their Homes due to Natural Disasters

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The ‘Programme to Provide New Houses to Plantation Communities who have lost their Homes due to Natural Disasters’ has been launched in the year 2025 with the aim of relocating around 3,250 plantation families who have been residing in temporary houses over a long period of time due to natural disasters, to safe settlements under a multi-phased plan. The said programme is expected to be implemented between the period of 2025 - 2027, and it has been planned to construct 1,400 new housing units and settle the beneficiary families during that period. The total cost of the programme is Rs. 4,500 million, and a provision amounting to Rs. 1,300 million has been allocated for the year 2025. Currently, houses are being constructed for 495 eligible beneficiary plantation families in the districts of Badulla, Kegalle, Nuwara Eliya, Ratnapura, Kandy, Matale, Colombo, Kalutara, Matara and Monaragala under the said programme. The Ministry of Plantation and Community Infrastructure is currently taking steps to secure foreign aid for the construction of houses for the remaining families which are not covered under this programme. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Plantation and Community Infrastructure to implement the programme to provide new houses for the plantation communities who have lost their homes due to natural disasters until 2027‑12‑31 and to secure provisions required for the purpose within the medium-term budgetary framework, was approved by the Cabinet.
