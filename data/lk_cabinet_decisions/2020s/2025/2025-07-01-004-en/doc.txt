Community Productive Model Villages programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been planned to implement the Community Productive Model Villages Programme based on the Participatory Rural Approach by selecting one village (approximately 50 families) from each of the Divisional Secretariat Divisions in the years 2025, 2026 and 2027, in line with the Clean Sri Lanka National Project, as a strategic tool to achieve its objectives islandwide. This programme aims to create the selected villages as Community Productive Model Villages based on the model criteria identified under the areas of education, economy, health and sanitation, household management and social well-being, culture and spiritual development and environmental conservation. The selection of suitable villages for the proposed programme will be carried out in collaboration with the District Secretaries and Divisional Secretaries. Under this programme, it is expected to empower all field officers belonging to the line Ministries serving in the Divisional Secretariats representing each selected village and to fulfill the needs of the villagers through those officers efficiently and effectively. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Industries and Entrepreneurship Development to implement the proposed programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
