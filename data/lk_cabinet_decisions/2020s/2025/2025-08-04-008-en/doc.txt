Revising the coverage limit of Group Insurance policy for the Hon. Members of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2023‑05-15 for the implementation of a group insurance policy for the Hon. Members of Parliament, subject to a maximum insurance coverage of Rs. 1 million per parliamentary year. Accordingly, relevant insurance coverages have been provided during the past period. At the time of presenting the Budget 2025, the Hon. President proposed to reduce the insurance coverage limit of Rs.1,000,000/- for a Member of Parliament to Rs.250,000/-. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to provide a group insurance policy for the Members of Parliament by limiting the insurance benefits for a Member of Parliament to Rs.250,000/- from the insurance year scheduled to commence on 2025‑10‑09 onwards, was approved by the Cabinet.
