Procurement for the supply of Instrument Sets used for bone fixation, fracture stabilization and alignment assurance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the procurement of instrument sets used for bone fixation, fracture stabilization and alignment assurance. Two (02) bids have been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the procurement of purchasing 24 items under SR No.21209104, 30 items under SR No.21209101 and 20 items under SR No.21209004 from the said instrument sets, to Medisurg International (Pvt.) Ltd., Sri Lanka for a sum of USD 435,000, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
