26th Session of the Intergovernmental Group (IGG) on Tea of the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations, 4th Annual Meeting of the Asian Tea Alliance (ATA) and the Centenary Celebration of the Tea Research Institute

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-16 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposals submitted by the Minister of Plantation and Community Infrastructure to conduct the 26th Session of the Intergovernmental Group on Tea of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, 4th Annual Meeting of the Asian Tea Alliance and the Centenary Celebration of the Tea Research Institute, from 9th to 14th November 2025, in Colombo and take necessary action relating to the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
