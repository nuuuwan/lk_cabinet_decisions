The National Cyber Security Strategy of Sri Lanka  - 2025-2029

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka's first Information and Cyber Security Strategy, introduced in 2018, has been implemented from the year 2019 to 2023. Based on the first Cyber Security Strategy and with the support of the World Bank, the Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Readiness Team (Sri Lanka CERT) has developed the 'National Cyber Security Strategy of Sri Lanka 2025-2029' on cyber security. The said Strategy has been drafted based on the strategic areas such as strengthening the legal and regulatory framework, improving knowledge, enhancing cyber security preparedness and strengthening response capabilities, and increasing collaboration, covering only the civilian domain related to cyber security in Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy for the implementation of the said 'National Cyber Security Strategy of Sri Lanka 2025-2029', was approved by the Cabinet.
