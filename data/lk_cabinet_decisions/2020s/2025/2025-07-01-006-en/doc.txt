Implementation of Research Projects on Palm Leaf Manuscript collections with Sri Lankan origin in Netherlands

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Based on a proposal submitted in response to an international call for research proposals on collections with colonial context by the Dutch Research Council, the Department of National Archives in Sri Lanka, together with five partners including the Free University of the Netherlands, have been awarded a competitive grant of EUR 384,248 to conduct research on palm leaf collections of Sri Lankan origin in Netherlands. The proposed research will be carried out between 2025 and 2029 under the title "Whose Knowledge, Whose Values? Palm Leaf Manuscripts and the Question of Colonial Collections in Dutch Heritage Institutions". The Agreement to be signed between the six joint stakeholders to implement the said project has received the clearance of the Attorney General, the recommendation of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism and the concurrence of the Department of External Resources. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to sign the proposed Agreement and to conduct the said Research during the period 2025 - 2029, was approved by the Cabinet.
