Calling for Investment Proposals for a plot of land owned by the Urban Development Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-16 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been planned to invite investment proposals for the plot of land in extent of 03 roods and 13.85 perches owned by the Urban Development Authority, located at No. 40, D.R. Wijewardena Mawatha, Colombo 10 and select a suitable investor to develop the land. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of  Urban Development, Construction and Housing to invite investment proposals to dispose the said plot of land on a 99-year lease basis, based on the updated valuation of the Chief Government Valuer for the purpose of selecting a suitable investor to implement a Mixed Development Project in the relevant land in accordance with the Urban Development Regulations, was approved by the Cabinet.
