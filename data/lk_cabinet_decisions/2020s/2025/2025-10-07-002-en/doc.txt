Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Consultations between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Malta

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka and the Republic of Malta, as island nations and members of the Commonwealth of Nations, have collaborated in multilateral forums on issues of mutual concern, including maritime affairs, climate change and sustainable development. This year also marks the 25th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign a Memorandum of Understanding on Bilateral Consultations between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Tourism of the Republic of Malta to promote and strengthen bilateral relations in the political, economic, trade, investment, consular and cultural aspects, as well as to exchange views on regional and international matters of mutual interest, was approved by the Cabinet.
