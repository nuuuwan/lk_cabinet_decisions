Closure of Non-Functioning State-Owned Enterprises

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Some State‑Owned Enterprises established long ago to achieve the objectives such as providing public services and promoting strategic economic activities have become inactive due to reasons such as continuation of those enterprises is not practical owing to the incompatibility of the objectives of their establishment with the present mandate and market requirements and financial underperformance. It has been identified as appropriate to liquidate and close down State-Owned Enterprises belonging to various categories such as Statutory Bodies, State Corporations and State-Owned Companies that do not make a meaningful contribution to the national economy or the public service delivery, instead of continuing to operate them as a burden to the Government. Accordingly, taking into consideration the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, the approval of the Cabinet was granted to formally liquidate and close down 33 State-Owned Enterprises that are not-functioning at present, in 02 stages under the supervision of a special liquidation unit to be established under the said Ministry.
