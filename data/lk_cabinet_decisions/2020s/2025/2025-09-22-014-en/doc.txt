Programme for Granting a Cost of Living Allowance for children in institutional care or guardianship and street children

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025.06.16 for the implementation of a programme to pay a monthly cost of living allowance of Rs. 5,000/- for children in institutional care or guardianship and street children. Accordingly, provision of the benefits of the said programme, which has been named as 'Artha', has been commenced from July 2025. It appears that, the said benefit could be provided with effect from January 2025, so that Rs.1,000 million allocated for the 'Artha' programme for the year 2025 can be utilized for the maximum benefit of the children. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Women and Child Affairs to consider the date of commencement of the benefits of the 'Artha' programme as 2025 01 01 and to credit the outstanding benefits due from that date to the accounts of the children, was approved by the Cabinet.
