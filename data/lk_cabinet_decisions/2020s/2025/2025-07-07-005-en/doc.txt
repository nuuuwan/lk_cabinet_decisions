Incorporation of a limited liability company to manage and operate the East Container Terminal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2021‑02‑01 for the Colombo South Port Project - Development of the East Container Terminal and the West Container Terminal. It has been decided at the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Sri Lanka Ports Authority to incorporate a limited liability company fully owned by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority to operate and manage the East Container Terminal. This would enable to accrue maximum economic benefits, achieve operational efficiency and compete with other private terminal operators. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to incorporate a Company named "Colombo East Container Terminal (Private) Limited", was approved by the Cabinet.
