Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding between the Chongqing Broadcasting Group of the People's Republic of China and the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Chongqing Broadcasting Group of the People's Republic of China and the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation with a view to promoting long-standing friendship and mutual understanding between the two countries, strengthening the corporation and exchanging training opportunities in the field of media. The proposed Memorandum of Understanding will provide the opportunity for the exchange of programmes between the People's Republic of China and Sri Lanka, promotion of learning and training opportunities, joint production of television programmes and obtaining publicity needed for the tourism industry. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between the Chongqing Broadcasting Group of the People's Republic of China and the Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation, was approved by the Cabinet.
