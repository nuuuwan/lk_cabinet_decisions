Ratification of the First Phase of the Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies 	of 	the World Trade Organization

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The need for a global agreement on regulating fisheries subsidies was affirmed at the 11th Ministerial Conference of the World Trade Organization held in Buenos Aires, Argentina in 2017. Accordingly, the World Trade Organization has concluded the work on the Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies of World Trade Organization at the 12th Ministerial Conference held in 2022. The said Agreement covers three (03) key pillars: the prohibition of subsidies contributing to illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing, prohibition of subsidies for overfished stocks, and the prohibition of subsidies that contribute to overcapacity and overfishing. Member countries of the World Trade Organization has initiated the ratification of Phase I of the Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies, which has been prepared including the first two (02) pillars mentioned above, since 2023. The Cabinet approved the joint proposal made by the Minister of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources and the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development to ratify Phase I of the Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies, enabling to strengthen Sri Lanka's leadership in ocean sustainability, strengthen Sri Lanka's trading position in key markets, build the necessary capacity to modernize Sri Lanka's fisheries sector, and gain benefits from the World Trade Organization's dedicated fisheries fund through technical assistance.
