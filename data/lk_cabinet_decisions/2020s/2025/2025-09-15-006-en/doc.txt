Paddy Purchasing Programme of the Government for the Yala Season 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government has allocated Rs.6,000 million for the paddy purchasing programme of the year 2025, and the Paddy Marketing Board has purchased 43,891 metric tons of paddy from 16 districts, spending Rs.5,288 million out of which, as of now. The Paddy Marketing Board has made plans to purchase the paddy harvest from the Polonnaruwa, Anuradhapura and Trincomalee zones, which are currently being harvested, during the upcoming Yala season. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to obtain additional provisions to further implement the Programme of Paddy Purchasing from farmers during the Yala Season 2025 under the government's guaranteed prices, was approved by the Cabinet.
