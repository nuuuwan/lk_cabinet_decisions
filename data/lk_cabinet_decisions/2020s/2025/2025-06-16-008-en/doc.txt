Procurement of 07 Nos. of Gantry cranes for Container Handling at the East Container Terminal and the Jaya Container Terminal

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-16 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sri Lanka Ports Authority has made provisions under the budget 2025 for the procurement of 07 nos of electric rubber-tyre gantry cranes for container operations at the East Container Terminal and the Jaya Container Terminal of the said Authority. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to procure 07 gantry cranes, following the international competitive bidding procedure, was approved by the Cabinet.
