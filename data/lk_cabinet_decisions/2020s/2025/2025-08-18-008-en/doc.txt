Re-opening of the Kurunchative (Elephant Pass-North) Saltern

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Although, measures have been taken to call for Expressions of Interest from local investors to resume the production and operational activities of the Kurunchative (Elephant Pass-North) Saltern, which had been halted due to the war situation, which prevailed in the Northern and Eastern Provinces, under the Public-Private Partnership method, it has not been able to achieve the expected outcome. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to invite Expressions of Interest internationally to resume the production activities of the Kurunchative (Elephant Pass-North) Saltern under the Public-Private Partnership method, was approved by the Cabinet.
