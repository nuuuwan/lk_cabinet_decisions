Procurement of 15,000 MT of Urea by State Fertilizer Company Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the cabinet meeting held on 2023 07 17 to import and sell fertilizer to farmers by State Fertilizer Company Limited since Maha season 2023/24, competing with the companies in the private sector. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation to import 15,000 MT of Urea by State Fertilizer Company Limited, following the international competitive bidding procedure, for sale in the form of straight and mixed fertilizer in the open market during the coming season, was approved by the Cabinet.
