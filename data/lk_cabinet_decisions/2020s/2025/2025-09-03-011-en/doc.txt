Rehabilitation and Modernization of the Sri Lanka Planetarium

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sri Lanka Planetarium has been designed by Dr. A.N.S. Kulasinghe and constructed as a major component of the Industrial Exhibition held in the year 1965. As the deterioration of the structure of the building and the obsolescence of its physical facilities and technical infrastructure are currently being observed, the need to modernize it while preserving its unique architectural features has been identified. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Science and Technology to assign the relevant task to the National Engineering Research and Development Centre (NERD), a premier state institution with expertise in prestressed concrete technology, based on the concurrence given by the National Procurement Commission, was approved by the Cabinet.
