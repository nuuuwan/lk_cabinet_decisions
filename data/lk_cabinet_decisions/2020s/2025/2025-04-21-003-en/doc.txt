Awarding of contract for Ground Handling Services of SriLankan Airlines Limited at Melbourne Airport in Australia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The existing contract for the provision of ground handling services at the Melbourne Airport, Australia is due to expire on 2025‑06‑30. Therefore, limited international competitive bids have been invited to select a ground handling service provider for the next 03 years. The Standing High Level Procurement Committee, after reviewing four (04) bids received for the same has recommended that the relevant contract be awarded to Dnata Airport Services Pty Limited. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to award the contract for the provision of ground handling services (Passenger, Ramp and Cargo Warehouse handling) for SriLankan Airlines at Melbourne Airport, to the said company at a total estimated cost of AUD 13,124,402, was approved by the Cabinet.
