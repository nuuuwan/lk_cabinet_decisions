Seasonal Worker Programme for Agriculture and Fisheries in Republic of Korea

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025-02-19 for the implementation of a pilot project to facilitate the deployment of Sri Lankan workers under the E-8 visa category (Seasonal Workers) in the Republic of Korea. Accordingly, discussions have been held through diplomatic channels with interested Counties of the Republic of Korea to enter into Memoranda of Understanding. As a result of the discussions held with the Yeongwol County in  Republic of Korea, it has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties. The proposed Memorandum of Understanding will provide the opportunity to Sri Lankans to work in the agricultural and fisheries villages of Yeongwol County for a short period of time (5 to 8 months) under the Seasonal Worker Programme to earn an income and earn foreign exchange for this Country. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the proposed Memoranda of Understanding. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the relevant Memorandum of Understanding with the Yeongwol County, was approved by the Cabinet.
