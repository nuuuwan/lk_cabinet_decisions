Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and the Sri Lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Royal Canadian Mounted Police and the Sri Lanka Police have reached a consensus to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties for the development of cooperation. It is anticipated to prevent transnational organized crimes, exchange information on suspects and crimes, conduct joint operations and obtain technical assistance. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism has extended its concurrence to the proposed Memorandum of Understanding. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to enter into the said Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
