Connecting Government Institutions that maintain Critical National Information Infrastructure to the National Cyber Security Operations Centre

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Cyber-attacks targeting Government institutions and financial institutions pose a risk of disruptions to the essential services provided by such institutions. Furthermore, the exposure of personal and sensitive data to other parties adversely affects citizens' personal security, national security, economic stability, public health and well-being of the society, as well as public trust in Government institutions. Therefore, with a view to minimizing such threats and risks, the National Cyber Security Operations Centre has been established under the guidance of the Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Readiness Team and the Ministry of Digital Economy. The National Cyber Technology Operations Centre provides 24-hours continuous facilities of monitoring, detecting and responding to cyber threats as well as detecting and issuing alerts of damages caused by any type of malware and unauthorized access. Accordingly, the proposal made by Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy to direct the relevant Secretaries to the Ministries and the Heads of Government Institutions to connect with the National Cyber Security Operations Centre and continue to obtain its professional services, was approved by the Cabinet.
