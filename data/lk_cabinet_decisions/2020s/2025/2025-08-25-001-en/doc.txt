Implementation of the Project ''Fostering Sustainable Economic Recovery through Improved Biodiversity and Sustainable Waste Management''

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

The Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government, together with the European Union, has prepared a project proposal to implement initiatives on biodiversity conservation and solid waste management in the Central, Uva, North Western, North Central and Northern Provinces. Protecting high-value biodiversity sites by restoring degraded ecosystems, implementing targeted conservation strategies and promoting sustainable land-use practices is one of the objectives of this project. Further, implementation of sustainable waste management practices in selected local authority areas, promotion of climate resilient practices, and conducting capacity building programmes for local communities, government agencies and the private sector are also expected through this project. The relevant project is planned to be implemented through a Grant of EURO 8 million of the European Union. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to sign the Financing Agreement with the European Union for obtaining the relevant Grant to implement the said Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
