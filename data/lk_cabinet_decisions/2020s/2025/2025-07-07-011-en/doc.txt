Project of Proper and Clean Maintenance of Reservoirs and Waterways in the Western Province under the “Clean Sri Lanka - 2025 programme”

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Many canals which are not maintained by any government agency are located in the Colombo-North and in densely populated areas in the Colombo city. Therefore, a project has been designed to implement under the "Clean Sri Lanka" programme to clean and maintain the secondary canals that are not so maintained and as well as to increase the frequency of cleaning the main canals around Colombo City. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to implement the proposed project through the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation under the "Clean Sri Lanka" programme with the following objectives:* Controlling flooding in the Colombo City by rainwater thereby minimizing public distress caused by the same.* Maintaining a clean, pleasant and healthy environment.* Enhancing public health and quality of life.* Increasing the city's attraction to tourists.* Improving the quality of water in inland waterways and water retention basins.
