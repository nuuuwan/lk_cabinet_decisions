The project for the Digital transformation of the General Education in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The People's Republic of China has agreed to provide funds amounting to US$ 20 million for the implementation of a project to digitalize the general education in Sri Lanka. During the official visit of the President to China in January this year, an agreement was reached between the two parties to implement the said project expeditiously. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to provide the equipment required for 500 selected schools and a model smart classroom under the proposed project, and to establish the Network Operations Management Unit, the Studio Room and the Conference Room under the project in the 'Isurupaya' premises.
