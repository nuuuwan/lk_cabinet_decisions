Improvement of (B115) Embilipitiya-Panamure-Buluthota Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Sri Lanka has obtained a loan of US$ 40 million from the OPEC Fund for International Development to improve the Rakwana -Suriyakanda Road section of the Galle - Deniyaya - Madampe Road. There is a balance of about Rs. 1,000 million of that loan, and it has been planned to use that money and government funds to improve a currently dilapidated Road section of 9.62 km from Kolonna to Suriyakanda of the Embilipitiya -Panamura - Buluthota (B115) Road. The said road section provides access to several tourist attractions such as Sinharaja Forest, Maduwanwela Walawwa, Wavulpane, Gongala, Handapanagala, Buluthota Waterfalls and Morning Side, thereby boosting the tourism industry as well. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to utilize the balance provisions from the Rakwana - Suriyakanda Road Project to improve the Embilipitiya - Panamura - Buluthota Road section and to meet the remaining financial requirement through the funds of the Government of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
