Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Colombo and the National Korea Maritime and Ocean University of the Republic of Korea, for Academic Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-02-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been planned to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Colombo and the National Korea Maritime and Ocean University of the Republic of Korea with a view to promoting educational and joint research programmes and organizing workshops and seminars jointly. Both parties have agreed to implement the activities of the proposed Memorandum of Understanding to be applicable basically to the Institute of Biochemistry, Molecular Biology and Biotechnology of the University of Colombo. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between the University of Colombo of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the National Korea Maritime and Ocean University of the Republic of Korea to be effective for a period of five (05) years, was approved by the Cabinet.
