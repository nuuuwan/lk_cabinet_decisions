Providing an allowance for Dhamma school teachers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Dhamma school education provides a good basis for directing the society towards spiritual development. Therefore, it has been identified that it is more suitable to provide a teachers allowance and uniform allowance to teachers serving in Dhamma schools without expectation of any personal gain to encourage them for obtaining their continuous services and protect their identity and also for the advancement of Dhamma education which is essential for the well-being of the society. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to pay a one-time annual payment of Rs.7,500/- as an allowance including teacher's allowance and uniform allowance to the teachers who have completed one year of continuous service in registered Dhamma Schools belonging to the Buddhist, Hindu, Islamic, Christian and Catholic religions, from the year 2026 onwards, was approved by the Cabinet.
