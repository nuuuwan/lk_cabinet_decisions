Amending the Control of Pesticides Act, No.33 of 1980

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the meeting held on 2023‑08‑07 for the amendment of the Cóntrol of Pesticides Act, No. 33 of 1980. It has been pointed out that the policy approval of the present Cabinet should be obtained in order to complete the preparation of the said Bill by the Legal Draftsman. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation, to grant policy approval of the Cabinet to proceed with the preparation of the Bill to amend the Cóntrol of Pesticides Act, No. 33 of 1980, was approved by the Cabinet.
