Renovation of the Karainagar Boatyard, owned by Cey-Nor Foundation Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- During the official visit made by the Hon. President in India in the month of December 2024, a Memorandum of Understanding was signed to obtain a Grant from the Government of India to renovate the Karainagar Boatyard. In terms of the said Memorandum of Understanding, the Government of Sri Lanka has been charged with the responsibility of securing technical consultancy services for the project and for exempting taxes on the goods and equipment relevant to the said Grant. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources to obtain the service of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau as the Consultant of the Project and to allocate Rs. 40 million from the General Treasury for expenses to be incurred by the Government of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
