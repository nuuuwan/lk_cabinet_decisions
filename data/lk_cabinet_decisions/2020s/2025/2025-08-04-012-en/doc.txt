Re-implementation of the housing projects of the Colombage Mawatha and Torrington Avenue under the Urban Regeneration Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑12‑30 to resume the initial works of the two housing projects in the Colombage Mawatha and Torrington Avenue which were implemented under the Urban Regeneration Project and had halted halfway. The Colombage Mawatha project consists of 615 housing units whereas the Torrington Avenue housing project consists of 115 housing units. The estimated cost for the implementation of the said projects is Rs.7.48 billion, and it is expected that it will take about 2 ½ years to complete the construction work. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to have the provisions required for the implementation of the said projects allocated from the year 2026 to the year 2028, was approved by the Cabinet.
