Appointment to the Post of Surveyor General

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mr. U.K.S.P. Wijesinghe, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Surveyors Service, who served as the Surveyor General, has retired from the Public Service with effect from 2025‑01‑22, upon the completion of 60 years of age. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation to appoint Mr. Y.G. Gnanathilaka, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Surveyors Service, to the vacant Post of Surveyor General with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
