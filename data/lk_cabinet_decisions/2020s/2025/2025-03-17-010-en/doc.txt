Repealing the Sri Lanka Accounting and Auditing Standards Act, No.15 of 1995, and Finalizing the Draft Financial Reporting Regulatory Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2021 05 24 to repeal the Sri Lanka Accounting and Auditing Standards Act No. 15 of 1995 and introduce a new Act. Accordingly, the Legal Draftsman has prepared the initial draft of the Financial Reporting Regulatory Bill, after obtaining the views of the Sri Lanka Accounting and Auditing Standards Monitoring Board and other relevant stakeholders. The Legal Draftsman has pointed out that the policy approval of the new Cabinet should be obtained to finalize the preparation of the said Bill. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet for the preparation of the said Bill, was approved by the Cabinet.
