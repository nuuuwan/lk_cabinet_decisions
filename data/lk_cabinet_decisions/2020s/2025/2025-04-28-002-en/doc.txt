Economic Census - 2025/26

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Economic Census was last conducted by the Department of Census and Statistics in the years 2013/14. The Department has planned to conduct the Economic Census in the years 2025 and 2026, covering agricultural and non-agricultural economic activities in two phases separately to update the data collected in that census. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to conduct the Economic Census in the years 2025 and 2026 covering the enumeration of agricultural activities in the year 2025 and non-agricultural activities in the year 2026, was approved by the Cabinet.
