Extending the concession on Embarkation Levy for Mattala Rajapaksha International Airport

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- As per a decision taken by the Cabinet on 2025-01-06, Airlines operating scheduled flights to and from Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport have been exempted from the Embarkation Levy. Under the said tax concession, some Airlines have already operated scheduled international flights to and from the said Airport, and some Airlines have expressed their intention to operate flights to the said Airport. It is expected that the continuation of the said concession on Embarkation Levy will enable attracting new scheduled international Airlines to the said Airport and continuation of the existing international flight operations by increasing competitiveness. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to further extend the current period of the concession on Embarkation Levy until 2027-06-26, was approved by the Cabinet.
