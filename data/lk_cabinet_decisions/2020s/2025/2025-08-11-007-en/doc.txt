Current status and strategic pathway in introducing the Hydrogen Economy in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka is seeking sustainable solutions to reduce carbon emissions and diversify its energy mix, and green hydrogen has been identified as a viable alternative energy source which is aligned with national and global climate goals. Accordingly, the Ministry of Energy has taken several important initial steps by now, including the launch of the National Hydrogen Roadmap and drafting of a comprehensive Renewable Hydrogen Policy. The Ministry of Energy intends to conduct Public Consultation Workshops and Key Stakeholder Workshops relating to this to improve the policy framework, overcome potential problems and build a broad consensus for successful implementation of the same. Accordingly, the Cabinet took into notice of the submissions made by the Minister of Energy regarding the steps being taken to develop the Hydrogen sector in Sri Lanka in accordance with local conditions and needs and in line with international safety standards and performance benchmarks.
