Awarding contracts for 03 packages of Phase 1 of the Section 3 of                    the Central Expressway Project (Pothuhera to Galagedara)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Phase 1 of the Section 3 of the Central Expressway project from Pothuhera to Rambukkana has been divided into 19 packages. In order to bring the Phase 1 of the said road (ch 0+000 km to ch 13+800 km) to an operational level, the balance works such as Aggregate Base Course, Asphalt concrete, road markings, installation of signboards, construction of buildings for expressway operations and installation of toll collection systems are required to be completed. Accordingly, national competitive bids have been invited under 03 construction packages for the said works. Based on the recommendations made by the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee after the evaluation of the bids received, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to award the contracts, to M/s Maga Engineering (Pvt) Ltd, the substantially responsive lowest bidder for all the said packages, was approved by Cabinet.
