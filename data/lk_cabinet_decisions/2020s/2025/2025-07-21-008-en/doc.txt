Awarding the Contract for Ground Handling Services of SriLankan Airlines Limited at Dhaka Airport (DAC) in Bangladesh

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ground Handling Services of SriLankan Airlines Limited at Dhaka Airport is being provided by Biman Bangladesh Airlines Limited, the sole service provider of ground handling services in that airport and the duration of the said contract is scheduled to expire on 2025‑09‑30. A proposal has been obtained from that company to extend the duration of the said project. Based on the recommendations submitted by the Bid Evaluation Committee and the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee after evaluating the same, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to award the relevant contract to the said company for a period of 03 years from 2025‑10‑01 to 2028‑09-30, was approved by the Cabinet.
