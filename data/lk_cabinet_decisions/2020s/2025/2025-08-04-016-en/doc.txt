Implementation of the Projects on Development of Sports Infrastructure Facilities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Developing human resources and sports infrastructure facilities in the field of sports to popularize sports is a main role of the Department of Sports Development. The Department of Sports Development has planned to implement three projects for the school system, public play grounds and youth clubs within a period of 03 years from the year 2025 with the aim of developing sports infrastructure facilities for the youth and elderly community, sports clubs and schools that do not have adequate sports facilities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports to implement the Projects of Creating a sports culture, Providing sports infrastructure facilities for schools in rural areas and youth clubs and Development of infrastructure facilities in identified sports schools, by the Department of Sports Development during the period of 2025-2027, was approved by the Cabinet.
