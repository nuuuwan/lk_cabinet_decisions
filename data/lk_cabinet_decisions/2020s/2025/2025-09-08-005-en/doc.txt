Appointment of a Cabinet Sub Committee for organizing activities of the 78th Independence Day Ceremony

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Taking into consideration a proposal submitted by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government, the Cabinet granted its approval to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee chaired by the Hon. Prime Minister and with the participation of the relevant Hon. Ministers concerned, to oversee and provide guidance pertaining to the activities of the 78th Independence Day Ceremony falling on 2026-02-04
