Establishment of the Institute of Real Estate Professionals, Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meetings held on 2023-10-23 and 2025‑01‑20 pertaining to introducing a new Act enabling to establish an institution called the "Institute of Real Estate Professionals, Sri Lanka" to regulate the professional activities of real estate practitioners in Sri Lanka. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman, as per the said approval. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development, Construction and Housing to publish the Institute of Real Estate Professionals, Sri Lanka Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter to submit it to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
