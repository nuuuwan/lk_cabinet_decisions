Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation and the Chinese Academy of Tropical Agricultural Sciences on Agricultural Cooperation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

The Ministry in charge of the subject of Agriculture has implemented the Project ‘Technical Assistance under South-South Cooperation with the People’s Republic of China’ with the aim of improving the banana, mango and pineapple value chains in Sri Lanka. This project, which was technically supported by the Chinese Academy of Tropical Agricultural Sciences, has introduced innovative technologies and practices that can significantly increase yields, improve the availability of planting materials, strengthen post-harvest practices and promote value addition across the fruit cultivation sector. It has been observed that it would be appropriate to establish a formal mechanism to continue the existing collaboration with the Chinese Academy of Tropical Agricultural Sciences beyond the current project period so that the benefits of the said project can be fully and sustainably realized. It is proposed to make the opportunity for germplasm exchange of fruit crops in accordance with the existing Acts and Regulations, promote exchange of scholars and experts to support technological advancement of both parties, and support capacity building of processors, traders and other value chain actors. The proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to sign the Memorandum of Understanding on Agricultural Cooperation between the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation and the Chinese Academy of Tropical Agricultural Sciences for that purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
