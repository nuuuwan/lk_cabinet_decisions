Implementation of the “District Development Programme - 2025”

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Provisions of Rs.2,000 million have been allocated through the Budget 2025 to implement the District Development Programme as a national level development programme covering all Districts in Sri Lanka. Under the said programme, it is expected to achieve the following objectives;  *  Infrastructure improvement  *  Promotion of domestic production capabilities  *  Enhancing service delivery mechanisms  *  improving trade and marketing facilities  *  Strengthening institutional capacityThe proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Fiance, Planning and Economic Development to allocate provisions provided through the Budget Estimate 2025 to each District for the implementation of the District Development Programme - 2025, based on the District Poverty Level of Sri Lanka published by the Department of Census and Statistics using the Multidimensional Poverty Index which is a global measure used to identify poverty, was approved by the Cabinet.
