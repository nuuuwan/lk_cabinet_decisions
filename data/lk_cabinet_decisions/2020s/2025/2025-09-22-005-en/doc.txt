National Policy for Older Persons in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted for the ‘National Charter and National Policy for Senior Citizens in Sri Lanka” in the year 2006. It has been recognized that the National Policy for Senior Citizens in Sri Lanka needs to be updated in line with the evolving global trends. Also, since the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs has predicted that the population aged 60 years or above will increase to 24.8% by the year 2052, it has been recognized that it is essential to promote and protect the welfare and rights of the elderly population. Accordingly, the ‘National Policy for Older Persons in Sri Lanka’ has been formulated enabling to facilitate the elders to live a socially, economically, physically and spiritually fulfilling life in line with the theme of ‘A Respectable Senior Citizen – A Meaningful Retirement’ under the National Policy Framework of the Government. Approval of the Cabinet was granted to the said National Policy presented by the Minister of Rural Development, Social Security and Community Empowerment.
