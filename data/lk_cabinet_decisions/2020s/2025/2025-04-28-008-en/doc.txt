Appointment to the Post of Commissioner General of Official Languages

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Post of Commissioner General of Official Languages has fallen vacant due to the appointment of Mr. W. Prince Senadheera, Special Grade Officer in the Sri Lanka Administrative Service who served in the said Post, to the Post of the Secretary, Ministry of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to appoint Mr. Thilak Nandana Hettiarachchi, Special  Grade Officer in the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as the Additional Secretary (Cultural Promotions) in the Ministry of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to the Post of Commissioner General of Official Languages with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
