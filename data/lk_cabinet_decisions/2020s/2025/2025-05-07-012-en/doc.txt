Appointment to the Post of Commissioner General of Examinations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The service period of Mr. H.J.M.C.A Jayasundara, who served in the post of Commissioner General of Examination, expired on 2025‑05‑06. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to appoint Mrs. A.K. Subhashini Indika Kumari Liyanage, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Education Administrative Service, presently serving in a Post of Commissioner of Examinations in the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka, to the vacant Post of Commissioner General of Examinations with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
