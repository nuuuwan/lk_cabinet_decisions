Submission of the Review Report on State-owned Non-Commercial Entities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It had been decided at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024-12-18 to appoint an Officials' Committee under the Chairmanship of the Secretary to the Prime Minister to review and submit a Report on State-owned Non-Commercial Institutions. The Committee has reviewed 160 institutions and has decided to seek the assistance of a sub-committee comprising of the members from the field to review 36 higher education institutions out of these, including universities. The recommendations of the Committee have been submitted by classifying the remaining 124 institutions other than the said institutions under 09 categories. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to implement the said recommendations, was approved by the Cabinet.
