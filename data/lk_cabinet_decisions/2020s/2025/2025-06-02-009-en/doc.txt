Establishment of a Metro Bus Unit for Urban Transportation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed through the Budget 2025 to introduce a comfortable bus fleet with state-of-the-art technology as a pilot project under the Sri Lanka Transport Board, for modernization of the public transport service. Under this, it is planned to deploy 100 low entry comfortable buses for passenger transportation along several major road corridors including Colombo, Makumbura (Kottawa), Kaduwela, Kadawatha and Moratuwa in the Western Province. Accordingly, taking into consideration a proposal submitted by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation, the Cabinet granted its approval to establish a subsidiary Company of the Sri Lanka Transport Board under the Companies Act No.07 of 2007 and implement the proposed project.
