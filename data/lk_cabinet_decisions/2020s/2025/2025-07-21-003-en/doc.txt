Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding on Diplomatic Training and Exchange of Information and Documentation with the Foreign Service Institute of the Republic of Maldives

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute of Sri Lanka, which has been established to train officers of the Sri Lanka Foreign Service, and the Foreign Service Institute of Maldives, with the aim of promoting mutual cooperation and enhance understanding. Through this Agreement, it is expected to exchange information on study programmes and other academic activities, exchange of academics, conference speakers and experts in areas of common interest, providing opportunities for diplomats of both countries in trainings offered by each other and organizing courses and seminars in the cities of Colombo and Male. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute of Sri Lanka and the Foreign Service Institute of Maldives, was approved by the Cabinet.
