Appointment to the post of Director General of the Department of Census and Statistics

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mrs. D.D.G.A. Seneviratne, who is presently serving as Director General of the Department of Census and Statistics is due to retire from the Public Service with effect from 2025-09-05 upon completion of the age of 60 years. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to appoint Mrs. K.M.D.S.D. Karunaratne, the most senior Additional Director General of the Department of Census and Statistics to the post of Director General of that Department falling vacant, with effect from the said date, was approved by the Cabinet.
