Appointment to the Post of Director of the Department of Cultural      Affairs

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The post of Director of the Department of Cultural Affairs is currently vacant. Mrs. K.S. Dilhani, Grade I officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, who is serving as a Senior Assistant Secretary of the Ministry of Defence, has been found suitable to be appointed to the said post. Taking into consideration the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs accordingly, the Cabinet decided to appoint Mrs. K.S. Dilhani, to the post of Director of the Department of Cultural Affairs.
