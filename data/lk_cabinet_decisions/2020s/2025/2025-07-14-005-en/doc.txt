Establishment of Treatment Plants for improvement of sewage 	management By Local Authorities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The lack of adequate sewage treatment plants has become an obstacle for fulfilling the sewage management service chain in the island. As a result, in certain instances, collected sewage is disposed to the open environment improperly. Under the National Policy on Waste Management, the need to improve the infrastructure facilities required for sewage  management has been identified.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government for the construction of mechanical sewage treatment plants in selected Local Authorities, utilizing Rs. 200 million from the provisions allocated for the Clean Sri Lanka Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
