Introducing a Concessional Loan Scheme to encourage Young Entrepreneurs in the Agriculture and Industrial Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The lack of concessional financial facilities to encourage investments has been identified as an obstacle to the creation of entrepreneurs in the agricultural sector. It has also been observed that this situation has resulted in reduction of the attractiveness of young entrepreneurs for agriculture, increase of the amount of uncultivated land and reduction of food production. Therefore, as per a budget proposal 2025, an amount of Rs. 500 million has been allocated to implement a concessional loan scheme for the development of entrepreneurs engaged in the agriculture and industry sector. This project will be implemented for a period of 05 years, and it has been proposed to provide a maximum loan facility of Rs. 05 million to a beneficiary. The interest to be charged from the beneficiaries under the relevant concessional loan scheme is 4% per annum of the outstanding loan amount. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation to implement the relevant concessional loan scheme jointly with the Bank of Ceylon, People's Bank and the Regional Development Bank, enabling to achieve Government's objective of creating 50,000 agricultural entrepreneurs within 05 years as well, was approved by the Cabinet.
