Making the Samurdhi Community based Banks and Samurdhi 	Community based Banking Societies subject to the Government 	Audit

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2024‑12‑30, to amend the Samurdhi Act No. 1 of 2013, as amended by the Divi Neguma (Amendment) Act No. 2 of 2017, to include an amendment for making Samurdhi Community based Banks and Samurdhi Community based Banking Societies subject to the Government Audit. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Minister of Rural Development, Social Security and Community Empowerment to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette, and thereafter submit the same to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
