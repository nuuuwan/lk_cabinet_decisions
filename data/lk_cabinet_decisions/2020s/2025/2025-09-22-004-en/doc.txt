Treaty on Transfer of Sentenced Persons between Sri Lanka and the Republic of the Philippines

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Transfer of Offenders Act, No.5 of 1995 contains provisions to enable a citizen of another country convicted and sentenced to imprisonment by a court in Sri Lanka to serve his sentence in the country of which he claims to be a citizen. Similarly, the Act also provides for a Sri Lankan citizen who has been sentenced to imprisonment in a particular country, to be transferred back to Sri Lanka to serve his balance period of the imprisonment. Accordingly, it has been proposed to sign a Treaty between Sri Lanka and the Republic of the Philippines on the transfer of sentenced persons. The proposed treaty will pave the way for the prisoners to return to their home countries for social rehabilitation and reintegration into their societies. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to sign the proposed Treaty and thereafter ratify the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
