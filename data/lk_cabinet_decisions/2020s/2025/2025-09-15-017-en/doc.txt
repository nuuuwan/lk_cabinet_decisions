Declaration of "a National Children's Day Week"

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Many countries, including Sri Lanka, celebrate 1st October as the "World Children's Day". This year, under the theme "Protect with Love - To Conquer the World", all institutions dealing with children have jointly planned to implement a series of national programmes for World Children's Day under the guidance of the Ministry of Women and Child Affairs and organization of the Department of Probation and Child Protection Services. The proposal made by the Minister of Women and Child Affairs to declare a "National Children's Day Week" starting from 2025-09-25 ending as at 2025-10-01, including the main national celebration and to implement the following programmes under the same, in parallel to national programmes for World Children's Day, was approved by the Cabinet.
