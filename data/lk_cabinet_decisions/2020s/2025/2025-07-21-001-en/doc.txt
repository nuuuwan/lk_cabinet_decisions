Seeking Cooperation of AI Singapore for the Development of Artificial Intelligence

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Discussions have been held with the "AI Singapore" operating under the National University of Singapore to explore active partnerships and opportunities for knowledge sharing which could contribute to the strategic objectives of Sri Lanka for the development of Artificial Intelligence. For this purpose, a proposal has been submitted for signing a Memorandum of Understanding to formalize the cooperation between the two countries thereby aiming to initiate joint research initiatives, artificial intelligence training programmes and mutual assistance for the development of artificial intelligence technologies enabling to face the local and global challenges. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Digital Economy of Sri Lanka and the National University of Singapore, was approved by the Cabinet.
