Progress of the Mega Scale Development Projects by the end of the First Quarter of the year 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Department of Project Management and Monitoring has presented the Performance Report on Mega Scale Development Projects having reviewed the performance data and information submitted by the relevant Ministries regarding the financial and physical progress of Mega Scale Development Projects implemented by Line Ministries by the end of the first quarter of 2025,  According to the said Report, by the end of the first quarter of 2025, 205 Large-Scale Development Projects including 21 newly added projects are at on-going status. Furthermore, it is expected to complete 145 projects including 74 projects that were scheduled to be completed before the first quarter of 2025 in the year 2025, 24 projects in 2026, 19 projects in 2027, and 09 projects in 2028. Accordingly, the proposals made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development for all relevant Ministries and other authorities to take necessary action to complete these projects within the targeted timeframes, was approved by the Cabinet.
