UK Sanctions on four Sri Lankans

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The attention of the Cabinet was drawn to the recent decision taken by the United Kingdom to impose sanctions on 04 Sri Lankans. The Cabinet decided to appoint a Ministerial Committee with the following composition to examine the above matter and submit a Report with recommendations to the Cabinet on the future action to be taken and to authorize the Committee to co-opt the servìces of any other officer/expert with expertise in the relevant subject matter deemed necessary for its deliberations:

*	Hon. Vijitha Herath
  Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism

*	Hon. Harshana Nanayakkara (Attorney-at-law)
  Minister of Justice and National Integration

*	Hon. Aruna Jayasekara
  Deputy Minister of Defence
