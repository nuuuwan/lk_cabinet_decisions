Establishment of a Trusted Digital Repository at the National Archives (Phase 01)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-16 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2021‑04‑05 granted approval to implement the Trusted Digital Repository at the National Archives Phase-01 Project. Through the proposed project, it is expected to install software essential for the preservation of information and providing access thereto, which is the primary function of the National Archives. The International Archival Council has agreed to provide a grant of Euro 7,000 to the National Archives through a fund of the said Council and it has been planned to utilize this amount to cover a portion of the cost of the proposed project. The implementation of the proposed project will enable the National Archives to provide its services to the general public more effectively and efficiently. Accordingly, the  proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to implement the proposed project for establishing a Trusted Digital Repository within the period from 2025-2027, was approved by the Cabinet.
