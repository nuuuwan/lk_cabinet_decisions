Reactivation of the Co operative Wholesale Establishment

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The then Cabinet, at its' meeting held on 2024 09 02, has decided to repeal the Co operative Wholesale Establishment Act No. 47 of 1949 and liquidate the said Establishment. However, the Co-operative Wholesale Establishment has the potential to play a crucial role in implementing the role of the Government in market operations with the objective of ensuring the continuous supply of quality goods and service to the general public at reasonable prices by promoting market competition in keeping with the policy framework of 'A Thriving Nation; A Beautiful Life',  Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Co-operative Development to rescind the previous  Cabinet decision pertaining to the liquidation of the Co-operative Wholesale Establishment and resume its' business activities based on a viable business plan, was approved by the Cabinet.
