Appointment to a Post of Deputy Secretary to the Treasury

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mr. H.C.D.L Silva, who served as a Deputy Secretary the Treasury, retired from the Public Service with effect from 2025‑10‑06 upon the completion of 60 years of age. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to appoint Mr. A.N. Hapugala, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Accountants’ Service, presently serving as the Director General of the Department of Treasury Operations, to the said vacant Post of Deputy Secretary to the Treasury, was approved by the Cabinet.
