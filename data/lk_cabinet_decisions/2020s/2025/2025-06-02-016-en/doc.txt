Agreement for the Transfer of Sentenced Persons between the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Republic of Latvia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Transfer of Offenders Act, No.5 of 1995, has been introduced to provide provisions for the transfer to a specified country of a citizen of such country convicted of an offence in Sri Lanka and for the transfer to Sri Lanka of any citizen of Sri Lanka convicted of an offence in a specified country. However, the two countries concerned need to enter into an Agreement to make applicable the provisions of the said Act. The Cabinet has granted its approval on 2023‑12‑05 to enter into an Agreement on the transfer of sentenced persons, in accordance with the agreement reached in conformity to the laws existing in both countries, Sri Lanka and Latvia. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration for obtaining the policy approval of the present Cabinet, to sign the relevant Agreement and thereafter ratify the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
