Procurement of Coal for the Lakvijaya Power Plant, Norochcholai for the forthcoming season of 2025-2026

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ceylon Electricity Board has estimated that the coal requirement of the Norochcholai Lakvijaya Power Plant for the season 2025/2026 will be 2.32 million MT  ± 10% (39 shipments). Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy, to invite bids for the procurement of coal from registered suppliers of Lanka Coal Company(Pvt) Ltd. for the said period , was approved by the Cabinet.
