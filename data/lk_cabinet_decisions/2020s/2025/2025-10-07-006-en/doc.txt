Construction of an Airport Facility Complex at the Colombo International Airport, Ratmalana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2019‑09‑17 to initiate the procurement process for the construction of an Airport Facility Complex at the Colombo International Airport, Ratmalana. Accordingly, bids were invited under the National Competitive bidding method and nine bidders have submitted their bids. Based on the recommendations made by the relevant Bid Evaluation Committee and the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, after evaluating the said bids, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation, to award the relevant contract to Sanken Construction (Private) Limited, the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, for a sum of Rs.3,046.29 million (without VAT), was approved by the Cabinet.
