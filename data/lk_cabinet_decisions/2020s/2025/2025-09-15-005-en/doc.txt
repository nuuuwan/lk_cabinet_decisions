Obtaining Grant Assistance of the Government of Japan for the Project for the Enhancement of Productivity in the Dairy Sector and for the Official Security Assistance to the Sri Lanka Navy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Japan has agreed to provide a Grant of Japanese Yen 963 million (approximately Rs. 1.94 billion), as Japanese Yen 463 million for the Project for the Enhancement of Productivity in the Dairy Sector and as Japanese Yen 500 million for the Project for Official Security Assistance to the Sri Lanka Navy, in the backdrop of long-standing cooperation between the two countries. Necessary equipment and technical assistance are due to be provided to improve the capacity of the Department of Animal Production and Health under the Project for the Enhancement of Productivity in the Dairy Sector, while donations are to be provided for the supply of drones for reconnaissance, surveillance and monitoring activities of the Sri Lanka Navy under the Project for Official Security Assistance to the Sri Lanka Navy. The President is scheduled to sign the Exchange of Notes and other documents related to these projects during his forthcoming state visit to Japan, and the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to pursue action accordingly, was approved by the Cabinet.
