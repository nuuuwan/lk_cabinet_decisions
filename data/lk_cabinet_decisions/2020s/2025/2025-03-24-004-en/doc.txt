Procurement for the supply of Lubricants for the Sapugaskanda Power Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited from the suppliers recommended by the Original Equipment Manufacturer as specified in the Operation and Maintenance Manual to award the contract for the supply of lubricants for SEMT Pielstick PC 4.2 Diesel Engines (Plant A) and MAN B&W 8L Diesel Engines (Plant B) installed at the Sapugaskanda  Power Station, from March 2025 to February 2026. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Energy to award the contract for the supply of 414,000 liters of lubricants for the Plant A to Lanka IOC PLC, and to award the contract for the supply of 389,000 liters of lubricants for the Plant B to Chevron Lubricants Lanka PLC., was approved by the Cabinet.
