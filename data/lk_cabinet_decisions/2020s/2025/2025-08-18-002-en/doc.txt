Providing fertilizer subsidy to farmers through Digital Implementation Methodology

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In providing fertilizer subsidy to the farmers by the Government, the need has arisen to ensure that the said subsidy is received by the relevant farmers on time and that the said financial assistance is fully utilized by them for fertilizer requirement for their farming activities. Therefore, the appropriateness of using a QR Code or a suitable digital methodology to carry out the provision of fertilizer to the farmers in a more systematic and efficient manner by facilitating the said follow-up purpose as well, has been identified. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development and Minister of Digital Economy and the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation to identify all farmers eligible for the fertilizer subsidy programme through the Regional Officers under the Ministry in charge of the subject of Agriculture and to develop a QR Code system or a suitable digital methodology to provide the fertilizer subsidy for farmers so qualified, was approved by the Cabinet.
