Implementation of the "Take Care - Surekemu Magathota" Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A school-based awareness project under the theme of "Take Care - Surekemu Magathota" has been planned to be implemented centering school children in order to make the road a safe place for all by preventing the daily increasing road accidents.  The project has been scheduled to be implemented by Independent Television Network Limited jointly with the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, the Ministry of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation, the Sri Lanka Police and the Department of Motor Traffic. The project is expected to be implemented in 100 Education Zones in coordination with school traffic pilot teams and student prefects. It is planned to get a total of 50,000 students with 500 students from each zone, directly involved in this programme. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to implement the proposed Project through Independent Television Network Limited, with the association of relevant institutions, under the supervision of the Ministry of Health and Mass Media, was approved by the Cabinet.
