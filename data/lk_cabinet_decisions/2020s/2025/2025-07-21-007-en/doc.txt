Awarding the contracts for the Aviation Insurance Policies and Aviation Insurance Services of SriLankan Airlines Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited under the Limited National Competitive Procurement Method for the procurement of 04 Aviation Insurance Policies for SriLankan Airlines Limited from 2025‑08‑11 to 2026‑08‑10 and for obtaining Aviation Insurance Services from 2025‑08‑11 to 2028‑08‑10. Three (03) bids have been submitted for the same. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to award the said contracts to Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General Limited, based on the recommendations of the Bid Evaluation Committee and the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
