Providing underutilized lands/properties under the State institutions  to Suitable Investors for optimal utilization

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed through the Budget 2025 to identify underutilized lands under the institutions such as the Land Reform Commission, Regional Plantation Companies, National Livestock Development Board, Sri Lanka State Plantations Corporation, and Janatha Estate Development Board, and provide the said lands for private investments including small and medium scale enterprises so that they could be utilized for economically productive projects.  Under this programme, investment opportunities are expected to be provided for plantation/agricultural activities, plantation-related tourism promotion activities, livestock development, hydroelectric power generation, solar power generation, factory-related production activities, inland fisheries industry, and other plantation-related economic innovations. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Land & Irrigation for calling Expressions of Interest to select suitable investors for the identified lands, was approved by the Cabinet.
