Amendment to the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No.15 of 1979 	(Conduct of Inquest of Death)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- At the meeting held on 2022‑10‑25, Cabinet has decided to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill, enabling to improve the existing legal framework and prevailing procedures and practices about the conduct of Inquests into Death of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act. Accordingly, the Legal Draftsman has prepared a preliminary draft for that purpose. As per the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration for granting policy approval of the new Cabinet to finalize the preparation of the said Bill, it was decided to grant the policy approval of the Cabinet to prepare the said Bill.
