Appointment to the Post of Commissioner General of Labour

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-17 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mr. H.K.K.A. Jayasundara, presently serving as the Commissioner General of Labour of the Department of Labour, has requested to be released from the Post of Commissioner General of Labour enabling him to serve in some other Post, as he has completed approximately a service period of 23 years in the said Department. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Labour to attach him to the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils, and Local Government, enabling to be appointed to some other suitable post and to appoint Mrs. H.M.D.N.K. Wataliyadda, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, to the Post of Commissioner General of Labour which falls vacant accordingly, was approved by the Cabinet.
