Procurement for the supply of 500,000 pre-filled syringe of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, 6,000IU in 0.6ml

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the procurement of supplying 500,000 pre-filled syringe of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, 6,000IU in 0.6ml used for the treatment of heart patients. Four bids have been received for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to M/s. Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd., India, the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, based on the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the High-Level Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
