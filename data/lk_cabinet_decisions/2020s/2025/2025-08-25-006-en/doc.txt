Procurement for the supply of 20,000 of Nanocrystal Silver Dressing (20cm x 40cm in size)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

International competitive bids have been invited for the procurement of 20,000 Nanocrystal Silver Dressings (20cm x 40cm in size) used for the treatment and management of wounds. Only one bid has been received for the same. Accordingly, based on the recommendation of the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to CIC Holdings PLC, Sri Lanka (Manufacturer: Smith & Nephew Medical Limited, United Kingdom) the only responsive bidder, for a sum of Rs. 639.5 million (+VAT), was approved by the Cabinet.
