Procurement of 05 power sets for the Department of Sri Lanka 	Railways

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Department of Sri Lanka Railways does not currently possess a number of power sets suitable to be deployed for suburban passenger trains. Therefore, there is a constant passenger congestion in the trains that are operated daily. Since most of the existing power sets have been in operation for over 20 years, they are constantly subject to sudden outages and breakdowns. Under that backdrop, it is observed that passengers who use the train for their daily activities are facing many inconveniences. At least 96 power sets are required to maintain the currently scheduled train services, and at the moment there is a shortage of several power sets. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to procure 05 power sets to be deployed in suburban and long distance services, was approved by the Cabinet.
