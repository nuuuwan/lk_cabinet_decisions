Appointment of a new Delimitation Committee to review the recommendations of the Delimitation Committee held in the years 2012 and 2021 and submit recommendations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A Delimitation Committee has been appointed in the year 2012 to establish and revise Divisional Secretariat Divisions and Grama Niladhari Divisions, and measures have been taken to implement only some of the recommendations of the said Committee. Subsequently, a Delimitation Committee has been appointed again in the year 2021, and no actions have been taken so far regarding the recommendations of the said Committee.  The proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to submit the recommendations of the said Committee Report to the relevant Regional Coordination Committees and District Coordination Committees and obtain their recommendations, and to appoint a new Delimitation Committee to review the said recommendations and submit a Report with recommendations, was approved by the Cabinet.
