Submission of the revised Welfare Benefits Payment Scheme for approval of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025-03-17 to revise the Aswesuma Welfare Benefits Payment Scheme by increasing the values ​​of the benefits paid as of now, under the above Scheme. Accordingly, the revised Aswesuma Welfare Benefits Payment Scheme had been published by the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2429/02 dated 2025-03-24. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to submit the said revised Aswesuma Welfare Benefits Payment Scheme to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
