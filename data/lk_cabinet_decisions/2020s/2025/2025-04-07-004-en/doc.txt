Awarding the contract for the purchase of 05 shipments of  Gasoline 92 Unl during the period from 2025 04 15 to 2025 10 14

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited form the registered suppliers of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation to purchase 05 shipments of Gasoline 92 Unl 300,000+/-5% each for a period of six(06) months from 2025.04.15 to 2025.10.14. Seven (07) suppliers have submitted bids for the purpose. The proposal made by the Minister of Energy, to award the said procurement to M/s Vitol Asia Pte.Ltd., Singapore, the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, based on the recommendations of the Standing Technical Evaluation Committee and the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
