Re-commencement of the currently suspended Health Care Infrastructure Projects

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Due to the Covid-19 pandemic and the subsequent economic crisis, 33 ongoing development projects in healthcare institutions have been suspended. Considering the current state of healthcare infrastructure and the increasing demand for services, it is essential to complete those projects duly. It has been identified that the total cost estimates and project timelines of the relevant projects need to be revised, due to the suspension of the projects and the increase in the cost of construction materials and labor due to inflation, prior to the re commencement of 17 projects out of them.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to revise the total cost estimates of the said projects and extend the project timelines, based on the recommendations given by the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
