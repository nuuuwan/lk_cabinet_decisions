Amendment to the Betting and Gaming Levy Act, No.40 of 1988

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The following proposals were made in the Budget Proposals - 2025 to increase the Government revenue.  *	Increasing the Gross Collection Levy from 15% to 18%.  *	Increasing the Casino Entrance Levy imposed on Sri Lankan citizens from USD 50 to USD100. 

	Accordingly, a Bill has been prepared by the Legal Draftsman to amend the Betting and Gaming Levy Act, No.40 of 1988, enabling to implement the said proposals. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the said Bill. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter to present the same in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
