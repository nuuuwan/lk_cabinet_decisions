Treaty between Sri Lanka and Maldives on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The absence of an Agreement on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters between Sri Lanka and the Maldives has led to many difficulties in engaging in joint investigations. Therefore, approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022‑09‑22 to enter into an Agreement between the two countries, the relevant Agreement has not yet been signed. The Maldivian Government has extended its concurrence to the proposed draft Agreement and the clearance of the Attorney General has also been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to sign the Treaty on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
