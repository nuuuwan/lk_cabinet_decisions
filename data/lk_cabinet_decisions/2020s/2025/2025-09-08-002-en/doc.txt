Ratification of the Agreement under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas beyond National Jurisdiction

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024-12-23 to sign the Agreement on Marine Biological Diversity of Areas beyond National Jurisdiction and establish an Inter Ministerial Committee in that regard. Accordingly, Sri Lanka has signed the said Agreement on 2025-02-20 and if Sri Lanka becomes one of the first 60 States to ratify and bring the Agreement into force and a founding member State thereof, Sri Lanka would be able to accrue numerous benefits including conservation and sustainable use of ocean’s resources and the equitable sharing of benefits derived from them. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism for Ratification of the Agreement on Biological Diversity of Areas beyond National Jurisdiction, was approved by the Cabinet.
