Appointment to the post of District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Kurunegala

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-03-10 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Since Mr. R.M.R.Rathnayake, who served as the District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Kurunegala, has retired from the public service on 2025‑03‑03, the said post has become vacant. Mr. D.M.K.C. Dissanayake, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, who is currently serving as the Secretary to the Provincial Chief Ministry of the North Western Province, has been identified as a suitable Officer for the said post. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to appoint Mr. D.M.K.C. Dissanayake as the District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Kurunegala, with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
