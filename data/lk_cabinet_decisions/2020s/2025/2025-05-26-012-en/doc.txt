Submission of Regulations made under the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act, No.2 of 2018, for approval of Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet of Ministers has granted approval on 2025-03-03 for the promulgation of Regulations on the 'Application for initiating an investigation on imposition of Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duty Regulations 2025' and the 'Procedure for Price Undertakings Regulations 2025' in accordance with the provisions of the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duty Act, No.2 of 2018 to safeguard local industries and entrepreneurs from dumping of the imported goods as well as subsidized goods from foreign countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Co-operative Development to submit the ''Application for Initiating an Investigation on Imposition of Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duty Regulations 2025, made under the provisions of the said Act and published by the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2429/32 dated 2025 03 27, for approval of Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
