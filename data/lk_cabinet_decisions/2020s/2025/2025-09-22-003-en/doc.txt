Signing a Treaty on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters between Sri Lanka and the Republic of Philippines

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The process of providing or seeking assistance by one State to another State in serving of judicial documents and gathering evidence for use in criminal cases is known as Mutual Legal Assistance. It is anticipated that entering into a formal Agreement on mutual legal assistance, will lead to a solid corporation and enables effective crime prevention, investigation, prosecution and suppression of crimes. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to sign an Agreement on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters between Sri Lanka and Philippine, was approved by the Cabinet.
