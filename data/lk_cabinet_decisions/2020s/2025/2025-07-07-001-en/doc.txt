Appointment of a Three-Member Committee to inquire into the activities of the Central Cultural Fund during the period from the year 2017 to 2020

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-07-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been reported that various financial irregularities have occurred in providing financial assistance to religious places and various activities by the Central Cultural Fund during the period from the year 2017 to 2020. It has been decided at the 223rd meeting of the Board of Governors of the Central Cultural Fund held on 2025‑05‑17 to appoint a Committee to inquire into the alleged irregularities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to appoint a Three-Member Committee under the Chairmanship of Justice G.M.W. Pradeep Jayathilaka, retired High Court Judge, for the said purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
