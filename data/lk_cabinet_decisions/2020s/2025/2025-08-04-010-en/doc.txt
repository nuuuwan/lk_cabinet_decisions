Development of the Pettah Central Bus Stand under the 'Clean Sri Lanka' Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Due to lack of repairs and modernizations over decades, the access roads, bus yards, passenger waiting areas, rest rooms for drivers and conductors, administrative buildings, sanitation and drainage systems of the Pettah Central Bus Stand are currently in a very dilapidated state. As a consequence, passengers using the Pettah Central Bus Stand, as well as drivers and conductors and the staff engaged in operational activities, face severe inconveniences. Therefore, the renovation and development of the Pettah Central Bus Stand to suit the present day has been identified as an urgent and timely need. It is estimated that approximately Rs.425 million will be incurred for the relevant modernization and development works. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to modernize and develop the Pettah Central Bus Stand, using the provisions allocated for the 'Clean Sri Lanka' programme, with the direct labor contribution of the Sri Lanka Air Force and with the contribution and cooperation of other relevant institutions including the Colombo Municipal Council, the Ceylon Electricity Board, the National Water Supply and Drainage Board, the Road Development Authority and the Urban Development Authority, was approved by the Cabinet.
