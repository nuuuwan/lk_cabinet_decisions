Rehabilitation of a section of the Southern Expressway from Kottawa to Dodangoda

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The section of the Southern Expressway from Kottawa to Dodangoda was opened for traffic in 2011 and no major repairs have been carried out so far. The expressway section from 0km to 19km and the expressway section from 19km to 34km have been identified as requiring urgent repairs under two (2) packages. Accordingly, the damaged expressway section from km 0km to 19km, covering a 19km stretch of 4-lane expressway, has been planned to be renovated under the Package 02. Bids have been invited from local contractors under the National Competitive Bidding Procedure and 05 bids have been received. The proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to award the relevant contract to        M/s Access-WKK Joint Venture, the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, for an amount of Rs.1,490.96 million, based on the recommendations of the Bid Evaluation Committee and the Standing High-Level Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
