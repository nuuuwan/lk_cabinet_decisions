Signing of a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka Institute of Biotechnology (Pvt) Ltd. and the University of Illinois of the United States of America

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-08-04 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka Institute of Biotechnology (Pvt) Ltd. and the University of Illinois of the United States of America facilitating activities and programmes such as human resource skills development in genetic research and exchange of postgraduate students and staff. The concurrence of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism has been received for the proposed draft Memorandum of Understanding. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Science and Technology to sign the said MoU between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
