Implementation of TB Component and HIV Component under the Global Fund to Fight Aids, Tuberculosis and Malaria (GFATM) Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-06-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Global Fund to Fight Aids, Tuberculosis and Malaria (GFATM) Project has been implemented since 2002 under the Ministry of Health and Mass Media to manage, prevent and control three diseases: HIV/AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria. Since 2021, only two components - TB and HIV have been implemented under the said project. It has been identified that it would be appropriate to implement the 2025-2027 Grant Cycle to achieve the set targets and objectives of the National Strategic Plan for TB and the National Strategic Plan for HIV 2023-2027 in order to end the HIV/AIDS epidemic in Sri Lanka.  The Global Fund to Fight Aids, Tuberculosis and Malaria has agreed to provide allocation of US$ 2.97 million for the National Strategic Plan for TB and allocation of US$ 6.38 million for the National Strategic Plan for HIV/AIDS during the period from 2025‑2027. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to implement the said two projects separately by utilizing the said allocations, was approved by the Cabinet.
