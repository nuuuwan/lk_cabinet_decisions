Appointment of Officers and Employees to the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption on temporary basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption has been given a higher level of independence by the Anti-Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023 and the said Act contains provisions for the appointment of a dedicated staff by the Commission. The Anti-Corruption Act also includes provisions for the appointment of any officer or employee of the Public Service, Provincial Public Service, Audit Service and State Corporation, to the Commission on a temporary basis. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to take necessary action to appoint suitable officers from among the officers serving in the Public Service, Police Service, Audit Service, Provincial Public Service as well as State Corporations on temporary basis in accordance with the provisions of subsections 26(4), 26(5), 26(6), 26(8) and 26(9) of the Anti-Corruption Act, in order to carry out the activities of the Commission efficiently until the permanent staff for the Commission is appointed, was approved by the Cabinet.
