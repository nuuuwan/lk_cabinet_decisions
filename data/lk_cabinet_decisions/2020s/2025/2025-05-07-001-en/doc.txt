Statement on financial performance of the Government - 1st Quarter 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- As per section 53(1) of the Public Financial Management Act, No.44 of 2024, the Minister in-charge of the subject of Finance shall submit a statement on financial performance including revenue and expenditure of the Government, to the Cabinet not later than 45 days after the end of each quarter. As per the said provision, the Statement on Financial Performance for the 1st quarter of the year 2025 ending on 2025‑03‑31 should be submitted to the Cabinet on or before 2025‑05‑15. Accordingly, the Cabinet noted the Report on the Financial Performance submitted by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, prepared including the following cumulative revenue and expenditure reported by all the Special Spending Units, Ministries, Departments and District Secretariats for the first quarter of the year 2025 from 2025‑01‑01 to 2025‑03‑31, based on the classifications stated in the Annual budget estimates 2025.
