Renewal of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Machinery  Manufacturing Cooperation between the Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development of Sri Lanka 	and the Ministry of Industry and Trade of Viet Nam

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-04-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed between the then Ministry in charge of the subject of Industry and the Ministry of Industry and Trade of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam in 2011 to promote bilateral cooperation in the Machinery and Mechanical appliances sector, including the Electrical Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing between the two countries. At the second meeting of the Sub-Committee on Trade between the two countries held in 2019, both parties have agreed to renew the said MoU. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to sign the updated Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Machinery Manufacturing Cooperation, which was amended facilitating the exchange of advanced technologies and providing access to training and capacity building activities for local stakeholders, was approved by the Cabinet.
