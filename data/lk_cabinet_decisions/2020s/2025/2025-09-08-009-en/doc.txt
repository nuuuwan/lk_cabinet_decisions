Allocation of additional provisions for Road Development Projects

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-09-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Out of the provisions of Rs.14,000 million allocated for the improvements of rural roads through the budget estimates for the year 2025 and rural bridges,  Rs.11,500 million has been allocated for rural road development, and subsequently, approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2025-08-11 to provide another Rs.5,000 million for the said program. The total value of projects identified for the implementation under the Rural Road Development Programme in each District by all the District Coordination Committees of the country is approximately Rs.26 billion. Taking these factors into consideration, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to allocate an additional provision of Rs.6,000 million for the Rural Road Rehabilitation and Improvement Programme by managing the local financing provisions allocated to the Ministry of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation for the year 2025.
