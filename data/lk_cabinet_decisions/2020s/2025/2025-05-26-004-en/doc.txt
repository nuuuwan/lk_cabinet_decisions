“Marine Nights :Awakening Colombo” Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2025-05-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The need for a structured approach has been identified to enhance the nighttime tourist activities thereby boosting the domestic economy, revitalizing tourism industry and enriching tourist experiences available in the Colombo City. Accordingly, it has been planned to implement a project to develop the Colombo Marine Drive spanning approximately 7.4 kilometers from Kollupitiya Railway Station to Dehiwala under the theme 'Marine Nights: Awakening Colombo'. There, it is expected to focus on the area of approximately 400 meters from Galle Face end to St. Thomas' Preparatory College. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism to implement the proposed project, was approved by the Cabinet.
