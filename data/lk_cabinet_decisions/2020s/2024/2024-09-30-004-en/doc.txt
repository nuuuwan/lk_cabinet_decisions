Appointment of Senior Advisors to the President

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In terms of paragraph (1) of Article 41 of the Constitution, the President is vested with the power to appoint his staff in consultation with the Cabinet of Ministers. Accordingly, it was decided to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet to appoint Mr. Duminda Hulangamuwa and Dr. A.A.J.Fernando as Senior Advisors to the President on Economic Affairs and Finance on honorary service basis, with effect from 2024-09-24.
