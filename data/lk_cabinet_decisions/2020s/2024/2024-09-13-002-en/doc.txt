Signing the Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Export Development Board and the International Trade Centre (ITC) to launch the ITC SheTrades Hub in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Export Development Board of Sri Lanka has proposed to sign a non-financial MoU with the International Trade Centre (ITC) to launch an "ITC SheTrades Hub in Sri Lanka", to increase the participation and competitiveness of Women-Led Businesses (WLBs) in Sri Lanka in international trade. Through this the women entrepreneurs will get training opportunities on topics related to international trade such as markets and investment opportunities and get the required technical assistance. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Export Development Board and the International Trade Centre, was approved by the Cabinet.
