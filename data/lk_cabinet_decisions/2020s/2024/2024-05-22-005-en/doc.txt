Obtaining the Affiliated membership of the Foundation for Environmental	Education for the project of establishing Blue Flag Beaches in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Various programmes have been introduced to protect the environment of the coastal areas crucial for the tourism industry and to ensure the safety and health conditions of the tourists visiting coastal regions. The Blue Flag Beach Certification Programme is one of the prominent programmes out of those programmes and is also a popular eco-friendly brand among the tourists. The Blue Flag Certification is issued under this programme for the beach in concern on meeting 32 criteria under four (04) main elements of water quality, environmental management, environmental education information and the people's safety. The Blue Flag Certification is issued by the Foundation of Environmental Education in Denmark and in order to issue the Blue Flag Certificates in Sri Lanka, it is necessary to have an organization in this country that has obtained the affiliated membership of the said institution. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to obtain the affiliated membership of the Foundation of Environmental Education in Denmark to the Marine Environment Protection Authority for two (02) years and enter into a Collaborative Agreement with the Foundation for Environmental Education for that purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
