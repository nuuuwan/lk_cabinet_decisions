Procurement of Supply of Food items (fresh, dry rations and others) required	for Tri-forces for year 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Defense to award the procurement for the supply of food items (fresh, dry rations and others) for the Tri- Forces for the year 2024 at a total value of 84.2 Billion Rupees (without VAT) to the suppliers recommended by the Cabinet appointed Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
