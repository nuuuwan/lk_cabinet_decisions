Digitization of Terrestrial Television Broadcasting Project to be implemented under the Japanese Loan Facility

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The approval of the Cabinet has been granted in the year 2009 to convert the Sri Lanka Television Broadcasting to a broadcasting with Digital technology in place of the existing Analog technology, in line with the global trends. Due to various reasons, the implementation of the said work has been delayed and approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022‑07‑27 to implement the Digitization of Terrestrial Television Broadcasting Project under the ISDB-T Japanese technology. Since the Japan International Cooperation Agency has agreed to provide the necessary funds for the project as of now, it has been possible to start the work of the project. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, National Integration, Social Security and Mass Media to appoint a Special Committee with the Chairmanship of the Secretary to the Ministry in charge of the subject of Mass Media and comprising of representatives from other relevant institutions to examine the present day requirements and technical matters so as to ensure the implementation Digitization of Terrestrial Television Broadcasting Project in a way that will benefit the citizens of Sri Lanka and submit a Report with the observations and recommendations to the Cabinet, was approved by the Cabinet.
