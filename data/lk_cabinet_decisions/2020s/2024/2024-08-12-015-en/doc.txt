Publishing the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (Amendment)	Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2022-05-02 has granted approval to amend the National Water Supply and Drainage Board Act. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has also been received for the Bill drafted by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Minister of Water Supply and Estate Infrastructure Development to publish the said (Amendment) Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter, present the same in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
