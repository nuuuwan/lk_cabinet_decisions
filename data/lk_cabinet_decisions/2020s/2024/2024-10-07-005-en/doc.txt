Installation of Exhibition Stall representing Sri Lanka at the TTG - 2024 Tourism Exhibition to be held in Milan, Italy from 9th to 11th October 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- 25 travel agents and hoteliers from the tourism industry will be participating in the TTG - 2024 Tourism Exhibition to be held in Milan, Italy from 09th to 11th October 2024. Accordingly, the Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau has arranged to allocate 100 square meters of the exhibition area to provide facilities for them. Although, the said Bureau has invited bids under the limited international competitive bidding procedure for the construction of the exhibition stalls, no any bid has been received. Accordingly, it was decided to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet for the facts submitted by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism stating that the arrangements have been made to procure relevant services from Prostand S.r.l, the official construction company of the event organizers, considering the limited time period.
