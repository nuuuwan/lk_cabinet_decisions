Amendment to the Medical Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-06 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2023‑10‑09 has granted approval to implement the recommendations submitted by the Committee appointed under the Chairmanship of the Secretary to the Prime Minister to submit recommendations to amend the provisions of the Medical Ordinance to formulate a National Policy including standards and criteria to be based for the recognition of Medical Degree courses of the internationally recognized Universities,  in Sri Lanka and to suit with the present day needs. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman to amend the Medical Ordinance based on the said recommendations. The proposal made by the Minister of Health to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
