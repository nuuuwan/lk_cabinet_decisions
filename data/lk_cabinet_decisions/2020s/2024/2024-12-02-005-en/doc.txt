Appointment to the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Kandy

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Post of District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Kandy has fallen vacant from 2024-11-25.  The proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to appoint Mr. U.P. Indika Anuruddha Piyadasa, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as Divisional Secretary, Ampara, to the Post of District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Kandy with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
