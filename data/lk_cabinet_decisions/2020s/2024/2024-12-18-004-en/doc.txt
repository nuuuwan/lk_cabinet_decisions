Financial Grant provided by the Government of India to fulfill the vehicle requirement of the Sri Lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of India has agreed to provide a financial Grant of 300 million  Sri Lankan Rupees as an assistance to fulfill the vehicle requirement of the Sri Lanka Police. It has been planned to provide Cabs by utilizing the said grant for duty requirements of Police Stations in the Northern Province. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties to obtain the said Grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
