Declaration of East Container Terminal Project as a Specified Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2021-02-01 to develop the East Container Terminal by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority in a phased out manner and to operate the same as a fully owned container terminal of the said Authority.  Accordingly, the contract for the relevant civil works has been awarded already and the construction is being carried out. Similarly, the procurement for the purchase of cranes required for the said container terminal has also been awarded. The private terminal operators and the service providers of the Port of Colombo have been provided various Tax concessions under the Strategic Development Act and the Board of Investment Law. Taking these matters also into consideration, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to declare the East Container Terminal as a “Specified Project” by the Minister in-charge of Finance, was approved by the Cabinet.
