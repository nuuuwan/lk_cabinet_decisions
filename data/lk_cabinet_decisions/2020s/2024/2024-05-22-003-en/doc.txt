Taking over the Base Hospital, Pottuvil by the Central Government and	developing as a specialized hospital for Medical Tourism in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Base Hospital, Pottuvil which had been providing services since early 19th century, is one of the oldest hospitals in Sri Lanka. Considering the current demand for health facilities in the area, it has been identified the appropriateness of converting the above hospital into a Base Hospital providing integrated health care with paying ward facilities for tourists, palliative care services, elderly care services and Ayurvedic treatment facilities for tourism. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to vest the Base Hospital, Pottuvil which is currently under the purview of the Ministry of Health-Eastern Province, with the Ministry of Health with the concurrence of the Governor of the Eastern Province and make the said hospital function as the Integrated Health Care Institution in the South-East region of the country including all the necessary facilities for the promotion of the Medical Tourism, was approved by the Cabinet.
