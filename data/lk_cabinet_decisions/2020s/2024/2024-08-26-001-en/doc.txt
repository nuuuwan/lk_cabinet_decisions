Signing of the Financing Agreement for the Second Phase of the Development Policy Financing Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The World Bank supports Sri Lanka's comprehensive reform agenda through the Development Policy Financing Programme, in parallel with the International Monetary Fund's Extended Fund Facility. The programme is a two-year programme, and its first phase has been successfully completed in the year 2023. The World Bank has agreed to provide an amount of USD 150.6 million (equivalent to 200 million US dollars) of Special Drawing Rights (SDR) to support the implementation of the Reform Plan of the Government under the second phase of the Development Policy Financing Programme. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to enter into the relevant Agreement with the International Development Association of the World Bank Group in relation to obtaining the said financial facility, was approved by the Cabinet.
