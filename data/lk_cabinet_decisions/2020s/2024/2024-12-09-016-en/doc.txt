Granting 55,000 MT of Muriate of Potash Fertilizer to the Government of Sri Lanka as a donation under the World Food Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted on 2024-05-06 to obtain 55,000MT of Muriate of Potash Fertilizer to the Government of Sri Lanka as a donation under the World Food Programme. The Tripartite Agreement related thereto has been signed on 2024-09-20 by the World Food Organization, the Uralchem - Uralkali Group and State Fertilizer Company Limited on behalf of the Government of Sri Lanka. The vessel carrying the said stock of fertilizer is scheduled to arrive in Sri Lanka on 2024-12-10 and the official ceremony of handing over the stock of fertilizer to the Government of Sri Lanka has been planned to be held in the Colombo Port on 2024-12-12. The Cabinet decided to distribute the said total stock of fertilizer for farmers at the rate of 50% to be utilized for Paddy and other Crops and the remaining 50% for Coconut cultivation, taking into consideration the aforesaid information furnished by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation.
