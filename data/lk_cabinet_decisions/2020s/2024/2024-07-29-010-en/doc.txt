Amendments to the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11	of 2021

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been identified the need to amend the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act in order to increase the viability of the Colombo Port City as a globally competitive Special Economic Zone by eliminating the obstacles to commence business operations indicated by the investors who are hoping to engage in businesses in the Colombo Port City. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021 by incorporating the relevant amendments, was approved by the Cabinet.
