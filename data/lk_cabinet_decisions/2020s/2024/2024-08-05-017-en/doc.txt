Eliminate the Pension Anomalies in the Public Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Pension anomalies have arisen for certain group of pensioners due to the periodic salary revisions made by the Government as well as various policy decisions taken by the Cabinet. Accordingly, it has been observed that some injustice has been caused to the Government officials who retired from 2016 01 01 to 2020 01 01, and by removing the said salary anomaly, it will be possible to provide relief to about 83,000 retired Government officials. Therefore, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to revise the pension of the said retired Government Officers so as to receive a maximum of Rs.2500/-, based on the salary, in accordance with the Schedule I of the Public Administration Circular No.03/2016 dates 2016 02 25, was approved by the Cabinet.
