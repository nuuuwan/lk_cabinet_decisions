Ratification of the Sri Lanka - Thailand Free Trade Agreement

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sri Lanka-Thailand Free Trade Agreement was signed by the Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security of Sri Lanka and the Minister of Commerce of Thailand on 2024-02-03 and the said Agreement was tabled in Parliament on 2024-07-12. As per Article 14.10 of the said Agreement, the Agreement will come into force on the thirtieth day after the date of the  notification of the second party, after the last notification made by one party to the other party stating that the internal legal requirements and procedures have been completed. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to ratify the Sri Lanka - Thailand Free Trade Agreement, based on the above matters, was approved by the Cabinet.
