Issuance of a New Digital Driving License

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approximately 15 years have lapsed by now since the commencement of issuing a driving license as a smart card and the Government has recognized the appropriateness of issuing a digital driving license, which is currently used in many countries in the world in keeping with the new technology, facilitating the exchange of data between related institutions. Accordingly, the Cabinet decided to take necessary action to introduce a new digital driving license expeditiously in place of the driving license being used at present and to continue to implement the methodology currently being adopted for the issuance of driving licenses, taking into consideration the proposals made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation.
