Mid  -Year  Fiscal  Position  Report  -  2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In  terms  of  the  Section  10  of  the  Fiscal  Management  (Responsibility)  Act,  No.3  of  2003,  the  Minister  in  charge  of  the  subject  of  Finance  is  required  to  table  the    Mid-Year  Fiscal  Position  Report  in  Parliament  and  release  the  report  to  the  public  by  the  last  day  of  the  month  of  June  in  year  2024.  Accordingly,  Mid-Year  Fiscal  Position  Report  has  been  published  on  the  website  of  the  Ministry  of  Finance,  Economic  Stabilization  and  National  Policies.  Accordingly,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  President  in  his  capacity  as  the  Minister  of  Finance,  Economic  Stabilization  and  National  Policies  to  table  the  said  Report  in  Parliament,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
