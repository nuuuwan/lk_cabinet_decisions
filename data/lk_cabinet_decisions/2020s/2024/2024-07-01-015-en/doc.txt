Obtaining Asian Development Bank Funds to implement the Power Sector	Reforms and Financial Sustainability Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Initial discussions have been held between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Asian Development Bank for the implementation of the Power Sector	 Reforms and Financial Sustainability Programme. The proposed programme consists of 03 sub‑programmes and it is expected to receive a loan amount of	USD 300 million by USD 100 million for each sub-programme. The	proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to obtain a USD 100 million loan from the Asian Development Bank to finance the proposed sub‑programme‑01 for the implementation of institutional structural reforms in the Ceylon Electricity Board and the Lanka Electricity Company (Pvt) Ltd., was approved by the Cabinet.
