Amendments  to  the  Companies  Act,  No.  07  of  2007

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It  has  been  identified  the  necessity  to  amend  the  Companies  Act  to  empower  the  domestic  law  to  combat  money  laundering,  terrorist  financing  and  other  unlawful  activities  and  accordingly  the  policy  approval  has  been  granted  at  the  Cabinet  meeting  held  on  2024‑07‑09  to  amend  the  said  Bill.  Clarence  of  the  Attorney  General  has  been  received  for  the  Bill  prepared  by  the  Legal  Draftsman  for  the  same.  As  such,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Industries  to  publish  the  said  amending  Bill  in  the  Government  Gazette  and  submit  the  same  thereafter,  to  Parliament,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
