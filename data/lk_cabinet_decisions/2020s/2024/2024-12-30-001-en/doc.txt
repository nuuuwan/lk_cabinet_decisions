Submission of the Appropriation Bill for the Financial Year 2025 to Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑11‑25 for the preparation of the Appropriation Bill for the Financial Year 2025. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Appropriation Bill for the Financial Year 2025 prepared accordingly by the Legal Draftsman. The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter present it in Parliament for approval.
