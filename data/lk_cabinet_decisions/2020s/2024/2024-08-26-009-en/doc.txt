Printing of School Textbooks required for free distribution in the year	2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2024‑04‑01 has granted approval to print 71 types of textbooks out of the 317 types of textbooks which are to be printed for the year 2025. National Competitive Bids have been invited to print 246 types of remaining school textbooks required to be free distributed to the school children in year 2025.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to get printed the textbooks of the grades other than the Grade 1, 6 and 10 from the Printing Companies who have submitted the lowest substancially responsive bids as per the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
