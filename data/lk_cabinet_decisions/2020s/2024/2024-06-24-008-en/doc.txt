75th Anniversary of Sri Lanka UNESCO partnership

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sri Lanka-UNESCO partnership completes its 75th anniversary in this year, and in order to commemorate the anniversary, the Sri Lanka UNESCO National Commission, the Ministry of Education, other relevant Ministries and Institutions have planned to implement several programmes together. The proposal made by the Minister of Education to conduct the 75th anniversary celebration on 2024‑07‑16 at Nelum Pokuna theatre, with the objectives of commemorating the 75 years partnership and achievements with UNESCO, promoting the tourism industry by making the both local and international community aware on the UNESCO listed cultural heritage in Sr Lanka, further increasing the existing awareness between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
