Regulations promulgated under the Intellectual Property Act, No.36 of 2003 for Registration of Geographical Indications

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2022‑01‑18 to  amend the Intellectual Property Act, No.36 of 2003, incorporating the provisions related to the registration of Geographical Indications. Accordingly, the Intellectual Property (Amendment) Act, No.8 of 2022, has been enacted, incorporating necessary provisions for the registration of Geographical Indications.  Accordingly, the Regulations for the Registration of Geographical Indications have been published through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2407/04 dated 2024‑10‑22. The proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development to present the said Regulations for approval in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
