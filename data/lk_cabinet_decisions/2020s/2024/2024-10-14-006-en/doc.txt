Enhancing the productivity of the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation by 	utilizing the finance received from transferring the land in Ekala	Transmission Station of the said Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The land where the Ekala Transmission Station is located which is owned by the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation has been transferred to Urban Development Authority for a sum of Rs.1,286 million. It has been decided at the Cabinet meeting held on 2023-02-27 that the said amount should be utilized for the restructuring of the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation as a self financing entity. As of now, Rs.525 million from that amount has been received by the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation and Rs.205 million out of that has been spent for the Voluntary Retirement Scheme implemented for the employees of the Corporation. The proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, National Integration, Social Security, and Mass Media to execute 25 functions relevant for the refurbishment of the Sri Lanka Broadcasting Corporation by using Rs.145.72 million from the balalnce amount, was approved by the Cabinet.
