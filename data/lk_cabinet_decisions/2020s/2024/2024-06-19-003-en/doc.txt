Cooperation between the Virginia Ports Authority and the Sri Lanka Ports 	Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Virginia Ports Authority and the Sri Lanka Ports Authority have agreed to enter into a Cooperation Agreement on cooperation in sharing competences between ports under the 'Port and Maritime Skills Development Programme' jointly implemented by the United States Embassy in Sri Lanka and the Colombo Plan. Accordingly, enhancement of professional capabilities of the executive staff of both parties are mainly expected through the entry into the proposed Cooperation Agreement. The Proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to sign the Cooperation Agreement for the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
