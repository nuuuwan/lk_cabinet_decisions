The Strategy to Promote the Commercial Fishing Industry in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- An Expert Committee has been appointed to explore and recommend possible strategies to promote high impact fisheries and sustainable practices in the Sri Lankan fishing industry. The said Committee has submitted its Report named “A Strategy to Promote Sustainable Export Oriented Commercial Fisheries to Support Sri Lanka's Envisaged Economic Transformation”. The said Strategic Framework has suggested strategic proposals to transform the fishing industry in Sri Lanka as a sector that will provide a major contribution for a sectoral growth of 12% and to earn an export revenue of USD 600 million annually by the year 2030, while ensuring the sustainability in line with the principle of Blue Economy. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President and the Minister of Fisheries, to adopt the said Strategic Framework submitted through the said Report as the strategic Framework of the Government pertaining to Sri Lankan Fishing Industry and to implement the said Strategy in keeping with a formal Strategic Framework, was approved by the Cabinet.
