Awarding the Term Contract for the procurement of four (04) shipments of Gas Oil (0.05% M.S.)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited from the registered suppliers for the term contract for the purchase of four (04) shipments of Gas Oil (0.05% M.S.) to Ceylon Petroleum Corporation for the period from 2024‑08‑01 to 2024‑12‑31. The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the contract for the purchase of four (04) shipments of Gas Oil 280,000 Bbls+/-5% during the relevant period to M/s Zhongxi Energy Development (Ningbo) Co. Ltd., China, based on the recommendation made by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee after evaluating the bids received accordingly, was approved by the Cabinet.
