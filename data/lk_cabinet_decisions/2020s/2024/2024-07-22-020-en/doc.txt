Tabling of Order gazetted under Colombo Port City Economic	Commission Act, No.11 of 2021 in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In terms of the Cabinet Decision dated 2024-04-29, the approval of the Cabinet has been granted to designate China Harbour Engineering Company (Lanka) Limited as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance. Accordingly, an Order has been promulgated under Section 53 of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No.11 of 2021 through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2383/34 dated 2024-05-09 designating that company as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to table the said Order in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
