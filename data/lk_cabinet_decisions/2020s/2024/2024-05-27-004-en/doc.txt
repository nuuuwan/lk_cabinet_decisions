Subjecting the Samurdhi Community Based Banks and Samurdhi	Community Based Banking Societies to the Government Audit

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-27 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- 1,092 Samurdhi Community Based Banks and 335 Samurdhi Community Based Bank Societies are functioning throughout the country for the promotion of microfinance activities. These Community Based Banks and Community Based Bank Societies are audited annually by the Internal Audit Division of the Department of Samurdhi Development as per the provisions of the Samurdhi Act and the provisions mentioning that they should be audited by the National Audit Office are not included in the Samurdhi Act. It has been identified that through auditing the Samurdhi Community Based Banks and Samurdhi Community Based Bank Societies by the National Audit Office as well, will enable them to maintain transparency in their operations and develop credibility and recognition. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Women, Child Affairs and Social Empowerment to amend the Samurdhi Act, No.1 of 2013 amended by the Divineguma (Amendment) Act, No.2 of 2017 imposing provisions for that purpose and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill for the same, was approved by the Cabinet.
