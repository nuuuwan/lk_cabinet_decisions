Japanese Grant Aid for Human Resources Development Scholarship	Programme - 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Japan offers Master's Degrees and Doctorate Degree opportunities to the executive level officials in the public sector in Sri Lanka under the Japanese Grant Aid for Human Resources Development Scholarship Programme. Three Frameworks have been completed under this Programme from year 2010 to 2021 and its Fourth Framework has been started in 2022 and is scheduled to end in year 2025. Under this Programme, 225 and 17 opportunities have been offered up to now to study Postgraduate courses and PhD courses respectively and the Government of Japan has agreed to provide a grant assistance of Japanese Yen 304 million (approximately Rs.578 million) for the third batch of the Fourth Framework. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to sign the relevant Exchange of Notes with the Government of Japan to obtain that grant assistance and enter into an agreement with the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) in that regard, was approved by the Cabinet.
