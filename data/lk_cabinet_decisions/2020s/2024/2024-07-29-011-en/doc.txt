Enactment of the new Constitution for Sri Lanka Cricket

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2024‑02‑19 has granted approval to appoint a Committee headed by the Justice Mr. K.T. Chithrasiri, retired Judge of the Supreme Court to draft a new Constitution for Sri Lanka Cricket complying to the recommendations of the Report of the Cabinet Sub-committee appointed under the chairmanship of the Minister of Foreign Affairs to study the issues faced by Sri Lanka Cricket and submit the recommendations to the Cabinet. Accordingly, the new Constitution for Sri Lanka Cricket has been drafted by the said Committee. The joint proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs, Minister of Power and Energy, Minister of Labour and Foreign Employment and Minister of Public Security to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to confirm and implement the proposed Constitution for Sri Lanka Cricket, was approved by the Cabinet.
