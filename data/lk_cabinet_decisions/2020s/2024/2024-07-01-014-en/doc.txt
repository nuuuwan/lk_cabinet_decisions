Comprehensive Strategies to uplift the Small and Medium Scale Enterprise Sector in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Micro, Small and Medium Scale Enterprises make a direct contribution for generating employment, fostering innovation and for economic growth in Sri Lanka. Due to the economic difficulties faced by Sri Lanka in the past period, the Micro, Small and Medium Scale entrepreneurs were severely affected. Therefore, taking into account the need to province investment and working capital assistance to revitalize those entrepreneurs, the Government has taken steps to implement a number of strategies. Accordingly, the project for re‑empowerment of the Micro, Small and Medium Scale Enterprises sector has been implemented under Asian Development Bank funds, in order to implement the loan projects in the Small and Medium Scale Enterprise sector and about 40 billion rupees have been allocated for the year 2024 for the same. In addition, approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑02‑24 for the implementation of an Incentive Loan Scheme worth 20 billion rupees with the aim of re-energizing the Micro, Small and Medium Scale entrepreneurs as well. It is planned to allocate 02 billion rupees from the said provisions and implement a special loan scheme for women entrepreneurs as well by creating a Revolving Fund and the said loan scheme is scheduled to be implemented from 2024‑07‑01. The information presented by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies was considered by the Cabinet.
