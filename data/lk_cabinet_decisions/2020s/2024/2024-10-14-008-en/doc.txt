Resignation from the Post of Secretary to the Leader of the Opposition of 	Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mr. Thisath Dewapriya Banadara Wijayagunawardana who served as Secretary to the Leader of the Opposition of Parliament has submitted a letter to resign from the said post, serving until 2024‑10‑09. As such, the Cabinet granted its concurrence for the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to accept his resignation.
