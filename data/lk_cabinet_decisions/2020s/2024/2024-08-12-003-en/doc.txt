Providing new Housing Units on resettlement basis for the houses of estate workers located irregularly within the Urban Development Project Site at Millewawatta, Horana

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Action is being taken by the Urban Development Authority to grant a blocks of land from the land located at Millewawatta, Horana for the development projects such as the Medical Drugs Manufacturing Village Project, Relocation of Welikada Prison Project, Small and Medium Scale Industries Project and the establishment of a University Village Project  under the Mahapola Higher Education Scholarship Fund. Old estate housing units located irregularly within the proposed land have to be removed expeditiously in order to implement the said projects. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to remove 54 irregular housing units existing being a hindrance to the proposed development projects in the Millewawatta land and provide the occupants of the said housing units each with a block of land in extent of 10 perches with a house free of charge, under 02 phases, on freehold basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
