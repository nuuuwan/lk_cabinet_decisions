Protection  of  Occupants  Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  approval  of  the  Cabinet  has  been  granted  at  the  meeting  held  on  2023-09-04  to  introduce  a  new  law  with  the  aim  to  protect  the  rights  of  the  tenant  and  the  landlord  equally  after  repealing  the  Rent  Act,  No.  7  of  1972.  Accordingly,  the  Bill  prepared  by  the  Legal  Draftsman  has  received  the  clearance  of  the  Attorney  General.  The  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Justice,  Prison  Affairs  and  Constitutional  Reforms  to  publish  the  said  Bill  in  the  Government  Gazette  and  thereafter  present  the  same  for  approval  in  Parliament,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
