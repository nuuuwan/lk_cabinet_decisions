Proceeds of Crime Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Cabinet approval has been granted on 2024-08-12 to publish the Proceeds of Crime Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman in the Government Gazette and present it in Parliament. However, after publishing the said Bill in the Government Gazette, several observations have been submitted by the International Monetary Fund.  Accordingly, the observations submitted by the International Monetary Fund have been referred to the Committee appointed by the then Minster of Justice, Prison Reforms and Constitutional Reforms under the Chairmanship of a Judge of the Supreme Court to prepare the initial draft relating to this Bill and the said Committee has recommended to amend the already prepared Proceeds of Crime Bill by incorporating certain further provisions. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to instruct the Legal Draftsman to further amend the Proceeds of Crime Bill, based on the recommendations of the said Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
