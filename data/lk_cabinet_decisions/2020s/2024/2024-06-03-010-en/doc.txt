Amendments to the Value Added Tax Act, No.14 of 2002

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal made by the Hon.President in his capacity as the Minister ofFinance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the Value Added Tax Act, No.14 of 2002 to prevent the unintended effects of the removal of exemptions from the VAT Act, maintain clarity of the Act, implement the VAT reforms introduced by the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, No.32 of 2023 and to incorporate the amendments identified as recommended by the Temporary VAT Committee appointed as per the instructions of the Integrated Committee headed by the Committee on Public Accounts including the Committee on Public Finance, the Committee on Ways and Means, the Sectoral Oversight Committee on National Economic and Physical Plans and the Sectoral Oversight Committee on Alleviating the Impact of the Economic Crisis and other amendments identified from time to time based on various Cabinet decisions, was approved by the Cabinet.
