Importation of eggs for the price stabilization of the local market

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In terms of the Cabinet Decision dated 2024-03-18, Sri Lanka State Trading (General) Corporation has imported 224 million eggs as of now. 95% of these  has been distributed to the consumers at an affordable price of Rs. 37/- per egg through the Lanka Sathosa outlet network and the deadline given for importing eggs has ended by 2024-04-30. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security to import the required stocks of eggs so as to stabilize the market price of eggs during the upcoming Ramadan and Christmas festival periods as well and to supply eggs required for the industry of bakery products including cakes, was approved by the Cabinet.
