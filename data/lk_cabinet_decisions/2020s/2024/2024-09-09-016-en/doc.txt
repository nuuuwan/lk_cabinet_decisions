Potential for Sri Lanka's Accession to BRICS

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2024‑01‑24 has decided to appoint an Officials' Committee to examine matters on Sri Lanka's Accession to BRICS and submit appropriate recommendations. The Officials' Committee appointed accordingly has submitted its Report having examined the pros and cons of Sri Lanka in accession to the BRICS and the New Development Bank to be established under the same. All stakeholders who attended to that have positively responded in obtaining membership of BRICS. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to apply for the membership of the BRICS and the New Development Bank to be established under the same by following the relevant diplomatic procedures, was approved by the Cabinet.
