Development of Railway Station Buildings as Commercial Centres

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Expressions of Interest have been invited to identify the investors interested in the development of railway stations in Kolpetty, Bambalapitiya, Wellawatta, Compannavidiya, Dehiwala and Mount Lavinia located in Colombo city under the Public‑Private Partnership Methodology. Since Expressions of Interest have been submitted for the development of Railway Stations located at various places in Sri Lanka, it has been found that there is a capacity to develop  railway stations located island wide. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minster of Transport and Highways to invite proposals on competitive basis to develop other railway stations as well under the Public‑Private Partnership Methodology and to select suitable investors, was approved by the Cabinet.
