Procurement of Printing and Supply of computerized draw lottery tickets for the Development Lotteries Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- National Competitive Procurement Process has been followed for printing and supply of lottery tickets of Lagna Wasanawa, Ada Kotipathi, Shanida, Super Boll, Jayoda, Kapruka and Sasiri for computer based draw, to the Development Lotteries Board. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Ministry of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, to award the contract for the printing and supply of lottery tickets to Toppan Forms (Colombo) Ltd., DPJ Holdings (Pvt) Ltd. and Grand Export (Pvt) Ltd, for a period of one year, based on the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and the recommendation of the Procurement Appeal Board, was approved by the Cabinet.
