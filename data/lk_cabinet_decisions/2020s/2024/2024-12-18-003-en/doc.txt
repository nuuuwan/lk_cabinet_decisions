Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Government of India (GoI) and the Government of Sri Lanka (GoSL) on Cooperation in the field of sharing successful Digital Solutions for Digital Transformation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- There is potential to gain expertise knowledge acquired by India as the global leader in information technology and digital innovation, in areas such as            e-governance, fintech, artificial intelligence and digital infrastructure for Digital Transformation in Sri Lanka through multiple engagements. Accordingly, initial consensus has been reached to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Ministry of Digital Economy of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Electronic and Information Technology of India. This will facilitate the strengthening of the much needed capacity building of officials in digital transformation and promote institutional cooperation between both countries in the implementation of digital transformational solutions. Accordingly, the proposal made by the                Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Digital Economy to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
