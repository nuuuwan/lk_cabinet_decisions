Indonesia - Sri Lanka Preferential Trade Agreement (ISLPTA)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Second Trade Negotiating Committee Meeting on Indonesia - Sri Lanka Preferential Trade Agreement was held on 16th and 15th July 2024 in Colombo. Accordingly, the Cabinet noted the matters furnished by the Hon. President to the effect that both parties have agreed to conclude the negotiations on Indonesia - Sri Lanka Preferential Trade Agreement by December 2024 and to sign the said Trade Agreement by March 2025.
