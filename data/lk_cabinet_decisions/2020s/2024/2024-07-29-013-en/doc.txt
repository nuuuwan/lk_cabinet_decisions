Introducing a new Act for the National Agency for Public-Private	Partnership

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The National Agency for Public-Private Partnership is expected to work jointly with the international organizations in respect of developing and managing the Public-Private Partnership programmes/projects and to identify such programmes and implementing those programmes in collaboration with the Public Sector Institutions. Legal validity should be enforced in order to run the said Agency more effectively and it is required to develop a legal framework regarding the relevant Partnership agreements as well. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill for the said purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
