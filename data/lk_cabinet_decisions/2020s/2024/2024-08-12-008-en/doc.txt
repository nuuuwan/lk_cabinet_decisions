Installation and maintenance of solar panels on the roofs of railway	stations and buildings belonging to the Department of Sri Lanka Railways

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- There are more than 300 railway stations owned by the Department of Railways and it has been identified that it is more appropriate to commence a project to generate electricity by solar power on those roofs. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to take necessary action to implement the said Project as a Public-Private Partnership Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
