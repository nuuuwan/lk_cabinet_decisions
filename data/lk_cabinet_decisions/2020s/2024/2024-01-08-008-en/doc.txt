Awarding of contract for Ground Handling Services of SriLankan Airlines Limited at Tokyo-Narita Airport (NRT), Japan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited following the limited international bidding procedure for the selection of a suitable service provider to provide ground handling services for the aircrafts of SriLankan Airlines Limited at Tokyo-Narita Airport, Japan and accordingly, 02 bids have been received. The proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the contract for the supply of  ground handling services at Tokyo-Narita Airport, Japan from 2024‑08‑01 to 2027‑07‑31, to  International Air Cargo Terminal Co. Ltd, the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, as per the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
