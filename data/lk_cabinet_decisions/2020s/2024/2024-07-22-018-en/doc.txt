Public Assets Management Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2022‑10‑17 has granted approval to identify and compile a National Asset Register of Non-financial immovable or movable assets fully owned, assigned, possessed, vested in, held or utilized by a public institution and to introduce a Law on state asset management, incorporating the provisions for updating the said Register from time to time and ensuring the receipt of proper returns of these Assets to the Government institutions. Accordingly, the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman has received the clearance of the Attorney General. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
