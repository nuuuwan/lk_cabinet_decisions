Introducing legislation for resolving disputes among Government entities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been found necessary to introduce appropriate legal provisions so as to resolve disputes arising among Government entities expeditiously. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to instruct the Legal Draftsman to 	prepare a Bill for that purpose in consultation with the Attorney General, was 	approved by 	the Cabinet.
