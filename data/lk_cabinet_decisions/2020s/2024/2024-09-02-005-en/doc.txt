Adoption of the Charter for the Upcountry Plantation Community of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Charter for the Upcountry Plantation Community has been drafted as a preliminary document to guide the Government's efforts to realize the Government's commitment for the full integration of the Upcountry Plantation Community into the wider Socio-Economic Structure of the country. By implementing the said Charter, it will be possible to address the challenges that the plantation community is constantly facing and to build an just society for them in Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Water Supply and Estate Infrastructure Development to implement the Charter for the Upcountry Plantation Community of Sri Lanka was approved by the Cabinet.
