Commemoration  of  the  100th  year  after  the  establishment  of  Berlin  	Buddhist  Centre  in  Germany

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A  centenary  celebration  is  scheduled  to  be  held  in  Berlin  to  mark  the  100  years  of  the  establishment  of  the  Buddhist  Center  in  Berlin,  Germany  by  the  year  2024.  In  parallel  with  the  said  centenary  programme,  arrangements  have  been  made  to  conduct  a  programme  centering  Colombo.  The  proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  Prime  Minister  to  launch  the  book  'Das  Buddhistische  Haus  (1924-2024)'  printed  by  Vijitha  Yapa  Publishers  and  to  issue  a  Commemorative  Stamp  by  the  Department  of  Posts  -  Sri  Lanka,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
