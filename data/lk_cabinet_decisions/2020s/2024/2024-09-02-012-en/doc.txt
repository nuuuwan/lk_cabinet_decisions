Memorandum of Understanding on Cooperation between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Rio Branco Institute of the Ministry of the Foreign Affairs of the Fedarative Republic of Brazil

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Rio Branco Institute of the Ministry of the Foreign Affairs of the Fedarative Republic of Brazil on the cooperation in the areas of mutual interest such as promoting close collaboration in training of diplomats, facilitating them for short and mid-term training programmes, exchange of information and experiences concerning their respective programmes of study and research and exchange of information and views related to international trends. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to sign the proposed Cooperation Agreement between two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
