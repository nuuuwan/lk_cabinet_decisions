Establishment of Futsal Indoor Stadiums at Commercial Level

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Futsal is a football-based game played on an artificial court and an indoor stadium with dimensions of 40 x 20 meters and height of 4 meters is used for this game. At present, several indoor stadiums have been built in urban areas in Sri Lanka. Since there is a high demand for this sport, it is expected to expand the sport Futsal by providing the land to a private investor on lease basis under the Public Private Partnership procedure and using the state-owned stadium premises and lands. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs to select a private investor following the Government Procurement Guildlines for the construction of futsal stadiums on lease basis for a maximum of 30 years, within the identified state-owned lands and stadium premises and to construct two artificial futsal tracks provided by the said investor as a Grant of the Government of Pakistan in Torrington and Sugathadasa Stadiums, was approved by the Cabinet.
