Agreement on Cooperation and Mutual Administrative Assistance related to 	Custom Matters between Sri Lanka and Russia

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Followed by a series of bilateral discussions held between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Russia, approval of the Cabinet has been granted on 2021-11-15 to sign a bilateral Agreement to facilitate legitimate trade affairs through a transparent and predictable Customs procedure, however, the Agreement has not been signed yet. Taking into consideration the benefits gained from the existing trade relations between the two countries and the emerging trends of the bilateral Customs cooperation, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism to sign the said Agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
