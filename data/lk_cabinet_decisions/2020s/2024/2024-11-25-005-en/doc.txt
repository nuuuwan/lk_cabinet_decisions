Preparation of the Budget for the year 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The new Government anticipates to prepare the budget for the year 2025 as a budget taking the initial steps towards the desired development, in a manner that will strengthen the people's confidence in the Government and fulfill the expectations of the public. Accordingly, Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to prepare the Budget for the year 2025 by obtaining proposals from all ministries, enabling to provide necessary provisions to implement the projects and programs identified by each Ministry under its purview, subject to the expenditure limits specified in the Public Financial Management Act, No. 44 of 2024 and the compliance to the Policy manifesto of the Government, and to present the Appropriation Bill for the year 2025 to Parliament on 2025 01 09 and conduct the Second Reading (Budget Speech) on 2025 02 17 and the Third Reading of the Budget Debate from 2025 02 27 to 2025 03 21.
