Amendment  to  the  Constitution  of  the  Democratic  Socialist  Republic  of  	Sri  	Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It  is  observed  that  there  is  an  inconsistency  among  Article  30(2),  Article  62(2)  and  Article  83(b)  of  the  Constitution  regarding  the  term  of  Office  of  the  President  and  the  duration  of  Parliament.  The  proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  President  to  prepare  a  draft  for  the  Constitution  to  amend  Article  83(b)  of  the  Constitution  by  substituting  the  words  “to  over  six  years”  with  the  words  “to  over  five  years”  to  avoid  doubt  and  eliminate  any  inconsistency  in  that  regard,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
