Granting a Service Extension of 06 months to Ms. Preethi Anoma Siriwardena, Director General of the National Child Protection Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Ms. Preethi Anoma Siriwardena, Director General of the National Child Protection Authority has retired from the Public Service with effect from 2024‑11‑02 on reaching 60 years of age. Several senior managerial positions of the National Child Protection Authority, such as the Deputy Director General, Director (Administration and Human Resources) and the Director (Finance) are vacant at the moment. Under the circumstances, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Women, Child and Youth Affairs, and Sports, to re‑employ Ms. Preethi Anoma Siriwardena in the said Post on contract basis for a period of 06 months, enabling to discharge the administrative and operational activities of the said Authority without interruption.
