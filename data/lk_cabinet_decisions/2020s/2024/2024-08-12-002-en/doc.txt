Domestic Ginger Production and Price Regulation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- At the Cabinet Meeting held on 2024‑07‑15, the  Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture and Plantation Industries and the Secretary, Ministry of Trade, Commerce and Food Security have been instructed to jointly explore the possibility of reducing the retail price of ginger to a price affordable to the consumers in the domestic market while facilitating the  ginger production and submit a Report with appropriate recommendations in that regard. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries; and the Minister of Trade Commerce and Food Security to import 3,000 MT of raw ginger through the Sri Lanka State Trading (General) Corporation in a phased out manner, within the ensuing period of 03 months, based on the recommendations in the Report submitted, was approved by the Cabinet.
