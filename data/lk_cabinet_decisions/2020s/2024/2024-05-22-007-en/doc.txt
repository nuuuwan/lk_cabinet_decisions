Procurement for the Supply, Delivery and Construction of 220kV 2nd underground Cable from Kerawalapitiya Grid Sub Station to Colombo Port Grid Sub Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been identified that it is very much essential to construct the 2nd underground Cable from Kerawalapitiya Grid Sub Station to the Colombo Port Grid Sub Station to supply electricity to meet the most essential commercial energy requirements for the Port City Development, Port Terminal Development and Mega scale mixed development projects in the Colombo metropolitan area. Accordingly, approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the meeting held on 2021 09 13 to call for bids for the project of Supply, Delivery and Construction of 220kV 2nd underground Cable from Kerawalapitiya Grid Sub Station to Colombo Port Grid Sub Station. Accordingly, after evaluating the bids received, the proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to award the relevant procurement to Consortium of Jiangsu ETERN Co. Ltd, China & LS Cable & System Limited (LS Cable) Korea which has been recommended by the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee as the substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder, was approved by the Cabinet.
