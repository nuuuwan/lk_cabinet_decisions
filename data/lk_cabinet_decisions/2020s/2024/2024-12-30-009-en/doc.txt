Construction of two storied steel structure warehouse for the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- National Competitive Bids have been invited for the construction of a two storied steel structure warehouse for the State Pharmaceuticals Manufacturing Corporation at Ratmalana. 10 bids have been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the relevant Contract to M/s Dockyard General Engineering Services (Pvt.) Ltd., the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, based on the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
