Tabling the Report of the Commission appointed in terms of the provisions of the Commission of Inquiry Act, No.17 of 1948 (Chapter 393)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A Commission of Inquiry has been appointed under the Chairmanship of Mr. A.H.M.D Nawaz, Judge of the Supreme Court as per the provisions of section 2 of the Commission of Inquiry Act, No.17 of 1948 (Chapter 393), in order to inquire into the investigation Reports submitted by the Commissions and Committees appointed in advance to investigate the violations of Human Rights and submit recommendations on the manner in which measures have been taken so far in accordance with the existing law and the measures to be taken to further implement the said recommendations in accordance with the existing Government Policy. The proposal made by the Hon. President to present the Report submitted by the said Commission in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
