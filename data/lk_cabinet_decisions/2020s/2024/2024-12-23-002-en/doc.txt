Memorandum of Understanding between the United Nations Institute for Training and Research and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the United Nations Institute for Training and Research (UNITAR) and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism for a period 03 years so as to provide a cooperative framework wherein the parties can develop and implement activities and facilitate for mutual benefit by utilizing the available resources. Under the said Memorandum of Understanding, it is expected to provide training and learning support, training for diplomats, disaster risk reduction, crisis management and  training on the use of geo-information technologies, particularly in relation to the implementation of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Employment and Tourism to sign the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
