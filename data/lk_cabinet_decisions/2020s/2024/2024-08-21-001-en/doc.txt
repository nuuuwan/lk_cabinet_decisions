Introduction of “Students' Artificial Intelligence (AI) Clubs” to the Education System

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been found that it is appropriate to provide opportunities for school students to engage in studies related to the artificial intelligence in order to achieve the goals of creating citizens who can face future global trends and obtaining their effective contribution to the economic development. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology and the Minister of Education to implement “Students' Artificial Intelligence (AI) Clubs” as a pilot project with the participation of students studying from Grade 6 to 9 in 100 selected schools and to extend this programme in the coming years to the other schools as well, based on the progress of the said project as an initial step, was approved by the Cabinet.
