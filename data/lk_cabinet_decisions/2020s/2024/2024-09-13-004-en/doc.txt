Conducting a study to improve Tuberculosis preventive therapy among the infected individuals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Tuberculosis is the second leading infectious disease in Sri Lanka and the leading cause of death among infectious diseases. In the year 2021, a guideline has been created for Sri Lanka also in line with the World Health Organization guidelines on TB Preventive Therapy. Accordingly, it is expected to conduct a study in Galle District to monitor the effectiveness of the care model proposed to be implemented under the National Programme for Tuberculosis Control and Chest Diseases, for which the International Union against Tuberculosis and Lung Disease has agreed to provide a financial grant of USD 387,280. The said study will be conducted jointly with the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and the National Programme for Tuberculosis Control and Chest Diseases of the Ministry of Health. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Education and the Minister of Health to sign the Memorandum of Understanding among the International Union against Tuberculosis and Lung Disease, the Ministry of Health and the University of Sri Jayewardenepura to conduct the Study, was approved by the Cabinet.
