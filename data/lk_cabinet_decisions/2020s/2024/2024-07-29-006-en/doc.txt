Procurement for the Supply of 45 Sets of Distal Femoral Nailing Set with Tan Implants

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been called for the procurement of 45 Sets of Distal Femoral Nailing Set with Tan Implants which are used in Orthopedic surgeries in cases of severe fractures to promote bone healing and alignment. 02 bids have been received for that. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant procurement to M/s Medisurg International, India, the substantially responsive lowest bidder based on the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
