Extension of the Project period of the Technological Education Development Programme under the loan from OPEC Fund for International Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Although the Technological Education Development Programme was initiated in the year 2018 and was scheduled to be completed by the year 2022, owing to the reasons such as Covid pandemic and the economic adversities arisen in the country, the said project could not be completed within the stipulated time period. Previously, approval of the Cabinet has been granted to extend the project period until 2024‑03‑30. Under the project, it has been planned to construct 36 technology laboratories across the 09 provinces, 04 laboratories designated for each province, and to upgrade 15 existing building laboratories. The physical progress of the project as of now is 65%. Further, it has been planned to utilize the foreign funds expected to be remained under the project to establish and upgrade Mini Technology Units for 135 schools where the technology stream is already in operation as well as to supply essential technical goods and equipment and for the capacity building of the teachers. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to further extend the project period up to 2025‑03‑30, so that anticipated objectives of the project could be achieved, was approved by the Cabinet.
