Vest the plots of land with old canals at New Hospital Road, Thalapathpitiya, Maharagama with the Urban Development Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A land in extent of 27.5 hectares located at Thalapathpitiya village of Maharagama Divisional Secretariat Division has been vested with the Urban Development Authority in the year 1984. However, the land plots in the above land which depict the already developed old canals located at New Hospital Road,Thalapathpitiya, Maharagama have not been formally transferred to the Urban Development Authority. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to vest the said land plots in the said Land which depict the old canals with the Urban Development Authority, as a free grant without charging a fee, was approved by the Cabinet.
