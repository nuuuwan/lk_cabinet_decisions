Renewal of Warranty, Operation and Maintenance Contract at Thambapavani Wind Power Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Full commercial operations of the Thambapavani Wind Power Station belonging to the Ceylon Electricity Board have been commenced on 2021‑05‑18. The Ceylon Electricity Board has signed a Contract Agreement with Vestas Wind Lanka (Pvt) Limited for securing warranty, operations and maintenance service of the said Power Plant for a period of three (03) years and the contract period of the said Agreement has expired on 2024‑05‑17. As such, a new service supplier is required to be selected for the said activities. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to select a suitable service supplier, following the Competitive Bidding Procedure, was approved by the Cabinet.
