Amendment of the National Minimum Wage of Workers Act, No.3 of 2016

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2024‑03‑25 has granted approval to amend	the National Minimum Wage of Workers Act, No.3 of 2016 enabling to 	increase the National Minimum Wage of Private Sector up to Rs. 17,500/- and	the National Minimum Daily Wage up to Rs.700/-. Accordingly, the Bill	prepared by the Legal Draftsman has received the clearance of the Attorney	General. The proposal made by the Minister of Labour and Foreign	Employment to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the	same thereafter, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
