Expansion of Benefits under the Agrahara Medical Insurance Scheme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2024‑08‑12 has granted approval to introduce a Medical Insurance Scheme delivering more facilities to the employees in the Public Sector and all pensioners with more attractive benefits from January 2025. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to charge a monthly subscription of Rs.1,000/- from the pension of all pensioners and the salary of all employees in Public Sector and credit the said amount to the National Insurance Trust Fund thereby implementing the Medical Insurance Scheme with proposed more facilities with effect from 2025, was approved by the Cabinet.
