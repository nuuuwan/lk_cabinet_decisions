Awarding the Procurement for the supply, installation, commissioning and 	maintenance of Core Banking Solution for the Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & 	Finance PLC (MBSL)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited following the National Competitive Procurement Procedure for the procurement for the supply, installation, commissioning and maintenance of Core Banking Solution for the Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance PLC. 04 institutions have submitted bids for the same. After evaluating the technical proposals and financial proposals submitted by the bidders, the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee have recommended the awarding of the relevant procurement to Scienter Technologies (PTE) Ltd. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism to award the said procurement, was approved by the Cabinet.
