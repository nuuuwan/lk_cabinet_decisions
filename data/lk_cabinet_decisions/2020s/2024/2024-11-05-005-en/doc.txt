Rehabilitation and Improvement of (B452) Walgama - Diyagama road section from 0+000km - 7+000km

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The OPEC Fund for International Development has provided a loan of US$ 40 million for the improvement of Rakwana - Suriyakanda road. Using the balance of that loan amount, the Road Development Authority has invited bids for the rehabilitation and improvement of the road section from 0+000 km to 7+000 km of Walgama - Diyagama Road in the Colombo District,. 08 bidders have submitted bids for the purpose. Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation, to award the contract for the improvement of Walgama-Diagama Road in the Colombo District, to M/s.Maga Engineering (Pvt) Ltd., for an amount of Rs.2.44 billion (excluding VAT), using the remaining loan provisions of the Rakwana - Suriyakanda Road Project, based on the recommendation of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee.
