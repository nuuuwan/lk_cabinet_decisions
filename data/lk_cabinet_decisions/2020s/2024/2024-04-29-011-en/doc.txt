Designating CHINA HARBOUR ENGINEERING COMPANY (LANKA)	LIMITED as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- CHINA HARBOUR ENGINEERING COMPANY (LANKA) LIMITED has been identified as a Secondary Business of Strategic Importance as a business engaged in business activities of generation of employment opportunities, sustainable development and urban amenity operations in the settlement of a residential community. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to grant the Exemptions or Incentives proposed in the Acts identified under Schedule II of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, to the respective business and to publish the Order for the same through a Gazette Notification, was approved by the Cabinet.
