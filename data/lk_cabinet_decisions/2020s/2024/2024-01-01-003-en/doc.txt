Signing a Memorandum of Understanding between the General Sir John	Kotelawala Defence University and the Turkish Aerospace Inc.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding pertaining to 	the Cooperation on Human Resources Development between the General Sir 	John Kotelawala Defencce University and the Turkish Aerospace Inc. It is 	possible to reap many benefits including receiving the opportunity for students 	in the General Sir John Kotelawala Defencce University to participate in the 	International Internship Programme at the Turkish Aerospace Inc. as an intern 	trainee. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity 	as the 	Minister of Defence to sign the proposed Memorandum of	Understanding, was approved by the Cabinet.
