Improving the infrastructure facilities in plantation schools under the Indian Government Aid

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2019‑06‑04 has granted approval to implement a project to improve infrastructure facilities in plantation schools through a Grant of Rs. 300 million of the Government of India whereas the concurrence of the Government of India has been granted to increase the total Grant for the said project up to  Rs. 600 million due to the increase of prices owing to the economic crisis which prevailed in Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to sign the relevant documents to obtain the said Grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
