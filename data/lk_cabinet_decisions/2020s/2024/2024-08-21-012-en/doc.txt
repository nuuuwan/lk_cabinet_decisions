Extension of the Project implementation period of CHEC PORT CITY	COLOMBO (PRIVATE) LIMITED

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑08‑05 to extend the Project implementation period for CHEC PORT CITYCOLOMBO (PRIVATE) LIMITED with the exemptions applicable under the Strategic Development Projects Act, from 2024-09-07 to 2027-06-30. Accordingly, an order was published through Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2396/60 dated 2024-08-09 as per the provisions of the Strategic Development Projects Act, No.14 of 2008. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to present the said Order for approval in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
