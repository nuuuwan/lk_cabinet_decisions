Resolution to be submitted to the Conference of Parties of the International Convention on Wetlands (Ramsar) by Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The 15th Conference of the Parties of the International Convention on Wetlands or the Ramsar Convention will be held in Zimbabwe in July 2025. Sri Lanka has drafted a Resolution on 'Rights of Nature in Wetlands' to be submitted to the said Conference. By the said Resolution, it is expected that the member countries would recognize the rights of nature in wetlands of their countries. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Environment to submit the said Resolution to the 15th Conference of the Ramsar Convention by Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
