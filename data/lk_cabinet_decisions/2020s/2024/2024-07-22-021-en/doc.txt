Establishment of the National Electricity Advisory Council and National	System Operator as per the provisions of the Act, No.36 of 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No.36 of 2024 has made provisions for the	restructuring of the corporate structure of the power industry in order to	efficiently manage its functions. Under which, the Power Sector Reform	Secretariat, National System Operator and National Electricity Advisory	Council shall be established. By now, the Power Sector Reform Secretariat has 	been established. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power and	Energy to establish National Electricity Advisory Council and to appoint Board	of Directors for the National System Operator as per the provisions of the	above Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
