Training of 7,500 Teachers on Robotics and Artificial Intelligence

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Digital Transformation has been recognized as a very significant component by the National Education Policy Framework, and for this purpose, the assistance  by the public and private sectors has been encouraged in order to use Artificial Intelligence and New Technologies. Accordingly, as per a project proposal submitted by Skills College of Technology (SCOT CAMPUS), an experienced institution  of conducting courses on Information Technology, Mechatronics and Robot Technology,  a pilot project has been successfully carried out. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology and the Minister of Education to train 7,500 teachers in selected schools, covering all the provinces in relation to the STEAM concept including Information Technology, Biotechnology, Engineering Technology, Mathematics, through the Skills College of Technology (SCOT CAMPUS) in three stages, was approved by the Cabinet.
