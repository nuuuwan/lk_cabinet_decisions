Extension of the Project implementation period granted to CHEC PORT CITY COLOMBO (PRIVATE) LIMITED with Strategic Development Project Concession

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Construction of the Colombo Port City, a project identified under the Strategic Development Projects Act, No.14 of 2008 was entrusted with the CHEC PORT CITY COLOMBO (PRIVATE) LIMITED in year 2016. The proposal made by the President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to extend the project period till 2027-06-30 in order to complete the remaining work of the said Project and commence its commercial operations, was approved by the Cabinet.
