Entering into a Memorandum of Understanding between Sri Lanka and 	the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on Cooperation of Promotion of Direct 	Investment

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia have agreed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with a view to attracting direct investment to the country through policy and legal reforms, provision of advance and integrated infrastructure, process reengineering etc. and improving the investment environment of both countries. Accordingly, the Cabinet noted the information presented by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion that the Minister of Foreign Affairs signed the relevant MoU during the Special Meeting of the World Economic Forum held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia on 28th and 29th of April, 2024.
