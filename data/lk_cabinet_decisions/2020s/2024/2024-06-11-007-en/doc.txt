Bill to establish the Institute for Food Science & Technology of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted to prepare a Bill to establish the Institute for Food Science & Technology of Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the Legal Draftsman has prepared the relevant Bill and the Attorney General has proposed a number of amendments for the same. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries to instruct the Legal Draftsman to expeditiously finalize the task of preparing the Institute for Food Science & Technology of Sri Lanka Bill by incorporating the said amendments as well, was approved by the Cabinet.
