Implementation of a Public Private Partnership Programme on improving 	sport skills of school level athletes targeting the Brisbane Olympic Games 	in 2032

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Sports and Youth Affairs have planned to implement a joint programme with the support of the private sector and civil society in view of developing Sri Lanka as a "Medal Winning Country" at the Brisbane Olympics 2032. Similarly, It is expected to provide the opportunity to the talented athletes to participate in the Olympics to be held in Los Angeles in 2028 and achieve a significant level of performance. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Education and the Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs to implement the proposed programme to achieve those objectives, was approved by the Cabinet.
