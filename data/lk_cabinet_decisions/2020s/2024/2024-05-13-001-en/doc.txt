Institutionalizing the Results-based Management in the Public Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Department of Project Management and Monitoring in collaboration with Line Ministries have prepared “Ministerial Results Frameworks” of 10 Ministries and institutions coming under their purview, under which a high amount of budgetary provisions is allocated. Said “Ministerial Results Frameworks” will ensure the achievement of development targets within the purview of the relevant institutions in a timely manner and achievement of intended outcomes, while adhering to the responsibilities of the hierarchy of each Ministry. Further, it is expected that this would also pave the way for a result-oriented institutional structure that measures the effectiveness of investments made to address citizen's needs urgently. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to entrust the responsibility of achieving the targets containing in the designed Ministerial Results Frameworks with the Secretaries to relevant Line Ministries and to assign the responsibility of supervising the performance of such activities to the National Operations Room, was approved by the Cabinet.
