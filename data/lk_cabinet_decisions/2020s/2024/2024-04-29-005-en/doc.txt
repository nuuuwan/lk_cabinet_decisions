Operation of a Gold Shop at Departure/Transit Area of the Bandaranaike 	International Airport (BIA), Katunayaka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The appropriateness of introducing a gold business at the Departure/Transit Area of the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayaka as a new business venture has been considered and proposals in that regard have been submitted by Airport and Aviation Services (Sri Lanka) (Private) Limited. Since this facility is not available at the Bandaranaike International Airport at present, Airport and Aviation Services (Sri Lanka) (Private) Limited has planned to establish a "Gold Shop" at the Departure/ Transit area at BIA. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to invite bids following the International Competitive Bidding Procedure to select a suitable operator to operate a Gold Shop at the Departure/ Transit area at BIA, was approved by the Cabinet.
