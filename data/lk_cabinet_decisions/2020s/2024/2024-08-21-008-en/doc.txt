Submission of Orders issued as per the provisions of the Ports and Airports Development Levy Act, No.18 of 2011 to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2023‑05‑29 has granted approval to remove the Ports and Airports Development Levy related to the HS Codes of the Tariff Liberalization Programme under the "Sri Lanka - Singapore Free Trade Agreement". Accordingly, the Order made as per the provisions of the Ports and Airports Development Levy Act, No.18 of 2011 has been published through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2377/39 dated 2024‑03‑28. As such, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the said Order, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
