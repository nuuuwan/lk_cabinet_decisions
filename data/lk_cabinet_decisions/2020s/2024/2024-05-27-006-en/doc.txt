Receiving financial Provisions to Construct the General Hospital and the 	Hospital for Children in Biyagama, Gampaha

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-27 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- As the demand for healthcare services in the Gampaha district is increasing day by day, the need to improve the healthcare service system in the said district has been identified. For that purpose, approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑02‑12 to construct a general hospital and a hospital for children equipped with all modern facilities under a public - private partnership approach and to take over 02 plots of land called Millagahawatta and Delgahawatta (07 acres and 03 roods) in the Biyagama Divisional Secretariat division, based on the assessed value of the Government. Sontell Group Hong Kong International Limited has submitted a proposal expressing its willingness to provide the necessary funds for the construction of the proposed hospital. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President and the Minister of Health to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the said Institution and the Government of Sri Lanka to secure funds for construction of the said hospital, was approved by the Cabinet.
