Regional Project on strengthening resilience of vulnerable	communications in Sri Lanka and India to increased impacts of climate	change

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Environment, Wildlife, Forest Resources, Water Supply, Plantation and Community Infrastructure in-collaboration with the United Nations World Food Programme has submitted a proposal for a regional project on Strengthening resilience of vulnerable communications in Sri Lanka and India to increased impacts of climate change to the Adaptation Fund and it has been approved by the Adaptation Fund Board. The proposed project will be implemented within a period of 05 years and a Financial Grant of around USD13 million will be provided to Sri Lanka and India for the project. The project is scheduled to be implemented in the districts of Monaragala, Trincomalee, Mannar, Mullaitivu, Vavuniya and Kurunegala in Sri Lanka. The proposal made by the Minister of Environment, Wildlife, Forest Resources, Water Supply, Plantation and Community Infrastructure to sign the Memorandum of Understanding to implement the said project between Sri Lanka and the World Food Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
