Implementing the Project on Development of a Sustainable Integrated Solid Waste Management and a Circular Economy proposed to be implemented focusing the Uva Province and the Northern Province on the Technical and Financial Assistance of Korea International Cooperation Agency

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Korea has extended its willingness to award a financial grant of US$ 10.20 million, to implement a project during the period from 2024-2028 for the establishment of an Integrated Solid Waste Management Mechanism at local authority level in the Uva and Northern Provinces and for solving management issues related thereto. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour to implement the proposed project with the technical assistance of the Korea International Cooperation Agency and the financial assistance of the Government of Korea, was approved by the Cabinet.
