Amendments to the Penal Code to strengthen the legal provisions to prohibit sexual harassment and sexual assault

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022 12 12 to introduce amendments to the Penal Code by making sexual harassment and all forms of sexual assaults including in public places, a crime. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
