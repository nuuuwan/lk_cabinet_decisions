Bilateral Air Services Agreement between the Government of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Government of the Kingdom of Thailand

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2018‑06‑19 to sign a  Bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and Thailand. The clearance of the Attorney General and the concurrence of the Minister of Foreign Affairs have been received for the Air Services Agreement prepared thereafter incorporating  amendments submitted by the Thailand party. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to sign the proposed Bilateral Air Services Agreement between the two countries, was approved by the Cabinet.
