Colombo  Port  City  (Fees)  Regulations  No.2  of  2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Colombo  Port  City  (Fees)  Regulations  have  been  imposed  through  the  Gazette  Notification  dated  2024‑06‑06  as  per  the  provisions  of  the  Colombo  Port  City  Economic  Commission  Act  No.  11  of  2021.  As  per  the  provisions  of  section  71  of  the  said  Act,  every  Regulation  so  made  should  be  submitted  to  Parliament  for  approval  within  03  three  months  from  the  date  of  publication  in  the  Gazette.  Accordingly,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  President  in  his  capacity  as  the  Minister  of  Investment  Promotion  to  submit  the  Colombo  Port  City  (Fees)  Regulations  No.2  of  2024  published  through  the  Gazette  Extraordinary  Notification  No.  2387/38  dated  2024‑06‑06  to  Parliament  for  approval,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
