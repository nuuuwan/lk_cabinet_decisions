Awarding the contract of site clearing at the Kankasanthurai Cement Factory by removing unusable materials scattered in the factory premises

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited following the National Competitive Bidding Procedure to collect iron and other metal parts scattered over the cement factory site located in Kankasanthurai and clean the factory premises. Six (06) bidders have come forward for this procurement, which was operated under the direct supervision of the Ministry of Industries, and three of them have been rejected due to disqualifications per the bidding documents. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to award the said procurement to M/s. Luminex PLC, substantially responsive lowest evaluated bidder as per the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed procurement committee, was appointed by the Cabinet.
