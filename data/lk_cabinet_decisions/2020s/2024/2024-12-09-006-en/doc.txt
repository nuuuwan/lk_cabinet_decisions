Augmentation of the Common Wastewater Treatment Plant at the Horana Export Procession Zone

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held 2023-06-05 for the augmentation of the Common Wastewater Treatment Plant at the Horana Export Procession Zone utilizing funds of the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka. Bids have been called for the purpose following the National Competitive Bidding Procedure and 11 Bids have been submitted.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to award the contract for the augmentation of the Common Wastewater Treatment Plant at the Horana Export Procession Zone to Luminex PLC, on the recommendation of the Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
