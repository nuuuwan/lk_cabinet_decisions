Appointment to act in the Post of Commissioner General for Registration of Persons

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Post of Commissioner General for Registration of Persons has become vacant from 2024‑09‑24. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security to appoint Mrs. M.S.P. Suriyapperuma, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as an Additional Secretary of the Ministry of Public Security, to act in the Post of Commissioner General for Registration of Persons with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
