Amendments to the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act No. 2 of 2018 and the Safeguard Measures Act No. 3 of 2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024 05 13 to publish the Economic Transformation Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter, to Parliament. During the preparation of the said Bill, it has been found necessary to make consequential amendments to the the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act No. 2 of 2018 and the Safeguard Measures Act No. 3 of 2018. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act No. 2 of 2018 and the Safeguard Measures Act No. 3 of 2018, was approved by the Cabinet.
