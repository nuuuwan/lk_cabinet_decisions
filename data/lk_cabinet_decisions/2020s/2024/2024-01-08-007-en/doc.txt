Cooperation programme for 2023-2025 between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Turkmenistan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Turkmenistan have agreed to sign a Cooperation Programme for the period from 2023 to 2025. The objectives of this Cooperation Program are to promote, strengthen and expand bilateral relations including political, economic and cultural cooperation between the two countries, as well as focusing on issues of  mutual interest. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to implement the proposed Cooperation Programme between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Turkmenistan, was approved by the Cabinet.
