Completion  of  the  work  of  the  Nanu  Oya  Railway  Station  Modernization  Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval  of  the  Cabinet  has  been  granted  at  the  Cabinet  meeting  held  on  2023‑04‑03  to  implement  the  Nanu  Oya  Railway  Station  Modernization  Project  utilizing  the  funds  of  the  Sri  Lanka  Tourism  Development  Authority  and  the  Sri  Lanka  Tourism  Promotion  Bureau.  Due  to  revision  in  the  prices  of  construction  materials,  the  contract  value  of  Rs.74.63  million  for  the  modernization  of  Nanu  Oya  railway  station  has  increased  to  Rs.101.81  million.  Accordingly,  Cabinet  approved  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Tourism  and  Lands  to  complete  the  relevant  modernization  works  under  the  Central  Engineering  Consultancy  Bureau,  on  the  recommendation  of  the  Department  of  Sri  Lanka  Railways  and  to  secure  additional  provisions  required  for  the  purpose  from  the  Sri  Lanka  Tourism  Development  Authority  and  the  Sri  Lanka  Tourism  Promotion  Bureau.
