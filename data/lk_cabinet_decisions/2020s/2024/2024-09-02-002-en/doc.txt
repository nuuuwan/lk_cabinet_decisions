The Convention between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Austria for the Elimination of Double Taxation and Prevention of Fiscal Evasion and Avoidance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Convention between the Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of Austria for the Elimination of Double Taxation and the prevention of Fiscal Evasion and Avoidance with respect to Taxes on Income and on Capital has been drafted and initialed at staff-level having negotiated with the tax authorities of the two countries. In line with the recent Conventions on Elimination of Double Taxation entered into Sri Lanka, interests and royalties will be taxed at a rate not exceeding 10 percent of the gross amount. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to sign the said Convention at the State level and submit the said Convention and Protocol, to Parliament, in terms of the provisions of the Inland Revenue Act, No.24 of 2017, was approved by the Cabinet.
