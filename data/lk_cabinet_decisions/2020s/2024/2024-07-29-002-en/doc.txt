Making the Kelaniya Vidyalankara Buddhist Conference Hall to the implementation condition

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The International Buddhist Conference Hall, which is being constructed in the Kelaniya Vidyalankara Pirivena premises, has not been able to be fully completed and made to the implementation condition due to external reasons which impacted on the same in the past. Therefore, the joint proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in his capacity as the Minister of Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils & Local Government  and the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to make required provisions in the year 2025 to carry out necessary  constructions to make the Conference Hall building to the implementation condition, was approved by the Cabinet.
