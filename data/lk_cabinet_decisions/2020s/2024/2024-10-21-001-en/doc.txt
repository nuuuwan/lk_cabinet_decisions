Issuance of Commemorative Stamps to celebrate Pakistan-Sri Lanka Diplomatic Relations

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka and Pakistan have completed 75 years since the establishment of diplomatic relations in 2022. An agreement has been reached between the two parties to issue 02 commemorative stamps, marking the long-standing diplomatic relationship between the two countries. The proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, National Integration, Social Security, and Mass Media to sign a Memorandum of Understanding for this purpose between the Department of Posts, Sri Lanka and the Pakistan Post, was approved by the Cabinet.
