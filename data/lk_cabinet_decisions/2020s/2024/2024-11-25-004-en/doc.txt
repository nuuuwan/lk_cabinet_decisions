Awarding the Contract for the procurement of 04 shipments of Gas Oil (0.05% M.S)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been called for the purchase of 04 shipments of Gas Oil (0.05% M.S) for the period from 2024 12 15 to 2025 06 14 from the registered suppliers of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation. Accordingly, bids have been submitted by 06 Bidders. The proposal made by the Minister of Energy, to award the relevant procurement to M/s BB Energy (Asia) Pte. Ltd, Singapore, as recommended by the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee after evaluating the said bids, was approved by the Cabinet.
