Term Contract for the Procurement of 05 Shipments of Gasoline 92 Unl during the Period from 2024-12-01 to 2025-05-31

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited under two payment options viz., payment at the time of issuance from storage tanks and payment in thirty days after the B/L date through L/C from suppliers registered in the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation, for the supply of 05 shipments of Gasoline 92 Unl (1.5 million barrels) under a term contract during the period from 2024‑12‑01 to 2025‑05‑31.  Accordingly, 07 bids have been received and 03 bids have been rejected at the initial evaluation. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Energy to award the relevant contract to M/s Vitol Asia Pte. Ltd., Singapore under the payment in thirty (30) days after the B/L date through L/C payment terms, based on the recommendations of the Standing Technical Evaluation Committee and the Special Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
