Bilateral Air Services Agreement between Sri Lanka and Kazakhstan

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- 	Although approval had been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2015‑05‑13 	to sign an Air Services Agreement between the aeronautical authorities of the Kazakhstan and Sri Lanka for the purpose of developing Air Services between the two countries, the said Air Services Agreement has not yet been signed. Under the proposed Air Services Agreement, it is expected to operate 07 frequencies per week 	from any point in Sri Lanka to Astana and/ or Almaty in Kazakhstan. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to sign the Air Services Agreement which has been agreed upon by both parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
