Appointment to the post of Government Analyst

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Mrs. Deepika Senevirathne, who served in the post of Government Analyst, has retired on 2024-10-11. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour to appoint Mrs. Pathirage Sandhya Kumuduni Rajapakshe, a Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Scientific Service, the next most Senior Officer as per the Seniority list of the Special Grade Officers in the Sri Lanka Scientific Service and currently serving in the post of Additional Government Analyst, for the said vacant post, was approved by the Cabinet.
