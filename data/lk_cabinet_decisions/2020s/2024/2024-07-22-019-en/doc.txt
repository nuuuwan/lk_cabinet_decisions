Submission of Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No.09 of 2024	issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969, for	approval in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No.09 of 2024 imposed under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969 and published in the Gazette Notification No.2390/19 dated 2024-06-28, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
