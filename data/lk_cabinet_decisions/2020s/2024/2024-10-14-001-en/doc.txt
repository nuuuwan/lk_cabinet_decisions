77th Independence Day Ceremony

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour to appoint a Cabinet Committee under the chairmanship of the Hon. President and with the participation of the Hon. Prime Minister and the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, National Integration, Social Security and Mass Media to provide necessary guidance and supervision pertaining to the conduct of the 77th Independence Day Ceremony falling on 2025‑02‑04 and to appoint a Steering Committee with the participation of all Heads of Institutions including all relevant Ministries, Departments, Tri Forces and Police, enabling to organize the Independence Day Ceremony in a splendid manner, was approved by the Cabinet.
