Selection of a Handling Agent for SriLankan Airlines Connecting Point	Services

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited to select a Handling Agent for SriLankan Airlines connecting Point Services by following the National Competitive Bidding Procedure. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to award the relevant Contract to Lanka Sportreizen, the sole substancially responsive bidder as per the recommedations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
