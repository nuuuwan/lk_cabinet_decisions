Procurement for the supply of Non Ionic Contrast Media

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited under the International Competitive Bidding Procedure for the purchase of  Non Ionic Contrast Media which is used for CT and X-ray Imaging. 06 Bids have been received for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health to award the relevant procurement to Unique Pharmaceuticals Laboratories, the substantially responsive lowest bidder as per the recommendation of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
