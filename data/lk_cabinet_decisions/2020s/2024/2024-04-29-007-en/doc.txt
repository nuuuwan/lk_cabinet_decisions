Signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Rwanda and Sri Lanka for exemption of Diplomatic, Official or Service Passport Holders from obtaining Visa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Even though approval of the Cabinet has been granted on 2019‑05‑07 to sign a Memorandum of Understanding for exemption of Diplomatic, Official or Service Passport holders from the requirement of obtaining Visa with the objective of developing bilateral relations between Sri Lanka and Rwanda, the said MoU could not be signed as of now. The proposal made by the Minister of Public Security to sign the said MoU between Sri Lanka and Rwanda for exemption of Diplomatic, Official or Service Passport holders from obtaining Visa for a period not exceeding 30 days, as agreed between two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
