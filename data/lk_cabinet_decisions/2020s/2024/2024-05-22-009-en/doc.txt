Introduction of a new law on Reciprocal Recognition, Registration and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022 07 18 to prepare a Bill to introduce a new law for Reciprocal Recognition, Registration and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments and to repeal the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Ordinance No. 14 of 1921. Accordingly, the Bill on the Reciprocal Recognition, Registration and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments, incorporating the provisions for recognition and registration of a foreign judgment awarded for the dissolution or annulment of a marriage or separation of the parties to a marriage, has been prepared by the Legal Draftsman. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the said Bill. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to publish the Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter submit the same to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
