200MW Natural Gas Fired Internal Combustion Engine Power Plant with Duel Fuel Capacity at Kerawalapitiya

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Project of 200MW Natural Gas Fired Internal Combustion Engine Power Plant with Duel Fuel Capacity at Kerawalapitiya is a project identified by the Long Term Generation Expansion Plan 2023-2042 approved by the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka. The land area adjacent to Kerawalapitiya Sobadhanavi Limited has been allocated for this project and it is expected to develop this power plant as an Independent Power Producer under the built‑own‑operate‑transfer (BOOT) basis for 20 years. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to identify a suitable investor by following the Competitive Bidding Procedure for the relevant task, was approved by the Cabinet.
