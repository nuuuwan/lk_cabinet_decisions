The Procurement Guidelines on Goods, Works and Non-Consulting Services - 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The National Procurement Commission, established in terms of Article 156B.(1) of the Constitution, is responsible for formulating fair, equitable, transparent, competitive and cost-effective procedures and guidelines for the procurement of goods and services, works, consultancy services and information systems through all Government institutions, in accordance with the powers vested with the said Commission, in terms of  Article 156C.(1) of the Constitution. Accordingly, the Procurement Guidelines on the Procurement of Goods, Works and Non-Consulting Services by the Government Institutions and for matters connected and incidental  thereto - 2024, has been published in the Gazette Extraordinary Notification      No.2412/01 dated 2024-11-25. The proposal made by the Hon. President to submit the said Guidelines to Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
