Renovation of the Communication Towers in Mirihana, Kikiliyamana and Vavuniya owned by the Sri Lanka Police

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In order to maintain the communication network of the Sri Lanka Police, communication towers have been established covering the entire island. Among them, the communication towers in Mirihana, Kikiliyamana and Vavuniya need to be repaired urgently. Accordingly, taking into consideration the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security, approval of the Cabinet was granted to award the consultancy services contract for the renovation of  relevant communication towers to the State Engineering Corporation of Sri Lanka, by securing required provisions within the medium term budgetary framework 2024-2026 and to award the contract for the renovation of said communication towers to an agency which is selected by following the open competitive bidding procedure.
