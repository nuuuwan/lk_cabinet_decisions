Amendments  to  the  Bills  of  Exchange  Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  Cabinet  at  its  meeting  held  on  2024‑01‑24  has  granted  approval  to  amend  or  remove  the  obsolete  provisions  in  the  Bills  of  Exchange  Ordinance  No.25  of  1927.  Accordingly,  the  clearance  of  the  Attorney  General  has  been  received  for  the  Bill  prepared  by  the  Legal  Draftsman.    The  joint  proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  President:  and  the  Minister  of  Justice,  Prison  Affairs  and  Constitutional  Reforms  to  publish  the  said  Bill  in  the  Government  Gazette  and  to  submit  the  same  thereafter  to  Parliament  for  approval,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
