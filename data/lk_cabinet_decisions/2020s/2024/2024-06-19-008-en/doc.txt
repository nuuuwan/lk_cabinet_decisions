Paving works of Jaya Container Terminal-V Yard in the Colombo Port

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Plans relating to the paving of the Jaya Container Terminal - V of the Colombo Port have been prepared by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to call for National Competitive Bids and select a suitable institution for this work, and to appoint a Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and a Technical Evaluation Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
