Submission  of  Regulations  to  Parliament  for  approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  Money  or  Value  Transfer  Service  Providers  Regulations  No.  1  of  2024  under  section  17  of  the  Payment  and  Settlement  Systems  Act,  No.  28  of  2005  to  be  read  with  section  11  therein  have  been  published  through  the  Gazette  Extraordinary  Notification  No.  2382/11  dated  2024‑04‑30.  The  Proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  President  in  his  capacity  as  the  Minister  of  Finance,  Economic  Stabilization  and  National  Policies  to  submit  the  said  Regulations  to  Parliament  for  approval,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
