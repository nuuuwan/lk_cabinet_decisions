Appointment of an Officer to Act in the Post of Director General of Combined Services

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Post of Director General of Combined Services has fallen vacant due to the appointment of Mr. S. Aloka Bandara, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service who was functioning in the said post, to the Post of Secretary to the Ministry of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour with effect from 2024‑10‑03. The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour, to appoint Ms. R.M.N.E.K. Ranasinghe, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as Additional Secretary of the Parliamentary Affairs Division of the Ministry of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour, to act in the Post of Director General of Combined Services, was approved by the Cabinet.
