Renovation of the Ekgaloya Circuit Bungalow, Ampara and the Riverstone  Circuit Bungalow (Dumbara Forest Resort)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Department of Forest Conservation has identified 09 circuit bungalows that need to be renovated based on the recommendations of the Committee appointed to recommend a suitable methodology for the effective use of state-owned holiday resorts and circuit bungalows.  The estimated provisions of Rs.166.93 million has been received for the same and the projects should be completed within this year. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Environment, Wildlife, Forest Resources, Water Supply, Plantation and Community Infrastructure to get the renovation activities of the Ekgaloya and Riverstone Circuit Bungalows carried out by Central Engineering Services (Pvt.) Ltd, under the supervision of the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau, was approved by the Cabinet.
