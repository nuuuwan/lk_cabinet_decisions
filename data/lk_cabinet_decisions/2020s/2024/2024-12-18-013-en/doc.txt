Amendments to the Trusts Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Financial Action Task Force which is an intergovernmental organization working for developing policies to combat money laundering, has stated the need to amend the provisions of the Trusts Ordinance in order to secure a favorable position in the upcoming Mutual Evaluation of Sri Lanka to be conducted in 2025. The Financial Intelligence Unit of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka and the Registrar General's Department have submitted the proposed amendments for the purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to amend the Trusts Ordinance incorporating the said proposed amendments, was approved by the Cabinet.
