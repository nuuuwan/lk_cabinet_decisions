Providing uniforms for Dhamma school teachers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- An annual teachers' allowance of Rs.5,000/- is currently paid to encourage the Buddhist, Hindu, Islamic, Christian and Catholic Dhamma school teachers in appreciation of their service. In addition to the above allowance, a uniform	has been provided for the Dhamma school teachers till the year 2022, and the	programme of providing uniforms is currently not being implemented. In consideration of a proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs regarding the provision of uniforms for Dhamma school teachers, the approval of the Cabinet has been granted to provide an allowance of Rs.7,500/- annually as an incentive 	teacher allowance of Rs.5,000/- and the uniform allowance of Rs.2,500/- for the Dhamma school teachers representing all the religions in year 2025.
