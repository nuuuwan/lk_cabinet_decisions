Technologies Sri Lanka (Incorporation) Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-02-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Hon. Gamini Waleboda, Member of Parliament, has submitted the Technologies Sri Lanka (Incorporation) Bill to Parliament as a Private Members' Bill. It has been submitted to the Minister of Education to obtain a Report on the same as per the provisions of Standing Order 52(6) of Parliament. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received to the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to submit a Report to Parliament recommending the said Bill, was approved by the Cabinet.
