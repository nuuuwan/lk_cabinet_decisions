An  Act  to  repeal  Rent  Act,  No.  7  of  1972

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  approval  of  the  Cabinet  has  been  granted  at  the  meeting  held  on      2023-09-04  to  prepare  a  Bill  to  repeal  the  Rent  Act,  No.  7  of  1972.  Accordingly,  the  Bill  prepared  by  the  Legal  Draftsman  has  received  the  clearance  of  the  Attorney  General.  The  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Justice,  Prison  Affairs  and  Constitutional  Reforms  to  publish  the  said  Bill  in  the  Government  Gazette  and  thereafter  present  the  same  for  approval  in  Parliament,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
