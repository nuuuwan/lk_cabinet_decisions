Preparation of Reports to be submitted to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- According to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Paris Agreement, Sri Lanka is obliged to prepare National Communication Reports on Climate Change and submit the same to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change. Accordingly, the First Biennial Transparency Report is scheduled to be submitted by 2024‑12‑31 and the Second Biennial Transparency Report by 2026‑12‑31. Also, Sri Lanka should submit its Fourth National Communication Report in 2027. The Ministry of Environment has submitted a Project Report to the Global Environment Facility in order to secure funds required to prepare such Reports and submit the same during the period of 2024‑2028. The Global Environment Facility has agreed to provide a Grant of US$1.23 million for the preparation of related reports based on the said Project Report. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Environment to enter into relevant Agreements to obtain the said Grant, was approved by the Cabinet.
