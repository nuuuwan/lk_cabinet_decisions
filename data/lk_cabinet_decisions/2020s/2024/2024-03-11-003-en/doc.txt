Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Standards 	Institution and the Standardization Administration of China

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-03-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to sign a Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Standards Institution and the Standardization Administration of China to ease non-tariff trade barriers between Sri Lanka and China. Clearance of the Attorney General and the concurrence of the Minister of Foreign Affairs have been received to the relevant draft MoU. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology to sign the said MoU, was approved by the Cabinet.
