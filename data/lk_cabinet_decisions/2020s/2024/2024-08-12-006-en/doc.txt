Selection Criteria Framework for Investments in Drinking Water Sector in 	Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Selection Criteria Framework for Investments in Drinking Water Sector in Sri Lanka have been prepared by the Ministry of Water Supply and Estate Infrastructure Development under the supervision of a Steering Committee comprising of experts in the relevant field, stakeholder agencies and academia. The proposal made by the Minister of Water Supply and Estate Infrastructure Development to implement the said Criteria Framework, was approved by the Cabinet.
