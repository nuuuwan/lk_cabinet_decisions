Project Agreement between the Sri Lanka Export Development Board and the Swiss State Secretariat for Economic Affairs on Global Textile and Clothing Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed to enter into a Project Agreement between the Swiss State Secretariat for Economic Affairs and the Sri Lanka Export Development Board to implement the second phase of the Global Textiles and Clothing Programme for Sri Lankan Textile and Clothing Sector. By entering into the said Agreement, Sri Lanka will be able to obtain the support of the Global Textiles and Clothing Programme for a transformation of Textile and Apparel Industry based on the shift towards quality green products by promoting digitization. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to sign the said agreement, was approved by the Cabinet.
