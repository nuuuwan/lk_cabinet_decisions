Amendment of the Sri Lanka Telecommunications Act, No.25 of 1991

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2024-01-01 to amend the Sri Lanka Telecommunications Act No. 25 of 1991. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General have been received for the Sri Lanka Telecommunications (Amendment) Bill, which has been prepared in all three languages by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter to submit it to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
