Information Technology Strategic Plan for the Inland Revenue	Department

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Information Technology Strategic Plan to support the digitization of the Inland Revenue Department as a structural benchmark under the IMF supported Extended Fund Facility Programme is required to be prepared by the Inland Revenue Department and approved by the Cabinet by the end of August 2024. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to implement the Information Technology Strategic Plan for the Inland Revenue Department prepared for the period of 2025-2027, was approved by the Cabinet.
