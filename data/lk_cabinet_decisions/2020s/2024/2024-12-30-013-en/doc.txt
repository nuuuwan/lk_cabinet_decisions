Reviewing the Recruitment Process and Cadre Management in the Public Service

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President to appoint an Officials' Committee chaired by the Secretary to the Prime Minister to review the staff of Commissions, Ministries, Departments and Statutory Institutions as well as Provincial Councils, make essential recruitments, and take necessary action to relocate cadres on the exigencies of service and ensure the optimal performance.
