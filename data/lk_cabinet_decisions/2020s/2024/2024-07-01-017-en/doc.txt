Third Amendment of the Assistance Agreement No.383-128 signed with the Government of the United States of America

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Sri Lanka and the Government of the United States of	America have entered into an Assistance Agreement on 2022-08-31 to obtain a	Grant to finance the “Programme for a Democratic, Prosperous and Resilient	Sri Lanka” and has granted USD 9.069 million to Sri Lanka accordingly. The	value of the grant has been revised up to USD 28.299 million in 2023, in 02	occassions.The United States Agency for International Development (USAID)	has proposed to further increase the value of the Grant up to USD 52.835	million. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity	as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to	amend the Assistance Agreement by increasing the Grant Fund under the	“Programme for a Democratic, Prosperous and Resilient Sri Lanka” up to USD 	52.835 million, was approved by the Cabinet.
