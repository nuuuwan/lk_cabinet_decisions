Distributing Uniform Materials provided as a Grant from the People's Republic of China, to School and Pirivena Students for the year 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-11-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The total requirement of school uniform materials for the year 2025 is about 11.82 million meters of fabric. The People's Republic of China has consented to provide the said total quantity, as a Grant. After the sample of materials, sent to the Ministry of Education, were tested by the Sri Lanka Institute of Textile and Apparel (SLITA), the said Institute has recommended that the fabric is suitable for use by school children. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Science and Technology, to accept the Grant of the Government of China to provide uniform materials to all School and Pirivena Students for the year 2025, was approved by the Cabinet.
