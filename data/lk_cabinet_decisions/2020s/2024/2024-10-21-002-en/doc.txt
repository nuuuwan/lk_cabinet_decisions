The Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting is scheduled to be held from 24th to 26th October 2024 in Apia, Samoa under the theme "One Resilient Common Future: Transforming Our Common Wealth". For the purpose, Cabinet decided to grant the concurrence for the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to make a delegation representing Sri Lanka comprised with the Senior Officials from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Sri Lankan High Commission in London, participate in the Meeting.
