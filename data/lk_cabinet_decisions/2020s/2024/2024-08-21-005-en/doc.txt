The Contract for Rehabilitation and Improvement of Access Roads to Kandy Multimodal Transport Terminal (KMTT)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Under the Kandy Multimodal Transport Terminal Development Project, bids have been invited for the award of the contract for the rehabilitation and improvement of access roads to the Kandy Multimodal Transport Terminal, following the local competitive bidding procedure. 10 bids have been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to award the relevant contract to Maga Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd, the substantially responsive bidder, as per the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
