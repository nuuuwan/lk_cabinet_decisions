National Climate Spatial Data Development Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Owing to the impacts of climate change, variability and extreme events, Sri Lanka's people, livelihoods, ecosystems, built infrastructure and investments are at a significant risk. In order to reduce that risk, a Climate Resilient Investment Roadmap for Sri Lanka has been formulated. In its implementation, the creation and improvement of a National Climate Spatial Data System has been identified as a fundamental and an essential step. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to implement the National Climatic Spatial Data Development Project together with M/s PROCLIME PTE. LTD. of Singapore, was approved by the Cabinet.
