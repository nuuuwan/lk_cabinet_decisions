Procurement for the supply of 900,000 Vials of 1g of Meropenem Injections

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International Competitive Bids have been invited for the supply of 900,000 vials of 1 gram of Meropenem Injections used for the treatment of severe bacterial infections. 05 bids have been received for the same and the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee has recommended M/s Genix Pharma (Pvt.) Ltd., Pakistan, the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, to award the said procurement. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to award the Procurement for the supply of 900,000 vials of 1 gram of Meropenem Injections to the said Company, was approved by the Cabinet.
