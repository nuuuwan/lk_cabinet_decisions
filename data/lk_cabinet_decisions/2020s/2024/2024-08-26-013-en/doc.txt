Providing the opportunity to the employees attached to the Samurdhi Development Authority to be absorbed into the Department of Samurdhi Development

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Employees who served attached to the then Samurdhi Development Authority have been provided with the opportunity at several occasions to be absorbed the then Department of Divineguma Development at their option, with a pension during the establishment of the Department of Divineguma Development by the Divineguma Act, No.01 of 2013. Nevertheless, it has been identified that there are approximately 3000 employees who were unable to revise their option on various grounds. Accordingly, the Cabinet granted its concurrence for the proposal made by the Hon. President to provide the opportunity to the said group of employees to be absorbed to the Department of Samurdhi Development in consultation with the Public Service Commission within the existing legal framework.
