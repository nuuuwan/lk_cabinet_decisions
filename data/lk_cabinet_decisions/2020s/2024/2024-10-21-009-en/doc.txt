Appointment to the Post of Director General of the Department of Coast Guard

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Rear Admiral P. Withana who was serving as the Director General of the Department of Coast Guard has retired upon the completion of 55 years of age. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to appoint Rear Admiral Y.R. Serasinghe presently serving as the Deputy Director General of the Department of Coast Guard, to the above vacant post, was approved by the Cabinet.
