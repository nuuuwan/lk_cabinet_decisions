Award of the Contract of Package 1R and 1S of Section 03 of the Central	Expressway Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The construction of the Section-03 of the Central Expressway Project is divided into 19 Contract packages, of which 17 packages from 1A to 1Q have already been awarded. Bids have been invited from the eligible local construction firms for Contract Packages 1R and 1S on competitive basis, on the recommendations of Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee. Accordingly, the proposals made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to award the Contract Packages 1R and 1S to M/s MAGA Engineering (Pvt) Ltd, the lowest substancially responsive bidder based on the recommedation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
