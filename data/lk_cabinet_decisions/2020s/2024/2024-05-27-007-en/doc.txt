Amending the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act, No.2 of 2018 and the Safeguard Measures Act, No.3 of 2018

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-27 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- During the preparation of the Economic Transformation Bill, it has been found the necessity of introducing consequential amendments to the Anti‑Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act, No.2 of 2018 and the Safeguard Measures Act, No.3 of 2018. The Bills for that purpose have been prepared by the Legal Draftsman and the Attorney General has given clearance for those Bills. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to publish the Amendment Bills for the Anti‑Dumping and Countervailing Duties Act, No.2 of 2018 and the Safeguard Measures Act, No.3 of 2018 in the Government Gazette and thereafter, present the same in Parliament for the approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
