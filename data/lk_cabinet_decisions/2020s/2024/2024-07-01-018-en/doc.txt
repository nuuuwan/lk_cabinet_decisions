Implementing the “Work Culture” Project to motivate Sri Lankan young	entrepreneurs and workforce

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The National Youth Services Council has prepared a project proposal for the implementation of the "Work Culture" Project, a national initiative, designed to uplift the economy of Sri Lanka by enhancing the productivity and efficiency among the youth. This project is expected to be implemented from June to August 2024 and aims to promote productivity, recognize excellence, engage youth, encourage a working culture and sustain motivation among Sri Lankan youth. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to implement the “Work Culture” Project to motivate young Sri Lankan entrepreneurs and workforce through the National Youth Services Council from the funds allocated to the Presidential Secretariat under the Youth Development Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
