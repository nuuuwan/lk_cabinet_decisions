Agriculture  Modernization  Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval  of  the  Cabinet  has  been  granted  at  the  meeting  held  on  2024‑03‑04  to  implement  the  first  stage  of  the  of  the  Agriculture  Modernization  Project.  As  the  second  stage  of  the  said  programme,  it  has  been  decided  to  select  another  03  Divisional  Secretariat  Divisions  from  each  district  and  implement  Agriculture  Modernization  Projects.  Accordingly,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Hon.  President,  to  implement  the  second  stage  of  the  Agriculture  Modernization  Project  utilizing  Rs.  750  million  from  the  provisions  allocated  in  the  Budget  for  the  year  2024,  to  75  Divisional  Secretariat  Divisions,  Rs.10  million  each  for  a  Divisional  Secretariat  Division,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
