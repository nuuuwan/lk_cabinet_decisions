Submission of an Order published under the Sri Lanka Export Development 	Act, No. 40 of 1979 to Parliament for approval

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- An Order has been published in the Gazette Extraordinary Notification                No.2400/25 dated 2024‑09‑05 under section 14 of the Sri Lanka Export Development Act, No. 40 of 1979 by the then Minister of Investment Promotion with the consent of the then Minister in charge of the subject of Finance, to reduce the CESS Levy by Rs.1/- per Kg on the importation of Clinker, Cement in bulk and Cement in retail bags and to export one million MT of rock boulders for construction purposes and to impose and recover a CESS Levy at the rate of Rs. 1,000/- per cubic meter, subject to the recommendation of the Minister in charge of the subject of Environment. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development to submit the said Order to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
