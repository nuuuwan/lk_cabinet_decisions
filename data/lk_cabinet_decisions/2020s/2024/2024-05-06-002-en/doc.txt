Establishment of the National Teachers Council

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-06 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been proposed through the National Education Policy Framework to establish a National Teachers Council in parallel with other professional service categories and international best practices for the professional advancement and enhancement of quality of teachers through continuous professional development. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President and the Minister of Education to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to establish a National Teachers Council based on the initial draft prepared having lengthy discussions with the relevant stakeholders in the field of education, was approved by the Cabinet.
