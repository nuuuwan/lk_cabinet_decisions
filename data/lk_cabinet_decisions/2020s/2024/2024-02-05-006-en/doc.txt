Declaring the 21st  of March as the National Seafarers’ Day

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-02-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In order to appreciate the contribution made by seafarers to the local economy of Sri Lanka, it has been recognized that it is appropriate to declare a National Seafarers' Day. It will enable creating opportunities to earn foreign exchange  through attracting interested people for the maritime profession and to increase the contribution of the maritime sector to the national economy by improving the services of the maritime sector. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to declare the 21st  day of March  every year as 'National Seafarers’  Day', was approved by the Cabinet.
