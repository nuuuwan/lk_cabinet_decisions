Procurement for the supply of 9,000 Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vials

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- International competitive bids have been invited for the purchase of 9,000  Tenecteplase Injection 40mg Vials, used to prevent death in patients with heart failures. 03 bids have been submitted for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health & Mass Media to award the relevant procurement to M/s ABC Pharma Services (Pvt.) Ltd., the lowest evaluated substantially responsive bidder, based on the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
