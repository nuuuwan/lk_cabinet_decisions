Allocation  of  Funds  from  the  Saudi  Fund  for  Development  to  complete  	the  	section  I  of  the  half  completed  work  of  the  Godagama-Homagama  	Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Funds  of  the  Saudi  Fund  for  Development  have  been  utilized  to  implement  the  Kurinchakerny  Bridge  Project  in  Trincomalee  District.  After  completion  of  the  procurement  process  of  the  said  project,  there  is  still  a  loan  balance  of  US$  10.5  and  it  has  been  decided  to  utilize  the  said  balance  amount  for  the  completion  of  Section  I  of  the  Godagama-Homagama  road,  which  is  half  completed.  In  order  to  complete  the  rest  of  the  said  road,  an  amount  of  US$  12  million  is  needed  and  the  Saudi  Fund  for  Development  has  agreed  to  provide  the  remaining  US$1.5  million  from  the  loan  amount  of  the  Road  Network  Development  Project  funded  by  the  Saudi  Fund  for  Development.  Accordingly,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Transport  and  Highways,  to  secure  funds  and  complete  section  I  of  the  Godagama-Homagama  road,  which  is  half  completed,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
