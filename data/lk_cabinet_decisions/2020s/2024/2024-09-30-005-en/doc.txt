Appointment to the Post of Director General of Government Information

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Post of Director General of Government Information has become vacant at present, since Mr. Dinith Chinthaka who served as the Director General of Government Information resigned from the said post. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, National Integration, Social Security and Mass Media to appoint Mr. H.S.K.J. Bandara, Grade I Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as the Director (Administration) of the Department of Government Information, to act full time in the Post of Director General of Government Information, with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
