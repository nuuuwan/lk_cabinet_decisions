National Disaster Management Plan - 2023-2030

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-02-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The preparation and implementation of the National Disaster Management Plan in	terms of the provisions of the Sri Lanka Disaster Management Act, No.13 of 2005 is 	one of the foremost responsibilities of the Disaster Management Centre. 	Accordingly, the National Disaster Management Plan 2023-2030 has been prepared 	in compliance with the Sendai Framework of Action, the Sustainable 	Development 	Goals and the Conventions on Climate Change and in coordination with all the 	relevant agencies. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in 	his 	capacity as the Minister of Defence to table the National Disaster Management Plan 	2023-2030 in	Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
