Establishment of the Enterprise Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- After a review made with the assistance of experts, taking into account the international best practices, it has been identified the need to establish a consolidated agency for more effective management and development of small and medium scale enterprises. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion and the Minister of Industry to establish the proposed institution called "Enterprise Sri Lanka" as a single entity dedicated for small and medium scale enterprises through re-modeling the institutional and legal frameworks of the Export Development Board, the National Enterprise Development Authority, Industrial Development Board, Sri Lanka Export Credit Insurance Corporation and the Small Enterprise Development Division of the Ministry of Sports and Youth Affairs and instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill for that purpose, was approved by the Cabinet.
