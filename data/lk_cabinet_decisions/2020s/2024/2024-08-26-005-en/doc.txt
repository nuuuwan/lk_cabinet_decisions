Alienation of a plot of Land of the Polonthalawa Farm belonging to the National Livestock Development Board to the Geoffrey Bawa Foundation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Manager's bungalow of the Polonthalawa farm belonging to the National Livestock Development Board has been designed by the renowned Sri Lankan  architect, Deshamanya Geoffrey Bawa. The Geoffrey Bawa Foundation has made a request to vest the said bungalow and the land of 5 acres where the said bungalow is located, on lease basis. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries to vest the said Manager's bungalow of the Polonthalawa farm and the plot of land of 05 acres where the said bungalow is located with the Geoffrey Bawa Foundation, on lease basis, was approved by the Cabinet.
