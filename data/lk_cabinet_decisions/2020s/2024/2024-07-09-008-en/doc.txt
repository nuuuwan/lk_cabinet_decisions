Obtaining  Ambulances  for  the  1990  Suwa  Seriya  Foundation  as  Free  	Grants

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- "1990  Suwa  Seriya  Foundation"  was  commenced  utilizing  297  Ambulances  obtained  from  TATA  Sons  (Pvt)  Ltd  under  a  grant  of  the  Government  of  India  in  the  year  2016  to  provide  pre-hospital  care  and  emergency  treatments.  At  present,  322  Ambulances  including  25  Ambulances  granted  by  the  Government  of  Sri  Lanka  have  been  deployed  for  the  same.  As  per  the  target  of  the    Suwa  Seriya  Foundation,  450  Ambulances  and  25  Back-up  Ambulances  are  required  to  provide  the  service  expeditiously  and  it  has  been  identified  that  there  is  a  shortage  of  150  Ambulances  at  present.  Accordingly,    TATA  Sons  (Pvt)  Ltd  of  India  has  agreed  to  provide  50  Ambulances  as  a  free  grant  whereas  the  Asian  Development  Bank  is  in  agreement  to  provide  45  Ambulances  as  a  grant.  As  such,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Health  to  take  necessary  action  to  obtain  the  said  Ambulances  to  the  "1990  Suwa  Seriya  Foundation",  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
