Renovation of Diyagama Mahinda Rajapaksa Sports Complex

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet Meeting held on 2024‑01‑08 to get the construction works of the new sub-projects that are currently being implemented and proposed to be implemented, done by the Sri Lanka Army. Accordingly, it has been planned to construct the roofs of the junior pavilion of the said ground and the second pavilion of the new athletics playground. The proposal made by the Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs to get the said construction work done by the Sri Lanka Army, was approved by the Cabinet.
