Appointment for the post of District Secretary/Government Agent in the	Administrative District of Hambantota

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Taking into consideration a request made by the Election Commission, the Cabinet decided to attach Mr. H.P. Sumanasekera who is currently serving in the post of District Secretary/Government Agent in the Administrative District of Hambantota, to the Ministry of Justice, Public Administration, Home Affairs, Provincial Councils, Local Government and Labour with immediate effect and to appoint Mr. A.B.I. De Silva who is currently serving as the Secretary to the Ministry of Sports and Youth Affairs, Rural Development, Cultural and Arts Affairs, Social Welfare, Probation and Child Care Services, Women's Affairs and Home Economic Enhancement, Housing and Construction and Manpower and Employment in the Southern Province, for the said post.
