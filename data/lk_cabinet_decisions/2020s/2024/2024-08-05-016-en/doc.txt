Re activating a Special Interest Scheme on Fixed Deposits of Senior Citizens

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- At present the interest rate on fixed deposits has depreciated significantly to 8.5%. As a result, majority of the senior citizen account holders have withdrawn their deposits. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to provide the annual interest rate of 10% for a period of 02 years for the fixed deposits of senior citizens over 60 years of age who deposited up to a maximum of one million rupees, was approved by the Cabinet.
