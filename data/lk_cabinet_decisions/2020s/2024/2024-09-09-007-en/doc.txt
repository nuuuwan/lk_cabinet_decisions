Proposal to dredge the sand deposits in the fishery harbour basins	managed by the Ceylon Fishery Harbour Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2024-02-05 has granted approval to call for Expressions of Interest for the purpose of removing sand deposits in 22 Harbours of the Ceylon Fishery Harbour Corporation. Accordingly, the Ceylon Fishery Harbour Corporation has called Expressions of Interest for the project proposals to carry out dredging services in the fishery harbour basins managed by the said Corporation free of charge and for the commercial utilization of dredged raw materials in Kirinda, Chilaw and Valachchenai fishery harbours. For that purpose, 03 companies have submitted proposals and the proposal made by the Minister of Fisheries to award the Contract for dredging at Kirinda, Chilaw and Valachchenai fishery harbours free of charge to the Supreme Global Holdings (Pvt) Ltd, based on the recommendations of the Technical Evaluation Committee and the Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
