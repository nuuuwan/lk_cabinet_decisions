Repatriation of Export Proceeds into Sri Lanka Rules No. 01 of 2024 under the Central Bank of Sri Lanka Act, No. 16 of 2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Exporters, representatives of exporters and certain government agencies have submitted requests to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka to relax the existing Rules of Export Proceeds, i.e. "Repatriation of Export Proceeds into Sri Lanka Rules No. 01 of 2022 (as amended)". With the enactment of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka Act, No. 16 of 2023, new Rules should be issued to fulfill the requests of the stakeholders.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the Repatriation of Export Proceeds into Sri Lanka Rules No. 01 of 2024, published through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2391/02 dated 2024-07-01, made under the provisions of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka Act, No. 16 of 2023, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
