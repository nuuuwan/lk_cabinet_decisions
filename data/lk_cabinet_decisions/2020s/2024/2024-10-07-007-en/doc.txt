Cancellation of Appointments made on the approval of the Cabinet of Ministers

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Action has been taken to establish 'the Office for Overseas Sri Lankans Affairs', 'Jathika Jana Sabha Secretariat in order to establish the Jana Sabha System to Strengthen Participatory Democracy' and to implement the 'Agriculture Modernization Programme' under the Presidential Secretariat as per the approval of the Cabinet and officers/instructors have been appointed for the same on contract basis. Accordingly, the Hon. President informed the Cabinet that action has been taken to terminate the service of Officials/Instructors recruited on contract basis for the above offices and programmes, with effect from 2024‑09‑30, since the relevant Ministries have the capacity to implement the functions expected through the said offices and programmes and the Cabinet granted its concurrence for the same.
