Agreement on the Maritime Transport Cooperation among the Member Countries of the Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation (BIMSTEC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-19 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation (BIMSTEC) has decided to enter into an Agreement on the Maritime Transport Cooperation among its member countries. The proposal regarding the signing of the Cooperation Agreement for the same was submitted to the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑01‑08 by the Minster of Ports, Shipping and Aviation and an Officials' Committee was appointed to further examine the said proposal. Concurrence of the Cabinet was granted for signing the Agreement on the Maritime Transport Cooperation which will be prepared taking also into consideration the recommendations mentioned in the said Officials' Committee Report submitted by the Hon. President.
