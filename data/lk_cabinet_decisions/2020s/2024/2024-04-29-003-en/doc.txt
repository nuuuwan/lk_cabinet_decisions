Kankesanthurai Harbour Rehabilitation Project

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-04-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted on 2017‑05‑02 to implement the Kankesanthurai Harbour Rehabilitation Project under the Indian Credit Line and approval has been granted on 2019‑12‑18 to award project management consultancy services. Owing to exceeding the estimated cost prepared by the consultancy services agency than the relevant loan amount, it has been impossible to proceed with the project activities as expected. Therefore, further discussions have been held with the Government of India regarding the possibility of completing the said project under the Public-Private Partnership Methodology. Accordingly, considering the importance of the said project, the Government of India has agreed to provide its total estimated cost as a Financial Grant. Accordingly, The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to accept the said Financial Grant and enter into the relevant agreements, was approved by the Cabinet.
