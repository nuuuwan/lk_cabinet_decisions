Extension of the service of the Director General of the Civil Security Department

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-07 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted to appoint Air Vice Marshal R.S Biyanwila to the Post of Director General of the Civil Security Department with effect from 2023‑09‑14. The duration of the said appointment has expired on 2024‑09‑13. Therefore, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to continue to employ him in the Post of Director General of the Civil Security Department up to 2024‑12‑31, was approved by the Cabinet.
