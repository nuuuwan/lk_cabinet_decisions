Obtaining support from the OPEC Fund for International Development for the Economic Stabilization and Recovery Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Sri Lanka has made a request to the OPEC Fund for International Development in order to secure funds to finance Sri Lanka's comprehensive reform agenda, in line with the Extended Fund Facility Programme supported by the International Monetary Fund. The said entity has agreed to grant a loan of US$ 50 million under the Policy Based Loan. The International Monetary Fund has also confirmed that the said loan complies with the parameters of the programme which is being implemented in Sri Lanka. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to enter into a loan agreement with the OPEC Fund for International Development to obtain the said loan, was approved by the Cabinet.
