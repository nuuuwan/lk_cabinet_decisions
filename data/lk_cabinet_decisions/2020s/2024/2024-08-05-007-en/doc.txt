Agreement on assigning equipment between the International Centre forResearch in Agroforestry and the Department of Export Agriculture

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Knuckles Project which is funded by the Green Climate Fund and receives technical assistance by the International Centre for Research in Agroforestry, is implemented by the Ministry of Irrigation to strengthen the climate resilience of subsistence farmers and agricultural plantation communities residing in the vulnerable river basins, water shed areas and downstream of the Knuckles Mountains. A Soil Spectroscopic Laboratory is to be established in the Department of Export Agriculture as one of the components operated under the above Project. The International Centre for Research in Agroforestry has agreed to provide the necessary equipment for that purpose. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries to enter into an agreement between the above institution and the Department of Export Agriculture to obtain the said equipment, was approved by the Cabinet.
