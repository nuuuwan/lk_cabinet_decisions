Ratification of the UNESCO Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions (2005) by Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In 2005, the UNESCO Organization has adopted a convention aimed at protecting and promoting the diversity of cultural expressions created in its member states. So far, 156 member states have accepted the said Convention, and Sri Lanka has not yet ratified the convention. A Committee consisting of Experts has been appointed to consider the ratification of the UNESCO Convention, and the concurrence of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the clearance of the Attorney General have been received for the recommendations of the said Committee. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs to ratify the UNESCO Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions (2005) by Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
