Setting apart of the plot of land located at the Assessment No.135, Perahera Mawatha, Kollupitiya, Colombo 03 belonging to the Urban Development Authority for a mixed development projection a  99-year lease  basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Proposals have been invited from the interested investors to set apart a plot of land of 35.95 perches at Perahera Mawatha, Kollupitiya, Colombo 03 belonging to the Urban Development Authority for a mixed development project on a 99-year lease basis. Accordingly, M/S GLOBAL LEASING LTD, which has been selected, has received the approval of the Board of Management of the Urban Development Authority and the Standing Cabinet appointed Negotiation Committee to set apart the relevant plot of land on a long-term lease basis.The proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to provide the said plot of land to the said Company on a 99-year lease basis to implement a mixed development project, was approved by the Cabinet.
