Procurement for the supply of Security Service for Hospitals/Institutions falling under the purview the Ministry of Health for the years 2024/25

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited from Rakna Arakshaka Lanka Ltd., LRDC Services (Pvt) Ltd., and the Civil Security Department for Hospitals and other Institutions falling under the purview the Ministry of Health and from the Regional Economic Development Agency functioning under the Central Provincial Council and the Wayamba Human Resources Development Authority functioning under the North-Western Provincial Council for Hospitals/Institutions located in the relevant Provinces, falling under the purview of the Ministry of Health by following the Limited Competitive Bidding Process. Accordingly, bids have been submitted by LRDC Services (Pvt) Ltd., for all Hospitals whereas the Regional Economic Development Agency functioning under the Central Provincial Council has submitted bids for Hospitals located in the said Province, falling under the Ministry of Health. As such, taking into consideration the recommendation of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee and the proposal submitted by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Health, the Cabinet granted its approval to award the relevant Contract for the supply of security service to M/s LRDC Services (Pvt) Ltd., for a period of 06 Months with effect from 2024‑10‑01.
