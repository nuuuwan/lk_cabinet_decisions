Introduction of a new Legislation on the Rights of the Indigenous People in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The joint proposal made by the then Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms and the Minister of Buddhasasana Religious and Cultural Affairs pertaining to introduction of a new legislation on the the rights of the indigenous people in Sri Lanka was taken up for discussion at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024‑05‑22. In the instance, It has been decided that it would be appropriate to further analyze the Concept Paper submitted for the preparation of the Bill, with the participation of all relevant parties. Accordingly, the relevant preliminary draft has been further improved, having conducted several rounds of discussions under the chairmanship of the Secretary to the Prime Minister and with the participation of the officials of the Ministries entrusted with the subjects of lands, wildlife and forest conservation, environment and finance, the officers of the Attorney General's Department and the officers of the Legal Draftsman's Department as well as the parties interested in the relevant field. As such, the joint proposal made by the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms and the Minister of Buddhasasana Religious and Cultural Affairs to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill based on the said preliminary draft, was approved by the Cabinet.
