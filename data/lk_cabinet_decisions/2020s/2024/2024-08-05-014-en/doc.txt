Memorandum of Understanding between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute and the Institute for Diplomatic Studies of Egypt

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- With the aim of nurturing and improving the historical friendship between Sri Lanka and Egypt, it has been recognized the need to mutually strengthen the Diplomatic Training Institutes of the two countries in order to exchange diplomatic knowledge between the two countries. Both parties have reached a compromise for facilitating the exchanging of diplomats, academics, officials, experts as well as researchers and also to share knowledge by arranging studies and conferences. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Foreign Affairs to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the Bandaranaike International Diplomatic Training Institute of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and the Institute for Diplomatic Studies of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Arab Republic of Egypt, was approved by the Cabinet.
