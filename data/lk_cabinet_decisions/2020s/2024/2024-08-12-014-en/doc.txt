Development of Pinnawala - Kitulgala Tourism Zone

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- A provision of Rs. 750 million has been allocated through a Budget Proposal of 2024 to the Ministry of Tourism and Lands for the development of the Pinnawala-Kitulgala Tourism Zone. The Department of National Planning has recommended to implement several Infrastructure Facilities Development Projects that have been identified under the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to implement the Projects identified with related to the Pinnawala-Kitulgala Tourism Belt Development Programme, was approved by the Cabinet.
