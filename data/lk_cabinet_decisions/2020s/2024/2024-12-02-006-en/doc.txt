Appointment to the post of Secretary to the Leader of the Opposition in Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to appoint Mr. M.G.C.S. Ranasingha, Grade I Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as a Senior Assistant Secretary of the Office of the Leader of the Opposition in Parliament, to act full time in the post of Secretary to the Leader of the Opposition in Parliament with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
