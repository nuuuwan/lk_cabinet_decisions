Importation of Cup Lumps and Cup Lump Sheets under the Scheme for Temporary Import for Export Processing (TIEP)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- There is an annual demand of about 60,240 metric tons of technically specified rubber for Solid Tire Manufacturing Factories in Sri Lanka, and due to the severe shortage of Ottapalu in the country, there is a situation where local manufacturers cannot meet that demand. Accordingly, Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries to grant permission to Technically Specified Rubber Factories in Sri Lanka registered with the Rubber Development Department and approved by the Rubber Research Institute of Sri Lanka, to import Cup Lumps and Low Dirt Cup Lump Sheets under the Scheme for Temporary Import for Export Processing.
