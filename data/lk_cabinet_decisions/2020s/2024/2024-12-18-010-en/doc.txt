Resurfacing of the Synthetic Running Tracks of the Sugathadasa National Sports Complex in keeping with International Standards

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Sugathadasa National Sports Complex is an internationally recognized first class sports complex, and is the only sports complex in Sri Lanka, which is equipped with 400, 200 and 80 meter synthetic running tracks with all the facilities of International Standards. The resurfacing of these synthetic running tracks had been carried out in year 2018, and Athletics Association of Sri Lanka has reported that the condition of the tracks is not suitable even for the national level sports competitions due to the numerous damages that exist on those tracks at present. with the aim of providing the necessary facilities to guide athletes to the national and international level, it has been identified that the renovation of these synthetic running tracks in conformity with the international standards should be a task of high national priority. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports to resurface the synthetic running tracks of the Sugathadasa National Sports Complex, prepare the football ground located in the middle, install the necessary electronic time machines and to renovate the light towers by following the prescribed procurement process, was approved by the Cabinet.
