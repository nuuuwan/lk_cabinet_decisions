Vesting the Gampola Old Court Complex land with the Urban Development Authority

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been decided at the Udapalatha Regional Coordinating Committee Meeting to modernize the old Court building located at the land where the Gampola Court Complex had been housed without demolishing it and to develop the said premises as a car park. Approval of the the Board of Management of the Urban Development Authority has also been granted to implement a Mixed Development Project in the said land. Accordingly, taking into consideration the proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing in that regard, the Cabinet granted approval to vest the land where the Gampola Old Court Complex is located with the Urban Development Authority to implement a Mixed Development Project  and a public car park under the Gampola Town Development Project subject to the payment of the value determined by the Chief Government Valuer by the Urban Development Authority to the Consolidated Fund.
