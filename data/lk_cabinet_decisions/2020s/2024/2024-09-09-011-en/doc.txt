Submission of Imports Control Regulations on Payment Terms No.11 of 2024 issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969 for approval of Parliament (Open Account for Colombo Port City)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Imports Control Regulations on Payment Terms No.11 of 2024 imposed under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No.1 of 1969 have been published through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2398/18 dated 2024-08-21. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to present the said Regulations for approval in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
