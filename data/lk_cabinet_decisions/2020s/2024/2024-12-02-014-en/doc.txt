Granting permission to import rice without obtaining Import Control Licenses

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet considered the suitability of temporarily removing the restrictions currently existing for the importation of rice, taking into consideration the scarcity of Nadu rice and other varieties of rice in the local market and the loss that has  occurred to the paddy cultivation due to the heavy rain persisted in the last few days. Accordingly, it was decided to grant permission with immediate effect to import rice to the country without obtaining import control licenses  until 2024-12-20.
