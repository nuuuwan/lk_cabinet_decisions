Maintenance of the Mahinda Rajapaksa National Tele-Cinema Park under the Public-Private Partnership

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been decided at the Cabinet meeting held on 2023‑05‑02 to obtain recommendations of the National Agency for Public‑Private Partnership in order to manage the Mahinda Rajapaksa National Tele-Cinema Park under the Public‑Private Partnership. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to follow the procurement process to identify a suitable investor to implement and maintain the Mahinda Rajapaksa National Tele-Cinema Park under the Public‑Private Partnership and to enter into a Lease Agreement with the investor so identified for a period of 30 years, based on the recommendations of the said Agency, was approved by the Cabinet.
