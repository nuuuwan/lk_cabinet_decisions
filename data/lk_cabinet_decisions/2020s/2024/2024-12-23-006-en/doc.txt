Fulfilling the responsibility of Local Authorities in creating a circular economy by streamlining their solid waste management activities

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government has planned to implement a project, in line with the “Clean Sri Lanka” programme as approved at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024-12-02, with the aim of ensuring the responsibility and commitment of all citizens and Local Authorities to engage in waste management activities, recognizing the importance of community participation and public awareness for sustainable urban solid waste management. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to implement the proposed Projects based on the Local Government authoritative areas, was approved by the Cabinet.
