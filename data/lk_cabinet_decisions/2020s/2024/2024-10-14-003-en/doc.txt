Grant awarded from the International Council on Archives to the National	Archives

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Fund for the International Development of Archives of the International Council on Archives has agreed to provide a Grant of Euro 7000 for the “Establishment of Atom (Access to memory) and Archivematica software platforms at the National Archives of Sri Lanka” engaging specialists in open-source softwares Atom and Archivematica for archives description (Metadata) and digital Conservation. The International Council on Archives has agreed to utilize the said Grant to cover a part of the costs for Stage-1 of the Project that has been initiated already for the establishment of a trusted Digital Repository at the National Archives. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, National Integration, Social Security, and Mass Media to sign the Project Charter with the International Council on Archives for obtaining the said Grant and to take other relevant steps in that regard, was approved by the Cabinet.
