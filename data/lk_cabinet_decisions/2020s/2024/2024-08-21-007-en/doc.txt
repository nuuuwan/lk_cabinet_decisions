Providing the fertilizer subsidy for farmers cultivating in paddy lands for Maha Season 2024/25

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-21 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- It has been observed that there is a possibility to ensure the food security in the country and to enhance the productivity in crops by providing a fertilizer subsidy for farmers for paddy and other field crops cultivating in paddy lands during the Maha Season 2024/25. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries to import fertilizer through State Fertilizer Company Limited, the state-owned fertilizer company, and the private sector for the Maha Season 2024/25 and to sell at a competitive price to the farmers and to provide a financial subsidy of Rs.15,000.00 per hectare for maximum of cultivated two hectares for paddy cultivation and other crops through the Department of Agrarian Development, was approved by the Cabinet.
