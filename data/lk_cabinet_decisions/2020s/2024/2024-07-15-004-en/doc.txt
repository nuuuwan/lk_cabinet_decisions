Leasing  out  a  plot  of  land  located  at  Mudun  Ela,  Kelaniya  to  Storight	Logistics  (Pvt)  Ltd.

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bitsuthra  IT  (Pvt)  Ltd.  has  submitted  a  request  to  provide  a  plot  of  land 	located near  the  Port  of  Colombo  for  logistics  services  and  storage  facilities  	upon the  approval  of  the  Board  of  Investment  of  Sri  Lanka.  Since  the  project	proposal  submitted  by  the  said  company  has  fulfilled  the  relevant  basic 	requirements,  a  new  company  named  Storight  Logistics  (Pvt)  Ltd.  has  been	established  as  per  the  provisions  of  the  Board  of  Investment.  A  plot  of  land  	with  the  extent  of  02  acres  1.08  perches  owned  by  the  Sri  Lanka  Land  	Development Corporation  located  in  Mudun  Ela  area,  Kelaniya  has  been  	identified  as suitable  for  the said  project.  Accordingly,the  proposal  made  by  	the Minister  of  Urban  Development  and  Housing  to  lease  out  the  said  plot  of  	land  to  Storight Logistics  (Pvt)  Ltd.  for  a  period  of  30  years  based  on  the  	current  valuation submitted  by  the  Government  Chief  Valuer,  was  approved  by  	the  Cabinet.
