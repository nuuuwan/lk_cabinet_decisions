Amending the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No.9 of 2003

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-11 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the meeting held on 2023 05 02 to draft a Bill to amend the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No.9 of 2003. The Attorney General has submitted observations for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman accordingly. The proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security to instruct the Legal Draftsman to expeditiously complete the drafting process of the relevant Bill, based on the aforesaid observations, was approved by the Cabinet.
