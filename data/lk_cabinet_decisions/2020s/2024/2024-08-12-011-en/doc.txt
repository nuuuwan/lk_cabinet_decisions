Submission of Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No.10 of 2024,	issued under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969, for	approval of Parliament - (Importation of Spices by Approved Enterprises	for Processing and Re-export)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to submit the Imports and Exports (Control) Regulations No.10 of 2024 made under the provisions of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 1 of 1969 and published through the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 2393/36 dated 2024-07-18, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
