Making provisions for recalling nominations and for calling fresh nominations for the Local Authorities Election which was due to be held in the year 2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- At the Cabinet meeting held on 20240902, it was decided to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Local Authorities Elections (Special Provisions) Bill to make provisions for recalling nominations and for calling fresh nominations for the Local Authorities Election which was due to be held in the year 2023.  At the meeting of Party Leaders in Parliament chaired by the Speaker of Parliament on 20241125, with the participation of the leaders of recognized political parties as well, a consensus has been reached to cancel the nominations called in 2023 and conduct the ensuing Local Authorities Election by calling fresh nominations.  Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare the Local Authorities Elections (Special Provisions) Bill, empowering the Election Commission to make provisions to recall the nominations already called for the Local Authorities Election and to call for fresh nominations, enabling to safeguard the right of all voters and the voting right of the youth community, was approved by the Cabinet.
