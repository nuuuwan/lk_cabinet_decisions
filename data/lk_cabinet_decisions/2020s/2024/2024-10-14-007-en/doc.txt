Completion of the remaining work of the construction project of quarters complex for married personnel of the Special Task Force at Kalapaluwawa

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet had been granted at the meeting held on 2016‑08‑23 for the construction of 36 quarters in the Kalapaluwa area, including 15 quarters for married Sub Inspectors of the Special Task Force and 21 quarters for married Police Sergeants and Police Constables. Accordingly, the relevant contract has been awarded to Construction Managers & Planners (Pvt) Ltd, to a sum of Rs.289.59 million (without VAT). The project duration has been extended several times, and as of now, about 95% of the construction work has been completed. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security, to extend the project duration up to 2024‑12‑31 and to revise the project cost as Rs.460.58 million, taking into account the drastic increase of raw materials and the impact caused by the tax revisions introduced by the Government, was approved by the Cabinet.
