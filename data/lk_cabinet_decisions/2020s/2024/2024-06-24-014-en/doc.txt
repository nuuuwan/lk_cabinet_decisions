Amendment to the National innovation Agency Act No. 22 of 2019

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- National innovation Agency established in the year 2022 makes national policies and submits recommendations pertaining to social, science and technology, defence, eco and service innovations. According to the present day needs, the necessity of introducing several amendments to the the National Innovation Agency Act No.22 of 2019 has arisen in order to achieve the objectives of the institution. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the said Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
