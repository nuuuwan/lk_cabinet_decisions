Obtaining a loan from the Asian Development Bank to implement the Water Supply and Sanitation Reform Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Government of Sri Lanka is planning to obtain 02 credit facilities worth USD 200 million from the Asian Development Bank for the implementation of the Water Supply and Sanitation Reform Programme. Under the said programme, it has been proposed to implement 02 sub-programmes each amounting to USD 100 million by the Government of Sri Lanka. The discussions relating to obtaining the loan amount of USD 100 million for its first programme “Strengthening climate resilience, inclusion, and environmental sustainability of water supply operation”, have been completed. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies pertaining to obtaining the said credit facility, was approved by the Cabinet.
