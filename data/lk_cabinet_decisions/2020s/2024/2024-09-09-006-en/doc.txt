Introduction of New Regulator for the Petroleum Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the meeting held on 2024‑04‑25, to appoint a Committee to examine matters and make recommendations on the establishment of a regulatory mechanism for the Petroleum, Liquefied Petroleum Gas, Liquefied Natural Gas and Lubricant Sectors in Sri Lanka. The Committee appointed accordingly has submitted recommendations to introduce a new regulator for the petroleum sector. The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to implement the said Committee recommendations and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft a Bill to regulate the Petroleum Industry, was approved by the Cabinet.
