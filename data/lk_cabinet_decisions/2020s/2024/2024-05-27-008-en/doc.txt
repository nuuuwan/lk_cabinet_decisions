Appointment of a Committee to deal with the salary anomalies in the Public Sector

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-27 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Taking into account the representations made from time to time by the various employee categories in the Public Service and the relevant Trade Unions pertaining to the prevailing salary anomalies among said employee categories, the Cabinet decided to appoint a Committee of Experts to examine the said salary anomalies and submit a Report enabling to rectify those anomalies through the Budget for the year 2025.
