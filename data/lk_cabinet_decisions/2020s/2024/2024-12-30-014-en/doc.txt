Appointment to the Post of Government Printer

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The term of Office of Mrs. G.K.D Liyanage, who served in the Post of Government Printer in the Department of Government Printing has expired on 2024‑12‑24 and a suitable officer has to be appointed to the said vacant post. Mr. K.G.P. Pushpa Kumara, a Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as an Additional Director General in the Department of National Budget, has been identified as suitable to be appointed to the said post. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to appoint Mr Pushpa Kumara to the post of Government Printer, was approved by the Cabinet.
