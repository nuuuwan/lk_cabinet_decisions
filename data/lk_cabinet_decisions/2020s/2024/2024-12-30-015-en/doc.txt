Amendment to the Anti-Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Since the enactment of the Anti-Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023, the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption has encountered significant legal, interpretation and technical issues in conducting investigations, prosecutions, administrative and court proceedings as per the said Act.  Therefore, the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption has proposed to amend certain sections of the said Act and to introduce several new sections in to the said Act. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President to appoint a Committee comprising of senior officers from the Ministry of Justice and National Integration, the Attorney General's Department and the Legal Draftman's Department, under the chairmanship of a Commissioner of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption, to examine the provisions of the Anti-Corruption Act, No.9 of 2023 and submit a comprehensive Report with recommendations on the sections that need to be essentially amended and the new sections that need to be incorporated, was approved by the Cabinet.
