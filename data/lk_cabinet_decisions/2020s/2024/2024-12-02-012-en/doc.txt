Vote on Account - 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the Cabinet Meeting held on 2024 11 25 to present a Vote on Account for the period of first 04 months of the year 2025, in Parliament and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to draft a Resolution for the purpose. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Resolution relating to the Vote on Account drafted by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to move the said Resolution in Parliament, was approved by the Cabinet.
