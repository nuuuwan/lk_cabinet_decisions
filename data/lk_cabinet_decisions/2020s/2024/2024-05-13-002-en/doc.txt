Implementation of a Voluntary Retirement Scheme for the Staff of the Sri Lanka Savings Bank

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The National Savings Bank, as of now, has decided to acquire the assets of the Sri Lanka Savings Bank which became a fully owned subsidiary of the National Savings Bank in the year 2019, and to absorb its employees. Accordingly, the absorption process of the employees is currently underway and it has been decided to introduce a Voluntary Retirement Scheme as an alternative and to make a payment for the employees who retire voluntarily, taking into account the market factors. The proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies to implement the said Voluntary Retirement Scheme, was approved by the Cabinet.
