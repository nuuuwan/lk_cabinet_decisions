Amendments to the Public Officers' Security Ordinance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- According to the Public Officers' Security Ordinance, by imposing a Security Bond for the Public Officers, it is expected to recover a possible loss that may be caused by the said Officer in the process of executing his duties from the aforesaid bond.  At the Cabinet meeting held on 2023‑11‑20, it has been decided to appoint an Officials' Committee to identify the amendments required to fully revise the Public Officers' Security Ordinance, enabling to conduct the said process in a simple and timely manner. Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Justice and National Integration to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill to amend the Public Officers' Security Ordinance, based on the Report submitted by the said Officials' Committee.
