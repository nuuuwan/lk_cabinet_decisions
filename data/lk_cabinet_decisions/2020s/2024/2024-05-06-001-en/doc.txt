Digitization of the General Education in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-06 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- As per a project proposal submitted pertaining to the digital transformation of the general education system in Sri Lanka, the Government of China has extended its willingness to provide funds for the said purpose. A feasibility study relating to the implementation of the said project has also been carried out as of now. Through the proposed project, it is expected to provide Delivering Class Rooms, Receiving Class Rooms to schools and a Data Centre, Studio Room and a Conference Room to the Ministry of Education in order to operate those functions. It will enable to get the support of the said equipment to carry out effectively and efficiently the activities of implementing the blended learning systematically, sharing resources, providing the knowledge of teachers with special teaching skills for the students in schools when necessary, avoiding the problematic situations that arise when there is a shortage of teachers and facilitating the proposed education reforms. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Education to implement the proposed project, based on the relevant feasibility study Report, was approved by the Cabinet.
