Signing a Memorandum of Understanding between the Sri Lanka Institute of Biotechnology (Pvt) Ltd. and the Ohio State University of the United States of America

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Sri Lanka Institute of Biotechnology (Pvt) Ltd is an Institute which comes under the purview of the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology, established with the sole shareholdership of the Treasury for the achievement of the objectives such as, establishing a centralized biotechnological background with modern technology, developing a culture of innovation to accelerate the biotechnology industry by providing incubation technology facilities for the innovators, developing the new biotechnology industry to meet the global market needs and introducing renewable medicines to the health sector in Sri Lanka. With the aim of promoting cooperation in the fields of research and higher education, it has been proposed to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between the above Institution and the Ohio State University of the United States of America, with the intention of facilitating the research, innovation, education and cultural exchange. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister in her capacity as the Minister of Education, Science and Technology to sign the said Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties, was approved by the Cabinet.
