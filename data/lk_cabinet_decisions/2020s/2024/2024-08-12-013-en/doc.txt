Proposed Framework for leasing of lands to develop an internationally	recognized School, University and Hospital in Colombo Port City

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Section 6(1)(c) of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No. 11 of 2021, has entrusted the power to the Commission to lease the lands located within the territory of Colombo Port City and the Government marketable lands which has been vested with the Commission. Accordingly, the Colombo Port City Economic Commission has invited proposals to select the investors for the development of projects of School, University and Hospital in the Colombo Port City. Asiri Port City Hospital (Private) Limited and Gateway International School (Pvt) Ltd. have submitted a proposal to invest approximately USD 100 million for the Hospital,  USD 25 million for the school and  USD 25 million for the University. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion to enter into Agreements for the lease of plots of land for the Hospital, School and University as per the provisions of Section 6(1)(c) of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, was approved by the Cabinet.
