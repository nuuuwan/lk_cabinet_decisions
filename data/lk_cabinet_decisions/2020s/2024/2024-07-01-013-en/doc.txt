Amendment of the Employees' Provident Fund Act, No.15 of 1958

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Employees' Provident Fund has been established by the Employees' Provident Fund Act, No. 15 of 1958 for the social security of the private and semi‑government sector employees who are not entitled to a pension. It has been identified the necessity of amending the said Act to increase the number of members covered by the Act in accordance with the current socio-economic changes and to provide quality and efficient service to the members to suit the present. The proposal made by the Minister of Labour and Foreign Employment to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill based on the initial Draft prepared accordingly, was approved by the Cabinet.
