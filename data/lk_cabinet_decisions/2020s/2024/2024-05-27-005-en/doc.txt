Compulsory Retirement Compensation Scheme to be implemented with 	the amalgamation of Ceylon Fertilizer Company Limited (CFC) and 	Colombo Commercial Fertilizer Company Limited (CCFC)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-27 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Under the Compulsory Retirement Compensation Scheme to be implemented for the employees relating to the amalgamation of Ceylon Fertilizer Company Limited and Colombo Commercial Fertilizer Company Limited, 267 employees have expressed their consent to retire. Accordingly, Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Minister of Agriculture and Plantation Industries to implement the Compulsory Retirement Compensation Scheme to be implemented for the employees relating to the amalgamation of Ceylon Fertilizer Company Limited and Colombo Commercial Fertilizer Company Limited and incur Rs.844 million from the funds of the two fertilizer companies in order to implement the proposed Compulsory Retirement Compensation Scheme, subject to the recommendations of the Officials' Committee appointed under the Chairmanship of the Secretary to the Prime Minister regarding the retirement of excess employees of State Owned Enterprises under the oluntary Retirement Scheme.
