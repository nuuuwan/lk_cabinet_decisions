Reconstruction of Kurinchakerny Bridge on Kinniya-Kurinchakerny	Road

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited under Single Stage One Envelope method for the contract of reconstructing the Kurinchakerny Bridge on Kinniya-Kurinchakerny Road from the remaining funds of the Project on Development of the Badulla - Chenkalady road under the Road Network Development Project funded by the Saudi Fund for Development. 09 bids have been received for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to award the said Contract to M/s ELS Construction (Pvt) Ltd, the lowest substantially responsive bidder as per the recommendations of the Cabinet Appointed Standing Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
