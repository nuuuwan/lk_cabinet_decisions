Introduction of Digital Technology for Pirivena Education

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-24 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Ministry of Technology together with the Ministry of Education have planned to implement a programme to empower the pirivena education by introducing digital technology for pirivena education. Through this, the teachers affiliated to the Pirivenas and Bhikkus/lay students will be able to utilize digital technology to explore and analyze the Sri Lankan national Buddhist heritage. Accordingly, the joint proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Technology, and the Minister of Education to implement the proposed programme so that at least two computers, a smart board and a printer can be provided for each pirivena, was approved by the Cabinet.
