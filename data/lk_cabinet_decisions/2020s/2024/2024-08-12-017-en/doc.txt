Designate COLOMBO MARINA DEVELOPMENT (PRIVATE) LIMITED as a Primary Business of Strategic Importance

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- COLOMBO MARINA DEVELOPMENT (PRIVATE) LIMITED, a fully owned company of Browns Investment PLC, has been leased out 04 land plots within the Authority Area of the Colombo Port City for the Marina Development Project. The project has been designed with a Capitainerie with a full capacity which can accommodate 243 vessels, club houses for guests, retail outlets including food and beverages, entertainment venues, event spaces and car parking spaces. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Investment Promotion, to designate COLOMBO MARINA DEVELOPMENT (PRIVATE) LIMITED, as a Business of Strategic Importance as per the provisions of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, No.11 of 2021, and to grant exemptions and incentives for the said Business, was approved by the Cabinet.
