Establishment of the National Building Research Organization by an Actin Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In terms of the Cabinet Decision dated 2021-10-05, the Legal Draftsman was instructed to prepare a Bill to provide a legal recognition for National Building Research Organization. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the National Building Research Organization Bill drafted by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the President in his capacity as the Minister of Defence to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and submit the same thereafter, to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
