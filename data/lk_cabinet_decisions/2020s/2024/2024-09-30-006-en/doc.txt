Appointment to act in the Post of Controller General of Immigration and Emigration

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Since Mr. I.S.H.J. Ilukpitiya who served as the Controller General of Immigration and Emigration has been remanded from 2024‑09‑25 on an Order at the Supreme Court, a suitable officer has to be appointed immediately to attend to the duties in the said post. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Public Security to appoint Mrs. B.M.D Nilusha Balasooriya, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, presently serving as an Additional Secretary of the Ministry of Public Security, to act in the the Post of Controller General of Immigration and Emigration, with immediate effect, was approved by the Cabinet.
