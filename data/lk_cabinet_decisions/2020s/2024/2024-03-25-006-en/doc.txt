Project for the establishment 70 MW, AC ground mounted solar PV power 	plants in 1 to 5 MW, AC capacity

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-03-25 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2023‑10‑02 has granted approval to award 	the relevant contracts for the supply of 51MW to establish Solar PV Power 	Plants at 5 Grid Substations on Build, Own and Operate basis with 20 years 	operational period under the Project for establishment of 70MW, AC ground 	mounted Solar PV Power Plants in 1-5 MW AC capacity at 11 Grid 	Substations. Proposals have been re-invited for the supply of 19 MW for the 	remaining 06 Grid Substations. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister 	of Power and Energy to award the contracts for the establishment of  Solar PV 	Power Plants at Ampara, Habarana, Kosgama, Kurunegala, Pannala and 	Weyangoda Grid Substations, to the relevant investors, as recommended by the 	Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee taking into consideration the 	proposals submitted, was approved by the Cabinet.
