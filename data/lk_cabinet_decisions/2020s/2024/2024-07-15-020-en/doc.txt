Amendments  to  the  Code  of  Criminal  Procedure  Act  (Increase  of  Fines)

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  Cabinet  at  its  meeting  held  on  2023‑11‑27  has  granted  approval  to  amend  the    the  Criminal  Procedure  Act  so  as  to  increase  the  fines  in  the  Code  of  Criminal  Procedure  based  on  the  current  monetary  value.  Accordingly,  the  clearance  of  the  Attorney  General  has  been  received  for  the  amended  Bill  prepared  by  the  Legal  Draftsman.  As  such,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Justice,  Prison  Affairs  and  Constitutional  Reforms  to  publish  the  said  Bill  in  the  Government  Gazette  and  submit  the  same  thereafter,  to  Parliament,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
