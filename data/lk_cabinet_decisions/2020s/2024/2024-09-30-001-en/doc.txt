Making Provisions for the General Election 2024

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- By the Gazette Extraordinary Notification No.2403/13 dated 2024‑09‑24, the 09th Parliament was dissolved and 2024‑11‑14 has been fixed as the date for the Parliamentary General Election. However, provisions required to conduct a Parliamentary General Election have not been allocated within the budget estimates 2024. In such case, it was decided to grant the concurrence of the Cabinet for the action taken by the Hon. President to issue Rs.05 billion from the Consolidated Fund for the year 2024, in terms of the powers vested with the President by paragraph (4) of Article 150 of the Constitution for the issuance of funds from the Consolidated Fund to conduct a Parliamentary General Election. Further, approval of the Cabinet was also granted to allocate Rs.06 billion for the expenses to be settled in the year 2025 for conducting the General Election, from the Vote on Account to be submitted to Parliament for the year 2025.
