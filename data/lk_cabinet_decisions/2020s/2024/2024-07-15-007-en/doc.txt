Awarding  the  Contract  for  provisioning  of  Automated  Seat  Upgrade	Bidding  Services  and  Associated  Ancillary  Services  for  SriLankan  	Airlines  	Limited

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- SriLankan  Airlines  Limited  is  currently  using  the  Plusgrade  system  for  Automated  Seat  Upgrade  Bidding  Services  and  Associated  Ancillary  Services.  This  service  increases  the  revenue  of  the  Airline  service  by  providing  an  opportunity  for  a  passenger  holding  an  economy  class  flight  ticket  to  bid  for  a  business  class  ticket  when  there  are  unreserved  seats  available  in  business  class.  Bids  have  been  invited  under  the  International  Competitive  Bidding  Procedure  for  maintaining  this  service,  and  02  bids  have  been  received  for  that.	The  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Ports,  Shipping  and  Aviation  to  evaluate  said  bids  and  thereafter,  to  award  the  Contract  for  provisioning  of  Automated	Seat  Upgrade  Bidding  Services  and  Associated  Ancillary  Services  for	SriLankan  Airlines  to  Plusgrade  Inc.  for  a  period  of  five  (05)  years,  based  on  the  recommendations  of  the  Technical  Evaluation  Committee  and  the  Standing  Cabinet    Appointed  Procurement  Committee,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
