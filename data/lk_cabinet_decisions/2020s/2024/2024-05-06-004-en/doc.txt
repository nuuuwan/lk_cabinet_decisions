Development of the 484 MW of Wind Power Plants in Mannar and Pooneryn

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-06 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2022‑03‑07 to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding for the development of Wind Power Plants in Mannar and Pooneryn by M/s Adani Green Energy Limited, India. Accordingly, Cabinet Appointed Negotiation Committee has been appointed to evaluate the Project Proposal submitted by the above company. The proposal made by the Minister of Power and Energy to determine the value of US$ 8.26 cents per Kilowatt hour as the final tariff (subject to payment in Sri Lankan Rupees as per the actual exchange rate) for the electricity generated under the proposed project and to award the tender to M/s Adani Green Energy SL Limited for purchasing of electricity for a period of 20 years, based on the recommendations of the said committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
