Establishment of a University of Science and Teachnology in Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-01-08 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- With the objective of preparing the foundation to create a cadre of skilled	professionals to take the leadership in critical sectors of new scientific 	discoveries, technological  innovation and sustainable development, it has been 	identified the appropriateness of establishing a University of Science and 	Technology in collaboration with a renowned university in United States of 	America. Such a university will cater to utilize cutting-edge methodologies, 	foster global 	academic connections and create an environment for research and 	development. The  proposal made by the Hon. President to select one partner 	university from among several identified universities including UC Berkeley, 	UC Riverside, UC Davis, University of Michigan and establish the proposed 	University of Science and Technology as a Public-Private Partnership in 	collaboration with the said university, was approved by the Cabinet.
