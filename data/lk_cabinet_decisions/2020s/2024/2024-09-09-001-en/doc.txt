Obtaining a loan facility from the Asian Development Bank for the Financial Sector Stability and Reform Programme of Sri Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the meeting held on 2023‑09‑25 to  obtain a Policy-Based Loan amounting to USD 400 million from the Asian Development Bank, under 02 sub‑programmes, in order to implement the Financial Sector Stability and Reform Programme of Sri lanka. Accordingly, a loan amounting to USD 200 million has been granted for the first sub‑programme by December 2023. In order to obtain USD 200 million under its second sub-program, 12 pre-policy activities have to be completed and as of now, most of the said policy activities have already been completed or are in the final stages of their completion. Therefore, the proposal made by the Hon. President, in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Stabilization and National Policies, to obtain a loan amount of USD 200 million from the Asian Development Bank under the second sub‑programme of the Financial Sector Stability and Reform Programme of Sri Lanka, was approved by the Cabinet.
