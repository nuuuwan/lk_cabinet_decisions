Issuance of a New Order under section 22 of the Foreign Exchange Act, No.12 of 2017

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-18 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In order to preserve the foreign exchange reserves in the country, Orders have been issued from time to time under section 22 of the Foreign Exchange Act,    No. 12 of 2017, since 2020‑04‑02, on the recommendations of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, to implement Capital Flow Measures by temporarily suspending and limiting outward remittances related to certain capital transactions. Taking into account the improvements in the domestic foreign exchange market and future prospects, 10 Orders have been issued, since June 2023 up to now, with the intention of gradually removing/relaxing certain suspensions/limitations, and the validity period of the existing Order is due to expire on 2024‑12‑19. The Central Bank of Sri Lanka has proposed that it is appropriate to issue a new Order to be effective until June 2025, further amending certain suspensions and limitations imposed under the existing Order. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to issue an Order under section 22 of the Foreign Exchange Act,  No.12 of 2017, for a period of six (06) months to be effective 2024‑12‑20, was approved by the Cabinet.
