Implementation   of  the  Tariff  Policy  and  Formula  to  review  the  tariff  for  	the  National  Water  Supply  and  Drainage  Board

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  new  Tariff  Policy  and  Formula  of  the  National  Water  Supply  and  Drainage  Board  was  discussed  at  the  Cabinet  meeting  held  on  2024-04-08,  where  it  has  been  directed  to  present  the  new  Tariff  Policy  and  Formula  by  incorporating    the  necessary  revisions  in  consultation  with  all  the  relevant  parties.  Accordingly,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Water  Supply  and  Estate  Infrastructure  Development  to  implement  the  revised  Water  Tariff  Policy  and  the  revised  Water  Tariff  Formula  prepared  accordingly,  from  year  2025,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
