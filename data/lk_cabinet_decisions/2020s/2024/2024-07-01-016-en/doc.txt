Conducting “Wigamanika Harasara” Programme

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Migrant workers were requested to contribute at least USD 500 million per month	through legal channels to reduce the trade deficit faced by Sri Lanka in early 2022.	Based on the positive response given by the migrant workers to that request, the	worker remittances have been raised up to USD 11 billion during the period from	June 2022 to May 2024. Therefore, the proposal made by the Minister of Labour and	Foreign Employment to implement a programme named as “Wigamanika Harasara”	in appreciation of the great contribution	made by the migrant workers during the	finance and economic recession.
