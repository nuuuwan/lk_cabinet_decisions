Uninterrupted  functioning  of  teaching  in  schools  and  patient  care	services  in  	hospitals

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  attention  of  the  Cabinet  was  drawn  to  the  pressure  imposed  and  irregular    actions  carried  out  by  certain  groups,  hindering  the  teaching  in  schools  during  school  hours  and  causing  disruption  to  the  patient  care  services  in  hospitals  and  the  Cabinet  decided  to  take  necessary  action  to  introduce  new  laws  to  prevent  the  injustices  caused  to  school  children  and  patients  through  such  unlawful  acts.
