Technology Promotion Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-13 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2023‑12‑18 to make necessary institutional reforms for digital transformation and to prepare a concept paper to amend the Information and Communication Technology Act, No. 27 of 2003. Accordingly, a concept paper has been prepared to introduce new legislation for digital transformation, and it has been proposed to establish a Technology Innovation Council and a Digital Transformation Agency. The proposal made by the Hon.President to introduce the Technology Promotion Act by repealing the Information and Communication Technology Act No. 27 of 2003 and to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill based on the concept paper that has been prepared, was approved by the Cabinet.
