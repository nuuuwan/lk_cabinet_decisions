The  First  Programme  of  Cooperation  in  the  fields  of  Education,  	Science,	Culture,  Mass  Media,  Youth  and  Sports  between  Sri  Lanka  	and  	Romania

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The  Government  of  the  Democratic  Socialist  Republic  of  Sri  Lanka  and  the  Government  of  Romania  have  entered  into  an  agreement  in  order  to  promote  cultural  cooperation  between  the  two  countries.  According  to  the  said  agreement,  the  relevant  parties  have  agreed  for  the  First  Programme  of  Cooperation  in  the  fields  of  Education,  Science,  Culture,  Mass  Media,  Youth  and  Sports.  The  proposal  made  by  the  Acting  Minister  of  Foreign  Affairs  to  sign  the  Memorandum  of  Understanding  on  the  implementation  of  the  said  Programme  of  Cooperation  during  the  official  visit  to  Romania  to  be  made  by  the  Minister  of  Foreign  Affairs,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
