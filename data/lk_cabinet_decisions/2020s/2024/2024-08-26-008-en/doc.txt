Administrative Arrangement between the Delegation of the European	Union arriving in Sri Lanka for observation of the Presidential Election -	2024, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-26 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Chairman of the Election Commission has invited the European Union to send an Election Observation Mission to the country to observe the Presidential Election 2024. Administrative Arrangements should be signed between the European Union Delegation visiting Sri Lanka and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs before starting the Election Observation activities under the regulations of the said Union. Accordingly, the Administrative Arrangements relating to Observation of Presidential Election 2024 have been signed between the two parties on 2024‑08‑05 and the information presented by the Minister of Foreign Affairs in that regard were noted by the Cabinet.
