International Interests in Mobile Equipment (Cape Town Convention) Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-09 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2023-01-02 has granted approval to introduce the necessary legal provisions to access the International Interests in Mobile Equipment (Cape Town Convention) and the Protocol of the International Interests in Mobile Equipment on matters specific to Aircraft Equipment. Accordingly, the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill drafted by the Legal Draftsman. The proposal made by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Aviation to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter, present the same in Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
