Providing relief to pensioners, paddy farmers and fishermen

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-14 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Cabinet approved the proposal made by the Hon. President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Economic Development, Policy Formulation, Planning and Tourism to manage, as appropriate, the provisions required to implement the Cabinet decisions taken to provide a monthly interim allowance of Rs.3,000/- for all pensioners, to increase the fertilizer subsidy up to Rs.25,000/- per hectare to a maximum of 2 hectares for farmers cultivating paddy in the Maha Season 2024/25, and to provide a financial assistance to fishing vessel owners, through the provisions allocated under the Development Programme of the Department of National Budget.
