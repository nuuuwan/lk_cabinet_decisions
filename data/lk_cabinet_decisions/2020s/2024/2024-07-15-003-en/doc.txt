Providing  a  land  located  at  Wedamulla,  Kelaniya  to  H.M.K.  Property	Developers  (Pvt)  Ltd.  for  a  Housing  Project  approved  by  Board  of	Investment  of  Sri  Lanka

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval  has  been  granted  previously  at  the  Cabinet  Sub-Committee  meeting	on  Investment  Promotion  to  grant  approval  to  provide  a  land  of  about  12  	acres in  extent  located  in  Wedamulla,  Kelaniya  owned  by  the  Sri  Lanka  Land	Development  Corporation  to  H.M.K.  Property  Developers  (Pvt)  Ltd.  However,	due  to  the  company's  failure  to  adhere  with  the  relevant  agreements,  no  steps	have  been  taken  so  far  to  hand  over  the  relevant  plot  of  land  to  the  said  	company.  H.M.K.  Property  Developers  (Pvt)  Ltd.  had  made  a  request  again  	through  the  Board  of  Investment  of  Sri  Lanka  to  obtain  the  relevant  plot  of  	land  in  order  to  implement  a  Housing  Project  under  03  stages  of  that  land.  	Accordingly,  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Urban  Development  and  	Housing  to  provide  a  plot  of  land  with  the  extent  of  08  acres  -  02  roods  -  	16.26  perches  identified  for  the  implementation  of  the  proposed  project,  to  the  	said  company  based  on  the  current  valuation  submitted  by  the  Government  	Chief  Valuer,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
