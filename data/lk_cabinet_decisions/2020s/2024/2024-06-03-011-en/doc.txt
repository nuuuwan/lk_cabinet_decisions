Providing relief to the communities who are affected by the SouthWest	Monsoon

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-06-03 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Thousands of families have become temporarily helpless due to heavy rains in many districts of the island, resulting in landslides, tree falls, floods andsevere damage to public infrastructure systems owing to the weather changes that are developing unexpectedly with the activation of the Southwest Monsoon this year. Therefore, the proposal made by the Hon. Prime Minister to provide relief for the families who are affected and implement a prompt programme with immediate effect to restore the damaged public infrastructure facilities to a state of normality by obtaining the service of Triforces and Civil Security Forces and with the fullest  contribution of other relevant Ministries, was approved by the Cabinet.
