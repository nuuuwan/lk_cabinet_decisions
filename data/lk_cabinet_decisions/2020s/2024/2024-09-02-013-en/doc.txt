Submission of the Registration of Persons (Amendment) Bill to Parliament

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-09-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Cabinet at its meeting held on 2020‑11‑16 has instructed the Legal Draftsman to draft a Bill to amend the Registration of Persons Act No. 32 of 1968 in order to facilitate the implementation of the Unified Digital Identity Framework in Sri Lanka. The clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the Bill prepared by the Legal Draftsman accordingly. The proposal made by the Minister of Public Security to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and to submit the same, thereafter to Parliament for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
