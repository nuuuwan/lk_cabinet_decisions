Establishment of the National Quality Council

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-01 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The necessity of establishing a National Quality Council has been pointed out through the National Quality Policy submitted in the year 2016, the National Quality Infrastructure Strategy 2018-2022 and the National Export Strategy of Sri Lanka 2018-2022. At present, National Quality Institutions and other National Quality Regulatory Agencies are functioning as independent institutions under the scopes of various Line Ministries. As such, the proposal made by the Minister of Trade, Commerce and Food Security to appoint a Working Committee with the representations of the relevant Line Ministries and National Quality Institutions to implement the proposal for the establishment of the National Quality Council, was approved by the Cabinet.
