Resettlement of unauthorized dwellers residing in Kelaniweli Railway Reserve from Pagiriwatta Railway Station to Padukka Railway Station

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-05 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The unauthorized dwellers residing in the railway reserve from Maligawatta Loco junction to Padukka Railway Station are resettled using the local funds under the Colombo Suburban Railway Project and the resettlement of the unauthorized dwellers in the section upto Kirulapana Railway Station has been completed so far. A large percentage of the residents of Railway Reserve in the section from Pagiriwatta Railway Station to Padukka Railway Station have been living insecurely and illegally in the Railway Reserve for over 30 years, out of which about 350 families have been identified for resettlement. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to provide housing facilities for the said families by using the housing units of the Colombage Mawatha Housing Complex belonging to the Urban Development Authority and the Housing Complex being built in the Kottawa Malapalla area by the Railway Project, was approved by the Cabinet.
