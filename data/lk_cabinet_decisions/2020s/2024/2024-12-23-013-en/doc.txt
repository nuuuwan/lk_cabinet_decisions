Appointment to the Post of Director General of Pensions

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-23 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The term of office of Mr. A. Jagath D. Dias who is serving as the Director General of the Department of Pensions, is due to expire on 2024 12 31 and a suitable officer has to be appointed to the said post with effect from 2025 01 01. Mr Chaminda Hettiarachchi, Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service, serving as the District Secretary/Government Agent of the Administrative District of Trincomalee, has been identified as suitable to be appointed to the said Post. Accordingly, the Proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government, to appoint him to the Post of Director General of Pensions, to be effective from 2025 01 01, was approved by the Cabinet.
