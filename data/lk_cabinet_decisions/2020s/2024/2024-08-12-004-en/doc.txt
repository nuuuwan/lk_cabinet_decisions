Implementation of the Sri Lanka Coastal Zone and Coastal Resources Management Plan - 2024-2029

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-08-12 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- In terms of the provisions of the Coast Conservation Act, No. 57 of 1981, it is required to prepare and implement a Coastal Zone and Coastal Resources Management Plan in order to manage the coastal zone in Sri Lanka and the said plan should be updated once in every five (05) years. Accordingly, the  'Sri Lanka Coastal Zone and Coastal Resources Management Plan - 2024- 2029' has been prepared by amending the plan prepared and implemented in the year 2018. The proposal made by the Minister of Urban Development and Housing to implement the said plan, was approved by the Cabinet.
