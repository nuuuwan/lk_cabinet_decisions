Procuring  medicinal  Ginger  required  for  manufacturing  purposes  in  the  Sri  Lanka  Ayurvedic  Drugs  Corporation

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-15 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Medicinal  ginger  is  used  as  an  ingredient  for  87  types  of  medicines  manufactured  by  the  Sri  Lanka  Ayurvedic  Drugs  Corporation,  and  currently  there  is  a  severe  shortage  of  medicinal  ginger  in  the  market.  There  is  a  stock  of  50  tons  of  medicinal  ginger  that  has  been  confiscated  by  the  Sri  Lanka  Customs,  and  the  the  proposal  made  by  the  Minister  of  Health  to  get  the  said  stock  of  medicinal  ginger  at  concessional  prices  to  the  Sri  Lanka  Ayurveda  Drugs  Corporation,  was  approved  by  the  Cabinet.
