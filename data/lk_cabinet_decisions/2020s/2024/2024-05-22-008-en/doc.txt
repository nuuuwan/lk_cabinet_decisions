Rescue, Rehabilitation and Insolvency Bill

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-05-22 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at its meeting held on 2023 06 26 to the Law Commission to discuss with the relevant parties and prepare a preliminary draft in order to introduce a unified Insolvency Law. Accordingly, with the technical expertise of the World Bank and in consultation with interested government and private sector stakeholders, the Law Commission has prepared a preliminary draft as a basis for the preparation of the Rescue, Rehabilitation and Insolvency Bill. The joint proposal made by the Hon. President and the Minister of Justice, Prison Affairs and Constitutional Reforms to instruct the Legal Draftsman to prepare a bill for the relevant purpose based on the said preliminary draft, was approved by the Cabinet.
