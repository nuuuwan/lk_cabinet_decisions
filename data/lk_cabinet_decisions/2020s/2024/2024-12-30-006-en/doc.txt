Operation of Unisex Branded Apparel Shops in the Departure/Transit area at Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-30 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Bids have been invited following the International Competitive Procurement Procedure to select operators for 04 shops/outlets with unisex branded apparel in the Departure/Transit area of the passenger terminal building of the Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake for a period of 03 years. 03 bidders have submitted bids for the same. Accordingly, the proposal made by the Minister of Transport, Highways, Ports and Civil Aviation to select M/s Odel PLC and M/s Insomnia (Pvt.) Ltd. as the operators for two outlets and to re-invite bids for the remaining outlets, based on the recommendations of the Standing Cabinet Appointed Procurement Committee, was approved by the Cabinet.
