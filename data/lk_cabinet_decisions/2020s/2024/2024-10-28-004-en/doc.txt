Granting the land where the Southern Provincial Advisory and Extension Centre of the Tea Research Institute of Sri Lanka is functioning, on long term lease basis

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-10-28 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- The Southern Provincial Advisory and Extension Centre of the Tea Research Institute of Sri Lanka has been operating in Kottawa, Galle since 1961 and the extent of land presently enjoyed by the said Centre is 69.63 acres. This premises is used for tea field demonstrations for tea growers, officials related to the tea industry, university students and the students who follow training courses and school students. The proposal made by the Minister of Environment, Wildlife, Forest Resources, Water Supply, Plantation and Community Infrastructure to grant the said land on long term lease basis, enabling to maintain the said Centre more formally and productively, was approved by the Cabinet.
