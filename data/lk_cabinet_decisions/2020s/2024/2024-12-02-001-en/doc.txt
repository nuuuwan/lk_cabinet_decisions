77th Independence Day Ceremony falling on February 04, 2025

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-12-02 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2024 10 14 to appoint a Cabinet Committee under the chairmanship of the Hon. President to guide, direct and monitor the organizing of the 77th Independence Day Commemoration Ceremony, which falls on 04-02-2025. The proposal made by the Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government to appoint a Cabinet Sub-Committee under the chairmanship of the Hon. President and with the participation of the Hon. Prime Minister to provide necessary guidance in organizing the 77th Independence Day Commemoration Ceremony falling on 04 February 2025, including the Ministers in charge of the relevant subjects who have been appointed as Members of the Cabinet after the Parliamentary Election, was approved by the Cabinet.
