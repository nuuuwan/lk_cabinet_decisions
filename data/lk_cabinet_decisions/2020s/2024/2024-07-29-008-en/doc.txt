Amending the National Transport Commission Act, No.37 of 1991

Press briefing of Cabinet Decision taken on 2024-07-29 (Subject to Confirmation at the next Meeting)

- Approval of the Cabinet has been granted at the Cabinet meeting held on 2022‑09‑22 to amend the National Transport Commission Act, No.37 of 1991. Accordingly, the Legal Draftsman has prepared a Bill and the clearance of the Attorney General has been received for the same. The proposal made by the Minister of Transport and Highways to publish the said Bill in the Government Gazette and thereafter, present the same in Parliament, for approval, was approved by the Cabinet.
